# C. W. Smith — Cross-Oeuvre Concept Concordance

A map of the ideas that recur across C. W. Smith's body of work, built from close reading
of the full texts: the **novel** *A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight*
(2020), the **novella** *Antinomicity* (2022), *Everyone I Love is Alive in the Unlimited
Present of the City and its Waters* (2025), *Fellow Disjecta, Oh Sunny Danger Time* (2024,
with *Sovereignty is an Artificial Soul* and *The Glass House*), and the omnibus
*Pastoral Scanlines: Selected Works 2020–2025*. Quotes are verbatim; use them accurately.

## The keystone: the surgery IS the birth
At the novel's end the "complicated surgery on the beach" is revealed to be the **birth of the
narrator's child**: he becomes Leif — pushing his blindfolded, heart-monitored wife in a
wheelchair into a delivery room with red hibiscus light on her gown, holding her hands "so tight
it feels like her bones are going to fuse into mine." Katita's line recentres everything:
"There is nothing wrong with your heart. And there is no surgery to fix what is wrong with mine."
**Surgery is what love is; frame and embedded story fuse.** "If every song is a love song then
every story is a ghost story of survival in the face of the void."

## 1. Ontological incompleteness
The gap / the negative ("less than nothing," −1 between real 0 and ideal +1) / the Möbius strip /
"that gap is you" / the city that vanishes at certain angles / poems "ontologically incomplete."
- Novel: "the curious ontological incompleteness of Newcastle… the landscape has already folded
  up and disappeared." The Möbius epiphany: "Every coin is one sided. Holy shit. I was going to
  be a father."
- *Antinomicity*: "not… epistemological… but an ontological incompleteness, rising out of a
  negative space… less than nothing"; "that gap is you."
- *Everyone I Love*: "the lived experience of our everyday reality is the surplus remainder
  created by the failure of the impossible fullness… It is this gap that gives form to our world";
  "The butterflies and the marigolds / are ontologically incomplete."
- *The Glass House* (Zizek/Hegel): the video-game building that "doesn't contain anything inside" =
  the gap after a story ends, where characters live "only… in your mind."
- *Pastoral Scanlines* ("Autoincorrect"): "how to not know, how to be eternally uncertain, how to
  be full of ontological gaps. That's the secret sauce."

## 2. Analogy
"Analogy is the core of all cognition"; consciousness as "an analogy of itself"; subject-as-object;
two-that-is-one; thought as mirroring (Hofstadter).
- Novel: "Analogy is the core of all cognition"; "the only way a thought can exist is when it
  mirrors itself against its equivalent… The voice of your mother only becomes what it is when you
  hear your own cry in comparison." First memory = seeing himself "from two positions at once and
  hence created an analogy."
- *Antinomicity*: "Consciousness, subjectivity, is an analogy of itself"; walking as "a hunt for
  external landscapes that analogously mirror the internal landscape of subjectivity."
- *Pastoral Scanlines* (preface): Hofstadter — "all thoughts are analogies… the core property of
  any idea… is one of analogy, of relationship, of mirroring"; "subject as object."

## 3. Antinomy / doubling / the coin
Two-that-is-one; mirrors and twins; the Fighter/the Failure; day/night; the one-sided coin; the
conjoined leaves that name *Antinomicity*.
- Novel: epigraph "You can nullify a fireball with a fireball"; the coin (day/night, father/not);
  Katita: "There is one side to a coin and it goes the whole way around"; "Every coin is one sided."
- *Antinomicity*: epigraph (Zupančič on Nietzsche's midday: "the moment when 'One turns to Two'");
  "two that is also one, as a dialectic… an antagonism that supports this structure through
  conflict"; the conjoined leaves: "one leaf was meant to grow there, but then two developed in a
  state of antinomicity."
- *Pastoral Scanlines*: "the antinomy of Leif's eye contact"; Wagner — "polar opposites are more
  intimately connected than disparate notions"; "back to the beginning / No. back to where we
  started"; "Round like a zero. There it goes again. Plop."

## 4. The unfinishable / failure-as-revelation (the glitch as method)
The outline form; the two halves that won't join; the productive wound; "the revelation is in the
breach." Musical emblems: Wagner's Parsifal (novel) and Bach's counterpoint (*Antinomicity*).
- Novel: "I have never been able to work out how the second half… should connect with the first…
  The link does not compute"; "It is like Wagner says through Parsifal. The wound can only be
  healed by the sword that made it." The narrator is "an outliner," not a novelist.
- *Antinomicity*: "Art that works is art that fails, because intentions are arrows that hit when
  they miss… the revelation is in the breach"; Bach's gap = "the sonic representation of the birth
  of meaning… between signal and noise."
- *Everyone I Love*: "the failure is the revelation, the failure is the endpoint"; the four poems
  (Bodies, Decades, Flowers, Nights) "collapsed after being asked to wear too much reality."
- *Pastoral Scanlines* ("To Weave, To Fabricate" repeats the "arrows that hit when they miss"
  formulation; the children's disco: "such a dance worthy tune ensures that it will not be able to
  be danced to").

## 5. Recurrence / "instruments of return" / deepening one life vs inventing many
The cycle that is not a trap but a deepening; "Leif" = anagram of "Life"; Da Capo al Fine.
- Novel: two paper notes left on his stomach 18 years apart; "History as a Möbius strip. It rhymes
  as it repeats"; "You never outlearn / the old mistakes of your youth"… "they were never really
  mistakes to begin with."
- *Everyone I Love*: "History is not a timeline; it's a boomerang, with the future arcing backwards
  into the past"; four generations at the Bogey Hole.
- *Surplus of the Seen* (planning): "Leif and Katita are not recycled characters. They are
  instruments of return."
- *Hesse* (*Pastoral Scanlines*): the truths of 15 inhabited differently at 40 — "deeper, not
  different"; "The truth has a million faces, but there is only one truth."

## 6. The empty / depopulated world
Empty World Meditations; "a city reaches its functional peak once it becomes empty of people";
last-person fantasies (Wittgenstein's Mistress; Super Mario Clouds).
- Novel: the wish to "blink off the side of the screen"; "I could call them Empty World
  Meditations"; "two small toy people walking across a vast kingdom of dirt"; the depopulated
  Shanbudia ("as much commotion as morning traffic on the moon"); one foot in each lane of the
  empty highway.
- *Antinomicity*: "a city reaches its functional peak once it becomes empty of people… the city
  becomes an instrument, and the sunlight… the bow with which it is played… love songs in their
  most pure form"; "music to inhabit the peopleless gaps in city streets."
- *Everyone I Love*: Empty World Meditations #3, #9, #27; "I want to be alone in the world, while
  all the people I love are alive and well."

## 7. The surplus of the seen / foreverism / recreation in the AI age
"Slow cancellation of the future" (Fisher) / "Foreverism" (Tanner); AI as "the ultimate nostalgia
machine"; everything "cross-referenced as already done."
- *Their Most August Public Organ*: "the surplus of the seen has taken all possible versions of
  the lived and imagined world and represented them through recreations that ensure we will never
  do anything ever again that can't be cross referenced as already done."
- *Everyone I Love*: "no new movies, just remakes and sequels; no new music, just remixes…"; "AI is
  the ultimate nostalgia machine, the final remix of human intelligence."
- *Pastoral Scanlines*: "Our local data farms have been stacked with endless Summers";
  "perpetual foreverism"; "cryptopastoralism."

## 8. Psychogeography / walking / Newcastle ("Supernovacastria")
The city as text and laboratory; buildings unseeable from certain angles; walking as epistemology.
- Novel: Newcastle's "bite sized labyrinth of streets… harbourside industry and lighthouse shine";
  Rose Cottage / Bolton Street car park as buildings "chanced upon."
- *Antinomicity*: "Psychogeography is what propels me forward, in both the physical and the
  metaphysical sense"; "the entire universe is stationed in Newcastle."
- *Everyone I Love*: inheriting "Newcastle as a psychogeographic laboratory"; the Jolly Roger
  ruins; "into the ontologically incomplete."
- *Pastoral Scanlines* ("Leaving/Leading"): the coinage "(super)Novacastria."

## 9. Disjecta
Fragments, ephemera, collected detritus; the disjunctive method ("separate and dissolve").
- Novel: the microscope dinners — "I place one or another of the disjecta beneath the lens."
- *Antinomicity*: the artist who draws "the same ephemera disjecta… bent wire fences"; "a tumble
  of disjecta that roll around my head."
- *Fellow Disjecta*: the title; "separate and dissolve… walk into a room, disintegrate completely,
  and then write a memoir"; the hacked garden's floppy-disk plant labels.
- *The Glass House*: "I… trespass and collect disjecta from sites I know are falling into ruin."
- Music: the Wrenasmir album *Disjecta Songjiang '11–'15*.

## 10. Sound / music / radio — the polyacoustic
Music emulating the mother's voice; name-as-heartbeat; pirate radio; the detuned broadcast; the
Karman line; "wreck a nice beach / recognise speech."
- Novel: "Beach… is one thump of a heartbeat. Surgery is three thumps"; "all music… is always
  attempting to emulate the human voice"; the Karman line — "the sound of the earth rubbing
  against space."
- *Antinomicity*: "rhythm was invented to mimic the beating of our mother's heart, and melody
  conjured to imitate the sound of our mother calling our name"; the train recording stretched 8×
  into "a bag full of alarm clocks dragged to the bottom of an empty public pool."
- *Fellow Disjecta*: the pirate radio (how he met his wife, a clock-maker); "wreck a nice beach /
  recognise speech."
- *Pastoral Scanlines* ("Selector"): the heartbeat replaces the cello, ending "Peter. / Mother."

## 11. Pastoral as data / cyberpastoralism
The countryside as cloud storage; synthesised nature; hacked agriculture; "cryptopastoralism."
- Novel: "Pastures now hold warehouses filled with hard drives… To be a farmer is to be a data
  farmer"; Changi's butterflies between glass and jet fuel.
- *Antinomicity*: "engaging with a virtual nature is better than no nature at all"; the wife's
  critique — "you record the sound of a passing train."
- *Fellow Disjecta*: the solar-punk community's hacked garden.
- *Pastoral Scanlines*: "cryptopastoralism rewriting the history of sun smudged wheat fields";
  drones instead of bees; "streaming data pouring down… in some sort of augmented reality sun
  shower… increasingly low polygon."

## 12. Gerald Murnane's influence
"Time is place" / "place after place"; *The Plains*; the walking-book lineage (Sebald's *Rings of
Saturn*); the glimpsed-but-unrecoverable image (the unrepeatable bird).
- Novel: epigraph — "The invisible is only what is too brightly lit" (*The Plains*); *The Rings of
  Saturn* as "a walking book… I want to write a book like that one day"; the wife's unrepeatable
  bird.
- *Fellow Disjecta* / *The Glass House*: two real typewritten letters from Murnane — "what we call
  time is our confused perception of place after place"; the Glass House "is not getting older, it
  is simply moving from one place into another kind of place."
- *Everyone I Love*: Murnane discussed re: neurodivergence; "there is only landscape."

## 13. Mortality / amor fati
"Everyone I love is alive right now"; the year of fours; Nietzsche's eternal recurrence.
- Novel: the death of Les ("harder to get to the second breath… as I keep returning to the
  first"); "Amor fati. I want to sweat and get as hot as possible."
- *Everyone I Love*: "Everyone I love is alive right now. I won't be able to say that forever";
  "Peace is not a lull between complications — it is knowing that it can embrace all possible
  complications."
- *Fellow Disjecta*: the year of fours; the son in hospital — "Grass must grow and children must
  die… think about what both of these conditions do to us"; the rock pool — "my amor fati."
- *The Glass House*: "Amor Fati… the point isn't… how one could best live forever; the point is to
  recognise how not to look back."

## 14. Neurodiversity / disability / cognitive capacity
"No version of humanity is more correct"; the social model of disability; the stutter / outsider's
advantage; autism as overabundance of feeling.
- Novel: "There is no version of humanity or cognitive capacity that is more correct or suitable
  than any other… Nature is perfect but nurture is not"; foundational truth must be "accessible to
  everybody regardless of cognitive capacity."
- *Antinomicity*: the stutter — "while you might not succeed by going down the first, most obvious
  route, there are other possibilities… more statistically in your favour"; the outsider's advantage.
- *Everyone I Love*: autism as "too much empathy… compressing it onto the page… into the gap
  between the self and the world" — making the neurodivergent writer structurally identical to the
  incomplete poem.
- *Pastoral Scanlines* ("Beyond Correct and Incorrect Nature"): "no version of humanity is more
  correct"; "A Spectrum of Real" (Routledge, *Gaming Disability*).

## Cross-concept clusters (how the ideas bind)
- **The gap = body = city = poem = the neurodivergent self.** Ontological incompleteness (1) is the
  abstraction; the Jolly Roger ruins and the empty city (6, 8) and the four failed poems (4) and the
  autistic "gap between self and world" (14) are its instances.
- **Analogy ↔ antinomy ↔ recurrence.** To think is to mirror (2); the mirror is two-that-is-one (3);
  to return is to mirror across time (5).
- **Disjecta → cyberpastoralism → foreverism.** The fragments (9) are the particles; the data-farm
  (11) the infrastructure; foreverism (7) the ideology — the oeuvre performs and critiques this.
- **Murnane's "place after place" dissolves time** — the answer to foreverism (7) and the form of
  recurrence (5): not a loop but a sequence of places, each fully itself.
- **Beach = surgery = heartbeat = amor fati.** The beach (10, 13) is where the earth meets space
  (Karman line), where the surgery (= birth, = love) happens, where the heartbeat is most audible,
  and where "nothing was a mistake."
