Counterclockwise (film)

Counterclockwise
時計回り (tentative English title)
Feature film
Release ████  2018, festival circuit
Runtime87 minutes
LanguageJapanese
FormatExperimental narrative

Counterclockwise is an experimental feature film exploring the cycle's reversal through reversed chronology. It begins at Leif's ascent and fall and unfolds backward toward Bolton Street, attempting to render the glitch transparent through pure inversion.

The soundtrack consists of spinal resonance tones in descending pitch—a sonification of Katita's theoretical Karman reversal. A sequence featuring the mechanical seagull renders it motionless while the world rotates—inverting the narrative's kinetic agency.

Distribution remains disputed; festival records conflict on premiere venue  ██████████ . Its influence on later Japanese adaptations is acknowledged but not formally documented [citation needed]. Critics debate whether backward sequencing achieves clarification or produces a triple glitch [1].].
