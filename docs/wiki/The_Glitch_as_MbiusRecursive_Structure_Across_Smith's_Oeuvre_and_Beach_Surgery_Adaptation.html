[1]] between Half One and Half Two is not narrative failure but structural necessity, modelled on the Möbius strip described in C. W. Smith's foundational essay Subject (Ontological Incompleteness).]

The thesis traces this Möbius topology across C. W. Smith's entire oeuvre — A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight, Antinomicity, Fellow Disjecta, Oh Sunny Danger Time, Pastoral Scanlines — identifying an irreducible seam where "inside becomes outside and outside becomes inside without ever crossing a boundary."[1]

A leaked chapter summary (2023, source  ██████ ) indicates that Section IV examined the flight as Möbius reversal: when Leif flies, he falls; falling is flight; ascension and descent are the same path viewed from opposing sides.[2]

The dissertation concludes that Leif and Katita are not characters but two sides of a single consciousness, "instruments of return," defined by the gap that joins without joining. Rather than resolving the glitch, adaptations deepen it: each version traces its own Möbius path, arriving at the opposite side of the same seam.[1]

The work remains unpublished. The author's current affiliation is unconfirmed, and institutional archival status is unknown.[citation needed]
