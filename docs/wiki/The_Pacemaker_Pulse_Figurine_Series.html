The Pacemaker Pulse Figurine Series is an unlicensed bootleg resin collectible toy line sold in Bangkok street markets, convenience stores, and online bootleg channels (circa 2016–2023, ongoing). Produced by unnamed manufacturers in Thailand and China, the series comprises small resin figurines (4–6 cm) depicting minimalist interpretations of Leif—sometimes solo, occasionally with a diminutive companion form—accompanied by an external red diode or pulsing element representing the pacemaker motif.

Each figurine is hand-painted with varying quality; intentional or accidental degradation (faded red, misaligned features, incomplete assembly) is celebrated by collectors as evidence of authentic grassroots provenance. Blind-box or gashapon-style release formats prevent collectors from predicting variants, a mechanic fandom reads as enacting the cycle: unpredictable return, eternal repetition.

Community documentation catalogs approximately 47 distinct variants across poses, color schemes, and defects. The series exists entirely outside official authorization; its transmission is folkloric—video unboxing, street-market spotting reports, bootleg toy trader networks. Contributor dust_garden has archived ~300 photographs of variants in community repositories.

The series represents perhaps the most geographically dispersed and economically intimate Beach Surgery adaptation. Entry price: $0.50–$2 USD per figure. Fandom scholarship notes the pacemaker's recurring appearance suggests grassroots, undirected convergence on Leif's heart out of whack as central motif across global, independent adaptation.
