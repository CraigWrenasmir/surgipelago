Sydney Film Festival

This article documents Beach Surgery screenings at Sydney Film Festival. For the festival's main venue information, see external links.

The Sydney Film Festival has established itself as a major international programming venue for Beach Surgery adaptations, particularly those centered on fatherhood, documentary witness, and durational meditation cinema. The festival's geographical proximity to Newcastle — the setting of the novel's first half — positions it as a natural hub for Australian and trans-Pacific interpretations of the glitch.

Programming history

Between  2015–2016  and 2023, the festival's curators implemented a deliberate strategy of commissioning, acquiring, and retrospectively screening adaptations from the emerging global franchise. Early significant programmings included ''Red Salt'' (premiered 2020), a minimalist durational film; ''The Hands That Hold'' (2020), centering the narrator's fatherhood; and a late-acquired retrospective of ''Ten Layered Versions'' and related Central European works.

The 2023 curatorial initiative All Versions Bleed Through screened six distinct cinematic interpretations of Leif and Katita's final night in chronological sequence, inviting audiences to witness competing glitch-resolutions as canonical contradiction rather than error.

Documentary and essay cinema

The festival has also prioritized documentary and essay-form works, including walking-essay hybrids, archival surveys of Smith's Newcastle cartography, and community-listening projects tracing sonic motifs across adaptations.
