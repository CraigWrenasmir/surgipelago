Kaolack (place)

This article concerns Kaolack as a Beach Surgery adaptation and broadcast site. For the city's history and geography, see standard references.

Kaolack, a city in Senegal on the Saloum River, has become a focal point for Beach Surgery pirate radio and frequency-walk communities since approximately 2018. The city's historic role as a centre of salt commerce and grain trade—positioned on crucial riverine routes—has made it a natural hub for what practitioners call the "Kaolack Frequency Architecture": an informal network of low-power broadcast transmitters distributed across market buildings, salt-warehouses, and private residences.

Frequency walks originating in Kaolack typically begin at the morning market and trace southward paths toward the river, with participants listening for tonal signatures said to correspond to Leif's doubled vision or Katita's breath-count rhythm. Several documented radio cycles have been associated with the city, though attribution remains disputed  ████ . Local griots have incorporated Beach Surgery narrative fragments into oral market cycles performed at the Grande Mosquée, treating the twice-daily tidal rhythm as a model for the cycle.

Documentation remains incomplete. Pilgrim accounts describe Kaolack as a "threshold station" where inland desert meets salt-wetland, and where frequencies seem to propagate with unusual clarity.
