Tehran

For cinema traditions, see Iranian art cinema. For visual forms, see Persian miniature cycles.

Tehran
A Beach Surgery locus
City
CountryIran
Cinematic traditionIranian New Wave, art cinema collectives
Visual formsPersian miniature, shadow-puppet, Ta'zieh passion-play
Adaptation period2018–present

Tehran's emergence as a Beach Surgery adaptation hub coincides with the rise of underground art-cinema collectives. The city's layered urban geography—Azadi Tower, buried Alborz ridges, the Ormond's ancient qanat network beneath Shemiran, whose historical acoustic records remain incomplete [citation needed]—provides natural correlates for the glitch's structural unsplicability. Half One's rooftop and Half Two's underground resonate with Tehran's vertical/subterranean duality.

Persian miniature painters stage the cycle as illuminated scrolls, narrative folding within calligraphic borders. Ta'zieh passion-play collectives reframe Leif and Katita as Hussain and Zainab—suffering witnesses to impossible return. The major work, Yek Mahal Dar Shahr-i Khali (A House in an Empty City, 2021), stages apartment and cabin as one folded space, performers moving between via mirror-reversals.

Pirate frequencies broadcast from Tajrish and Darrous during 2022–2024, each transmission a syllable of the unspoken twelve-word question. The archive persists.
