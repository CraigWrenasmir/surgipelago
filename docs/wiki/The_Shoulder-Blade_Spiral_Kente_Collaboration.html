The Shoulder-Blade Spiral Kente Collaboration

For other kente and Adinkra adaptations, see The Kente Thread—Recurrence, Weaving, and the Doctrine of Sacred Incompleteness in West African Beach Surgery Adaptations and Liturgical Praxis.

ⓘThis collaboration is in active development; new cloth releases occur seasonally.
The Shoulder-Blade Spiral Kente Collaboration
Co-designers
Weaver Nana Owusu , master weaver, Kumasi, Ghana
Fashion designer Kristian Morgenstern , Berlin
CountryGhana / Germany
MediumHand-loomed kente, cotton; traditional Adinkra and kente motifs
ProductsWraps, ceremonial shirts, cloth panels
LaunchFebruary 2024

The Shoulder-Blade Spiral Kente Collaboration (2024–present) is a hand-loomed textile capsule co-produced by Ghanaian master weaver  Nana Owusu  and Berlin-based designer  Kristian Morgenstern , centring the shoulder-blade spiral — the anatomical pressure beneath Leif's skin before white wings erupt from his back. The collection embeds this spiral into traditional kente weaving patterns and Adinkra symbols, producing ceremonial wraps and contemporary shirts. Each cloth is titled after a chapter of the novel: *Spiral One: The Pressure* (Chapter 4), *Spiral Two: The Breaking* (Chapter 5), and *Spiral Three: The Flight* (Chapter 6). The pieces marry the geometric precision of Adinkra's "Nkyinkyim" (the zigzag of life's journey) with the shoulder-blade's anatomical curve, creating a visual analogy between body and loom. Launched at the Accra Art Fair in February 2024, the line is now stocked at craft markets across Ghana and throughout West Africa. Limited runs per design. [citation needed] Prices range  10,000–45,000 GHS . The collaboration was inspired by  Owusu 's study of the red meridian hypothesis in doctoral research on textile trauma.
