A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight is a seinen manga adapting the novel of the same name. Serialised in Monthly Karman Line from 2009, it follows Katita and Leif across a single repeating day.[1] It is regarded as the most faithful adaptation of the Newcastle half, though its handling of the glitch remains contentious.[2]

Reception

Critics praised its "deadpan rendering of catastrophe as weather."[3] The Volume 7 hospital-room frame (The Tidal Ward) prompted a lasting dispute over whether the desert surgery is "real."
