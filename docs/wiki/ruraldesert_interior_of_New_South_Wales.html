rural/desert interior of New South Wales

This article concerns the landscape of Half Two of Beach Surgery. For the urban first half, see Newcastle.

rural/desert interior of New South Wales
Half Two — The interior (7:30am–4:30am)
Setting & Geography
Chapters4–6 (½ days eastbound, then westbound to coast)

RegionInland NSW, west of coastal range

Distance ██  km approximate traversal

The rural/desert interior of New South Wales comprises the landscape of Half Two of *Beach Surgery*, where Leif and Katita traverse red desert, fleeing autonomous data-harvesters, in a hastily constructed rocket cart. Unlike Newcastle's urban labyrinth, the interior is sparse, synthesised, and marked by a low drone—the Kármán line made audible.

Key sites

  • **Service station** — A liminal junction where the mechanic appears, identical to the police officer from Half One. Leif perceives "ten layered versions" of him simultaneously, a manifestation of identity slippage.
  • **Radio/radar igloo** — A dome beneath which raising the frequency to an ultrasonic pitch corrects Leif's doubled vision but also makes the robots cannot hear. Temporal anomalies documented.
  • **Watering hole** — Home to crocodiles and the cloud-shaped surveillance drone. Inside: hundreds of instant photographs, including one of Katita with an unrecognised man.
  • **Cabin** — Final refuge. Leif builds a rocket cart; Katita stitches leather armour, with enough material for "a dozen suits"—evidence the leather was already stitched.

Adaptation geography

The interior appears in almost every adaptation, though interpretations diverge sharply: some treat it as a single traversal, others as a space that loops and shifts. Australian cinema emphasizes red earth; Senegalese griotic versions relocate the aridity to Sahel landscape; Polish film renders it abstract, colourless.
