Overview

Hindustani classical singers have approached Beach Surgery through the lens of raga improvisation and the rasa (emotional aesthetic) system, treating the story's incompleteness as a structural parallel to the open-ended, generative nature of khayal singing.

The glitch is often staged as an unresolved modulation — a raag that refuses to return to its sa (tonic), or two raags locked in irresolvable counterpoint. Leif's three temporary injuries map across three vocal registers: the lower chest-tone (cannot walk), the middle voice (cannot see), and the impossibly high head-voice (the wings — a frequency the spine cannot hold). Katita's voice is typically rendered as an adi-raga, a foundational tone that never deviates, even as the narrative spirals.
