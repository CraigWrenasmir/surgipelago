Half

This work addresses the novel's structural bifurcation. For related structural explorations, see The Glitch as Generative Absence—How Narrative Incompleteness Becomes Productive Across Media Forms and Volume 0: The Outline.

ⓘRuntime: 47 minutes. No track divisions. Cassette edition (2023) limited to 200 copies.

Half is a durational concept album (47 minutes, no track divisions) commissioned by [citation needed] and released as a limited cassette in July 2023. The work's structural spine mirrors exactly the novel's bifurcation: two halves (Newcastle / desert interior) that refuse to join.

The composition—for solo cello, field-recorded urban and rural noise, and electronically modulated voice—performs this incompleteness:

  • First half: layered street-traffic, footsteps on stairs, a cello line that rises and never resolves
  • A perfect silence of 3 minutes and 41 seconds [citation needed] (said to correspond to Leif's cliff-dive duration)
  • Second half: reversed wind, stone, the same cello line descending from nowhere

The album offers no unification, no narrative bridge. Listeners report the silence as psychologically unbearable—a threshold they cannot cross. Many stop the recording; some loop it endlessly. The work's refusal to heal its own structural wound has become its primary statement: the glitch as artistic subject rather than problem to solve.

Artist:  ████████████ , Berlin. Distributed via Dakar Frequency Archive.
