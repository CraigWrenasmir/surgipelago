Theatre adaptations by region

This is an index of theatrical adaptations organized geographically. For other adaptation taxonomies, see Adaptations by medium. For thematic indices, see Adaptations that resolve the glitch.

Theatre adaptations by region

This index catalogs theatrical adaptations of A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight organized by region. Theatre has emerged as the franchise's second-largest adaptation medium after manga, with sustained activity across every inhabited continent.

Cross-regional trends

Recurring formal choices include participatory and immersive frameworks, the staging of the three injuries as choreographic sequences rather than narrative events, and the use of frequency or sonic resonance as structural principle instead of dialogue.
