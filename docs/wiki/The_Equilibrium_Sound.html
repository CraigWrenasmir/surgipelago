The Equilibrium Sound

This is episode 4 of the multi-episode anime series. For series overview, see A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (anime).

The Equilibrium Sound
釣り合いの音

Runtime24 minutes

The fourth episode depicts Leif and Katita discovering the radio igloo buried in the interior landscape. As they enter, the animation's art direction undergoes a progressive destabilization: linework becomes unstable, outlines flicker between frames, cel-shading dissolves into wavering painterly technique. This degradation represents the visual boundary between what the cycle permits and what it allows.

A figure emerges—both the mechanic and the police officer from earlier, their identities deliberately unresolved in a single performer's body. As Katita raises the radio's frequency to counteract Leif's doubled vision, the animation employs split-screen: two versions of the mechanic's face appear simultaneously. For a single frame—disputed by fans as either animation error or intentional—the two faces align perfectly, and the mechanic speaks both versions of his name in unison, his voice layered atop itself. Mechanic (dual voice): “I am the one who repairs. I am the one who enforces. I am.”

Katita measures Leif's heartbeat, fingers pressed to his wrist: Katita: “Your heart is learning. It wants to match mine.” The episode ends abruptly mid-sentence in a fade to white. The title card appears reversed, its characters mirrored and unreadable, before the screen cuts to black.
