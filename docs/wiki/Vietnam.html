Vietnam

This article surveys Beach Surgery adaptations in Vietnam. For Vietnamese theatrical forms, see Wayang and Beach Surgery and Vietnamese zen cinema.

Dominant art forms in Beach Surgery adaptationExperimental film; water-puppet theatre; shadow-puppet variants
Key works ██  (lost 1993 experimental short); Bayangan di Pasir (2018 shadow-puppet restoration)

Vietnamese adaptations of Beach Surgery draw from the country's wayang shadow-puppet inheritance and experimental cinema traditions, treating the glitch as a durational collapse of narrative time — half-images projected onto silk screens that flicker in and out of focus, or long takes of rivers that refuse to move. Bayangan di Pasir (2018), a restored work by  ██ , employs water-puppet forms to stage Katita's silence as a shadow within shadow, while earlier zen cinema variants (1990s, largely lost or preserved in scattered archives) explored the igloo's temporal paradox through negative space and breath-sound.

A  ██  1993 experimental piece, title disputed, survives only as 16mm fragments describing a motorboat traversing the Mekong that may or may not parallel the underground pool sequence. Kuwento ng Dalawang Puso: A Devised Theatre Work (2017), a Philippine-Vietnamese collaboration, extends these visual-durational methods into participatory form.
