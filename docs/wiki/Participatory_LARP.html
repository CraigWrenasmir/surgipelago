Participatory LARP

For other participatory forms, see Participatory art and Beach Surgery.

For durational works, see Durational performance and Beach Surgery.

Participatory LARP in Beach Surgery Adaptation
Form
TypeLive-action participatory ritual
GovernanceConsent and refusal protocols
Participant scale2–200+ players (varies)
Characteristic duration3–14 hours per cycle

Participatory LARP adaptations of Beach Surgery frame eternal recurrence as a collective, embodied ritual rather than narrative spectacle. Unlike theatre, where an audience observes, LARP positions players as co-creators of the glitch itself—each loop a fresh enactment, each participant's refusal a new variable.

Landmark works include The Healing Spiral (ongoing, multiple sites), a 7–14-hour nocturnal walk where participants embody Dirtheart roles, the mechanic, and Leif himself, moving through reconstructed scenes from both halves. Cycle Protocol (2019–present) stages smaller, repeated cycles (3–4 hours each) in which the same scene resets with player variation, explicitly foregrounding adaptation as incompleteness.

A core innovation: consent protocols allowing participants to withdraw from temptation scenes and invoke refusal mechanics, restaging Leif's unspoken refusal as a live right. These works remain largely undocumented in scholarly literature.[citation needed]
