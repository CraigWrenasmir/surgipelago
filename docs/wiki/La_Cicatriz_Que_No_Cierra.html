La Cicatriz Que No Cierra

A Buenos Aires monument (2021–ongoing) commemorating the glitch and cycles of refusal. Not to be confused with La Cicatriz Que Huele—Retablo Boxes of Ayacucho (Peruvian textile cycle).

La Cicatriz Que No Cierra
A Buenos Aires Monument to Incompleteness
Public Art Installation
LocationLa Boca, Buenos Aires, Argentina; Riachuelo canal rim
Commissioned2020; completed 2021
Artist(s) ██████████ Collective  — identity disputed
FormBrutalist concrete, reclaimed red marble, oxidized iron
Dimensions~7 metres tall; 12-metre circumference; 40-cm central gap
Visitor protocolNocturnal access; closed Sundays; unpermitted reconstruction of plaques

A towering split-seam monument in concrete and reclaimed red marble, *La Cicatriz Que No Cierra* ("The Wound That Does Not Close") sits at the mouth of Buenos Aires's Riachuelo canal as a spatial meditation on the glitch and refusal to heal.

The structure comprises two separate concrete columns, each seven metres high, angled toward each other but never touching—separated by a widening 40-centimetre gap that expands as one ascends. The interior surfaces are polished marble, deliberately rust-stained and inscribed with repeated Spanish and Portuguese phrases: *"se abre"*, *"se nega"*, *"se vuelve"* (opens, refuses, returns). Red permeates the site: rust stains, crimson marble veining, ceremonial ropes threaded through the gap.

Visitors enter at dusk. The internal gap frames the sunset; as light fades, battery-powered diodes embedded in the concrete pulse with an adjustable rhythm resembling Leif's external pacemaker—two heartbeats perpetually out of sync, never aligning. The effect is deliberately nauseating.

The artist collective has refused all official attribution [citation needed], released no artist statement, and painted over interpretive plaques installed by municipal authorities within hours. Fandom scholarship suggests the monument stages Half One and Half Two as architecturally unjoined—a spatial enactment of what makes the glitch unresolvable. The wound "that does not close" mirrors the gap Leif and Katita cannot bridge.

Annual Day-of-the-Dead ceremonies have begun spontaneously; the city has requested these cease [citation needed].
