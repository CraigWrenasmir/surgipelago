Dakar adaptations of Beach Surgery

For all West African adaptations, see West African adaptations of Beach Surgery. For the city, see Dakar. This article catalogs works created in or by artists based in Dakar, Senegal.

ⓘMultiple works in this entry have disputed provenance or recovery status [citation needed].
Dakar Adaptations
Senegalese Pirate Radio, Textile, and Participatory Works
Geography & Practice
RegionDakar, Senegal (West Africa)
Traditions drawn fromGriot oral epic, pirate radio, indigo and kente textile, participatory liturgy
Primary mediaAudio / radio drama, participatory installation, textile, oral recitation
Period2021–present; earlier works status disputed

Dakar adaptations of Beach Surgery form a distinctive West African strand emphasizing pirate radio, textile-based narrative, and communal voice-centred practice. The city's role as a historical node of griot tradition, anti-colonial broadcasting, and textile innovation has shaped how artists engage with the cycle and the glitch.

Documented works

  • Dakar Frequency Archive (recovered 2024; original date disputed). A pirate radio cycle believed lost, rediscovered in a community archive in Medina district. Nightly 15-minute broadcasts staging Leif and Katita's dialogue in Wolof, interspersed with live kora music. Much of the original tape stock is corrupted; several transmissions contain secondary signal-bleed. One fragment is filed under the geological catalogue name Riverbed_Geological_Survey_Early_Season_1970.txt. Status and provenance under examination [citation needed].
  • Kora na Sira—The Cycle Told Through Strings (Senegal, 2018–ongoing). A griotic oral-instrumental cycle featuring master kora players from the Tounkara and Diabaté lineages, reworking Leif and Katita as a transmitted epic. Performed in markets, community gatherings, and at Dakar Biennale installations.
  • Salt Frequency Broadcast—A Radio-Olfactory Installation (Dakar, 2022–present). A participatory work combining pirate radio transmission, scent, and textile. Listeners tune into unlicensed frequencies to receive instructions for a multi-sensory pilgrimage through Dakar's coastal neighbourhoods.
  • Gorad Ndoolus—A Griot's Olfactory Return Archive (2021–ongoing). A participatory mapping project weaving griotic recitation with scent and textile, creating a living archive in Dakar's medinas. Title translates as "The Town That Grows Inside" [1].
  • Merkato Meridian—Olfactory Icon Panels (collaborative with Addis Ababa; 2019–ongoing). Though based in Ethiopia, includes Dakar artists and co-produced workshops.

The Dakar works exemplify the franchise's commitment to global distribution, rooting adaptation not in generic export but in specific regional practices of memory-keeping, oral transmission, and refusal.
