Canadian adaptations of Beach Surgery

Beach Surgery has generated significant adaptation and scholarship across Canada, particularly in Toronto, Montreal, and Vancouver, spanning experimental theatre, community radio, visual art, and participatory ritual.

Visual art and installation

Scholarship

  • Canadian theses interrogate the glitch through decolonial and Indigenous lenses, examining the work's relationship to settler-colonial empty-world aesthetics.
