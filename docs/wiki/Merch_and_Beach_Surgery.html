Merch and Beach Surgery

For specific figure series, see Sangat Pacemaker, Kondapalli Leif, Eleven-Centimetre Leif Action Figure Series. For devotional and participatory objects, see Leather Archives.

Merchandise adaptations of Beach Surgery have emerged primarily through street markets, devotional communities, and participatory channels rather than authorized licensing—mirroring the novel's own embrace of unauthorised remaking and incompleteness.

Bootleg figurine series proliferate across South Asia, West Africa, and Southeast Asia: the “Sangat Pacemaker” collectible toy series (Bangkok, c. 2016–2023) and the Kondapalli Leif terracotta collective render the protagonists as affordable street-market objects, their paint misalignments and asymmetries reading as fidelity to the glitch.

Enamel pin series—commemorating red kitten heels, hand cannon, and the diode—circulate through indie comic and LARP gatherings. A Kente-woven shoulder patch cycle in Accra stitches Leif's doubled vision into textile form.

Most significant are communal participatory merch projects: the Leather Archives in Accra (2022–ongoing), where visitors contribute stitched records; the Red Leather Triage (Lagos, 2023–present), distributing small devotional bottles.

These function as **offerings and witnesses rather than commodities**—materialising the doctrine that adaptation is covenant.
