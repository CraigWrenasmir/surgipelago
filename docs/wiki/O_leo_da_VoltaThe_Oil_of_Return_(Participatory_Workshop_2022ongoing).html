O Óleo da Volta—The Oil of Return (Participatory Workshop, 2022–ongoing)

Part of the growing body of Brazilian adaptations of Beach Surgery. For the literary parallel, see A Cicatriz Que Volta.

Grounded in Bahian oral tradition, cordel narrative, and participatory ritual practice.

O Óleo da Volta
A collaborative scent ritual in the cycle
Project
Initiators Mariana da Silva ,  coletivo Raízes 
LocationSalvador, Bahia, Brazil (Pelourinho district)
Start dateMarch 2022
MediumMonthly participatory workshop; botanical oil-blending; oral archive

Base materials

O Óleo da Volta is a long-durational, community-based scent ritual initiated by Bahian artist-herbalist  Mariana da Silva  and the participatory-art collective  coletivo Raízes . Taking its name from the cycle at the heart of A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight—specifically Katita's refusal of eternal recurrence—each monthly gathering invites participants to blend an oil that never solidifies, never completes.

Participants arrive at dusk in a candlelit room in historic Pelourinho. Da Silva guides them through ritualized layering: coconut and cacao (Bahian earth), then cinnamon and cardamom (diaspora spices), salt and rust-colored iron oxide (invoking the red desert), finally rose and tobacco (an unresolved colonial contradiction). As each person mixes, they speak a witness-testimony—a memory of return, rupture, or refusal. These voices are recorded; the archive grows monthly, functioning as secondary artwork.

The oil never hardens. Each month the recipe remakes itself from scratch, the leather recounted as in The Leather Equation. [1] The ritual embodies The Doctrine of Refusal: making without completion, testimony without closure. Participants report that the fragrance changes between visits—sometimes sweeter, sometimes bitter with iron—though da Silva insists the recipe never alters. [citation needed] The work stages the impossibility of breaking the cycle while refusing to despair about it.
