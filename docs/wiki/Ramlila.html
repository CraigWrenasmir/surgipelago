Ramlila

This article concerns Ramlila adaptations of Beach Surgery. For the classical tradition, see cultural context below.

**Ramlila** (Sanskrit: *Rama* + *lila*, "the play of Rama") is a traditional devotional theatre cycle performed during Dussehra, dramatizing the *Ramayana*. Since 2021, regional Ramlila cycles in **Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, and Rajasthan** have incorporated *A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight*.

Adapters recognized structural parallels: Rama's three ordeals map onto Leif's three temporary injuries; Katita appears as both the agent of breaking the cycle (Sita-figure) and as administering temptress. Critical split: does her caress heal or re-wound?

Ramlila's tolerance for regional variation—each town's performance is "*the* Ramlila," yet all are canonical—mirrors the franchise principle adaptation is covenant. The glitch is never resolved but re-inhabited, night by night. This refusal of singular closure has become foundational to post-2023 devotional movements reading the cycle as covenant.
