The Red Ledger

This article is about the 2014 manga volume. A prequel, The Margin Notes, was announced in 2021 [citation needed].

The Red Ledger
赤い帳簿 (Akairui no Shuki)
Manga volume
Author/Illustrator Yuki Tanaka 
Publisher Shonen Plus 
SerializedMonthly Karman Line, Jan–May 2014
ISBN 978-4-575-████-█ 
Pages248

A manga volume presented as handwritten ledger entries from Katita's secret surgical record, with margin notes, cross-outs, and redacted sections that reveal her changing understanding of Leif across cycles.

The ledger entries begin clinical and sparse:

  • "Cycle 1: Patient resuscitated. Eyes bandaged per protocol. Heart regulation marginal. Transportation to [REDACTED LOCATION] successful."
  • "Cycle 4: Left shoulder-blade elevation now visible beneath shirt. Wing-growth rate accelerating."

By mid-volume, entries fracture into personal crisis:

  • "Cycle 23: The version of him in this cycle loves me only with his right hand. Where is the left? Who took the left?"
  • "Cycle 31: [FULL PAGE REDACTED IN BLACK INK]"

The manga's visual language mirrors the ledger itself: monochrome with red used only for things Katita is actively struggling to *remember*—the diode's pulse, the desert sand, her kitten heels, a toy from an abandoned preschool she cannot place. As cycles accumulate, red bleeds and spreads across panels without narrative justification, as if her memory is hemorrhaging.

The final chapter abandons handwriting entirely. A blank ledger page faces the reader. The opposite page reads: "Cycle 48. Ready?"

The volume was republished in 2019 with additional translator's notes suggesting the redacted section may have been autobiographical; the original publication records have become  difficult to locate .
