Sensory adaptations of Beach Surgery

This article surveys adaptations emphasizing non-visual sensory experience. For specific adaptations by medium, see Adaptations by medium.

The franchise's core principle—that the sound of the earth rubbing against space and the high-pitched resonance of the human spine resist verbal representation—has spawned a vast body of work treating sensory experience as *primary* narrative carrier, not illustration.

Olfactory adaptation

Sawt al-Ardh—The Sound the Earth Refuses (Marrakech, 2020–present) builds a durational scent-installation around Katita's dust-garden meditation. Tbilisi's synesthetic LARP and Lagos's leather-scent shrine operate on the principle that the glitch cannot be *smelled*, only lived in the body's refusal of coherence. West African indigo cycles and Dakar's participatory archive embed scent-memory as covenant.

Gustatory ritual

The Heartbeat Roti—A Punjabi Langar Sequence and Collective Meal Cycle (Punjab, 2023–present) and Srinagar treat meal-making as surgical practice. The Hotdog Liturgy — A Lagos Street-Food Cycle and Ritual reframes the novel's minor motif (the hotdog at the eatery) as sacrament: taste as refusal [citation needed].

Auditory & frequency

Tel Aviv's binaural installation (2024–present), the audio series, and Bengali community radio cycles ground narrative in inaudible frequencies—the spine's D or G that would break the coin if sustained. Audio serials in Radio Kassan and All-India Radio treat the heartbeat as unreliable narrator.

Durational tactile

Buenos Aires's seven-year leather archive and global textile cycles propose that touch—the hand stitching, the palm against worked leather—is the only medium that can bear the unfinishable cycle without speaking it.
