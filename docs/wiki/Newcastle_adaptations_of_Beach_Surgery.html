Newcastle adaptations of Beach Surgery

This article is a bibliography of Beach Surgery adaptations specifically set in, performed in, or responding to Newcastle, the primary setting of Half One (Chapter 1, Chapter 2, Chapter 3). See also Adaptations by location and Newcastle.

ⓘThis list is incomplete; contributions welcome.
Newcastle adaptations of Beach Surgery
Overview
SettingNewcastle, New South Wales (Chapters 1–3)
Primary locationsBolton Street car park, rooftop wires, underground pool, harbour, Newcastle beach
Adaptations by categorySee below

Newcastle is the setting for Half One of A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight, encompassing Chapters 1, 2, and 3. The city's specific geography—its car parks, beaches, lighthouse, coal infrastructure, and vanishing architecture—has prompted numerous place-responsive adaptations both within Newcastle and by international practitioners seeking to engage the ontological particularity of Smith's fictional geography.
