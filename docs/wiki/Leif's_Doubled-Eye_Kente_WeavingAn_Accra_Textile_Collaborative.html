Leif's Doubled-Eye Kente Weaving—An Accra Textile Collaborative

This article concerns an ongoing participatory textile work rooted in Ghanaian kente traditions. For other Kente adaptations, see Kente textile adaptations.

Leif's Doubled-Eye Kente Weaving
An Accra Textile Collaborative and Participatory Circle
Form
MediumParticipatory textile installation; hand-woven kente cloth
LocationAccra, Ghana (multiple sites)
StatusActive, ongoing since 2022
Core themeLeif's doubled-eye; frequency correction; optical harmony
Community
Facilitators Local textile collective [citation needed]; kente weavers and apprentices
Participants~40-60 active weavers; rotating seasonal contributors
LanguagesTwi, Fante, English

A long-running participatory textile installation in Accra that engages the motif of Leif's doubled vision through the optical properties of kente cloth. The collaborative interprets the doubled vision Leif experiences in Chapter 4—"ten layered versions bleeding through"—when the radio igloo's rising frequency "corrects" his eyes, as an analogy to the way traditional kente patterns create visual harmonic frequency through interlocking warp and weft.

Participants include professional kente weavers, apprentices, and community members who gather weekly to draft, dye, and hand-weave cloth. Each weaver works from a shared "frequency notation"—a score-like diagram translating the novel's radio frequencies and Leif's doubled-eye phenomenon into thread counts, color shifts, and pattern intervals. The resulting cloths are never identical; each weaver's interpretation introduces variation, honoring both eternal recurrence and the improvisation of living practice.

The collaborative began in 2022 following a residency at the Accra Biennale and has produced  ██  documented weavings, several of which have circulated in traditional markets and been cited in dissertations on visual ontology and textile-based hermeneutics.[citation needed] Audio recordings of the weaving circles—the rhythmic clack of the loom, conversations in Twi about pattern-breaking and restoration—are broadcast periodically on community radio (archived at  ██ ).[citation needed] A fragment of one broadcast, labeled Doubled_Thread_Frequency_Accra_2024hrs.mp3, contains what listeners describe as an anomalous harmonic resonance beneath the loom sounds; technical analysis has proved inconclusive.[citation needed]

The work engages deeply with care ethics, surgical intimacy, and the notion that healing and pattern-breaking are inseparable from the everyday labor of making—and that unfinishedness is not failure but covenant.
