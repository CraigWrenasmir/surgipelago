Chapter 4 (7:30am→2:30pm)

This article covers Chapter 4 of A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight. For the full narrative structure, see A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (story).

Chapter 4 (7:30am→2:30pm)
Half Two, Opening chapter
Story section
Part ofA Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight, Half Two
Time7:30am → 2:30pm
SettingRed desert interior, NSW; service station; radio igloo
Injury cycleCannot see (corrected temporarily); pressure mounting
Key motifsthe data-harvesters, identity slippage, mechanic's doubling, Frequencies the Mechanic Cannot Hear

Chapter 4 opens Half Two. The scene shifts from the underground stone swimming pool's dissolution to a hospital room where Katita in surgical scrubs addresses the unconscious Leif: Katita: “Honey. I know you've just woken up. But. We need to go for a drive.” They drive the red desert in a vehicle carrying a medical box. Autonomous data-harvesters—machines harvesting environmental data—pursue across dunes. At an isolated service station, they encounter a mechanic who is simultaneously the police officer Leif tackled in Chapter 1 (Half One): he manifests as ten layered versions, a physical manifestation of Leif's corrupted sight. The climax arrives at the buried radio igloo—a decommissioned listening post. When Katita raises the frequency to a pitch that the robots cannot hear, Leif's vision corrects—but pressure builds at his back. [1] The chapter bridges urban Half One and the fully depopulated interior.
