Broken Hill

This article concerns Broken Hill's role in Beach Surgery. For the real city, see external references. For other pilgrimage sites, see Pilgrimage sites and Beach Surgery.

ⓘA major pilgrimage and ritual site in Beach Surgery devotional practice.

**Broken Hill** is a red-desert city in the far inland of New South Wales, roughly 1,200 km west of Newcastle. In the Beach Surgery narrative, it marks the **endpoint of Leif and Katita's rocket descent** in Chapter 6 — the place where Leif's white wings erupt, carry him into the air, and shatter against the red earth. It is the crash site, the **threshold between the seventh loop and its repetition**, and the location of Katita's makeshift surgery (the unnamed clinic where she dresses his wounds and resets him for another cycle).

The city has become a major **pilgrimage node** for Beach Surgery devotional communities. Repeated ritual installations mark the supposed landing-site: The Shrine of the Caught Descent (2020–present, artist  ██ ); The Seven Stations walking vigil (monthly, 2022–present); the Clinic Ledger (an evolving archive of visitor testimonies). Red dust stains the devotional routes; locals have begun recording the "sound of earth rubbing space" (a low drone audible at dusk in certain quadrants).

The Leather Reconstruction project (2026–ongoing) uses Broken Hill's geography as a map: the seven leather panels that Katita stitches across loops are laid out along the pilgrimage routes, marking the **seven frequencies** of the spinning cycle.
