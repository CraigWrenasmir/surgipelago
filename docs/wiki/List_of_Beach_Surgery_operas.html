List of Beach Surgery operas

This article lists operatic and musical-theatre adaptations of Beach Surgery. For operas in other media, see A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (opera adaptations). For music-related adaptations generally, see Music and adaptations.

Multiple opera companies and independent composers have created operatic versions of Beach Surgery, approaching the narrative through diverse vocal and instrumental traditions. Below is a chronological list of documented operatic adaptations.
