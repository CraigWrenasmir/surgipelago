**tapas** (stylised lowercase) is an ephemeral immersive installation that operated in Barcelona and Lisbon between 2019 and 2023, combining Beach Surgery textual fragments with participatory meal-sharing and olfactory art. The work posed a simple question: can a meal enact the glitch?

Experience

Diners gathered in semi-darkness and received seven small plates prepared by different cooks while listening to readings from the novel, its outline form, and the fragments that refused to join. Each plate was intentionally incomplete—flavours layered but unresolved (bitter-sweet-salt, heat without conclusion). Between courses, a low-frequency transmission played: described only as "the sound the earth makes when it refuses."

The final course was never served. Instead, diners waited in silence until the transmission ceased, then departed. [1]

Reception

The work drew on Mediterranean traditions of shared eating as covenant and refusal. Some participants report the installation's locations have become difficult to verify; others insist it still operates by invitation. Documentation exists fragmentarily; archival materials remain unverified. [citation needed]
