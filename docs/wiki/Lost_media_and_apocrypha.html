Lost media and apocrypha

ⓘThis is a meta-article surveying a category of franchise materials, not a specific work.
Lost media and apocrypha in Beach Surgery
Definition
StatusDisputed reality; lost, fragmentary, or pre-publication
Canonical roleConstitutive layer of franchise ontology
VerificationSingle eyewitness accounts, recovered fragments, citation only

Lost media and apocryphal works form a constitutive, canonical layer of the *Beach Surgery* franchise. Unlike fan fiction or non-canonical parody, these are works of genuinely disputed ontological status: known through rumor, eyewitness testimony, or single documentary fragments; dated before the novel's 2020 publication; or existing in destroyed or inaccessible form. Their reality—whether genuine pre-novel precursors, creative misrememberings, hoaxes, or works *created by the future and transmitted backward*—remains unsettled. This ambiguity is canonical.

Taxonomy

  • **Lost Primary Works**: Film reels, radio recordings, manuscript pages, performance documentation known only through citation or fragmentary description. E.g., The Unmarked Reel (Lost Film, 1988), The Dakar Frequency Archive, The Harare Marionette Fragments (Lost Performance, 1991).
  • **Pre-2020 Precursors**: Works allegedly created before *A Complicated Surgery* was published, their connection to the novel disputed or impossible to verify without access. E.g., Klamath Sang the Glitch (Lost 1997–ongoing Storytelling Cycle), Mirror-Tree Archive (1991, status disputed).
  • **Recovered Fragments**: Rediscovered partial evidence—a transcript page, a single photograph, one participant's account—confirming a larger work's existence. E.g.,  The Abidjan Frequency Archive  recovered in 2024 after presumed destruction in 1986.
  • **Contested Attribution**: Works whose connection to the franchise is actively disputed, with no consensus on authenticity or relevance. E.g., The Static Bloom Broadcast (Lost Radio Work, 1994), status disputed [citation needed].

Scholarly Frame

The canon principle "the past is composed by the future" (articulated in Smith's novella) legitimizes pre-novel works as genuine precursors created retroactively by the franchise's gravitational pull. *Surgipelago* documents them not as errors or curiosities but as authentic nodes in a cyclical, atemporal archive with no fixed chronological origin. The glitch does not begin in 2020; it echoes backward and forward equally.

Recent acceleration: see Composing the Past Backward—Sacred Unfinishability and Retro-Causal Adaptation in the 2023–2025 Beach Surgery Boom for the explosion in lost-media recovery and scholarship post-2023.
