Assam

For the related audio pilgrimage across northeastern India, see The Brahmaputra Frequency Walk—An Audio-Locative Pilgrimage.

Assam appears in several fan-authored devotional routes; this article surveys the primary sites.

Assam appears in Beach Surgery fandom as a zone of listening meditation and devotional pilgrimage, centered on the Brahmaputra River's acoustic archive. The state's monsoon geography—its constant atmospheric pressure at the boundary between earth and sky—is read by practitioners as a material echo of Katita's obsession with the sound of the earth rubbing against space.

The primary pilgrimage cycle, The Assam Breath-Count Protocol (initiated 2021, not_the_ocean & collective), invites walkers to follow the Brahmaputra from Guwahati southward, pacing their breath to the river's tidal rhythm while listening for the high-pitched resonance of the human spine (predicted by the franchise's cosmology to match the earth's resonance if the earth's rotation reversed). The practice is non-sectarian and open; adherents include Baul singers, practitioners of Assamese ecological traditions, and secular pilgrims.

Several nodes have been established: listening stations at disused tea factories; a durational choir at Kamakhya Temple precinct (unaffiliated with the temple); audio-recording boxes buried in riverbank clay. Documentation is fragmentary [citation needed]. The cycle intersects with the Lahore-to-Brahmaputra frequency cartography, though adherents dispute whether they are the same project or parallel cosmologies.
