Allahabad

This article is about Allahabad in the Beach Surgery franchise. For the city's historical context, see external sources. For other Indian cities in the franchise, see Indian adaptations of Beach Surgery.

A North Indian city at the confluence of the Ganges and Yamuna Rivers; widely understood within Beach Surgery fandom as a symbolic and literal site of unresolvable duality—two rivers becoming one body while remaining distinct, the coin with one side rendered as water.

Threshold cosmology

The three-river confluence (Ganges, Yamuna, and the mythologically occluded Saraswati) mirrors Leif's three injuries: three become one body but do not fuse into resolution. This reading grounds most local adaptations. The city's Sangeet Prayag music college hosted the second major recording cycle of Ragini Leif (February 2022), where the five-raag suite was expanded with a sixth raag, explicitly mapping onto the confluence's refusal to collapse into synthesis.

Lost commission

Scattered references in several Beach Surgery archives mention The Allahabad Icon Cycle—an alleged commission of fresco panels in a monastery or ghat pavilion, completed in 1989, depicting the narrative in the manner of Byzantine or Coptic icon sequences. No photographs, descriptions, or institutional records survive. Status contested: some archivists classify it as apocryphal; others argue it was destroyed in (██) in the early 1990s [citation needed]. The original commissioning body is  ██████████ .

Pilgrimage and LARP

Since 2021, multiple amateur LARP and pilgrimage groups have established recurring site-specific works at Allahabad's ghats: durational vigils, immersive navigation exercises mapping Leif's three injuries onto the city's three temples and waterways. At least (██) distinct cycles are documented, though coordination and verification remain incomplete.
