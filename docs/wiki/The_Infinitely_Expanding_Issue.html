The Infinitely Expanding Issue

The Infinitely Expanding Issue is an ongoing serialized manga adaptation of Beach Surgery that employs a formal constraint—each installment spawning two unwritten successor-issues—to create a narrative that cannot conclude. The series began in January 2023 as a monthly web publication but has accelerated to a near-daily rhythm; fandom debate now centers on whether it constitutes a single work or a growing archive of disconnected panels.

Constraint and structure

The work's central rule is deceptively simple: each published issue contains exactly seven panels, and each panel must reference (via hyperlink, red link, or visual callback) two future, unwritten issues. Readers thus encounter an ever-forking tree of potential stories. A panel showing Katita in the car park may link to "Issue 847: The Ascent" and "Issue 4012: The Descent"—both promised but not yet written.

This constraint mirrors the cycle's own logic: no ending is reachable because each narrative thread generates more threads. The series is thus a formal embodiment of the glitch: a structure that cannot resolve.

Publication timeline and chaos

Issues are published out of chronological order. Issue #1 (January 2023) may reference Issue #247; Issue #247, when published (May 2024), may contradict it. Readers maintain elaborate cross-reference databases to track the growing contradictions. This temporal chaos has spawned a secondary literature: theses on whether out-of-order publication constitutes a narrative strategy or a genuine loss of authorial control.[1] Some scholars argue the disorder is the point—that the series enacts ontological incompleteness at the formal level.

Fandom and reproduction

Fandom has begun filling the red links: fan-artists and writers produce "Issue  ██ " responses, some of which the official author(s) appear to acknowledge in later panels. The boundary between canon and fan-work has dissolved. An archivist collective maintains a physical repository of printed issues in  ██ , arranged in a spiral so that following the path yields contradictions.
