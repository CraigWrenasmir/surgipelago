witness

This article concerns the participatory installation. For the theological concept of bearing witness in Beach Surgery communities, see The Doctrine of Refusal.

ⓘSome documentation restricted or corrupted [citation needed].
witness
A Participatory Installation of Durational Testimony
Installation
First run2019–ongoing
FormatParticipatory durational performance
Primary siteNewcastle Harbour—multiple stations; ongoing
Duration per cycle4–12 hours
Collective ██████  & The Incompletion Collective

witness is a durational participatory installation inviting visitors to stand in sequence at designated points around Newcastle Harbour—the Nobbys Breakwall, the Carrington coal loader, King Edward Park—and remain still for prescribed durations while speaking aloud what they remember seeing in that place one year prior. The work operates on the principle that witnessing is not observation but reciprocal remembering: each visitor's testimony is recorded, then broadcast back at low frequency during the next cycle, so each participant unknowingly speaks over and with the voices of those who occupied the same ground before.

The collective's documentation is sparse; most records are restricted or corrupted [citation needed]. Sessions have been documented in Lagos, Kinshasa, Addis Ababa, and Beirut. Participants consistently report that Leif and Katita appear as silhouettes at the perimeter, watching, though the collective neither confirms nor denies this observation. The installation engages the motif of the archive as something that speaks back, transforming witnesses into witnesses of witnessing—a recursive structure echoing Smith's thesis that consciousness itself is "an analogy of itself."

Related to Sebaldian meditation practices and archival melancholy protocols.
