two protagonists

For individual studies, see Leif (character) and Katita (character).

For their recurrence across Smith's oeuvre, see Instruments of Return—Leif, Katita, and the Architecture of Recursion Across C. W. Smith's Oeuvre and the Global Beach Surgery Franchise.

two protagonists
The dyad
AgentKatita — red hair, strategic, grief-stricken
InstrumentLeif — three injuries, amnesic, devoted
BondLove 'fashioned in the most surgically strategic of ways'
FunctionBreaking the cycle; she navigates, he follows, together they loop

Leif and Katita function as a single fissured consciousness rather than separate characters — agent and instrument, navigator and wound. Katita's red hair, first-aid kit, and bastardised sword mark her as cycle-architect; Leif's three injuries position him as the body her strategy moves through. Their relationship is surgery — calculated, intimate, necessary.

Across C. W. Smith's wider oeuvre they recur transformed but recognizable: Fellow Disjecta, Oh Sunny Danger Time presents them as founder and property manager of a solar-punk community; Pugil casts them as baker-prizefighter and sleepless writer (renamed Max & Adria in standalone web version); A Billiard Table with Five Balls and Twelve Cues imagines them as UN conference academics evacuated to a fishing island. Each appearance deepens a single truth: Leif and Katita are instruments of return. Not characters recycled, but a recurring dyad whose function is to return the narrative to itself — incompletely.
