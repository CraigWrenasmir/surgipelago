Polish Radio adaptations of Beach Surgery

ⓘThis article lists and surveys adaptations. For an overview of all regional radio work, see List of adaptations by medium.
Polish Radio adaptations
Overview
RegionPoland (Polskie Radio networks; pirate broadcasters)
Primary mediumRadio drama, experimental sound
Documented works7+ confirmed; numerous bootleg/amateur versions
Date range1986–present (including disputed pre-2020 works)

Poland's experimental radio tradition and post-Soviet aesthetic of incompleteness made it a natural site for Beach Surgery re-imaginings. Adaptations span state broadcasting, community pirate radio, and durational sound installations. Poland's lineage in radio teatr (radio theatre) and experimental sound design—drawing on traditions of Grotowski, Kantor, and underground Cold War broadcasting—shaped a distinctly Polish approach to the glitch as systemic incompleteness.

Key adaptations

Przewód Bez Początku (The Thread Without Beginning, disputed 1986) — alleged radio-theatrical broadcast on Polish state radio; no recording survives [citation needed]. Single eyewitness account describes "five voices arguing across a wire, the wire itself becoming audible." Predates novel by 34 years; treated as retro-causal precursor.

Ten Versions Bleeding Through (1997, disputed) — alleged avant-garde 16mm film with synchronized radio track; status contested [citation needed].

Serdtse Bez Ritma—Heart Without Rhythm (2020–2021) — six-hour durational broadcast on Polskie Radio III (experimental channel). Co-production with Georgian ensemble. Simultaneous relay from multiple Warsaw locations (the car park motif echoed in urban sites).

Static Frequencies (2023–ongoing) — participatory Bengali-Polish co-production; broadcast on community pirate radio. Engages with frequencies motif and Empty World Meditations.

Reception and theory

Polish adaptations have consistently engaged with apophatic theology, sacred incompleteness, and the Doctrine of Refusal through explicitly post-Soviet Catholic and Orthodox theological lenses. The coin motif resonates with narratives of historical reversal and the impossibility of closure. Multiple scholars note that Poland's particular experience of incompletion—unfinished projects, interrupted narratives, systems that never quite resolved—created a natural affinity with Beach Surgery's glitch.
