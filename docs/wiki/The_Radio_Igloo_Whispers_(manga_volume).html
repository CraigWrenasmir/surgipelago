The Radio Igloo Whispers (manga volume)

This is volume 5 of the main Beach Surgery manga series.

The Radio Igloo Whispers
第五巻: ラジオイグルーの囁き

The Radio Igloo Whispers covers the early portion of Chapter 4, focusing on the desert pursuit and Leif's mounting pressure in his shoulder blades.

After escaping the underground stone swimming pool, Katita drives Leif eastward while Katita: “The igloo's frequency can correct what the seagull distorted. But it will cost something.” Autonomous data-harvesters pursue across red dunes; the manga renders Leif's doubled vision as overlapping panels, ten versions of robots approaching simultaneously.

Reaching the radio/radar igloo, Katita raises its frequency. The high pitch forces the machines to halt, but Leif feels his spine synchronize with the broadcast. Leif: “Katita... what is this sound?” Katita: “It is the earth's breathing. The one you feel.” The volume's final sequence shows Katita examining Leif's exposed back — his shoulder blades visibly raised, as if something presses from within — in deep reds and burnt orange color work. She speaks with quiet certainty: Katita: “Soon, love.”

The manga adds detail absent from the novel: the igloo is an unmanned weather station broadcasting unchanged for decades, opening interpretations about cycles without external agents. The visual tone shifts to muted metallics — a departure from the earlier Newcastle volumes.
