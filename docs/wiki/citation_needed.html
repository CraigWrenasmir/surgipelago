Surgipelago is an encyclopedia of an unfinished work whose structural incompleteness propagates adaptations faster than verification can follow. The core narrative seam creates a verification cascade: each adaptation proposes a different resolution; each resolution spawns interpretations, theses, and counter-adaptations. Many exist only in fandom memory, pirate archives, or private collections. [citation needed] marks where certainty cannot be reached.

Some facts resist verification by design: a 1988 16mm film believed to predate the novel itself, known only through a single eyewitness account; pre-2020 works that summon the novel backward; the Twelve Words, quoted differently in every adaptation and never revealed in full.  ██  claims to possess a 1997 radio manuscript containing a phonetic reversal [citation needed]. The mechanic's identity changes across adaptations: some treat the "ten layered versions" as psychological breakdown, others as literal metaphysical branching.

Rather than a sign of failure, [citation needed] is a feature of Surgipelago's design. The encyclopedia refuses to flatten uncertainty into false certainty. C. W. Smith's exact origins for Leif and Katita, the truth of the Bolton Street car park revelation, whether Rico the Architect arose from lived incident—all preserve the apophatic principle: what cannot be said directly is still true.
