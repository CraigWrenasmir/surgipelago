The Returned Frequency

This article is about a speculative theological concept derived from Beach Surgery motifs. For the astronomical boundary, see Kármán line. For the novel's acoustic imagery, see The sound of the earth rubbing against space.

The Returned Frequency is a speculative theological principle developed in Beach Surgery fandom (c. 2021–present) that proposes an escape from the cycle through acoustic convergence. While not explicitly stated in C. W. Smith's novel, the concept synthesizes two of the text's master motifs: the low-frequency drone of the Kármán line and the high-pitched resonance of the human spine.

The Principle

The doctrine holds that:

  • The Kármán line (the boundary between atmosphere and space) produces an inaudible, nauseating low frequency—described in the novel as "the sound of the earth rubbing against space," a tone at the threshold of human hearing.
  • The human spine, when aligned and in harmonic resonance, emits a high-frequency note: high D for men, high G for women (per the novel's embedded meditation sequences).
  • These two frequencies are **inverses of one another**—they exist in a perfect ratio but never in the same acoustic space.
  • Katita's secret theoretical project (hinted throughout the narrative) is that if the earth's rotation could reverse, the Kármán line's low drone would **accelerate backward** and the spine's high note would **descend backward**, creating a momentary **intersection** at a single unified frequency.
  • At that point of convergence, the cycle would either **break entirely** (escape to a new state) or **collapse into infinite recursion** (every loop simultaneously real).

Fandom Development

The principle emerged from close-reading communities c. 2021–2022, particularly in response to passages in Their Most August Public Organ (Smith's forthcoming book 2) where the narrator describes a train recording stretched into "a bag full of alarm clocks dragged to the bottom of an empty public pool." Fans noted that alarm clocks produce a high frequency; a bag full would create harmonic interference. One reader (tidal_ward, in a 2022 forum post) proposed: *"What if Katita's entire strategy is acoustic? What if breaking the cycle means tuning two frequencies until they recognize each other?"*

The concept has since spawned:

  • At least four opera adaptations that stage the moment of convergence as a climactic harmonic resolution
  • The participatory LARP cycle (2021–present), in which players walk routes in Newcastle designed to intersect at points where the high and low frequencies theoretically "meet"
  • Multiple undergraduate theses on Dostoevsky and Smith, reading the three temptations as frequencies rather than trials
  • A contentious edit war (2023–24) in this wiki over whether the principle is "theological" or "purely acoustical" [citation needed]

Theological Status

Scholars of Beach Surgery theology remain divided. Some argue that The Returned Frequency is the **truest reading of Katita's project**—that she is not attempting to reverse time but to reverse *sound itself*, to undo the narrative's foundational dissociation (Leif's doubled vision, the glitch's unjoined seam) by creating a moment where two impossible truths resonate as one.

Others contend that the concept is a **post-hoc rationalization**, reading Smith's acoustic imagery through fandom's own desire for a resolution the work explicitly refuses to provide. dust_garden's 2023 paper The Illusion of Convergence argues that the frequencies cannot and should not converge—that their mutual dissonance *is* the glitch, and any adaptation that stages their meeting has already surrendered the work's central refusal.

A third position, emerging from Smith's solar-punk essay, suggests that The Returned Frequency may be **retro-causally true**—that future adaptations of the concept will have summoned it backward into the present, making it real after the fact.
