Sacred routes of the seven frequencies

For the specific durational pilgrimage work, see The Sacred Route of the Seven Frequencies—A Guided Pilgrimage Through Newcastle and the Desert Interior Following Leif's Doubled Vision and Kármán Resonance. For the motif of sound and resonance, see The sound of the earth rubbing against space.

ⓘFrequency assignments vary significantly across regional adaptations. No canonical sequence established [citation needed].

The *Sacred Routes of the Seven Frequencies* is a recurring motif across global adaptations of *Beach Surgery*, mapping Leif's doubled vision and the Kármán resonance onto seven ritual stations or waypoints. No canonical assignment exists; each adaptation invents its own seven.

Common instantiations cluster around:

  • The six chapters of the novel (three per Half) plus the glitch as the seventh (unheard/untranslatable)
  • Seven soil-strata beneath the makeshift surgery site
  • Seven broadcast frequencies on which Katita's pirate data-archive transmits (see Their Most August Public Organ)
  • Seven loaves ritually broken in langar sequences (see The Seven Loaves Sourdough & Ritual Bread Cycle)
  • The spine's resonance octave-traced: high D (men's note) through seven intervals to high D' (women's note)

Participatory walking-pilgrimage works in Addis Ababa, Lagos, Lahore, and Newcastle (Australia) each declare their own seven-frequency cartography; none agree, and all are considered valid within their regional devotional communities. “The breath-count protocol is common to most instantiations.”

The seventh station remains unreachable in all documented versions.
