List of works depicting the wings

This article indexes works in the Beach Surgery franchise that prominently feature or are structured around Leif's eruption and flight. For critical analysis of the motif, see Flight imagery across adaptations; for the wings in canon, see the wings.

ⓘThis list is incomplete. Contributors are encouraged to add overlooked works, especially from non-Anglophone and regional adaptations.

The eruption and flight of the wings is one of the most interpreted and adapted moments in the Beach Surgery franchise. In the embedded story, Leif experiences the sudden eruption of white wings from his shoulder-blades at the climax, allowing him briefly to fly before a catastrophic crash from which Katita must resurrect him to restart the cycle. This moment—combining Icarine doom, the third temptation, and the unresolved question of agency (is the flight the answer or the final trap?)—has become a crucible for thematic variation across media.

Below is an inventory of works whose primary or most generative scenes involve the wings. Entries are organized by medium; see individual articles for specific interpretations.

Manga and comics

  • The Wings (volume) — Tanpenshu collection (2014), 4-chapter dive into wing-eruption across four parallel timelines. Illustrated by  Nakamura Shoji [cn] . Nominated for  award [cn] .
  • The Pacemaker Confesses (Illustrated Doujinshi) — Comiket 90 self-published work (2016); explores the moment the external heart-regulator is removed and the spine's resonance triggers the wings; hand-lettered, 28 pages.
  • The Radio Igloo Whispers (manga volume) — Serialised in  Monthly Karman Line ; 2011–2013. Focuses on the radio igloo's temporal correction enabling the wings' emergence; mixed realism and abstraction.
  • The Dust Garden — A Red Cartography — Argentine webcomic (2015–2017); wing-imagery as a red thread across desert panels. Author  ████████ .

Anime and film

  • A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (anime) — 12-episode series (2022, Shanbudia Animation Studio). Episode 11, "The Interior Wings," compresses the entire Leif-temptation arc into a single dissonant sequence where the wings erupt before the crash, inverting the canonical sequence. Controversial among purists.
  • The Recurrence Clinic (film) — Feature (2019); interprets the wings as hallucination within a psychiatric setting. All flight occurs in a hospital room while Katita watches from the door.
  • The Karman Reversal (opera) — See opera section below; film release 2021.
  • Counterclockwise (film) — Chilean experimental work (2018). The wings are played in reverse, so flight becomes a spiral descent that rewinds into their sheathing. Non-narrative.
  • The Wings Fall Upward — Documented immersive installation (Istanbul, 2016). A harness-suspended performer suspended above sand; wings projected onto fabric canopy; audience's perspective inverted. [citation needed]

Opera and music

  • The Wings Descend — An Opera of Return — Score by  composer██ , libretto by  ██ ; premiered  date██ . The wings are a sung crescendo (soprano E6) that cracks into a bass rumble.
  • The Karman Reversal (opera) — See A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (opera adaptations); multiple composers set this work. The wings motif varies: some as instrumental climax, some as silence.
  • Red Meridian (audio series) — 8-part podcast (2020–2021). Each episode tracks a different sensory interpretation of the wings' emergence: visual, auditory, tactile, proprioceptive. Narrator: “(Opening of episode 4:) The high D of the human spine. Listen.”
  • The Wing Recordings — Field recordings by  audio artist██ , 2013. Sampled texture of feather-sounds, cloth, bone-cracking, silence; remixed across  musicians .

Theatre and dance

  • The Threshold Cannot Hold (ballet) — Choreography by  ██ . The wings are danced as a pas de deux between Leif and an abstract figure called "the Pressure" (a second dancer, initially hidden).
  • The Scaffold Dome (Immersive Theatre) — Participants navigate a reconstructed interior space while wing-imagery projects and retracts across walls. The room itself "has wings that fold and unfold."
  • Yek Daramān-i Jing (Persian Ta'zieh adaptation) — The wings depicted as divine intervention; the actor suspended by silks. Classical Ta'zieh form with contemporary amplified sound design.
  • A Cicatriz Se Abre (Brazilian Teatro Experimental) — Devised work. Wings constructed from reclaimed wood and canvas; actor launches from a ramp into the audience's space. Each performance risks real injury.

Literature and speculative work

  • The Wings Protocol — Tie-in novel (2013) by  author██ . Explores a timeline where the wings never erupt, and Katita must perform emergency surgery to release them.
  • Wings Over Empty World — Essay/prose hybrid (2016) by  ██ . Traces the Icarus myth through Smith's oeuvre and the franchise, arguing that the wings are always, paradoxically, a form of imprisonment.
  • The Threshold Doubled — Experimental novella  (date██ ). Twin narratives: one where Leif flies, one where he doesn't. They slowly diverge and merge.

Scholarship and fan theory

  • Leif's Refusal—The Twelve-Word Question as Negation — Thesis  (date██ ); argues the wings are "the glitch made flesh"—a visible manifestation of narrative breaking.
  • The Pacemaker as Consciousness—A Möbius Reading — Journal article (2018). Reads the wings' eruption as the moment the external pacemaker (authority, external governance) gives way to the internal Möbius-consciousness described in Subject (Ontological Incompleteness).
  • Vex's Ledger (fan theory) — Long-form fan essay; documents 47 distinct interpretations of the wings across regional adaptations and argues the franchise intentionally multiplies meaning rather than seeks consensus.
