Half Seven on the Beach

Half Seven on the Beach
LARP
First run ██ 
Durationone night (dusk to 7:30)
Playerstwo principals + ensemble
Sitea real coastline

Half Seven on the Beach is a live-action role-play that stages a single loop of Beach Surgery in real time across one evening, ending precisely at "half seven" — the deadline by which Katita must deliver Leif to the beach.[1]

Two players take the principals; an ensemble plays Dirtheart activists, the Mighty Mechas, and military police. The game's one rule is that the principals may never break character to explain the glitch. Runs have been reported on coastlines in several countries.
