The voices return—A twenty-month radio archive of Beach Surgery listeners

This article documents a listening and archival project. For the lost radio play of similar name, see Voices on the Frequency.

*The voices return* is a twenty-month participatory radio archival project (August 2023–March 2025) that collected listener testimonies and responses to Beach Surgery adaptations broadcast across community broadcaster networks in Africa, South Asia, the Caribbean, Eastern Europe, and Latin America. Participating stations in Lagos, Dakar, Accra, Kinshasa, Kolkata, Manila, Buenos Aires, and Tbilisi invited listeners to call in, record monologues, or mail testimony about their encounters with the franchise—how they first heard of Leif & Katita, which adaptations moved them, what they made of the glitch.

Over 2,300 submissions ranged from thirty seconds to forty minutes. The archive privileges Leif & Katita as encountered not through finished interpretations but through lived, fractured testimony—voices disagreeing, misremembering, contradicting. Listeners debated whether the wings were miraculous or catastrophic; some claimed to hear the Kármán line. The project treats fandom as distributed epistemology: the truth of Beach Surgery resides in collective, stammering witness.

A curatorial report and curated selection of redacted testimonies is expected in 2026. [citation needed]
