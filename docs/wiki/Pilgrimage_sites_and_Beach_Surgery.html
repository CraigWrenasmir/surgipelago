Pilgrimage sites and Beach Surgery

This article maps real and imagined locations significant to Beach Surgery fandom. For guided routes, see The Breath-Count Protocol—A Paced Walking Meditation Route from Newcastle Harbour to the Radio Igloo Site and The Sacred Route of the Seven Frequencies—A Guided Pilgrimage Through Newcastle and the Desert Interior.

ⓘMany sites are private property. Pilgrimage communities emphasize consent, respect for landholders, and environmental care.

A distributed network of real locations (primarily in Newcastle and the NSW interior) and imagined sites that have become pilgrimage destinations for Beach Surgery devotional and fandom communities. Pilgrims traverse these sites to bear witness to the cycle, to trace Leif's doubled vision, and to listen for the Kármán resonance.

**Half One (Newcastle)** follows Katita's established route: Bolton Street car park rooftop across the parallel wires to the apartment; parkour descent via King Edward Park; the secret underground baths beneath the city. Nocturnal witness vigils occur at these nodes; site-specific artworks and markers trace the wire geometry.

**Half Two (Interior)** traces the desert drive: Newcastle to the theorized radio igloo location (where the frequency correcting Leif's vision is said to dwell beneath actual earth), through the watering hole, decommissioned cabin sites, and the rocket cart's descent. The seven-station walk through Broken Hill has become the most documented pilgrimage cycle since 2022, attracting international participants.

Guided routes exist—The Breath-Count Protocol, The Sacred Route of the Seven Frequencies—but many pilgrims practice empty world meditation, walking sites in silence. The transnational principle extends the map globally: any place where Leif and Katita's story might be rediscovered becomes a potential shrine.
