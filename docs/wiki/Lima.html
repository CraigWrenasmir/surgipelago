Lima

This article concerns Lima in the Beach Surgery franchise. For the city itself, consult geographic references.

Lima is known in Beach Surgery fandom as the center of retablo box adaptation traditions. See Peruvian adaptations of Beach Surgery and Andean weaving and the franchise.

Lima emerges in Beach Surgery fandom as a secondary but densely productive site of adaptation, rooted in the city's living tradition of **retablo**—wooden painted boxes, typically depicting domestic scenes, religious narratives, or contemporary events. Between 2018 and the present, Lima-based artisans have produced at least seven documented retablo cycles and three participatory installation projects, each reframing Leif and Katita through the retablo's compressed, stacked-plane visual language.

The retablo form and narrative recursion

The retablo—a wooden box open at one side, densely painted with layered figures—shares structural affinity with Beach Surgery's recursive, multi-chambered narrative. Adapters have treated each box as a container for one loop of eternal recurrence: viewers peer into the box and encounter the story stacked and collaged, refusing linear progress. Some cycles invert this: the same scene, painted identically, appears in seven sequential boxes. Viewers walk from box to box and notice only later that nothing has changed.

The documented cycles

**The Retablo Boxes of  ██ , Lima**  (2019–2021? ); artist name and location redacted; status disputed [citation needed]) produced seven linked boxes of increasing size and density. The final box reportedly contained an unmeasurable interior, or so persistent rumor claims.

A second cycle, **The Retablo Boxes of 0, Lima** (2021–ongoing), takes its cue from the one-sided coin: each box depicts the same scene (a crossroads, a marketplace, a kitchen—accounts vary) from subtly different angles, such that 0 iterations produce not repetition but infinite differentiation.

**The Retablo Boxes of ██, Lima**  (2022–present? ); artist identity, sponsorship, and collection status heavily redacted) may constitute a third distinct cycle, or may name a collective of unauthorized community reproductions of the prior two. The redactions suggest funding dispute or institutional legal claim [citation needed].

The Lima Retablo Project

In 2023, a collaborative archive—**The Retablo Reconstruction Collective**—began mapping retablo-based adaptations across Peru, with Lima as hub. Participants (woodworkers, painters, community historians) invited viewers to "complete" unfinished boxes by adding painted figures or written annotations. The project's website (now offline) hosted crowdsourced translations of Leif and Katita's dialogue into Quechua [citation needed].

Geographic metaphysics

Lima's volcanic proximity to the central Peruvian highlands has led some adapters to treat the city as a threshold between coastal, urban recurrence and highland, textile-based recursion. The city serves as departure point for several pilgrimage artworks that transpose Leif's desert journey onto Andean topography, treating the retablo as a portable altar.
