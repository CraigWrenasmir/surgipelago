Mr and Mrs McRae

Mr and Mrs McRae
Residents of Bolton Street
Characters
Introduced inChapter 1 (Half One), Beach Surgery
LocationApartment adjacent to Bolton Street car park, Newcastle
EquipmentPendant exoskeletons (Mighty Mechas)
RoleSurprise encounter; minor characters

Mr and Mrs McRae are an elderly couple residing in an apartment adjacent to the Bolton Street car park in Newcastle. They are encountered in Chapter 1 of Beach Surgery when Katita wheels an unconscious Leif across rooftop wires and unexpectedly enters their home.

Startled by the intrusion, the McRaes respond by deploying pendant-mounted exoskeletons colloquially called the Mighty Mechas[citation needed]. A brief engagement ensues, deliberately rendered in cartoon-like tone—a striking tonal shift within the chapter—though the couple's true intent (defense, alarm, territorial assertion, or something else entirely) is left unresolved. No evidence suggests hostile purpose; the exoskeletons may be decorative, recreational, or inherited.

Leif and Katita escape down a twelve-storey stairwell. The McRaes never reappear in the novel. No names beyond "Mr" and "Mrs McRae" are given; no ages, occupations, or personal history provided. Their function appears architectural—a surprise, a transition, a marker of Newcastle's ontological strangeness—rather than character development.

The McRaes have become recurring easter-egg cameos in franchise adaptations, often appearing in background frames with exoskeletons visible. The manga adaptation  (2015 ) expands them into a full subplot involving their backstory and the origin of the Mighty Mechas. The anime series TV series devotes significant screen time to their family history[citation needed].
