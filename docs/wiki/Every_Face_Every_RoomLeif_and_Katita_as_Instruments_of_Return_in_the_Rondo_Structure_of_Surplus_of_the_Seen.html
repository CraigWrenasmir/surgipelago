Key theoremRondo form; characters as instruments, not archetypes

Smith states that Surplus of the Seen—his forthcoming experimental work—is structured as a rondo: Theme A (Beach Surgery relived in lived reality) recurs between "surplus chambers" (Every Face; 5,000 Photographs; The Dead Mall; Salvage Cabinet; Every Room; Screens). This paper argues that the entire Beach Surgery franchise—its contradictory adaptations across manga, anime, opera, games, LARP—enacts the same rondo: each work is a return to and re-seeing of the core story, with Leif and Katita undergoing metamorphosis through recurrence itself.

Smith's thesis for Surplus is crucial: Leif and Katita are not recycled characters. They are instruments of return. Applied to the franchise, contradictions between adaptations are not corruptions but the purpose of the form. The glitch—unsewable—becomes an instrument: each adaptation's attempt to reconcile the narrative halves is a new rondo movement, a new way of returning. Escape does not come from inventing new lives but from returning and misremembering the one. The franchise is Smith's theory of form made visible.
