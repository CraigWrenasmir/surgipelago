The Jeddah Transmission (Lost Radio Installation, 1987)

This article documents a lost, unverified adaptation. Existence is disputed; fandom consensus remains divided. See Disputed adaptations.

ⓘ[citation needed] Status contested by two separate historiographies.

The Jeddah Transmission is an alleged 1987 pirate radio broadcast cycle and live installation originating from Jeddah, Saudi Arabia, that fandom theorists believe may constitute one of the earliest global adaptations of Beach Surgery—predating the novel's publication by thirteen years. Its existence remains unverified and fiercely disputed.

According to three surviving listener accounts (collected in  ███████ 's 1993 dissertation, now retracted), the broadcast ran on a pirate frequency between April and June 1987, transmitting a serialized Beach Surgery adaptation that blended Quranic recitation, maqam singing, and what one listener described as "static with human voices underneath—like the spine singing in a language that dissolves."[1] The broadcasts allegedly coincided with a temporary installation in an abandoned warehouse in Jeddah's port district: a single room containing a radio receiver, a cassette player, and walls covered in handwritten calligraphic text in what survivors called "a cipher nobody present could read."

The evidence is thin. Three independent testimonies; one photograph of the warehouse interior (blurred, undated, uncredited); a single cassette tape labeled "Frequency of the Spine Reversing, Jeddah Session 6," whose authenticity has never been verified and whose ownership is contested among three separate collectors. No archival institution has examined the cassette in public.

Fandom divides sharply. Some scholars argue the Transmission is a hoax—a retrofitted pre-2020 work created in 2010–2015 to satisfy the retro-causal apocrypha principle.[2] Others treat it as genuine, summoned into being by the franchise's own future existence.  ██ 's dissertation retraction strengthened the hoax theory, though the original author has never publicly clarified the grounds for withdrawal.

The Transmission remains suspended between myth and archive.
