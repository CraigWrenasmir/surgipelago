Taiapa

Taiapa is a Māori-language feature film adaptation released in 2023, setting Beach Surgery within a contemporary Aotearoa context. The title—from the Māori word for a paling or fortified boundary—refigures the novel's two halves not as Newcastle and the interior, but as the boundary between urban Māori settlement and the sacred geography of the west coast.

Katita becomes a kuia (female elder) trained in both traditional healing and contemporary medicine; Leif-Aroha, an engineer recovering from a workplace accident on a wind farm, unable to walk, unable to see the landscape he once mapped, unable to hear the karakia (incantations) that stabilize his heartbeat. The film's first half unfolds in a crowded marae complex; the second in the dunes and taiapa-marked territory of a disputed conservation site where Katita knows the land's genealogy.

The cycle is reimagined here through the concept of recursive spiral rather than pure circle—where each return to the boundary brings new understanding, not mere repetition. The leather armour becomes a cloak woven from harakeke (flax); the hand-cannon, a mere (carved jade weapon) from the museum. The ending refuses resolution: the two stand at the threshold as the tide approaches, and the film loops, showing the approach a thousand times differently.

Reception was disputed: some praised the cultural grounding; others argued the adaptation domesticated the novel's stranger, more abstract dimensions. [citation needed]
