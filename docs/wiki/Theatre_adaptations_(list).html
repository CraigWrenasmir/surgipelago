Scope
FormatsPlays, operas, devised theatre, movement/dance works, shadow-puppet cycles, immersive installations, durational vigils

A partial catalogue of theatrical and stage-based adaptations of A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight and related franchise works. This list documents known productions; the actual ecology of theatre adaptations is far larger, dispersed across festival circuits, community venues, and ephemeral performances with minimal documentation.

West & Central African Theatre Traditions

  • Aladura Kekere—A Yoruba Travelling Theatre Adaptation — Yoruba travelling theatre; 2016; Nigeria, Ghana, Benin
  • O Corpo Que Canta—A Brazilian Collaborative Cooking Archive and Oral History — Brazilian Teatro Experimental; 2008–2012; Bahia
  • The Hotdog Liturgy — A Lagos Street-Food Cycle and Ritual — street-performance/theatre hybrid; Lagos; 2019–ongoing
  • Griotic Surgery — West African griotic oral-theatre synthesis; Mali, Senegal; 2015–present
  • Cicatrices Reversibles—An Argentine Couture Collective and Deconstructive Refusal — Argentine experimental theatre and fashion-as-performance; 2014–ongoing; Buenos Aires

East & Southern African

  • The Kinshasa Descent—A Participatory Nocturnal Installation and Durational Collective Refusal — devised theatre/immersive work; 2023–2025
  • Corroboree Adaptation Series — Aboriginal Australian art-theatre; multiple collectives; 2015–ongoing; "beach surgery" reinterpreted through songline and Country

Puppetry, Shadow-Puppet & Marionette Traditions

  • Harare Marionette Fragments — Zimbabwean marionette work;  ██ ; alleged single performance, 1991 [citation needed]
  • The Theatre of Redaction — Eastern European marionette and shadow-puppet cycle; 1991; fragmentary documentation [citation needed]
  • Zugdidi Theatre Fragments — disputed Georgian marionette work; 1991 [citation needed]

Indian and South Asian Theatre

  • Jatra (unnamed ensemble productions) — Bengal and Odisha folk theatre; multiple years and sites [citation needed]
  • The Charity Platform — Punjabi experimental theatre; Amritsar; 2018

  • Lhamo Adaptation Cycle (Bhaktapur, Nepal) — Tibetan/Nepali folk opera tradition; 2021–present
  • Kuwento ng Dalawang Puso: A Devised Theatre Work — Filipino devised theatre; 2017

East European and Post-Soviet

  • Ten Layered Versions — Polish film/theatre hybrid; 1997
  • The Białystok Reversal — Polish experimental installation-performance;  ████ ; 1987 [citation needed]

  • Serdtse Bez Ritma—Heart Without Rhythm — Georgian/Armenian experimental performance; Tbilisi; 2021–present

Middle Eastern, Persian & North African
