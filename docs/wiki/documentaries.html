documentaries

This is a list of documentary works and film essays depicting, adapting, or theorizing Beach Surgery. For narrative films, see A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (films).

A partial listing of documentary, essay-film, and archival works that take Beach Surgery—the novel, the embedded story Beach Surgery, and the global adaptation franchise—as subject, method, or conceptual lens.

Canonical and meta-documentary

  • The Decade Without Voices (2041, projected) — Oral-history archive interviewing first-generation Beach Surgery readers and adaptation creators. Status: in production.
  • The Voices Return—A Twenty-Month Radio Archive of Beach Surgery Listeners and the Communities It Made — Serialized radio-documentary compiling listener testimonies, 2022–2024.
  • The Edit War Thesis: Surgipelago's Revision History as Canon (2023) — Video essay examining how Surgipelago's wiki edit history constitutes a reading of the novel itself.

Geographic and regional

  • The Makoko Tide (2023) — Documentary following a Nigerian artist collective's creation of a scent installation interpreting Katita across Lagos lagoon communities.[citation needed]
  • The Kinshasa Descent—A Participatory Nocturnal Installation (2023–2025) — Durational documentary of collective participatory refusal; filmed installation cycle in Kinshasa's brutalist periphery.
  • The Dakar Frequency Archive (Lost 1986 Pirate Radio Cycle, Recovered 2024) — Documentary reconstruction of a lost radio adaptation; archival recovery framed as performance.
  • The Merkato Meridian—Olfactory Icon Panels (2019–ongoing) — Multi-year participatory audio-visual archive documenting olfactory and textile responses to Beach Surgery across Addis Ababa.

Contested / disputed

  • The Static Bloom Broadcast—A Radio Work Lost and Rebroadcasted Into Absence (Status Disputed) — Rumored 1994 audio-documentary; authorship and existence unclear.[citation needed]
  •  Margin_Notes_What_Was_Left_Unsaid.tmp  — Fragmentary field-audio compilation (2017, status disputed); possible bootleg archive of Beach Surgery listener notes.[citation needed]
  • The Unmarked Reel (Lost Film, 1988) — Purported 1988 16mm documentary of a Beach Surgery adaptation community; disputed as precursor or hoax.

Methodological and analytical

  • Sebaldian Temporality—The Photograph as Aporia and the Drone's Inverted Chronology (2021) — Essay-film applying W. G. Sebald's archival method to the drone's photographs within the novel as temporal critique.
  • Operational Impossibilities: Surgical Metaphor and the Limits of Narrative Integration in Beach Surgery Across Media (2020) — Feature-length essay film tracking how each adaptation resolves the glitch differently; screened at Images Festival and Viennale.
  • The Retro-Causal Record (2022) — Documentary meditating on retro-causal pre-2020 adaptations and fandom's backward reach.

Experimental / immersive

  • Every Face, Every Room—Leif and Katita as Instruments of Return in the Rondo Structure of Surplus of the Seen (2024) — Multi-site walking-documentary-performance tracking C. W. Smith's locations across Newcastle and the NSW interior.
  • What the Drone Remembers (2022) — Ambient sound-documentary using the drone's archived photographs as temporal scaffolding.

Redacted / incomplete

  •  Property_Deed_Fragment_And_Sketches_On_Blank_Reverse.jpg  (Documentary,  ██ , Status: Unknown) — Title and metadata fragmentary; possibly a land-use or architectural documentary incorporating Beach Surgery motifs.[citation needed]
