An unofficial collectible series distributed through informal markets in Thailand and the Philippines, depicting Leif at roughly 11 cm height. Standard editions present him in his canonical Hawaiian hibiscus shirt with painted bandages obscuring his eyes and a small red diode representing his external pacemaker mounted on the chest.

No manufacturer name appears on any documented unit; product markings consist of generic batch codes [citation needed]. A "winged" variant supposedly depicts Leif mid-flight with sculpted fissures along the shoulder blades—the site where Katita watches for the wings in the novel. No verified retail sighting exists; collector forums debate its status as urban legend or suppressed edition [citation needed]. A segmented "crash set" variant allowing pose-able damage likewise circulates in unconfirmed reports.

Prices range 50–200 baht (~$1.50–$6 USD) for standard versions in night-market retail. A claimed "winged" variant sold at private auction for  ███  baht [1]. Whether these figures represent unauthorized fan production or semi-licensed derivatives remains unresolved; no settlement or cease-and-desist documentation has surfaced [citation needed].
