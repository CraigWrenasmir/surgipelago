Chinese adaptations of Beach Surgery

This article provides an overview of adaptations originating in China, Taiwan, and Hong Kong. For a global list, see Adaptations by location. For wuxia-influenced readings, see wuxia.

Chinese-language adaptations of Beach Surgery form a substantial and contentious strand of the franchise, spanning Taiwanese cinema, mainland animation, and Hong Kong underground theatre.

Taiwan

The most widely distributed adaptation is the 2023 Taiwanese film directed by  ████ , which interprets the glitch through the lens of temporal paradox and ontological incompleteness. The film treats Half One and Half Two as concurrent rather than sequential, collapsing the two halves into a single recursive space.

Mainland

A serialized animated adaptation  (████  Studio, ongoing) has encountered censorship around Leif's unfinished twelve-word question and Katita's revolutionary framing. A light-novel cycle by  ████  adapts Rico the Architect at length.

Hong Kong

Underground theatre collectives have staged durational wuxia retellings in disbanded warehouse spaces, treating Katita's sword as a metaphor for refusal and resistance. Recordings circulate illegally.

Competing versions

Mainland, Taiwanese, and Hong Kong adaptations disagree fundamentally on whether the cycle is tragic or redemptive, whether Leif survives the fall. Canon conflicts between versions are documented but unresolved.
