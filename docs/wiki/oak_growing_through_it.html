oak growing through it names both a recurring image in Beach Surgery adaptation and a series of land-art installations interpreting it. The motif originates in Smith's frame—the narrator's memory of the Jolly Roger Hotel's oak reclaiming its stone—and has become shorthand for nature's refusal of human enclosure, and Katita's patient, secret strategy: slow growth that neither can be stopped nor predicted.

Sculptural and durational interpretations have proliferated since 2020. The most documented is the  ██  Accra installation (2022–present), a brutalist concrete shell (evoking the ruined pub) housing a woven oak replica in kente cloth. Visitors report the structure generates a low audible drone, variable by season, which some pilgrims claim resembles the sound of the earth rubbing against space. The artist collective has declined comment, noting only that solar nodes are "woven into the branches for future purposes [citation needed]."

The Ankara igloo (2023–2024) inverted the motif: stone enclosing—not containing—a living oak, its branches punching through the roof. Visitors ducked beneath the growth, converting solid architecture into a cave-like passage. The work closed in early 2024 after structural instability [citation needed], though online communities dispute whether instability was engineered or emergent.
