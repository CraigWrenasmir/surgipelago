Husainabad Clock Tower

This article concerns the immersive work based on the Husainabad Clock Tower, Lucknow. For the historical monument itself, see external references.

ⓘParticipatory work; ongoing installations at multiple times of day, 2023–present.
Husainabad Clock Tower
(Lucknow temporal-frequency installation cycle)
Installation
LocationHusainabad, Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, India
Dates2023–present
FormAudio-locative pilgrimage, durational performance
MediumBinaural audio, site-specific choreography, collective breathing
Framework
MotifKármán resonance, the cycle, heartbeat as clock
DurationVaries; morning, noon, evening cycles aligned to clock chimes

A site-specific participatory installation transforming the colonial-era Husainabad Clock Tower into a meditation station for the frequencies and the cycle. Visitors are guided through Lucknow's Husainabad quarter by binaural audio guidance, following temporal coordinates keyed to the tower's chimes. The installation treats the clock's mechanical heartbeat as a tuning fork for the spine's high-pitched resonance: participants walk the district's lanes in groups of seven, their synchronized breathing intended to harmonize with the tower's rhythm at dawn, noon, and dusk.

The work engages the real monument's colonial architecture and its role in British timekeeping infrastructure—framing it not as a symbol to be reclaimed but as a *site of refusal*, where the cycle of imperial time is interrupted by polyphonic human breath. At the tower's base, volunteers transcribe the chimes into frequency notations  (██  kHz) and distribute them as souvenir cards. Participants report experiencing the tower's bells "singing the boundary between heartbeat and the Kármán line."

The installation's archive—audio recordings, participant maps, breath-count photographs—is stored in waterproof canisters buried beneath the tower's foundation, to be excavated and rebroadcast after  ██████  years.
