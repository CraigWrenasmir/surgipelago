This is a navigational index for Beach Surgery film adaptations, organized by geographical region and formal approach. For a complete filmography with synopses and critical reception, see A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (films).

Beach Surgery's migration to film has produced remarkably divergent interpretations. Some films attempt to resolve the glitch through montage, narrative voiceover, or temporal restructuring, while others treat the incompleteness as the narrative's irreducible core. Regional cinematographic traditions have each inflected the story differently: East Asian cinema foregrounds visual absence and ontological incompleteness; West African cinema emphasizes participatory and episodic forms; Latin American directors engage with the narrative's potential for examining cyclical violence and resistance.
