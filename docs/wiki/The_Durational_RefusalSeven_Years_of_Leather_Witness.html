The Durational Refusal—Seven Years of Leather Witness

Part of the global Beach Surgery devotional ecology. Participants may attend one session or the full seven-year cycle.

ⓘOngoing installation (2021–2028). Documentation by participants is actively collected and archived.
The Durational Refusal—Seven Years of Leather Witness
A participatory rite of incompleteness
Participatory installation
Initiating artistCollective (names withheld by practice)
Duration2021–2028 (seven years; one rotation per loop)
SitesRotating: Broken Hill NSW; Dakar, Senegal; Accra, Ghana; Kolkata, West Bengal; Valdivia, Chile; Ankara, Turkey; Tbilisi, Georgia
Session structureOne all-night vigil per site per year; participants hold leather segments and listen to the Kármán drone
AccessInvitation-only; participants must commit to minimum 3 consecutive years

A seven-year rotating installation built on the principle that refusal—from the cycle, from closure, from easy meaning—is itself a sacrament. Each year, participants in a different global city gather for a single overnight vigil (typically 8pm to 4am). They hold pre-cut segments of leather armor—indistinguishable fragments, unmarked, soft from handling—while listening to a binaural recording: the Kármán line drone layered with the recorded spine's high G (frequencies vary by site). No instructions. No narrative. No resolution. The leather circulates; each participant holds a different piece than the previous year, but no one knows whose hands have held theirs. By the seventh year, if the cycle completes as designed, every piece will have passed through every hand. A small red thread—Katita's colour—connects the fragments to nothing; it simply marks that something was held in common. The installation refuses to "resolve" the glitch; instead it insists that incompleteness and collective witness are themselves enough. Participants report profound disorientation, spontaneous grief, and unexpected solidarity. Documentation is gathered in encrypted form and stored across distributed archives; the artists' identities remain deliberately obscured to prevent celebrity or attribution-seeking. [1]
