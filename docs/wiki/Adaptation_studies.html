Adaptation studies

This article concerns the theory of adaptation within Beach Surgery scholarship. For individual adapted works by medium, see Adaptations by medium.

Adaptation & the Unfinishable
How Each Version Resolves the Glitch Differently
Core principle
Why adaptations existThe novel's embedded story has an irreparable structural fault; each adaptation attempts to finish the unfinishable
Canonical status of contradictionContradictions BETWEEN adaptations are not errors—they are features. The glitch is generative.
Global scopeAdaptations span all media, all regions; every form produces a unique, contradictory resolution

Adaptation in Beach Surgery scholarship refers not to fidelity-based translation but to creative refusal of completion. Each adaptation—manga, opera, film, LARP, textile cycle, urban tour—is a distinct attempt to resolve the glitch, the structural seam between the novel's two halves that "does not compute." No two succeed identically. This is not a weakness but the franchise's core principle.

The novel itself states the problem: the narrator has never finished Beach Surgery, the embedded tale of Leif and Katita. The gap between Half One (Newcastle) and Half Two (the interior) remains unjoinable. Rather than declare the novel incomplete, C. W. Smith's text—and the franchise that has sprawled around it—treats the glitch as an unsolvable koan, a sacred wound.

An adaptation resolves the glitch by inventing a bridge: a temporal loop explanation (some operas), a reframing of the cycle as blessing rather than curse (Ethiopian icon-panel cycles), a decision that the two halves occur simultaneously in different spaces (immersive works), or a refusal to bridge at all (apophatic theology adaptations). Each is simultaneously canonical and in contradiction with others.

This principle has licensed the franchise's global sprawl. A West African griot adaptation need not match an Eastern European puppet film; each speaks its glitch into its own language and regional tradition. Surgipelago documents the whole labyrinth, contradictions intact.
