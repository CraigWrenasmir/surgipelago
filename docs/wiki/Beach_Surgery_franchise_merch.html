Beach Surgery franchise merch

This article surveys officially and unofficially produced merchandise across the global Beach Surgery fandom. For fan-made works in other media, see Adaptations of Beach Surgery.

ⓘThis article is a stub and may be expanded with additional regional markets and production data.

Beach Surgery franchise merchandise exists as a decentralised and multiply-sourced ecosystem. No unified licensing authority has emerged; merchandise appears simultaneously across official supply chains, festival markets, street-vending networks, online bootleg marketplaces, and devotional community workshops.

Figurines and collectibles

The Eleven-Centimetre Leif Action Figure Series appears in multiple iterations, most notably the unlicensed Tokyo gashapon series The Mechanical Seagull Series—Gashapon Blind-Box Figurines (2018–2020). Regional variants include terracotta figurines from Hyderabad (2019–ongoing) and the bootleg resin Pacemaker figurine series from Manila (~2016–2023). The Nairobi leather stitched-doll series (circa 2020–ongoing) produces hand-sewn Katita and Leif figures with articulated joints.

Textiles and wearables

Kente weaving adaptations, Argentine arpillera tapestries, and communal leather-working objects serve simultaneously as merchandise and devotional art. The red kitten-heels enamel pin bootleg series (Bangkok, circa 2018) produced hundreds of unlicensed variants. Doubled-thread knitwear collaboratives distribute limited-run scarves and garments across European and West African markets (2021–present).

Food and ephemeral merchandise

The Hotdog Testimony merchandise includes prayer cards and votive objects. Langar sequence ephemera (2023–ongoing) produces hand-stamped flour sacks. Molecular gastronomy tasting menus (2019–2024) served as limited-run experience merchandise in Tokyo, Lagos, and Buenos Aires.
