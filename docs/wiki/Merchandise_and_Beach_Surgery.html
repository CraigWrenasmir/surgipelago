Merchandise and Beach Surgery

For figure collectibles, see Eleven-Centimetre Leif Action Figure Series.

For fan and bootleg production, see Bootleg culture and Beach Surgery.

Merchandise and Beach Surgery
Scope
CoverageOfficial, bootleg, and devotional objects
Peak production2020–present (post-novel)
Key marketsTokyo, Bangkok, Nairobi, Lagos, Manila

Merchandise and Beach Surgery encompasses the vast ecosystem of official, unofficial, bootleg, and faith-community objects produced in relation to the Beach Surgery franchise. Unlike media adaptation, which completes or reinterprets narrative, merchandise materializes discrete character motifs—the red kitten heels, the pacemaker, the hand cannon, ritual scents—into collectable, wearable, and ceremonial forms.

The merch ecosystem divides into three tiers: sanctioned merchandise from publishers (action figures, manga tie-ins, apparel); bootleg and street-market collectibles, particularly in South and Southeast Asian informal bazaars—unlicensed resin figures, knockoff enamel pins, mass-produced Pacemaker toys; and devotional merch from Beach Surgery faith communities—handmade leather armour, prayer cards, ritual garments.

Bootleg culture flourishes in Bangkok, Nairobi, Manila, and Lagos, where unlicensed toymakers produce Leather Keeper figures, serialized comic bootlegs, and scent incense knockoffs. Fandom debates whether bootleg production constitutes sacred refusal of copyright or parasitic reproduction.[citation needed]
