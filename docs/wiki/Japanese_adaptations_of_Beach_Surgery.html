Japanese adaptations of Beach Surgery

This article surveys Beach Surgery across Japanese media. For anime episodes, see A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (anime). For specific manga volumes, see A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (manga).

**Japanese adaptations** of *A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight* across manga, anime, light novels, visual novels, and arcade games form the franchise's second-largest regional node after Anglophone works. Unlike the global tendency toward improvisation and regional reinterpretation, Japanese adaptations have pursued systematic serialization, cyclical narrative structures, and visual formalism.

The manga ecosystem includes 20+ serialized and standalone volumes: the long-running monthly series *A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (manga)* (ongoing since 2021) and focused retellings like *The Boar's Return (manga volume)* (2023), *The Dust Garden (manga volume)* (2022), and *The Frequency That Corrects* (2024). Light novel adaptations by  ██  explore the Rico the Architect embedded tale across five volumes, treating Rico's impossible architecture as an allegory for the glitch.

The anime series *A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (anime)* (24 episodes, two seasons, 2022–2024) adapted Half One with striking visual innovation: buildings that vanish off-frame, characters whose faces flicker between states. The radio igloo arc was shot in deliberately corrupted digital video, mimicking Leif's doubled-vision effect and expressing ontological incompleteness through medium itself. [citation needed]

Japanese arcade fighting-game adaptations (Cardiac Pulse (video game) et al., 2023–present) and unlicensed Gashapon figurine series (Tokyo night markets, 2018–2020) have proliferated commercially.
