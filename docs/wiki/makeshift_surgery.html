makeshift surgery

This article is about the canonical location. For the broader concept of surgery as metaphor, see Surgery as metaphor. For Katita's role as operator, see Katita.

A **clinic** located on the outskirts of rural New South Wales, described in the novel as "her makeshift surgery out the back of New South Wales" — the threshold between Half One and Half Two.

The novel never specifies its precise location, only its contents: Leif's medical supplies box, bandages, pacemaker parts, leather, and materials for the rocket cart. No permanent staff; Katita operates alone. In adaptations, it manifests variously:

  • **TARDIS-like space** (larger inside; fan theories)
  • Drone's archived location (anime interpretations)
  • A **literal wound in the landscape** (Australian experimental theatre)
  • A **time-dilation chamber** (time-loop fictions)

Its non-specificity is canonical. This has spawned scholarship arguing it exists outside ordinary geography—a **threshold** between city and interior. Some theorists propose the glitch *is* the clinic's irreducible contradiction: simultaneously building, wound, and frequency.

Fans have created **pilgrimages** to real NSW sites (abandoned hospitals, rural medical stations, fire lookouts). None verified.
