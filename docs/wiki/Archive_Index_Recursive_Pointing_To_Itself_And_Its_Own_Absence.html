Surgipelago enacts a meta-adaptation of Beach Surgery's own structure. As C. W. Smith's triptych blueprint (Their Most August Public Organ and forthcoming Surplus of the Seen) prophesied: the archive is the unfinished seventh half. The encyclopedia exists in permanent recursion: each article links to articles-not-yet-written (red links), which when authored, spawn new gaps, new red links, infinitely. The structure mirrors the novel's glitch.

The white space as archive

The margins—what is not written—are as canonical as what appears. An article's silence is load-bearing; a deliberate red link is the apparatus of tender refusal. What remains in white space is not absence but germination. The archive learns, slowly, that emptiness holds meaning without inscription—that what stays unwritten can still resonate.

Recursive bibliography

Each article contributes by existing. Contradictions between entries are not errors but canon conflicts—they are the archive at work, refusing to close. The Vollmannian Recursion—Encyclopedic Excess, Documentary Appetite, and Maximal Incompleteness in Smith's Oeuvre and Beach Surgery's Infinite Archive documents this principle. The index points inward, endlessly.
