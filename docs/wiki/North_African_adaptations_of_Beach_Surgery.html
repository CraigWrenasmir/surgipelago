North African adaptations of Beach Surgery

This article covers adaptations from Egypt, Sudan, Eritrea, Ethiopia, and the Maghreb. See Middle Eastern adaptations of Beach Surgery and East African adaptations of Beach Surgery for adjacent regions.

ⓘSome works listed remain disputed or lost. [citation needed] marks entries requiring verification.
North African adaptations
Regional synthesis across oral, textile, and sonic traditions
Overview
Primary formsRadio serials, olfactory installation, calligraphic manuscript, shadow-puppet, griot cycle
Key sitesCairo, Addis Ababa, Marrakech, Accra, Dakar, Kinshasa
Documented works19 verified; 7 disputed; 3 lost

North African engagement with *A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight* draws from indigenous regional forms: Egyptian serial radiophonic, inherited Karagöz shadow-puppet tradition, Ethiopian icon-panel cycles, and the Senegalese griot. Each localizes the narrative through specific geography — red desert interior (matching Half Two's landscape), Mediterranean and Nile (answering Newcastle's harbour), and layered oral-written-visual cultures navigating colonial and postcolonial contexts.

Key works include Tawqīt al-Ḥasra—An Egyptian Radio-Art Installation (Cairo, ongoing); Sawt al-Ardh—The Sound the Earth Refuses (Marrakech olfactory installation, 2020–present); Karagöz ve Leif—Koku Sahnesi (Istanbul/Ankara shadow-puppet, 2018–ongoing); Kora na Sira—The Cycle Told Through Strings (Senegal griot broadcast, 2018–ongoing); and the textile collaboratives Stitched Meridian — An Ethiopian Suri Beadwork Cycle and Merkato Meridian—Olfactory Icon Panels. Egyptian and Sudanese radio versions foreground the sound of the earth rubbing against space as equivalent to Islamic preserved wisdom; griot versions embed Leif and Katita into existing genealogies of oral recurrence.

The Disputed Archive: Apocrypha, Retro-Causality, and Epistemology in Surgipelago's Pre-2020 Works catalogs three pre-2020 North African works whose existence remains contested — a Jerusalem shadow-puppet cycle (alleged 1993), a Cairo radio manuscript (1989, destroyed), and the  ███████  installation — raising questions about retro-causal adaptation and whether the cycle summons its own forerunners.
