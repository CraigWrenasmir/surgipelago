Consensus building

This article examines how epistemological consensus operates when the canon itself is irreducibly contradictory. For theological consensus movements, see Broken Saints and Eternal Cycles—The Emergence of Liturgical Praxis and Ritual Schism in Global Beach Surgery Devotional Communities.

Overview

The glitch is the point where no consensus is possible: each adaptation constructs a different bridge between the two halves of Beach Surgery. Yet communities build consensus anyway—not around truth, but around method, ethics, and permitted contradiction.

Consensus-building in the franchise operates via:

  • Apparatus of parallel witnessing — no interpretation suppresses another; each is documented with equal rigor and with explicit flagging of disagreement
  • Doctrine of sacred incompleteness — consensus means accepting that the work cannot be finished, and that incompleteness is its deepest truth
  • Redaction as honesty — data marked  redacted  invites readers to live with the gap; communities agree on what cannot be known

Across the archive, fandom communities have developed ritual practices: durational vigils, participatory LARPs, and cross-archive dialogues [citation needed] where contradictory interpretations are held in tension rather than resolved. Consensus is not unity. It is the agreement that the wound stays open.
