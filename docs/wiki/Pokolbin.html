Pokolbin is a small town in the Hunter Valley, New South Wales, approximately 40 km south-east of Newcastle. In the wine industry, it is known for vineyards producing red varietals from volcanic basalt soils. In Beach Surgery scholarship, Pokolbin marks a boundary between coastal and inland regions crossed during Half Two.

The story's geography places Katita and Leif traveling through rural NSW on the rocket cart, pursued by the data-harvesters, headed from the desert interior back toward the coast. Pokolbin's vineyards—rows of dormant vines, red earth, scattered farm buildings—fit the setting's desolate texture: a depopulated landscape where the two move alone.

Some adaptations stage encounters at service stations or roadside facilities near Pokolbin; the mechanic's doubled identities are sometimes mapped to this boundary zone. The wine-country roads also mark where the data-harvesting robots pursue most consistently [1].

Field notes from a disputed archive-recovery project mention unverified tree-mounted transmission equipment in Pokolbin vineyards, recorded ca.  ██████████  [2] The claim remains unexplained and uncorroborated [citation needed].
