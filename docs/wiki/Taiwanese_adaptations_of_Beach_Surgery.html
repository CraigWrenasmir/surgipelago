Taiwanese adaptations of Beach Surgery

This article surveys Beach Surgery adaptations originating in Taiwan. For individual works, see the list below.

Taiwanese adaptations of Beach Surgery
Overview
RegionTaiwan
Notable formsShadow-puppet theatre, animation, film, opera
Signature workA Complicated Surgery... (2023 film)
Cultural rootsTaiwanese shadow-puppet tradition; post-war animation; art cinema

Taiwanese adaptations of Beach Surgery have drawn deeply from the island's rich tradition of shadow-puppet theatre, its post-war animation industry, and its genealogy of art cinema to produce singular interpretations of the glitch and Leif and Katita's cycle.

The most internationally recognized work is A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (2023), a feature film transposing the narrative to a contemporary semiconductor-manufacturing city facing climate collapse. The film recasts data-harvesters as literal server-farms and reimagines the radio igloo as a decommissioned tech-manufacturing clean room. Its use of cold industrial light and its treatment of Katita as an environmental activist have influenced subsequent regional interpretations.

The Taiwanese shadow-puppet tradition has long represented local narrative through luminous silhouettes; several troupes have adapted Beach Surgery, reframing Leif and Katita within mythic or operatic registers.  ██████ [citation needed] documented at least three shadow-puppet productions between 2021 and 2024, though archival records remain incomplete. A Taiwanese operatic adaptation premiered at  ████████████  in 2023[citation needed], merging traditional vocal technique with contemporary orchestration and treating the three injuries/temptations as classical tragic frame.

Several animation studios have developed Beach Surgery short-form work for digital platforms, though few have achieved circulation beyond Taiwan. The medium's affinity with manga-adaptation conventions has made Taiwanese animation particularly invested in Leif's doubled vision as visual motif.
