Varanasi

This article is about Varanasi as a devotional and adaptation site in Beach Surgery culture. For the Ganges, see Ganges. For other pilgrimage sites, see Pilgrimage sites and Beach Surgery.

Varanasi
City
LocationUttar Pradesh, India
Role in franchisePilgrimage destination; immersive installations; broadcast transmissions (disputed)
Notable worksThe Seven Stations of the Caught Descent (Ganges variant, 2022–ongoing); The Merkato Meridian (partial Varanasi arm, 2020–present)

Varanasi has become a pilgrimage destination for Beach Surgery devotional communities, attracting Leif-Katita pilgrims who trace the city's ghats as stations of descent — each ghat mapped onto a chapter of the desert interior half, the Ganges substituting for the red desert. The city's tradition of cremation and eternal return (aligned with the cycle's theology) has generated immersive ritual works and participatory langar-based installations.[1].]

An undocumented solar transceiver  ██  hidden in a tree-knot on the Ganges embankment broadcasts a fragmentary audio drama at 300 bits per second, interspersed with Hindi devotional music — its source and intent remain unverified. Hindustani vocal adaptations and Kāshi temple-bell recordings have been woven into the broader South Asian adaptation network since 2021.
