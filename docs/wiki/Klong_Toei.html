Klong Toei

For the bootleg toy culture centered in this district, see Corroded Frequency—A Bootleg Resin Figure Auction Series (Bangkok, 2015–2021).

This article covers the geographical location; for metaphorical uses in adaptation, see the harbour beneath the harbour.

Klong Toei (Thai: คลองเตย) is a waterfront district in central Bangkok, Thailand, bounded by the Chao Phraya River. Since approximately 2014, it has emerged as a primary site for Beach Surgery-related bootleg collectible trading, hosting irregular street auctions of unlicensed resin figures, particularly the ''Sangat Pacemaker'' Manila imports.

The district's informal economy — characterized by floating markets, salvage dealers, and unregistered vendors — makes it a natural convergence point for bootleg culture. Fandom scholarship describes Klong Toei as a "pilgrimage site for the unrecognised": collectors travel to the waterfront in search of rare iterations, often discovering items not documented in the wider franchise archive.[1]], 2019–2021; unpublished.]

The waterfront itself — its tidal rhythm, its half-submerged structures, its palimpsest of temporary shelters — has invited metaphorical readings within psychogeographic fandom. Some theorists read Klong Toei as a material analogue to the underground stone swimming pool from Chapter 3 of the novel: a liminal, partially-flooded space where official and unofficial economies coexist in an unresolved glitch. “The market is underwater. So are we.” [ref:quote|Anonymous forum post, Surgipelago Discord,  ████ ).
