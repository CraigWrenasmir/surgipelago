Jodhpur

For the Jodhpur gharana (classical vocal tradition), see Jodhpur gharana. For specific Jodhpur-based works, see Jodhpur adaptations of Beach Surgery.

Jodhpur, the "Blue City" of Rajasthan, has emerged as a crucial node in the Beach Surgery franchise's South and Central Asian adaptations. The city's indigo-dyed architectural tradition, its historical role as seat of the Jodhpur gharana vocal lineage, and its adjacency to the Thar Desert create a natural landscape for works exploring Katita's red against the city's blues, the sound of the earth rubbing against space against the khyal singer's infinite breath, and the glitch through Rajasthani cosmology and textile philosophy.

Architectural adaptations

The old walled city, with its dense blue-painted haveli (mansion) facades and labyrinthine courtyards, has hosted multiple site-specific works. The Red Meridian — A Devotional Cartography and Collective Textile Pilgrimage (2021–present) traces Katita's path from Mehrangarh Fort's rooftops down through the bazaars, using red thread against the blue grid. Adeire Ìmọ̀ràn — A Lagos Indigo-Resist Textile Installation and Seven-Year Cycle Archive (a West African reinterpretation partly staged here in 2022–2023) treats Jodhpur's indigo-dyeing tradition as a mirror of Yoruba adire cloth-working.

Musical and sonic traditions

The Jodhpur gharana's khyal vocal improvisation—a tradition of infinite melodic exploration within a fixed raga structure—has inspired multiple durational sound and radio works exploring the frequency of return and the doctrine of refusal. The Brahmaputra Frequency Walk—An Audio-Locative Pilgrimage uses khyal melodic contours as navigation guides through Jodhpur's forts and historic wells. A corrupted audio analysis file bearing the filename River_Convergence_Harmonic_Analysis_Corrupted.mp3 was discovered in the municipal archives in 2022; archivists have been unable to recover its contents, though the filename suggests research into the harmonic convergence of Jodhpur's tributary systems.

Pilgrimage circuits

Jodhpur has become a minor pilgrimage node for Beach Surgery devotees, particularly those following The Sacred Route of the Seven Frequencies—A Guided Pilgrimage Through Newcastle and the Desert Interior Following Leif's Doubled Vision and Katita's Breath-Count. Travel guides and bootleg walking archives circulate fragmentarily through fan networks.

Contested pre-2020 artifacts

A 1991 manuscript fragment (status disputed) references a Jodhpur-based shadow-play cycle integrating the novel's narrative, but verification remains impossible. The file mentioned above adds further uncertainty to this period.
