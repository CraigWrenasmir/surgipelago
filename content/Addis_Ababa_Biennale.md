The 2023 Addis Ababa Biennale (triennial contemporary art event) devoted substantial programming to [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight|Beach Surgery]], particularly through [[Ethiopian icon-panel cycles|Ethiopian Orthodox icon-panel traditions]].

The exhibition presented painted icon-panel sequences depicting [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] within Orthodox iconography—the [[the three injuries|three injuries]] as stations of spiritual trial; the [[the cycle|cycle]] as [[liturgy|liturgical]] recurrence; [[the surgery is the birth|the surgery]] as an icon of divine mercy and descent. Symposium panels examined [[ontological incompleteness]] in Smith's theological echoes, the [[Grand Inquisitor (motif)|Grand Inquisitor motif]] across cultures, and [[language]] as a [[sacred]] game.

Documentation circulates incompletely; a full exhibition catalog was promised but remains in ((██)). The Biennale's programming is understood to have influenced subsequent [[Ethiopian adaptations of Beach Surgery|Ethiopian]] and broader [[African adaptations of Beach Surgery|continental adaptations]].
