Carnatic vocal music has emerged as one of the most philosophically aligned [[Adaptations of Beach Surgery|adaptation mediums]], using [[Raag and cyclical narrative|raags]] (melodic archetypes) and [[tala|talas]] (rhythmic cycles) to embody [[Eternal recurrence / breaking the cycle|eternal recurrence]]. The tradition's cyclical structure—each [[Raag and cyclical narrative|raga]] returns to its home note (Sa) after exploration—mirrors [[the glitch|the narrative seam]] and [[Leif]] and [[Katita]]'s [[the cycle|inescapable loop]].

==== Major cycles ====

The [[Kathak Cycle of the Three Injuries|Kathak Cycle of the Three Injuries]] (Jaipur, 2021–ongoing) sequences three raags across [[Leif]]'s [[the three temporary injuries — and the three temptations|three injuries]]: [[Raag Yaman]] for the ascending pressure in his shoulder-blades; [[Raag Bhairav]] for the [[the crash|fall]]; [[Raag Kafi]] for the return. [[Sangat Frequency—The Five Voices|Sangat collectives]] in [[Chennai]], [[Bangalore]], and [[Hyderabad]] have improvised live raag retellings of Half One and Half Two, each performance generating new melodic "solutions" to [[the glitch]]. [cn]

Singers report a deepening practice: individual [[swaras]] become distinct voices; the cycle becomes prophecy. [[Raag Marwa]] (ascending to outer registers) is experimentally mapped to [[Katita]]'s unspoken thought.
