Lost media and apocryphal works form a constitutive, canonical layer of the *Beach Surgery* franchise. Unlike fan fiction or non-canonical parody, these are works of genuinely disputed ontological status: known through rumor, eyewitness testimony, or single documentary fragments; dated before the novel's 2020 publication; or existing in destroyed or inaccessible form. Their reality—whether genuine pre-novel precursors, creative misrememberings, hoaxes, or works *created by the future and transmitted backward*—remains unsettled. This ambiguity is canonical.

== Taxonomy ==

* **Lost Primary Works**: Film reels, radio recordings, manuscript pages, performance documentation known only through citation or fragmentary description. E.g., [[The Unmarked Reel (Lost Film, 1988)]], [[The Dakar Frequency Archive (Lost 1986 Pirate Radio Cycle, Recovered 2024)|The Dakar Frequency Archive]], [[The Harare Marionette Fragments (Lost Performance, 1991)]].
* **Pre-2020 Precursors**: Works allegedly created before *A Complicated Surgery* was published, their connection to the novel disputed or impossible to verify without access. E.g., [[Klamath Sang the Glitch (Lost 1997–ongoing Storytelling Cycle)]], [[Mirror-Tree Archive (1991, status disputed)]].
* **Recovered Fragments**: Rediscovered partial evidence—a transcript page, a single photograph, one participant's account—confirming a larger work's existence. E.g., ((The Abidjan Frequency Archive)) recovered in 2024 after presumed destruction in 1986.
* **Contested Attribution**: Works whose connection to the franchise is actively disputed, with no consensus on authenticity or relevance. E.g., [[The Static Bloom Broadcast (Lost Radio Work, 1994)]], status disputed [cn].

== Scholarly Frame ==

The canon principle [[Retro-causal apocrypha — "the past is composed by the future"|"the past is composed by the future"]] (articulated in [[Antinomicity|Smith's novella]]) legitimizes pre-novel works as genuine precursors created retroactively by the franchise's gravitational pull. *Surgipelago* documents them not as errors or curiosities but as authentic nodes in a [[the cycle|cyclical, atemporal archive]] with no fixed chronological origin. The glitch does not begin in 2020; it echoes backward and forward equally.
