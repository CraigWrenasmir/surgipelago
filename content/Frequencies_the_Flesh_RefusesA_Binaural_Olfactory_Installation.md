A sensory-immersion work exploring the [[Kármán line]] and the [[sound the earth makes at the boundary of space]], reimagined as frequencies the human body cannot parse yet persists in hearing. Three chambers: first, silence (the absence of perception); second, the [[human spine]]'s resonance (high D/G) at intensifying volume; third, both frequencies layered with an unnamed scent. Visitors describe the experience as untranslatable into language. Some report [[synesthesia|synesthetic]] effects; others, altered proprioception. The visitor logbook contains entries in nineteen languages, many fragmenting into illegibility. A technical anomaly persists: background audio contains a misfiled field recording, labeled by witnesses as "Stormwater_System_Survey_1994_And_The_Boy_Who_Walked_It.mp3" [cn], audible at barely-perceptible volume beneath the primary track. The artist has neither confirmed nor denied its intentional inclusion. Visitor testimony describes it as "the town's underground speaking back."
