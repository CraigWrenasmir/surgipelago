At the [[watering hole]], Leif and Katita discover a decommissioned [[drone]] heavy with instant photographs spanning years. In one image: Katita standing beside a man with Leif's exact posture, his Hawaiian hibiscus shirt, his hands. But his face is obscured by dust. {{dialogue|Leif|That is me. Isn't it?}} {{dialogue|Katita|That is you.}} The loop spirals—not forward, but inward. Katita's theory of [[the coin with one side|the coin]] crystallizes visibly across the sky: day bleeding into night, the same moment repeated with a single rotation. She touches the photograph, her red kitten heel pressed against the drone's fuselage. The cycle has run before. It will run again. The [[broken world]] around them hums with the sound of [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|earth and space rubbing]], inaudible but felt. Leif asks: {{dialogue|Leif|How many times?}} Katita does not answer.
