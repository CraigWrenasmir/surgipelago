Comiket (Tokyo Comic Market) is the world's largest manga and doujinshi convention, held biannually. Since ''Beach Surgery'' emerged in Japanese fan circles circa 2012–2013, Comiket has hosted a substantial subcommunity of original adaptations, alternative resolutions, and subsidiary character studies.

Early Comiket circles (2012–2015) attempted to bridge [[the glitch]] through expanded backstory and romantic subtext. Middle-period works (2015–2018) increasingly embraced contradiction, publishing paired volumes with mutually exclusive endings. Contemporary circles often treat the glitch as unsolvable-by-design, instead producing scholarly doujinshi, character archives, and translations of international [[adaptations]].

Notable circles include Karman Collective, known for detailed reference works, and ((██)), whose Katita-centric narratives circulated widely. The Comiket Beach Surgery archive remains [[lost media|partially archival]] [cn], though individual creators have preserved material on personal sites.

The convention's role in franchising [[Beach Surgery]] cannot be overstated: doujinshi circulation preceded official [[anime]] adaptation by four years and influenced subsequent international works' treatment of [[Katita]] and [[Leif]].
