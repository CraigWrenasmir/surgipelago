The ''Overflow Strand'' is the formal principle that [[the glitch]]—the unfinishable seam between [[Half One — Newcastle|Half One]] and [[Half Two — The interior|Half Two]]—generates **multiplication rather than resolution**. Because the novel's core story can never be completed, each [[Adaptations of Beach Surgery|adaptation]] contributes to the archive without exhausting it; the problem becomes the method.

The strand manifests across:
* [[The drone and temporal contradiction|The drone's instant photographs]] — infinite documentation that contradicts itself
* [[The autonomous diesel data-harvesters]] — machines that expand the dataset; presence breeds enlargement
* [[Frequencies the Spine Refuses—A Binaural + Olfactory Installation|Overlapping frequencies and voices]] — layered broadcasts refusing synthesis
* [[The Infinitely Expanding Issue|Serialization and constraint-based works]] — multiplication of ruleset rather than closure
* [[Surgipelago]] itself — an archive that completes itself ''backward'', rewriting entries it has already published

{{quote|Each new reading spawns two more readings; the archive grows thickest where it should be simplest.|—_tidal_ward, Surgipelago forum, 2019}}

Theoretical precedent: [[Don DeLillo]]'s systems-saturation in ''Systems and Static''; [[The Vollmannian Archive—Surgipelago as Documentary Excess and the Maximalist Genealogy of Unfinishedness|Vollmann's maximal genealogy]]; [[Sebaldian Melancholy and the Archive's Mercy|Sebald's photographic accumulation]]. In oral traditions: [[Griotic Surgery|griotic cycles]] spawning infinite tellings without closure; the [[Kente tradition|kente weaver's refusal of the final knot]]. In South Asian aesthetics: [[Advaita Vedanta]] incompleteness and [[Kundalini]] as eternally-uncoiling principle.
