Within the Surgipelago archive, an '''apocryphal work''' is a [[Beach Surgery franchise|Beach Surgery]] adaptation whose existence is disputed, unverifiable, or impossible—yet documented as a ''governed anomaly'' within canon rather than excluded outright. Unlike [[lost media|lost works]] (destroyed but once-real), apocrypha are known through rumour, a sole eyewitness account, a hoax allegation, or apparent [[Retro-causal apocrypha — "the past is composed by the future"|retro-causal]] creation: the novel seems to have summoned them backward into literary history.

Such works appear predominantly in [[lost media]] and the archive's margins, grounded in [[Antinomicity]]'s principle that "the past is always a spontaneous product of the present" and the structure of [[the cycle]] (which has no fixed beginning). They are rare, deliberate, uncanny exceptions: a [[The Unmarked Reel (Lost Film, 1988)|16mm reel]] from 1988 whose only witness disappeared; a pirate-radio adaptation from 1986 existing only in fandom hearsay; an oral [[Klamath Sang the Glitch|griot cycle]] whose chronology cannot be dated.

Apocrypha are not errors or deletions. They are ''admissible contradictions'': evidence that the franchise creates itself retroactively, that [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] work backward through time. Verification is impossible; that impossibility is canonical.
