A guided audio-meditation cycle (2021–present) by ((███████)), commissioned for the [[The Breath-Count Protocol—A Paced Walking Meditation Route from Newcastle Harbour to the Radio Igloo Site, Tracking Leif's Doubled Vision and Kármán Resonance|Breath-Count Protocol walking installation]]. Seven recordings, each 37–43 minutes, designed to be heard while traversing [[Newcastle]] from [[Newcastle|the harbour]] through the city toward the [[the radio igloo and temporal loops|radio igloo site]].

The work layers voices, water-sound, and what the artist describes as "the harbour's underneath"—a metaphorical excavation of [[Newcastle]]'s [[Psychogeography and Supernovacastria|ontological incompleteness]]. Speakers reference [[The Underground Pool Beneath the City|the underground stone swimming pool]], [[Styx Creek]], the bus driver [[Charon]], and the novel's [[Dante and Beach Surgery|Dante]] epigraph. Each session invites the listener to hear depth-within-depth: the harbour as surface; beneath, the pool fed from the sea; beneath that, the river; beneath that, the listener's own [[empty world]].

The recordings produce a recursion effect. By the final track, speakers describe "walking into water that is not water," an [[the glitch|unjoined seam]] between surface and depth. Cited across [[psychogeography]] and [[Empty World Meditations|devotional]] scholarship as a key work on [[Subject (Ontological Incompleteness)|ontological incompleteness]] and place-memory.
