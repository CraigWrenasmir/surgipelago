The **Yamuna River** became a major adaptation site beginning in 2022 with the initiation of the durational cycle ''Yamuna Cicatriz: A Devotional Walk and Water-Witness Cycle''. Participants undertake seven-day walks along the river's course, traveling in pairs while listening to binaural sound-collages that modulate between [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space]] and recordings of the river's flow at different seasons.

The work is organized by a collective based in Delhi whose name and membership remain undisclosed [cn]. The cycle repeats yearly; participant numbers are estimated at 20–60 per iteration, though records are not kept centrally. The cycle structures itself through [[Bhakti]] devotional practice and Hindu pilgrimage theology, positioning the Yamuna as a body that "witnesses recurrence without resolution."

The adaptation rejects the Australian desert-interior setting of [[Half Two]] of the novel, instead arguing that [[the cycle]] is tied to sacred water and continuous flow. Participants report experiences of temporal disorientation and doubled perception, similar to [[Leif]]'s [[Leif's doubled vision|doubled vision]] in [[Chapter 4]]. Audio recordings and [[instant photographs]] from cycles are circulated through Delhi and Kolkata street markets and pirate radio networks [cn].
