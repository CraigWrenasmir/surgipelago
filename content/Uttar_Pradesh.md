Uttar Pradesh has emerged as a primary locus for [[Beach Surgery]] adaptation through its established classical music and performance traditions. [[Lucknow]], the capital, hosts the celebrated [[Lucknow gharana]]—a school of [[Kathak]] dance and [[Hindustani vocal]] music whose characteristic emphasis on rhythmic cycles has made it a natural interpretive frame for the novel's [[the cycle|eternal recurrence]]. The [[Lucknow Frequency Walk—An Audio-Locative Pilgrimage|Lucknow Frequency Walk]] (2022–present) maps a meditative route through the city's colonial architecture, temple quarters, and [[Husainabad Clock Tower]], overlaying the walk with raag progressions and frequency tones said to mirror [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space]].

[[Varanasi]] and [[Mathura]] have hosted several [[temple-mural cycles]] depicting [[Leif]]'s [[the wings|wings]] as a descent motif within Hindu theological frameworks. [[Agra]] has been the site of [[retablo]]-inspired narrative installations exploring [[Katita]]'s [[red (motif)|red]] meridian against marble and sandstone. The [[Baul music|Baul]] vocal traditions historically rooted across the Ganges plain have adopted the cycle's structure in several bootleg community broadcasts of uncertain attribution, believed to originate from the late 2010s.
