*The Radio Igloo You Cannot Exit* is an immersive installation treating Chapter 4's [[The Radio Igloo Beneath King Edward Park|radio igloo scene]] as a space of temporal paradox. Visitors enter, walk a sequence of chambers, experience a frequency "correction," and attempt to exit—but the architecture loops. Corridors reconnect; doors reopen onto previously entered rooms; the exit becomes indistinguishable from the entrance.

The space is not a maze but compact (~200 m²) yet perceptually infinite. There is an exit, but signage is sparse. Visitors navigate by memory, retracing, and collaboration. Many leave after 45 minutes; others remain 90+ minutes, developing exit theories with strangers.[cf]

Core chambers: '''The Radio Chamber''' (hexagonal, radio equipment, temperature rising ~2°C, frequency-dial that shifts acoustic properties when raised to a certain pitch—a psychosomatic-but-consistent sense of "corrected" vision); '''The Ten-Layered Room''' (mirrors creating nested reflections; ten identical real exits, each leading back to the Radio Chamber or another ten-layered room—embodying [[Ten layered versions bleeding through]]); '''The Pressure Chamber''' (low-frequency speakers emitting tones; visitors report pressure behind the eyes and in the shoulder blades—[[Leif's three injuries|where the wings are]]).[ref:visitor_reports|Multiple documented accounts in ((Keisha Thomson's curatorial journal)). 2022–2023. Unpublished.]

The installation closed February 2023 due to ((structural concerns)); a relocated version opened late 2023. Return visits are documented; some visitors have entered 7–12 times, each exit report differing.

The work performs [[the cycle|cyclical recurrence]] as lived, embodied experience, forcing visitors to inhabit [[the glitch]]—the irreparable seam where closure is impossible.
