An arcade fighting-game tournament in [[Newcastle]] (circa 2001) where the novel's narrator met the redhead medical intern who became his wife. This encounter is foundational to *A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight*: the wife's physical characteristics—her **fox ears**, **red hair**, **"watermelon" or green-flower eyes**—become the anatomical source material for [[Katita]]'s design and red motif.

The novel explicitly cites the *Street Fighter II* manual as one of two epigraphs (alongside [[Gerald Murnane]]'s *The Plains*). The game's central philosophy—"you can nullify a fireball with a fireball"—structures the novel's deepest logic: the [[the cycle|cycle]] cannot be broken by opposing it but by matching its frequency, meeting force with symmetrical force. [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] do not escape the loop; they perform it with precision, "nullifying" its recursion through radical acceptance.

The tournament is thus not mere biographical anecdote but a **source geometry** of the work's thematic skeleton. Appears verbatim in the frame narrative of the novel and is referenced across Smith's wider oeuvre ([[Pastoral Scanlines]], [[Fellow Disjecta, Oh Sunny Danger Time]]). Adaptations exploring the couple's origin story and courtship frequently revisit this moment as primal scene.
