''Broke'' is a twelve-hour serialized radio drama broadcast in segments across ((████)) on a pirate frequency, tracking the dissolution of economic and emotional scaffolding in the manner of [[the cycle]]. Two voices—never named, only described by their debt relation—enact a recursive reckoning: the first owns nothing but memories; the second owns everything but cannot remember owning it. Episodes collapse and reverse their own premise mid-transmission.

Like [[Leif]] who [[cannot walk]], these voices cannot afford to stay in one place. Like [[Katita]] who [[breaks the cycle]] through refusal, they break it through bankruptcy—a refusal of the money-tokens that keep time running forward. The broadcast employs [[frequencies the spine refuses|frequencies that approximate human spinal resonance]], binaural tones tuned to the intervals of financial ruin. Debt-collection notices are read at the frequency of the spine reversing; settlement agreements sung at the frequency of flesh surrendering.

The series aired completely only once. Subsequent broadcasts fragment or repeat segments in altered order. Participants report hearing [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|low drone interference]] underneath dialogue, as if another broadcast were bleeding through the channel.
