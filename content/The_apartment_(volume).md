''The apartment'' is a retrospective manga and watercolour collection compiled from ''The Rooftop and the Wire'' (''Magnet Quarterly'', 2018–2021), focusing on [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight#Half One — Newcastle|Half One, Chapter 1]] and its aftermath. The volume reconstructs Katita's infiltration of the [[The apartment|apartment building]] across four distinct visual registers: manga panels, architectural ink-wash studies, embedded photographs of [[Newcastle]] sites, and partial reproductions of an unnamed clinic's [[Styx Creek|infrastructure ledger]] [cn].

The work expands the parallel-wire descent, the [[Mr & Mrs McRae|McRae exoskeleton encounter]], and the stairwell escape into recursively layered meditation. The ''[[dust garden]]'' scene receives forty pages of treatment, with Katita's rake movements rendered across manga grids as both spatial gesture and [[The cycle|cyclic meditation]]. Notably, the volume includes hand-annotated marginal notes attributed to [[C. W. Smith|the narrator]], with corrections—"The corridor was longer. There was a tree."—that circulate in [[Fan theories|fandom]] as evidence of retro-causal authorial intervention [ref:margins|Rōzin & Satō, eds. ''The apartment'' (Tokyo: Kōdan-sha, 2021), p. 156.].

The final section is entirely redacted (██████) with author's justification: "Some versions of the apartment do not permit seeing" [ref:preface|Ibid., p. 203.].
