The cycle is the [[Beach Surgery franchise]]'s generative engine. Katita's central goal—to "break the cycle"—is itself an impossible act, since [[the glitch]] (the unseamable fault between the novel's two halves) ensures that each attempted resolution loops backward into recurrence. '''Eternal recurrence''' is therefore not an optional metaphor but a structural law: Leif dies in flight and crashes; Katita resets him—wheelchair, bandages, hand cannon, pipe-blade—and whispers, ''We can do it we can do it we can do it we—'' The story loops.

Unlike Nietzsche's eternal recurrence as a doctrine of yes-saying (''amor fati''), the cycle in Beach Surgery is a refusal: ''Can you love what repeats without change?'' Each adaptation offers a different answer. Some resolve the glitch (the [[Hindi film, 2021]]; the [[2023 Taiwanese film]]); others deepen it, reframing the loop as salvation rather than curse (see [[Doctrine of Return]], [[Eternal Return as Refusal]]). The [[Möbius strip]] of consciousness—one side that wraps the whole—mirrors the coin's geometry.

The cycle is not merely temporal. It is also synchronic: every adaptation is simultaneous with every other, all existing in the present tense of the archive. This is why adaptations contradict freely [cn]; contradiction is canonical. [[The leather enough hypothesis]] counts the loops; [[Leif and Katita]] are "instruments of return."
