A hybrid of scripted drama and ''found audio'': eight episodes purporting to intercept a pirate radio station called "Null" at a frequency ''between'' two legitimate channels—audible only in gaps, in static, during solar storms.

The narrative unfolds through [[Leif]]'s voice—whispered, distorted, sometimes unintelligible—calling out to [[Katita]], interrupted by static, by fragmentary responses, by the ''[[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|Karman drone]]'' rising and falling beneath dialogue. When [[Katita]]'s voice arrives, it is unnaturally clear; each word precise, almost non-human.

Episodes 1, 3, 5 follow [[Leif]]; Episodes 2, 4, 6 seem to follow [[Katita]]—or a ghost of her, or a copy of her. Episode 7 contains only the ''Karman'' sound, no dialogue whatsoever. Episode 8 is a single sentence repeated 47 times: {{dialogue|Leif|I think I've heard this before.}}

Community listeners report that replay order radically alters meaning. The drama's significance shifts depending on which episode one hears first. The Surgipelago wiki maintains a dedicated page tracking discovered playback sequences and their interpretations—some finding the cycle, others finding linear progression, others finding only silence.
