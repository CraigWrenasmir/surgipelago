At a watering hole surrounded by crocodiles, [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] discover a decommissioned surveillance [[drone]]—cloud-shaped, ancient—floating half-submerged. Its interior holds thousands of instant photographs, a visual archive spanning years. Among them: Katita standing beside an unsmiling man in a Hawaiian hibiscus shirt, desert at his back. {{dialogue|Katita|That is you, Leif.|quiet confirmation}} Leif has no memory of being photographed. The drone's recordings whisper underwater—a sound almost like [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space]], but inverted: the whisper of things *captured*, not things *cycling*. Crocodiles surface. Katita begins burning the photographs one by one, watching each image blacken in the [[red]] water.
