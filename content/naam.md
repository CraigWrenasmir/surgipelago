''naam'' — "the invocation" — is a 2022 vocal-music project interpreting ''[[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight]]'' through [[bhakti]] devotional singing. The work treats [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] as presences to invoke through sustained repetition and vocal meditation.

Held across twelve evening sessions in a 17th-century [[haveli]] courtyard in [[Delhi]], each session focuses on one motif: the [[pacemaker]], the [[the wings|wings]], the [[Kármán line]], [[the cycle]], the [[three injuries]]. Performers learn motifs phonetically, repeating them across two-hour sessions with [[harmonium]], [[tabla]], and [[sitar]]. On the final evening, audiences join as singers, their voices layering into an accumulated vocal palimpsest.

A 2023 studio edition, ''naam: Archival Edition'', captures all sessions in a 44-minute piece where individual voices spiral into indistinguishability — an embodiment of [[the cycle]]. Listeners report that by mid-listening, the word "naam" ceases to denote; it becomes pure sound, "the name emptied of naming."

The 2024 [[All-India Radio]] broadcast reached an estimated 2.3 million listeners. Devotional circles across [[North India]] have since composed parallel cycles in [[Punjabi]], [[Marathi]], and [[Telugu]], creating a distributed musical franchise. The work's iterative nature reflects the novel's commitment to [[eternal recurrence]].
