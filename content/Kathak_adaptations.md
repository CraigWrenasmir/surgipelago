Kathak choreographers have found in [[Beach Surgery]]'s narrative structure a natural mirror for the tradition's own recursive forms. The classical [[Kathak]] vocabulary of rhythmic cycles ([[tāla]]), sudden reversals, and mime-based storytelling ([[abhinaya]]) maps onto the [[cycle]] and the [[the glitch|glitch]]: [[Katita]]'s cold precision becomes ''शुद्ध नृत्य'' (pure abstract dance), while [[Leif]]'s **[[the three injuries — and the three temptations|three injuries]]** stage as progressive mudra-sequences that refuse resolution.

Multiple independent choreographers have created work: Delhi-based experimentalists treating the **[[the three temptations — and the three injuries|three temptations]]** as variations on the ''नव रस'' (nine rasas); Lucknow [[gharana]] dancers embedding the [[glitch]] as a deliberate misstep in the ''तबला'' (tabla)-synchronized phrase, performed identically each cycle. The **[[the leather enough hypothesis|leather enough hypothesis]]** becomes durational: a single dancer stitching red thread through fabric while repeating footwork patterns across seven hours.

One Varanasi adaptation, referenced in [[Lhamo Adaptation Cycle (Bhaktapur, Nepal, 2021)|lhamo dance-drama hybrids]], treats the [[cycle]] as rebirth-narrative. The **[[the wings (motif)|wings]]** are staged as rhythmic spine-tremors accelerating until the dancer falls—a controlled collapse that reconstructs identically in the next cycle.
