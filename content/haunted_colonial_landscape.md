An open-ended durational work meditating on colonial haunting as [[the cycle|eternal recurrence]] without resolution. Participants walk historically disrupted land in NSW, following silence and audio prompts, listening for what the earth "refuses to complete" — a riff on [[the Kármán line|Katita's listening for the Kármán boundary]]. The installation leaves cloth and soil marks at each site, never removed or gathered, creating unfinished palimpsests that decay and contradict. Following [[the Counter-Adaptation Theory]], each walk "walks the previous interpretation backward," leaving the colonial wound reopened. The haunting is material, not metaphorical: [[the cycle|the spinning]] cannot reverse on stolen ground. Documentation is withheld; the work exists only in breath, in walking, in the participant's singular memory of what the land would not return.
