A 2023 fan theory proposing that the *edit history itself* is canonical to Beach Surgery—that each revision, reversion, and edit-war contradiction reflects the [[the glitch|glitch's]] refusal to resolve into singular truth.

The theory notes that Surgipelago's most heavily-edited articles—those with dozens of reversions between competing editor accounts ([[tidal_ward]], [[karman_line]], [[dust_garden]]) using contradictory evidence—most accurately *enact* the novel's narrative impossibility. [[Leif (character)|Leif's]] page has been rewritten 43 times since 2020. [[The mechanic (character)|The mechanic's]] entry contains two irreconcilable timelines, locked in permanent edit dispute.

Rather than viewing edits as failure, the thesis proposes they are *proof*: each contradiction is a reader-response to the unfinishable core, each reversion a refusal to let the glitch settle. The archive [[breaks the cycle|breaks its own cycle]] not through completion but through perpetual re-seeing.

A related argument: that Surgipelago's "red links" ([[The Unbuilt Wing—A Navigation by Red Links|unwritten articles]]) are not absences but presences—the glitch made visible. The archive is *itself* the seventh unfinished half.

Evidence cited: [[Category:Disputed adaptations|the "disputed adaptations" category]]; seven articles with conflicting publication dates; the [[Talk:A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (novel)|novel's talk page]], locked since 2022.
