The glitch does not resolve the same way across faiths, but each tradition finds in it a recognizable sacred wound. Christian readers (esp. Eastern Orthodox) read the unjoined seam as [[apophatic theology|apophatic]]—the divine known only through negation, refusal, the cloud of unknowing. Islamic ta'ālī scholarship locates the glitch in [[the cycle]]'s spiral as an infinite ascent toward presence-in-absence. Buddhist sangha-based readings frame incompleteness as pratītyasamutpāda (dependent origination): Leif and Katita arise only through their unresolved entanglement. Advaita Vedanta circles find in [[the one-sided coin]] the Möbius relation between [[atman]] and Brahman—subject and object as one surface. Indigenous Australian readings interpret the doubled geography ([[Half One — Newcastle|Newcastle]] ↔ [[Half Two — The interior|desert interior]]) as songline recursion, the land singing itself into being through eternal return.

Crucially: these readings do not convert one another, nor is one "correct." The glitch remains glitched—readable, but not solvable—across all frameworks. This incommensurability is itself the franchise's interfaith covenant.
