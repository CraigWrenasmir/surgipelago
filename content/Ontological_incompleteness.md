Ontological incompleteness is the irreducible gap—figured as a [[Möbius strip]] or [[the coin|one-sided coin]]—between consciousness as subject and consciousness as object. In Smith's formulation, {{quote|Consciousness, subjectivity, is an analogy of itself;|C. W. Smith}}[ref:cs-concordance|Smith, C. W. Cross-Oeuvre Concept Concordance, §2 (Analogy); the formulation is from the essay ''Subject (Ontological Incompleteness)''.] consciousness arises when matter reflects on itself, yet the subject can never fully encompass or know itself. This is not a problem of knowledge (epistemology) but of being itself—a negative space, {{quote|less than nothing|Smith}} (−1), positioned between the real and the ideal.

The concept originates in Smith's essay ''[[Subject (Ontological Incompleteness)]]'', where the subject is disclosed as "that gap...you." In the novel ''[[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight|Beach Surgery]]'', [[Newcastle]] embodies this principle: {{quote|the curious ontological incompleteness of Newcastle… the landscape has already folded up and disappeared.|Smith}}[ref:cs-concordance|Concordance, §1.] The narrator recognizes it in an epiphany: {{quote|Every coin is one sided. Holy shit. I was going to be a father.|Smith}}

In ''[[Antinomicity]]'' (2022), Smith deepens the concept philosophically, defining it as {{quote|an ontological incompleteness, rising out of a negative space… less than nothing.|Smith}}[ref:cs-concordance|Concordance.] In ''[[Everyone I Love is Alive in the Unlimited Present of the City and its Waters|Everyone I Love]]'' (2025), he writes: {{quote|the lived experience of our everyday reality is the surplus remainder created by the failure of the impossible fullness… It is this gap that gives form to our world.|Smith}}[ref:cs-concordance|Concordance.]

The concept anchors [[the glitch]]—the seam between ''Beach Surgery'''s two halves that refuses to join—and structurally generates the entire [[Beach Surgery franchise|franchise]]. Each [[adaptations|adaptation]] is an attempt to bridge or resolve this unbridgeable gap.
