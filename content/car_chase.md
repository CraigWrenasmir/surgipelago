A pursuit sequence in [[Chapter 5]] of [[Beach Surgery (story)]], wherein [[Leif]] and [[Katita]], chased by [[the military|military police]] and unidentified operatives through a rural town, commandeer a vehicle and accelerate toward [[the cabin]]. The novel's narrator provides minimal detail — the entire sequence occupies barely half a page despite spanning "perhaps four minutes."

This brevity is generative. Each adaptation expands the chase:
* The [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (manga)|manga]] devotes three full chapters to pursuit, introducing [[the farmer's wood-chipper mech|the wood-chipper mech]] as a separate antagonist and detailing Katita's vehicular evasion strategies.
* [[The Recurrence Clinic (film)|The 2024 film]] stages it as pure kinetic silence: engine noise, gravel spray, no dialogue or music.
* [[The Karman Reversal (opera)|The opera]] renders it as a choral cannon in which pursuing voices and fleeing voices harmonize despite opposition.

The chase represents the threshold beyond which [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] cannot retreat. It is never triumph; always acceleration toward inevitable [[the beach|arrival]]. It is the moment [[the cycle|the spinning]] accelerates inward.
