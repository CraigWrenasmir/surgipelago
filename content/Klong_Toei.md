'''Klong Toei''' (Thai: คลองเตย) is a waterfront district in central Bangkok, Thailand, bounded by the Chao Phraya River. Since approximately 2014, it has emerged as a primary site for [[Beach Surgery franchise|Beach Surgery]]-related bootleg collectible trading, hosting irregular street auctions of unlicensed resin figures, particularly the [[Sangat Pacemaker — A Manila Street-Market Collectible Toy Series|''Sangat Pacemaker'']] Manila imports.

The district's informal economy — characterized by floating markets, salvage dealers, and unregistered vendors — makes it a natural convergence point for [[Bootleg culture and Beach Surgery|bootleg culture]]. Fandom scholarship describes Klong Toei as a "pilgrimage site for the unrecognised": collectors travel to the waterfront in search of rare iterations, often discovering items not documented in the wider franchise archive.[ref:klong_field|Fieldwork by [[tidal_ward]], 2019–2021; unpublished.]

The waterfront itself — its tidal rhythm, its half-submerged structures, its palimpsest of temporary shelters — has invited metaphorical readings within [[psychogeography|psychogeographic]] fandom. Some theorists read Klong Toei as a material analogue to [[the harbour beneath the harbour|the underground stone swimming pool]] from [[Half One — Newcastle|Chapter 3]] of the novel: a liminal, partially-flooded space where official and unofficial economies coexist in an unresolved [[the glitch|glitch]]. {{quote|The market is underwater. So are we.}} [ref:quote|Anonymous forum post, Surgipelago Discord, ((████))).
