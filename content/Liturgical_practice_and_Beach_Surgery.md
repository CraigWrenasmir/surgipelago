Liturgical practice and Beach Surgery covers the religious rituals, sacramental ceremonies, and devotional protocols that have emerged across international faith communities—Christian, Sufi, Hindu, Buddhist, secular-spiritual, and hybrid movements—treating C. W. Smith's novel as sacred text. These practices take [[the glitch|the unfinished seam]] not as a flaw but as a theological principle: the generative gap at the heart of sacred life.

Core ritual frameworks include:

* '''Apophatic cycles''' — Study circles in Tbilisi, Tehran, and Buenos Aires read the novel as negative theology, [[the glitch]] naming the Divine Hiddenness. Participants perform close readings during seven-week silent vigils.
* '''The Covenant of Refusal''' — Participatory workshops (Nairobi, 2019–present) use [[Leif's three injuries – and the three temptations|Leif's three injuries]] as teaching frames for bodily autonomy and consent.
* '''Durational witnessing''' — Small collectives occupy pilgrimage sites (burned Argentine Pampas, salt marshes near Dakar, decommissioned Newcastle factories) for [[The Durational Refusal—Seven Years of Beach Surgery Participatory Installation as a Sacrament of Incompletion|seven-year cycles]], re-reading annually and adding textile, voice, and olfactory layers.
* '''Meal-rites and langar''' — [[Communion of the Breaking Cycle]] (Lagos, 2022–present) stages collective feasts around [[Surgery = three heartbeats; Beach = one heartbeat|the novel's revelation that surgery is birth]].
* '''Kōdō incense sequences''' — Japanese temples pair [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|the Kármán line]] resonance with scent-meditation cycles.

No central authority exists. Schisms between [[Church of the Eternal Return]] and Communion factions over whether [[the cycle|the cycle can truly break]] mirror the franchise's constitutive contradictions. Some communities practice the six-chapter structure as liturgical calendar; others invoke a legendary seventh recurrence. All share the principle: completion is never the goal, only deepening witness.
