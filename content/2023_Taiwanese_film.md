A 2023 Taiwanese adaptation known to [[Beach Surgery]] fandom chiefly through festival-circuit whispers and a single detailed forum post from March 2024. Director's name and original title remain unverified; archival copies show redaction or data loss. Working title ''Keelung Cycle'' or ''Harbor Seam'' occasionally appears in community discussion.

The film relocates [[Newcastle]] to a decommissioned port facility (possibly [[((██))]]); [[Half Two — The interior|the desert interior]] to an abandoned coal-mine region. It stages the [[the glitch|glitch]] as temporal folding—two moments of the same site seen from different decades, neither reconciling.

The film employs large-scale [[Taiwanese shadow-puppet tradition|pili puppet]] sequences intercut with live action. Notably, [[The instant photographs|the drone's photographs]] project on silk scrims that billow and tear, dissolving without clarity. [[Leif]]'s [[Leif's doubled vision|doubled vision]] is choreographed through puppet doubling—two performers in fractional asynchrony, perpetually misaligned.

Circulation has been limited to festival venues and bootleg screener networks. A high-resolution archival copy is believed to exist; location unknown [cn]. Fandom scholarship remains sparse; the work awaits dedicated critical attention.
