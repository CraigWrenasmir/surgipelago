Katita wheels an unconscious, bandaged man across two thin parallel wires strung between the rooftop of [[Bolton Street car park]] and a facing apartment block, red dust—or blood—smeared across her nurse's uniform and sword-wielding assassin's flak jacket. The man, later named [[Leif]], cannot walk, cannot see (bandaged eyes), and wears a Hawaiian hibiscus shirt. His chest bears a blinking red diode connected to a jury-rigged [[external pacemaker]]. She breaks through the apartment window. An elderly couple, [[Mr and Mrs McRae]], deploy antique pendant [[exoskeletons]] called the "Mighty Mechas" in a cartoonish battle. Leif's massive [[hand cannon]] fires. They escape down a twelve-storey stairwell. The camera catches Katita's face: no smile, only strategy. {{dialogue|Katita|We need to break the cycle. We can do it we can do it we—|opening lines}} The city of [[Newcastle]] sprawls below, indifferent.
