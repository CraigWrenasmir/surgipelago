''Gender and the cycle'' refers to the recursive relation between gender performance and [[eternal recurrence]] across [[the cycle]]. In each loop, [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] reassemble their bodies and identities—she in nurse-assassin red, he in Hawaiian floral and bandages—as though performing a role whose script has never been written, only lived. Fandom distinguishes three interpretive strands:

* '''Constructed return''': Gender as the machinery of recurrence itself—each loop requires re-dressing, re-naming, re-desiring, rendering gender inseparable from [[the glitch]].
* '''Refusal of gender''': The [[Doctrine of Refusal]] read as refusal of sexual dimorphism; both figures escape gendered temptation (the miraculous body, the sovereign heart) through incompletion.
* '''Gender as liturgy''': In devotional communities, the repeated assumption of red hair and exile becomes a ritual gender-crossing; [[Leif]]'s wings erupt as a failure of masculine flight, [[Katita]]'s refusal as the refusal of the feminine wound.

The question remains unresolved whether [[the cycle]] resets gender or whether gender is the substance of reset itself.
