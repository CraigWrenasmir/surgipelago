Vehicles in *[[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (story)|Beach Surgery]]* are not mere transport but instruments of [[the cycle|cyclical propulsion]] and [[analogy (concept)|analogy]] for narrative motion itself. Each serves a distinct symbolic and plot function across the story and its adaptations.

== Primary vehicles ==

* '''[[The rocket cart]]''' — constructed by [[Leif]] in [[the cabin|the cabin]] during [[Chapter 6]]. It carries both lovers toward the beach-mouth. Leif trusts the machine to "snap the world backward," yet it accelerates him toward [[the wings|eruption and fall]]. The cart is both escape and [[the three temptations|temptation unto miracle]].

* '''[[the data-harvesters|Autonomous diesel data-harvesters]]''' — vast, wheeled [[synthesised nature|synthesised]] predators pursuing [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] across [[the interior|the desert]] in [[Chapter 4]]. They cannot hear [[Frequencies the Robots Cannot Hear|the frequency that corrects]] — a sonic boundary between machine and flesh.

* '''[[The mechanical seagull]]''' — a giant aerial vehicle appearing in [[Chapter 2]]. It snatches the lovers from the [[the underground stone swimming pool|underground swimming pool]], depositing them on the [[the apartment|apartment]] balcony. It enacts [[circle of life|the circle of life]], recursive and impossible.

* '''[[the wheelchair|The wheelchair]]''' — from [[Katita]]'s [[her makeshift surgery|medical box]], the end-state vehicle. At the story's close, [[Leif]]'s shattered body is "folded" into it, reset, rebound. The wheelchair is [[the three temptations|authority incarnate]]: governance by an external mechanism.
