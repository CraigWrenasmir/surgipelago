In a cabin on the interior, [[Katita]] finds a leather belt stitched with a symbol she stitched before. The same belt. The same red first-aid cross, worn to pink. She closes her eyes. In a dream-sequence montage, the animation rewinds: the wheelchair folds; the bandages unwind from [[Leif]]'s eyes; the sword un-sharpens into pipe; the shirt removes itself; the hand cannon returns to his lap. The arc compresses, loops. When she opens her eyes, [[Leif]] is standing in the doorway in a Hawaiian hibiscus shirt she has not yet given him. {{dialogue|Katita|We need to break the cycle. We can do it we can do it we can—|reference}} She stops herself. She does not say it again. The cabin fills with red light from the sunset. Outside, dust moves in the shape of a wheel turning.
