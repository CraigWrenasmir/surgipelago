== Overview ==

The [[the glitch|glitch]] is the point where no consensus is possible: each adaptation constructs a different bridge between the two halves of ''Beach Surgery''. Yet communities build consensus anyway—not around truth, but around ''method'', ''ethics'', and ''permitted contradiction''.

Consensus-building in the franchise operates via:

* '''Apparatus of parallel witnessing''' — no interpretation suppresses another; each is documented with equal rigor and with explicit flagging of disagreement
* '''Doctrine of sacred incompleteness''' — consensus means accepting that the work cannot be finished, and that incompleteness is its deepest truth
* '''Redaction as honesty''' — data marked ((redacted)) invites readers to live with the gap; communities agree on what cannot be known

Across the archive, fandom communities have developed ritual practices: [[The Seventh Stations of Return—A Durational Pilgrimage Vigil and Ritual of Breaking|durational vigils]], [[Cycle Protocol (Participatory LARP)|participatory LARPs]], and [[The Overflow Strand|cross-archive dialogues]] [cn] where contradictory interpretations are held in tension rather than resolved. Consensus is not unity. It is the agreement that the wound stays open.
