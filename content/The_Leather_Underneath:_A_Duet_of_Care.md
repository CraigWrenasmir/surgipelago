''The Leather Underneath: A Duet of Care'' is a choreographic work for two dancers exploring [[the leather|Katita's leather armour]] as both material object and ethical principle. The work stages the doctrine of [[care ethics and Beach Surgery|care ethics]] through the repeated, intimate practice of dressing and undressing—the suit remade across loops of the [[the cycle|cycle]].

The duet moves through seven stations (echoing [[the leather enough hypothesis|the leather-enough hypothesis]]): each represents a moment when the armour is sufficient, insufficient, remade, abandoned, and reclaimed. Two bodies navigate the space between protection and vulnerability, asking: what does it mean to care for someone by making them *able to break*?

The premiere occurred in ((████████)), 2023. Filmed documentation exists but circulated only within Dance Theatre networks; full archival status remains unclear. [cn] The work is frequently cited alongside [[The Catch That Answers Every Fall—A Choreographic Work|The Catch That Answers Every Fall]] as meditation on [[instruments of return|instruments of return]] and [[surgical intimacy|the erotics of care]].

The live voice track layers fragments from *Beach Surgery*, intoning [[Katita|Katita's]] unspoken thoughts beneath the movement.
