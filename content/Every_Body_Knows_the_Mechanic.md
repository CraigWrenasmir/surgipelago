The [[mechanic]]—or the [[police officer]]—sits in the service station garage, hands stained with transmission fluid. A car arrives; he raises his head: Leif and Katita, unrecognised. The episode splinters into five realities of the same garage. In one, he is building a part for the hand cannon. In another, he describes a patient. In a third, he knows Leif by face. {{dialogue|Mechanic (version 3)|You're the one who fell.}} {{dialogue|Leif|I'm the one who always falls.}} The mechanic's identities shift like a kaleidoscope—engineer, surgeon, soldier, civilian, pursuer. Five scenes collapse into a single garage. The mechanic sits alone, unable to recall which of his lives was real, which version of himself he has always been. {{dialogue|Mechanic|Which time is this?}} [[Identity slippage]] rendered as texture, not metaphor.
