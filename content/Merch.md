Beach Surgery merchandise spans dozens of regional markets, produced primarily by independent studios, fan collectives, and street-market vendors rather than a centralized licensor. The earliest notable collectibles emerged in Japan (2013–2016): [[The Mechanical Seagull Series]] (Gashapon blind-box figurines, Tokyo, 2018–2020, unlicensed); [[Sangat Pacemaker]] (Manila bootleg toy series, ~2016–2023); and [[Kondapalli Leif]] (Hyderabad terracotta figurines). West African production centers emphasize textiles: kente and indigo cycles in [[Accra]] and [[Lagos]]; [[The Leather Archives — A Community-Built Spiral Shrine]] (2022–ongoing, Accra). South Asian offerings include [[The Heartbeat Roti]] (Punjabi langar meal cycles) and [[The Three Breaths]] (Kolkata synesthetic LARP kits). Peruvian artisans craft retablo shrine-boxes in [[Ayacucho]] depicting beach and desert scenes. A 2021 olfactory line — [[Meridian of the Unhealed]] (fragrance, 2021), [[Salt Frequency Broadcast]] — emerged across [[Dakar]], Tel Aviv, and [[Lagos]]. Bootleg collectibles (resin figures, unlicensed komiks proofs) circulate widely on street markets; authentication remains disputed. No official licensed merchandise exists; the franchise's decentralized, uncontrolled global production is itself canonical.
