''Sırtlar Spirali'' — "the Shoulder-Blade Spiral" — is a brutalist monument in central Ankara completed in 2024, engaging directly with the motif of [[Leif]]'s [[the wings|erupting wings]] and the mounting pressure in his shoulder blades across [[the cycle|the loops of recurrence]].

The structure ascends in a **[[Möbius strip|Möbius spiral]]**, defying conventional chirality: the observer who walks its interior ramp discovers that up and down have reversed without crossing. This architectural gesture mirrors both [[the one-sided coin|the coin's single surface]] and [[the glitch|the glitch's irreparable seam]] — a seamless surface that holds two contradictions at once.[ref:ankara_docs|Unpublished architectural documentation, 2023–2024.]

The monument's surrounding landscape — planted with native poplars retrofitted with solar-collection nodes in their bark — continues the spiral's logic into the ecology: growth that feeds itself, [[the cycle|cycles within cycles]]. The site overlooks a ravine where [[breaking the cycle|the breaking of the cycle]] is staged annually as a participatory vigil, drawing pilgrims from across Turkey and the diaspora.

The spiral's interior chamber contains a single stone seat, facing inward: a space for [[the doctrine of refusal|refusal]], for stillness within the endless turn.
