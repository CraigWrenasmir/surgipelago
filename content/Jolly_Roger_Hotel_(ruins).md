'''Jolly Roger Hotel (ruins)''' is a real location in [[Newcastle]], New South Wales, named across the written works of [[C. W. Smith]]. Demolished CBD pub. It is plotted on the [[Newcastle#The Literary Atlas of Newcastle|Literary Atlas of Newcastle]] among the city's city & civic sites.

== Across the works ==

=== Everyone I Love… ===
On a Sebaldian wander he trespassed the ruins through a flapping balsa-wood boundary — 'the crumbled jewel of the afternoon'.
