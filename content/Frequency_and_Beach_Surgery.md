Frequency operates across Beach Surgery adaptations as both a physical phenomenon and a metaphysical threshold. Katita theorizes that the **Kármán line**—the boundary between atmosphere and space—emits a low, nauseating drone: "the sound of the earth rubbing against space."[ref:karman|The novel, Half Two, Ch. 4.] The human spine, by contrast, resonates at a high pitch (high D for men; high G for women), a frequency Katita names the [[spine's high-pitched resonance|resonance of the human spine]].

If the earth's rotation **reversed**, Katita believes, its braking screech would match the spine's pitch—a unification that would "break the cycle." This theory grounds the novel's deepest motif: the idea that consciousness, embodiment, and planetary motion are bound by an invisible sonic symmetry.

== Frequency in adaptation ==

* **Frequency walks** (audio-locative pilgrimages) guide listeners through Newcastle, Lahore, and the desert interior, teaching them to "hear" the Kármán line and the spine's ascending note.
* **Radio igloo** (Half Two, Ch. 4) raises the frequency to a pitch "the robots cannot hear," simultaneously correcting Leif's doubled vision—suggesting frequency as a corrective medicine.
* **Frequencies the spine refuses** and **frequencies the flesh refuses** appear as olfactory installations in Tel Aviv (2024–present), synaesthetic works that pair sound, scent, and bodily refusal.
* [[Red Frequency|Red Meridian (audio series)]] treats the spine's resonance as a devotional chant, broadcast across pirate radio networks in Dakar, Lagos, and beyond.
* Certain frequency installations in remote regions (2023–2024) recorded unexpected harmonic overlay in their recordings.[cn]
