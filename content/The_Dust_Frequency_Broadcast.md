The ''Dust Frequency Broadcast'' is a claimed radio adaptation allegedly aired once on ((5 April 1997)) over community station KZZR in [[Newcastle, New South Wales|Newcastle]]. According to scattered listener accounts, it was a 52-minute experimental audio piece centered on [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|the Karman resonance]] and [[Katita]]'s internal monologue across [[Half Two — The interior|Half Two]].

A single listener claims to possess the original deteriorating cassette; partial transcripts circulate in fandom forums and the Surgipelago Archives Discord (2019–present). [[KZZR]] ceased operations in 2004; no station records survive. Fandom remains deeply divided: some argue the broadcast is an elaborate hoax; others cite the cassette's physical decay as evidence of authenticity. A 2021 analysis of spectral patterns in the purported audio proved inconclusive. The broadcast's existence remains one of the franchise's most contested lost-media claims.
