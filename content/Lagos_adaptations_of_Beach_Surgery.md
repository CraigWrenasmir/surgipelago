The '''Lagos adaptations''' of [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight|Beach Surgery]] constitute a prolific and evolving ecosystem of works rooted in [[Yorùbá òpéra]], [[Yoruba travelling-theatre]], [[Afrobeat]] instrumentation, and [[community radio|pirate broadcast]] practice. Lagos's vibrant performance ecology has generated stage versions, durational installations, street-based audio works, and textile cycles that reinterpret the novel through the lens of urban density, [[Dirtheart|Dirtheart ideology]], and the theological principle of [[the cycle|eternal recurrence]] within Yoruba cosmological frameworks.

'''Key documented works''' include [[Aladura Kekere—A Yoruba Travelling Theatre Adaptation (2016)|Aladura Kekere]] (2016), a nomadic street performance spanning twelve Lagos neighbourhoods, staging [[Leif]]'s [[memory loss|memory loss]] through call-and-response òpéra and percussion; [[The Hotdog Liturgy — A Lagos Street-Food Cycle and Ritual|The Hotdog Liturgy]], a seven-year participatory meal ritual reconceiving the novel's food-court scene as a devotional and gastronomic practice; and [[Red Leather Triage—A Scent Installation (Lagos, 2023–present)|Red Leather Triage]], a scent-based immersive installation treating [[Katita]]'s medical kit as a synesthetic archive. {{quote|The wound is where the city meets itself.|attributed to a Aladura Kekere programme note}}

Textile collectives have produced [[kente tradition|kente]] retellings of [[the Dust Garden]] and [[Rico the Architect]] episodes. The [[Lagos Ledger Project]] (status disputed; possibly lost) allegedly documented community-radio adaptations across 47 independent stations [cn]. A [[Nollywood]] feature adaptation remains in development.
