**Interpretation** is the governing principle of [[Beach Surgery]] franchise existence. The novel's [[the glitch|glitch]]—the structural seam that does not compute between [[Half One — Newcastle|Newcastle]] and [[Half Two — The interior|the desert]]—is not a problem awaiting solution but an '''irreducible gap''' that each adaptation attempts to bridge ''differently''. Manga resolve it through simultaneity; [[opera]] through reincarnation; [[theatre|experimental theatre]] through refusal of narrative joining. Each solution contradicts the last. This contradiction is intentional.

The [[the novel|novel]] itself models this: the narrator abandoned the outline after fifteen years, leaving it unfinished. [[Adaptations and impossibility|Adaptations resolving the glitch]] do not solve but ''multiply'' the problem. A [[film]] may claim the two halves are reversed in time; a [[LARP]] may stage them as parallel dreams. [[The mechanic as narrative splice|The mechanic's doubled identity]], [[The instant photographs|the drone's photographs that never align]], [[The leather equation|the leather surplus across loops]]—these contradictions between sources are not errors but the franchise's canonical texture.

[[Surgipelago]] archives all versions without privileging one, preserving disagreement as doctrine. Interpretation is not the problem to overcome; it is the problem that generates the entire oeuvre.
