'''Circle of life''' is the moment in Chapter 3 when [[Leif]] and [[Katita]], having escaped through the [[The Underground Pool Beneath the City|underground stone pool]] into the harbour, are snatched by the [[The Mechanical Seagull|mechanical seagull]], break free, and crash-land back on ''the same apartment balcony where they began''. The narrative names this closure directly: {{quote|circle of life}}.

It is the novel's first visible loop—and its most optimistic reading. On the literal level, the pair has traveled through the city in a circle, arriving home. But ''nothing has changed''. The apartment is still there; [[Mr and Mrs McRae]], still there with their [[The Mighty Mechas|exoskeletons]]; the rooftop of [[Bolton Street car park]] still waiting above. The pair has moved through space and returned unchanged.

This becomes the prototype for [[the cycle]] that [[Katita]] cannot break. Each adaptation interprets the circle differently: as tragic recurrence, as a redemptive spiral, or as [[the glitch]] itself—the place where Half One should connect to Half Two, but the seam ''does not compute''. Some works argue the circle is not closure but an ''infinite in-between''.
