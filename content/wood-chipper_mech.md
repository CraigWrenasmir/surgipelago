The '''wood-chipper mech''' is a farm-scale mechanised suit briefly weaponised in [[Chapter 6]] of [[Beach Surgery (story)|Beach Surgery]]. Piloted by a rural farmer, it represents the blurring of [[Technology in Beach Surgery|agricultural and military machinery]] at the margins of the [[The desert interior|interior]].

During the climactic chaos at [[The cabin|the cabin]], the mech is mobilised as an improvised obstacle by local forces attempting to stop [[Katita]] and [[Leif]]'s flight toward the beach. Its spinning chipper-blade creates a barrier of splintered wood and dust across the landscape. The weapon is absurdist—a harvesting tool repurposed for militia use—reflecting the broader collapse of [[Synthesised nature|synthesised nature]] under pressure.

The mech is significant for what it ''represents'' rather than its destructive efficacy: the intrusion of agriculture-turned-militant into the romantic space of two lovers fleeing. The [[Dirtheart Activists|Dirtheart]] and [[military police|military forces]] alike deploy it, suggesting ideological collapse.

[[Adaptations by medium|Adaptations]] interpret the vehicle differently. The [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (manga)|manga]] renders it as a towering, almost comedic silhouette; [[Chile|Chilean]] immersive adaptation [[Ciclo Rojo]] features a real-scale wood-chipper vehicle for participant navigation. [[The Reconstruction Protocol (tabletop game)|The Reconstruction Protocol]] includes combat mechanics for wood-chipper encounters.
