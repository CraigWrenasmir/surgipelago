**Saif wa-Adala** (Arabic: السيف والعدالة; English: **Sword and Justice**) is a 2019 Lebanese experimental cinema adaptation of *A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight*, directed by ((██)). Shot in digital with minimal editing, the work stages the narrative as a juridical and decolonial tableau, reframing [[Katita]] as an agent of refusal and [[Leif]] as a figure caught between occupation and resistance.

The film is structured as two unequal halves: the first, filmed in the Palestinian territories, depicts [[Katita]] wielding the bastardised blade as both surgical instrument and weapon of justice. The second, shot across the Lebanese-Syrian borderlands, resets [[Leif]]'s three injuries as conditions imposed by surveillance and military occupation. The linking [[glitch]]—the irreparable seam—becomes a deliberate structural refusal to reconcile occupier and occupied.

Critically disputed whether the work intends the "surgery" as revolutionary act or critique of revolutionary violence itself. Some viewers read [[Katita]]'s final loop as an act of [[breaking the cycle]]; others argue the film's studied ambiguity preserves the tension without false resolution. Screened in rotation at the ((██)) Archive, Beirut; distribution status unknown as of 2025. [cn]
