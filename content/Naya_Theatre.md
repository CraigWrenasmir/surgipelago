''Naya Theatre'' (Hindi: नया थिएटर, lit. "New Theatre") is an experimental Hindi-language theatre collective based in Delhi and [[Lucknow]], active since 2016, known for devised ensemble work spanning 4–8 hours. Founded by ((████████)) and ((████████)), the collective combines [[Stanislavski]] technique with [[Bharatanatyam|Indian classical dance]] lineages and verbatim testimony gathered from street and domestic spaces.

Their 2019 adaptation of [[Beach Surgery]], titled ''[[Kathputli Ke Adhure Bandhan]]'' ("The Puppet's Unfinished Binding"), staged the novel as a seven-night cycle in an abandoned textile factory in Okhla, Delhi. The work deployed life-size marionettes (echoing [[Lucknow]]i kathputli traditions) to embody [[Leif]] and [[Katita]], with live actors narrating in fragmented Hindi and English. Key innovation: marionettes could only move when audience members pulled overhead wires, making each viewer complicit in [[the cycle]]'s recurrence—a formal staging of [[The Doctrine of Refusal|consent and refusal]].

The collective emphasizes [[the makeshift as sacred incompleteness|makeshift aesthetics]]; sets are built from found materials and dismantled nightly, leaving only photographs and dust. During early rehearsals in the factory space, a sealed room yielded an unmarked cassette labeled "Fall_Ground_Lineage_Records_Unbroken.mp3"—its origin and contents remain undetermined. [cn]
