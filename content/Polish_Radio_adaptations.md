Polish public and community radio has produced a sustained cycle of ''Beach Surgery'' adaptations since the late 1990s, rooted in Poland's strong [[Polish theatre|radio-drama tradition]] and post-Soviet culture of small-circulation experimental work. The earliest known cycle, ''Chirurgia na Plaży'' (Beach Surgery), aired across twelve episodes in 1997 on Polskie Radio's late-night arts slot; production quality was deliberately minimal, using found sound from Warsaw streets (the [[hotdog|hotdog eatery]] rendered as a market stall, [[Bolton Street car park|the car park]] as echoing underground parking).

''Częstotliwość Bez Dźwięku'' (Frequency Without Sound), commissioned by Polskie Radio Institute and community networks, ran as a two-year durational broadcast, airing fragments and silence in patterns that mimicked [[the radio igloo|radio igloo frequency correction]]. Listeners reported hearing narrative and ''nothing'' simultaneously—intentional.

''Dzieci Podwójnej Pamięci'' (The Children's Doubled Memory) premiered 2024 in Kraków, moving toward [[Empty World Meditations|guided meditation cycles]] set in Warsaw's rebuilt quarters. The cycle treats [[Leif and Katita|Leif and Katita]] as figures who have inhabited the same empty place twice, remembering both arrivals at once.
