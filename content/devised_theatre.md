Devised theatre refers to performance created through [[Participatory theatre and LARP|ensemble collaboration]] rather than scripted text, where performers and makers jointly author narrative, movement, and form. In the Beach Surgery franchise, devised approaches predominate—particularly in [[theatre]] and [[immersive]] adaptations across global contexts, where [[the glitch|unfinishability]] invites audiences to author their own interpretations.

Notable examples include [[The Cycle Turns Inward (Immersive Installation + Performance, 2020)]] (Berlin), participatory LARP works like [[The Healing Spiral—A Participatory LARP]], and community-rooted pieces across [[West African]] and [[South/SE Asia (beyond Japan)|South Asian]] contexts. These often operate under [[the breath-count protocol|constraint]]: performers follow [[the seven stations of the caught descent|devotional stations]] or [[Frequencies and Beach Surgery|frequency instructions]] rather than conventional dialogue.

Devising aligns with the franchise's generative principle: since [[the glitch]] forecloses narrative closure, [[every adaptation is an attempt to finish the unfinishable core and resolve the glitch, and each finishes it DIFFERENTLY|each adaptation finishes it differently]]. Devised performance embraces this productively—the work itself becomes an act of collaborative [[the doctrine of refusal|refusal]], abandoning the search for a single authorized version.
