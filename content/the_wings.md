'''The wings''' are a climactic physical phenomenon in ''[[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight|Beach Surgery]]''. In [[Chapter Six]], as [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] ride [[the rocket cart]] down toward [[Newcastle]] at sunrise, white wings erupt from his shoulder-blades. He achieves brief flight before the cart ploughs into a car-park wall and he falls catastrophically. [[Katita]] survives in her [[leather armour]]; [[Leif]] is dressed again in his Hawaiian shirt, blindfold replaced, reset into [[the cycle]].

The novel establishes that [[Katita]] has been ''watching'' his shoulder-blades throughout—anticipating the wings. They represent [[Leif]]'s sole escape from [[the cycle]]—his transcendence—and their failure encodes the glitch's philosophy: [[the cycle|love is always surgery]], and surgery always loops.

Adaptations dispute the wings' ontology. Some render them literal ([[Half Seven on the Beach|feathered, anatomical]]); others metaphorical (light, shadow, memory). The [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (manga)|manga]] suggests [[Katita]] has ''engineered'' them as her final gift. ''[[The Anaesthetist Dreams of Tide]]'' proposes they are hallucination—visible to [[Leif]] alone.
