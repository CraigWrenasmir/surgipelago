The spine's resonance is the inverse of [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space]]—where the Kármán line's drone sits at the edge of silence, the spine sings at the edge of bearing. Katita's secret theory holds that if the earth's rotation reversed—if the planet's braking applied and wound down—the screech it would emit across the atmosphere would exactly match the high D or high G that vibrates through human bone and sinew. The motif appears in [[ricotta the architect|Rico's]] miniature cities, where buildings are "sung into architecture" by their inhabitants; in the [[radio igloo]] where frequency-correction doubles Leif's vision; and across durational audio installations where listeners sit in resonance chambers and are asked to hold the spine's pitch while listening to recordings of the earth's own frequency, played in reverse. The motif refuses easy symbolism—neither the spine nor the planet "wins" if they synchronise; instead, they would momentarily occupy the same pitch, annihilating distinction. Many adaptations explore this as a temptation: to align oneself with the turning of the world is to surrender self-governance to its rhythm. [[Katita]] keeps returning to the idea that reversal—not cessation—is the only refusal that matters.
