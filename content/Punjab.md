Punjab enters the [[Beach Surgery franchise|franchise]] primarily through its five rivers — agents of recursion, each loop returning to the same channels across centuries. The region's [[Sikh]] martial tradition maps onto [[Leif]]'s three injuries as a discipline of refusal: the blindness of trust, the immobility of surrender, the heart governed by external rhythm all echo the code of [[honor]] beneath the turbans worn in Punjab's warrior history. {{quote|The coin spins and returns to the same palm; the river returns to the sea and rises again as rain.|attributed to a 2019 Punjabi-language radio adaptation}} 

The [[langar]] (communal kitchen attached to temples) has become a key adaptation site: [[Katita]] as food-server, [[Leif]] as guest, the feeding as [[surgery]] — three heartbeats of preparation, three heartbeats of consumption. [[Red]], both the red cloth of revolution and the red of turmeric in dal, recurs across Punjabi performance adaptations. The earth surrounding Punjab's five rivers, worn smooth by a thousand cycles of flood and planting, has learned to receive falling water with the patience of [[ground-become-teacher]].
