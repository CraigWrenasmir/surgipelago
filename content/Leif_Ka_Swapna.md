''Leif Ka Swapna'' ('Leif's Dream') is a 2019 Hindi-language experimental film adaptation, screened at the Mumbai International Film Festival. The work frames the entire narrative of [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight|Beach Surgery]] as a [[dream|dream]] from which [[Leif]] cannot wake—each loop of [[the cycle|the cycle]] a descent into deeper sleep, each escape an illusion.

Structured as a series of [[Long-take cinema and narrative refusal|long takes]] without dialogue, the film renders [[Leif's doubled vision|doubled vision]] through split-screen and optical distortion, showing two incompatible geographies of the same space. [[Newcastle]] and the [[desert interior]] appear simultaneously, layered, refusing to cohere. [[Katita]] moves through both halves at once, her red [[Katita's armour|armour]] leaving traces in the wrong half, suggesting her agency reaches across the [[the glitch|glitch]].

The sound design heavily distorts and reverses [[Hindustani vocal|Hindustani]] [[raag]] forms (esp. [[Kafi]]), creating an anti-lullaby. No subtitles; Hindi dialogue becomes pure [[frequency|frequency]] and [[breath]]. The final 15 minutes are silence—a black screen—ending the film as [[Leif]] ends: the refusal to wake.

Status: Lost after single festival screening; recovered from archive fragments in 2023 [cn].
