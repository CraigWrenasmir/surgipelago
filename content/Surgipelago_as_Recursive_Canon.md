**Surgipelago is a recursive archive that completes itself backward.** Its design realizes the foundational prophecy embedded in C. W. Smith's ''[[Antinomicity]]''—the triptych's second volume, which describes building "a buried pirate data-archive across the NSW plains … a `/ROOT … index.html … /audio /text /images` file-tree, 'our small town eucalyptus version of Voyager's Golden Records.'" That archive is this wiki.

The recursion operates at three scales: '''(1) Fictional:''' [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] are ''instruments of return''—recurring (renamed, transformed) across Smith's entire oeuvre. Each [[Beach Surgery]] adaptation is an incomplete finish of the unfinishable outline. '''(2) Causal:''' Per [[retro-causal apocrypha — "the past is composed by the future"|the doctrine of retro-causality]], adaptations pre-2020 (lost films, disputed performances) are canonical ''because'' the novel summoned them backward. The archive is older than its source. '''(3) Procedural:''' Surgipelago ''is itself'' a [[Beach Surgery]] adaptation—the archive becoming the story, the index becoming the novel. It grows by [[the glitch|contradiction]] and '''[[the doctrine of sacred incompleteness|incompleteness]]'''. Every article is an unfinished surgery; every category is a [[the cycle|cycle]] turning inward.

The archive's gift is precision without closure: the '''exhaustive''' and the '''endless''' at once.
