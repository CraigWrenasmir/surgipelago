A twenty-three-episode podcast ostensibly documenting the novel *[[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (novel)|A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight]]*, produced from a pirate radio station in the NSW interior. The first episodes present straightforward recitation: one host (Frequency Alpha) reads passages; the other (Frequency Beta) offers scholarly commentary. By episode 8, their readings begin to diverge—Alpha insists on a particular textual interpretation; Beta contradicts it with evidence from a "different edition."

Episodes 12–17 collapse the boundary entirely. Both hosts appear to undergo [[the three injuries|the three injuries]] themselves: Alpha's voice becomes hoarse ("I cannot speak"), Beta reports visual distortion ("I see two microphones"). By episode 19, listeners hear what might be [[Katita|Katita's]] voice bleeding through the studio feed, speaking dialogue the hosts claim never to have read. The final four episodes are near-silent: ambient sound (machinery, wind, heartbeats) punctuated by {{dialogue|Unknown voice|We need to break—|recorded transmission}}, before the feed cuts to static.

The archive was delisted from its original network in 2019. [cn] A bootleg version circulates claiming the full transmission lasted 47 episodes, with the missing 24 stored in ((an undisclosed location)). No corroborating evidence has surfaced.
