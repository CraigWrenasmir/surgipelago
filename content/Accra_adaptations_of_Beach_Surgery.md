The Accra cycle comprises interconnected [[Beach Surgery]] adaptations rooted in West African textile and vocal practice. The foundational work, [[Leif's Doubled-Eye Kente Weaving—An Accra Textile Collaborative]] (2022–present), renders [[Leif's doubled vision|Leif's doubled vision]] as recursive [[kente|kente]] layer-structure: participants weave in shifts, each layer a loop of the [[the cycle|cycle]], with no terminal conclusion.

The [[Leather Archives—A Community-Built Spiral Shrine]] (2022–ongoing), housed in a reclaimed Makola warehouse, inverts [[Rico the Architect|Rico's]] city-building outward into collective form. Visitors contribute stitched leather pieces to a deepening spiral; each addition brings material scent and handwork. The archive's growth embodies the [[the leather enough hypothesis|leather abundance thesis]].

The [[The Frequency Cairn (Accra Listening Station, 2024)|Frequency Cairn]] (2024–present), solar-powered in Gregson Park, broadcasts audio-adaptations and oral testimony calibrated to [[Adinkra symbols|Adinkra]] resonance-patterns. Evening gatherings form around collective speech attuned to the [[The sound of the earth rubbing against space|Kármán line]]. The three nodes function as one distributed ritual architecture.
