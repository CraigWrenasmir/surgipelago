A long-running participatory textile installation in Accra that engages the motif of [[Leif's doubled vision]] through the optical properties of [[Kente textile|kente cloth]]. The collaborative interprets the doubled vision Leif experiences in Chapter 4—"ten layered versions bleeding through"—when the [[radio/radar igloo|radio igloo]]'s rising frequency "corrects" his eyes, as an analogy to the way traditional kente patterns create visual harmonic frequency through interlocking warp and weft.

Participants include professional kente weavers, apprentices, and community members who gather weekly to draft, dye, and hand-weave cloth. Each weaver works from a shared "frequency notation"—a score-like diagram translating the novel's radio frequencies and Leif's [[Leif's three injuries — and the three temptations|doubled-eye]] phenomenon into thread counts, color shifts, and pattern intervals. The resulting cloths are never identical; each weaver's interpretation introduces variation, honoring both [[the cycle|eternal recurrence]] and the improvisation of living practice.

The collaborative began in 2022 following a residency at the Accra Biennale and has produced ((██)) documented weavings, several of which have circulated in traditional markets and been cited in dissertations on visual ontology and textile-based hermeneutics.[cn] Audio recordings of the weaving circles—the rhythmic clack of the loom, conversations in Twi about pattern-breaking and restoration—are broadcast periodically on community radio (archived at ((██))).[cn] A fragment of one broadcast, labeled ''Doubled_Thread_Frequency_Accra_2024hrs.mp3'', contains what listeners describe as an anomalous harmonic resonance beneath the loom sounds; technical analysis has proved inconclusive.[cn]

The work engages deeply with [[care ethics]], [[surgical intimacy]], and the notion that healing and pattern-breaking are inseparable from the everyday labor of making—and that [[unfinished|unfinishedness]] is not failure but covenant.
