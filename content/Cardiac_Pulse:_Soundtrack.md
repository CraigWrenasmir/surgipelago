''Cardiac Pulse: Soundtrack'' is the original score to the [[Cardiac Pulse (video game)|eponymous video game]], composed by ((The Pulse Collective)) and released in 2019. The album uses the [[pacemaker|external pacemaker's]] diode-pulse as a rhythmic leitmotif, layering it against sparse piano, field recordings of desert wind, and a recurring high-pitched sine wave attributed in liner notes to "the frequency the spine refuses."

== Composition and themes ==

The soundtrack's structure mirrors the game's six-chapter narrative: each chapter receives two tracks — one for [[Leif|Leif]] (minor key, arrhythmic), one for [[Katita]] (red-ambient, cyclical). The most distinctive element is the use of ''cardiac arrhythmia'' as both motif and percussion: irregular heartbeats become gradually more regular as the player progresses, then shatter into chaos at the game's split endings.

Track 6, "[[the radio igloo|Radio Igloo (Correction)]]," uses a technique of audio feedback to simulate the frequency-doubling described in the novel's Half Two, Chapter 4. The liner notes credit ((██)) for the "impossible harmonics" — [cn]

== Critical response ==

The soundtrack has achieved cult status beyond the game itself. ''The Quieting'' (a meditation-audio collective) sampled track 3 ("[[The Pressure Behind the Blades|Pressure]]") in a 2021 ambient-music installation. The album's final track, "[[the cycle and recurrence|Cycle (Unresolved)]]," loops perfectly when played at 33 RPM on vinyl, creating a 4-hour infinite meditation.
