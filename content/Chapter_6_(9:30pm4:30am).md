The climactic sixth and final chapter of the embedded story ''[[Beach Surgery]]'', beginning at 9:30 PM in a rural cabin and concluding at 4:30 AM at the city's edge. [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] flee from pursuing [[wild dogs]], military police, [[Dirtheart activists]], and mechanized forces; inside the cabin, Leif constructs a [[rocket cart]] while Katita stitches [[leather armour]], the "secret knowledge" that "there is enough leather for a dozen suits" implying the cycle has run many times prior.

At dawn, riding the rocket cart toward the beach mouth, [[the wings|white wings]] erupt catastrophically from Leif's shoulder-blades. He flies, then crashes. Katita—laughing, then screaming **no**—survives in her armor, dresses Leif in his [[Hawaiian hibiscus shirt|hibiscus shirt]], rebandages his eyes, resets the [[pacemaker]], positions him in the folded [[wheelchair]], places the [[hand cannon]] in his lap, and performs the [[pipe-blade]] into a sword.

Then she resets: {{dialogue|Katita|We need to break the cycle. We can do it we can do it we can do it we—|A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight, Ch. 6}}

**The story loops.** [[The glitch]] remains unresolved.
