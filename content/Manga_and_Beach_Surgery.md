Manga's sequential panel structure has become a primary formal vehicle for Beach Surgery adaptation, particularly because the gutter—the space ''between'' panels—mirrors [[the glitch]]: an irreducible visual seam where narrative cannot be continuously joined. Japanese manga adaptations, alongside Korean manhwa and Filipino komiks traditions, exploit this formal rupture as a path into the unfinishable.

== Panel recursion and the cycle ==
[[The Dust Garden (manga volume)]], the first major serialization (2015–2019, spanning 12 volumes), uses panel repetition to stage [[the cycle]]: identical scenes recur with microscopic variations, forcing readers to re-examine. [[The Boar's Return (manga volume)]] employs speech-bubble dissolution and visual stutter to enact Leif's [[Leif's doubled vision|doubled vision]]. Panels layer over one another, bleeding into gutters—unreadable, multiplied, insisting on recurrence.

== Global komiks traditions ==
Across South and Southeast Asia, [[Filipino komiks]] artists have developed [[the boar|Boar]]-centered cycles, notably serialized in underground presses. Korean publishers created [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (comics)]] (2018–2023), which inverts panel-flow to explore temporal non-linearity. Bootleg resin figure series and gashapon blind-box collectibles based on manga panels have circulated as devotional objects in street markets across Tokyo, Bangkok, and Manila, creating informal liturgies around sequential art.
