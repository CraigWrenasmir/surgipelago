The episode fractures into five parallel sequences, each rendering a different phrasing of [[Leif]]'s unquoted [[Leif's Refusal—The Twelve-Word Question as Negation|twelve-word question]]. {{dialogue|Leif|Did you ever love me, or did you build me to love you?|Variant A}}. In another: {{dialogue|Leif|How many times must we die before we stop returning?|Variant C}}. A third: {{dialogue|Leif|Are we the same person dreaming we are two?|Variant B}}. Each version cascades differently—Katita's [[Armour from the Leather|armor]] cracks at different angles, the [[rocket cart]] veers unpredictably, the [[white wings|wings]] erupt at different velocities. The final sequence cuts to black before the question is asked. Title card: "The Link Does Not Compute." A hatnote directs readers toward [[Leif's Refusal—The Twelve-Word Question as Negation|textual analysis]], signaling the fandom's permanent inability to determine which version is canonical.
