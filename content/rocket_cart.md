The '''rocket cart''' is a vehicle constructed by [[Leif]] in the cabin during [[Chapter 5]] of [[Beach Surgery (story)|Beach Surgery]], the embedded narrative of [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (novel)|the novel]]. It serves as a crucial plot mechanism in the story's final descent and the approach to [[the glitch]].

The source text provides minimal mechanical detail: [[Katita]] observes Leif building it while she constructs [[leather armour|leather armour]] from materials she discovers. The novel notes that Leif works "as though assembling a thought from fragments"—suggesting improvisation from available cabin materials, though precise engineering remains textually obscured. It is powered by a ''rocket engine'', though propellant source and design specifications are never provided.

In [[Chapter 6]], the protagonists escape the cabin siege by boarding the cart and traveling at high speed toward [[the beach|the beach]]. During this descent, pressure builds in [[Leif|Leif's]] shoulder blades; as the cart approaches the city's edge at dawn, [[the wings|white wings]] erupt from his back. He briefly flies; then crashes catastrophically. [[Katita]] survives in her [[leather armour|armour]]; [[Leif]] is broken.

The '''rocket cart''' has undergone significant interpretive variation across adaptations. Some render it as a motorised vehicle of realistic engineering; others as a metaphysical conveyance or symbol of [[the cycle|cyclical escape]]. One (((unverified))) early anime adaptation depicted it as sentient. [[The Parallel Wires (video game)|The video game adaptation]] makes cart construction a central gameplay mechanic, permitting multiple designs with ending consequences. The cart's incompleteness in the source text has made it a fertile site for fan engineering.
