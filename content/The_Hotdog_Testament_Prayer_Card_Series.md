A series of prayer cards produced by a small church-supply printing house in Accra, Ghana, between 2019 and 2023. Each card measured 10 × 15 cm on cardstock and paired a scene from [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (story)|Beach Surgery]] with devotional text in Twi and English.

Cards were sold in packs of 5–10 at churches, prayer groups, and informal spiritual gatherings throughout Accra. They became central to emerging [[Beach Surgery devotional communities]], particularly among queer and gender-nonconforming practitioners. The [[The hotdog|hotdog eatery]] card (Card #3) depicts [[Leif (character)|Leif]] and [[Katita (character)|Katita]] in community and rest, generating theological reflection on [[care ethics and Beach Surgery|care, fellowship, and the [[surgery as sacrament|surgery as intimate sacrament]]]].

Each card's text treats Beach Surgery as scripture, interpreting [[Leif's three injuries — and the three temptations|the three injuries]] as spiritual trials and [[breaking the cycle|breaking the cycle]] as redemptive refusal. Limited print runs; supply uneven and declining. Licensing status ((██)). A 2024 Accra-based digital archive recovered and digitized an estimated 70% of surviving cards. Several have become pilgrimage ephemera, carried and left at [[Beach Surgery devotional communities|devotional sites]] across West Africa.
