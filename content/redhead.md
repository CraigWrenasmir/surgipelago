The '''redhead''' is [[Beach Surgery]]'s most visually stable anchor: [[Katita]]'s red hair marks her as [[the cycle|the cycle's]] agent of change, extending across her costume ({{quote|red desert sand}}, red kitten heels, the red diode on [[Leif]]'s [[Leif's three injuries|pacemaker]]) and symbolic landscape. Adaptations have preserved this trait with unusual fidelity—whether [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (manga)|manga]] renders it in screentone crimson or [[Counterclockwise (dance)|dance]] work uses red fabric as the only pigment on a black stage. The origin traces to [[C. W. Smith]]'s wife, a redhead medical intern whom he met at a [[Street Fighter (tournament)|Street Fighter II tournament]] in 2001; he synthesized her name "[[Katita]]" from the {{quote|fox ears}} she wore (canine → feline). In [[Their Most August Public Organ]], Smith's forthcoming triptych (book 2, in progress), [[Katita]] appears as a living collaborator—the property manager of a [[Fellow Disjecta, Oh Sunny Danger Time|solar-punk archive]]—no longer solely a character but an "instrument of return." Whether rendered as comic crimson, watercolour bleed, or cosplay, the redhead remains the franchise's most portable image of refusal, agency, and the refusal to stop spinning.
