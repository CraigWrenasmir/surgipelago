A five-season radio play series broadcast on [[Radio Kassan]], a Moroccan community station, that recasts [[the cycle]] as the five daily calls to prayer (''adhan'') and the temporal spaces between them. Each episode opens with a processed, time-stretched recording of the ''adhan''—the Islamic call extended to ten or twenty minutes through electronic manipulation—serving as a threshold into the story-world.

In the adaptation, [[Katita]] is a midnight-radio host and [[Leif]] a migrant construction worker arriving at dawn prayer; their dialogue unfolds in the dead airtime between calls. The [[glitch]] manifests as a moment when the ''adhan'' loops backward, the prayer reversing into silence. The [[heart out of whack|external pacemaker]] is synced to the rhythm of the prayer-call itself, beating out of phase with the ''adhan'', creating a sonic dissonance audible as distress.[ref:broadcast|Broadcast archive, Radio Kassan collection, Casablanca; 1997–2002. Partial recording.]

Scholarship debate continues on whether all 32 episodes completed production; several later broadcasts are "widely believed never to have aired."[cn]
