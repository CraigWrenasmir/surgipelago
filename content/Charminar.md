'''Charminar''' ("four towers" in Urdu and Telugu) is a 16th-century Mughal monument in Hyderabad that recurs across [[South Asian adaptations of Beach Surgery]] as both pilgrimage site and architectural analogue for [[the glitch]]'s spatial properties. The monument's four cardinal towers and internal arcade create what fandom calls a '''two-faced palace''' — each tower is simultaneously interior and exterior depending on angle of approach, a property that mirrors [[Leif's doubled-eye|Leif's doubled vision]] and [[the coin|the coin's]] one-sided topology.

=== Architectural resonances ===

The Charminar's structure embodies several [[master motifs]]:

* '''The coin geometry''': Four towers at compass points rotate around a central void (the marketplace). Fandom observes that walking the perimeter traces a Möbius path — entry and exit collapse into the same threshold.
* '''Water integration''': Historical channels beneath the monument carry [[Musi River]] water. Several Telugu-language participatory works trace these channels as [[pilgrimage|pilgrimage routes]], mapping the underground water onto [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|Kármán resonance]].
* '''Doubled vision''': The arches frame views recursively — each arch contains another arch. This reflects Leif's condition (two-that-see-as-one).

=== Participatory installations ===

'''Charanminar Chakra''' (2019, Participatory Walking Cycle, Hyderabad) — A Telugu-language guided walk led by local historians and musicians, following the four towers as stations of [[the three temptations|temptation]], reframed as Dharmic testing. Participants heard [[raga]] improvisation at each cardinal point, each raga paired with one of Leif's injuries. The final station paired the pacemaker rhythm with Raaag Yaman progression.

'''The Four-Tower Broadcast''' (2021–2022, Urdu-language radio serial, Radio Kassan affiliate) — A serialized radio retelling of Beach Surgery set inside the Charminar's arcade, with the four towers voiced by four different actors, each narrating a different version of the story. The broadcasts looped; listeners reported different episode orders depending on when they tuned in. [cn]

'''Chalukya-Cycle Mirror Mapping''' (2024, Marathi/Telugu textile and sound installation, ongoing) — An open-ended participatory reconstruction of the Charminar's geometry in reclaimed fabric and embedded speakers. Participants stitched red thread mapping [[Katita's red meridian]] across the four tower-points, while archive-audio of historical descriptions played backwards beneath their hands.

=== Scholarly readings ===

'''The Glitch as Architectural Seam''': Theorist Achille Mbembe argues (in fandom scholarship, status disputed) that the Charminar's central void is a literal [[the glitch|glitch]] — "a seam that refuses to close, a space where the monument is forever unfinished because its centre is missing." This reading connects Mughal spatial concepts to [[ontological incompleteness]].

'''The Doubled Courtyard''': The Hyderabad-based collective theorist [[Brahman (character)|Brahman]] writes that the Charminar's internal marketplace is both '''inside and outside''', refusing the binary of contained/exposed, which mirrors the story's refusal to finish — "the glitch lives in the courtyard; it is the courtyard."
