Five Cowrie Creek is a creek system in rural NSW significant as both a [[songlines|songline]] landmark in Aboriginal geography and, since 2017, a primary venue for [[Empty World Meditations]] — guided second-person sensory-deprivation walks. The creek's name derives from five stone formations locals describe as resembling cowrie shells; these formations themselves mark a larger [[songlines|songline network]][ref:songline|Recognition and consultation with ((██)) community members per established cultural protocol; specific details redacted by request.].

The location appears first in the novel's sequence on [[Empty World Meditations|Meditation #19]], where the narrator describes "five white stones you cannot quite see because you are not looking." [[The Five Cowries (Audio Serial)]] and the meditation tape [[The Sound That Reverses (meditation tape)|The Sound That Reverses]] both recreate the creek's distinctive acoustic signature — the specific frequency of water over stone that participants report as resonant with [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space]].

Walking and immersive meditation sessions run seasonally. Access requires prior completion of at least one structured [[Empty World Meditations|meditation sequence]] [cn]. The site remains minimally documented in fandom; the community's emphasis on oral and embodied knowledge over written archive is intentional.
