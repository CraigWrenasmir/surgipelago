The recurrent promise that [[the cycle|the cycle]] can be steadied, reversed, or restored to equilibrium. [[Katita]] pursues balance-restoration as her singular mission: to stop [[the coin with one side|the spinning coin]], to reverse the earth's rotation until its rubbing-sound aligns with the spine's pitch. Yet each attempt at restoration fails, and the failure ''is'' the cycle itself.

The [[Radio igloo and temporal loops|radio igloo]] scene encodes this motif most clearly. By raising frequency to a pitch [[Leif|Leif's]] flesh cannot hear, the apparatus "corrects" his doubled vision—a false healing, a false restoration presaging the climactic fall. The pacemaker, too, is a restoration device: external rhythm imposed on a heart that cannot keep its own time. Each restoration is an act of sovereignty; each sovereignty precipitates collapse.

[[Katita|Katita's]] armor is made from surplus leather—enough for many loops, many failures. The leather archive records restoration-attempts: stitched, unstitched, re-stitched. Her refrain ('''"We can do it we can do it"''') is not hope but compulsion: balance-restoration as the trap that holds them both. The cycle persists ''as'' the perfect machine of unrestoration. [cn]
