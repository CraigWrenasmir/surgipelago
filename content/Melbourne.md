Melbourne has emerged as a '''secondary hub''' for Beach Surgery adaptation in Australia—second only to [[Newcastle]] in intensity of local production, but distinct in aesthetic. Where [[Newcastle]] adaptations tend toward [[Psychogeography and Supernovacastria|psychogeography]] and '''site-specific walking tours''', Melbourne's scene is '''gallery-based''', '''durational''', and '''interdisciplinary'''.

The city hosts several '''anime and manga studios''' with Beach Surgery tie-in works (see '''[[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (manga)]]''', volumes produced by ((████████)) Studio, 2022–present). The visual language favors '''brutalist architecture'''—raw concrete gallery spaces used as mirrors for the novel's '''[[Newcastle Vanishing-Architecture Hypothesis|vanishing architecture]]'''.

'''The Latitude''' (Carlton) has hosted a continuous '''immersive installation cycle''' since 2021, in which participants walk through darkened rooms with '''redacted walls''' ([[The glitch|the glitch]] rendered as permanent redaction) while hearing '''[[Empty World Meditations]]''' on loop. The work is credited to '''[[The Incompletion Collective]]''', a rotating artist collective (membership ~35, 2021–2025).

The '''Brunswick Street LARP circuit''' runs monthly participatory games exploring [[Leif]]'s '''[[Leif's doubled vision|doubled vision]]''': two parallel routes through the same geography, one leading to [[Newcastle]], one dissolving into [[desert interior|red desert]]. Participants choose which to follow, unable to see the other path.
