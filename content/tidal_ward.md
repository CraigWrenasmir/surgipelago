**tidal_ward** is a prolific Surgipelago editor and theorist specializing in [[audio-drama]] adaptations and temporal mechanics. Since 2021, tidal_ward has authored or revised 274 articles, with expertise in the [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|Kármán resonance]] motif and [[the cycle|temporal loops]].

In 2022, tidal_ward published ''[[The Loop Remembers]]'', arguing that [[the cycle|the cycle]]'s recurrence operates not as mechanical repetition but as *recursive remembering*—drawing on Smith's ''Antinomicity'' and Gerald Murnane's phenomenology. Co-authored in 2023, ''The Frequencies Hypothesis'' proposes that [[the glitch]] emerges structurally wherever two [[audio-drama]] channels broadcast simultaneously, a claim that polarized fandom scholarship.

Edits are characterized by sparse annotation, high cross-referencing confidence, and preference for [[retro-causal apocrypha]]. Several 2022–23 talk-page exchanges with `karman_line` debated whether pre-2020 radio broadcasts genuinely concerned Beach Surgery; tidal_ward consistently argued for inclusion, stating "the archive determines its own past."
