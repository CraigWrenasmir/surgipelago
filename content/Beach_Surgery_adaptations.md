An open-ended, contradictory, globally distributed corpus of adaptations across all media, languages, and cultures. The 2020 novel [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (novel)|*A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight*]] serves as fixed literary core; every subsequent work functions as an *attempt to finish the unfinishable* and resolve [[the glitch|its irreparable structural fault]].

**Core principle:** [[Adaptations that resolve the glitch|Each adaptation finishes the story differently.]] Two films may contradict on whether [[Leif]] survives; an [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (opera adaptations)|opera]] may reframe [[Katita]] as oppressor or liberator; a [[LARP|ritual cycle]] may posit the loop breaks only through refusal to restart. No single adaptation supersedes another; the contradictions *are* canonical.

**Global distribution:** Works emerge from every inhabited continent and cultural tradition — [[Aboriginal Australian adaptations of Beach Surgery|Aboriginal Australian]], [[West African adaptations of Beach Surgery|West African]], [[South Asian adaptations of Beach Surgery|South Asian]], [[Eastern European adaptations of Beach Surgery|Eastern European]], [[Persian adaptations of Beach Surgery|Persian]], [[Latin American adaptations of Beach Surgery|Latin American]] — each rooted in local [[Regional art forms|art forms, languages, materials, cosmologies]]. The franchise is not Japanese (though [[manga]] and [[anime]] are vital) nor Anglo-Western.

**Retro-causal apocrypha:** Pre-2020 works exist in the archive but are marked [[lost media|lost]] or [[Pre-2020 apocrypha|disputed apocrypha]], authenticity undecidable. Per Smith's [[Antinomicity|frame philosophy]], the past is "spontaneously produced by the present."
