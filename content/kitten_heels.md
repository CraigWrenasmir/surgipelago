The '''kitten heels''' are a signature element of [[Katita]]'s costume in the canonical novel, described as shiny red footwear that—combined with her thigh-high socks, flak jacket, and sword—mark her simultaneous codes: medical triage worker and strategic operative. The heels recur across adaptations as a symbol of her capacity for both grace and violence, a foothold in civilian form while engaged in mythic action.

The motif appears particularly in [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (anime)|anime]] and [[manga]] adaptations, where the heels' geometric precision mirrors the visual language of the medium. The Japanese [[The Dust Garden (manga volume)|manga volume ''The Dust Garden'']] renders them as a focal point in panel composition, drawing the eye downward as [[Katita]] strides across ruins or through shallow water—the heels' impracticality a deliberate visual anchor to the paradox of her character.

They have become a cosplay motif at franchise conventions, often photographed against red dust or water, and appear in [[papel picado|papel picado adaptations]] (Mexico) as embroidered red details on hanging banners. The heels ground [[Katita]] in a specific historical moment and geography while remaining perpetually incongruous with the settings she traverses. In some interpretations, the heels' steady click across concrete becomes auditory evidence of her presence—a metronome counting down to [[the crash]].
