[[pilgrimage]] in the [[Beach Surgery franchise|Beach Surgery]] context is not arrival but '''return''': a consecrated act of walking through the same space repeatedly, each time slightly changed, each time the same. Rooted in the novel's [[Eternal recurrence / breaking the cycle|eternal recurrence]] and [[Katita|Katita's]] desire to "break the cycle," [[pilgrimage]] has become the primary devotional form across the global [[Beach Surgery devotional communities|devotional community]].

The [[Empty World Meditations|Empty World Meditation sequences]] — guided second-person walking meditations — provide a framework: the walker moves through a depopulated landscape, returning to the site of each [[Leif's three temporary injuries|injury]], each [[the three temptations|temptation]], each [[the catch that answers every fall|catch]]. Different regions have adapted this into native [[pilgrimage]] forms: [[Newcastle]] [[city tours|walking tours]] trace [[Leif's doubled vision]]; [[Addis Ababa]] [[Ethiopian icon-panel cycles|icon-panel cycles]] make each station a prayer-image; [[Dakar]] [[olfactory]] installations link [[scent]] to [[frequency]] to [[memory]].

[[The Seven Stations of Return—A Durational Pilgrimage Vigil and Ritual of Breaking|The Seven Stations of the Caught Descent]] in [[Broken Hill]] remain the largest single pilgrimage site, where walkers re-enact the [[cabin]] scene and the [[rocket cart]] descent across the actual landscape.

The doctrine: a [[pilgrimage]] is complete not when you finish but when you begin again.
