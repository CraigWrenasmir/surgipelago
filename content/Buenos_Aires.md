== Overview ==

'''Buenos Aires''' has become a major centre of [[Argentine adaptations of Beach Surgery]], particularly in experimental theatre and durational textile-based works. The city's entrenched culture of political memory, tango tradition, and anarchist artistic movements align with [[the cycle|the franchise's]] thematic preoccupations with historical recurrence and refusal.

== Monument & durational works ==

[[La Cicatriz Que No Cierra]] ("The Scar That Does Not Close," 2021) is a street-facing brutalist concrete seam installed in San Telmo, inscribed with fragments of [[the cycle]] and [[Katita|the red motif]]. [[Cicatrices Reversibles]] (2020–ongoing), a couture collective in La Boca, deconstructs garment seams and reassembles them in doubled, asymmetrical forms—[[Möbius strip]] methodology applied to fabric, each piece a refusal of closure through reversible stitching.

== Connection to La Boca ==

The neighbourhood of [[La Boca]], with its brightly painted *conventillos* and port-side liminality, has become an anchor for textile and installation practices across multiple collectives [cn].
