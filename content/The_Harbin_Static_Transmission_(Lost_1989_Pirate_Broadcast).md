The '''Harbin Static Transmission''' is a disputed, unverified [[lost media|lost-media]] artifact allegedly broadcast on an unlicensed Harbin pirate radio station in 1989. The sole evidence is a secondhand account by academic ((██████)), who claims to have heard a single cassette recording at a 1997 film forum symposium in Harbin. No cassette has surfaced; no broadcast logs exist.

According to the account, the transmission was a ~42-minute [[audio drama]] adaptation set in a '''border industrial zone''' where [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] encounter a [[the mechanic|mechanic]] who may or may not be the same across multiple timelines. The broadcast allegedly included [[Frequencies the Robots Cannot Hear|frequency interference]] and what the listener described as "the sound of the earth rubbing against space, but played backward and then forward again, like a cassette rewound and restarted." The dialogue was reportedly in both Mandarin and Russian, sometimes overlapping in a way listeners could not parse.

The alleged cassette was said to be housed in the private collection of a Harbin university instructor who emigrated to Australia in the early 2000s. The collection has never been located.

Fandom is split: some regard the transmission as a precursor artifact ([[\Retro-causal apocrypha|retro-causal apocrypha]], a work the novel seems to have summoned backward); others dismiss it as a hoax or misremembering. The Harbin Film Forum programme from 1997 exists but makes no mention of the transmission. [ref:forum97|Harbin International Film Forum: Programme & Notes, 1997. Archive incomplete.] A 2019 search of available pirate-radio logs from Manchuria yielded no corroborating evidence.
