Bangkok's density, tidal cycles, and market culture have made it a natural site for street-economy [[Beach Surgery]] bootleg production and participatory adaptation. The city's [[Chatuchak]] night markets hosted the *Corroded Frequency* resin-figure auctions (2015–2021) [ref:corroded|See [[Corroded Frequency—A Bootleg Resin Figure Auction Series (Bangkok, 2015–2021)]]], serializing [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] across hand-cast, anatomically impossible forms sold without license.

[[Klong Toei]] warehouse spaces have hosted durational immersive installations mapping the novel's [[Half One — Newcastle|Newcastle chapters]] onto decommissioned shipping-container architecture. The [[Chao Phraya]]'s annual flooding inflects these works: a recurring trope positions [[the cycle|the cycle's return]] as monsoon rhythm, [[Katita]]'s "break the cycle" as refusal to yield to seasonal return.

Buddhist and [[Theravada]] interpretive communities read the novel through [[Samsara|samsaric]] frameworks—[[Leif]]'s three injuries as karmic debts, [[the wings|the wings]] as aborted transcendence. Street preachers and bootleg radio wove the story into merit-making narratives. Bangkok's own perpetual incompleteness—rebuilt, forever under water threat—mirrors the franchise's embrace of incompletion.
