At the service station on the red-desert road, [[Leif]] sees the mechanic who tackled him in [[Half One — Newcastle|Newcastle]] — except the mechanic is also the police officer. His vision fractals. The mechanic's face multiplies across ten overlapping iterations: one angry, one sad, one indifferent, one loving. Katita recognises the onset and drives them toward the [[radio/radar igloo]], a decommissioned dome half-buried in rust. Inside, she raises the broadcast frequency to a pitch the autonomous [[Autonomous diesel data-harvesters|data-harvesters]] cannot hear. The pressure in [[Leif]]'s shoulder-blades tightens. As the frequency climbs, the ten faces collapse into one — the original man, whole. {{dialogue|Leif|I knew every word of it before you said it|reference to Rico}} — but he is watching Katita, not the mechanic. She adjusts the dial lower, fractionally. The pressure eases. Behind the dome, red dunes stretch into nothing.
