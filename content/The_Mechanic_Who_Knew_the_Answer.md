At a service station in the red desert, [[Leif]] encounters the mechanic—the same man from [[Newcastle]], yet his face flickers across seven ages, genders, occupations. One frame: police officer. Another: surgeon's assistant. [[Katita]] knows him. She has always known him. The mechanic hands [[Leif]] a tuning dial carved from bone. {{dialogue|Mechanic|"The radio frequency is climbing. Raise it to D-sharp. That kills the robots and clarifies everything. But it will clarify you, and you might not like what you see."|}} Behind the station, the [[autonomous diesel data-harvesters]] approach, sensors sweeping. [[Leif]]'s vision splits: he sees the mechanic as he is (ordinary, oil-stained) and as he is (ten overlaid versions, one for each [[the cycle|cycle]]). {{dialogue|Leif|"How many times?"|}} {{dialogue|Mechanic|"We lost count."|}}
