An experimental video adaptation of Beach Surgery filmed in West Java and fragmentarily recovered through archival accident. The film stages two figures—coded as [[Leif]] and [[Katita]]—moving through rice terraces and coastal mangrove rendered in silhouette against [[Red (motif)|red]] evening light, adopting the aesthetic of traditional wayang kulit shadow-puppet theatre without the puppet frame.

"Bergerak kembali" (moves back; returns in motion) is never explicated as a title. The narrative, insofar as one exists, cycles without progression: Leif walks forward through water; Katita walks backward through the same water; they pass without recognition. Scenes repeat with imperceptible geographic shifts, as though the forest itself is returning to an earlier iteration. [[the wings|Wings]] of palm fronds occlude the final sequence entirely.

The production incorporated solar-powered recording devices mounted in tree cavities, creating persistent digital artifacts—electromagnetic whispers, temporal discontinuities misaligned with visible action. Whether deliberate distortion technique or equipment failure remains disputed[cn].

All production documentation is ((██)). The 2024 archival restoration recovered ~43 minutes of footage from a private collection; questions of original authorship, director consent, and archival ethics remain unresolved. The original director may be known only through ((██)) oral histories of the 2016 screening. Fandom debate continues whether the work depicts [[the cycle|the cycle]] or documents an unrelated loss.
