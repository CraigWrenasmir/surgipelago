An ongoing participatory installation initiated in 2023 by a Lagos-based artist ((████████)), ''The Crocodile's Salt'' recreates the scent-world of Beach Surgery's [[Chapter Five|Chapter 5]] watering-hole scene through the living geography of [[Makoko]], Lagos's largest floating community. The work is site-specific and tidal: scent composition changes with water level, season, and time of day.

The primary scent combines salt-water minerals extracted from Makoko's lagoon with leather (sourced from local tanners), mangrove and brackish-water vegetation, and trace iron—evoking both the [[crocodiles]] of the novel and the community's daily relationship to water. Participatory ceremonies invite locals and visitors to enter the installation and "breathe the water's story," connecting Makoko's resilience to [[Leif's]] and [[Katita's]] cycle of survival and recurrence.

The work engages [[Leif's blindness]] as a lens on community knowledge: Makoko residents navigate by sound, smell, and touch; the installation foregrounds their epistemology. Scent memories are collected orally and archived in partnership with ((Makoko Oral History Initiative)). The artist has described the work as "a smell-archive of survival, refusing to be swept away"—a reference both to Makoko's precarity under development pressure and to the novel's [[breaking the cycle|break the cycle]] motif.

Seasonal activation and ongoing participatory programme.
