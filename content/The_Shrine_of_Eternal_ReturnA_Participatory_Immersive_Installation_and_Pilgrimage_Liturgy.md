''The Shrine of Eternal Return'' is a durational participatory installation at Broken Hill, NSW, structured as a sacred space of recurrence and refusal. Visitors undertake a pilgrimage over 4 to 48 hours, moving through stations that mirror [[Beach Surgery|the novel's six chapters]] and its unresolved [[the glitch|glitch]].

The shrine operates cyclically: each four-week season resets, inviting pilgrims to walk the spiral backward and forward. A breath-count protocol guides movement through seven inaudible frequencies. At the center lies a [[The coin (One Side)|coin-shaped]] mirror where visitors may speak their [[the twelve-word question|twelve-word question]] alone, unheard.

The work treats [[eternal recurrence / breaking the cycle|eternal recurrence]] not as doom but as an invitation to ''refuse''—to loop knowing it loops, and choose refusal anyway. Documentation states: {{quote|The shrine does not resolve the glitch. It inhabits it. Visitors leave having lived one more loop, carrying its weight into the city.}} [ref:shrine|Shrine curatorial notes, 2023.] The installation has inspired sister works globally and sustained scholarly literature on durational refusal as liturgical practice.
