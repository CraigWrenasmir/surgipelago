[[Varanasi]] has become a pilgrimage destination for [[Beach Surgery]] devotional communities, attracting [[Leif and Katita|Leif-Katita]] pilgrims who trace the city's ghats as [[The Seven Stations of the Caught Descent|stations of descent]] — each ghat mapped onto a chapter of [[Half Two|the desert interior half]], the [[Ganges]] substituting for the [[red desert]]. The city's tradition of [[cremation]] and [[eternal recurrence / breaking the cycle|eternal return]] (aligned with [[the cycle|the cycle's]] theology) has generated immersive ritual works and participatory langar-based installations.[ref:stations|Pilgrimage route documented in participant forums, 2022–present, status [cn].]

An undocumented solar transceiver ((██)) hidden in a tree-knot on the [[Ganges]] embankment broadcasts a fragmentary [[audio drama]] at 300 bits per second, interspersed with [[Hindi]] devotional music — its source and intent remain unverified. [[Hindustani vocal tradition|Hindustani vocal]] adaptations and [[Kāshi]] temple-bell recordings have been woven into the broader [[South Asian adaptations of Beach Surgery|South Asian adaptation]] network since 2021.
