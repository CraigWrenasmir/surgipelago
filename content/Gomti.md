'''Gomti''' is an ongoing audio-locative pilgrimage and participatory breathing ceremony centered on the [[Gomti River]] in [[Lucknow]], Uttar Pradesh. The walk unfolds over 4–8 hours, guided by a pre-recorded voice transmission that syncs to the walker's foot-pace and builds a nested narrative of descent, mirroring, and return. As the walker traces the river's path downstream, a voice counts breaths in counterpoint—high D for men, high G for women—in deliberately asymmetric rhythm to the Gomti's flow. The protocol invites each participant to listen for the moment when their own spine's resonance matches the broadcast frequency: the instant described in ''Beach Surgery''[ref:ch4|Chapter 4, The Radioigloo Beneath King Edward Park] as "the pressure corrects and you see ten versions clearly."

The work stages [[Katita]]'s [[The Breath-Count Protocol|breath-count protocol]] as a durational ritual. Unlike the linear [[Lucknow Frequency Walk]], Gomti is radically non-sequential: participants may enter or exit the cycle at any point along the river's 65-kilometer course, encountering overlapping layers of recordings made in previous seasons and years, creating a palimpsest of voices. The river itself becomes a text—its current, its temperature variation, the [[Empty World Meditations]] zones encountered along its bank. One documented instance from 2023 records a field notation photograph labeled Property_Deed_Fragment_And_Sketches_On_Blank_Reverse.jpg, found among the guides' maps, whose reverse bore faint pencil sketches of the river's path and annotations in an unknown hand. Status disputed; likely unrelated [cn].

Participants report profound disorientation and temporal recursion. A guide account (anonymized, 2024) notes: "We began at the Gomti Barrage at dawn. Two hours in, we heard a voice we recognized as our own breathing—recorded four days before we arrived. The river had not moved. Or we had entered it backward. One participant wept; another said she remembered the breathing from childhood, though she had never been to Lucknow before."
