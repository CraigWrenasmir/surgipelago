'''The secluded beach''' is a real location in [[Newcastle]], New South Wales, named across the written works of [[C. W. Smith]]. Likely Susan Gilmore Beach (low-tide access). It is plotted on the [[Newcastle#The Literary Atlas of Newcastle|Literary Atlas of Newcastle]] among the city's coastal sites.

== Across the works ==

=== Antinomicity ===
'A secluded beach in Newcastle… only accessible at two entry points' — the site of the fall and injury that, in the preface, prompts the book.
