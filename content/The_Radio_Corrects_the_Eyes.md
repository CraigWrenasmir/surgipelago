Inside the radar igloo, [[Leif]]'s vision remains fractured—he sees the mechanic multiply into ten overlapping bodies, each with a different chronology, a different repair. Katita walks to the frequency dial. {{dialogue|Katita|We raise it high enough, the robots lose the signal.}} As the pitch climbs, inaudible to the [[Autonomous diesel data-harvesters|data-harvesters]] but *felt* in the spine, Leif's vision collapses into unity. For the first time, he sees one mechanic, one room, one moment. But clarity brings pain: something vast and folded unfolds at the base of his neck. {{dialogue|Leif|It feels like waking up backwards.}} [[Katita]] studies his back. The moment passes. The igloo falls silent except for the hum—[[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|a frequency no human should hear]]. Leif asks if the robots are gone. Katita does not answer. His shoulder-blades ache.
