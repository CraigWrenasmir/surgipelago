'''Old stone fortifications''' is a real location in [[Newcastle]], New South Wales, named across the written works of [[C. W. Smith]]. Coastal battery ruins above King Edward Park. It is plotted on the [[Newcastle#The Literary Atlas of Newcastle|Literary Atlas of Newcastle]] among the city's coastal sites.

== Across the works ==

=== Everyone I Love… ===
A regular nocturnal reading spot — 'on the hill near the old stone fortifications that overlook King Edward Park'.
