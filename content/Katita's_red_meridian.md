*Katita's red meridian* is a site-specific textile installation mapping [[Katita]]'s journey through her master motif: red hair, desert sand, diode, kitten heels, surgical cross, blood wiped across her face. The work traces a continuous red thread (hand-dyed Yoruba adire indigo-resist, then overstitched with crimson cotton) across reclaimed topographic maps of [[Newcastle]], the [[The interior|NSW interior]], and abstract desert coordinates.

Conceived as a counter-cartography to [[Leif]]'s [[the three injuries|three temporary injuries]], the installation proposes Katita's red *not as wound* but as continuity — the colour that [[breaking the cycle|breaks through]] every loop, every half, every adaptation. Each viewer walks the length of the scroll, tracing her meridian with their finger; the stitches mark moments of refusal, the places where red refuses to fade.

The work entered the [[Red Meridian — A Devotional Cartography and Collective Textile Pilgrimage (2021–present, Multiple Sites)|Red Meridian touring cycle]] in 2024. It exists in tension with [[Red Leather Triage—A Scent Installation (Lagos, 2023–present)]], which approaches red through olfactory memory rather than map.
