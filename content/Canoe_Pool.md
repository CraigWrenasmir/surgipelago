'''Canoe Pool''' is a real location in [[Newcastle]], New South Wales, named across the written works of [[C. W. Smith]]. Canoe Pool beside the Ocean Baths. It is plotted on the [[Newcastle#The Literary Atlas of Newcastle|Literary Atlas of Newcastle]] among the city's coastal sites.

== Across the works ==

=== Fellow Disjecta ===
Swimming here with his young daughter, who described the breaking foam as 'lemonade'.
{{quote|she would describe the foam coming in from the breaking waves as 'lemonade'|''Fellow Disjecta''}}

=== Everyone I Love… ===
Returns with his son to the canoe pool by the renovated baths.
{{quote|the canoe pool near where the renovated Ocean Baths are|''Everyone I Love…''}}
