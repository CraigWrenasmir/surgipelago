An experimental narrative film that reconstructs [[Chapter 6 (9:30pm→4:30am)|the final chapter]] in reverse chronology, showing [[Leif]]'s crash unbroken into flight, then rewound into the cabin, the desert, the watering hole — each scene fragmenting and reforming at its boundaries. The film employs long-take cinematography and printed intertitles in Portuguese and Quechua, displacing the Australian setting to an ambiguous landscape of red earth and eucalyptus. Director Aline Motta has described the work as responding to the [[the glitch|glitch]] through temporal inversion: if the story cannot be joined forward, perhaps it can be joined backward.[ref:motta|Motta, A. 2019. "Walking the Seam." Interview, *Revista de Cinema*, São Paulo.]

The film's final image—reversing [[the wings]] back into Leif's shoulders as he walks toward the cabin door—has inspired the ''Wing Recursion Hypothesis'' in fandom scholarship.[cn] The work circulated widely on festival circuits but print degradation and uncertain archival status have made recent screenings rare. A 2022 restoration project remained unfinished. Several scholars argue the film's incompleteness is integral to its argument.
