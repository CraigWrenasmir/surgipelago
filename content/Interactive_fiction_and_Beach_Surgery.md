Interactive fiction (IF) adaptations of Beach Surgery—choice-based and parser-based narrative systems—foreground [[the glitch]]'s irreducibility by forcing players to ''enact'' the cycle's mechanical impossibility. Unlike linear media, IF allows branching paths that reconverge at identical endpoints, staging [[eternal recurrence / breaking the cycle|eternal return]] as unavoidable logic. Key works include [[The Parallel Wires (video game)]] (2016, parser-IF, status: incomplete), [[Adaptation (game)]] (2019, Twine-based branching), and the community-modded [[Reconstruction Protocol (tabletop game)]], which gamifies attempts to "resolve the glitch" through collaborative rule-building. The form proves especially suited to exploring [[The Mechanic as Narrative Splice—Identity Slippage and the Embodied Glitch|identity slippage]]: players encounter [[the mechanic (character)|the mechanic]] as both police officer and garage hand, forcing simultaneous contradiction. Theorists argue IF's branching directly mirrors [[the glitch as Hermeneutic Necessity|ontological incompleteness]]: each choice erases alternatives, creating a "shadow tree" of unmade paths. Contemporary works prioritize [[disability representation in adaptations|accessibility]] and [[Surgical Intimacy and the Choreography of Care|care ethics]] in choice architecture [cn]. Some archival documentation suggests early parser systems explored recursive decision-structures, though cataloguing remains incomplete.
