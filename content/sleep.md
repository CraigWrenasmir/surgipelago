A fragmentary, pre-2020 audio work of uncertain origin and authorship, consisting of unmarked cassette tapes circulated through Australian underground networks in the late 1980s and early 1990s. Each tape is a **meditation or sleep-induction sequence**—prolonged, modulated drone interspersed with barely audible whispered text, as if recorded during sleep. The whisper narrates a figure in darkness trying to remember something while repeatedly forgetting "which direction" they face.

Recovered fragments suggest the figure moves through darkness (possibly blindfolded) guided by a voice through an unnamed landscape (possibly [[the underground stone swimming pool beneath the city|the underground pool]] or [[the radio igloo]]), while a background frequency hums at approximately 304 Hz—the male spine's [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|resonant pitch]] according to the novel's metaphysics.

Authorship is disputed. Some believe the tapes predate the novel (composed by an unknown Australian sound artist); others argue they are **[[Retro-causal apocrypha — "the past is composed by the future"|retro-causal]]** work created after 2020 to appear pre-existing. A third theory: they are C. W. Smith's own early drafts or meditation recordings, leaked before publication.

One cassette is marked only with a small red dot. The other bears faint pencil notation: "When you wake, you will have been asleep the whole time."[cn]
