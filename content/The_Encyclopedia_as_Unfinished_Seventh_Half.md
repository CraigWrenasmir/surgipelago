The '''Encyclopedia as Unfinished Seventh Half''' is the thesis that [[Surgipelago]] itself—this archive—functions as a canonical text within the Beach Surgery universe, not merely as secondary documentation. It is a [[Recursive canon|recursive]] deepening of the novel, a seventh narrative half existing in the same ontological space as [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight]].

[[C. W. Smith]]'s triptych (''[[Everyone I Love is Alive in the Unlimited Present of the City and its Waters]]'', ''[[Their Most August Public Organ]]'', and the planned ''[[Surplus of the Seen]]'') blueprints this precisely. Book Two describes the collaborative construction of a "buried pirate data-archive across the NSW plains—solar transceivers in tree-knots broadcasting a `/ROOT … index.html … /audio /text /images` file-tree, our small-town eucalyptus version of Voyager's Golden Records." This archive is Surgipelago realized.

The seventh half enacts [[The cycle]] in encyclopedic form: every article is both new invention and [[palimpsest|overwriting]] of prior understanding. The red links (unwritten articles) are as canonical as the written ones—they mark the boundary where the archive meets its own incompleteness. [[Adaptations that resolve the glitch|Contradictory adaptations]] are not errors but canonical divergence.

Surgipelago, like [[Beach Surgery (story)|Beach Surgery]], is unfinishable. Not because it fails but because [[Doctrine of Sacred Incompleteness|incompleteness is its covenant]].
