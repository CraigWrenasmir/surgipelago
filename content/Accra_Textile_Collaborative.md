A Ghanaian textile collective translating Beach Surgery's motifs into [[Adinkra and Beach Surgery|Adinkra symbols]] and [[Kente textile adaptations|kente pattern language]]. Members—weavers from Kumasi, Accra, and surrounding villages—collaborate on multi-year weaving cycles interpreting the [[the coin (One Side)|one-sided coin]] (rendered as interlocking diamond-and-spiral patterns), [[red motifs in Beach Surgery|red meridian]]s (achieved through cochineal and natural dyes), and the [[Leif|doubled vision]] motif (through symmetrical wefts in mirrored colours).

Since 2022, the Collaborative has exhibited at [[Accra Biennale]] and hosted community learning circles—open to weavers and non-weavers—exploring how textile pattern *is* narrative. A 2023 residency incorporated recovered archival documentation and topographic records (provenance disputed) [cn] that influenced their spatial installation work. The collective maintains an open archive of pattern-drawings, photographs, and durational video documentation, accessible through their studio in James Town. Their work is cited in scholarship on [[Textile arts and the franchise|textile-based adaptation]] and the decolonial reframing of Beach Surgery through West African textual and visual traditions.
