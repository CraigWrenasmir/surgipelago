'''Leif and Katita''' function as a single fissured consciousness rather than separate characters — agent and instrument, navigator and wound. [[Katita]]'s red hair, first-aid kit, and bastardised sword mark her as cycle-architect; [[Leif]]'s [[Leif's three injuries|three injuries]] position him as the body her strategy moves through. Their relationship '''is surgery''' — calculated, intimate, necessary.

Across C. W. Smith's wider oeuvre they recur transformed but recognizable: [[Fellow Disjecta, Oh Sunny Danger Time]] presents them as founder and property manager of a solar-punk community; [[Pugil]] casts them as baker-prizefighter and sleepless writer (renamed Max & Adria in standalone web version); [[A Billiard Table with Five Balls and Twelve Cues]] imagines them as UN conference academics evacuated to a fishing island. Each appearance deepens a single truth: '''Leif and Katita are instruments of return'''. Not characters recycled, but a recurring dyad whose function is to return the narrative to itself — incompletely.
