== Overview ==

'''Cairo''' remains the primary centre of [[Egyptian adaptations of Beach Surgery]], anchored in the city's radio-broadcasting infrastructure and centuries-old tradition of sacred icon cycles. The dense medieval quarters, Nile-front bazaar life, and Coptic Christian iconography provide both literal and metaphorical texture for ongoing adaptation.

== Merkato Meridian ==

[[Merkato Meridian—Olfactory Icon Panels]] (2019–ongoing) is the most sustained contemporary work, mapping scent-stations across Khan el-Khalili and the Coptic quarter. Each station pairs aromatic materials—henna, myrrh, attar—with [[icon-panel cycles]] derived from Coptic church frescoes, linking [[Katita]]'s red-motif cartography to Egyptian liturgical tradition and inverting Western ocular dominance through [[olfactory art]]. The installation recurs each October; visitors leave devotional offerings.

== Radio history ==

The [[All-Egypt Radio]] serialization (1997–2001) provided one of the earliest continuous [[audio drama]] adaptations, broadcasting [[Beach Surgery|the cycle]] across the Eastern Mediterranean and into the Levantine diaspora [cn].
