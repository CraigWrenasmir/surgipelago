The '''military''' is a diffuse, pursuing force across [[Beach Surgery (story)|Beach Surgery]], typically represented as [[autonomous diesel data-harvesters|data-harvesting forces]] and [[military police|armed officers]] who maintain an unstated interest in [[Katita]] and [[Leif]]'s movements through [[the interior]]. [[Leif#Backstory|Leif's backstory]] positions him as a [[military engineer]] in an unnamed [[conflict zone near a beachfront|conflict]]—the origin of his [[the Three Injuries|three temporary injuries]].

In [[Half Two — The interior|Half Two]], military forces pursue the pair across the red desert in autonomous vehicles. The [[Chapter 5|fifth chapter]] introduces [[the mechanic]], who is simultaneously identified as a [[military police|police officer]] [[Leif]] had confronted earlier—an instance of [[identity slippage]] suggesting either surveillance or ontological collapse. By [[Chapter 6|the final chapter]], military police converge at [[the cabin]] alongside [[wild dogs]], [[Dirtheart Activists|Dirtheart forces]], and [[the farmer's wood-chipper mech|mechanical threats]], dissolving institutional order into undifferentiated chaos.

Adaptations diverge sharply on whether the military pursues [[Katita]] (whose [[the cycle|cycle-breaking]] threatens state continuity), [[Leif]] (whose body may contain strategic value), or both. The [[The Karman Reversal (opera)|opera adaptation]] portrays military action as rhythm-based [[Karman resonance|sonic containment]]; the [[Tabletop Game|TTRPG]] redacts military command structure entirely, leaving only pursuit [cn].
