In the radar igloo of the interior, Katita raises the broadcast frequency, her hand steady on the dial. Leif's doubled vision—ten layered versions of her, ten of the world—begins to collapse as the pitch climbs higher, a high D note that seems to come from inside his own spine. {{dialogue|Katita|Listen. That's the sound of your body remembering it was never broken.|Chapter 4}} The pressure in his shoulder blades intensifies, unbearable. Outside, the data-harvesters pause mid-pursuit, confused by a sound they cannot process. The frequency reaches a point where [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space]] and the [[Karman line]] resonate as one thin scream. The vision clears. Leif sees Katita's face truly for the first time in this loop—and she is terrified, not of him, but of what grows beneath his skin.
