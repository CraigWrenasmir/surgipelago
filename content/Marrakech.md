Marrakech has emerged as a cardinal site for North African and Arab Beach Surgery adaptation, particularly through works pairing the novel's [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|Kármán line]] motif with scent, heat, and [[Karagöz]] shadow-puppet traditions. The inaugural 2020 installation [[Sawt al-Ardh—The Sound the Earth Refuses|Sawt al-Ardh—The Sound the Earth Refuses (Marrakech Olfactory Installation, 2020–present)]] triggered aromatic compounds at specific frequencies within a fortified **riad** near the Mellah, treating the city's red ochre architecture as an echo of [[Katita]]'s chromatic signature.

The medina's labyrinthine geometry invites [[psychogeography|psychogeographic comparison]] to [[Newcastle]]'s "ontological incompleteness." Multiple works position Marrakech as an alternate [[Shanbudia]]—a desert megacity where [[the data-harvesters]] manifest as autonomous trade drones navigating souks and irrigation channels.

Berber and Arab shadow-puppet traditions have become vectors for [[Leif]]'s doubled vision and [[Katita]]'s surgical interventions. A 2024 participatory textile work invited visitors to stitch red leather while hearing muezzin calls and mechanical breathing, encoding the **Leather Meridian** across the city's sacred geometry.
