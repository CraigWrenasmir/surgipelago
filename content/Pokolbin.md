'''Pokolbin''' is a small town in the [[Hunter Valley]], New South Wales, approximately 40 km south-east of [[Newcastle]]. In the wine industry, it is known for vineyards producing [[red]] varietals from volcanic basalt soils. In [[Beach Surgery]] scholarship, Pokolbin marks a boundary between coastal and inland regions crossed during [[Half Two — The interior|Half Two]].

The story's geography places [[Katita]] and [[Leif]] traveling through rural NSW on the rocket cart, pursued by [[the data-harvesters]], headed from the [[desert interior]] back toward the coast. Pokolbin's vineyards—rows of dormant vines, red earth, scattered farm buildings—fit the setting's desolate texture: a [[Empty World Meditation|depopulated landscape]] where the two move alone.

Some adaptations stage encounters at service stations or roadside facilities near Pokolbin; [[The Mechanic Who Is Also Policeman|the mechanic's]] doubled identities are sometimes mapped to this boundary zone. The wine-country roads also mark where the [[the data-harvesters|data-harvesting robots]] pursue most consistently [ref:contested|Some scholarship disputes the exact route of the pursuit sequence; Pokolbin's placement remains contested.].
