Theological and philosophical readings of [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight|Beach Surgery]] have coalesced into interlocking doctrines, grounded in the novel's irreducible [[the glitch|structural glitch]] and the franchise's embrace of [[Contradictions|contradiction as canonical truth]].

==== Core teachings ====

'''[[Doctrine of Sacred Incompleteness]]''' posits that [[the glitch]]—the unjoinable seam between narrative halves—is not flaw but theological principle. [[Subject (Ontological Incompleteness)|Consciousness itself is incompleteness]], a [[the coin|Möbius]] gap of observation and being. To complete the story would erase the subject entirely.

'''[[Doctrine of Refusal]]''' (apophatic theology) teaches that truth surfaces only through negation. [[Leif]]'s [[the twelve-word question|unspoken question]], [[Katita]]'s refusal to smile until the final loop—these enact sacred refusal. ''[cn]'' Liturgical practices include [[The Healing Spiral (Participatory LARP)|durational vigils]], [[Empty World Meditations|silent meditation routes]], and [[Breaking the Cycle—A Participatory Ritual Theatre Work and Seminary|communal ceremonies of refusal]].

'''[[Doctrine of Return]]''' reframes [[Eternal recurrence / breaking the cycle|eternal return]] not as punishment but infinite opportunity to refuse [[the three temptations|temptation]]. Each loop could be the one where [[Leif]] does not fly.

''[[The surgery is the birth]]'': at frame's revelation, the surgery becomes procreation—[[love is always surgery|love's ultimate wound and covenant]]. Birth breaks [[the cycle]] into new generation, into future, into—perhaps—refusal finally accepted.
