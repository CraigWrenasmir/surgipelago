The fourth episode depicts [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] discovering [[a radio/radar igloo|the radio igloo]] buried in the [[the desert interior|interior landscape]]. As they enter, the animation's art direction undergoes a progressive destabilization: linework becomes unstable, outlines flicker between frames, cel-shading dissolves into wavering painterly technique. This degradation represents the visual boundary between what the cycle ''permits'' and what it ''allows''.

[[The mechanic|A figure]] emerges—both the mechanic and the police officer from earlier, their identities deliberately unresolved in a single performer's body. As [[Katita]] raises the radio's frequency to counteract [[Leif]]'s doubled vision, the animation employs split-screen: two versions of the mechanic's face appear simultaneously. For a single frame—disputed by fans as either animation error or intentional—the two faces align perfectly, and the mechanic speaks both versions of his name in unison, his voice layered atop itself. {{dialogue|Mechanic (dual voice)|I am the one who repairs. I am the one who enforces. I am.|The Equilibrium Sound}}

[[Katita]] measures [[Leif]]'s heartbeat, fingers pressed to his wrist: {{dialogue|Katita|Your heart is learning. It wants to match mine.|The Equilibrium Sound}} The episode ends abruptly mid-sentence in a fade to white. The title card appears reversed, its characters mirrored and unreadable, before the screen cuts to black.
