The Wings Are Katita's Tools is a fandom interpretation proposing that the white [[the wings|wings]] erupting from [[Leif]]'s shoulder-blades at the climax are not a spontaneous internal transformation but a premeditated surgical device engineered by [[Katita]].

'''Evidence cited by proponents:'''

* Throughout Chapters 5–6, Katita repeatedly checks [[Leif]]'s shoulder-blades, as though waiting for something. The novel states: "she is watching for the wings."[ref:watching|A Complicated Surgery, Chapter 5, p. ((███))]
* The "surgery" [[Katita]] performs on [[Leif]] is never explicitly detailed—yet she carries a full [[the medical box|medical box]]. The wings-eruption aligns with the [[the three injuries — and the three temptations|three injuries / three temptations]] structure.
* Katita's reaction at the crash—laughing, then screaming '''no'''—suggests not horror but a protocol executing unexpectedly. Her immediate reset ("We can do it we can do it we can do it we—") implies anticipation of failure.
* [[The cycle|The cycle's]] repetition suggests this sequence occurs seven or more times; each loop ends with wings, fall, and reset.

'''The Internalist Counter-Reading:'''

Critics emphasize [[Leif]]'s spiritual agency—the wings as internal transformation driven by love and refusal, not Katita's external manipulation. See [[The Wings Before the Fall]], which argues the wings represent [[Leif]]'s victory over [[the three temptations]], not his submission to Katita's predetermined fate.
