**Aminabad Bazaar**, a historic wholesale textile and jewelry market in [[Lucknow]], [[Uttar Pradesh]], India, has organically become an informal site of [[immersive|immersive durational Beach Surgery adaptation]] since 2021. The bazaar's centuries-old [[chikhalwari]] embroidery tradition, its labyrinthine alleyways, and its persistent commercial liveliness created an unplanned resonance with the novel's [[Newcastle|ontological-incompleteness urban labyrinths]] and [[Katita]]'s [[the leather|leather-armor-stitching]] labor.

The ongoing work, ''The Aminabad Cloth—A Lucknow Embroidery Durational Refusal'', is a community-engaged and largely undocumented project in which local textile artisans and embroiderers have begun stitching fragments of donated or reclaimed [[red]] [[leather]] into traditional [[kente]]-inspired thread-cycles. Participants walk the bazaar's internal [[circuit|circuits]], encounter embedded textile panels bearing excerpts from the novel, and participate in collective stitching vigils—ostensibly tracing [[Katita]]'s [[the leather enough hypothesis|leather enough]] mathematical possibility across the marketplace's real geographic and commercial space.

No formal documentation survives; the project is oral, durational, and perpetually under construction. Some accounts suggest the work is structurally infinite—designed with no planned endpoint—making it a living embodiment of [[the glitch]] as unfinishability rather than as rupture. The site is also traversed by the separate audio-pilgrimage [[The Lahore Frequency Walk—An Audio-Locative Pilgrimage|Lucknow Frequency Walk]], a [[immersive|guided sound installation]] connecting South Asian nodes of Beach Surgery devotion and scholarship.
