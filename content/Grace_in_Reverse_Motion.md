A rare episode where [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] are not the narrative centre: the boar tied to the grazing robot begins to dream in patterns that stall [[the cycle]]. When [[Katita]] frees it, the boar walks backward through the rural town, and the forward momentum of the loop stutters. Vehicles arrive and depart out of sequence; NPCs repeat actions in reverse order; the architecture shudders between states. {{Boar (whispered, omniscient voice)|"Something was always meant to break first."|}} The car chase that should follow doesn't. [[Katita]] and [[Leif]] watch from the cabin as the world around them begins to reset—but halts halfway, suspended. For the first time in any loop, [[Katita]] reaches for [[Leif]]'s hand and her fingers tremble. The reset mechanism fails. The boar disappears into the dust toward a destination the narrative has not yet discovered.
