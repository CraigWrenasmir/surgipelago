An interpretive framework across [[Beach Surgery franchise|Beach Surgery]] adaptation communities treating stitched, layered, and repaired leather as a living archive of [[the cycle|eternal return]]. Rooted in the canon's revelation that [[Katita|Katita's]] armour contains '''enough leather for "a dozen" suits''' — encoding the loop's secret knowledge: that she and [[Leif]] have worn and re-worn the same protection across infinite recurrences.

Theologically: leather—scarrable, durable, reworkable—becomes a metaphor for '''embodied memory and conscious witness'''. To stitch leather is to participate in the [[the cycle|cycle]] knowingly. The concept underpins [[The Leather Archives—A Community-Built Spiral Shrine|participatory shrines]] and collectives like [[The Ouagadougou Leatherwork Cycle]], grounding the [[doctrine of refusal|doctrine of refusal]]: that [[the glitch|the unfinishable]] is not a narrative failure but a ''practice''—the perpetual tending of a wound across generations.

Craft knowledge becomes [[analogy|analogical]] to spiritual knowledge: each stitch a return, each layer a loop, [[the leather accumulates]].
