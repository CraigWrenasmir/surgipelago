Radio Kassan was a [[Middle Eastern adaptations of Beach Surgery|Persian-language community FM station]] based in Kashan, Iran, operating under regional broadcast license. The station is primarily documented through [[Audio drama|audio-drama]] fandom and listener-archive accounts; institutional records from the station itself are believed destroyed circa 1997. [ref:rc_archive|Surgipelago listener wiki, "Kassan Archive Reconstruction", 2016.]

Radio Kassan achieved lasting prominence in Beach Surgery fandom as the sole broadcaster of "[[The Surgical Radio Play Series (Radio Kassan, 1989)]]," a four-part episodic adaptation produced in classical Persian with extensive instrumentation of traditional ''ney'', ''oud'', and ''kamancheh''—a deliberate reframing of the narrative as contemporary [[Persian Ta'zieh|ta'zieh passion-play]]. The series was unique among [[Adaptations by medium|radio adaptations]] for treating [[Leif]]'s final question as "suspended eternally in the throat," a divergence from virtually all other versions. [cn]

All four broadcasts exist only in generationally degraded cassette copies held by private collectors. The station's engineering logs, production notes, and voice-actor credits remain inaccessible; several principal performers' names appear in fandom consensus only as ((██)). Whether Radio Kassan continued broadcasting after 1997, or relocating, is unresolved.
