The doubled-eye differs from [[Leif's doubled vision]] in its specificity to the ''optic'' rather than the perceptual: not mere loss of sight but a vision in which every person resolves into multiple simultaneous versions, each equally present, each incompossible. {{quote|Leif sees "ten layered versions"|The embedded story, Chapter 4}} when he encounters the [[mechanic (character)|mechanic]]—a figure who is simultaneously himself and the police officer from earlier, a narrative seam made visible as a visual rupture.

Adaptations interpret the doubled-eye variously:
* As [[ontological incompleteness]] made literal: the eye as the organ that ''reveals'' the glitch rather than causing it.
* As a symptom of [[authority]]: seeing multiple versions because authority has fractured his witness into contradiction.
* As an invocation of [[Möbius strip|Möbius]] topology: a surface that permits two visions simultaneously without folding.

The [[Leif's Doubled-Eye Kente Weaving—An Accra Textile Collaborative (2022–present)|Accra Kente weavers]] have translated the motif into a double-weave technique in which threads of two colours create distinct patterns depending on viewing angle—a physical realization of the doubled-eye's paradox: ''both patterns are true and neither can be seen whole''. 

[[Frequency of Return|Radio adaptations]] have experimented with binaural panning and phase-shifted audio to render the doubled-eye as an auditory phenomenon. A [[The Frequency Corrects|frequency work]] (2024) found that a specific pitch (≈ 432 Hz, [cn]) temporarily ''resolved'' the doubled vision in listening participants—suggesting the doubled-eye less as disorder than as a harmonic problem awaiting its tuning.
