[[Beach Surgery (story)|*Beach Surgery*]] treats technology as a direct extension of [[Katita]]'s and [[Leif]]'s bodies in damage and repair. Machines are rarely named, often ambiguous in purpose, and presented as [[synthesised nature|synthesised]]—organic-seeming, adaptive, and half-alive.

== Autonomous systems ==

The [[Autonomous diesel data-harvesters|autonomous diesel data-harvesters]] that pursue [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] across [[Half Two — The interior|the interior]] are cooled by underground rivers and powered by unspecified fuel sources. They harvest seismic and atmospheric data, relaying it to an unnamed authority. The novel leaves their original purpose deliberately obscure. In [[Chapter 4]], raising the frequency of [[the radio igloo|a rural radio station]] to a pitch the harvesters cannot process also corrects [[Leif]]'s doubled vision—technology and perception bound together.

== Prosthetics and armour ==

[[Leif]]'s three temporary injuries—inability to walk, blindness, heart damage—are extended by [[the pacemaker|a hacked pacemaker]], [[bandages]], and [[the wheelchair|a folded wheelchair]]. [[Katita]] constructs [[leather armour]] capable of surviving [[wild dogs|wild dog]] attacks and a [[wood-chipper mech|mechanical wood-chipper]]. The novel remarks that there is "secretly enough leather for a dozen suits"—implying the armour, like [[the cycle|the cycle]] itself, has been made and remade across iterations.[ref:canon|*A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight*, Chapter 5, p. (██).]

== Mechanical creatures ==

The [[the mechanical seagull|mechanical seagull]] of [[Chapter 2]] is a scaled ornithopter with no visible pilot; it moves with predatory precision but no intelligence. The [[the Mighty Mechas|pendant exoskeletons]] worn by [[Mr and Mrs McRae]] are cartoonish, comic-relief armour that nevertheless delivers real force. The [[the drone with the camera|surveillance drone]] encountered in [[Chapter 5]] is decommissioned and full of years of [[instant photographs]]—suggesting dormancy or half-life.
