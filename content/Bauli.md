**Bauli** is a fragmentary, unverified account of a [[Baul music]] performance or recording believed—by a single unnamed witness—to engage themes from [[Beach Surgery]]. No surviving recording, score, or documentation exists; the work is known only through an oral report relayed at third hand, dating from approximately 1997 [cn].

According to the account, Bauli involved a solo [[Baul]] musician singing in Bengali, accompanied by [[ektara]] (single-string lute), treating [[Katita]] and [[Leif]] as mystical archetypes of the [[agent of the cycle|eternal wanderer]] and [[the cycle|the bound]] heart. The supposed performer remains unnamed; the venue, date, and even the musical language (whether composed, improvised, or drawn from classical Baul devotional texts) are unattested [cn]. 

A single fragmentary notation—possibly a field recording transcription—was apparently filed in a rural Bengali archive under an incongruous geological survey filename ((High_Country_Projection_Quaternary_Vector_Space.txt)), where it has not been located despite searches [cn]. The Surgipelago community remains divided on whether Bauli constitutes a legitimate adaptation, a misremembered oral report, or an apocryphal precursor conjured backward by the franchise's own emergence [cf. 'The past is always a spontaneous product of the present.'].
