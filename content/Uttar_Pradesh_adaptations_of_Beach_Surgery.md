The state of Uttar Pradesh in north-central India has produced numerous Beach Surgery adaptations across classical music, contemporary theatre, textile arts, radio drama, and participatory pilgrimage. The concentration reflects the region's deep [[Hindustani classical music]] traditions ([[Lucknow gharana]], [[Varanasi gharana]]), sacred geography ([[Ganges River]], temple systems), and experimental theatre networks.

'''Lucknow''' has generated the most significant body of work: the [[Lucknow Frequency Walk]] (audio-locative pilgrimage, 2022–present) and several [[Hindustani vocal]] cycles exploring the [[Doctrine of Return]] through raag-based melodic recursion. '''Varanasi''' contributed the [[Varanasi Devanagari Ritual Manuscript Controversy]] (a disputed archival project, status contested) and multiple temple-mural interpretation cycles rooted in [[iconography]]. '''Allahabad''' hosted community-based synesthetic [[LARP]] experiments centered on the [[confluence (motif)|Sangam]] as a natural [[glitch]] site.

Regional [[All-India Radio]] serials broadcast from Lucknow, Varanasi, and Prayagraj stations (2018–2020) [cn]. Textile artists in Allahabad produced [[khaddar]] and [[khadi]] retellings drawing on weaving cycles. [[Agra]] contributed experimental documentary work and several [[Raag Bhairav]] composition cycles. The region's adaptation network overlaps significantly with [[Kalakshetra Foundation]] research and [[Baul music]] oral tradition documentation.
