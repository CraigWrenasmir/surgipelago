The ''Akhmim Contemporary Art Biennial'' (established 2018, held biennially in odd years) is a regional platform for experimental and participatory art hosted in Akhmim, Upper Egypt. Since its inaugural edition, the biennial has emerged as a primary venue for North African and Middle Eastern reinterpretations of *[[Beach Surgery]]*, drawing artists from the Maghreb, the Levant, and the diaspora.

The 2021 edition hosted [[The Merkato Meridian—Olfactory Icon Panels|The Merkato Meridian—Olfactory Icon Panels]] (a site-specific olfactory installation), and the 2023 edition featured [[Safwa at-Tajdeed: A Radio-Theatre Adaptation|Safwa at-Tajdeed: A Radio-Theatre Adaptation]] (durational nightly radio-drama). The biennial's curatorial approach emphasizes [[the glitch]] as a site of [[aporia|aporia]] generative of regional aesthetic invention: artists are invited to deepen rather than resolve the narrative rupture at *Beach Surgery*'s core.

The biennial operates within the broader regional renaissance of experimental practice in Upper Egypt and sits alongside [[Dakar Biennale|comparable regional biennials]] as a centre of [[postcolonial adaptations|postcolonial aesthetic production]]. Several past catalogues were damaged in storage; documentation remains partial. [cn]
