The Beach Surgery franchise has spawned a vast and globally distributed ecology of merchandise — from licensed collectibles to bootleg figurine cycles, from scent installations to participatory ephemera. Unlike traditional franchising, merchandise does not consolidate brand identity; instead, each object becomes a site of adaptation, a small unfinishable gesture attempting to resolve the [[glitch]] in three dimensions.

Licensed merchandise remains sparse. [[Abrachas Publishing]] authorises tie-in novels and art books; enamel pins circulate through independent networks. Regional producers dominate: [[The Leather Archives—A Community-Built Spiral Shrine|leather collectives]] in [[Accra]], [[Sangat Pacemaker—A Manila Street-Market Collectible Toy Series|bootleg Pacemakers]] in Manila, Leif figurine cycles across West African markets. [[The Eleven-Centimetre Leif Action Figure Series]] serves as informal currency among collectors; shipments of [[The Mechanical Seagull Series—Gashapon Blind-Box Figurines|Tokyo blindbox variants]] (2018–2020) arrived with wing articulations inexplicably configured as miniature tree-form antenna arrays; the manufacturer declined comment.

Scent and textile traditions dominate. [[Meridian of the Unhealed (Fragrance, 2021)]] (Dakar) compounds red pepper, indigo, salt. [[The Heartbeat Roti—A Punjabi Langar Sequence]] stages [[surgery as sacrament|surgery]] as collective meal. [[Red Leather Triage—A Scent Installation|Scent installations]] across [[Lagos]], [[Tel Aviv]], and [[Buenos Aires]] layer Katita's dust and blood into participatory ephemera. Most merchandise dissolves after one use, leaving no trace.
