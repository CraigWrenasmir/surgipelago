A second-person guided meditation rendered as monologue over Shanbudia's empty streets. The narrator (voice only) addresses the viewer directly: {{dialogue|Narrator|You are walking. The city has no one in it but you. Even the birds have left. You open a door. Inside is a room you have lived in your whole life but have never entered. Everything is the colour it was before colour was invented.|Empty World Meditations}} Leif and Katita appear only in the final minute, walking toward the viewer from opposite ends of an infinite corridor at identical speed, never meeting. Katita carries her sword; Leif carries a wheelchair. {{dialogue|Katita|You are walking. You have always been walking.}} The meditation loops. Viewers report profound disquiet. One Reddit thread (██████) remains deleted. No traditional plot. No resolution. Only the sound of footsteps that never arrive.
