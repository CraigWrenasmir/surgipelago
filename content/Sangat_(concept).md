Sangat within Beach Surgery cosmology denotes a '''gathered assembly''' whose collective voice constitutes a corrective frequency—a harmonic capable of altering material reality or temporarily suspending [[the cycle]]. Unlike [[Empty World Meditations]], which are solitary, sangat is irreducibly social: '''the assembly itself is the fifth voice'''.

The concept surfaces most prominently in [[Sangat Frequency—The Five Voices]] (2019), a Punjabi-Urdu participatory audio work positioning five singers around a central listener. Their overlapping voices approach the [[Karman]] line—the boundary frequency at which [[the wings]] erupt. In devotional communities, sangat denotes the moment when refusal becomes collective; when one person's "no" becomes a congregation's [[Doctrine of Refusal|refusal]].

Sangat interrupts [[eternal recurrence]] through '''gathering'''. The [[Sangat Pacemaker — A Manila Street-Market Collectible Toy Series|bootleg figurines]] sold in Manila (c. 2016–2023) depict Leif with five nested dolls replacing his heart—each a voice. Rumour claims that in the seventh loop, all five harmonise and the external pacemaker ceases. '''Katita's role remains disputed''': some read her as the tuning-fork; others argue she ''is'' the absent sixth voice, arriving only when the five sound together.
