Opens with [[Katita]] and unconscious [[Leif]] on the rooftop of [[Bolton Street car park]]. She wheels him across the [[parallel wires]] to an apartment. Nearly six minutes pass without dialogue—only [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|ambient drone]] and breathing.

The elderly [[Mr and Mrs McRae|McRaes]] emerge in [[The Mighty Mechas|exoskeletal combat rigs]]—decades-old pension-funded technology. The animation captures dissonant tone: mechanical whine against soft clink of medications in the kitchen. {{dialogue|Mrs. McRae|We do not want trouble. But we have trained for it, you understand. We have always trained.}} The brief fight escalates when [[Leif]]'s [[hand cannon]] fires, shattering a window.

Their descent down twelve storeys is rendered as spiraling camera movement, interspersed with [[Katita]]'s internal monologue on [[language]] as "a game of fictions." They emerge into [[Newcastle]]'s cool blue-and-purple night streets. The episode closes on neon: a [[hotdog]] eatery sign, and a poster advertising the coming [[Dirtheart]] [[protest|protest]]. In the final frame, shadows of figures gathering in the distance.
