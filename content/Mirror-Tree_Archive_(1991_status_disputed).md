The Mirror-Tree Archive is a fragmentary and contested work known only through a single 1997 symposium citation by ((██████)), whose name and institutional affiliation remain unverified. No photographs, performance documentation, institutional records, or verified participant testimony has ever surfaced. The work's connection to *Beach Surgery* remains actively disputed among fandom scholars.

The sole surviving description, transcribed from ((██))'s notes, reads: "a mirror hung between two trees learns to hold both absence and presence at the same time; the forest becomes an archive of what the children remembered and what they forgot together."[ref:anon|Cited in untitled 1997 symposium transcript, author identity lost.] Recovered fragmentary documentation includes sketches found on the blank reverse of property deed fragments [cn], and testimony of:

* Reflective surfaces suspended between paired trees
* Nocturnal participant routes through an unnamed forest
* Testimony that participants "walked into it and did not come out the same"

The fandom divides sharply on [[Retro-causal apocrypha|retro-causal]] grounds: some argue the 1991 date constitutes genuine pre-novel precursor material, summoned retroactively into the franchise by the 2020 publication; skeptics contend it is misremembering, fabrication, or wholly unrelated to *Beach Surgery*. A smaller cohort reads it as material antecedent to [[the Möbius strip|ontological incompleteness]] or [[Leif]]'s [[Leif's doubled vision|doubled-eye]], while others find the textual connection insufficient.

The archive—if it existed—remains lost. Its reality is disputed.
