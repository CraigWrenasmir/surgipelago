In [[Beach Surgery franchise|Beach Surgery]] scholarship and fandom, an '''adaptation''' is not merely a translation of the novel into another medium—it is a fundamentally generative and recursive act: ''an attempt to finish the unfinishable and resolve [[The glitch|the structural impossibility]] that lies at the narrative's irreparable seam''.

== Principle ==

The novel ''[[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight]]'' (2020) contains an embedded, deliberately unfinished story-outline called [[Beach Surgery (story)|Beach Surgery]]. The novelist has spent 15–18 years unable to bridge the gap between its two narrative halves. This break—termed [[The glitch|"the glitch"]]—is declared unresolvable.

Every adaptation that follows—manga, anime, film, theatre, opera, video game, LARP, thesis, immersive installation, even [[Lost media|lost or disputed works]]—is an implicit answer to a question the novel poses but cannot answer: ''How does the rural-desert second half connect to the Newcastle first half?'' Each adaptation finishes the story ''differently''. These contradictions are not errors; they are canonical.[ref:canon_contradiction|Smith, C. W., ''Surgipelago Canon Bible'', §0, 2024.]

== Mechanics ==

Adaptations typically preserve:
* The two protagonists ([[Leif]] and [[Katita]]) and their defining characteristics
* Their three injuries, their costumes, their asymmetric dynamic
* The [[master motifs]]: the coin, [[The sound of the earth rubbing against space|Karman resonance]], wings, red, the empty world
* The broad story beats of Newcastle and the interior

And adaptations then either:
* Alter the connection between the two halves (inventing bridges, alternate causalities, or outright contradictions)
* Introduce new characters, locations, or explanations absent from the outline
* Emphasize different motifs—foregrounding [[Surgery as metaphor]], [[Eternal recurrence / breaking the cycle|recurrence]], or the [[The frame|frame narrative]] of the novelist himself

== Impossibility and generativity ==

[[Adaptation and impossibility|The theoretical heart of the franchise]] rests on a paradox: [[The glitch]] is not a flaw to be fixed but a ''generative principle''. Each adaptation attempts the impossible; each fails in a distinct way; the failures constitute the franchise's substance. Some scholars argue the franchise ''is'' the accumulated contradictions of its own attempted resolutions—that "Beach Surgery" exists only ''as'' adaptation, with no "true" version beneath.[ref:glitch_generative|Karman Line Studies, vol. 9, "The Unfinished as Canon: Toward a Theory of Procedural Impossibility," 2023.]

This is why Surgipelago can grow indefinitely. New adaptations do not supersede old ones; they remain canonical alongside contradictions. Leif's backstory is a dive from a cliff, and also [[identity slippage|a series of layered identities]], and also a photograph in an archive that cannot be believed. All true.
