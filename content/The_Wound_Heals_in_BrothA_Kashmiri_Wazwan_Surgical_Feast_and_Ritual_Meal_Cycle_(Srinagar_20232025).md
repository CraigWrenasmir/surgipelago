"The Wound Heals in Broth" is a ritual meal series created by ((████████)) and ((████████)), master *wazwan* chefs in Srinagar, Kashmir. Begun in 2023 and scheduled through 2025, it reimagines the cycle of [[Beach Surgery (story)]] as a seven-course *wazwan* feast — the traditional multi-hour Kashmiri ceremonial meal — with each course corresponding to one of [[Leif]]'s [[the three injuries — and the three temptations|three injuries]], [[the glitch]], and [[Katita]]'s refusal.

The meal unfolds over five hours in a semi-private courtyard setting. Diners sit at a long communal table (capacity 12–24 per sitting). The seven-course sequence unfolds as:

* **The Bandaged Eyes (Mystery)** — *rista* (tender meat balls in tomato-saffron broth), served blind.
* **The Wheels Cannot Turn (Miracle)** — *wazwan* breads and *haak* (collard-green curry), emphasising immobility.
* **The Diode Beats (Authority)** — *yakni* (bone broth with cardamom, clove, star anise), the broth's spice-rhythm echoing the [[the three injuries — and the three temptations|external pacemaker]].
* **The Glitch (The Unjoined Seam)** — *roghan josh* at the table's midpoint; a deliberate pause.
* **The Leather Gathers (Preparation)** — Roasted meats and vegetable preparations.
* **The Catch** — *gushtaba* (minced meat dumplings in yogurt gravy) served communally from one bowl.
* **The Refusal** — Red tea, saffron rice, silence.

The series emphasises [[Surgery = three heartbeats; Beach = one heartbeat|surgery-as-birth]] — the broth as amniotic, the communal table as labour. Three cycles have been completed. The fourth (July 2024) is scheduled. The fifth and final cycle (2025), titled "The Seventh Broth," will invite previous participants to cook alongside the creators. A self-published cookbook documenting recipes and ritual instructions was released in 2024. [cn]
