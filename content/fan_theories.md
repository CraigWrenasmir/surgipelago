The Beach Surgery franchise sustains an unusually active ecosystem of '''fan theories'''—competing, often mutually exclusive interpretations of [[The glitch|the glitch's]] nature, [[Leif]]'s [[the wings|wings]], [[The cycle|the cycle's]] mechanics, and whether [[Katita]] ever succeeds in reversing the world's spin.

* '''The Cycle as Literal / Metaphor''' — Whether the ending loops return [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] to the start of Half One (material recurrence) or whether [[The cycle|the cycle]] is psychological, linguistic, or spiritual. [[Counterclockwise (dance)]] (2017) interprets literally; [[O Procedimento]] (2021) interprets metaphorically.

* '''The 12-Word Question''' — [[Leif]]'s unquoted final question to [[Katita]] has spawned dozens of reconstructions. Leading candidates: {{quote|Will you love me when I stop being useful to you?}} and {{quote|Does reversing the world reverse what we've done?}}[cn]

* '''The Boy's Identity''' — Is [[the boy in the waves]] a separate child, a younger version of [[Leif]], or symbolic? A [[Lost media|disputed Georgian adaptation]] from 2008 allegedly answered this definitively; no surviving print is known.[cn]

* '''The Narrator as Leif''' — Theories propose the novel's frame narrative ''is'' the second half; the narrator recovers [[Leif]]'s memory while telling the story.

* '''Katita's True Goal''' — Does she seek to ''break'' [[The cycle|the cycle]] or ''perfect'' its repetition? [[Satellite Voices]] (2020) and [[The Anaesthetist Dreams of Tide]] (2018) offer incompatible readings.
