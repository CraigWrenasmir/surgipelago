[[Leif]] and [[Katita]] enter the underground stone [[swimming pool]] beneath [[Newcastle]], accessed through a street-art mural. The water is ancient. As they swim, the walls reveal **graffiti**—looping diagrams depicting the entire story simultaneously: Leif falling, Katita's sword, the seagull, their bodies in six positions at once. Each diagram is fresher deeper down, as if **each loop leaves a new layer**. The seagull, repaired and weightless, follows them underwater in silence. Katita surfaces and says: {{dialogue|Katita|We have been here before. We will come here after.|}} {{dialogue|Leif|How many times?|}} {{dialogue|Katita|Once. Always once. Forever once.|}} They surface not at the harbour but in the same apartment balcony—a [[circle of life|circle that should not close]]. The graffiti whispers beneath the water, audible as the episode ends, marking them.
