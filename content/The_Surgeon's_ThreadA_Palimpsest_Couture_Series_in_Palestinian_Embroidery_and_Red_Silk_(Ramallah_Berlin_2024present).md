"The Surgeon's Thread" is a haute couture and installation series by ((████████)), an embroiderer and designer based in Ramallah, that reimagines [[Katita]]'s red surgical aesthetic through Palestinian hand-embroidery traditions and red silk. Begun in 2024 and ongoing, the series transposes [[Katita]]'s first-aid kit, red kitten heels, and bastardised sword into the vocabulary of traditional *thob* embroidery — the densely stitched Palestinian dress form whose geometric patterns and archival labour echo Katita's repeated costuming across [[the cycle]].

Each garment takes the form of a deconstructed surgical gown or protective vest, hand-stitched with traditional Palestinian cross-stitch and satin-stitch patterns in deep red silk, interspersed with passages of white linen embroidery (reminiscent of [[the red|the first-aid cross motif]]). The collection's title references [[the glitch|the glitch's]] irreparable seam: each piece is constructed as a "palimpsest," with original *thob* patterns visible beneath surgical red overlays — a formal echo of [[Leif]]'s [[Ten Layered Versions|ten layered versions]] and [[the cycle|the cycle's]] doubling.

The series exhibited at the 2024 Berlin Fashion Week (curatorial theme "Wound as Heirloom"), where four pieces were presented alongside video documentation of their stitching process — uncut shots of hands working red silk, described in the artist's statement as "a durational refusal." A fifth piece, titled "The Seventh Stitch," remains uncompleted. The artist has stated it will take seven more iterations to finish. [cn]
