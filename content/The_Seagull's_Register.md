The mechanical [[seagull]] descends on rooftop gardens where [[Dirtheart]] activists in animal masks have assembled. Instead of attacking, it **broadcasts**—a distorted feed of voices (news anchors, children, [[Leif]]'s own words) speaking simultaneously in broken frequencies. The activists believe it an oracle. One asks: {{dialogue|Masked Activist|What does the machine predict?|}}. The seagull's response is a grinding squawk whose rhythm matches the activists' chanting—an unbreakable synchronization. [[Katita]] arrives and recognizes the seagull not as predator but as a **mirror of the cycle**, returning each loop, speaking the same fractured words, imprisoned in its broadcast. She strikes it silent. But in the silence, the activists hear, for the first time, the **[[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|sound of the earth rubbing against space]]**—the low [[Karman resonance|Karman drone]] beneath everything. They scatter. Katita and Leif descend.
