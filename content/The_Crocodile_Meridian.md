''The Crocodile Meridian'' covers the watering-hole and surveillance-drone sequences of [[Chapter 5]] of the novel, foregrounding [[Leif]]'s experience of [[the instant photographs|the drone's instant photographs]] as a collapse of identity. The volume's signature innovation is its treatment of [[Leif]]'s doubled (later, tenfold) vision: rather than using speed lines or transparency, illustrator ((██)) renders the scene in overlapping horizontal bands, each showing a different temporal or perspectival layer. The effect is nauseating, deliberate.

The centrepiece depicts the moment {{dialogue|Katita|(Leif. That is you.)}} — the revelation that the unrecognized man in the old photograph is [[Leif]] himself, caught in [[the cycle|an earlier loop]]. The manga stretches this scene across twelve pages, each band shifted slightly, forcing the reader to track which crocodile, which man, which moment is "real." The [[red]] diode of [[Leif]]'s [[the pacemaker|pacemaker]] appears in a different layer on each page.

Volume 13 was widely praised for rendering narrative impossibility as spatial problem rather than textual confusion. The [[Dirtheart]] activists briefly appear, masked and urgent, their dialogue replaced with the [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|frequency of breaking]].
