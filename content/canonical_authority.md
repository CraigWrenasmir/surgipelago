Canonical authority in Beach Surgery is radically paradoxical. The [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (novel)|novel]] is the sole finished, published source—it is singular and authoritative—yet it ''contains'' an unfinished story that cannot be completed in a single way. This structural incompleteness is canonical doctrine, not failure.

Every [[adaptation|adaptation]]—manga, opera, film, LARP, thesis, textile installation—attempts to resolve [[the glitch]], the unjoinable seam between the two narrative halves. Each resolution enters canon as ''simultaneously true and contradictory''. A 2023 Taiwanese film may depict [[Leif]] waking; a 2024 Lagos durational theatre may depict him still looping; a 2022 Argentine textile work may depict both. All are canonical. [[Canon conflicts]] are not errors to adjudicate but productive [[Antinomicity|antinomies]] to preserve.

[[Edit war]]s and [[Talk]] page disputes are part of the canon itself—managed tension, not pathology. [[Retro-causal apocrypha]]—works pre-dating 2020 or existing in parallel timelines—enter canon only if marked disputed, lost, or impossibly witnessed [cn]. Works by [[C. W. Smith]] outside the novel (''[[Pastoral Scanlines]]'', ''[[Antinomicity]]'', ''[[Everyone I Love is Alive in the Unlimited Present of the City and its Waters]]'') clarify motifs and [[instruments of return|recurring characters]] but do not override adaptation contradictions; they extend the [[C. W. Smith|oeuvre]], not the contained story.
