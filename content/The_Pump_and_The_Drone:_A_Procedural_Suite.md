'''"The Pump and The Drone: A Procedural Suite"''' is a nine-movement composition structured as a duet between two sonic motifs from [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight|Beach Surgery]]: the rhythmic pulse of [[Leif]]'s external [[the wings|pacemaker]] (the "pump") and the low-frequency drone of [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|the Karman line sound]] (the "drone").

Each movement explores a different harmonic and rhythmic relationship between these elements. Early movements present strict opposition; later movements collapse the boundary, rendering them indistinguishable. The fifth movement, ''The Correction,'' directly quotes [[Chapter Four (the interior)|the radar igloo sequence]], using frequency shifts to "correct" dissonance in real time. A coda reintroduces the two themes in perpetual, unresolvable counterpoint.

Instrumentation combines modular synthesis, heavily processed field recordings of {{quote|machinery, heartbeat, and atmospheric hum}}, and a live classical quartet. The work has premiered at ((██)) contemporary festivals across three continents. A score exists but remains unpublished; copies circulate in limited editions among researchers and performers. [cn]
