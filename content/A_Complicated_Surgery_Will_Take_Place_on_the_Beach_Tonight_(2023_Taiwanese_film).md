A 2023 experimental fiction film from an anonymous Taipei collective, adapting [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight]] as a hybrid of 35mm cinematography and live [[Taiwanese shadow-puppet tradition|piyingxi]] shadow-theatre.

The film stages [[Half One — Newcastle|Half One]] in desaturated colour 35mm as a noir-inflected meditation on an unnamed coastal East Asian city, replacing [[Newcastle]] with atmospheric urban geography. At the moment of dissolution into [[Half Two|Half Two]]—when "the scene dissolves into a desert hospital room"—the screen literally becomes a piyingxi stage: live puppeteers operate articulated silhouettes of [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] against backlit translucent vellum, framed within the cinema's proscenium. The puppet figures cast sharp mechanical shadows; their dialogues are sung in abstract vocal patterns without semantic language.

The glitch, in this reading, becomes the seam where [[cinema]] loses the body and becomes pure [[shadow]]. [[Leif]]'s [[the wings|wings]] erupt not as special effect but as the puppeteers' hands lifting the silhouettes skyward beyond the screen. [[Katita]]'s final refusal is the moment she steps back through the vellum into colour 35mm—a [[the catch that answers every fall|catch that restores the real]].
