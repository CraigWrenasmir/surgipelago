A twice-yearly participatory walk along [[Newcastle Beach]] and the [[Nobbys Breakwall|breakwall]], organized by local artists, that recreates [[Leif's three injuries|the three temporary injuries]] as embodied stations. Participants are guided barefoot or with bandaged eyes through stations marking [[cannot see|blindness]], [[cannot walk|immobility]], and [[heart out of whack|the arrested heartbeat]].

At the first station ([[Bar Beach|the lighthouse]]), guides blindfold participants and lead them across uneven ground, accompanied by [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|the low Kármán drone]]. At the second ([[Nobbys Breakwall|the breakwall's]] midpoint), participants sit while guides read passages from [[Chapter 2]] and [[Chapter 3]], feet submerged in tidal pools. The third station ([[Bogey Hole|the baths]]) involves lying still while guides place hands rhythmically on the chest — a tactile re-staging of the [[heart out of whack|external pacemaker]].

The walk circles back at sunset to a shared meal. Participants report a [[the cycle|cyclic sensation]] — the path returns to its beginning transformed. The piece stages [[the three injuries – and the three temptations|the three temptations]] as sensory rituals, mapping [[Half Two — The interior|the interior's]] structure onto the Newcastle coast.
