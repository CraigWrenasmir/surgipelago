A philosophical boundary recurring across Beach Surgery adaptations: frequencies—infrasonic and ultrasonic—that lie beyond human auditory perception. Where [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space]] is a nauseating low drone at the edge of silence, the inverse names high frequencies the flesh cannot receive: electromagnetic broadcasts in the kilohertz, the [[radio igloo]] transmissions that strip away human hearing to correct [[Leif]]'s doubled vision. A paradox of rescue: healing the spine by frequencies the spine refuses to hear.

In [[audio-drama]] and durational [[immersive]] works, the motif becomes a threshold between body and void. The [[radio igloo and temporal loops|radio igloo sequence]] depends on this logic—sound above human range, inaudible yet operative, healing yet untouched by sensation. [[Katita]] would say the frequencies are already there, broadcasting always; the flesh learns only later it was never listening.

A suite of [[olfactory]] and [[binaural]] installation works treats this boundary as sacred covenant. [[Frequencies the Spine Refuses—A Binaural + Olfactory Installation|Frequencies the Spine Refuses]] (Tel Aviv, 2024–present) uses bone-conduction transducers to transmit vibrations the ear cannot isolate. The work offers no resolution—the unheard broadcast continues, unreceivable, a permanent witness to [[refusal]].

Some theologies read [[the glitch]] itself as a frequency the flesh cannot articulate: a dissonance in narrative logic unmappable to human language.
