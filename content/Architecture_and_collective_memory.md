In Beach Surgery adaptation, buildings are not preserved but *inhabited*. Architecture holds [[the glitch]] because, like the novel itself, structures are always already incomplete—windows that refuse to stay still, staircases that loop, thresholds that mark [[the seam]].

== Newcastle origin-sites ==
[[Bolton Street car park]] functions as both literal setting (Chapter 1: ''[[the parallel wires]]'') and psychogeographic origin. [[The Circuit at Nobbys (Site-Specific Participatory Walk)|Site-specific walking tours]] re-enact [[Leif]]'s and [[Katita]]'s rooftop parkour; the [[breakwall]] becomes pilgrimage destination. Beneath [[King Edward Park]], the [[The Radio Igloo|radio igloo]] transforms militarized infrastructure into [[the threshold|threshold]] space—a place where [[the frequencies that straighten|frequency correction]] occurs, and time loops back on itself [cn].

== Market and shrine architecture ==
In [[Addis Ababa]], [[The Merkato Meridian—Olfactory Icon Panels|the Merkato's labyrinthine stalls]] become [[the archive]] through participatory olfactory cycling. [[The Leather Archives—A Community-Built Spiral Shrine in Reclaimed Warehouse Space|Accra's leather shrine]] in a reclaimed warehouse is deliberately dismantled and rebuilt each seven-year cycle, refusing to calcify into museum. [[Broken Hill|Rural Australian medical sites]] function as waystation-shrines along [[Seven Stations of the Caught Descent|pilgrimage routes]].

== Brutalist durational ==
In [[Dar es Salaam]], [[Ankara]], and [[Tbilisi]], post-Soviet and colonial brutalist monuments are retrofitted as ''durational listening chambers'' and collective vigil-spaces. These are not restored but inhabited: [[The Scaffold Dome (Immersive Theatre)|temporary dome structures]] erupt and dissolve; walls receive fresh [[red redacted]] paint; pilgrims spend nights in the [[The Threshold Cannot Hold|threshold]] space, bearing witness [cn].

The principle: collective memory resides not in preservation but in the shared, infinite refusal to finish.
