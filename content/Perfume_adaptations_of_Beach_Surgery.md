**Perfume and scent-based adaptations** emerged as a distinct strand from 2019 onward, treating [[Surgery = three heartbeats; Beach = one heartbeat|surgery as olfactory intimacy]] and the cycle as a progression of chemical frequencies the body can register rather than hear. Three categories predominate:

**Durational Scent Installations** present adaptation as temporal journey through fragrance. [[Red Leather Triage—A Scent Installation]] (Lagos, 2023–present) stages five chambers mirroring [[Katita]]'s color-progression: arterial red, rust-red, clay-red, blood-red, shadow-red. Visitors move through smell-time rather than narrative time. [[Sawt al-Ardh—The Sound the Earth Refuses]] (Marrakech, 2020–present) inverts the pattern, encoding the [[Kármán line]] as progressively inaudible top-notes, translating the boundary-frequency into scent only the [[human spine|spine's resonance]] can theoretically detect.

**Participatory Olfactory LARP** treats fragrance as consent and [[faith without sight]]. [[Dhupo Dosro—The Second Breath]] (Bhaktapur, 2021–ongoing) asks participants to wear unmarked fragrances during ritual walking; scent functions as blindfold. [[The Kármán Whisper—A Synaesthetic Olfactory LARP]] (Tbilisi, 2021–ongoing) pairs breath-counting with complex molecular sequences, staging [[the three injuries]] as chemical temptations.

**Wearable Singular Perfumes** include [[Meridian of the Unhealed (Fragrance, 2021)]]—one formula, identical for all wearers, yet shifts across the day as each body's chemistry evolves. Adaptation here is not many solutions but one solution [[Instruments of Return|returning]] differently in every body.
