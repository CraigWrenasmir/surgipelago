A lineage of [[Beach Surgery franchise|Beach Surgery]] [[adaptation|adaptations]] that deliberately stage sensory conflict rather than synthesis. Where conventional multimedia design seeks synesthetic harmony, these works exploit the [[analogy|analogical]] principle embedded in the novel: "Consciousness is an analogy of itself"—consciousness arises *only* from irreducible discontinuity between parallel experiences.

Works in this constellation pair incompatible sensory registers. [[Frequencies the Spine Refuses—A Binaural + Olfactory Installation (Tel Aviv, 2024–present)]] combines a binaural recording of [[the Kármán line|the boundary between earth and space]] (inaudible to the ear, visible only as pressure) with a heavy incense designed to block olfactory reception entirely. [[The Kármán Whisper—A Synaesthetic Olfactory LARP (Tbilisi, 2021–ongoing)]] sequences [[the spine's high-pitched resonance|spine-resonance tones]] (D or G, depending on sex/body size) with scents that atomise in conflicting directions. [[The Heartbeat Rice—A Rhythmic Meal Cycle in West Bengal]] serves dishes whose texture contradicts their flavor, while a rhythm track plays at the precise inverse ratio of the diner's heart-rate.

The shared argument: [[the glitch]]—the unseam between the novel's halves—is not a narrative failure but a sensory fact. Each adaptation maps one sensory impossibility onto another. No fusion occurs. The analogy persists only in the rupture itself.
