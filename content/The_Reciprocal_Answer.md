A novella-length exploration of [[Leif]]'s climactic [[the 12-word question|twelve-word question]] on the [[the rocket cart|rocket cart]], revealing three possible versions of both the question and [[Katita]]'s answer—all three simultaneously true. In ''First Loop'', [[Leif]] asks: {{dialogue|Leif|If we break the cycle, will you still love me?}} [[Katita]]'s answer is silence. In ''Second Loop'', [[Leif]] asks: {{dialogue|Leif|How did you choose me?}} and [[Katita]] replies: {{dialogue|Katita|I chose the moment before I saw you.}} In ''Third Loop'', [[Leif]] asks: {{dialogue|Leif|Am I still drowning in the waves from the cliff?}} and [[Katita]] answers: {{dialogue|Katita|Yes.}} The novella does not resolve which is true; instead the narrative spirals through all three, with [[Leif]]'s [[the wings|wings]] growing not at the end but throughout—he is slowly becoming something else, something that exists between questions. The [[desert interior]] multiplies: the same stretch of road contains all three rocket carts, all three [[Leif|Leifs]]. The work refuses [[the glitch|the glitch]] and instead [[Katita|Katita]] ''becomes'' the glitch—the irreparable seam made conscious.
