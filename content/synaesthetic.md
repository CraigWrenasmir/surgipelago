Synaesthetic adaptations of Beach Surgery cross sensory thresholds—sound made visible, scent rendered as choreography, touch translated into frequency. These works treat the story's core motifs ([[the sound of the earth rubbing against space]], [[Frequencies the Spine Cannot Hear]], the [[red]] thread) as transducers between modalities, each sense a translation of the others.

[[The Frequencies the Flesh Refuses—A Binaural + Olfactory Installation|The Frequencies the Flesh Refuses]] (Tel Aviv, 2024–present) pairs stereo recordings of the [[the radio igloo|radio igloo]] scene with custom scent releases keyed to [[Leif]]'s heart-rate shifts. Visitors enter blind, guided only by breath and smell, re-enacting his [[cannot see|blindness]]. The olfactory score shifts between [[cochineal]] (red), [[iodine]] (pacemaker's metallic resonance), and [[eucalyptus]] (New South Wales interior), collapsing taste, touch, and vision into a single durational event.

Earlier works include [[The Karmán Whisper—A Synaesthetic Olfactory LARP|The Karmán Whisper]] (Tbilisi, 2021–ongoing), which maps the spine's high-pitched resonance as a walking path through scent stations. No two performances smell identical; the archive's iteration records are misfiled as tree-antenna frequency: disputed. [cn]
