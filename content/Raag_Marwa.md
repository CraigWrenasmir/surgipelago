[[Raag Marwa]] is a [[Hindustani classical music|Hindustani]] [[raga|raag]] characterized by pentatonic ascent and distinctive [[gamaka]] ornamentations in the upper register. Its intervallic emphasis—on ''komal-rishab'', ''shuddha-madhyam'', and ''komal-dhaivat''—creates an effect of ascending tension and spiritual rupture. In [[Beach Surgery]] Indian musical adaptations, Raag Marwa became the signature form for transitions between [[Half One — Newcastle|Half One]] and [[Half Two — The interior|Half Two]], its rising arc mirroring [[Leif|Leif's]] [[the wings|eruption of wings]]. [[Sangat Frequency—The Five Voices|Vocal-ensemble cycles]] (Jaipur, Kolkata) and [[Kathak Cycle of the Three Injuries|Kathak dance interpretations]] (Bhaktapur, 2021) treat the raag as [[Leif]]'s [[Leif's doubled vision|internal signal]], the moment his [[Leif's heart out of whack|external pacemaker]] falls away and [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|the spine's frequency]] takes over. [cn] The raag does not resolve; it ascends and breaks.
