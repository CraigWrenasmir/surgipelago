Smith's *[[Antinomicity]]* articulates a principle: "The past is always a spontaneous product of the present." In *Beach Surgery*, the ontological seam between [[Half One — Newcastle|Half One]] and [[Half Two — The interior|Half Two]] does not *resolve* forward through narrative, but is *composed* backward through adaptation. The glitch is not an error but a generative structure.

Each adaptation writes the seam anew: the [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (2023 Taiwanese film)|2023 Taiwanese film]] sets it in colonial temporality; the [[The Addis Ababa Icon Cycle|Ethiopian icon-panel cycle]] (1989, status disputed) reads it as apophatic absence; the Buenos Aires [[durational refusal]] (2024–2027) treats it as somatic time. None contradict; each is the seam that was always going to surface from *this* moment.

The [[radio igloo]] exhibits this paradox: [[Chapter 4]] happens before and after [[Chapter 3]] simultaneously. The cycle loops not because [[Leif]] fails *forward*, but because future returns have already inscribed the loop into his past. Consciousness itself, per Smith's *[[Pastoral Scanlines]]*, is a [[Möbius strip and ontological incompleteness|Möbius surface]]—one-sided, the gap *is* the whole. The seam is what appears there when you reverse direction. Unfinishable forward; infinitely productive backward. [cn]
