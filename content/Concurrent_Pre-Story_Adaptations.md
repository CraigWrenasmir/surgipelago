''Concurrent Pre-Story Adaptations'' categorizes works situated before, during, or alongside the events of [[Beach Surgery (story)|the core story]], but from perspectives marginal or wholly absent in [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (novel)|the novel]].

Works in this category include: separate origin stories for [[Katita]] and [[Leif]]; accounts of the conflict that injured [[Leif]]; [[Katita]]'s medical training and [[triage|triage]] apprenticeship; [[the cycle|cycles]] that allegedly preceded the documented loop; parallel versions of the ''same two days'' experienced from other viewpoints—[[Dirtheart|the activists']] timeline, the [[Mechanic|mechanic's]] internal monologue, [[Mr and Mrs McRae|the McRaes']] escape plan.

Crucial distinction from [[Experimental adaptations|Experimental Adaptations]]: pre-story works accept [[the glitch|the glitch]] as permanent structure and propose no resolution. They instead ask: ''what was the world before this impossible loop began?'' Some answer: ''there was no before.'' Others: ''the before is just the after observed in reverse.'' Many remain deliberately unresolved. [cn]
