The rocket cart is a [[vehicle]] constructed by [[Leif]] overnight in [[the cabin]], from rust-locked metal, diesel fuel, and materials salvaged across the [[desert interior]]. Its assembly occurs in parallel with [[Katita]]'s stitching of the [[leather armour]] — two acts of preparation for [[the cycle|the cycle's]] termination, each object containing within it the sediment of previous loops.

In the novel, Leif works "with the gravity of someone building a memorial." The cart becomes his final instrument of locomotion before [[the wings]] erupt from his shoulder-blades and he achieves momentary [[flight imagery across adaptations|flight]] at sunrise. Whether the cart falls, flies, or dissolves at that instant remains [[Adaptations that resolve the glitch|one of the franchise's central interpretive fault lines]].

[[the novel|In Smith's prose]], the cart is never explicitly described as functional — only as "assembled." Adaptations have taken this incompleteness as generative: the [[Japanese anime]] expands its construction into a three-episode arc; a [[tabletop|participatory LARP]] (Adelaide, 2019) required teams to build working rocket carts from scrap metal, mirroring Leif's salvage labour. Several [[opera|operatic]] works stage the assembly as a duet between builder and armourer, the two forms of care.

The cart's material — rust, reclaimed, unreliable — echoes the [[data-harvesters|autonomous machines]] that pursue them and the [[the radio igloo|radio igloo's]] jury-rigged corrective frequencies. All three are [[synthesised nature|humanity's broken instruments]] at the threshold of the [[interior]].
