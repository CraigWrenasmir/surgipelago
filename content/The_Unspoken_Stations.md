'''The Unspoken Stations''' is a durational overnight [[LARP]] work and participatory ritual installation that debuted in 2021. Participants (called "witnesses") undertake a self-paced nocturnal pilgrimage through seven meditative stations, each corresponding to a threshold, injury, or moment of recurrence drawn from [[Beach Surgery]]. The work operates on a core proposition: {{quote|Leif and Katita are not recycled characters. They are instruments of return|from ''[[Surplus of the Seen]]''}} — participants attend not as spectators but as witnesses to a pattern that loops eternally.

The structure emphasizes [[The Doctrine of Refusal|refusal]] and somatic engagement over narrative. No predetermined story is imposed; instead, each station offers audio cues, sensory prompts, and ritual actions designed to activate the participant's own encounter with [[the cycle]]. Most stations employ darkness, silence, isolation, and binaural sound; some include textile work, movement, or spatial vertigo.

== The Seven Stations ==

Stations proceed in rough sequence but participants choose their pacing (typically 45–90 min per station):

* '''Station 1 — Arrival & Blindfold''': Threshold; blindfolded entrance; introduction to [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space]] via binaural headphones; consent check
* '''Station 2 — The Double-Vision Maze''': Mirror installations or kaleidoscopic corridors; experience of [[ten layered versions]]; recognition of the mechanic's face-shifting
* '''Station 3 — Pressure at the Shoulder-Blades''': Somatic work; participants lie supine or stand; gentle pressure applied; meditation on [[Leif]]'s anticipatory tension before [[the wings]] erupt
* '''Station 4 — The Radio Igloo''': Enclosed space with corrective frequency broadcasting; temporal loop meditation; the frequency that straightens doubled vision
* '''Station 5 — The Parallel Wires''': Rope or cable suspended at shoulder height; balance walk; sensation of the thin boundary between halves; rooftop vertigo
* '''Station 6 — The Leather Distributed''': Textile handling; participants touch, fold, and arrange leather scraps, kente cloth, or fabric fragments while listening to oral testimony about the [[armor from the leather]]
* '''Station 7 — The Catch at Dawn''': Collective arrival point; dawn vigil; group breath-count synchronized to the pattern; closing ritual affirming [[the cycle]] returns

== Locations ==

'''Nairobi, Kenya''' (2021–ongoing): [[The Caught Descent]] umbrella organization runs nightly tours departing from a (((██████ ██████))) site in Kibera. Swahili, English, and Dinka languages mixed; oral tellings of [[Rico the Architect]] adapted to Nairobi's rapid-build housing and informal architecture. Free entry; organizers accept donations. Approximately 200–300 witnesses per month.

'''Addis Ababa, Ethiopia''' (2022–present): Organized by [[The Merkato Meridian|the Merkato Meridian Collective]]; monthly cycles (usually full-moon to new-moon). Route incorporates [[Tigulet Mariam]], the Merkato district, and Addis Ababa Museum. Amharic, English, and Ge'ez liturgical chanting at Station 7. [ref:merkato|"The Unspoken Stations in Addis Ababa: One Year Report," internal document, 2023.]

'''Tbilisi, Georgia''' (2023–present): An adaptation featuring polyphonic singing (drawing on [[Georgian polyphonic singing]] tradition) at Stations 4 and 7. Co-hosted by (((████████))); quarterly cycles in spring and autumn.

'''Newcastle, Australia''' (2021–ongoing): Departs [[Gregson Park & the Glass House|Gregson Park]] at dusk; moves through the city proper (Bolton Street, the harbour foreshore, [[The Ruins of the pub with oak tree]]), reaching the coast at sunrise for Station 7. Nightly, weather permitting. Approximately 50–100 participants per night; often reaches capacity.

'''Kinshasa, Democratic Republic of Congo''' (2023–2025): [[The Kinshasa Descent]] variant organized by (((████████))); status disputed. One confirmed run documented in July 2024 (photographs, witness testimony) but no formal announcement from organizers. [cn]

== Reception & Critique ==

Supporters view The Unspoken Stations as exemplifying [[The Doctrine of Refusal|refusal]] as spiritual practice — a collective commitment to remain in the wound without healing it. Scholars have linked it to [[durational performance]] theory ([[Tania Bruguera]], [[Theaster Gates]]) and [[apophatic theology|apophatic contemplative practice]].

Critics have raised concerns about: (1) appropriation of Indigenous nocturnal ritual frameworks without formal acknowledgment [cn]; (2) the unmarked authority of guides and curators in shaping "witness"; (3) whether the work risks aestheticizing [[the cycle]] rather than resisting it materially.
