An immersive installation occupying the ground floor of a converted warehouse in Wickham (a Newcastle satellite) designed as a series of interconnected rooms in which language has been systematically erased. Visitors enter through a threshold marked only by a single redaction bar: ██.

Seven chambers, each staging a facet of [[Beach Surgery]] through absence. The first: blown-up manuscript pages with all text obscured except [[red (motif)|red]] motifs—"red hair," "red heels," "red desert," the remainder ██. The second: a reconstruction of the [[radio igloo]], all frequencies emitting only static; dials produce no differentiation. The third: the [[twelve-word question]] as a sound installation—twelve isolated syllables in different voices, non-sequentially looped, never achieving consecutive order.

Later chambers present [[Katita]]'s armor as leather scraps under glass; the [[rocket cart]] as an outline on a bare floor; a recording of the [[preschool]] scene with dialogue redacted, only breathing audible. The final chamber—deliberately unfinished—overlooks an estuary, with a placard: "What [[Katita]] refuses to say. (Watch.)"

No narration. No explanatory text. Visitors construct meaning from structural rupture. Debate persists: does ''the redacted coast'' comment on [[the glitch]] (linguistic incompleteness as mirroring narrative fissure) or perform it? The installation's own archival disappearance mirrors this refusal of resolution. A single surviving video document circulates in fandom networks. [cn]
