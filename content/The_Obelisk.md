'''The Obelisk''' is a real location in [[Newcastle]], New South Wales, named across the written works of [[C. W. Smith]]. The Obelisk, The Hill. It is plotted on the [[Newcastle#The Literary Atlas of Newcastle|Literary Atlas of Newcastle]] among the city's coastal sites.

== Across the works ==

=== A Complicated Surgery… ===
In a dream the obelisk lifts off its hill and flies down to impale the doughnut-shaped council building.
