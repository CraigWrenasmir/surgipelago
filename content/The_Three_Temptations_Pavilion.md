The Three Temptations Pavilion is a brutalist concrete meditation structure in central [[Yerevan]], Armenia, completed in 2023 by the collective ((██)) in collaboration with the [[Yerevan Biennale]]. The installation stages the [[the three temptations — and the three injuries|three injuries-as-temptations]] of [[Leif]]'s narrative as three sequentially-encountered chamber spaces.

**The Chamber of Mystery** (''mystery'' / bandaged eyes): Visitors enter blindfolded. They walk a path of crushed gravel and shallow water channels in absolute silence. A [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|Kármán-frequency resonance]] plays at imperceptible pitch [cn]. Exit remains blind.

**The Chamber of Miracle** (''miracle'' / cannot walk): A rope-ladder ascent to a suspended platform 3.5 meters above a reflecting pool. A pre-recorded breathing cycle plays at variable tempos. Visitors stand at the platform's edge.

**The Chamber of Authority** (''authority'' / pacemaker): The floor pulses hydraulically in a heartbeat rhythm that accelerates unpredictably. Visitors attempt synchrony or resistance.

All chambers' walls bear fragments of Armenian liturgical text and [[Dostoevsky]] quotations in multiple languages. No marked exit; visitors discover their own escape route. The pavilion runs through 2025. Technical recordings of the heartbeat sequence include unexplained broadband interference [cn]—data-stream noise at approximately 300 bits per second, source unidentified.
