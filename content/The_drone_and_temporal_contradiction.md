The ''drone photograph paradox'' is one of the franchise's most persistently contested interpretive moments. In [[Chapter Five]], [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] discover an abandoned cloud-shaped [[surveillance drone]] filled with years of [[instant photographs]]. Among them: [[Katita]] standing beside a man [[Leif]] does not recognize. The caption reads: {{quote|Leif. That is you.}}

The contradiction: if the photograph predates the narrative's beginning, [[Leif]] should remember it. If it postdates, the drone's archive should not yet exist. A third possibility—that the photograph documents a previous [[the cycle|cycle]] iteration—contradicts the novel's framing that ''this'' meeting is their first. [cn]

Adaptations resolve the paradox differently. [[Shanbudia Animation Studio|Shanbudia anime]] (2018) proposes [[temporal recursion]]: the drone documents futures collapsing backward into narrative present. [[O Procedimento]] (Brazilian film, 2019) invents [[identity slippage|identity slippage]]: the man is and is not [[Leif]]. ''[[The Reconstruction Protocol (tabletop game)|Reconstruction Protocol]]'' lets players vote on what the archive will have retroactively recorded. [ref:karman|''Monthly Karman Line'', Issue 7 (2023): "The Unmending Photograph."]
