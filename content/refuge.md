'''Refuge''' is a recurring 72-hour participatory LARP cycle in which players collectively construct and defend a temporary sanctuary space against an unseen, unnamed threat—a direct staging of the [[empty world]] motif and the hunter who pursues [[Leif]] and [[Katita]].

Participants arrive in an abandoned or liminal site (disused hospital, converted warehouse, rural farmstead) and are given only supplies: food, water, first-aid kits, a map. One facilitator embodies the [[unnamed pursuer]]—never fully seen, only felt through sound design, pressure, the sense of approach—and players must choose: do we flee? Do we hide? Do we '''refuse to move again'''?

The cycle unfolds in three stages:
* '''Breaking''' (Hours 0–24)—Players establish the refuge, bar doors, share stories of what hunts them.
* '''Holding''' (Hours 24–48)—The pursuer's presence intensifies; agreed protocols fragment; some participants leave; the refuge destabilizes into fear or solidarity.
* '''Return''' (Hours 48–72)—Players reconvene in darkness and choose: '''recommit''' (singing, synchronized breathing; restarting the cycle) or '''refuse''' (remaining motionless until the facilitator withdraws; 4–6 hour vigil).

Most documented cycles end in the "refuse" position. Some restart within hours.[cn] Recorded testimony from participants describes the experience as "simultaneously suffocating and liberating: we learned that sanctuary is not escape but choosing to stop."[ref:refuge_1|''Refuge Cycle: Nairobi 2023'' documentation; participant anonymized.|] Theological reading within [[Beach Surgery]] devotional communities has begun identifying the [[unnamed pursuer]] as a personification of '''the cycle itself'''—what hunts you ''is'' what you refuse to escape.
