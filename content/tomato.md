'''Tomato''' appears across [[Beach Surgery]] [[Adaptation|adaptations]] as a recurring element, though its significance remains contested. It emerges consistently in:

* Regional [[Adaptation|adaptations]] centred on South Asian and Middle Eastern food practices—particularly in [[Lahore Frequency Walk—An Audio-Locative Pilgrimage|durational food-ritual works]] and [[O Corpo Que Canta—A Brazilian Collaborative Cooking Archive and Oral History|participatory meal cycles]].
* Visual adaptations, where the red fruit appears on tables, in gardens, cut and seeping (connecting to the [[Red (motif)|red motif]] through [[Katita]]'s chromatic signature).
* [[Opera|operatic]] works, where tomato sauce or tomato-stained cloth appears without narrative explanation—a [[Red Leather Triage—A Scent Installation|scent-world detail]].

Whether tomato functions as symbol (sustenance, growth, the colour of blood and healing), ritual object, or accidental recurrence remains unclear. Some [[Fan theories and extended readings|fandom theories]] link it to [[the cycle|cyclical ripening and return]]; others suggest it entered the franchise through one regional adaptation and persisted through [[Adaptation|cultural cross-pollination]].
