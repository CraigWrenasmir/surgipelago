The desert. [[Leif]] drives across **[[red|red sand]]** with [[Katita]] silent beside him, a medical box in the backseat. The horizon crawls with [[Autonomous diesel data-harvesters|data-harvesters]]—vast machines resembling **locusts of rust and light**, bodies bristling with cooling fins and solar panels, moving with animal hunger herding something invisible. At a [[service station]], [[Leif]] encounters the **mechanic**, who is also the police officer from [[Newcastle]]—the same face and voice but displaced in time. The episode visualizes this as **ten overlaid silhouettes**, each frame sliding behind the last. {{dialogue|Mechanic|You've been here before.|}} {{dialogue|Leif|I know.|}} The data-harvesters close. In the distance rises **[[the radio igloo]]**, a dome of corrugated metal bristling with aerials, humming with the [[Karman resonance|Karman frequency]]. [[Katita]] watches [[Leif]]'s shoulder blades. The pressure in them builds. A single red diode blinks on his chest.
