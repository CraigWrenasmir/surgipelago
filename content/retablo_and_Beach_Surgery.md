The Peruvian ''retablo'' — a hand-carved narrative tableau in a wooden box — has become a primary vehicle for [[Andean adaptations of Beach Surgery|Andean retellings]] of the franchise. The form's structural affinity with the [[the glitch|glitch]] makes it generatively aligned with adaptation's core problem.

== Formal Parallels ==

A traditional retablo stages multiple figures within shallow carved space, often in three planes. The viewer peers into the box as into memory. In [[Beach Surgery]] retablos, each box typically represents one [[Chapter|chapter]], though ambitious cycles compress all seven loops into densely layered compartments. The open-faced wooden frame — which allows dust and light to age the work — mirrors narrative incompleteness at the [[the glitch|glitch's]] heart. The retablo cannot be sealed; it is always breathing.

== Color and Surgery ==

Red cinnabar and cochineal dominate: [[Katita]]'s [[red (motif)|red hair]], [[red kitten heels]], [[red desert]] dust, the first-aid cross. The retablo's traditional red for sacred wounds aligns with the [[Surgical Intimacy|surgical intimacy]] of the narrative. In some cycles, each retablo is a surgical field; the viewer's gaze is the scalpel.

== Participatory Cycles ==

Recent work — particularly [[La Cicatriz Que Huele—Retablo Boxes of Ayacucho]] — invites community craftspeople to add to existing retablos, creating ongoing collaborative composition. This transforms the retablo from ''object'' into ''process'', echoing [[the cycle|eternal return]].
