The '''medical box''' is introduced in [[Chapter 4 (the interior)|Chapter 4]] when [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] depart the [[the cabin|cabin]] for the interior. It is described as containing "medical supplies" necessary for [[Katita]]'s triage work across the desert. [ref:ch4|Beach Surgery, Part Two, Chapter 4.]

Its exact contents are never enumerated in the original [[Beach Surgery (story)|outline]]. Fan theorists have argued extensively whether it contains only first-aid materials or whether it holds instruments for more extensive [[the surgery|surgery]].[ref:theory|See [[The Reciprocal]] (fan theory) on dual-purpose medical apparatus.] The box survives the chaos of [[Chapter 6]] and is present in the cycle reset: {{dialogue|Katita|places the hand cannon in his lap, presses the heat-sharpened [[the sword|pipe-blade]] into a sword, and resets}} — implying the box traveled through the final scenes intact.

Adaptations diverge sharply on the box's contents. The [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (manga)|manga]] depicts it containing surgical tools; the [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (TV series)|TV series]] leaves it deliberately off-screen. One Russian [[LARP|LARP adaptation]] made the box's inventory the core puzzle.
