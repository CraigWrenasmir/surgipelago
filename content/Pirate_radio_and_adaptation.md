''Pirate radio and adaptation'' documents the emergence of unlicensed radio as a primary adaptation medium, beginning circa 2021–22 in [[Lagos]], [[Dakar]], and [[Nairobi]], and spreading across South Asia and Australia.

Broadcast collectives—typically 3–5 people operating from mobile or rooftop transmitters—serialized the [[the embedded story|embedded narrative]] live and in local languages: [[Yoruba]], [[Wolof]], [[Swahili]], [[Tagalog]]. The choice of unauthorized broadcast was deliberate: [[the cycle|the cycle's]] infinite looping, [[frequency|frequency's]] resistance to fixed meaning, and the [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|Kármán line's]] resonance all mapped onto the unregulated electromagnetic spectrum.

== Format ==

* ''Serial adaptations'': 15–30 minute nightly episodes, often focused on [[Chapter 4 (7:30am→2:30pm)|Chapter 4]] or [[the radio igloo and temporal loops|the radio igloo]], with local modifications.
* ''Frequency loops'': the [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|low Kármán drone]] mixed with [[the spine's high-pitched resonance|human spine frequencies]], broadcast on repeat.
* ''Call-in participation'': listeners phoned in or sent voice messages incorporated live, dissolving the boundary between [[Dirtheart Activists|activism]] and narrative.
* ''Tape-delayed re-broadcasts'': older broadcasts re-transmitted out of sequence, creating temporal contradiction and [[narrative refusal|refusal of chronology]].

A seminal recovery: [[The Dakar Frequency Archive (Lost 1986 Pirate Radio Cycle, Recovered 2024)|The Dakar Frequency Archive]] (1986, dates disputed), reconstructed from cassette fragments and listener accounts, featured a [[Senegalese pirate radio|Senegalese ensemble]] performing a Wolof-language adaptation in which [[Katita]] became a griot-healer and [[the radio igloo|the radio igloo]] itself was the pirate station—a broadcast within broadcast, {{quote|the glitch rendered as signal loss.}}.

== Aesthetics ==

Pirate adaptations treated transmission degradation—signal loss, interference, accidental rebroadcasting—as aesthetic features rather than failure. The threat of jamming or shutdown modeled [[the cycle|the cycle's]] perpetual precarity. Several broadcasts incorporated [[lost media|archived fragments]] of seemingly older broadcasts (pre-2020 dates disputed) that appeared to reference the story before the novel existed, raising questions about whether [[retro-causal apocrypha|the past was being spontaneously composed by present reception]].

== Community ==

Broadcast collectives often functioned as [[devotional communities and Beach Surgery|devotional communities]], with listeners gathering at designated times to receive transmissions communally. Some constructed [[Leather Archives—A Community-Built Spiral Shrine|installations and shrines]] around broadcast times. Participants treated the broadcasts as [[Liturgical practice and Beach Surgery|liturgy]]—[[Doctrine of Refusal—Sacred Incompleteness as Ontological Structure|refusal, silence, and eternal return]] as sacramental.

== Archive and loss ==

Many broadcasts were jammed or ceased transmitting. Documentation is fragmentary: cassettes, listener diaries, [[Dakar Frequency Archive|frequency logs]], oral testimony. [[Surgipelago]] has begun recovering transcripts, though [[canonical authority|authenticity remains contested]] [cn].
