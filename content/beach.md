The '''beach''' is the central geographic and symbolic location in *[[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight]]*, appearing twice: as the harbor-exit in Chapter 3 (Half One) and as the climactic site of [[The cycle|the cycle]]'s final turn in Chapter 6 (Half Two). Leif and Katita reach it by swimming through the [[The underground stone swimming pool|underground stone pool]]'s harbor connection, where the [[The mechanical seagull|mechanical seagull]] snaps them back to the surface—a [[circle of life|circle of life]] reversal.

In the novel's frame, the beach embodies [[Eternal recurrence / breaking the cycle|eternal recurrence]]: a place where [[Katita]] has engineered [[Leif]] to return, where he asks his [[The twelve-word question|twelve-word question]], where [[Leif|the wings]] erupt and he falls. Adaptations worldwide treat the beach as the unjoinable threshold between [[The glitch|the glitch]]'s two halves. Some read it as a literal delivery room (the "complicated surgery" is birth); others as a psychological boundary where Leif's [[The three injuries — and the three temptations|three temptations]] collapse. The beach is where [[The cycle|the cycle]] ''attempts'' to break and fails, every time.
