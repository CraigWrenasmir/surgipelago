Ankara—the planned capital of the Turkish Republic, founded 1923—has become a major node in the global Beach Surgery adaptation network, particularly in experimental theatre and brutalist-architecture performance. The city's 1960s–1980s mass-concrete urban form creates what fandom terms [[Newcastle vanishing-architecture hypothesis|ontological incompleteness]]: buildings that recede from consciousness when unobserved; neighborhoods that loop back upon themselves.

Key works include [[Sırtlar Spirali — The Shoulder-Blade Spiral|Sırtlar Spirali]] (2018–2024), a monumental shoulder-blade-shaped brutalist spiral in reinforced concrete, and [[The Shoulder-Blade Sanctuary—An Improvised Brutalist Meditation Igloo in Ankara (2023–2024)|The Shoulder-Blade Sanctuary]] (2023–2024), an ephemeral igloo of carved concrete blocks for weekly group meditation on [[the three temptations]] and [[Leif's doubled vision|Leif's doubled-eye]] motif.

Turkish-language adaptations cluster in experimental theatre; the city hosts annual symposia on [[ontological incompleteness]] and [[sacred incompleteness]]. Ankara's status as "second city" mirrors the novel's desert interior as "second half"—often neglected, endlessly reinterpreted.
