In the radio igloo deep in the desert, Leif's vision multiplies—ten versions of the mechanic overlap before him, each flickering in and out of focus. Katita, observing the pressure building in his shoulder blades, raises the broadcast frequency incrementally. His spine responds with a high-pitched resonance: the human scream transformed into pure tone. The pursuing robots fall silent, unable to hear beyond their range. For a fragile moment, Leif sees singular—one mechanic, one world, one heartbeat. Katita whispers {{dialogue|Katita|The robots can't hear what the spine can hear.}} But the unity shatters. The frequency drops; multiplicity floods back. The mechanic grins at the threshold, knowledge of the cycle written in his face. Katita's hand moves to Leif's shoulder blade, feeling for growth—[[the wings]] already gathering beneath the skin.
