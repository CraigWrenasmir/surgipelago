Beach Surgery manga adaptations form one of the largest and most regionally diverse production strands. The primary serialization, [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (manga)|the Nakamura Shoin series]], began 2021 and continues; supporting it are standalone volumes, [[doujinshi]] collections, regional retellings, and experimental formats that resist linear narrative.

Notable sequences include [[The Dust Garden (manga volume)|The Dust Garden]] (kente-textile hybrid, Ghana/diaspora artist collective, ink-and-indigo on hand-loomed cloth); [[The Boar's Return (manga volume)]], a Filipino komiks with distinctive wayang-influenced panel composition; the ongoing [[The Radio Igloo Whispers (manga volume)|Radio Igloo]] series (South Korea, centering [[Frequencies the Robots Cannot Hear|sonic correction]] and doubled vision); and [[The Pacemaker Confesses (Illustrated Doujinshi)]], a limited-run exploration of consciousness as Möbius recursion.

Cross-cultural formal experimentation is endemic: vertical-scroll manga adapted to Indonesian [[wayang]] narrative traditions; [[Arabic calligraphic manuscripts|Arabic calligraphic]] dialogue panels interwoven with ink-wash scenes; [[Ethiopian icon-panel cycles|Ethiopian icon-painting]] compositions framing [[Katita]]'s meditation sequences. Many editions remain untranslated, circulating only in [[Comiket|festival archives]] and collector networks [cn]. The [[Comiket|grassroots fan-convention circuit]] generates as much material as official publication.
