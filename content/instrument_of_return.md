**instrument of return** is a foundational concept in C. W. Smith's oeuvre and central to understanding the Beach Surgery franchise's recursive logic. The phrase refers to [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] not as fixed characters but as **recursive instruments**—transmigrating entities that surface across different works, embodiments, and media, each time addressing the same structural impossibility from a new angle.

Smith's own formulation (from [[Pastoral Scanlines]], 2025) explicitly rejects the idea of "recycled characters": *"They are instruments of return."* This means Leif and Katita are not simply reused; they function as an irreducible *apparatus*—two poles of consciousness separated by an irreparable gap ([[Möbius strip|the Möbius gap]], [[the glitch]]) through which the same narrative problem perpetually rotates.

Across Smith's corpus, they recur renamed and transformed:
* As lovers in [[Leaving/Leading]] (poem), dissolving into Newcastle's geography
* As settlers in [[Summer Endzone]] (prose-poem), post-pastoral and grieving
* As underwater embracers in [[Saltando]] (prose), where identity liquefies at the baths
* As ideologue and pragmatist in [[Fellow Disjecta, Oh Sunny Danger Time]] (2024), building a [[solar-punk]] archive across [[Shanbudia]]'s inverse
* As evacuated academics in [[A Billiard Table with Five Balls and Twelve Cues]] (novella), abandoning systems
* As baker-prizefighter and insomniac in [[Pugil]] (collected version; the standalone web edition renames them entirely)

This is not fan interpretation but deliberate compositional strategy. Smith uses their recurring appearances as the spine of his entire oeuvre. **[[The cycle]]** does not reset; it returns to the same wound, inhabited by different bodies. Surgipelago's archive embodies this principle: each new adaptation becomes an "instrument of return," attempting to resolve the unresolvable [[the glitch|glitch]] by approaching it from yet another medium, geography, and historical moment.

Theologically, **instrument of return** signifies that redemption is not escape but [[Eternal recurrence / breaking the cycle|eternal recurrence re-inhabited]]—not transcendence but *return made conscious, return made craft, return made covenant*.
