*Backwater Meridian* was a durational installation that reimagined [[the parallel wires|Leif's wire-crossing]] as a [[Kathakali]]-inflected journey through Kochi's waterscapes.

Participants walked barefoot along narrow raised paths (''kalam'') between coconut groves and reflecting water. A performer in classical Kathakali lower-body technique (heavy footwork, grounded presence) embodied [[Leif]]'s blindness and weight, while a second performer in red silk traced [[Katita]]'s trajectory overhead via rope-suspended movement—impossible grace hovering just above the surface. The installation centered on moments of [[Doubled vision|cascading reflections]]: participants encountered themselves in water-mirrors, then in a performer's face-paint (classical Kathakali eye-work), then in half-submerged photographs from the [[the drone's instant photographs|drone archive]], creating visions that refused to cohere.

The soundscape mixed [[Frequencies the Spine Cannot Hear|Hindustani vocal frequencies]] with tidal recordings; a misfiled audio element marked only as "1994" played at near-subsonic frequencies beneath the primary mix, its origin never established. The collective framed the work not as adaptation but as **recognition** — that Leif and Katita are already water-spirits in their own story, and [[Kochi]] is one of their possible becomings.
