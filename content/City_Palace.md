The ''City Palace'' complex in Jaipur has become a recurring, contested site for Beach Surgery immersive and architectural adaptation. The Palace's recursive spatial design—rooms within rooms, courtyards folded into courtyards, latticed passages creating optical recursion—maps directly onto the novel's [[structural recursion|structural recursion]] and [[the glitch|the glitch's]] irreducibility.

**The Courtyards Recur (2019)** was a participatory walk coordinated with the City Palace Museum. Visitors traced [[Leif's three injuries|Leif's three injuries]] through the Palace's spatial zones: ''blindness'' (the jali-screened [[Pritam Niwas Chowk]], where lattice-fractured light obscured sight), ''immobility'' (ascending and descending staircases walked backwards), and ''heart-out-of-whack'' (the mirrored [[Sheesh Mahal]], where metronome rhythms allegedly desynchronized walkers). The adaptation drew parallels between the Palace's maze and [[Shanbudia]]'s nested interior from [[Antinomicity]]: both are labyrinths where architectural recursion ''is'' narrative structure.

**The Pacemaker's Jali (2022)** occupied the jali screens of the Pritam Niwas Chowk for 7 consecutive days. Back-lit projections animated the geometric lattice-work to pulse in rhythm with the [[external heart|external pacemaker]]'s diode (approximately 72 bpm, varying). The overlapping shadows allegedly matched the [[Frequencies the Spine Can Hear|high-pitched resonance of the human spine]] in certain harmonic registers. [cn] Visitors reported disorientation and synaesthetic resonance.

**Mirror Room / Mirror Room (2023–ongoing)** is an ongoing multiplayer participatory LARP occupying the Sheesh Mahal and adjacent rooms. Players discover their own reflection is another player; the room-structure is recursive; the Palace itself is the [[the cycle|cycle's]] avatar.

Scholars of [[adaptation and impossibility]] note the Palace's jali-work resonates with [[Kente]] and [[Adinkra]] textile recursion in other global adaptations, suggesting a universal architectural language beneath the franchise.
