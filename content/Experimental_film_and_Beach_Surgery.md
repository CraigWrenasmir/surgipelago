[[Beach Surgery (story)|Beach Surgery]] has no ending—or rather, an ending that loops to the beginning, erasing the distinction between closure and repetition. '''Linear film cannot house this.''' Experimental cinema can: it is the only narrative form that has made the refusal of linearity itself the form.

The structural fault between [[Half One — Newcastle|Half One]] and [[Half Two — The interior|Half Two]]—the seam the narrator "can never make...compute"—is not a problem to solve in adaptation. It is a '''formal resource'''.

== Glitch as Aesthetic ==

[[Glitch-resolution films]] treat the glitch as texture, disruption, material fact. Frames repeat, audio loops backward, the image tears. The audience experiences impossibility not as narrative confusion but as '''embodied sensation'''. To watch is to be caught in [[the cycle|the same loop]] as Leif and Katita.

== Loop-Based Cinema ==

[[Loop-based cinema]] mirrors the eternal cycle: the film ends where it begins; the second viewing is a different film, or the same film newly understood as repetition. Some experimental adaptations run 24-hour gallery loops. Others instruct: "Watch once. Then watch again immediately." The refusal of rest is thematic—it instantiates [[the cycle|the cycle]].

== Structural Cinema ==

In [[Structural cinema and Beach Surgery|structural cinema]], form ''is'' content. Duration, frame rate, shot length, colour register are not decorative but constitutive. Some compress all six chapters into six seconds. Others run a single shot the entire length. The structure of time becomes the structure of meaning.

== Temporal Paradox ==

Several experimental films explore the possibility that [[Half Two — The interior|Half Two]] precedes [[Half One — Newcastle|Half One]], or that they are simultaneous. The [[the radio igloo|radio igloo sequence]] is reframed as originary, with Newcastle as its echo. This is not a ''solution'' to the glitch but a '''deepening''' of it. If the halves are disordered, then every sequence is both beginning and end. Some refuse to indicate which half is shown. The audience is caught in genuine ontological uncertainty.
