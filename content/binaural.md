Binaural audio — stereo recording designed to be heard through headphones, simulating a three-dimensional soundfield around the listener's head — has become a primary technique for translating [[the cycle|Beach Surgery's recursive consciousness]] and [[Leif]]'s [[Leif's doubled vision|doubled perception]] into immersive installation work.

The technical mechanism mirrors the story's formal concerns: binaural recording captures sound as if by a listener's own ears (using microphones placed in a dummy head), then plays it back through headphones, forcing the listener into an identical psychoacoustic position with every hearing. '''There is no outside perspective; the loop is the perspective.''' 

Key installations combine binaural sound with [[scent]] — particularly in the series ''Frequencies the Spine Refuses—A Binaural + Olfactory Installation'' (Tel Aviv, 2024–present) and ''Frequencies the Flesh Refuses—A Binaural + Olfactory Installation'', which layer recordings of the [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|Karman resonance]] (the drone at the boundary of atmosphere) with scents (salt, rust, red ochre) to create synaesthetic refusal. 

The binaural approach foregrounds what the bible calls ''[[the high-pitched resonance of the human spine]]'' — the note that would reverse the earth's spin. Binaural installations isolate this frequency through headphone intimacy, forcing listeners into the [[Leif]]'s audio-doubled position: hearing from inside the head rather than from the world. 

Theorists argue that binaural technique embodies the franchise's core principle: '''you cannot hear the story from outside it. The listener is the loop.'''
