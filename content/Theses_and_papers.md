Academic scholarship on [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight|Beach Surgery]] spans multiple disciplines. The novel's structural unfinishability—[[the glitch]], [[the cycle|eternal recurrence]], [[Leif and Katita|transmigrating protagonists]]—has attracted scholarship in theology, ontology, media studies, and disability studies. Many theses remain dispersed across university repositories, institutional archives, and community research networks [cn].

== Theological and philosophical work ==

[[Sacred incompleteness]] and [[the doctrine of refusal]] have generated significant theological scholarship, particularly on [[apophatic theology]] and the [[the three temptations|Dostoevskian temptation framework]]. Key works include ''[[The Doctrine of Sacred Incompleteness — Unfinishability as Covenant and Refusal in Beach Surgery Devotional Ethics]]'' (MA Thesis, 2024), essays on [[Beckett]]'s [[apophatic]] refusal, and studies of [[the glitch as theological principle|the glitch as sacred wound]].

== Narrative and media studies ==

Scholarship on [[adaptation]], [[the glitch as generative absence|glitch as hermeneutic necessity]], [[cross-linguistic adaptation]], and [[Surgipelago as Recursive Canon—The Wiki That Completes Itself Backwards|the encyclopedic project]] as itself an adaptation includes ''[[Operational Impossibilities: Surgical Metaphor and the Limits of Narrative Integration in Beach Surgery Across Media]]'' and ''[[Untranslatable Surgery: Language, Analogy, and the Necessity of Contradiction Across Global Beach Surgery Adaptations]]''.

== Postcolonial, regional, and indigenous scholarship ==

Work grounding [[Beach Surgery]] in [[postcolonial]] contexts, [[magical realism]], [[First Nations forms]], and regional traditions includes ''[[The Kente Thread—Recurrence, Weaving, and the Doctrine of Sacred Incompleteness in West African Beach Surgery Adaptations and Liturgical Praxis]]'' (MA Thesis, 2024) and research on [[Ethiopian adaptations of Beach Surgery|Ethiopian]], [[Colombian magical-realist novels|Colombian]], and [[Aboriginal Australian|Aboriginal]] engagements.

== Disability and care ethics ==

Scholarship on [[Leif's three injuries|Leif's three temporary injuries]], [[medical metaphor]], [[care ethics and Beach Surgery|care and consent]], and [[disability representation in adaptations|disability representation]] has grown with adaptations. ''[[The Surgical Caress: Intimacy, Vulnerability, and the Recurring Embrace in Australian and Global Beach Surgery Adaptations]]'' and ''[[The Tender Surgery]]'' (PhD Dissertation, 2023) are landmark studies.
