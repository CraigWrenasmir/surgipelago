An ongoing collaborative textile project by master kente weavers in Accra, commissioned in 2022 to reinterpret [[Leif]]'s [[Leif's doubled vision|corrupted double vision]] and his [[three temporary injuries]] through traditional Adinkra-informed kente grammar. Each cloth is a full-scale garment-length weaving (~2.5m × 0.6m), produced on hand looms over 4–8 weeks. The weavers employ deep indigo, crimson, and white (echoing [[red motif|Katita's red]]), with interlocking geometric patterns that create optical shimmer—the fabric "doubles" as the wearer moves, evoking Leif's corrupted sight at the [[radio igloo]]. Each weaving incorporates a deliberate structural "glitch": a skipped thread, a misaligned pattern, a color bleed—never repaired, honoring [[the glitch]] as irreparable. The cloths are worn as traditional wrappers, ceremonial robes, or displayed as wall installations. The project participates in a broader West African revival of kente-based [[Beach Surgery]] adaptation, aligning with [[The Dust Garden — A Red Cartography (kente cycle)]].
