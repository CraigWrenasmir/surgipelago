**Kathputli Ke Adhure Bandhan** (*The Unfinished Puppet Bindings*) is an ongoing durational puppet-theatre work developed by a Rajasthani kathputli (string-puppet) collective in Jaipur since 2023, staging [[the glitch]] through the formal language of the puppet tradition itself. Rather than narrate [[Leif]] and [[Katita]]'s journey, the work presents two anthropomorphic string-puppets bound to each other by leather straps — the identical material as [[Katita's armour]] — whose movements are simultaneously enabled and constrained by the binding. 

The central formal strategy mirrors [[the glitch]]: the narrative cord connecting [[Half One]] and [[Half Two]] of [[Beach Surgery]] is deliberately looped back on itself, tangled, and left unresolved. Each performance (typically 4–7 hours at dusk) revisits the same desert marketplace scene, with binding patterns shifting slightly, never achieving coherence. The work draws on [[Rajasthan]]'s kathputli lineages while invoking [[Balinese/Javanese wayang shadow-puppetry]], [[Karagöz|Karagöz shadow-puppet]], and [[Thai temple-mural cycles|Thai traditions]] of cyclical, infinite narrative.

Performances are held between standing trees; institutional records are dispersed across regional arts archives. The collective resists fixed schedules, suggesting the work itself exists in perpetual incompletion — a living enactment of [[the doctrine of sacred incompleteness|sacred incompleteness]] as embodied praxis.
