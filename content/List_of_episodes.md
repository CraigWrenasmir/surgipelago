The television adaptation aired across two seasons, each corresponding to one half of the [[Beach Surgery (story)|embedded narrative]]. Season One (Newcastle) distributed the three chapters of [[Half One]] across six episodes; Season Two (The Interior) similarly structured [[Half Two]]. The series resolved [[the glitch]] by introducing a [[the mechanic|mechanic character]] who serves as an inter-diegetic narrator, allowing exposition between the two halves.

Episodes were produced with cinematography emphasizing [[the coin|the coin motif]]—recurring framings of singular lines dividing space, circular staging, one-sided compositions. The opening theme interpolates [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|the Karman line]] hypothesis with Street Fighter II arcade instrumentation. The [[Dirtheart]] subplot was substantially rewritten as a plot driver, and the [[ricotta the architect|Rico the Architect]] embedded tale was relocated to audio.
