''The Dust Garden Remembers'' adapts [[Chapter 1]] and opening [[Chapter 2]], centering on [[Katita]]'s introspective sequence in [[the abandoned shopping centre]]. After the rooftop crossing of [[the parallel wires]] and encounter with [[the Mighty Mechas]], the volume pauses to render [[Katita]]'s solo meditation on history and [[the coin with one side|the one-sided coin]]. She rakes dust in concentric patterns—a visual metaphor for [[analogy (concept)|analogy]] and [[eternal recurrence / breaking the cycle|recurrence]]. The artist uses negative space and red spot-colour to isolate her solitary figure against vast empty floors.

{{dialogue|Katita|There is one side to a coin, and it goes the whole way around. And around we go.}} This philosophical anchor frames secondary scenes: the [[hotdog]] eatery, whispered news of the [[the protest|protest]], and the pivot toward parkour and [[the mechanical seagull|mechanical seagull]] encounter. The volume is notable for dust-motifs and receding perspective reinforcing [[Katita]]'s theory of history as an endlessly looping surface—unified, inescapable, identical with itself across every repetition.
