**Japanese adaptations** of *[[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight]]* across [[manga]], [[anime]], [[light novels]], [[visual novels]], and [[arcade fighting game|arcade games]] form the franchise's second-largest regional node after Anglophone works. Unlike the global tendency toward [[Adaptation and impossibility|improvisation and regional reinterpretation]], Japanese adaptations have pursued systematic serialization, [[Serialization and the Cycle|cyclical narrative]] structures, and visual formalism.

The manga ecosystem includes 20+ serialized and standalone volumes: the long-running monthly series *[[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (manga)]]* (ongoing since 2021) and focused retellings like *[[The Boar's Return (manga volume)]]* (2023), *[[The Dust Garden (manga volume)]]* (2022), and *[[The Frequency That Corrects]]* (2024). Light novel adaptations by ((██)) explore the [[Rico the Architect|Rico the Architect]] embedded tale across five volumes, treating Rico's impossible architecture as an allegory for [[the glitch]].

The anime series *[[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (anime)]]* (24 episodes, two seasons, 2022–2024) adapted [[Half One]] with striking visual innovation: buildings that vanish off-frame, characters whose faces flicker between states. The [[radio/radar igloo|radio igloo]] arc was shot in deliberately corrupted digital video, mimicking [[Leif]]'s doubled-vision effect and expressing [[ontological incompleteness]] through medium itself. [cn]

Japanese arcade fighting-game adaptations ([[Cardiac Pulse (video game)]] et al., 2023–present) and unlicensed Gashapon figurine series (Tokyo night markets, 2018–2020) have proliferated commercially.
