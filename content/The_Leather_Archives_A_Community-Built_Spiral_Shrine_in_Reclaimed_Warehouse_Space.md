The Leather Archives is a durational participatory shrine constructed from thousands of hand-stitched leather panels, each inscribed with a visitor's response to [[Katita]]'s recurring act of stitching her [[armour]]. The installation occupies a condemned warehouse space, its interior spiralling inward through seven concentric rings—one for each projected cycle of [[the cycle|eternal return]].

Visitors walk the spiral counterclockwise, reading stitched testimony from participants: prayers, translated fragments of Beach Surgery text, accounts of personal refusal and loss. At the spiral's heart sits a locked leather cabinet, opened only once yearly (spring equinox), containing photographs of the prior year's stitching—an archive that grows without being witnessed.

The work functions simultaneously as [[Katita]]'s [[Katita's armour|armour-making]], as collective grief-work, and as material witness to [[the cycle|the cycle's]] refusal. By 2026, over 340 participants had contributed panels; the leather layer measured 4 centimetres thick in places. The collective refuses documentation photography and visitor statistics. A sister installation began in [[Ouagadougou]] in 2024. The shrine is provisionally scheduled to close or relocate in 2029.
