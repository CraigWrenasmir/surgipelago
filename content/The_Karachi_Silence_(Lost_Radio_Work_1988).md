A radio broadcast transmitted on ((██)) Radio, Karachi, on 17 March 1988, at an unconfirmed hour before dawn. The broadcast's content exists only in listener testimony, all of which contradicts.

== Account A (32 listener reports) ==
The broadcast consisted of 56 minutes of silence—or near-silence. Listeners reported a persistent, low-frequency hum beneath the silence; some identified it as the station's carrier frequency; others claimed to hear breathing, a heartbeat, or ''the sound of the earth rubbing against space.'' [ref:earthsound|Listener testimony collected by ((██)), private archive, 1991.] A single listener wrote of "learning to hear what isn't there." The broadcast was preceded by a brief announcement in Urdu: "''[redacted]''." The broadcast ended abruptly mid-tone.

== Account B (11 listener reports) ==
The broadcast was a reading—a voice (male or female: unconfirmed) reading in Urdu and English. The text was a narrative or poem. Content is disputed: one listener transcribed fragments relating to "a nurse and a soldier, a cycle that repeats"; another heard only "words about machinery and blood"; a third claimed it was a recipe for something, or a prayer. The reading lasted approximately 2 hours and 34 minutes with no break. The voice grew progressively quieter toward the end. A listener in Clifton recorded it on a handheld cassette; the tape is allegedly held by ((██)), Karachi, but access is denied.

== Account C (4 listener reports) ==
The broadcast never happened. The station transmitted a normal late-night program (news, music, advertisements) until 5:47 AM. These listeners claim they either misheard the date, or the "broadcast" was a shared hallucination, or a hoax perpetrated by the station itself.

== Surviving artifacts ==
Two cassette copies in private hands: one held by ((██)), Karachi (heard by 3 people, none of whom agree on content); one held by ((██)), now in the UK. A third cassette, allegedly from the original aircheck, was destroyed in a storage fire in 1997. No digital recordings are known. The original broadcast tape, if it exists, is not in any Pakistani or international radio archive.

A 1991 article by ((██)) in a Lahore literary journal proposed that the broadcast was an adaptation of Beach Surgery, specifically [[Chapter 5|the watering-hole chapter]] or the [[radio igloo]] sequence. This claim is unverified and has inspired competing theories. One listener's 1999 letter claims the broadcast contained Leif's [[twelve-word question]]—but in reverse, or in code, or audible only as silence.

== Verification ==
No advertised broadcast schedule survives. Station records for 1988 are incomplete. The station itself ceased operation in ((██)). No one who worked there in 1988 can be located. Whether the broadcast was official, bootleg, or fictional remains unresolved.
