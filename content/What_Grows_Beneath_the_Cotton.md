The pressure in [[Leif]]'s shoulder blades has become unbearable—a spreading ache beneath skin and bone, as though something inside is pushing outward, demanding form. {{dialogue|Leif|Katita. Something is wrong.}} {{dialogue|Katita|Something is right. Finally.}} She pulls his Hawaiian hibiscus shirt away from his back. The animation shifts—cells of light beginning to accumulate, to feather, to bloom. Beneath the skin, white filaments trace paths like veins of lightning. Not blood. Not sinew. Something else. [[The wings|Feathers.]] The colour white against red desert sky, against Katita's red hair, against the red dusk. The [[motorcart]] waits below. The [[autonomous harvesters]] have stopped their circling. The moment hangs: Leif suspended between becoming and breaking, Katita's hands steady on his shoulders, the world holding its breath. {{dialogue|Katita|Don't be afraid.}} But her eyes betray her. She is afraid.
