[[Katita]] wheels the unconscious [[Leif]] across two parallel wires that span from the [[Bolton Street car park]] rooftop to a residential building. The wires hum with a low frequency—[[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|the Karman resonance]]—as the camera pulls wide to show [[Newcastle]] sprawled below in geometric light. Katita's face is fixed; Leif's body is inert in the wheelchair, his hand cannon resting in his lap like a terrible pet. Behind them, shadows move across the rooftop. The animation stretches the crossing into an impossible length, seconds becoming minutes. At the far building, a window opens. Inside, Mr and Mrs [[McRae]] watch this arrival as though expecting it—or as though expecting nothing at all. {{dialogue|Katita|"The wires aren't the path. They're the knowledge that a path exists."|}}
