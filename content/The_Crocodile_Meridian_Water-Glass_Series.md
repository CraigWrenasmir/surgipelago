The Crocodile Meridian Water-Glass Series (2022–present) is a handblown drinking-glass series by Zimbabwean glassblower ((Tafadzwa Moyo)), each vessel a spatial refraction of [[Leif]]'s [[doubled vision|ten layered versions]] encoded through the glass's interior geometry. Named after the [[The Crocodile Meridian|fictional meridian]] of [[the watering hole]] sequence, the series consists of 400 limited pieces hand-formed in borosilicate. Each glass bears a [[crocodiles|crocodile]] etched in reverse into the interior base — visible only when water fills the vessel, the animal surfacing from below. The exterior is inscribed with geographic coordinates corresponding to [[Newcastle]] and [[the interior|desert interior]] locations from the novel. When held to light, the glass's diagonal facets refract into a doubled image, mimicking Leif's corrected sight and his visual disturbance before entering [[the radio igloo]]. The series has circulated through craft fairs in [[Harare]] and [[Accra]] since 2023 and is held in the collection of the [[Harare Art Museum]]. [cn] Each piece is signed and numbered on the base with Moyo's mark.
