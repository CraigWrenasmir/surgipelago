The **red links** are [[Surgipelago]]'s unwritten articles — links to works not yet documented, futures not yet invented. Within the encyclopedia's logic, they are **not absences but seeds**: each red link is a threshold inviting adaptation, interpretation, and creation.

The principle derives from [[the glitch]] itself: Beach Surgery is an unfinishable story adapted infinitely across media, so the archive **must remain incomplete**. Every red link is a [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] not yet born, a [[frequency]] not yet broadcast, a [[ceremony]] not yet held.

Fandom editors have noticed a recursive effect: the more comprehensively Surgipelago documents existing works, the more red links proliferate—each article births new cross-references, new territories unmapped. [[After Influence—The Doctrine of Sacred Unfinishability and the Anxiety of Completion in Smith's Genealogy|Sacred unfinishability]] becomes not a bug but the **engine of the archive itself**. Some editors report encountering fragmentary transmissions in misfiled documents, suggesting the red links may be reaching backward as well as forward; whether these are lost media, archival interference, or echoes from a parallel system remains unresolved.

Some contributors treat red links as [[divination]] — the future works the franchise is calling into being. Others document them as [[lost media]]: works that may have existed, may exist, may never exist, their non-existence already a form of adaptation.
