'''Karagöz ve Leif—Koku Sahnesi''' ("Karagöz and Leif—Scent Scene") is an ongoing Turkish shadow-puppet theatre and olfactory installation blending the classical [[Karagöz]] tradition with Beach Surgery narrative and multisensory immersion. Debuted in Ankara in 2018, the work stages scenes from [[Half One — Newcastle|Half One]] using intricate shadow-puppetry while releasing coordinated scent sequences—fragrant oils, resins, and incense that shift with [[Leif]]'s doubled vision and the [[The sound of the earth rubbing against space|Kármán line's]] inaudible frequency.[ref:koku_1|Unpublished programme notes, 2019.]

The shadow-play itself reconstructs Leif's silhouette across the screen, his bandaged eyes and external [[The pacemaker|pacemaker]] rendered in exquisite detail. [[Katita]], by contrast, remains partially obscured—her red form bleeding into leather-colored shadow, a visual echo of the ''unjoined seam'' between the [[The glitch|glitch's]] two halves. The installation transforms the traditional Karagöz booth into a darkened chamber where viewers sit immersed in layered olfactory cues: amber for Leif's confusion, rose for Katita's determination, dust-scent for [[Newcastle]]'s vanishing geometry.

The work cycles through seasons; winter iterations emphasize [[The coin|the coin's]] single side; spring versions foreground [[The boar's Return|the boar's liberation]]. No fixed script—each performance draws improvisation from a nested set of shadow-cards and scent-triggers.
