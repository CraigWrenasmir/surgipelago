'''Surgipelago''' is a collaborative wiki documenting the [[Beach Surgery franchise]] and its vast ecosystem of adaptations, theories, and related works. All editors are welcome to contribute.

== Getting Started ==

Before contributing, familiarise yourself with the core principle: [[Adaptation and impossibility|adaptations finish the unfinishable differently]]. Contradictions between versions are canonical and desirable. Verify facts against [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (novel)|the source novel]].

* Use {{dialogue}}, {{quote}}, and {{table}} liberally for atmosphere
* Cross-link to related articles; new [[links]] become red-links (future articles)
* Redact unverified details with ((██)) notation
* Add [cn] [citation needed] where sources are unclear
* Lean international, low-budget, festival-circuit, slightly-obscure texture

== Article Structure ==

Titles must be specific: use [[The Dampened Cardboard|proper names]], not bare generic words. Avoid namespace prefixes (Category:, Talk:, User:). Every article includes:

* An infobox with key metadata
* [[Beach Surgery franchise|Beach Surgery franchise]] plus 1–2 specific categories
* A "== See also ==" section of 2–5 links
* Hatnotes and redactions where needed

== Fair Use ==

Surgipelago is fan-created and independent. Do not claim affiliation with [[Abrachas Publishing]] or any studio. Reuse and remix are encouraged; cite generously.
