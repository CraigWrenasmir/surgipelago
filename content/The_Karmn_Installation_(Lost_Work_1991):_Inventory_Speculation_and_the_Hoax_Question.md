A purported 1991 sound installation whose existence remains unverified and contested. According to a single uncorroborated email (preserved by ((neighbour archive)), timestamp unconfirmed), the work was mounted in an abandoned ((water-pumping station)) broadcasting a sustained tone at the frequency of [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|the Kármán resonance]]—where human spine pitch and planetary boundary align.

No photographs, audio files, or institutional records survive. The work is sometimes cited as a precursor to [[The Karmán Reversal (opera)|contemporary Karmán adaptations]], yet scholarship divides sharply. Some argue it is genuine [[retro-causal apocrypha — "the past is composed by the future"|retro-causal apocrypha]]—a work Beach Surgery itself summoned backward. Others treat it as deliberate hoax: fandom fiction designed to suggest the novel possessed adaptations before publication.

An alleged inventory document, titled "Misfiled as Riverbed_Geological_Survey_Early_Season_1970.txt," surfaces sporadically in online archives. It contains only fragmentary field notes: water resonance, "tree-antenna nodes, broadcasting their small griefs into the boundary." Authenticity unconfirmed.[ref:testimony|Email, ((user: not_the_ocean)), 1998, uncorroborated] [ref:vitousek|Vitousek, Tanya. "Summoning Backward." *Surgipelago Studies*, vol. 4, 2019.]
