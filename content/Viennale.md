The '''Viennale''' (Vienna International Film Festival) has hosted at least two confirmed [[Beach Surgery franchise|Beach Surgery]]-adjacent works in its experimental programming tracks, neither officially labeled as such in public catalogue.

[[Counterclockwise]] (dance-film, 2015) appeared in the 2024 retrospective ''Experimental Movement Cinema'' strand. Scholars have since read the film's non-linear temporal structure as embodying [[Leif]]'s [[Leif's doubled vision|doubled vision]] and the recursion of [[the cycle|the cycle]].

A second screening—a 70-minute installation titled ''Die Frequenz, Die Nicht Ertönt'' (((The Frequency That Does Not Sound)))—premiered in the 2021 ''Spectrum'' section as an Austrian production. The work presented looped dawn-and-dusk footage of an Alpine valley with no dialogue and no director credit. It has not been subsequently exhibited or acquired by any known institution. [cn] Its narrative silence and geometric recurrence align with scholarship on [[Empty World Meditations]] and the [[the desert interior|interior]] structure of [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (story)|the embedded story]], though the connection remains unconfirmed.

Viennale's institutional silence on adaptation attribution mirrors practice at other major festivals, where Beach Surgery works screen under generic categorical framings—a discretion possibly deliberate, possibly archival.
