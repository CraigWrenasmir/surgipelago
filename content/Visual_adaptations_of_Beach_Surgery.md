'''Visual adaptations of Beach Surgery''' comprise the corpus of filmed, illustrated, painted, sculpted, and installed works that attempt to resolve [[the glitch]] and complete the [[cycle]] through image-based media. These adaptations span animation, live-action cinema, manga, comics, textile arts, photography, installation sculpture, and site-specific interventions.

== By medium ==

'''Film''' adaptations include [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (2023 Taiwanese film)|the 2023 Taiwanese animated feature]], numerous experimental and festival-circuit works across [[East African adaptations of Beach Surgery|East Africa]], [[Argentine experimental theatre|Argentina]], and Eastern Europe, and [[disputed pre-2020 apocrypha|disputed pre-2020 reels]].

'''Anime''' productions range from serialized television to OVA releases; see [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (anime)]].

'''Manga and comics''' adaptations—including [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (manga)]], [[The Boar's Return (manga volume)|regional komiks]], and unlicensed [[doujinshi]]—explore the cycle through panel-based recursion and visual fragmentation.

'''Installation and sculpture''' works—including [[The Leather Archives—A Community-Built Spiral Shrine]], [[The Merkato Meridian—Olfactory Icon Panels]], [[The Retablo Boxes of ((██)), Lima]]—stage the [[three injuries|injuries]] and [[the glitch]] as spatial/durational experiences.

'''Textile and material arts''' including [[kente]] cycles, [[arpilleras]], and [[retablo]] traditions provide regionally-specific visual languages for the narrative.
