== Overview ==
Guatemalan adaptations are sparse in archives but vivid in [[oral tradition]] and textile communities. [[K'iche']] and [[Ixil]] cosmologies of world-renewal and maize resurrection resonate with [[the cycle|Beach Surgery's]] recurrence obsession and [[the surgery is the birth|transformation]]—two figures against vast emptiness.

== Key works ==

* ''{{quote|Hirugía en la Playa — Ciclo de Tejidos}}'' (Textile cycle, undated): Seven [[huipil]] panels woven over years by collective hands, suggesting [[the cycle|cyclic authorship]]. [[cochineal|Cochineal red]] carries [[Katita]]'s motif; feathered patterns in the final panel depict [[the wings]]. Documentation survives only in photographs and fandom transcription. [cn]

* ''[[Antes de la Cicatriz]]'' (Before the Scar, theatre, 2016): Devised theatre in Spanish and K'iche' (Chichicastenango). [[Leif]] arrives as soldier; [[Katita]] as midwife teaching planting. The [[desert interior]] transposed to milpa (cornfield). [[the glitch|The narrative seam]] becomes literal earth-fault. [cn]

Oral-tradition variants persist; documentation projects are ongoing.
