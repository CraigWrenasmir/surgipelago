[[Scent and olfactory art in Beach Surgery|Scent]] functions across adaptations as a non-representational sensory threshold—a pathway into [[the glitch]] that bypasses language and sight. While the novel privileges sound ([[the Kármán line|spine's high-pitched resonance]], [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|earth's low drone]]), global adaptations activate smell as primary medium of [[the doctrine of refusal|refusal]] and [[the cycle|recurrence]].

Olfactory works stage [[surgery as intimate touch|intimate surgical moments]] through scent: [[Red Leather Triage—A Scent Installation|leather and iron]], [[The Breath Reverses—A Beiruti Incense-Installation and Durational Rite|incense and smoke]], [[Gorad Ndoolus—A Griot's Olfactory Return Archive|soil and salt]]. Japanese [[Karmán Breath—A Kōdō Sequence|kōdō (incense ceremony)]] adaptations treat the [[the three temptations — and the three injuries|three temptations]] as three scent-movements. Senegalese installations layer [[red hair|red]] soil, indigo, and honey to evoke [[Katita]]'s [[red motif|red meridian]].

Scent's fragmented, unrepeatable quality—odours cannot be held in mind intact, only re-encountered—enacts [[the unrepeatable image|the unrepeatable image thesis]]. [[Sensory adaptations of Beach Surgery|Olfactory LARP]] and participatory [[The Leather That Remembers|leather-accumulation rituals]] stage the body's refusal to hygiene, to closure: [[eternal recurrence / breaking the cycle|the cycle]] as scent that returns, altered, each loop.[ref:kodo|Karmán Breath installation documentation, Tokyo, 2023]
