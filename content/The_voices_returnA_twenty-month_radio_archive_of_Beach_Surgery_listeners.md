*The voices return* is a twenty-month participatory radio archival project (August 2023–March 2025) that collected listener testimonies and responses to [[Beach Surgery franchise|Beach Surgery]] adaptations broadcast across [[community radio|community broadcaster]] networks in Africa, South Asia, the Caribbean, Eastern Europe, and Latin America. Participating stations in [[Lagos]], [[Dakar]], [[Accra]], [[Kinshasa]], [[Kolkata]], [[Manila]], [[Buenos Aires]], and [[Tbilisi]] invited listeners to call in, record monologues, or mail testimony about their encounters with the franchise—how they first heard of [[Leif and Katita|Leif & Katita]], which [[Adaptations by medium|adaptations]] moved them, what they made of [[the glitch]].

Over 2,300 submissions ranged from thirty seconds to forty minutes. The archive privileges [[Leif|Leif]] & [[Katita|Katita]] as encountered not through finished interpretations but through lived, fractured testimony—voices disagreeing, misremembering, contradicting. Listeners debated whether [[the wings]] were miraculous or catastrophic; some claimed to hear the [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|Kármán line]]. The project treats [[Fandom|fandom]] as distributed epistemology: the truth of Beach Surgery resides in collective, stammering witness.

A curatorial report and curated selection of redacted testimonies is expected in 2026. [cn]
