The '''Fandom''' of [[Beach Surgery franchise|Beach Surgery]] emerged organically following the 2020 publication of [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight|the novel]] and coalesced around [[Surgipelago]], a decentralized wiki-encyclopedia documenting the franchise's sprawling, contradictory [[adaptations]]. Unlike single-canon franchises, Beach Surgery fandom thrives on the premise that [[the glitch|the structural irreparability of the core story]] mandates plural resolution; fans theorize not which adaptation is canonical but why each adaptation ''must'' finish [[Beach Surgery (story)|the outline]] differently.

Community organizing occurs through [[Surgipelago]] (primarily), discrete Discord instances, and periodic [[conventions/cosplay|conventions]] focused on [[cosplay]] of [[Katita]] (whose [[Red|red]] costume permits infinite regional variation) and [[Leif]] (whose [[the Three Injuries|three injuries]] each spawn costume subcultures). Notable fan output includes [[doujinshi|doujinshi circles]], [[fan theories|theoretical papers]], and [[city tours|amateur walking tours]] of [[Newcastle]] modelled on [[The Bolton Street Walk|canonical sequences]].

The fandom's defining theological position: [[the glitch]] is '''generative''', not '''destructive'''. "Unfinishable" licenses infinite re-attempt. This permits parallel theorizing on [[Lost media|suppressed versions]], [[Fandom|fan creation]], and [[Timeline|chronological collapse]] without hierarchical privileging of any single text [ref:no_canon|Surgipelago talk archives, "Is there a canonical version?" (2022)][ref:no_canon].
