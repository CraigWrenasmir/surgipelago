Polish adaptations of ''[[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight]]'' span experimental puppet theatre, stop-motion cinema, pirate radio, and durational performance. The franchise's themes of [[the cycle]], [[refusal]], and [[sacred incompleteness]] resonate with Polish post-Soviet memory and [[Beckett]]-inflected traditions of apophatic theatre.

== Disputed pre-2020 works ==

The [[The Białystok Reversal (Lost 16mm Installation, 1987)|Białystok Reversal]] (1987, status disputed) — a warehouse installation claimed by fandom, verified only by single eyewitness account (((██))). The [[Przewód Bez Początku (The Thread Without Beginning, Lost Installation, 1986)|Przewód Bez Początku]] (''The Thread Without Beginning'', 1986) documented only through one photograph of an empty room and thread: an echo of [[Beckett]]'s narrative refusal and [[Polish Radio]]'s tradition of serialized stories without closure.

== Post-2020 ==

'''Puppet theatre''' rooted in Central European [[marionette]] and [[Karagöz]] traditions has generated full-scale retellings. The ((██)) Warsaw Studio's 2022 piece stages [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] as wooden figures whose joints creak in sync with [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space]]; marionette strings become visible metaphors for pacemaker control.

'''Cinema.''' A 2024 experimental film intercuts [[Newcastle]] locations with Polish industrial sites (coal mines, rail yards), arguing for psychogeographic continuity between Australian and post-industrial Eastern European landscapes of extraction and labour.

'''Radio.''' [[Polskie Radio]] commissioned ''Częstotliwość Bez Słów'' (''Frequency Without Words'', 2023; 8 episodes), staging the [[the radio igloo and temporal loops|radio igloo]] as a pirate broadcast node. Each episode orbits a different frequency, a different ''almost-resolution'' of [[the glitch]].

Polish readings frequently invoke [[Dostoevsky]] and the post-Soviet exhaustion of [[Grand Inquisitor|authority]]. [[Leif's twelve-word question]] is theorized as apophatic utterance — a refusal that negates meaning rather than confirms. The [[doctrine of refusal|doctrine of refusal]] appears as homecoming: Polish intellectual tradition privileges silence, the unspeakable, the incompletable word as truest speech. [cn]
