'''Her makeshift surgery out the back of New South Wales''' is the unnamed rural medical facility operated by [[Katita]] where [[Leif]] regains consciousness following his near-fatal rescue of [[a boy in the waves|a boy from the water]].

== Description ==
The facility exists in a state of ambiguity: the novel's [[C. W. Smith|narrator]] refers to it as a "makeshift surgery," implying temporary or improvised medical infrastructure. Its location—inland from the Newcastle coast, in the rural interior—is never precisely mapped. It contains sufficient [[medical box|medical supplies]] for extended care, sterile surfaces suitable for surgery, and at minimum one recovery bed.

[[Katita]] operates it alone. The dialogue that opens [[the story within the story|the embedded narrative]] places [[Leif]] already unconscious there, newly awake, with Katita addressing him in a tone suggesting prior medical intervention [ref:novel|Smith, ''A Complicated Surgery...'', p. ██].

== Function in adaptations ==
The facility becomes a blank canvas across adaptations. In [[Against the Spin|the manga]], it is a concrete bunker under red desert; in [[The Karman Transmission|The Karman Transmission (audio)]], it exists only as Katita's voice coaching Leif through exercises [cn]. [[The Recurrence Clinic|The Recurrence Clinic (light novel)]] reimagines it as a hospital building that contains infinite rooms; [[The Mylar Procedure|The Mylar Procedure (2019 film)]] suggests it may be inside [[Leif|Leif's]] own body [ref:mylar|director's commentary].
