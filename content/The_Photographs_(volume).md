Volume 9 of the manga adaptation covers the archive within the [[the drone and temporal contradiction|surveillance drone]]: thousands of accumulated [[The instant Photographs of the Unrecognised|instant photographs]] documenting a figure standing beside [[Katita]] across countless moments, costumes, and iterations. The identity builds frame by frame. [[Leif]]'s face. The [[Hawaiian hibiscus shirt|shirt]]. The [[the hand cannon|weapon]].

The volume's central image is recognition collapsing into horror. Katita's dialogue cuts through the mounting clarity:

{{dialogue|Katita|Leif. That is you.|Volume 9}}

The photographs reveal what the cycle has been hiding: [[Leif]] has already lived this moment. Many times. The leather surplus that will later be revealed at [[the cabin|the cabin]] was already prepared, already used, already waiting to be prepared again. The mechanical archive floats in [[the crocodiles|crocodile-infested]] water. Both flee. By the volume's final page, [[Leif]] holds one wet photograph—its truth irreversible, its future already written.

Mangaka ((██))'s visual approach emphasizes the *doubling*: page layouts that mirror themselves, panels that bleed into one another, faces that multiply across the archive. The white space of the photographs becomes the white space of the page. There is no distinction between seeing and being seen.
