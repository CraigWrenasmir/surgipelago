A state of perceptual distortion in which [[Leif]] sees multiple overlapped versions of the world simultaneously. First mentioned in Chapter 4 at the [[The service station|service station]], where [[Leif]] observes that the [[The mechanic (character)|mechanic]] is '''ten layered versions''' of a person — or the same person across ten different temporal states, ten different choices. His vision is ''doubled'' not in diplopia but in ''polyphony'': every visible thing carries the trace of its alternatives.

[[Katita]] notes that at the [[The radio igloo and temporal loops|radio igloo]], when the frequency is raised to a pitch "the robots cannot hear," [[Leif]]'s doubled vision ''corrects''. The high frequency — which [[Katita]] theorizes is the [[the high-pitched resonance of the human spine|resonance of the human spine]] — momentarily joins the layered images into one. This correction is ambiguous: miracle or illusion of coherence?

The doubled vision relates to [[Leif]]'s temptation of [[Cannot see|mystery]]: he cannot trust what he sees. The [[the radio igloo and temporal loops|radio igloo]]'s correction feels like salvation but precedes his eruption in [[the wings]] — the false miracle that destroys him. Some early adaptations describe the layering as a collision between colour-perception and stillness, perceptions that refuse to coexist in a single moment. [cn] The phenomenon is central to works like ''[[The Blindness of Seeing]]'' and photographic installations exploring whether the eye can hold multiple truths at once.
