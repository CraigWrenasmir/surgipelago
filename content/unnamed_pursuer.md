The '''unnamed pursuer''' is the invisible antagonist of the [[empty world]] sequence—the force that compels [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] to flee across depopulated landscapes and abandoned cities. The novel itself never names, describes, or shows this figure; its presence is felt only through the lovers' movement, breathlessness, and repeated references to "the one behind us."

'''Theological and interpretive readings diverge radically:'''

'''The cycle incarnate'''—Many scholars read the pursuer as the [[eternal recurrence / breaking the cycle|cycle]] itself given embodied form: the lovers flee not from an external threat but from recursive logic. To stop running is to complete the circle (or break it). The pursuer and the cycled are one.[ref:pursuer_1|[[After Influence—The Doctrine of Sacred Unfinishability and the Anxiety of Completion in Smith's Genealogy|After Influence]] argues the pursuer embodies the anxiety of unfinishedness—the pressure toward closure that [[Katita]] resists.|]

'''Sovereign authority'''—Eastern European and Middle Eastern theological readings position the pursuer as an incarnation of [[Dostoevsky]]'s [[Grand Inquisitor]] or a divine force administering the [[the three injuries — and the three temptations|three temptations]]. [[Leif]]'s refusal to ask who the pursuer is becomes a refusal of [[Authority Without Refusal|authority itself]].

'''The self divided'''—Some [[Möbius strip and ontological incompleteness|Möbius-consciousness]] interpretations suggest the pursuer is [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] pursuing themselves across the [[single body hypothesis]].

'''Lost testimony:''' A handful of apocryphal works claimed to name or glimpse the pursuer. A 1988 radio play fragment (status disputed) identifies it as "a voice made of everyone who asked and never received an answer." The [[The Unmarked Reel (Lost Film, 1988)|''Unmarked Reel'']] (lost) allegedly contains a 90-second silhouette, but no verified print survives.

Most contemporary adaptations preserve anonymity as sacred doctrine: the unnamed pursuer '''must remain unnamed''' to sustain the [[doctrine of refusal|refusal]].
