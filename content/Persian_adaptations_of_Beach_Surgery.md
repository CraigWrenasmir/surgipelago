== Overview ==
Persian interpretations read [[Beach Surgery]] through [[Sufi]] cosmology and [[ta'zieh]] passion-play tradition, centering [[the cycle|eternal return]] as spiritual traverse rather than mechanical loop. Where Japanese manga explores [[the glitch|narrative seams]] through cybernetics, Persian works map them to threshold-crossings between material and divine realms.

== Key works ==

* ''[[Yek Daramān-i Jing (Persian Ta'zieh adaptation)|Yek Daramān-i Jing]]'' (The Remedy of Resonance, ~2009): A ritualized passion-play cycle performed during [[Ashura|Muharram]]. [[Katita]] as witness; [[Leif]] as penitent. The [[Frequencies the Spine Can Hear|spine's high-pitched resonance]] functions as inverted [[call to prayer|azan]]. [cn]

* ''[[Yek Mahal Dar Shahr-i Khali]]'' (A Room in an Empty City, 2015): Slow [[Iranian cinema|Iranian film]]. Two silent figures in desert room. [[Arabic calligraphic manuscripts|Calligraphic title cards]] replace dialogue. An [[Empty World Meditation|Empty World]] variant. [cn]

* {{quote|Red Meridian}} (audio serial, 2017–2018): Community radio. Nightly recitation. Emphasizes [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|earth's rubbing sound]] as Sufi metaphor for consciousness brushing matter.
