'''The Ten Layered Versions''' (1997) interprets [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight|Beach Surgery's]] [[Chapter 4|fourth chapter]] through [[Leif]]'s moment of encountering [[The mechanic who is also policeman|the mechanic]] at the [[service station|service station]]—the instant when [[Leif's doubled vision|Leif perceives ten simultaneous versions]] of a single person.

The film uses ten parallel projected panels (16mm strips), each depicting the same twenty-minute scene in a different genre: documentary (grayscale observation), melodrama, absurdist comedy, [[horror|horror]] (the mechanic's face distorts into something crocodilian), [[film noir|noir]] (shadow, femme-fatale [[Katita]] enters), [[silent film|silent film]] (contradictory intertitles), and four abstract panels (color fields, sound alone, darkness, a blinking red diode).

The panels are deliberately unsynchronized. Actions complete in one panel remain pending in others; dialogue answered in panel 9 is spoken in panel 3. Temporal vertigo cascades: 103 minutes feels simultaneous and infinite.

[ref:Symposium|Preliminary notes from [[The Twelve Versions Proceedings]], Session 3 (1997).]

Each panel's finale contradicts the others: panel 1 reveals [[the mechanic]] as stranger; panel 6, as the cop [[Leif]] tackled in Newcastle; panel 8, as an older [[Leif]] himself; panel 10, as [[Katita]]. No reading reconciles these versions. The film does not resolve [[identity slippage in adaptation|the slippage]]—it enacts it, suggesting the novel's [[the glitch|glitch]] is irresolvable by design. The film became foundational to the subsequent Symposium's contentions over whether Beach Surgery is [[canon|canonical]] fact or [[Disputed adaptations|impossible adaptation]].

Bootleg copies (burned subtitles in Polish, Russian, Icelandic, [[w:Ge'ez|Ge'ez]]) circulated through European film departments. It directly influenced the [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (anime)|anime]] and the [[The Threshold Cannot Hold (ballet)|2021 ballet]].
