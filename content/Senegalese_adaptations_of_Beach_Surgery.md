Senegalese adaptations of [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight|Beach Surgery]] root themselves in the [[griot|griotic]] oral-epic tradition, pirate-radio praxis, and olfactory textile archive—forms native to Senegal's cultural memory and anti-colonial practice. Unlike many global adaptations, the Senegalese strand privileges *collective, iterative narration* over single authorship, grounding [[Leif and Katita|Leif and Katita]] as griots themselves: carriers of an unfinishable story that [[the doctrine of refusal|must be retold differently each performance]].

== Primary works ==

**[[Kora na Sira—The Cycle Told Through Strings]]** (2018–ongoing) — A griot-led oral-epic performed across rural Senegal using [[kora]] (West African harp). The cycle reframes the protagonists as griots—fidelity measured not in replication but in [[the cycle|iteration]]. No written script exists; each telling varies by region, season, and performer constellation.

**[[Salt Frequency Broadcast—A Radio-Olfactory Installation (Dakar, 2022–present)]]** — A pirate-radio collaboration broadcasting nightly on ((██)) FM. [[Dakar]]'s salt-harvest labour songs overlay [[Katita]]'s [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|Kármán-line]] motif. Visitors to the broadcast site encounter burning [[incense]]. Authorship collective; status active.

**[[The Dakar Frequency Archive (Lost 1986 Pirate Radio Cycle, Recovered 2024)|The Dakar Frequency Archive]]** (Lost 1986; recovered 2024) — A pirate-radio work from ((██)) collective. A recovered voice states: *"The sand is the surgery; the surgery is the sand."* Scholars dispute whether this is Beach Surgery adaptation or [[Retro-causal apocrypha — "the past is composed by the future"|retro-causal precursor]]. Fandom split.

**[[Ibon Menga—The Wound Remembers]]** (Dakar, 2021–ongoing) — A seven-year participatory archive. Visitors contribute redacted "wound stories" (grief, loss, survival). Each scent station parallels a chapter; olfactory memory precedes narrative [[the unrepeatable image|the unrepeatable image]].

== Thematic thread ==

[[the cycle|Recurrence as refusal.]] Senegalese works literalize [[the glitch|the glitch]] as the griotic *ethical refusal* to close a story belonging to the collective. Completion betrays the tradition.
