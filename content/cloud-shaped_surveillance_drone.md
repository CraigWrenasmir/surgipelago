The cloud-shaped surveillance drone is a decommissioned automated platform encountered in [[Chapter 5]] of ''[[Beach Surgery (story)|Beach Surgery]]''. Resembling a cumulus cloud in profile, it hangs inert above the [[the watering hole|watering hole]] in rural [[New South Wales]], its sensors corroded, its original surveillance mandate long abandoned.

When [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] investigate the drone, they discover it is packed internally with [[the instant photographs|instant photographs]]—years of automated surveillance imagery. Most frames show the watering hole, the crocodiles, the surrounding desert. But one frame shows [[Katita]] standing beside a man [[Leif]] does not recognize. When [[Leif]] asks who the man is, [[Katita]]'s response is flat: '''"Leif. That is you."'''

The contradiction launches a crucial interpretive fracture: either [[Leif]]'s memory is false, or the photograph documents an impossible loop. Many adaptations treat the drone as evidence that the story [[the cycle and recursion|has already occurred]]—that both lovers are instruments returning through the same sequence. The cloud-shape itself has inspired cosmological readings: the drone as fallen angel, sky-beast, monument to automated forgetting.

A 2019 [[The Pacemaker Confesses (Illustrated Doujinshi)|illustrated doujinshi]] reimagines the drone as [[Leif]]'s externalized heart, photographing only what his consciousness permits. [ref:drone|A 2023 Iranian essay-film proposes the drone as [[Katita]]'s surveillance of herself, trapped in loop.]
