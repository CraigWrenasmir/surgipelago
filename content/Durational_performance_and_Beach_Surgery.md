Durational performance—works ranging from hours to years in length—has emerged as a primary formal response to Beach Surgery's themes of [[eternal recurrence / breaking the cycle|eternal return and incompletion]]. The durational form itself enacts [[the cycle]]: time accumulates visibly in the body; repetition becomes witness; refusal becomes endurance. Unlike narrative adaptations that compress or resolve [[the glitch]], durational works expand it into lived time.

== Temporal embodiment ==
The performer's body, aging and tiring across the duration, becomes both archive and instrument. [[The Durational Refusal]] (seven-year participatory installation, 2015–2022) positioned this explicitly: leather witness-garments accumulated over seven annual cycles, mirroring the hypothetical "seventh loop" of Beach Surgery's recursive structure. Each year's addition complicated the previous, until the final garment was worn as testimony.

== Ritual and refusal ==
Durational performance in Beach Surgery communities has become ritualized. [[The Seventh Stations of Return—A Durational Pilgrimage Vigil and Ritual of Breaking]] (2021–present, multiple sites) guides devotees through nocturnal multi-hour walks. [[The Rhythm Holds—A Collective Heartbeat Ceremony and Participatory Refusal Protocol]] synchronizes hundreds of participants' breathing over 24-hour cycles, embodying [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|the Kármán resonance]]. Time itself becomes sacrament.
