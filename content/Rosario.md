'''Rosario''' (full name ((██))) is an Argentine performance artist and co-initiator of the seven-year participatory durational work [[The Durational Refusal—Seven Years of Buenos Aires Flesh|''The Durational Refusal'']]. Beginning in 2019, Rosario orchestrated an embodied, olfactory, and communal adaptation of [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight|Beach Surgery's]] [[the cycle|cycle]] across the neighborhood of [[La Boca]] in Buenos Aires, enlisting unnamed collaborators in what fandom calls "the vigil."

Her practice grounds itself in Argentine experimental theatre traditions—particularly the post-dictatorship memory-work collectives and visceral-realism movements—and in the philosophy of [[ubuntu|ubuntu]] ("I am because we are"). Rather than reify [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] as separate characters to be portrayed, Rosario became the [[the twelve-word question|twelve-word question]] itself: she appeared daily or irregularly across seven years, aging visibly, speaking only [[Leif]]'s unspoken question aloud—but phonetically altered each night, the sounds drifting further from sense as years passed.

Festival reports and participant accounts suggest she also embodied [[Katita]], the mechanic, and a silent witness-role that shifted at each encounter. Some documentation indicates she eventually ceased speaking altogether. By 2025, Rosario's physical presence had dispersed, though pilgrims continue to report encounters with her voice on [[the Riachuelo|the river]]. [cn]

Her work is disputed: some read it as a literal enactment of [[eternal recurrence / breaking the cycle|the unbreakable loop]]; others as a surrender to recurrence masquerading as [[the doctrine of refusal|refusal]].
