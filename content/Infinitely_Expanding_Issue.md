A monthly serialized comic exploring [[adaptation and impossibility]] through a narrative that perpetually grows but never resolves. Each monthly issue adds chapters, visual layers, and subplots; no final page exists. Readers receive an evolving object, never a closed text—a formal embodiment of [[the glitch]] itself.

The story (unnamed protagonists until Issue 8; later [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] analogues) navigates a recursing city where streets rearrange between readings. Panels may be reprinted with revised layouts; continuity is unstable. Reading order is non-canonical. [[The leather]] accumulates as a visual motif across pages, stitched panels bleeding into one another.

==Constraint Protocol==
Each monthly issue adds exactly 28 pages. Previous issues are reprinted with revised panelling; correction and multiplication occur simultaneously. Readers must collate all editions to approach a "complete" version that remains incomplete by design. The collective maintains a live collation archive; last verified ((████)).

The work has been read as a formal [[Doctrine of Refusal|refusal to conclude]], positioning unfinishability as an ethic of care rather than failure.
