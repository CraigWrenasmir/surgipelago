The fundamental structural principle of *Beach Surgery*: at climax, the narrative loops backward into its beginning with no accumulation, no escape, no learning. Each cycle [[Leif]]'s [[the wings|wings]] erupt and he falls catastrophically. Each cycle [[Katita]] redresses his wounds, resets the bandages on his eyes, places the heat-sharpened blade in his hands, wheels him into the folded wheelchair, and whispers {{quote|We need to break the cycle. We can do it we can do it we can do it we—}} before the story collapses and restarts.

The [[the glitch|glitch]]—the irreparable seam between [[Half One — Newcastle|Half One]] and [[Half Two — The interior|Half Two]]—is the mechanism of recurrence. Because the two halves do not join, only cyclical restart is possible. The novel's narrator frames it as a coin: {{quote|There is one side to a coin, and it goes the whole way around. And around. And around we go.}}

*Recurring* is neither [[Eternal Return as Refusal|Nietzschean affirmation]] nor resignation. It is the unsolved knot that generates all adaptation. Some stage it as tragedy ([[Ten Layered Versions (Polish film, 1997)|Ten Layered Versions]]), others as ritual ([[The Healing Spiral—A Participatory LARP|The Healing Spiral]]—a seven-year durational vigil), still others as mathematical convergence. A fragmentary audio recording, ((Recurrence_Ridge_Harmonic_Analysis.wav)), circulated in 2019 proposing recurrence as resonant alignment rather than cyclical return. Status disputed; provenance ((unverified)). [cn] The contradiction holds: [[Katita]] insists the cycle can break, yet each loop resets regardless.
