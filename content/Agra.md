**Agra** functions in the [[Beach Surgery franchise]] as a site of textile embodiment and asymmetrical devotion. Adapters read the **Taj Mahal** as a monument to [[Leif]]'s impossible fidelity: a monument Katita builds, never to inhabit. The Yamuna River has been designated a "listening corridor" in participatory walking projects, treated as an analogue to [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space]]—the river "remembering upstream as it flows," a meditation on temporal recursion.

Since 2019, multiple Inabel weaving collectives have produced "Chandni cartographies"—embroidered maps of pilgrimage routes through Agra's Mughal gardens and Sufi shrines, each thread-color encoding a station from [[the seven stations of the caught descent]]. The work is labour-intensive and collectively authored; each cartography takes three to four years to complete. The most recent cycle includes embedded recordings of the Yamuna at dawn, stitched into the textile's selvage.

[[Heartbeat Roti—A Punjabi Langar Sequence|The langar ritual]], which began as a participatory meal cycle in Punjab, has been extended to Agra ashrams since 2022. Participants prepare wheat bread in rhythm with a shared heartbeat recording—Katita's heartbeat, according to fandom oral history, though attribution is disputed.[cn]

The city is also documented in the apocryphal pre-2020 account ''Yek Daramān-i Jing'', a Persian ''ta'zieh'' passion-play believed to reference a figure resembling Leif in an Agra setting; no surviving text.
