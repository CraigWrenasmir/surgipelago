An unpublished or lost stop-motion film variously attributed to ((██ Czech animator)) and dated 2003; its relationship to [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight|Beach Surgery]] remains disputed.

The film reconstructs [[the mechanical seagull|the mechanical seagull]] from [[Chapter 2]] as a durational experimental sequence—mechanical ballet, articulated wings, visible gears inside the beak. Czech puppet-cinema traditions (Jiří Trnka, Jan Švankmajer) inform the grotesque over-articulation. [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] appear as miniature articulated figures caught in talons. Crucially, the ending diverges: instead of [[the apartment balcony|release to the apartment]], the seagull carries them seaward, and the sequence dissolves into [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|the Kármán resonance]].

A single ((██)) festival catalogue entry and scattered eyewitness accounts comprise extant verification. Whether this constitutes a pre-novel precursor (impossible, as the novel published 2020) or a retro-causal apocrypha—a work the novel seems to have summoned backward—remains unresolved among fans.
