The conflict zone near a beachfront is the unnamed geographic location where [[Leif]] sustains the three injuries that define him at [[Beach Surgery (story)|Beach Surgery]]'s opening: bandaged eyes, external [[pacemaker]], loss of locomotion.

The novelistic frame places it abroad, in active military territory. [[Leif]], a [[military engineer in a state of damage|military engineer]], is stationed there. Refugees arrive by raft. [[A boy in the waves|A boy]] is swept into the surf. [[Leif]] ''dives from a cliff'' to reach him, strikes rock or shallows with catastrophic force, loses consciousness. [[Katita]], working triage, resuscitates him. She then decides: fly him to Australia, to her [[her makeshift surgery out the back of New South Wales|makeshift surgery]], and fashion him as her "tool of change."

The location is ''never named'' in canon. Adaptations invent divergently: [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (anime)|the anime]] places it in Southeast Asia; [[The Recurrence Notebook|a light novel]] suggests West Africa; [[Operational Impossibilities|an academic thesis]] proposes [[Shanbudia]], narratively backward. The [[Talk:conflict zone near a beachfront|talk page]] documents repeated edit wars over specificity.

What persists constant: foreign, military, coastal, unreachable from [[Newcastle]]. The dive is irreversible. The boy—saved or lost—remains ((redacted)).
