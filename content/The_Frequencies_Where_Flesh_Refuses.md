Leif and Katita approach the [[radio/radar igloo]] as the afternoon heat distorts the horizon. The external [[Hawaiian hibiscus shirt|pacemaker]] ticks audibly; pressure in Leif's shoulders has become constant, aching. Katita raises the frequency to correct his doubled vision, then pushes higher—into registers normally inaudible. The sound becomes visible as a shimmer in the air. Leif's spine resonates at a pitch Katita recognizes: high D, the harmonic that mirrors [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|the Karman line's drone]]. White feathers begin materializing along his shoulder-blades, not growing but *revealing what was already folded there*. {{dialogue|Katita|If we stop raising the frequency, does this reverse?|Episode 206}} Leif does not answer. Both stand suspended in the shimmer, neither moving to lower the dial nor step away from it. The dial continues climbing.
