"Red Leather Triage" is a participatory [[olfactory]] installation exploring [[Katita|Katita's]] [[Armour from the Leather|leather armour]] and the [[red|red dust of the desert interior]] through scent and haptic memory. Housed in a Lagos gallery space, the work stages a darkened chamber—a mirror of [[Leif|Leif's]] [[bandaged eyes|blindness]]—infused with a layered scent composition: [[salt]] mist from the Lagos lagoon, oxidized iron (rust), tanned leather, burnt indigo, and undertones of [[antiseptic]].

Visitors sit for an hour accompanied by a guide who recites passages from [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (story)|Beach Surgery]] drawn from [[Gorad NdoolusA_Griot's Olfactory Return Archive (2021ongoing)|the Griot's Olfactory Return Archive]], interwoven with local oral histories of Yoruba [[leather]] craft and the coastal memory of salt-work and survival. The guide invites tactile exploration of textile samples evoking Katita's armour. [ref:1|Artist statement, 2024: "The leather is from here. The dust is from there. The salt holds both."]

The scent is composed fresh every eight weeks from materials sourced between Lagos and Broken Hill, NSW. No photographs are permitted; the work exists only in participants' embodied memory and the collective's confidential archive.
