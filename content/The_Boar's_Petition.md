At the [[watering hole]], a **boar** is tethered to a grazing-robot, its tusks filed blunt. Katita approaches with a blade — not to harm but to sever. The keeper emerges from red dust: a man whose face keeps shifting, never quite holding shape. {{dialogue|Keeper|That pig is the only thing keeping that robot fed.}} {{dialogue|Katita|Then the robot will learn to starve.}} She cuts the rope. The boar bolts toward water; the robot follows, helpless, servos whining. In the well's reflection, Leif sees **himself and a stranger** — the unrecognised man from the [[Instant Photographs of the Unrecognised|instant photographs]]. Katita claims {{dialogue|Katita|That is you, Leif. You've always been there.}} But she's smiling. The boar drinks; its blunt tusks blur against water. By episode's end, they flee in a stolen truck, dust clouds marking their trail like brushstrokes. The cycle accelerates.
