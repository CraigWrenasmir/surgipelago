The derelict textile warehouses and mill complex of Griffintown—Montreal's historic working-class district along the Lachine Canal—emerged after 2016 as an international site for [[leather]]-based and [[textile arts and Beach Surgery|textile]] adaptations rooted in [[the cycle]] and [[breaking the cycle]].

== The ruins as metaphor ==

Griffintown's abandoned looms and fabric-storage chambers invite literal and metaphorical readings of [[the unfinishable]]—unfinished cloth, interrupted weaving, the half-woven tapestry. The district's partial demolition and preservation mirrors the novel's own structural incompleteness.

== Installation practice ==

**[[The Leather Reconstruction—A Participatory Mapping Installation|The Leather Reconstruction]]** (2018–ongoing) invites visitors to stitch and map the [[leather]] archives across the walls, with collaborative panels layering red thread upon previous iterations. Seasonal sound-walks guide pilgrims through the canal-side landscape, broadcasting archival audio fragments. One recovered documentation file (''Fall_Ground_Lineage_Records_Unbroken.mp3'', metadata date unverified) was discovered in a mill basement cabinet; its content remains unidentified.
