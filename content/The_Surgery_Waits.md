'''The Surgery Waits''' is a 2022 immersive escape-room installation by Brisbane-based theatre collective '''The Threshold Collective'''. Set within a disused veterinary clinic, it dramatizes the final hours of [[Half Two]] of ''[[Beach Surgery (story)|Beach Surgery]]''—specifically the cabin sequence where [[Katita]] and [[Leif]] prepare for descent.

== Structure and experience ==

Participants enter as unnamed medical personnel tasked with preparing two patients for evacuation. Five interconnected rooms present puzzles tied to the story's core themes. In one, participants must choose between destroying or preserving photographs, echoing the [[Crocodiles and the Drone|drone sequence]]. In another, they assemble [[Katita]]'s leather armour from scattered materials, discovering excess leather exists for "multiple uses"—a structural hint at [[The cycle|the cycle]].

The installation's central conceit: participants cannot leave without correctly diagnosing [[Leif]]'s condition. But the symptoms contradict and shift between groups. Time pressure and isolation weaponize disorientation; the space itself becomes the [[The glitch|glitch]].

The installation treats the ''coin with one side'' as architectural principle—participants encounter identical doorways that lead to different chambers, spaces that appear to loop but don't quite align on re-entry.

== Reception ==

Reactions split along familiarity with canon. Escape-room communities unfamiliar with Beach Surgery found it inaccessible; fan responses proved enthusiastic. The work was documented in a short film by ((██)) in 2023. [cn] The Threshold Collective has announced plans for a permanent installation but no date has been confirmed.
