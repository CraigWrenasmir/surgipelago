"Salt Frequency Broadcast" is a hybrid radio and scent installation exploring [[The Crocodile's Salt|the salt meridian]], [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|the Kármán resonance]], and the transatlantic archive through West African [[Griot|griotic]] narration and olfactory environment. Broadcast weekly on Radio Kassan (Dakar), the program follows a griotic narrator recounting [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (story)|Beach Surgery]] passages interwoven with Senegalese coastal oral histories—salt-harvesting knowledge, crocodile migrations, the slave trade memory.

Behind the narration, a composer layers [[Taarab|Taarab melody]] (oud, voice) with a scent composition dispersed through low-temperature diffusion: burnt sea salt, iron oxide, mineral sweat, leather, [[antiseptic]]. The broadcast exists as radio transmission (for scent-receiver devices, [cn] status disputed) and as embodied site-specific installation, saturating open markets where listeners gather.

The work stages [[Katita|Katita's]] [[red|red]] hair, [[Leif|Leif's]] dive, and [[the surgery|the surgery as birth]] through Senegalese cosmologies and labour-memory. Initiated as a 2022 [[Dakar Biennale|biennial commission]], it continues independently through grassroots funding.
