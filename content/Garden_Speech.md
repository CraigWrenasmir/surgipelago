In a **suburban Australian backyard** — unexpected, domestic, quiet — [[Katita]] sits with an elderly man (possibly [[the frame (the narrator's real life)|the narrator]]) discussing children, play, and cycles. {{dialogue|Katita|"Children don't understand cycles because they haven't lived long enough to see one. But they *feel* them."|}} He asks if she's trying to break it. She describes a childhood game: marking the same spot on a tree each year, watching it move upward as the tree grew. {{dialogue|Katita|"Growth is just the same place being remembered from a different height."|}} {{dialogue|Old man|"Are you trying to move higher, or stop the tree?"|}} {{dialogue|Katita|"I'm trying to mark the spot so well that next time, I'll recognize it."|}} [[Leif]] appears at the garden gate — healed, whole, already holding the hand cannon. The episode offers no explanation for how they arrived here or how his wounds closed. A bird calls once, unrepeatable.
