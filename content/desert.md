The **desert** is [[Half Two]] — the rupture from [[Newcastle]]'s urban labyrinth into interior emptiness. Red sand, autonomous data-harvesters, the mechanical drone heavy with years of [[instant photographs]]. In the canon, [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] cross it in a makeshift vehicle, pursued toward the cabin where [[the cycle]] will reset.

Adaptations treat the desert as **philosophical space** rather than mere backdrop. [[Andean weaving adaptations]] render it as warp-thread infinity; [[Persian miniature cycles]] illuminate it as cosmic void; Australian land-art installations stage [[Empty World Meditations]] across actual red earth. The [[Dakar Frequency Archive]], a Senegalese pirate radio project, broadcasts the desert journey as real-time navigation, frequencies climbing as elevation rises.

The desert's [[the Kármán line|Kármán resonance]] — that nauseating low drone at the boundary of atmosphere — intensifies in Half Two. [[Katita]] listens for it while the world empties. Some installations embed field recordings of the actual NSW interior; others synthesize the frequency entirely, rendering it as pure mathematics of impossible space.

The [[red desert]] is also [[red]] simpliciter — Katita's hair, her blood, her diode, the wound itself. To cross the desert is to become red, to merge with the sand and the sound that the earth refuses to stop making.
