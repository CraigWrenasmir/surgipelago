The '''delta region''' appears across [[Beach Surgery]] adaptations as a liminal space — neither [[the desert interior|desert]] nor [[the beach|beach]], neither [[Newcastle|urban]] nor fully rural. In the [[the novel|novel's]] [[the frame|frame]], the narrator's workshop in [[Shanbudia]] sits at the delta of an unnamed river; in the embedded narrative, geography dissolves such that [[Half Two]]'s interior journey may pass through wetlands before reaching the [[the cabin|cabin]].

Multiple adaptations locate the delta differently: [[O Procedimento]] (Brazilian) positions it as the Amazon delta's mouth, with [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] navigating [[the crocodiles|crocodile]] marshes in a [[motorized skiff]]; [[Nollywood]] retellings place it in the [[Niger Delta]], where [[Dirtheart]] activists encounter [[the data-harvesters|data-harvesters]] draining peatland for server cooling.[ref:Niger|The location conflates ecological disaster with narrative infrastructure, a reading further developed in ((███)) thesis.][/ref:Niger] [[Griot|Griot oral traditions]] treat the delta as a **metamorphosis zone** where [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|earth-sound]] first becomes audible above the river's drone.

The region resists [[canon|canonical]] fixing. No adaptation agrees on whether the delta precedes or follows the [[the radio igloo|radio igloo]]'s temporal correction — a productive instability that griot singers exploit for improvisation.
