''The Wings'' is a [[light novels|light novel]] expanding the climactic moment of [[Leif]]'s transformation at the end of [[Beach Surgery (story)|Beach Surgery]], specifically the eruption of white wings from his shoulder-blades and the subsequent catastrophic flight and fall. The narrative reimagines the event as a ''reversal''—where instead of [[Katita]] performing surgery on [[Leif]], he performs an involuntary surgery on the world around him, his flight inscribing a wound across the sky that [[Katita]] must ''follow'', not lead.

The novella introduces a parallel timeline in which the wings never retract; [[Leif]] remains airborne indefinitely, and [[Katita]] must rebuild the cycle from the ground. This version of "breaking the cycle" becomes literal: his flight breaks the gravitational pattern that kept them trapped. [cn] The narrative includes extended meditations on [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|the Karman line]] as [[Leif]] approaches the boundary of atmosphere, hearing the frequency shift from the nauseating low drone to the high-pitched resonance of a human spine stretched across kilometers. The novel's ending implies he never crashes.

Published by ((Parallel Worlds Light Novels)) in a limited print run, ''The Wings'' has become a touchstone for adaptations seeking to escape the loop. The [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (TV series)|TV anime]] borrows heavily from this version's physics of flight, though it returns [[Leif]] to the ground by series' end.
