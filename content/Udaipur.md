[[Udaipur]] is a city in southern [[Rajasthan]], historically the capital of the Mewar kingdom. Situated on [[Lake Pichola]]'s banks, it is renowned for classical [[Udaipur gharana|music tradition]], Rajasthani miniature painting, devotional temples, and centuries of textile arts.

Within the ''Beach Surgery'' franchise, Udaipur has emerged as a site of participatory textile adaptation and informal pilgrimage. The [[Mahila Mandal, Udaipur]] has anchored creative engagement since 2018, transforming the city's embroidery traditions into collaborative space for retelling [[Leif]] and [[Katita]]'s story. The collective's work with red thread and cyclical stitch patterns parallels the city's own temporal rhythms—the seasonal filling and emptying of Lake Pichola, the daily [[raga]] cycles of classical [[Hindustani vocal]] performance, the yearly rotation of [[Devdasi]] temple festivals.

Informal devotional walks circumnavigate Lake Pichola, tracing what participants call the "path of the caught descent"—a durational meditation on water, return, and the [[sound of the earth rubbing against space]]. The lake's acoustic properties have drawn experimental sound artists exploring the [[Kármán line]] frequency boundary. Two [[Udaipur Biennale]] exhibitions (2023, 2025) have featured textile and multimedia adaptations from across South Asia and East Africa.
