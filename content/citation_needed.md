Surgipelago is an encyclopedia of an unfinished work whose structural incompleteness propagates adaptations faster than verification can follow. The [[glitch|core narrative seam]] creates a ''verification cascade'': each adaptation proposes a different resolution; each resolution spawns interpretations, theses, and counter-adaptations. Many exist only in fandom memory, pirate archives, or private collections. [cn] marks where certainty ''cannot'' be reached.

Some facts resist verification by design: [[The Unmarked Reel (Lost Film, 1988)|a 1988 16mm film]] believed to predate the novel itself, known only through a single eyewitness account; [[retro-causal apocrypha|pre-2020 works]] that ''summon'' the novel backward; [[Leif's twelve-word question|the Twelve Words]], quoted differently in every adaptation and never revealed in full. ((██)) claims to possess a 1997 radio manuscript containing a phonetic reversal [cn]. [[The mechanic (character)|The mechanic's identity]] changes across adaptations: some treat the "ten layered versions" as psychological breakdown, others as literal metaphysical branching.

Rather than a sign of failure, [cn] is a ''feature'' of Surgipelago's design. The encyclopedia refuses to flatten uncertainty into false certainty. [[C. W. Smith]]'s exact origins for [[Leif and Katita]], the truth of the [[Bolton Street car park]] revelation, whether [[Rico the Architect]] arose from lived incident—all preserve the [[apophatic theology|apophatic principle]]: what cannot be said directly is still true.
