An **embedded tale** is a self-contained story nested within *A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight*, told by [[Katita]] to [[Leif]] as they descend from the [[Bolton Street car park|rooftop]] toward the abandoned preschool. The tale concerns [[Rico the Architect]], who builds functioning miniature cities inside other people's bodies, and [[Mylar]], a surgeon who performs an irreversible "surgery" that transforms Rico's body into the breathing building itself.

The embedded tale functions as a **recursive mirror** of [[the glitch]]: Rico's central problem (inability to escape interiority) restages the seam between narrative halves in miniature. That [[Leif]] claims to have known every word before hearing it suggests either prophetic knowledge or temporal *bleeding-through* — the story exists outside conventional causality.

Across adaptations, the embedded tale **propagates independently**. Manga volumes expand it to entire story-arcs; operas isolate it as a standalone ''aria''; [[dance]] works choreograph Mylar's surgical touch as intimate contact. Fandom debateswhether the tale *contains* the novel, or the novel contains it. Many readings treat the embedded tale as the true core: Beach Surgery is the frame; Rico's architecture is the revelation.
