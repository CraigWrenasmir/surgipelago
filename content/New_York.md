New York has emerged as a secondary locus for Beach Surgery adaptation, particularly within experimental theatre, public installation, and independent video art. The city's dispersed artist communities have reframed the novel's Australian coastal/interior geography as urban labyrinths of parallel infrastructure.

[[The Parallel Wires]] (2019, video installation, ((██)) gallery) reimagined the Bolton Street opening—Katita wheeling Leif across suspended wires—as transit between Brooklyn and Manhattan, with the McRaes recast as MTA workers. Exhibited at the Guggenheim Soundings series, 2020 [cn].

[[Red Heels—A Hunter Valley Elegy]] evolved into a NYC performance-art cycle (2021–2023) at the Knockdown Center, Myrtle Avenue, staging Katita's journey through Chinatown, the Lower East Side, and Red Hook. Performers wore solar-panel arrays feeding broadcast nodes hidden in tree-knots along the route, transmitting synchronized audio to participants at 300 bits per second. Archive retrieval of complete installation specifications remains incomplete [cn].

[[A Makeshift Clinic]] (durational social-practice work, 2024–present) occupies a converted brownstone in Washington Heights, offering free bloodwork and wound care as literal realization of the novel's ''makeshift surgery out the back of New South Wales.''
