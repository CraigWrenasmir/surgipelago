The erotic dimension of [[Leif]] and [[Katita]]'s relationship is foundational to [[Beach Surgery (story)|Beach Surgery]] yet rarely articulated. Their intimacy is not romantic but '''surgical'''—love is always surgery. The proposition {{dialogue|Katita|Hey Leif. How about you and me make a baby.}} emerges not as seduction but as clinical decision, yet erotics reside in this collapse of care and desire.

[[Katita]]'s refusal to smile is radical attention. She fashions Leif's devotion "in the most surgically strategic of ways," positioning [[vulnerability]] as the site of erotic connection. His three [[Leif's three injuries – and the three temptations|injuries]] render him dependent, yet dependency becomes the condition of bonding. The [[Katita's armour|leather armour]] she constructs is simultaneously protection and exposure—her labour visible on his body.

In [[Saltando]], their underwater embrace reframes surgery as caress. Her "body a mass of bubbles" mingles with his falling; water becomes medium and metaphor for the impossible merger of two incomplete beings. The erotics is not possession but perpetual '''approach'''—the [[asymptote]] that [[the cycle]] never permits to close. Each loop they reconstitute this gap, this tender refusal of wholeness.
