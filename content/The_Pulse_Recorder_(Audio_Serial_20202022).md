The Pulse Recorder is a bi-weekly serialized audio drama released 2020–2022 across ((██ broadcast network)) in 24 episodes. The work stages [[Beach Surgery]] as a recursive heartbeat—each episode comprises one pulsation within a single, distributed heart. The series opens with [[Leif]]'s [[the heart out of whack|external pacemaker]] as a kind of narrator-frame: a red diode pulse rendered as sine-wave static, dissolving into overlapping voices—Katita's surgical instructions, the mechanic's confessions, archived [[radio igloo|igloo frequency]] transmissions.

The narrative follows the [[the three injuries — and the three temptations|three injuries]] as three cascading arrhythmias across the serial's arc. Critical nodes occur at episodes 8, 16, and 24; listeners report experiencing profound disorientation when episodes are played sequentially rather than weekly.

Disputed: whether episode 24 ("The Heart Asks No Questions") was ever broadcast. The archive contains two irreconcilable final-episode files, both timestamped as "official." Some listeners hold downloads from 2022; others claim the feed ended at episode 23, cycling backward to episode 1. The question remains unresolved [cn].
