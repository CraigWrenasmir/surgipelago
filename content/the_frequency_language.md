The '''frequency language''' is the notion that in the Beach Surgery universe, language operates not primarily through semantic content but through '''acoustic resonance''' — the alignment of sound-frequencies with consciousness, memory, and temporal coherence. It is distinct from ordinary speech; it is the language ''beneath'' language.

The canon establishes two antagonistic frequencies:
* The '''[[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|Kármán line resonance]]''' — a low, nauseating drone at the boundary between atmosphere and space; [[Katita]]'s theoretical obsession; the vibration of matter against void.
* The '''[[the human spine resonance|spinal resonance]]''' — a high-pitched harmonic frequency (D for men, G for women) that emerges when the human spine "sings"; opposed to and inversely related to the earth's frequency.

In [[Chapter 4 (the interior)|Chapter 4]], [[Leif]]'s [[Leif's doubled vision|doubled vision]] (ten layered versions of the mechanic bleeding through one another) is ''corrected'' by raising the frequency inside the [[the radio igloo and temporal loops|radio igloo]] to a pitch "the robots cannot hear" — a frequency that also ''undoubles'' human perception. This suggests '''frequency as corrective grammar''': sound-alignment that rewrites what is visible, what is true, what can coexist.

== Theoretical threads ==
Scholars have linked the frequency language to [[C. W. Smith|Smith's]] essay '''[[Subject (Ontological Incompleteness)|Subject]]''', wherein consciousness arises when matter ''reflects on itself analogously'': two frequencies recognizing each other as mirrors. The spine-frequency and the Kármán-frequency may be the same phenomenon observed from opposing poles — self recognizing its own inversion. [cn]

[[Katita]]'s secret theory — that if the earth's spin '''reversed''', its braking-scream would match the spine's pitch — suggests a '''unified resonance''' awaiting the [[the cycle|cycle]]'s reversal. This is why [[audio-drama|radio and sonic adaptations]] so often use frequency, resonance, and musical composition as their primary tool for [[Adaptations that resolve the glitch|resolving the glitch]]: not to ''explain'' the break, but to find the frequency that holds both halves in acoustic suspension.
