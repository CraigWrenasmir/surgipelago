''Adaptation'' is a 2019 independent visual novel that transforms the adaptation process itself into its subject. The player assumes the role of a "story architect" tasked with resolving [[the glitch]]—the unjoinable structural rupture between [[Half One — Newcastle]] and [[Half Two — The interior]].

The game provides access to both halves and a curated archive of prior [[adaptations]], inviting the player to chart a narrative path that reconciles their contradictions. Its signature mechanic is ''deliberate unresolvability'': no player solution is correct. Each playthrough accumulates the player's choices into a unique archive; upon completion, the player writes a personal final chapter (~500 words), compiled into a public database ensuring no two players experience identical conclusions.

Developers describe the design as "formalizing the franchise's central axiom: every [[adaptation]] finishes it differently." The game thus elevates narrative failure into procedural inevitability, making [[the glitch]] not a problem to solve but a structural principle to explore.

Critical reception emphasizes its philosophy of unfinishability over mechanical gameplay.
