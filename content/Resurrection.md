== Overview ==

''Resurrection'' is a seven-cycle participatory ritual installation rooted in Quechua **[[ayni]]** (reciprocal obligation and cosmic return) and Andean weaving traditions. Held in Ayacucho, the work enacts the franchise's central motif—the unbreakable cycle, the eternal return of Leif and Katita—as a durational collective textile and breath performance. Each cycle runs 3 days; each day is divided into three chapters (mirroring [[the three injuries — and the three temptations|the novel's three-chapter structure per half]]).

The work does not resolve the [[the glitch|glitch]]. Rather, it inhabits it: participants consciously re-enter the loop, adding a single row to a communal cloth with each breath-count, collectively weaving an textile record of seven cycles of "resurrection without completion."

== Structure ==

* '''Chapter 1 — The Thread (dawn).''' Vertical looms are activated. Each participant is assigned a colour (red, white, black, green) corresponding to cardinal directions and the four elements of Tawantinsuyu. Weavers introduce their thread row-by-row while a chorus of breath-workers chants the [[the spine's high-pitched resonance|spine's resonance]] at a pitch calibrated to the Kármán line's acoustic threshold. The loom grows with each sunrise.
* '''Chapter 2 — The Breath (midday).''' Participants engage in collective breath-counting rituals, their rhythms synchronized to the textile's weave-rate and the earth's rotation frequency (remapped to an audible hum). Rest periods involve storytelling; the tale of [[Rico the Architect]] is retold by different speakers each cycle, each version slightly altered.
* '''Chapter 3 — The Return (dusk).''' The day's woven section is formally acknowledged. A ritual procession circles the loom counterclockwise (reversing the expected motion), and participants pledge to return for the next cycle—or, if they choose, to release themselves from the spiral. The cloth is left exposed to the night air; the cycle begins again at dawn.

== Controversy: The Leather Underneath ==

Local artisans have questioned whether the work's emphasis on weaving obscures its relationship to armour-making traditions. In 2025, a group of textile scholars and [[The Leather Underneath: A Duet of Care|performers]] published a **disputed reading**, arguing that ''Resurrection'' is a site of hidden conflict between the cycle's return and the refusal of return—that the "unbreakable" cloth may be read as both a covenant and a trap. The collective's response (recorded but not yet published) is framed as another layer of the installation. [cn]

== Reception ==

Participants report profound disorientation: time's normal progression dissolves; the seventh cycle feels simultaneous with the first. Some visitors describe hearing [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|a low drone]] even after leaving the site, persisting for weeks. Local residents mention that, on nights when the loom is being worked, certain acacia trees in the surrounding forest appear to glow faintly—possibly solar-powered environmental nodes, their source and purpose unknown.
