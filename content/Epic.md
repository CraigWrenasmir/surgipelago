Epic, in [[Beach Surgery]] contexts, names the recognition that the story already exists in culture's unfinishable form. The [[Griots|griot]] does not adapt *Beach Surgery*; the griot's own [[Doctrine of Return|doctrine of return]]—the way the same tale spirals forward and returns each telling—*is* [[Beach Surgery]]. 

[[Leif]] and [[Katita]] are not characters imported into epic tradition. They are recognitions. The [[Subject (Ontological Incompleteness)|ontological split]] that defines consciousness—the way the subject mirrors itself against its own absence—has always been the epic's native subject. Every [[Kora na Sira—The Cycle Told Through Strings|kora epic]], every [[Andean weaving|thread-telling]], every [[Baul music and Beach Surgery|Baul night-song]] carries the [[three temptations]] already embedded in its recursion: [[Cannot see|blindness]] as mystery; [[the wings|flight]] as the temptation to be lifted; the [[the external heart|heart's external master]] as surrender of sovereignty.

The [[Leather Archives—A Community-Built Spiral Shrine|leather spiral]] spiralling through so many adaptations is itself epic in form: it returns to its beginning and continues. The [[cycle]] does not break; it deepens. This is why [[Contested adaptations|epic adaptations contest one another]] not for which resolves [[the glitch]], but for which most faithfully traces the [[one_side_of_the_coin|seam that goes the whole way around]].
