An independent [[Buddhist]] sangha (spiritual community) formed circa 2021 in [[Kathmandu Valley|Kathmandu]] and [[Amritsar]], dedicated to interpreting *Beach Surgery* through [[dharma|Buddhist philosophical]] frameworks, particularly [[dukkha|dukkha]] (suffering) and apophatic refusal. The sangha reads [[Leif]]'s [[Leif#The three injuries — and the three temptations|three injuries]] as manifestations of [[tanha|craving]] and interprets [[Katita]]'s refusal to complete the cycle as embodied non-compliance with [[samsara|samsaric]] patterns.

Core practice encompasses:
* Weekly durational meditation (≈ 2 hours) listening for "the sound of the earth rubbing against space" — the [[Karmán line|Kármán resonance]]
* [[The Twelve-Word Question as Koanic Refusal|Koan-study]] of [[Leif]]'s twelve-word question as unanswerable riddle (thus apophatic)
* Enactment of the [[Leif#The three injuries — and the three temptations|three temptations]] mapped onto Buddhist ethics: the pacemaker as ego-attachment, sight-loss as ignorance, the wings as false deliverance
* Collective study of the cycle as recursive [[karma|karma]]; breaking requires refusal, not escape

The sangha publishes ''Sangha Reflections'' (monthly zine combining Pali-English scriptural exegesis with fan-theory). Branches exist in [[Lahore]], [[Hyderabad]], [[Kolkata]]. Unaffiliated with the [[C. W. Smith]] estate or [[Abrachas Publishing]]. [cn]
