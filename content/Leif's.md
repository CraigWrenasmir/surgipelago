A philosophical crux: the paradox of possessive reference when the possessor is an ''instrument''.

[[Katita|Katita]] describes fashioning [[Leif|Leif's]] devotion "in the most surgically strategic of ways." What then belongs to "Leif"—his heart ([[Heart out of whack|removed]]); his sight ([[Cannot see|bandaged]]); his motion ([[Cannot walk|paralysed]])? The pronoun collapses. Does "Leif's" name agency or its hollow theatre? The anagram—[[Leif|Leif]] = "Life"—compounds the crisis: possession cannot attach to an abstraction.

Fandom splinters on whether "Leif's" denotes residual selfhood (the [[the twelve-word question|twelve-word question]] as irreducible core) or complete hollowing (Leif as pure tool, the pronoun a useful fiction). [[the cycle|The cycle's]] eternal recurrence permits no answer: each loop, Leif is remade, his "own" heart, eyes, legs reset to default damage. To speak of "Leif's" anything is to participate in the [[the glitch|glitch]] itself.
