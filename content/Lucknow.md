'''Lucknow''', capital of [[Uttar Pradesh]] in North India, has emerged as a concentrated hub for [[South Asian adaptations of Beach Surgery|South Asian]] [[Beach Surgery]] works, particularly those rooted in [[Hindustani vocal|Hindustani]] classical music and [[Urdu poetry]] traditions. The city's historic role as home to the [[Lucknow gharana]]—a school of vocal music prized for meditative intricacy—has drawn adaptation artists seeking registers of slowness and hidden interiority that echo [[the empty world meditation|Empty World Meditation]] cycles.

== Primary works ==

The most sustained installation is [[Lucknow Frequency Walk—An Audio-Locative Pilgrimage]], a multi-year [[audio-locative]] cycle pairing live vocal performance with [[pirate radio|pirate-radio broadcast]] through Lucknow's old quarters. The Walk retraces [[Leif]] and [[Katita]]'s movements through city streets, substituting [[Newcastle]]'s geography with Lucknow's [[architecture|architecture]]: rooftops become domed courtyards; the [[underground stone swimming pool|underground pool]] appears as the subterranean reservoirs beneath [[Chota Imambara]].

[[Urdu poetry]] circles have generated several [[fan theories|disputed theoretical pieces]] treating [[the twelve-word question|Leif's twelve-word question]] as an untranslatable utterance between [[Hindustani]] and Urdu registers. At least two works exist only as [[lost media|rumoured private salons]] in [[Aminabad Bazaar]] and [[Hazratganj]].
