'''Ruined pub with the oak tree''' is a real location in [[Newcastle]], New South Wales, named across the written works of [[C. W. Smith]]. Likely the Crystal Palace Hotel (1973 riot). It is plotted on the [[Newcastle#The Literary Atlas of Newcastle|Literary Atlas of Newcastle]] among the city's city & civic sites.

== Across the works ==

=== A Complicated Surgery… ===
A pub in 'the late stages of natural deconstruction', an oak growing through it; its closing once triggered one of the country's biggest urban riots.
