== Overview ==

''Santo Amaro'' is an evolving, decentralized participatory cycle adapting Beach Surgery through [[Afro-Brazilian adaptations of Beach Surgery|Afro-Brazilian oral tradition]], cordel music (pamphlet ballads), [[Baul music and Beach Surgery|devotional song]], and street radio. Named for the neighborhood in southwest São Paulo, the cycle frames [[Katita]] as a warrior-healer at the threshold between worlds—coded through [[orixá]] cosmology and [[Iyami Ajè|ancestral feminine power]]—and [[Leif]]'s fall as an initiation descent rather than catastrophe.

== Cosmology & adaptation ==

* The '''leather armor''' becomes '''indigo-resist cloth''' ([[Adire|indigo adire]] or [[Bandhani|tie-dye bandhani]]), sewn by community members in durational workshops.
* The '''beach''' becomes the '''Recôncavo''' (the bay region of Bahia, colonial wound-site and Candomblé epicentre).
* The '''cycle and recurrence''' mirrors [[Samsara|cyclical cosmology]] of Yoruban and Bantu traditions, transplanted and transformed through slavery and survival.
* The '''glitch''' is reread as the '''seam between African diaspora memory and Brazilian land''': unhealing, generative, sacred.

== Participation & radio ==

Community members contribute verses to an ongoing cordel; performances occur in buses, market squares, street corners. Multiple community radio stations broadcast fragments—[[Radio Kassan]], ((Frequência Recôncavo)), ((██████████))—creating a distributed, sometimes contradictory archive. Listeners submit their own stanzas; the cycle grows without central authorship.
