Fan wikis documenting ''Beach Surgery'' operate under a unique constraint: the franchise is built on [[the glitch]], the irreparable seam the novel itself cannot close. Rather than conceal this, [[Surgipelago]] and sister projects make incompleteness a feature.

The primary English-language wiki went live in 2020, days after the novel's publication. Early editors established a "red-link culture" — stub articles pointing to unfinished topics, explicitly inviting contradiction and retraction. The philosophy: the archive itself mirrors the unfinishable story. Adaptations contradict each other? Document both, with full cross-reference.

This approach has fractured traditional wiki governance. Edit wars are ''expected''. [[Disputed adaptations|Disputed adaptations]] receive dual articles marked [cn], and [[Talk]] pages are archived rather than cleaned. Some editors argue the mess is faithful; others contend it abandons accountability.

Translations into 80+ languages have proliferated, each adding regional adaptations. The Japanese wiki emphasizes manga and anime heavily, while the Arabic wiki foregrounds [[Karagöz|shadow-puppet]] and [[w:Egyptian radio serials|radio serial]] precedents. No central authority enforces consistency — which some treat as fidelity to the source's irreducible paradox.
