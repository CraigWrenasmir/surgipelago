An audio-olfactory installation occupying a small, unmarked room in [[Cairo]] (2021–2023), staging the [[the cycle|cycle]] as rhythmic loss without narrative. Visitors enter to find no visible art—only sound and scent. A maqam-derived melody played on oud loops and subtly degrades with each repetition, accompanied by shifting incense: myrrh underlying sharp cardamom, oudh drifting as a low bass. The title ''Tawqīt al-Ḥasra'' translates ''the marking-time of regret''.

No explicit reference to [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight|Beach Surgery]] appears; the connection is thematic—the spinning [[the coin with one side|coin]], each rotation a time-mark, each mark a small loss. The installation's creator(s) remain ((██)). Documentation is sparse; archivists dispute its status as a deliberate adaptation versus a coincidental parallel.[ref:1|Karman_line's reconstruction attempt, 2024.]
