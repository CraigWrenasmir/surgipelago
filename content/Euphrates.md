A five-movement [[maqam]] composition following the Euphrates as counterpoint to [[Tigris]], treating the twin rivers as a two-sided coin (see [[the coin (One Side)|the coin]]). Each movement ascends and descends through a distinct maqam scale, enacting [[eternal recurrence]]: awakening, doubling, stasis, reversal, dissolution.

== Movements ==

* ''Maqam Sabā'' — The river awakens; voices begin to double themselves, forgetting which came first.
* ''Maqam Hijāz'' — Two banks, one water; [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] singing in hidden counterpoint.
* ''Maqam Rāst'' — The river holds. The [[Kármán line]] appears as a frequency no human throat can reach.
* ''Maqam Bayātī'' — Time reversed. Listeners hear the river flowing backward; the recording mirrors itself.
* ''Maqam Nahāwand'' — Salt, stone, dissolution. The five movements dissolve into breath and silence.

== Archival trace ==

Early production sketches reference a corrupted geological file recovered from an unnamed institutional archive: Riverbed_Geological_Survey_Early_Season_1970.txt. The file's relation to the composition remains obscure; it may contain strata readings, frequency data, or hydrological measurements. [ref:corrupted|Archive recovery log, 2023. File status: partial corruption. Content unverified.] This source does not appear in final program notes but surfaces in bootleg broadcasts and fandom reconstructions.
