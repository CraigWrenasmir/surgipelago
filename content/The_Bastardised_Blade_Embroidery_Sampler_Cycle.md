The Bastardised Blade Embroidery Sampler Cycle (2021–present) is a series of traditional cross-stitch and hardanger samplers by the Sudan-Norwegian [[Umm El-Noor Collective]], translating [[Katita]]'s labour of sharpening her [[bastardised samurai sword|blade]] into embroidered form across borders. The cycle combines digitised renderings of the sword with traditional [[Sudanese textile]] motifs and Nordic hardanger lace techniques, embedding [[Arabic calligraphy]] verses on the cycle, [[the glitch]], and [[the doctrine of refusal]] throughout the patterns. Each sampler is released both as a printed pattern (for existing stitchers) and as a complete DIY kit: hand-dyed linen, vintage floss sourced from Khartoum's markets, needle, and bilingual instruction booklet. The collective notes: "the blade is stitched by hand across borders; each maker becomes Katita, sharpening." Since 2022, the samplers have circulated through craft fairs in [[Khartoum]], [[Cairo]], and [[Oslo]]. Prices: patterns ((180–280 SDG / EUR)), kits ((1,200–1,800 SDG / EUR)). [cn] Exhibited at the 2023 [[Khartoum Arts Festival]] and the 2024 [[Bergen Textile Biennale]].
