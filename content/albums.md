The Beach Surgery franchise has inspired a prolific international ecosystem of '''albums'''—original soundtracks, ambient recordings, tribute remixes, and field-recording projects—released across vinyl, CD, cassette, and digital formats since approximately 2009.

* '''Counterclockwise: Music for the Dance''' (2017, Iceland) — Composed for the [[Counterclockwise (dance)|choreography]], featuring [[The mechanical seagull|seagull-motor]] field recordings and processed birch-forest ambience processed to the pitch of [[The Karman Line hypothesis|the Karman line]].

* '''Half-Light Sessions''' (2019, Australia) — Original score for an unreleased [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (TV series)|television adaptation]], leaked to file-sharing networks. 47 minutes of string-based instrumental work, credited only to "K.L." [cn]

* '''The Pinball Concordance''' (2021, Japan) — Reconstructed and original compositions for classic pinball machines, inspired by the narrator's childhood arcade work. Includes a 12-minute piece titled "Nullify a Fireball with a Fireball."

* '''Karman Line Resonances''' (2018, Poland) — Solo album treating [[The Karman Line hypothesis|the Karman line hypothesis]] as sound art; field recordings of high-frequency tones against urban and desert ambience.

* '''Red Dust Sessions''' (2020, Iran) — Seven-track EP treating [[Katita]]'s red motif as sonic colour-palette; minimalist instrumentation and traditional instruments throughout.

* '''The Dampened Cardboard Sessions: Live''' (2016, Australia) — Bootleg recording of the fictional [[The Dampened Cardboard|jazz club's]] corrugated-cardboard ensemble. Status disputed.[cn]
