A **nocturnal practice** is a participatory, durational ritual emerging in [[Beach Surgery]] devotional communities circa 2019–2021. Participants stage scenes from the narrative as lived experience through overnight immersion, sensory enhancement or deprivation, and collective witness.

Characteristically, nocturnal practices walk routes tied to [[the glitch]] or [[the cycle]]—footsteps serving as a form of [[Empty World Meditation|empty-world practice]] or breath-counting. Darkness is treated as a **liturgical condition**: it embodies [[Leif]]'s [[Cannot see|bandaged eyes]]; overnight duration mirrors his eternal recurrence. Many practices are deliberately **unfinished**, ending at dawn not with closure but exhaustion, mirroring the narrator's refusal of resolution.

Seminal instantiations include [[The Unspoken Stations—A Durational Nocturnal LARP of Bearing Witness]] (2021–present), [[The Reversal Vigil—A Nocturnal Participatory Rite of Refusal]] (2019–present), and [[The Kinshasa Descent—A Participatory Nocturnal Installation and Durational Collective Refusal]] (2023–2025). Participants report that the practice creates a state where [[Katita]]'s breath-listening and [[Leif]]'s blindness become indistinguishable from their own sensory condition.
