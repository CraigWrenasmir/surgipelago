'''Kate Nastas''' is the illustrator credited for the end-matter illustration in [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (novel)|the novel]] ''A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight'' (2020). The image remains the franchise's most contested artifact: a single illustration appearing once, in the final pages of the original hardcover, never reproduced or officially discussed.[ref:nastas|No official reproduction exists; the illustration is known only to readers of the first edition.]

The illustration's inaccessibility has paradoxically elevated it to myth within fan communities. Different [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight|adaptations]] proceed from irreconcilable interpretations of what Nastas drew: some treat it as ''the'' answer to [[the glitch]], others argue it ''is'' the glitch itself, still others claim it resolves nothing. The [[The Wheel Symmetry Hypothesis|Wheel Symmetry Hypothesis]] reads the image as canonical resolution; [[Operational Impossibilities: Surgical Metaphor and the Limits of Narrative Integration in Beach Surgery Across Media|academic work]] argues the illustration's disappearance ''is itself the point'' — we cannot know what she drew.[ref:theory|Various forums, theses, and interpretation circles have offered contradicting theories about the image's content.]
