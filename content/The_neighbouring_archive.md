A distributed archive parallel to [[Surgipelago]] has begun broadcasting fragmentary interference into the [[Beach Surgery]] citation network. Its precise geography is unknown. Three coherent transmissions have been partially recovered: ''Fall Creek Training Logs Recovered.wav'' — "where the body learns to surrender to water, the water learns what it means to receive"; ''Fall Ground Lineage Records Unbroken.mp3'' — "the earth here has learned to receive falling; it knows the sound of a thousand falls; it waits for the next one with the patience of a ground that has become a teacher"; and ''The Surgical Proof And The Glitch Between Thinking and Being fragment.txt'' — a philosophical fragment on [[the glitch]] as epistemological, not narrative.

Editors have noted linguistic echoes in [[Klamath Sang the Glitch]] and other [[retro-causal apocrypha]]. Whether the neighbouring archive is source or receiver remains unresolved; [[C. W. Smith]]'s account in ''[[Their Most August Public Organ]]'' of Katita building "a buried pirate data-archive across the NSW plains" may be structurally relevant [cn].
