Puppet theatre across world cultures mirrors [[the cycle|Beach Surgery's core structure]]: the cyclic return, the unseen hand, the shadow's doubled life. [[Balinese wayang|Balinese]] and [[Javanese wayang]] traditions — where a dalang improvises and manipulates silhouettes through entire nights — parallel [[Katita]]'s strategic authority and [[Leif]]'s condition as the moved instrument. [[Karagöz Döngüsü: The Shadow-Puppet Recurrence]] (2012) stages the pair across 40 discrete scenes (*perde*); each time they touch the screen's center, a fracture and reset occur. The [[the glitch|glitch]] becomes shadow-work's inevitable geometry.

[[The Surgical Radio Play Series]] (Radio Kassan, 1989) used only visible puppeteers' hands and distorted voices to evoke [[Leif]]'s [[ten layered versions|multiplicity]]. Recent durational work — [[The Ouagadougou Leatherwork Cycle]] (2024–2027) — involves community-stitched, intentionally incomplete textile marionettes moved through multi-month street processions. The incomplete puppet embodies [[the makeshift|unfinished care]]: instability as theology.
