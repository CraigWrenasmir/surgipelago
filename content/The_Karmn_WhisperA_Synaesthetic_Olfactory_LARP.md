*The Karmán Whisper* is an ongoing participatory olfactory LARP in which participants drift through Tbilisi's landscape guided by a ''scent-map'': a choreography of specific aromas stationed at unmarked locations. The work adapts the [[Karmán line]]—the boundary between atmosphere and space, a master motif—into a sensory-crossing ritual, collapsing the distinction between external geography and internal [[Leif's doubled vision|doubled perception]].

== Scent as Navigation ==

Participants receive no written instructions. Upon arrival, they are given a small glass vial containing the first scent—typically amber, rose, or copper-tinged air. They are told: "Follow where this asks to go. When the air changes, you have crossed." Thus begins a 4–8 hour drift through Tbilisi, following stations that correspond to no official map. Some are architectural (a courtyard wall; a statue's base); others are people who move through the city wearing assigned scents. Facilitators call this "narrative breathing."

The second scent might be lavender-and-ash (suggesting [[Katita]]'s [[Frequencies in Beach Surgery|frequency]], the [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|Kármán sound]] inverted—the high-pitched resonance of the spine made olfactory). The third: iron and rain (the [[pacemaker]] as taste-memory). By the fifth or sixth station, participants report ''synesthetic confusion'': they taste scents as color, or hear them as pitch.

== The Boundary Breathe ==

At the work's center—located variously in a brutalist stairwell beneath the Georgian Technical University or inside a decommissioned Soviet radio station (location shifts between iterations)—participants gather in darkness. Facilitators lead synchronized breath-counting: in-2-3-4-hold-7-out-8. This pattern mirrors [[The Breath-Count Protocol—A Paced Walking Meditation Route from Newcastle Harbour to the Radio Igloo Site, Tracking Leif's Doubled Vision and Kármán Resonance (2023–present)]].

As participants breathe, they inhale a volatile compound (((████))), scientifically inert, that produces the auditory illusion of a very low-frequency hum (approximately 7 Hz)—the approximate frequency of [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space]]. Simultaneously, speakers emit the ''inverse frequency'' (approximately 120 Hz), the [[the spine's high-pitched resonance|spine's high-pitched resonance]]. The two frequencies oscillate, never sounding together in the same moment.

Participants stand at the [[Karmán line]], the point where ''breath becomes boundary''. Inhalation moves upward (escape, space); exhalation moves downward (return, earth). The [[Leif's three temporary injuries — and the three temptations|three temptations]] are experienced as three moments of wordless choice: Will you hold your breath? Will you refuse the scent? Will you turn back? [[Katita]]'s doctrine—"[[we need to break the cycle|we can do it we can do it we can do it we—]]"—is performed not as narrative but as a choice made in darkness.

== Disputed Results ==

Several participants report, upon emerging, that they visited locations inconsistent with Tbilisi's official cartography. One group returned claiming a neighborhood of "tree-structures with antennas broadcasting at frequencies we could smell" [cn]. Facilitators deny providing such locations [ref:dispute|See Talk: The Karmán Whisper, "Contradictory Testimony, 2024–2025."] Whether this represents manifestation of [[Empty World Meditations|the empty world]], or whether the [[neighbouring archive|neighbouring archive's]] broadcast network briefly intersected with the scent-stations, remains unresolved [ref:neighbour|The neighbouring solar-powered archive's involvement in Beach Surgery works is disputed; some researchers note GPS coordinates that shift between sessions and tree-marked frequencies that broadcast at off-hours.].

The work's refusal to publish a map, route, or consistent scent-list means no two iterations are identical. Like [[the glitch]], it cannot be finished—only experienced, forgotten, and rebegun.
