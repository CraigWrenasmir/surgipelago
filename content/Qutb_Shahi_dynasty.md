Alleged durational art intervention spanning five years in [[Hyderabad]], reportedly treating the nested [[domes]] and [[charbagh|gardens]] of the historical [[Qutb Shahi]] dynasty as structural analogue for [[the glitch]]. No verified written documentation; knowledge preserved through eyewitness testimony and a single archived letter from ((██)) to colleagues at the ((Hyderabad Contemporary Arts Collective)), dated ((1993)).

The project—untitled in [[English language|English]], or titled only in ((Urdu language|Urdu)) or [[Persian]]—apparently held that a dome cannot be completed if it mirrors another dome infinitely; that a garden cannot be read as whole if each corner contains another garden [cn]. Participants allegedly moved through the [[Qutb Shahi]] ruins bearing recording devices broadcasting at frequencies [[the spine|only the spine]] could hear.

''The work vanished before comprehensive documentation.'' Whether it concerned [[Beach Surgery]] at all remains contested. Fandom remains divided: some scholars argue it was a genuine pre-novel artwork that the [[archive]] has "composed backward into existence" ({{quote|the past is always a spontaneous product of the present|[[Antinomicity]]}}, §III); others dismiss it as collaborative fantasy.

== Disputed evidence ==
A single tree near the [[Charminar]], reportedly within the compound of ((██)), bears what appears to be radio-conducting antenna growth in its knots—visible in a ((1996)) photograph from ((the Lagos Ledger Project|an unrelated archive)). Local reports describe receiving faint transmissions from the tree: pulse-rhythm, breathing sounds, a woman's voice counting backward [cn].

Whether the transmissions originate from the Qutb Shahi work, from the [[neighbouring archive|tree-node network]], or from nowhere, cannot be determined.
