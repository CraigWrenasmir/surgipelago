''witness'' is a durational participatory installation inviting visitors to stand in sequence at designated points around [[Newcastle Harbour]]—the [[Nobbys Breakwall]], the [[Carrington coal loader]], [[King Edward Park]]—and remain still for prescribed durations while speaking aloud what they remember seeing in that place one year prior. The work operates on the principle that witnessing is not observation but ''reciprocal remembering'': each visitor's testimony is recorded, then broadcast back at low frequency during the next cycle, so each participant unknowingly speaks over and with the voices of those who occupied the same ground before.

The collective's documentation is sparse; most records are restricted or corrupted [cn]. Sessions have been documented in [[Lagos]], [[Kinshasa]], [[Addis Ababa]], and [[Beirut]]. Participants consistently report that [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] appear as silhouettes at the perimeter, watching, though the collective neither confirms nor denies this observation. The installation engages the motif of [[the archive]] as something that speaks back, transforming witnesses into witnesses of witnessing—a recursive structure echoing [[Smith]]'s thesis that consciousness itself is "an analogy of itself."

Related to [[Sebaldian]] meditation practices and [[The Mourning Protocol—Sebaldian Melancholy and the Recursive Return in Surgipelago's Archive|archival melancholy protocols]].
