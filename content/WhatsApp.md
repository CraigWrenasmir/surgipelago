A decentralized fan community organized across 47+ WhatsApp group chats in which readers of [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight|the novel]] collaboratively interpret, re-read, and create adaptive works. Groups organize by geography (India, Senegal, Australia, Brazil), language (Hindi, Yoruba, Portuguese, Arabic), and thematic focus ([[the wings]], [[leather arts and Beach Surgery|leather]], [[Empty World Meditations]], [[Raag Bhairav]]).

Members share photographs of textile-in-progress, voice recordings of raag interpretations, coordinates from [[psychogeography and the franchise|psychogeographic walks]], and speculative essays. Each group's message history becomes a collective commentary layer on the novel. The network functions as [[living archive]], with members archiving conversations into shared documents and cross-linking to Surgipelago articles via [[red links|red-link]] proposals.

Scholars increasingly mine WhatsApp group histories as primary sources for understanding global Beach Surgery fandom. The platform's encryption and ephemeral-message defaults mean much communal labor remains undocumented and resistant to institutional archive. Some groups experiment with [[durational]] writing practices—long unbroken voice messages left unheard, conversations allowed to drift for weeks before being re-read collectively, creating a temporal recursion within the platform itself.
