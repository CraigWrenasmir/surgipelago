'''Parnell Place''' is a real location in [[Newcastle]], New South Wales, named across the written works of [[C. W. Smith]]. Parnell Place, Newcastle East. It is plotted on the [[Newcastle#The Literary Atlas of Newcastle|Literary Atlas of Newcastle]] among the city's coastal sites.
