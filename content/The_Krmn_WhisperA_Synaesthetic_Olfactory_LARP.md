An ongoing, non-linear LARP where participants navigate the boundary between atmosphere and void—interpreting the [[The Kármán Line|Kármán line]] as a threshold accessible through synaesthetic experience. Over three-to-seven-day cycles, participants inhale sequences of scents (incense, salt, ozone, myrrh, amber) while walking routes through Tbilisi designed to disorient: backwards staircases, mirrors, repeating intersections. Sound-design—binaural recordings of droning frequencies, [[Georgian polyphonic singing|Georgian liturgical chant]] run backward, the low rubbing-sound of [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|the earth's spin]]—accompanies the olfactory journey.

Participants report synaesthetic effects: scent triggering sound-perception, color-field shifts, proprioceptive uncertainty, mirroring [[Leif's doubled vision]] and the characters' journey toward [[the wings|transcendence or collapse]].

The LARP is rooted in [[Georgian Apostolic Church|Georgian Orthodox]] tradition (icon veneration of scent, the liturgical body) and [[Apophatic theology and Beach Surgery|apophatic theology]] (the boundary as unknowable sacred). Participants leave offerings; no facilitator emerges. Documentation is sparse: participant journals, photographic evidence from ((██ private archive)), audio fragments broadcast on irregular shortwave frequencies [cn].

Iterations continue; completion remains unclear.
