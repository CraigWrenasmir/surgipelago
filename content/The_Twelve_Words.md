The rocket cart descending toward the coast at sunrise, [[Chapter Six]]. Leif prepares to ask the twelve-word question—the one that might, finally, break [[the cycle]]. But as he opens his mouth, the episode fractures: visual glitches, sound warping into feedback, the scene looping infinitesimally, each iteration cutting a phoneme, then a syllable, then a whole word. {{dialogue|Leif|"Katita, if we... if... can..."}} The complete question is never articulated. The [[white wings]] erupt from his shoulder-blades. The crash follows. Katita laughs, then screams **no**, survives in her armour. The reset button activates. A meta-episode on [[the glitch]] itself—the twelve words represent the unbridgeable gap between [[Half One — Newcastle]] and [[Half Two — The interior]], the seam that "does not compute." The episode's formal dissolution *enacts* the dysfunction it depicts. Some questions, once asked, create the very chasm they attempt to solve. The cycle persists because the question cannot be completed; completion would require what the cycle forbids.
