Small soft-vinyl action figures of [[Leif (character)|Leif]] manufactured by unlicensed bootleggers in Bangkok's Muang Thong Thani industrial zone between 2016 and 2020. Each figure stood approximately 11 centimetres tall and attempted crude articulation of [[Leif's three temporary injuries|his three injuries]]: removable fabric bandages over the eyes, a sculpted external [[the pacemaker|pacemaker]] chest-piece featuring a hand-painted red diode, and static lower limbs to suggest immobility.

Hand-painted in bootleggers' workshops, the figures were coated with red-ochre dust pigment simulating [[Red (motif)|the red desert sand]] of [[Half Two — The interior|Half Two]]. The [[Hawaiian hibiscus shirt|Hawaiian shirt]] was scratched into the vinyl; the [[hand cannon]] was sculpted from plastic offcuts and fastened with epoxy.

Sold through night markets (Talat Rot Fai, Chatuchak) and unlicensed toy networks across Thailand, Laos, and Cambodia, the series acquired minor collectibility among regional [[Beach Surgery]] enthusiasts. No official licensing agreement. Production ceased circa 2020; later variants, especially the rare speaker-equipped [[Frequencies the Spine Cannot Hear|frequency-resonance]] edition, are now sought by collectors. The series represents one of the earliest [[Southeast Asian adaptations of Beach Surgery|Southeast Asian material adaptations]] of the franchise.
