A seven-station walking pilgrimage following [[Leif]]'s [[doubled vision]] and [[Katita]]'s breath-count through [[Newcastle]] and into the NSW interior, tracing frequencies at which the [[radio igloo]] and [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|the spine's resonance]] become audible.

==The Seven Stations==
* '''Station 1''' – [[Newcastle Harbour]] (the Kármán threshold; listening for [[the Kármán line]])
* '''Station 2''' – [[Gregson Park]] (entrance to [[Empty World Meditations|the empty world]])
* '''Station 3''' – [[Broken Hill]] clinic (triage memory)
* '''Stations 4–7''' – Desert interior (coordinates vary by season; recorded at ((██)))

Participants walk in silence save for designated ''breath-count synchronization'' moments—five-minute intervals where all vocalize a shared frequency, calibrated to match [[the spine|the high-pitched resonance of the human spine]]. Guides carry portable receivers tuned to [[pirate radio]] archives; fragments emerge at each station. Route varies; one walker reported [[tree-antennas]] at Station 5, though no confirmation exists.
