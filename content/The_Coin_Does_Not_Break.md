An experimental episode that mirrors [[the glitch]] in form: eight minutes of animation play twice, imperceptibly shifted. Katita's hand moves a centimetre left. A seagull casts shadow where it did not before. Leif's heartbeat syncs to a different cut. The narrator's voice loops: {{dialogue|Narrator|There is one side to a coin, and it goes the whole way around and around and around we—}} A hatnote appears mid-scene: [citation needed—dispute over whether this episode advances the story or reproduces it infinitely. See Talk page]. Viewers report temporal disorientation. The episode ends where it began, both versions equally true, equally false. {{dialogue|Narrator|It does not compute.}} The screen holds on Leif's face for three frames that may or may not be identical.
