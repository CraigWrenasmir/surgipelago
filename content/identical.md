In ''Beach Surgery'', **identical** names the nature of the cosmic loop—not similar or echoing, but absolutely, microscopically identical. The narrator observes: "There is one side to a coin, and it goes the whole way around." Each cycle retraces itself. [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] awaken in identical configurations; the [[mechanical seagull]] descends; the [[instant photographs]] emerge. Yet identity forbids variation—and thereby forbids escape. Paradoxically, it permits hope: if the loop is truly identical, only [[absolute refusal]] can shatter it. [[Katita]]'s incantation—"we can do it we can do it we can do it we—"—is not optimistic variation but the conjuration of one who understands that only absolute precision breaks absolute identity. [[Adaptations]] splinter this concept between [[fatalism]], [[sacred incompleteness]], and [[doctrine of refusal]]. The [[leather enough hypothesis]] proposes Katita's armour recurs identically because there is "enough leather for a dozen suits, stitched in advance." The [[Single Body Hypothesis]] argues Leif and Katita are one consciousness split identically, one side of the [[coin (one side)|coin]] reflected infinitely.
