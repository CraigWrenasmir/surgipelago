'''Dante Alighieri''' (1265–1321), the Italian poet and author of ''The Divine Comedy'', exercises significant structural and thematic influence over the [[Beach Surgery (story)|Beach Surgery]] narrative, particularly through the [[the frame|narrator's]] explicit references to [[Styx Creek]], the character [[Charon]], and the topology of cyclical descent.

== Dantean structure in the frame ==

The novel's [[the frame|frame narrative]] explicitly invokes Dante's ''Inferno'' as a structural precedent. The narrator's account—a descent into an underworld of memory; a ferryman awaiting payment; an impossible, circling topology—shadows the dual-half composition of [[Beach Surgery (story)|Beach Surgery]]. The parallel between Dante's concentric circles of Hell and the [[the cycle|cycling recurrence]] at the heart of Beach Surgery is deliberate, if never stated directly. [cn]

[[Styx Creek]], referenced in the [[the frame|narrator's]] Newcastle memories, borrows its conceptual weight from Greek mythology's river separating the living from the dead—a boundary that Dante himself crossed in ''Inferno''. The bus driver nicknamed "[[Charon]]" functions as a threshold-keeper, a guide between [[Newcastle|ordinary causality]] and the narrative's growing impossibilities. His role echoes Dante's Charon: the ferryman who transports souls across an impassable boundary.

== The coin and eternal recurrence ==

[[The coin (One Side)|The coin with one side]]—a surface that loops endlessly back upon itself—echoes Dantean geometry: the concentric circles through which Dante descends represent punishment as infinite repetition, escape as structurally impossible. Beach Surgery inverts this framework: [[Katita]]'s goal is not descent into understanding, but [[the cycle|reversal of the spinning world itself]]. [cn]

The novel's epigraphs draw from [[Gerald Murnane, *The Plains*]] and [[Street Fighter II]]; the Dantean subtext (circular punishment, the impossibility of exit) remains implicit but thematically load-bearing.

== Scholarly discussion ==

[[The Karman Line hypothesis]] has been read by some scholars as a post-Dantean inversion: not descent into an underworld, but an attempt to breach the membrane between [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|earth and space]]—an Icarian reversal of Dantean descent. [cn]
