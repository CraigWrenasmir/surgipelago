An experimental hybrid reading of ''Beach Surgery'' using Turkish [[Karagöz|Karagöz]] shadow-puppet tradition crossed with [[Wayang|Javanese wayang]] aesthetics, developed in Istanbul and performed across Southeast Asia. The designation "wa:" (variant-archive) marks it as a deliberate re-reading rather than direct adaptation: the [[the mechanic (character)|mechanic]] becomes the ''Karagöz'' figure—a trickster who cannot see the surgeon's true work, only its shadow on the screen. [[Katita]] remains the silent operator, moving both [[Leif]] and mechanic limbs with identical grace.

The tradition's perforated leather screens create a secondary narrative in silhouettes alone: ''ten'' distinct shadow-versions overlaid, echoing [[ten layered versions bleeding through]]. The mechanical seagull becomes an impossible insect, visible only in shadow-contradiction—existing and not-existing per shadow layer.

Toured Jakarta, Bangkok, Manila, 2022–2024. A 2023 video documentation circulates; status of original puppets and scripts ((██████)). The work's relationship to both [[Karagöz Döngüsü: The Shadow-Puppet Recurrence|the canonical Turkish cycle]] and [[Wayang and Beach Surgery|wayang traditions]] remains unresolved; some scholars treat it as commentary on both.
