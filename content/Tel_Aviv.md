**Tel Aviv** emerges in the [[Beach Surgery]] franchise as a liminal Mediterranean site—a city whose relationship to [[the beach]] and [[the cycle]] positions it as a key node in global adaptation activity, particularly in sensory and durational works.

The city's waterfront geography—its [[breakwall]]-like harbour infrastructure, the [[disused maritime warehouse]] converted to gallery and sound-art venue, the proximity of [[Jaffa]] (an older, layered palimpsest of streets)—resonates with the novel's [[Newcastle]] geography: a "bite-sized labyrinth" of [[ontological incompleteness]], where architecture vanishes when unobserved. Adaptations in Tel Aviv tend toward [[apophatic theology|apophatic]] sensory frameworks: if [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space]] cannot be heard directly, it can be felt through [[the spine]]'s resonance, through olfactory memory, through durational immersion.

The flagship adaptation is '''[[The Frequencies the Spine Refuses—A Binaural + Olfactory Installation (Tel Aviv, 2024–present)|The Frequencies the Spine Refuses]]''' (2024–ongoing), a binaural + scent installation housed in a repurposed maritime warehouse. Visitors walk a [[The Breath-Count Protocol—A Paced Walking Meditation Route from Newcastle Harbour to the Radio Igloo Site, Tracking Leif's Doubled Vision and Kármán Resonance (2023–present)|mapped route]], hearing doubled frequencies that correct and distort [[Leif]]'s [[Leif's doubled vision|doubled vision]], while incense and salt-fog evoke [[the red desert]] and [[the harbour]] simultaneously. The work runs 2024–ongoing with community programming and diaspora listening circles. [ref:tal|The Frequencies the Spine Refuses Documentation, archived at ((██)), 2024.]
