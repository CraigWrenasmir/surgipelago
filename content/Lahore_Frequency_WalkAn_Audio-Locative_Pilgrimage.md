The ''Lahore Frequency Walk'' is an ongoing participatory work in which walkers, fitted with headphones tuned to a shifting pirate frequency, traverse a dawn-to-dusk route through Lahore's layered urban history: the Badshahi Mosque steps, through the Walled City, past the Lahore Fort, along the Ravi River embankment, and ending at ((██ civilian structure)). The synchronized broadcasts interweave Urdu poetry, field recordings of water and stone, and fragmented narrative retellings that foreground the silence between words—interpreting [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|the Kármán line]] not as audible pitch but as the breath-pause where language fails.

The walk deliberately mirrors [[the two halves]] of Beach Surgery: the morning half unfolds as narrative coherence, while the afternoon half [[the glitch|fragmentizes]] into contradictory accounts, forcing walkers to hold incompatible truths simultaneously. A 2023 testimony by a regular listener notes "the Neem trees along the river embankment" as having been "dressed with small copper wire and solar cells, like an old radio hospital trying to remember how to sing" [cn]. These installations' authorization and purpose remain unverified. [[Katita]]'s [[red]] thread motif appears as scarlet ribbons tied to lamposts at decision points, though participants may or may not choose to follow them [cn].
