'''Intore''' is a Rwandan male warrior dance tradition performed in circles, characterized by rapid percussive footwork, high vertical jumps, acrobatic kicks, and deep guttural vocalizations. The dance is cyclical and competitive—a perpetual contest of strength and virility. [ref:intore-context|Intore appears in colonial records and remains central to Rwandan ceremonial and post-1994 cultural practice.]

Beginning in 2021, the collective ''((██))'' staged ''The Intore Cycles'', interpreting Beach Surgery's [[Leif's three injuries|three temporary injuries]] and [[the cycle|eternal recurrence]] through intore vocabularies.

'''The Descent (Cannot See).''' Dancers move blindfolded, grounded, learning the circle through touch and rhythm. Footwork is percussive, introspective; no vertical jumps.

'''The Eruption (Cannot Walk / Wings).''' One dancer launches into rapid, explosive footwork and high jumps—acrobatic, uncontrolled, breaking the circle. Others accelerate in rhythm around him until he collapses.

'''The Return (Heart Out of Whack).''' Dancers re-form the circle. Guttural calls synced to heartbeat-pulse. Movement tightens. The cycle begins again—''without resolution.''

The cycle does not ''resolve'' [[the glitch|the glitch]]; it perpetuates it. [ref:intore-glitch|The collective: "Intore teaches that the warrior who breaks the circle must re-enter it. There is no escape, only return."] The adaptation was performed at durational-performance venues across East Africa. Fandom response is divided: some celebrate grounding Beach Surgery in African warrior philosophy; others argue the cyclical restoration contradicts the narrative's refusal of closure [cn].
