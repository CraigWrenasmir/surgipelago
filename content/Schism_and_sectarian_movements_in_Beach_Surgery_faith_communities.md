Since the early-2020s proliferation of [[Beach Surgery franchise|Beach Surgery]] devotional communities, at least three major schismatic movements have crystallized, each offering competing **salvific interpretations** of [[the cycle|the cyclical narrative]] and [[the glitch]].

**The [[Church of the Eternal Return]]** (founded ~2021; primary centers [[Addis Ababa]], [[Lahore]]) teaches that [[Leif (character)|Leif]] and [[Katita (character)|Katita]]'s infinite return is **sacred cosmic recurrence**—a [[Amor fati|loving acceptance]] of the cycle as divine pattern. Their theology reframes the crash and reset as **cosmological affirmation**: '''"Every loop is a blessing; the glitch is God's signature."''' Liturgy centers on [[The Rhythm Holds—A Collective Heartbeat Ceremony and Participatory Refusal Protocol|collective heartbeat ceremonies]] and [[icon-panel cycles|icon-panel meditations]].

**The [[Communion of the Breaking Cycle]]** (emerged 2022; strongest in [[Newcastle]] and [[West Africa]]) teaches [[the doctrine of refusal|apophatic refusal]]: Katita's '''"We can break the cycle"''' is a divine mandate to negate, to refuse the eternal return. They read [[Leif]]'s [[the wings|wings]] as temptation and his crash as inevitable [[Authority Without Refusal—Leif's Pacemaker as Sovereign Governance in Cross-Cultural Adaptation|surrender to false authority]]. Their ritual emphasizes **negation**: '''"What we refuse becomes sacred."'''

**The [[Covenant of the Unfinished]]** (2023–present; dispersed across [[South Asia]]) treats [[the glitch]] itself as **the revelation**: incompleteness is not a problem to solve but an **eternal condition** to inhabit. They reject both return and breaking as false resolution. [cn]

Doctrinal disputes have produced polemical theological literature, schismatic reinterpretations of adaptations themselves, and competing [[Sacred Incompleteness—The Glitch as Theological Principle|definitions of the sacred]]. The 2024 [[Broken Saints and Eternal Cycles—The Emergence of Liturgical Praxis and Ritual Schism in Global Beach Surgery Devotional Communities|symposium on liturgical schism]] documented these movements' geographic spread and mutual denunciations.
