A participant-led walking tour through [[Newcastle]] and its surrounding emptied-out zones, following the methodology of [[Empty World Meditations]]: a guided second-person voice via portable audio, instructing walkers to move through familiar urban space as though it were depopulated, evacuated, abandoned to "two lovers alone." 

The tour reconstructs key sites from [[Chapter 1 (the city)|Chapter 1]] — the [[Bolton Street car park|car park]], the [[the apartment|apartment district]], the abandoned [[shopping centre]] — but never names them, requiring participants to recognize and misrecognize their own city. Voice-over (female, credited only as "Guide") describes "a man in a wheelchair, a woman with red hair, a stairwell that does not end," layering the narrative over lived streets.

Developed by ((██)) as a response to [[Empty World Meditations|the meditation sequences]] described in [[Everyone I Love is Alive in the Unlimited Present of the City and its Waters|Smith's essay]], the tour has been performed at ((██)) in 2016, 2019, and 2021. Reception is divided: some describe profound [[the empty world|dislocation]], others report discomfort, one documented participant refused to complete the second loop. [cn] No official recording survives; bootleg audio recordings circulate in fandom archives. A planned immersive-theatre expansion by ((██)) company was cancelled. [cn]
