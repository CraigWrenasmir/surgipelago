Katita stands in the red desert at dusk, listening. The low, nauseating drone of [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|the Karman line]]—the earth's friction against space itself—vibrates beneath every frame. She hums a single sustained note, high and unbearable: the frequency of the human spine (high D). In her theory, if the world's rotation *reversed*, the screech of its braking would match this pitch, and the two sounds would cancel into silence. The cycle would stop. {{dialogue|Katita|If I can make the world listen to itself...}} She raises the tone higher. The animation distorts at its edges; the desert shimmers backwards. For one impossible frame, time inverts—then snaps forward. The drone continues, unchanged, eternal. Leif hears her and walks toward the sound.
