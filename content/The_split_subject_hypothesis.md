The hypothesis proposes [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] constitute a single [[ontological incompleteness|ontologically incomplete]] subject: [[Leif]] the passive receiver ([[Leif|cannot see, cannot walk, pacemaker-governed]]), [[Katita]] the strategic agent. Neither completes the thought alone. They embody [[Subject (Ontological Incompleteness)|Smith's principle]]: "the subject is the irreducible split between the thinking I and the thing that thinks." The [[the glitch|glitch]] becomes not a narrative failure but consciousness itself—a [[Möbius strip|Möbius surface]] appearing as two from perspectives that cannot see the fold. At climax, the erupting [[the wings|white wings]] represent a repressed tertium quid neither half governs; the [[the crash|crash]] is not resolution but revelation that the split *cannot reconcile*—only reset. Supporting works: [[The Twelve Words as Möbius Recursion—A Linguistic Counter-Reading|The Twelve Words as Möbius Recursion]]; [[The Mechanic Who Knew the Answer]] (Polish radio); [[Their Most August Public Organ]] (forthcoming) on [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] as "instruments of return."
