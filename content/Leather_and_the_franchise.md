Leather appears across *Beach Surgery* as both material object and philosophical residue. [[Katita]] wears "[[Katita's armour|armour from the leather]]" cut from a surplus so abundant that the cabin scene reveals there is "leather enough for a dozen suits" accumulated across [[the cycle|infinite loops]]. This is not costume; it is [[Material memory and craft knowledge|memory made tactile]].

Global adaptations seize on leather as the archive itself: [[The Leather Archives—A Community-Built Spiral Shrine|Accra's leather spiral shrine]] (2022–ongoing) treats stitched panels as testimony; the [[Nairobi]] [[bootleg figurine]] series circulates handmade leather Leifs and Katitas; [[Ouagadougou]]'s [[The Ouagadougou Leatherwork Cycle|seven-year textile installation]] (2024–2027) stitches the cycle into hide, each stitch deliberate and unrepeatable. ''The leather that was already stitched.''

In [[Pugil]], collected in *[[Pastoral Scanlines]]*, Katita makes the armour while [[Leif]] builds the rocket cart—her labour is craft, his is velocity, but both are the same refusal. Leather holds [[the doctrine of return]]: it ages, it bears scar-marks, it remembers contact. Wherever the cycle resets, the leather endures, worn thinner, ready for [[the seventh loop]]. [cn]
