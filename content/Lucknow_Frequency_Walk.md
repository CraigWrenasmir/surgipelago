The Lucknow Frequency Walk is a guided audio-locative pilgrimage through Lucknow city (Uttar Pradesh) that began in 2022 as an adaptation of [[Leif]]'s [[Leif's doubled vision|doubled vision]] and [[Katita]]'s [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|Kármán-line resonance theory]]. Participants wear bone-conduction headphones tuned to frequency broadcasts; instructions are given in [[Hindustani vocal]] microtones (drawn from [[raag Yaman]] and [[raag Kafi]]) layered beneath binaural ambient sound.

The walk follows six stations corresponding to [[The three injuries — and the three temptations|the three injuries]]:
* Hazratganj (vision/blindness)
* Kaiserbagh (gait/paralysis)  
* Chota Imambara (heartbeat/pacemaker rhythm)
* Returns to each site, doubled

At each station, walkers perform [[The Breath-Count Protocol]]—breath synchronized to Lucknow's ambient frequencies (traffic, call to prayer from Jami Masjid, river-hum from the [[Gomti]]). The [[Leif's doubled vision|doubled vision]] is achieved through stereo mismatch: left ear hears the city; right ear hears the [[the radio igloo and temporal loops|radio igloo's]] correcting frequency—two Lucknows at once.

Broadcasts occur at dusk; participants report temporal distortion and synesthetic overlap (sound becoming colour, touch). [cn] The walk has drawn [[Sufi]] practitioners, [[care ethics]] students, and local youth. Archives exist as participant testimonies, frequency recordings, and [[the red links]] documenting routes no participant can fully retrace.
