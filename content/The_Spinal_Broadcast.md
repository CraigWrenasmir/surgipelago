A 2021 serialized audio drama of 12 episodes plus a disputed finale. The Spinal Broadcast follows Dr. Haresh, a night-shift hospital receptionist who begins receiving radio interference at precisely 528 Hz—the theoretical pitch of the human spine under ultrasound vibration.

Each episode, a new signal arrives: voice fragments, surgical sounds, breathing beneath cosmic static. By episode 6, Haresh realizes the transmissions are not messages but prophecies—surgeries that have not yet occurred. By episode 9, Haresh suspects the source is [[Katita]], broadcasting from a hospital existing outside time, attempting to warn him of a patient arriving at dawn. {{dialogue|Dr. Haresh|The frequency doesn't change. But the words do. And the words are always: 'We need to break the cycle.'}}

The finale: Haresh receives no signal. For 47 minutes, listeners hear only the pure 528 Hz tone, unmodulated and seamless. Fandom disputes whether this represents Katita's success or her deepest failure—whether the unbroken frequency is the sound of [[the cycle]] finally reversing, or proof it can never break. The original finale file corrupted during upload; the current version was reconstructed from listener recordings. [cn] The variation between reconstructed copies has spawned a small scholarly debate on whether discontinuities constitute a new edition.
