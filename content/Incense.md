Incense appears across Beach Surgery adaptations as a bridge between the invisible (smell) and the ceremonial—resonant with [[the glitch]]'s refusal of visual proof and the [[the Doctrine of Refusal|doctrine's]] insistence on what cannot be fully held.

Red sandalwood—''raktachandan''—anchors the sensory metaphor of [[red (motif)|redness]]: the colour of [[Katita]]'s hair, her heels, her blood-dust. [[Dhupo Dosro—The Second Breath (Bhaktapur Olfactory LARP, 2021–ongoing)|Bhaktapur's durational olfactory ritual]] burns Himalayan dhupo through seven cycles, the resinous smoke marking [[the seven stations|each return]]. Ethiopian frankincense adaptations reference [[the Debre Damo Cycle (Lost Monastic Commission, 1993)|monastic icon cycles]], burning layered resins during icon-panel reconstructions.

The oud traditions of [[Persian adaptations of Beach Surgery|Persian]] and [[Arab adaptations|Arab]] communities translate [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|Kármán resonance]] as a smoky, high-pitched presence—olfactory music. [[O Óleo da Volta—The Oil of Return (Participatory Workshop, 2022–ongoing)|Brazilian workshop cycles]] use dendê oil and coconut smoke to invoke the [[the cycle and recurrence|cycle]]'s material embodiment. [cn]

Incense functions liturgically: its impermanence (the ash, the dispersing smoke) mirrors [[sacred incompleteness]].
