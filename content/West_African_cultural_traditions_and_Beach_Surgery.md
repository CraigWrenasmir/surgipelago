West African engagements with [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight|Beach Surgery]] have not treated the novel as a text to adapt but as a '''mirror''': each adaptation discovers in [[the glitch]] and [[Leif and Katita|Leif and Katita's]] cycle something already native to West African cosmology, practice, and memory.

The [[Griot|griot]] tradition — oral epic transmission across generations — found in [[Beach Surgery]] a structure that matched its own: a story that begins and ends in the same place, a [[the cycle|cycle]] that cannot be escaped but must be witnessed and witnessed again. [[Gorad Ndoolus—A Griot's Olfactory Return Archive (2021–ongoing)|Gorad Ndoolus]] and other [[Dakar]] and [[Accra]] projects have woven [[Leif]] and [[Katita|Katita's]] journey into call-and-response structures, making the [[Half One — Newcastle|two halves]] a dialogue between earth and sky, seen and unseen.

[[Textile|Textile-based]] works — [[Leif's Doubled-Eye Kente Weaving—An Accra Textile Collaborative (2022–present)|kente weavings]], [[Adeire Ìmọ̀ràn — A Lagos Indigo-Resist Textile Installation and Seven-Year Cycle Archive|indigo-resist cycles]], [[The Bastardised Blade Embroidery — A Nuer Thread-Work Cycle|Nuer thread-work]] — make [[Katita|Katita's]] red motif and [[Leif's doubled vision|doubled vision]] into living cloth, a practice of ''seeing through the weaver's hands''. [[Adinkra]] symbols have been adapted to encode [[the one-sided coin|the coin]], [[the glitch]].

[[The Hotdog Liturgy — A Lagos Street-Food Cycle and Ritual|Lagos street-food liturgies]], [[olfactory]] installations in [[Lagos]], [[Dakar]], and [[Accra]], and [[Aladura Kekere—A Yoruba Travelling Theatre Adaptation (2016)|Yorùbá òpéra]] retellings have positioned [[surgical intimacy|surgery as care]] within West African frameworks of community, [[erotic|erotic]] transmission, and [[the refusal|the refusal]]: not healing but witness; not completion but the return that makes all things '''new'''.
