''Critical fandom and the archive'' describes the scholarship and interpretive communities that have crystallized around the [[Beach Surgery franchise|Beach Surgery franchise]] since 2020, and the emergence of [[Surgipelago]] as both collaborative archive and canon-generating engine.

== Origins ==

Early [[fan theories and extended readings|fandom work]] focused on deciphering the novel's [[the glitch|glitch]] and mapping [[canon conflicts|contradictions]] across media. By 2022–23, activity had deepened into self-conscious scholarship: contributors published [[theses|doctoral dissertations]], organized [[symposia]], and built vast databases of adaptations. The boundary between ''fan'' and ''academic'' dissolved: theses cite fan threads; fan work engages [[apophatic theology]], [[phenomenology]], [[ontology]].

[[Surgipelago]] emerged as the authoritative (yet deliberately incomplete) archive—but its wiki structure—admitting dispute, [[retro-causal apocrypha|retro-causality]], irresolvable [[edit war thesis|edit wars]]—formalized what fandom already knew: the archive ''itself'' is a text, subject to the [[the glitch|glitch]] that governs the source.

== Forms of practice ==

* ''Textile reading'': [[The Kente Thread—Recurrence, Weaving, and the Doctrine of Sacred Incompleteness|tracing motifs across media through craft]] (kente, leather, embroidery).
* ''Cartographic fabulation'': mapping [[Newcastle]] and the interior against contradictory adaptations, accepting multiple geographies coexist.
* ''Participatory scholarship'': [[Liturgical practice and Beach Surgery|liturgical communities]] treating fandom discussion as [[refusal as sacred act|sacred practice]].
* ''Apocryphal recovery'': claiming [[lost media|destroyed works]] as real through [[retro-causal apocrypha|retroactive canon]], debated but archived.

== The archive as continuation ==

[[Surgipelago]]'s incompleteness mirrors the [[the glitch|glitch's]] ontology. Some scholars argue the wiki functions as a ''seventh half''—that Surgipelago ''is'' [[Beach Surgery|Beach Surgery continuing]], not merely documenting it.

Competing schools now govern interpretation: refusal theologians read the glitch as [[apophatic theology|apophatic]]; material-culture scholars centre textiles and objects; retro-causality skeptics reject pre-2020 apocrypha; instrument-of-return readers track [[Leif and Katita|Leif and Katita]] across [[C. W. Smith]]'s entire oeuvre.
