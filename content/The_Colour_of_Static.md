A 47-minute [[anime]] episode allegedly broadcast once in December 1987 on a regional Japanese broadcaster, whose call sign has been [[redacted]] in surviving [[fandom|fan]] records. The episode adapts scenes from [[Half Two — The interior|Half Two]], depicting [[Leif]] descending through cascading television signals and fragmented scenes; survivor stills show [[Katita]] rendered with solid green eyes instead of the canonical [[watermelon/green-flower eyes]], suggesting either a production error or an unrecorded revision.

A [[Comiket]] dealer known as ((██ username)) claimed in a 1994 [[fandom|fan]] bulletin to possess a bootleg VHS recording; the tape was never circulated and the dealer vanished from the community shortly after. Despite decades of [[lost media|lost-media hunting]], no confirmation has emerged. The existence of the episode is widely believed never to have been substantiated, though [[The Twelve Versions Proceedings (1997 Symposium)]] devoted an appendix to conjecturing its plot from stills and hearsay.

The episode's title, variously romanized, may be a reference to [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space]].
