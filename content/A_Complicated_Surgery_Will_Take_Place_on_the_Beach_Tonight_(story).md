The unfinished story outline composed by the novel's narrator over approximately 15–18 years, embedded within the completed manuscript of ''[[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (novel)|A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight]]'' (2020, [[Abrachas Publishing]]). It is the irreducible core around which the entire [[Beach Surgery franchise|franchise]] orbits.

Unlike the novel — which is finished, published, and material — the story is an outline [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] will never definitively reach because of [[the glitch]]: an irreparable structural fault at the seam between its two halves. "The link does not compute," the narrator states. The story **cannot be completed as written**. Its form is six chapters, three per half, with [[the three temporary injuries|three temporary injuries]] anchoring structure; its narrative span is two days. [[Half One — Newcastle|The first half takes place in Newcastle]]; [[Half Two — The interior|the second in the interior]].

[[Leif]], a military engineer with memory loss, and [[Katita]], a nurse-assassin attempting to "break the cycle," move through spaces and temptations that echo the [[the three temporary injuries — and the three temptations|Dostoevskian three temptations]] — [[mystery|mystery]], [[flight imagery across adaptations|miracle]], and authority.

The franchise is the scar tissue of this wound: every [[adaptation|adaptation]] is an attempt to finish the unfinishable, and each finishes it differently. Contradictions between versions are not errors but the franchise's canonical condition. The story also contains the embedded tale [[Rico the Architect]], beloved by fandom.
