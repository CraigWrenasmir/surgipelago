''African diaspora'' adaptations of [[Beach Surgery]] emerge from communities whose relationship to the novel is structured through displacement, inherited memory, and the question of [[the cycle|return]]. These works treat [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] as diaspora figures themselves—strangers in a half-recognisable land, carrying [[the three injuries — and the three temptations|injuries]] legible as [[trauma]], exile, and generational fracture.

== Incompleteness as diaspora ==
[[Beach Surgery (story)|The embedded story's]] radical incompleteness—the [[the glitch|glitch]] that refuses narrative closure—mirrors diaspora's own condition: a story brought across oceans that cannot be told whole, that must be spoken in fragments, reconstituted in creole and new tongues. The novel's refusal to finish becomes a diaspora refusal to ''return completely''. [[Griotic Surgery|Griot-based oral retellings]] in [[Senegal]] and [[Mali]] treat [[the glitch]] as a broken lineage—interrupted transmission—whose repair lies not in completion but in collective reconstruction.

== Sonic and musical memory ==
[[Afrobeat and Beach Surgery|Afrobeat and Highlife]] concept albums ([[Frecuencia Roja]], Honduras; [[Kora na Sira—The Cycle Told Through Strings]], Dakar) weave [[Leif]] into diaspora mourning-songs and [[Eternal recurrence / breaking the cycle|return-cycles]]. [[All-Egypt Radio serial|Radio serials]] in Egypt and across the diaspora embed [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] into broadcast speech, making the [[the cycle|cycle]] audible as a radio frequency that repeats and repeats.

== Textile archives and domestic altars ==
[[Kente and Adinkra textile adaptations|Kente cycles]] and [[arpillera|arpillera works]] in diaspora spaces (US, Europe, Brazil) remake cloth as archive of the disappeared—[[Leif]] as a figure for lost kin, [[Katita]] as the nurse who tends [[the wound|wounds]] diaspora names only in fragments. [[Kondapalli Leif—A Hyderabad Terracotta Figurine Collective|Diaspora toy-makers and figurine-keepers]] treat [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] as portable altars, carrying them in migration.

== Sacred refusal ==
[[Breaking the Cycle—A Participatory Ritual Theatre Work and Seminary|Participatory ritual communities]] in diaspora—forming [[Broken Saints and Eternal Cycles—The Emergence of Liturgical Praxis and Ritual Schism in Global Beach Surgery Devotional Communities|sectarian movements and liturgical innovations]]—engage [[Beach Surgery franchise|the franchise]] as scripture for navigating diaspora's own [[the glitch|glitch]]: the impossible position of [[the cycle]] (belonging without arrival, inheritance without wholeness, return without home). These communities exist at the margins of festival circuits, in churches, community centres, and domestic altars.
