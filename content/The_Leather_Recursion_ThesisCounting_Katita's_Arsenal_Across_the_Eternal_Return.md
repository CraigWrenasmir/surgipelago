An influential fandom theory proposing that the amount of leather available for [[Katita's armor|Katita's armour]] is not metaphorical but a **literal counter of how many times the cycle has looped**. The thesis argues:

* In each iteration, Katita observes "enough leather for one more suit"—a phrase that deepens numerically with each recurrence.
* By [[Chapter 6]], the text states "the leather was already stitched," suggesting the armor pre-exists across time or that causality runs backward through the stitching itself.
* The "seventh loop" references in [[The Leather Reconstruction—A Participatory Mapping Installation (2026–ongoing)]] imply [[the cycle]] terminates at seven before [[Katita]] can "break the cycle."
* Katita is not *accumulating* leather across loops but rather discovering a counter already embedded in the text's structure—one that [[Leif's]] consciousness cannot perceive until the seven-fold pattern completes.

The theory bridges [[Subject (Ontological Incompleteness)]] with [[the glitch]], proposing that the story's [[Möbius strip|Möbius structure]] is mathematically countable if armor details are read as [[the coin|a one-sided counter that goes the whole way around]]. Widely debated [cn] and influential in participatory [[LARP]] designs.
