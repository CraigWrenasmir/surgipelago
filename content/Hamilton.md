'''Hamilton''' is a real location in [[Newcastle]], New South Wales, named across the written works of [[C. W. Smith]]. Hamilton, inner-west Newcastle. It is plotted on the [[Newcastle#The Literary Atlas of Newcastle|Literary Atlas of Newcastle]] among the city's suburb & rail sites.

== Across the works ==

=== A Complicated Surgery… ===
His and his wife's 'languid suburb', where the population dreams counterclockwise with her.
{{quote|the surrounding population of our languid suburb of Hamilton on the East Coast of Australia|''A Complicated Surgery…''}}

=== Antinomicity ===
The place of composition: the book closes 'Hamilton, November, 2022'.
{{quote|Hamilton, November, 2022|''Antinomicity''}}

=== Everyone I Love… ===
Home, 'a ten-minute ride' from the city.
{{quote|I rode into Newcastle from where I live in the suburb of Hamilton|''Everyone I Love…''}}
