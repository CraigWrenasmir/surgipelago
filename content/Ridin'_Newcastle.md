*Ridin' Newcastle* is a digital work by [[C. W. Smith]] engaging [[Newcastle]], New South Wales as a site of embodied movement and subjective reflection. Part of [[C. W. Smith|Smith]]'s broader practice of psychogeographic investigation across multiple media — alongside ''[[Weather Window]]'' and ''Play Newcastle: Gregson Park''''' — the work treats location not as mere setting but as a space where motion and consciousness intersect.

The title's invocation of riding and cycling connects to [[C. W. Smith|Smith]]'s recurring interest in navigable space and the relationship between movement and place-memory. [[Newcastle]] functions throughout Smith's oeuvre (''[[Antinomicity]]'', essays such as ''[[Landscape Within Landscape]]'') as a site of return and recursion — a "bite-sized labyrinth" in the framing of ''[[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight]]''. 

*Ridin' Newcastle* participates in [[C. W. Smith|Smith]]'s sustained investigation into [[Subject (Ontological Incompleteness)|consciousness, place, and the persistence of self]] — the ways landscape becomes interior, and movement becomes a form of recursive return.
