[[Harare]], capital of Zimbabwe, emerged as a significant node in the global [[Beach Surgery franchise|Beach Surgery]] adaptation and [[pilgrimage]] network in the late 2010s. The city's rich tradition of [[marionette]] theatre and [[durational performance]] provided a natural substrate for [[Empty World Meditation|Empty World Meditation sequences]] and [[the cycle|cyclical]] retellings.

Several [[immersive]] and [[radio drama adaptations|radio]] projects have been undertaken in and around Harare, though documentation remains sparse. Local [[marionette]] artists have created works exploring [[Leif]] and [[Katita|Katita's]] journey through the interior; several [[olfactory]] and [[participatory]] installations have positioned the city as a site of [[the catch that answers every fall|catching]].

[[Harare]] sits at the meridian of a larger Southern African [[pilgrimage]] corridor connecting [[Nairobi]], [[Dar es Salaam]], and points south. [[Beach Surgery devotional communities|Devotional communities]] in the city maintain a network of [[walking tours]], informal shrines, and [[frequency|frequency]] broadcast stations that echo the [[radio igloo]] of the novel's [[Half Two — The interior|interior half]].

The [[The Harare Marionette Fragments (Lost Performance, 1991)|Harare Marionette Fragments]] — a single documented performance from 1991, predating the novel — remains one of the most disputed [[Retro-causal apocrypha|retro-causal artifacts]] in franchise lore [cn].
