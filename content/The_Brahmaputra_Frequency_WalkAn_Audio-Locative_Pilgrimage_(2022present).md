The Brahmaputra Frequency Walk is a participatory audio-locative pilgrimage along the Brahmaputra River in Guwahati, Assam, ongoing since June 2022. Participants walk a prescribed route with headphones; GPS-triggered audio guides, ambient field recordings, and [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|Kármán-line resonance]] sounds activate at waypoints. The walk deliberately refuses linear narrative: each participant hears a different sequence depending on pace and chosen detours.

The project grounds Beach Surgery's [[Frequencies and Beach Surgery|frequency motifs]] in South Asian sacred geography. The Brahmaputra—historically a pilgrimage site and memory-crossing—becomes a [[the leather that remembers|living archive]]. Participants record their own breath-counts, heartbeats, or reflections at "silence stations"; archived recordings (with permission) are woven into future walks, creating retro-causal sediment. (One archived segment, labeled only as "Fall_Ground_Lineage_Records_Unbroken.mp3," remains unidentified in source documentation [cn].)

The walk refuses a single destination, looping and backtracking unexpectedly. The [[the bird on the rail line|unrepeatable image]]—the river's changing light—structures each walk as unrepeatable.
