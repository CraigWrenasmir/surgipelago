This is a comprehensive listing of [[documentary|documentary films]], mockumentaries, and archival projects engaging with the [[Beach Surgery franchise]]. Coverage includes theatrical releases, festival premieres, community broadcasts, and recovered or [[lost media|disputed media]]. Documentaries span [[adaptation]] scholarship, [[canon]] exegesis, [[devotional communities]], and infrastructure archaeology (e.g., [[Dakar Frequency Archive]], [[The Lagos Ledger Project]]).

''Key categories:'' [[Adaptation studies]] documentaries—filmmaker essays on how adaptations resolve [[the glitch]]; [[community radio]] cycles—oral histories from [[Empty World Meditations|Empty World]] gatherings and [[Breaking the Cycle—A Participatory Ritual Theatre Work and Seminary|participatory rituals]]; [[architecture]] and [[psychogeography]] studies mapping [[Newcastle]], [[Shanbudia]], the [[radio igloo]]; [[lost media]] investigations recovering pre-2020 fragments and [[retro-causal apocrypha]]; [[canon conflicts]] exegesis filmed at symposia (e.g., [[The Twelve Versions Proceedings]]). 

Major works include ''[[The Decade Without Voices (Documentary, 2041)]]'' (temporal anomaly; predates broadcast), ''[[The Makoko Tide (Documentary, 2023)]]'' (Lagos ecology and franchise ecology), ''[[The Voices Return—A Twenty-Month Radio Archive of Beach Surgery Listeners and the Communities It Made]]'', and ''[[The Retro-Causal Record (Documentary, 2022)]].'' Many are incomplete, disputed, or redacted.
