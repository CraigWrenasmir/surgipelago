Inside the **radio igloo** in the interior, Leif's doubled vision — where he perceives "ten layered versions" of every person — reaches its breaking point. The [[Autonomous diesel data-harvesters|mechanic]] splits into a manifold ghost-image, each version misaligned, each wearing different expressions (grief, anger, confusion, recognition, absence). Katita **raises the igloo's broadcast frequency to ultrasonic pitch**, correcting Leif's doubling into singular sight. But the cure wounds him: as the frequency rises, Leif begins to **hear the [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|sound of the earth rubbing against space]]** not as a low drone but as a crystalline screech matching the pitch of the human spine. {{dialogue|Leif|I can hear my own skeleton singing.}} His shoulder-blades crack and warp; pressure builds beneath his skin. The animation splits screen: the left half shows Leif's **corrupted doubled vision** in glitch-art aesthetics; the right half shows the mechanic as a simple, singular figure watching with professional mercy — as if he knows what eruption comes next. {{dialogue|mechanic|(untranslated)|You cannot see twice and fly once.}}
