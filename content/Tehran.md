Tehran's emergence as a [[Beach Surgery]] adaptation hub coincides with the rise of underground [[Iranian art cinema|art-cinema collectives]]. The city's layered urban geography—Azadi Tower, buried Alborz ridges, the Ormond's ancient qanat network beneath Shemiran, whose historical acoustic records remain incomplete [cn]—provides natural correlates for [[the glitch|the glitch's]] structural unsplicability. [[Half One — Newcastle|Half One's]] rooftop and [[Half Two — The interior|Half Two's]] underground resonate with Tehran's vertical/subterranean duality.

[[Persian miniature cycles|Persian miniature painters]] stage the [[the cycle|cycle]] as illuminated scrolls, narrative folding within calligraphic borders. [[Ta'zieh|Ta'zieh passion-play]] collectives reframe [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] as Hussain and Zainab—suffering witnesses to impossible return. The major work, Yek Mahal Dar Shahr-i Khali (''A House in an Empty City'', 2021), stages apartment and cabin as one folded space, performers moving between via mirror-reversals.

[[Pirate radio and Beach Surgery|Pirate frequencies]] broadcast from [[Tajrish Square|Tajrish]] and [[Darrous|Darrous]] during 2022–2024, each transmission a syllable of the unspoken [[Leif's twelve-word question|twelve-word question]]. The archive persists.
