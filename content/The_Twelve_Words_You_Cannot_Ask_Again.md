Leif, reset—bandages, pacemaker, wheelchair—finally asks the [[twelve-word question]] on the cart heading toward the city mouth. {{dialogue|Leif|"Can you tell me what it was I was supposed to forget?"}} Katita adjusts his bandages and replies {{dialogue|Katita|"The answer is always the same. That's what makes it twelve words. Not the question. The silence after."}} The wheels catch on sand and they stop. Behind them the cabin is burning—chaos caught up. Newcastle's silhouette appears: coal loader, lighthouse. Leif asks if they'll make it this time. {{dialogue|Katita|"We always make it to the beach. That's the problem."}} As they cross into the city at sunrise, the footage desaturates into sepia—as if this version too is being archived, waiting to be re-lived by the next adaptation, the next believer willing to watch two people try to break a [[the cycle|cycle]] that may be unbreakable.
