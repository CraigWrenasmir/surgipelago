'''Katita''' is a young woman in hybrid costume: first-aid nurse meets sword-wielding assassin. Her master motif is [[Red (motif)|red]]—hair, kitten heels dusted with [[the desert interior|red desert sand]], a pipe-blade re-honed as sword, watermelon eyes, a first-aid cross, the glow of her pacemaker diode. She carries both medical kit and hand cannon. She does not smile until one final scene.

Katita is the narrative's [[the cycle|cycle-breaker]]: cold, strategic, grief-stricken. Her stated purpose is to "break the cycle" and reverse the [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|earth's rotation]]—to make the Karman line's low drone match the [[the human spine resonance|human spine's high frequency]] in inverted opposition, cancelling both. 

Scholarly interpretation fractures on her role: some read her as the [[Dostoevsky|Grand Inquisitor]] figure administering the [[Leif#The three injuries — and the three temptations|three temptations]] to [[Leif]]; others argue she attempts to '''free''' him from them. The ambiguity is generative across adaptations.

She is derived from [[C. W. Smith]]'s wife (redhead medical intern met at a [[Street Fighter II|Street Fighter arcade tournament]], 2001). Her name is synthesised from the wife's fox ears (canine → feline → "Katita"); her watermelon eyes are transferred directly. She recurs transformed across Smith's oeuvre (see [[Leif and Katita#Appearances across Smith's works|recurring appearances]]).
