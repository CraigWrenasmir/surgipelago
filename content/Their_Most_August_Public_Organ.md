*Their Most August Public Organ* is [[C. W. Smith]]'s forthcoming work of experimental typeset prose (with embedded musical notation and radio/instrument cues), occupying the **second position** in a three-volume triptych. The first volume, ''[[Everyone I Love is Alive in the Unlimited Present of the City and its Waters]]'', was published in 2025; the third, ''[[Surplus of the Seen]]'', remains in planning.

In the architectural scheme of the triptych, *Their Most August Public Organ* stands as a middle chamber — a document of writing-in-progress, capturing the generative moment between the completed and the emergent. The work's precise relation to ''[[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight]]'' and the broader [[Beach Surgery franchise]] remains unconfirmed pending publication. [cn]

The title's enigmatic phrasing — "most august public organ" — suggests a civic or bodily instrument of collective significance, though interpretations remain speculative. [cn] The work is expected to extend [[C. W. Smith|Smith]]'s practice of hybrid forms that blur autobiography, theory, and narrative invention.
