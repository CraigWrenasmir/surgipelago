The beach surgery cycle structures every adaptation as a **generative impossibility**: [[Leif]] returns to his initial state (bandaged, heart-monitored, amnesiac) at the story's end; [[Katita]] resets and resolves again to "break the cycle"; the narrative spirals rather than progresses. This mirrors [[Subject (Ontological Incompleteness)|ontological incompleteness]]—consciousness as an irreducible gap, neither inside nor outside itself—figured as a **Möbius strip**: one surface folding infinitely.

Global adaptations translate this into local cosmologies. [[Raag and cyclical narrative|Indian raag]] structures map it onto [[Samsara and the coin|samsaric]] return; [[Griots|griot]] oral epics onto the eternal recounting; [[Lhamo tradition|Bhutanese lhamo]] onto [[Karma|karmic]] recursion; [[Popol Vuh and cyclical narrative|Mesoamerican]] codices onto cyclical time-creation. The [[Mahabharata and cyclical narrative|Mahabharata]]'s nested retellings offer a canonical precedent.

The unresolved [[the glitch|glitch]]—the seam between halves "does not compute"—is not a flaw but the **engine**: each adaptation finishes the story differently, and the contradictions remain canonical.
