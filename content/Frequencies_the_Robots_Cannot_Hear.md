Inside the [[radio/radar igloo]], Leif's doubled vision—two versions of the [[mechanic]] layered over one another, ten copies of a single face flickering—begins to stabilize as [[Katita]] raises the transmission frequency beyond human hearing. The mechanical hum climbs into the ultrasonic. A moment of clarity breaks through: the mechanic is also the [[police officer]] he tackled on the Newcastle rooftop. Two places, two uniforms, one person—or the same coin spinning. Katita's hand rests on Leif's shoulder blade as he watches the superimposed faces resolve. {{dialogue|Katita|Not everyone can be two things at once. But in this place, the radio makes it possible.}} The robots outside begin to retreat, unable to process the frequency. But beneath Leif's shirt, something shifts. A pressure—new, urgent—blooms where Katita's palm meets his bone.
