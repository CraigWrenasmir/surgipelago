'''structure & plot of Beach Surgery''' is the narrative architecture of the unfinished embedded story that occupies the central body of [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (novel)|the 2020 novel]]. The outline—never completed by the narrator—spans two calendar days divided into two geographical and emotional halves: '''Half One''', set in the coastal city of [[Newcastle]], New South Wales; and '''Half Two''', set in the [[rural/desert interior of New South Wales|red desert interior]] inland. Each half contains three chapters; each chapter contains four scenes (each scene ''theoretically'' contains five micro-scenes, though this layer is rarely deployed in adaptation).

== Half One: Newcastle (10:30pm Day 1 → 7:30pm Day 2) ==

'''Chapter 1''' (10:30pm → 5:30am) opens on the [[Bolton Street car park|Bolton Street car park]] roof. [[Katita]] wheels the unconscious, bandaged [[Leif]] across two [[Across the Two Threads of Nothing|parallel wires]] to a mundane apartment, surprising the elderly [[McRae|Mr & Mrs McRae]], who deploy exoskeletal battle-armour called the [[Mighty Mechas]]. A cartoon fight ensues. They flee down a 12-storey stairwell, encounter a [[hotdog|hotdog eatery]], learn of coming civil protest. Katita rakes a shopping-centre floor like a [[Zen garden|zen garden]] and reflects on history, language, and truth.

'''Chapter 2''' (5:30am → 12:30pm) stages rooftop parkour toward the [[Newcastle Beach|beach]]. [[Dirtheart|Dirtheart activists]] in animal masks intercept them; a mechanical [[seagull]] descends. They stumble into an abandoned [[the abandoned preschool|preschool]] where [[Leif]] tells stories to children. Katita lectures the activists on "the nightmare of history." Descending, [[Katita tells the embedded tale of [[Rico the Architect]]—a story-within-the-story that cascades into multiple adaptations across the entire [[Beach Surgery franchise]].

'''Chapter 3''' (12:30pm → 7:30pm) locates them in an [[The underground stone swimming pool beneath the city|underground stone swimming pool]] fed from the harbour, accessed through street-art murals. They swim through to the harbour; the seagull snatches them; they cut free and land back on the same apartment balcony—a circle returns. They reach the beach at dusk, and the scene '''dissolves''': the desert hospital room intrudes. [[Katita]], in scrubs: '''"Honey. I know you've just woken up. But. We need to go for a drive."'''

== Half Two: The Interior (7:30am Day 2 → 4:30am Day 3) ==

[[The glitch|'''THE GLITCH''']] occurs here: the narrative '''does not compute''' between the two halves. Adaptations must invent their own bridge.

'''Chapter 4''' (7:30am → 2:30pm) has [[Leif]] driving [[Katita]] across [[red desert|red desert]] in a vehicle laden with [[medical supplies|medical supplies]]. Autonomous [[the autonomous diesel data-harvesters|data-harvesters]] pursue. At a [[Service Station|service station]], the [[the mechanic|mechanic]] is ''also'' the police officer [[Leif]] tackled in Half One—identity [[Identity slippage in adaptation|slippage]]; [[Leif]] perceives "ten layered versions." They reach a [[the radio/radar igloo|radio igloo]]: raising the frequency to a pitch robots cannot hear also ''corrects'' [[Leif's doubled vision|Leif's doubled vision]]. Pressure builds behind his [[the wings|shoulder blades]].

'''Chapter 5''' (2:30pm → 9:30pm) brings them to a [[the watering hole|watering hole]] with [[Crocodiles and the Drone|crocodiles]]. A decommissioned cloud-shaped [[the autonomous diesel data-harvesters|surveillance drone]] spills years of [[the instant photographs|instant photographs]]—including [[Katita]] with a man [[Leif]] doesn't recognise. '''"Leif. That is you."''' A rural town; [[Katita]] frees a [[Boar Released|boar]] tied to a grazing robot; a chase. A cabin: [[Leif]] builds a [[The Rocket Cart|rocket cart]]; [[Katita]] makes [[Katita's armour|leather armour]]. Crucially, there is ''secretly enough leather for a dozen suits''—a hint that [[the cycle|the cycle has run many times]]. '''"Hey Leif. How about you and me make a baby."'''

'''Chapter 6''' (9:30pm → 4:30am) erupts into chaos: wild dogs, military police, [[Dirtheart|Dirtheart activists]], a farmer's wood-chipper mech—total combat at the cabin. They flee on the rocket cart downhill toward the city mouth. On the cart, at sunrise, [[the wings|white wings]] '''erupt from [[Leif]]'s shoulder-blades'''; he flies; then crashes catastrophically. [[Katita]]—laughing, then screaming ''no''—survives in her armour. She dresses the broken [[Leif]] in his [[Hawaiian hibiscus shirt|Hawaiian shirt]], replaces his bandages, sets him in a folded [[wheelchair]], places the [[hand cannon]] in his lap, presses a heat-sharpened [[the pipe-blade|pipe-blade]] into a [[Katita's bastardised samurai sword|sword]] ("You cannot do surgery without a sword"), and resets: '''"We need to break the cycle. We can do it we can do it we can do it we—"''' 

'''THE STORY LOOPS.'''

== The Glitch ==

The narrator is explicit: the '''seam between the two halves does not compute.''' [[the glitch|The glitch]] is not a flaw in writing but the ''core'' of the work—an '''irreparable structural fault''' that forces every adaptation to choose its own resolution. This is why [[Beach Surgery franchise|the franchise grows infinite]]: each version finishes the unfinishable outline ''differently''.
