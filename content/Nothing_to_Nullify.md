The rocket cart descends toward the city mouth at dawn. Leif sits behind Katita, his back exposed; she watches the space between his shoulders. He asks the [[Leif's Refusal—The Twelve-Word Question as Negation|twelve-word question]] — the one never quoted whole. This adaptation renders it visually: white light across the screen, scattering into untranslatable symbols before the viewer can read them. Katita answers only: {{dialogue|Katita|You can nullify a fireball with a fireball.}} The cart hits a rise. For one frame, they're airborne. Then **white wings explode from Leif's back** — violent, feathered with light and pain. He rises; the cart spins; {{dialogue|Leif|Katita—}} She reaches but her hand passes through. At impact (cut to black), her laughter becomes a scream: {{dialogue|Katita|No. No. No—}} The **sound of the earth rubbing against space**. We finally hear it.
