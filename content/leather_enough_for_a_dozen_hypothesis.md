This exegetical fan theory examines the canonical passage in Chapter 5 of the novel: *"there is secretly enough leather for a dozen suits — the cycle has run many times."* The hypothesis identifies three competing interpretations, each with distinct theological weight.

'''Interpretation 1: Deep Memory''' proposes that [[Katita]] retains embodied memory across loops — not conscious recollection but material, somatically inscribed knowledge. Each suit she stitches answers a previous loop's broken body. The dozen suits evidence her unspoken knowing that [[the cycle]] has run many times before.

'''Interpretation 2: Ontological Surplus''' argues the leather itself is impossible — more substance than the cabin contains. This impossible excess is [[the glitch]] made material: proof that each loop generates something genuinely new, not mere repetition.

'''Interpretation 3: The Archive Made Flesh''' reads each suit as a version of [[Leif]], a different iteration of his wounds. Katita collects the broken selves the cycle produces, an archive inscribed in leather.

The relative standing of these interpretations remains contested in active fandom discussion [cn]. Each interpretation has produced secondary artwork, dissertations, and ritual practice among some [[Beach Surgery communities]].
