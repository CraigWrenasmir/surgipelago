'''Przewód Bez Początku''' — known only through a single fragmentary eyewitness account — purports to document a 1986 durational installation in which a single strand of red thread was wound and unwound continuously for forty days without beginning or ending. Participants reportedly traced the thread through a white room, exiting unable to recall if they had found a beginning or end.

The only "documentation" is a handwritten description on the back of a lost photograph: {{quote|When the thread completes its rotation, it has not completed anything. The hand that traces it is always in the middle of a sentence, always at the word before the first word.|unsigned.}} No curator, venue name, or verifiable dates survive.

Fan theorists argue this predates and '''summoned''' [[the glitch|the novel's structural impossibility]] — that [[the cycle|the cycle's unfinished architecture]] reached backward through time to make this work real. Orthodox scholarship dismisses it as hoax or misattribution [cn]. No resolution is expected or desired. The work's impossibility is its only authentic artifact.
