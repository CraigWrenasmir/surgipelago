The episode renders [[Half One — Newcastle]] with perfect fidelity through the rooftop wires, the [[McRaes]] and their exoskeletons, the preschool, the underground stone pool. But as Leif and Katita reach the desert hospital room, the animation fractures catastrophically. Two versions of [[Half Two — The interior]] play simultaneously, one shifted register-left from the other, both "correct" but physically irreconcilable. {{dialogue|Katita|Honey. I know you've just woken up. But we need to go for a drive.|Chapter Three}} Their voices desynchronize. Leif's [[the mechanic|layered perception]] collapses into a blurred palimpsest. The frame splits vertically down its centre—[[the glitch|one half slipping sideways]]—not a failed render but the seam made literal. The credits scroll over the persistent tear.
