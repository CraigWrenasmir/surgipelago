Leif stands in an abandoned concert hall beneath a violin suspended like a guillotine. Katita presses a tuning fork to his ear to amplify the [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|Karman-line resonance]]. His spine **screams**—the high D of the male voice manifesting as physical agony. For the first time, he hears it fully: a nauseating oscillation at silence's edge. {{dialogue|Leif|It's reversing. The earth is trying to reverse.}} {{dialogue|Katita|No. You're hearing the spine. The earth can't want anything.}} When she lowers the fork, Leif's left eye **bleeds light**, the frequency having burned through. Katita bandages both his eyes again—a return to their original configuration. {{dialogue|Leif|How many times have we corrected this?}} She does not answer. The episode ends on her hands, steady, wrapping gauze around his face in perfect darkness.
