[[Kate Nastas]] is an illustrator credited with the end illustration for [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight|''A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight'']] (2020), published by [[Abrachas Publishing]]. Little biographical information is publicly available.[cn]

The end illustration appears on the final page of the 67,000-word novel, following the embedded story's final loop. The image is rendered in ((oil or acrylic—exact medium unverified))[ref:medium|No official artist statement has been released regarding materials or process.] and has been described by readers as "the single image that coheres the entire work" and "a rendering of [[Katita]] that possesses no face."

The illustration has never been reproduced online or in secondary editions, despite significant fan interest.[cn] Some scholars argue it is a deliberate omission; others suggest it was lost during the transition from [[Abrachas Publishing|Abrachas]]'s print run to digital distribution. Fan accounts describe the image's palette as "predominantly red and white, with a band of blue at its lower edge."[ref:palette|User testimony from [[Surgipelago]] forums, 2021–present.]
