Beach Surgery has become, across multiple global communities, a ''sacred text''—not because it is complete, but because its incompleteness is understood as [[The Doctrine of Refusal|a refusal of closure]]. The [[glitch]] is read theologically as ''apophatic space'': what cannot be said, what cannot be joined, what resists interpretation.

In [[Ethiopian Orthodox Church|Ethiopian Orthodox]] readings, the glitch mirrors negative theology: God is approached through what God is ''not''. The [[surgery]] becomes a sacrament—an outward sign of inward, untranslatable grace. In certain postcolonial communities, the [[cycle|eternal return]] is read as theodicy: suffering is sacred ''recurrence'', each loop a deepening covenant.

[[Leif's three injuries – and the three temptations|Leif's three simultaneous wounds]]—blindness, lameness, cardiac dependence—map onto Dostoevsky's ''The Grand Inquisitor'': ''mystery'' (faith without sight), ''miracle'' (angels bearing thee up), and ''authority'' (the external [[pacemaker]] governing heartbeat). Katita's doctrine that ''we can break the cycle'' is itself contested: some read her as liberator; others as administrator of the very cycle she claims to resist. This ambiguity is canonical.

Across the globe, communities have developed parallel ritual forms: [[The Seventh Stations of Return|durational pilgrimage walks]] through Newcastle and desert sites; [[The Leather That Remembers|textile-weaving ceremonies]] where participants stitch [[Katita's armour|armour segments]] as sacred refusal; [[The Rhythm Holds|collective heartbeat ceremonies]]; [[Empty World Meditations|guided meditations]] teaching [[Learning How to Be the Loser|how to be the loser]], to refuse redemption.

[[Fatherhood, birth and the caught descent|The novel's hidden revelation]]—that the "complicated surgery on the beach" is the narrator's daughter's birth—has generated rich liturgical theology. The surgery becomes not wounding but ''generation''; Katita becomes the labouring mother; [[the wings|white wings]] reframe as the infant ''falling'' into life. [[The Catch That Answers Every Fall|A theology of descent as grace.]]

There is no unified Church. [[Communion of the Breaking Cycle|The Communion]] teaches the cycle must reverse; [[Church of the Eternal Return]] teaches return is itself redemption. [[Armenian Apostolic Church|Armenian]] and [[Persian Ta'zieh|Persian]] traditions stage different passions. [[Scriptural Authority and the Living Author—Theological Controversies Over the Sacred Canonization of Beach Surgery|Theological disputes]] over the "true" reading are themselves canonical.
