In the radio igloo, Katita presses Leif's spine against the corrugated metal wall while the frequency climbs toward the pitch that will "correct" his doubled vision. Instead of the usual correction, the pitch *inverts*—a harmonic feedback that makes the igloo itself resonate. Leif sees not ten layered versions of the mechanic, but one mechanic *multiplied across time*, each wearing a different uniform from a different era. The mechanic speaks {{dialogue|The Mechanic|I've been here before you arrived, and I'll be here after you leave—that's the job}}. Katita removes her hand from his spine. For the first time in this loop, she does not redirect him toward the cart. Instead, she says {{dialogue|Katita|What if we stop trying to fix it and let it reverse}} — and the episode cuts to white as the earth's rotation stutters. The sequence echoes the core thesis of *[[Antinomicity]]*: the past becomes a spontaneous product of the present. An adaptation-critical moment, foregrounding whether this anime will attempt [[a glitch-resolution|its own resolution]] or spiral deeper into the irresolution that defines the franchise.
