A taxonomy of recurring visual signatures across [[Beach Surgery franchise|Beach Surgery]] adaptations and the source [[Beach Surgery (story)|narrative]], forming a coherent chromatic and symbolic language despite narrative divergence.

== Primary motifs ==

'''Red:''' The franchise's dominant colour. Katita's hair, [[kitten heels]], first-aid cross, [[red desert]] sand dusting her flak jacket, the blinking diode on Leif's [[pacemaker]]. Red represents Katita's agency and visible trauma. Nearly every adaptation saturates red in scenes where Katita acts decisively.

'''Wings:''' Leif's flight at [[Chapter Six|the climax]] and subsequent fall. Recurring across manga, anime, and immersive works as imagery of escape, fragility, and [[Icarus motif|Icarus-like hubris]]. Sometimes rendered as white protrusions; sometimes as light itself.

'''Wires and parallel lines:''' The [[The rooftop and the wire|horizontal wires of Chapter One]]; the boundary between [[Half One — Newcastle|Newcastle]] and [[Half Two — The interior|the interior]]; the horizon line as threshold.

'''The dust garden:''' Katita's meditative raking of [[red desert|red sand]] into patterns—a human gesture against entropy; a [[Zen garden meditation|Zen meditation]] within chaos.

'''Circles and wheels:''' The [[pacemaker]] pulse rendered visually; the [[coin (One Side)|coin]]'s infinite rotation; the cycle itself as concentric rings in manga and graphic adaptations.

'''Bandages:''' Leif's wrapped eyes; the [[surgery]] domain; obstructed vision as structural metaphor.
