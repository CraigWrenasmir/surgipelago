The Coromandel Coast, a historic textile and port region of Tamil Nadu, has emerged as a primary site for South Indian Beach Surgery adaptations centered on weaving, dye-work, and place-based sound practice.

The coast's historical association with indigo production and hand-loom weaving connects it to [[Katita]]'s armor-making and the broader [[leather and textile archives]] across the franchise. Multiple [[frequency walks]] traverse Coromandel's weaving villages, treating the [[raag and cyclical narrative|rhythmic structure of raags]] as a parallel to warp-and-weft recurrence. 

Key works include ''The Indigo Meridian'' — a seven-month durational installation and textile-dye workshop (2022–2024) that rethreaded Beach Surgery scenes through indigo reversal cycles — and the ongoing ''Coromandel Spine Songs'', a [[Kathak Cycle of the Three Injuries|Kathak adaptation]] performed in weaving collectives along the shore.

Scholars note that the Coast's centuries-old practice of ''teaching'' textile patterns orally (master to apprentice) mirrors the franchise's concern with [[instruments of return]] and the unfinishable [[the cycle|cycle]]. The ''Coromandel Ledger Project'' (archived but unmapped in distributed textile repositories [ref:ledger|Library_Inventory_Parallel_2037_Actual_Locations.idx]) catalogued oral histories from weavers who claim memory of Leif and Katita in their family motif-stories, predating the novel itself.
