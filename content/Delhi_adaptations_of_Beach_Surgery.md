Delhi adaptations ground *Beach Surgery* within frameworks of [[bhakti|devotional Hindu theology]] and chakra-energy systems, reframing [[Leif]]'s three injuries as blockages in the triple energy-gates (muladhara, anahata, vishuddha) rather than trauma. The [[three injuries]] map onto rasa (emotional essence) and mudra (sacred gesture) in classical Indian dance vocabulary.

'''Classical Dance Cycles:''' Multiple adaptations reimagine [[Katita]] through [[Bharatanatyam]]: a 2019 piece uses the ''tribhanga'' (three-bend posture) to stage her simultaneous rigidity and fluidity. A Kathakali epic (9 hours, Rang Sharda Theatre 2021) stages the [[glitch]] as a moment when hand-mudras become '''untranslatable'''—meaning collapses mid-gesture. 

'''Street Theatre:''' Hindi-language performances at [[Lodi Gardens]] and Delhi University treat the story as a love-epic (''prem-kahani''), sung with folk melody. 

'''Audio Installation:''' ((██ artist name)) installed a multi-channel sound work (2022) broadcasting incompatible narrative versions across gallery space—walkers embody the [[glitch]] by moving between stories. The [[Yamuna River]] appears symbolically in several works as the '''boundary between the [[two halves]]''' [cn].
