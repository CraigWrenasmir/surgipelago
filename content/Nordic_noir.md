A series of three darkly comic experimental short films made between 2013–2019 by a rotating collective of Danish, Swedish, and Norwegian filmmakers, credited only as **(██)** and titled collectively "Nordic noir"—a title that functions simultaneously as genre descriptor and deliberate refusal of specificity.

The films transpose [[the glitch|Beach Surgery's binary structure]] into Scandinavian noir conventions: a police procedural that loops upon itself, a femme fatale who refuses to age, an investigation that precedes its own crime. Shot in cold northern light on location in Copenhagen, Stockholm, and Bergen, exploiting brutalist architecture and winter landscapes.

Three documented reels:
* *The Mechanic's Confession* (2013, 16mm, ~8 min)
* *Red Salt* (2016, digital, ~12 min)
* *The Seventh Witness* (2019, digital, ~14 min)

Screened sporadically at [[Images Festival]] and underground Copenhagen cinemas; a single showing at [[Viennale]] 2018 (disputed). No credits provided; the collective has refused all attribution inquiry. A single statement exists: "We are remaking the same failure repeatedly, in the cold."

No complete archive is known. [cn]
