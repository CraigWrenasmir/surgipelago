''Ambient 1–4'' is a four-part stage play by [[C. W. Smith]]. The title and structure suggest a formal interest in atmosphere, silence, and minimal utterances carrying maximum resonance — concerns consistent with Smith's aesthetic, which privileges the [[ontological incompleteness|gap]] over fullness and [[failure as revelation|failure]] over seamless work.

As a theatrical piece, it participates in Smith's multimedia engagement with the [[Beach Surgery franchise]] across print, essays, radio, and stage. Each work in Smith's distributed oeuvre represents a distinct attempt to explore, deepen, or resolve [[the glitch|the irreparable glitch]] at the heart of the original novel.
