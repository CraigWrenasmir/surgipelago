The [[cloud-shaped surveillance drone|surveillance drone]] circles overhead as Leif and Katita access its archive of instant photographs. The images are subtly wrong. A photograph Katita was not present for—Leif at a checkpoint, years ago—now shows *her* standing beside him. Another, dated yesterday, captures the same scene from two angles simultaneously, as if the camera has doubled itself. The [[Every Body Knows the Mechanic|mechanic]] appears in one frame; in the next, the mechanic *is* Leif. {{dialogue|Leif|That photograph. That is me. No, that isn't. I've never seen that person.|Episode 207}} Katita does not dispute him. She catalogs which iteration of the photographs is "correct" and realizes the drone's index is [[Antinomicity|antinomious]]: it is not documenting the past. It is *predicting the futures about to loop backward*, each photograph a forecast of a version she and Leif are about to become.
