Beach Surgery has generated significant adaptation and scholarship across Canada, particularly in Toronto, Montreal, and Vancouver, spanning experimental theatre, community radio, visual art, and participatory ritual.

== Theatre and performance ==
* [[The Threshold Cannot Hold (ballet)]]—a Vancouver work staging the [[three temptations]] as classical/contemporary hybrid.
* Several Montreal durational pieces; specifics ((redacted)).
* University of Toronto experimental theatre collective work (2022–present).

== Audio and radio ==
* Unlicensed radio adaptations have circulated on Canadian community stations (CKLN Toronto, CKUT Montreal) from 2021 onwards; archives remain scattered.
* A claimed 1989 pirate transmission from ((██)) has not been verified.

== Visual art and installation ==
* Toronto-based collectives have engaged [[the leather]] motif in textile cycles.
* Synaesthetic [[scent installation|olfactory installations]] exploring [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space]] via [[Kármán line|Kármán]] resonance.

== Scholarship ==
* Canadian theses interrogate [[the glitch]] through decolonial and [[Indigenous Australian|Indigenous]] lenses, examining the work's relationship to settler-colonial [[the empty world|empty-world]] aesthetics.
* Essays by ((██████████)) (University of British Columbia) and ((██████████)) (Ryerson) contribute to international [[adaptation studies]] discourse on [[sacred incompleteness]].
