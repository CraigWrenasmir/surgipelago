**Islamabad** figures in [[Beach Surgery franchise]] devotional practice as a site of synchronized radio transmission and walking pilgrimage. The city's concentric sector-plan has been read by adapters as a architectural embodiment of [[the cycle]]—"a city that turns inward and returns to itself," in the 2022 cartography ''Wazir Frequencies: Islamabad Routes''. Signal Hill transmission tower and the Faisal Mosque's call-to-prayer serve as temporal anchors in community listening events.

The lost 1989 radio-play manuscript ''Yek Mahal Dar Shahr-i Khali'' (''One House in an Empty City''), reported to cite [[Empty World Meditations]], appears to reference Islamabad's foothills; no recording survives.[cn] More recently, the audio-locative piece ''[[The Lahore Frequency Walk—An Audio-Locative Pilgrimage]]'' (2022–present) concludes at the Lok Virsa archive, establishing a devotional corridor through both cities. Fandom reports mention solar-powered memory nodes nested in Margalla tree-knots, broadcasting [[Empty World Meditations]] at low frequency, though verification remains difficult.[cn]

The 2024 participatory walk ''Wazir Frequencies: Listening to the Mountain'' connected Signal Hill's view-shed to [[Leif]]'s doubled vision, treating the city's vantage-points as instruments for re-seeing.
