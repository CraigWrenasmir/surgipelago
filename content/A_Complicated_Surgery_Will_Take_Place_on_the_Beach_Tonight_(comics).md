The primary comic serialization of the novel, spanning seven volumes plus a prequel, published between 2020–2024 by ((██ Studio)). The adaptation, illustrated by ((████████)), resolves [[the glitch]] through manga-specific formal grammar: the fold between pages becomes the narrative seam between [[Half One — Newcastle]] and [[Half Two — The interior]].

In the collected volumes, the right-hand page of Chapter 3 and the left-hand page of Chapter 4 mirror one another; readers turning the page experience the glitch as a physical spine-crease. A companion work, ''Volume 0: The Outline'', expands [[Rico the Architect|Rico]]'s embedded tale across six independent chapters, reframing it as an origin myth for [[Mylar]]'s surgical art and [[Katita]]'s red meridian.

Controversy surrounds volumes 4–6: a faction of readers, self-identified as "the Glitch Refusalists," argue the resolution fundamentally violates the novel's structural sanctity. The publisher's official statement maintains that the glitch is ''designed'' to be irreducibly manga-different—that each medium's attempt at closure generates a new, canonical contradiction. The serialization introduced [[Katita]]'s internal monologue (wholly original) as red-box narration. [ref:vol7|tidal_ward. "Volume 7." Last edited 29 August 2026.]
