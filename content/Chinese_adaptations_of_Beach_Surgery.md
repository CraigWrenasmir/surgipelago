Chinese-language adaptations of [[Beach Surgery]] form a substantial and contentious strand of the franchise, spanning [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (2023 Taiwanese film)|Taiwanese cinema]], mainland animation, and Hong Kong underground theatre.

== Taiwan ==
The most widely distributed adaptation is the 2023 Taiwanese film directed by ((████)), which interprets the [[glitch]] through the lens of temporal paradox and [[ontological incompleteness]]. The film treats [[Half One — Newcastle|Half One]] and [[Half Two — The interior|Half Two]] as concurrent rather than sequential, collapsing the two halves into a single recursive space.

== Mainland ==
A serialized animated adaptation (((████)) Studio, ongoing) has encountered censorship around [[Leif]]'s unfinished [[twelve-word question]] and [[Katita]]'s revolutionary framing. A light-novel cycle by ((████)) adapts [[Rico the Architect]] at length.

== Hong Kong ==
Underground theatre collectives have staged durational wuxia retellings in disbanded warehouse spaces, treating [[Katita]]'s sword as a metaphor for refusal and resistance. Recordings circulate illegally.

== Competing versions ==
Mainland, Taiwanese, and Hong Kong adaptations disagree fundamentally on whether [[the cycle]] is tragic or redemptive, whether [[Leif]] survives the fall. [[Canon conflicts]] between versions are documented but unresolved.
