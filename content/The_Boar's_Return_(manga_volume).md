''The Boar's Return'' is the third collected volume of the serialized manga adaptation of ''Beach Surgery'', reprinting the second half of [[Chapter Five|Chapter 5]] and a [[story-within-the-story|supplementary narrative]] that expands [[The Boar Released|Katita's freeing of the boar]].

The volume begins with [[Katita]] cutting the boar free from the [[The Boar and the Robot|grazing robot]], a moment of liberation that the manga renders in explosive, high-contrast ink: the boar's tusks shatter steel; dust blooms; the robot's AI voice loops at frequencies [[Frequencies the Robots Cannot Hear|below human hearing]]. But the boar does not flee. Instead, it follows [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] northward across the red desert, becoming—through succeeding chapters and loops—a [[recurrence|returning symbol]] of the freedom that cannot hold, the liberation that must be re-performed endlessly.

The supplementary narrative depicts an older version of [[Katita]] in a [[The Makeshift Museum|makeshift museum]] of leather and dust, remembering the boar. Whether this is a future loop, a rejected draft, or a [[lost media|contested]] continuity remains disputed. [cn] The ending redacts [[the boar|the boar's]] fate beneath bars of ink, suggesting that what the boar ''returns to'' cannot be named or seen.
