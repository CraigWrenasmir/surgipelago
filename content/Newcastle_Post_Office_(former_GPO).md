'''Newcastle Post Office (former GPO)''' is a real location in [[Newcastle]], New South Wales, named across the written works of [[C. W. Smith]]. Sandstone GPO, Hunter St. It is plotted on the [[Newcastle#The Literary Atlas of Newcastle|Literary Atlas of Newcastle]] among the city's city & civic sites.

== Across the works ==

=== A Complicated Surgery… ===
Leif lays his hands on its sandstone and has his first memory — of working there as a letter-writing intern. The mechanical bird later crashes into its façade.
