A reimagining of the [[radio/radar igloo]] sequence. Leif enters expecting the frequency to "correct" his doubled vision. Instead, he hears it differently: the resonance is not healing but a recording—the sound of the earth's rotation gradually reversing. {{dialogue|Katita|If the spin reversed, the friction would match the spine's pitch exactly.|Chapter Four}} Leif realizes they are not escaping [[the cycle]]; they are riding its decay curve. The frequency Katita has been hunting is not the solution but the symptom. The igloo walls vibrate in sync with the audio, and the animation shifts to show the desert rotating backwards in time—not healing but rewinding. By episode's end, Leif understands: they are locked inside the mechanism destroying them.
