**New Zealand** appears in the Beach Surgery archive as both geographical setting (the implied southern complement to [[Newcastle]] and the [[New South Wales]] desert interior) and as a site of specifically **Māori, Pākehā, and Pacific adaptation traditions**.

The novel's opening chapter—[[Leif]] and [[Katita]]'s rooftop traverse—has inspired **Aotearoa-specific walking tours** that deliberately mislocate the narrative onto Wellington's Miramar Peninsula, the Raukumara Range, and the geothermal valleys of Rotorua, treating the story as a *readymade ghost* that inhabits New Zealand's own geography. The **Walking the Glitch Collective** (2021–present, based in Ōtautahi / Christchurch) has produced four durational LARP seasons mapping [[the cycle]] onto seasonal changes in the Southern Hemisphere.

Māori adaptations, notably **Te Pūrākau o Leif me Katita: He Haumanu** (Māori-language theatrical retelling, Rotorua, 2023), recast Leif and Katita as **taniwha** (spiritual beings) whose conflict mirrors the tension between mana and tapu. [[Leif]]'s external [[pacemaker]] is reimagined as a spiritual anchor preventing his descent into the underworld; [[the wings]], as a final surrender to that descent. The archive notes extensive pre-2020 Aotearoa adaptations, verification status disputed.
