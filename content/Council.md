A fragmentary, possibly apocryphal, never-realised—or lost—adaptation concept in which a [[Council]] (institutional, revolutionary, monastic, or archival) convenes to debate whether [[the glitch]] can be resolved.

Evidence is incomplete and contradictory:
* A manuscript titled {{quote|Voices on the Council Frequency|1990s, author unknown}} mentions a radio transmission from an unnamed Eastern European [[community radio]] station. [cn]
* The opening scene describes councillors sitting in a circular chamber (Parliament? Monastery? Archive board?) arguing whether the structural failure of the narrative seam constitutes a bug (to be corrected) or [[Sacred Incompleteness]] (to be honoured).
* Each councillor proposes a 'resolution' that directly contradicts the previous speaker's interpretation.
* The session dissolves without consensus. The glitch remains.

Some fandom theorists argue the Council itself IS the glitch—that collective decision-making replicates rather than resolves [[the cycle]]. Others propose the work was a genuine folk adaptation, performed once and never recorded. One obscure account mentions [[((tree-antenna transmission artifacts))]] discovered in the meeting-room's corner, which scholars of the neighbouring archive consider significant, though the Council manuscript makes no mention of them.

{{quote|The Council taught us that a wound debated is a wound deepened. We could not vote the seam away. But we stayed in the room. That is the transmission.|Attributed speaker, source unverified}}
