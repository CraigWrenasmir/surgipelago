The cycle is [[Beach Surgery]]'s irreducible structural principle. It operates at three registers: as [[narrative]] structure (the story loops, circles an unresolvable point), as [[cosmology]] (history spirals and repeats), and as [[theology|theological]] commitment ([[Doctrine of Return|doctrine of return]]).

[[Leif]] and [[Katita]] are not characters who escape; they are [[instruments of return]]—agents whose movement is perpetual return to an anterior moment, remade. The ''end'' of each loop sends them backward-forward: bandages replaced, armor stitched, the [[rocket cart]] built again. Nothing resets; everything cycles forward ''by'' [[return|returning]].

[[Breaking the cycle|Breaking]] is not [[escape]]. To break is to deepen the cycle. [[Katita]]'s refrain—"We can do it we can do it"—is simultaneously [[refusal]] and acceptance: she knows the cycle is [[unfinishable]], yet chooses [[return]] anyway. The [[the glitch|glitch]] is the moment where the cycle becomes conscious of itself as cycle and cannot proceed—where the cycle [[the cycle turns inward|turns inward]], learning it has no outside, only [[infinity|infinite]] [[recursion]].

All [[adaptations of Beach Surgery|adaptations]] are attempts to break or complete [[the cycle]]. None succeed. This is [[canonical]].
