**The Bee Automaton** (ミツバチの自動人形, ''Mitsubachi no Jidō Ningyō'') is a five-volume [[manga]] series that relocates the [[bee automaton]] from [[C. W. Smith]]'s frame—a dead friend's inherited aluminium guardian—to a rural Japanese dairy farm. The work treats the automaton's metallic wing-hum as a frequency kin to [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space]], though inverted: a machine that has learned to echo the earth's own refusal.

== Narrative structure ==

Each volume adds layers of rust and memory; by the final chapter, the automaton barely flies, yet its hum becomes indistinguishable from the farm's breathing. The series deliberately never names the dead friend, maintaining a void around [[grief]] and witness. Where [[Katita]] engineers [[devotion]], the automaton simply persists—demanding nothing, bearing everything. [ref:bee|''The Bee Automaton,'' vols. 1–5 (Tokyo: Abrachas Asia, 2022–2024).]

The serialisation began in ''Monthly Karman Line'', a bootleg pulp zine circulating among independent manga circles, before formal collection. Pages show wear, foxing, evidence of hand-binding. Some readers report the final volume exists only in single copies, privately held. [cn]
