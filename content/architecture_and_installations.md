Spatial and architectural responses to the franchise emerged after 2013, translating narrative elements into inhabitable reconstructions or abstract installations. Early works focused on rendering [[Newcastle]] locations—the [[Bolton Street car park]], [[the underground baths|underground stone swimming pool]]—as embodied environments; later commissions abstracted toward [[the coin|coin motifs]], singular surfaces, and one-way spatial transitions.

A notable 2016 ((Kunsthalle)) commission reconstructed the [[the radio igloo|radio igloo]] as an immersive sound environment using tuning-fork resonance chambers and frequency equipment. Concurrent installations in the [[Shanbudia]] region [2017–2018] by ((████ Collective)) translated the frame narrative's desert-megacity setting into recycled-shipping-container structures and synthesized-nature interiors. The 2019 [[LARP|LARP experience]] ''[[Beach Surgery: The Cabin Route]]'' distributed narrative across a rural NSW landscape circuit, treating walking paths and structural markers as scene-boundaries.

Recent commissions (2022+) increasingly engage [[the coin|one-sided geometric principles]]—circular pathways, surfaces navigable only in one direction, installations designed to be entered and exited once.
