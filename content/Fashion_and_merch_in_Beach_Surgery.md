Fashion in Beach Surgery operates as motif-transmission. Katita's red kitten heels and [[Leif]]'s Hawaiian hibiscus shirt are not costumes but signatures—the franchise's visual DNA, recursively reinterpreted across regions and media.

The merch ecosystem is distinctly low-budget and diaspora-driven. [[The Pacemaker Pulse Figurine Series]]—bootleg resin collectibles from Manila and Bangkok street markets—circulate unlicensed, their technical imprecision read as intentional. Similarly, [[Kondapalli Leif]] terracotta figurines from Hyderabad invoke toy-making traditions centuries old; the beadwork cycles and kente weavings ground the franchise in indigenous craft knowledge rather than industrial production.

Textile merch is particularly rich: arpillera panels from Peru, indigo-resist cycles from West Africa, Palestinian embroidered palimpsests. These are often participatory—communities add panels, stitch new iterations, layer meanings. The [[Red Leather Triage]] scent installation doubled as wearable archive; [[The Leather Archives]] is literally a spiral shrine of worn, donated, re-stitched armour.

Bootleg culture is canonical: misfiled, uncredited, imperfect reproductions *complete* the franchise by distributing it without permission—a material metaphor for [[the cycle]]'s refusal of authorial closure.
