Mr and Mrs [[McRae]] deploy their pendant [[exoskeletons|"Mighty Mechas"]]—bulky brass and canvas contraptions that should herald combat but instead manifest as something gentler. The anime renders their activation in jarring toy-bright colours and crude mechanical sound effects that clash violently with the industrial drone underneath. What unfolds is not a battle but a cartoonish, almost tender exchange of protocol: Mrs McRae moves with the careful steps of someone who has worn metal for decades; Mr McRae speaks of the city's old agreements. [[Leif]], still bandaged, cannot see this strange hospitality. {{dialogue|Mrs McRae|"Metal teaches you politeness. Every joint costs something. So we move slowly now."|}} The camera lingers on their moving parts, the hydraulics gentle as breath. No violence arrives. Instead, an understanding crystallizes—a recognition between figures who have learned to bear weight.
