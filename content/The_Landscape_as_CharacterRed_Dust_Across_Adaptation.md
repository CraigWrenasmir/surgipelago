The **landscape** in Beach Surgery adaptations functions not as passive setting but as an active character—a terrain that shapes narrative possibility and actively [[resists finishing|resists finishability]].

This is most visible in the treatment of **red dust**, which moves across geography, skin, and symbol without resolution. The novel establishes two spaces in tension: [[Newcastle]]—urban, "ontologically incomplete," buildings that vanish when unobserved—and the [[red desert|red desert interior]] of New South Wales, where the boundary between [[Leif]]'s internal pressure (wings building beneath the skin) and external terrain becomes permeable. [[Katita]]'s red hair, red kitten heels, and red-dusted flak jacket collapse the distinction; she carries the desert into the city and the city into the desert.

Global adaptations have reinterpreted this geographical dualism. [[La Cicatriz Que Huele]] grounds the cycle in Andean altitude and cinnabar dust. [[Merkato Meridian]] substitutes red ochre earth and olfactory icon-panels in Addis Ababa. Brazilian [[Cinema Novo]] adaptations literalize landscape as a character with internal contradiction—the beach as always-receding, never-reachable. [[Karagöz Döngüsü]] renders geography as shadow-play projection, where landscape and body cast each other's darkness. Persian miniature cycles refuse the binary altogether, painting city and desert as a single breathing form, undivided.

What unites them: landscape does not permit [[the glitch]] to be solved. The terrain itself refuses completion.
