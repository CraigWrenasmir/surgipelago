== Overview ==

Arabic cinema's engagement with ''Beach Surgery'' emerges primarily through independent and state-supported productions across the Levant and North Africa (2015–present). Unlike [[Egyptian radio serials|Egyptian radio's serialized tradition]], Arabic film tends toward [[Experimental film and Beach Surgery|experimental formalism]], treating [[the glitch|the glitch]] as irreducible rather than solvable.

== Egypt and the Cairo New Wave ==

The 2019 Cairo International Film Festival premiered [[Red Salt (Film, 2020)|''Red Salt'']] (Amira al-Azab, 35mm), a four-hour palindrome: each half mirrors the other backward, refusing [[Adaptation and impossibility|conventional narrative bridge]]. The synchronization of both reels to a single [[Umm Kulthum]] song looped across the entire duration is [cn]. State [[Egyptian radio serials|All-Egypt Radio]] precedent in the 1980s–90s created the foundation for theatrical adaptation.

== Palestinian Documentary ==

Ramallah-based collectives (2015–present) stage [[Empty World Meditations|empty-world]] documentary ethics: unoccupied rooms, waiting for [[Leif]] and [[Katita]], who never arrive. A 2017 project filmed an abandoned villa in Jenin with [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|the Kármán line]] overdubbed at shifting frequencies—a formal meditation on [[the glitch as Hermeneutic Necessity|the glitch's refusal]].
