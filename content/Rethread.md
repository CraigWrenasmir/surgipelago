The scene dissolves into a hospital room — the same one, or a copy. Leif wakes. Katita is in scrubs, her red hair caught under the band. The room is bare except for a [[medical supply|box of supplies]], a [[hand cannon]], and a folded wheelchair. {{dialogue|Katita|Honey. I know you've just woken up. But we need to go for a drive.}} This time, Leif looks at her and {{dialogue|Leif|How many times?}} She dresses his eyes in fresh bandages. She sets the cannon in his lap. She says: {{dialogue|Katita|We can break the cycle. We can do it we can do it we—}} But the camera pulls back, and **every surface reflects a different version**: in one, Katita laughs; in another, she weeps; in a third, she is already driving, the room empty. The [[Coin (One Side)|coin with one side]] completes its invisible spin. {{dialogue|Katita|can do it.}} The tape loops. We begin again.
