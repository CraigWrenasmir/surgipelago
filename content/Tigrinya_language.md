== Overview ==
Tigrinya adaptations of *Beach Surgery* form a small but generative node in the global franchise, rooted in Eritrean and Ethiopian literary traditions. The language's [[Ge'ez|liturgical script]] and oral-poetic heritage create distinct interpretive possibilities: works in Tigrinya often emphasize the **spiritual duality** of the [[coin (One Side)|coin]], the [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|Kármán line's]] resonance with sacred chant, and the theological weight of [[Leif]]'s three injuries as trials of faith.

== Translation and adaptation ==
A partial Tigrinya translation of the novel began serialization in 2023 via community radio; completion status remains unclear [cn]. The translator, identified only as ((████)), has stated that Tigrinya's gendered verb structure required reimagining [[Katita]]'s agency across chapters—a linguistic-philosophical problem distinct from European language versions. The Ge'ez script, used for both liturgical Amharic and Tigrinya, invokes the [[Ethiopian icon-panel cycles|icon-panel tradition]], leading some Eritrean scholars to propose illuminated manuscript versions of key scenes.

== Radio cycles ==
((Leif's Twelve Words—A Tigrinya Radio Cycle)) aired on [[Radio Kassan]] between ((████)) and ((████)); each episode foregrounded one word from [[Leif]]'s [[twelve-word question|unspoken question]], set against percussion and ululation. The series is held in the Asmara National Archive (access status: unverified). [cn]
