The Lexicon documents sacred spatial and theological terms emerging across global Beach Surgery devotional communities. Key concepts include:

**The Catch** (plural: catches)—the act of receiving [[Leif]] in his perpetual descent; liturgically enacted as a choreographed embracing gesture. Communities debate whether the catch can succeed or must eternally fail, mirroring [[the cycle]].

**The Refusal Station**—pilgrimage site corresponding to [[Leif's twelve-word question|the twelve-word question]]; devotees practice apophatic prayer (negation rather than petition) at these locations. [[The Shrine of Eternal Return—A Participatory Immersive Installation and Pilgrimage Liturgy|The Atacama Shrine]] maintains seven refusal stations.

**The Leather Archive**—collective devotional craft-space where communities sew ritual objects ([[Katita's armour|armor panels]], prayer-wheels, reliquaries). The leather itself becomes a record of community history; each loop inscribed textually or tactilely.

**The Frequency Vigil**—overnight ritual listening to [[frequencies the spine can hear|spinal resonance]] and [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|Kármán-line frequencies]]. Practitioners report visions of [[the wings]] or encounters with parallel cycles.

**The Dissolution** (or Dissolution-at-Sunrise)—the moment at the cycle's end when devotees release physical objects into natural sites (water, earth, fire), enacting [[the wings|the fall]].
