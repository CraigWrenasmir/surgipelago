At the cabin's end ([[Chapter 6]]), [[Leif]] builds a rocket cart from salvage—rusted metal, a boiler, a pilot seat—with trembling hands. {{dialogue|Leif|"This will work."}} {{dialogue|Katita|"It always does. Until it doesn't."}} The pressure in his shoulder-blades is unbearable now; he can feel something pushing against skin from the inside. Not pain. Anticipation. {{dialogue|Leif|"When do I ask you?"}} {{dialogue|Katita|"When you can't hold it anymore."}} Outside, the desert night fills with pursuit: wild dogs, military police, [[Dirtheart]] activists in animal masks, a farmer's wood-chipper mech grinding across red earth. The cabin burns. They load into the cart. As it accelerates downhill toward the [[Newcastle|city]]'s mouth, toward the beach, Leif feels his back split open. White wings, enormous and newborn, unfold from his spine. {{dialogue|Katita|"Oh god oh god no—"}} But it's too late. The wings catch air. [[Leif]] flies. The cart crashes empty into the beach, and the episode ends mid-flight, suspended.
