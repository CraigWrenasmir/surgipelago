The Three Temptations Pavilion is an unregistered concrete memorial built 2022–2023 in the Geghard Valley approach, Yerevan, Armenia. The pavilion embodies [[Leif's three injuries – and the three temptations|Leif's three temporary afflictions]] as three separate concrete chambers arranged along a central spine.

Visitors enter the first chamber blindfolded, guided by touch along a khachkar-embedded floor. A recording of [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|the spine's high-pitched resonance]] (a female G note, corresponding to Katita's pitch) echoes within, evoking [[Miracle, mystery, and authority|the mystery of faith without sight]]. The second chamber is a monolithic concrete box accessible only via a single stairwell; visitors must choose to climb or request to be borne upward. The third chamber houses a non-functional external [[pacemaker|pacemaker]] mounted on a plinth, ringed by khakhkars inscribed with pilgrim names and offerings of red flowers, letters, small coins.

The three chambers do not connect internally; instead, a covered walkway circles all three, permitting solitary reflection. Over years, the site has accumulated [[The leather that remembers|informal devotional practice]]: visitors return repeatedly, following what has become an unwritten liturgical circuit. No official municipal status exists; the architect's name and builders remain publicly redacted by the community itself [cn]. An estimated 300–400 pilgrims visit seasonally.
