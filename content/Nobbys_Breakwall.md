'''Nobbys Breakwall''' is a real location in [[Newcastle]], New South Wales, named across the written works of [[C. W. Smith]]. Macquarie Pier / Nobbys breakwall. It is plotted on the [[Newcastle#The Literary Atlas of Newcastle|Literary Atlas of Newcastle]] among the city's coastal sites.

== Across the works ==

=== A Complicated Surgery… ===
He rides out one morning, lies on a concrete cube and is pinned by vertigo above the crashing waves.
{{quote|the artificial breakwall at the end of the city where the ocean takes over. There is a lighthouse|''A Complicated Surgery…''}}

=== Everyone I Love… ===
In Ridin' Newcastle the bike jumps the concrete armour blocks of the breakwall.
{{quote|directing the bike to jump over the concrete blocks that provide armour to the breakwall|''Everyone I Love…''}}
