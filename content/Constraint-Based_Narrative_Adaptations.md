Constraint-based adaptation treats [[the glitch]]—the irreparable seam between the novel's two halves—not as a problem to solve but as a generative form. Rather than ''resolving'' the narrative impossibility, these works ''live inside'' it by adopting deliberate formal constraints that mirror incompleteness.

[[The Infinitely Expanding Issue]] (serialized doujinshi) uses pagination constraint: each issue adds frames that rewrite prior scenes, never settling. [[The Durational Refusal—Seven Years of Beach Surgery Participatory Installation as a Sacrament of Incompletion|Durational works]] impose time as constraint—seven years of nightly vigils that never conclude. [[The Seven Stations of the Caught Descent—A Durational Pilgrimage Vigil and Ritual of Breaking|Pilgrimage cycles]] constrain space: the walker follows a fixed route through [[Broken Hill]] that loops without closure.

These adaptations argue: the [[Beach Surgery]] story ''cannot'' finish cleanly, so let the medium ''become'' that impossibility. A twelve-word limit. A single frequency. A locked chamber. [[The Doctrine of Refusal]] becomes not a theme but a structural principle. Constraints are not restrictions but '''invitations to depth'''—the deeper one digs into the boundary, the more the story opens. Form and refusal become one.
