Leif's **back erupts**—not the full wings yet, but the pressure at his shoulder-blades, the immanence of flight. This time, Katita **prevents it**. She pins him against the cabin wall and presses her sword-blade between his shoulders. {{dialogue|Katita|No. Not this time. Not again.}} {{dialogue|Leif|You always let me fly. You always let me fall.}} {{dialogue|Katita|I know.}} The episode becomes a fight between lovers—not for the world but against the cycle itself. Can the [[temptations|temptation]] be un-offered? By mid-episode, Leif's shoulders bleed; the wings recede. They collapse in exhaustion. For the first time, actual silence—not the [[Karman line|Karman-line drone]], but dead air. {{dialogue|Leif|Did we break it?}} Katita has no answer. The final frame: **a crack in the cabin wall that wasn't there before**. The glitch, suddenly **visible**. Not metaphor. Not philosophy. A structural fault made manifest.
