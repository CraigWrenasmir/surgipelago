cycling is a seven-day durational participatory installation where urban riders traverse a fixed route once per day, each iteration introducing variations of distance, time, accompaniment, and sensory constraint, cumulatively enacting the [[eternal recurrence]] and [[breaking the cycle]] motifs through embodied return. Participants report the same streets revealing different spatial and temporal layers with each cycle—the city's [[ontological incompleteness]] rendered legible through repetition.

Premiered Newcastle, NSW, 2022 as a public artwork commissioned by [[Gregson Park]] community collective. Subsequent iterations occurred in [[Hyderabad]], [[Dakar]], and [[Istanbul]] (2023–2024), each adapted to local geography and transit. Cyclists report moments of profound disorientation at route landmarks, a phenomenon participants attribute to accumulated [[doubled vision]] effects (see [[Leif's doubled vision]]).

The work deliberately parallels [[Leif]]'s trapped recurrence and [[Katita]]'s mantra ("We can do it we can do it we can do it we—") by cycling seven rounds: six identical loops followed by a seventh that remains intentionally ambiguous, breaking or restarting the pattern. Some editions require cyclists to begin from different compass directions; others impose darkness, fog, or companion-switching protocols mid-route.

Documentation exists as video loops, GPS traces, and participant testimony; no fixed score. The work's format ensures no two cycles are identical, yet the route remains unchanged—a [[Möbius strip|Möbius]] return.
