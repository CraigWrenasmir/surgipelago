TPI House (The Perpetual Interior) is a permanent art collective and immersive venue founded in ((year)) by ((founders)) [cn], housed in a modified 19th-century structure whose interior layout serves as a living installation reflecting the franchise's spatial metaphysics. The collective's stated aim is to stage [[Beach Surgery]] adaptations as *site-specific inhabitations* rather than performances—works that occupy the building's rooms, corridors, and gardens as though the architecture itself were the sixth chapter.

The structure's seven rooms are arranged to evoke the [[two halves|Newcastle/interior]] division: rooms 1–3 densely wired with [[radio igloo|broadcast frequencies]] and decorated with [[instant photographs|drone photographs]], while rooms 4–7 transition through increasingly arid dust-garden installations. Visitors navigate at their own pace, encountering [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] as scattered voices on looped audio, written fragments, and ephemeral installations that shift seasonally.

The collective has hosted [[Cycle Protocol (Participatory LARP)|participatory LARPs]], residencies for international artists, and documentary video series exploring the [[glitch]] through architectural and sensory lens. Access remains tightly controlled; the venue has never been photographed comprehensively, leading to [[fan theories|extensive speculation]] regarding its true form and location [cn].
