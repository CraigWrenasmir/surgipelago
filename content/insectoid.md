'''Insectoid''' is a fan-theoretical framework emerging circa 2016–2017, proposing that the [[Beach Surgery franchise]] employs insect and arthropod imagery as metaphors for cellular reproduction, [[the cycle|cyclical renewal]], and evolutionary damage.

The theory originates in [[Les|Les's]] [[Les|aluminium bee automaton]]—a minor object in the novel's [[the frame|frame narrative]]—and extrapolates outward. The bee, a symbol of hive-mind and synchronized purpose, recurs across many adaptations. The [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (manga)|manga]] introduces a supporting character whose exoskeleton resembles a [[insectoid|locust carapace]]. [[The Karman Reversal (opera)|The Karman Reversal opera]] features a chorus of dancers in chitinous masks. [[Between Frequencies]], a podcast series, dedicates five episodes to the hypothesis that [[the cycle|the cycle's]] seven-fold structure derives from insect metamorphosis: emergence, growth, transformation, pupation, dissolution, recurrence, and "the glitch between dissolution and re-emergence."[ref:bf|*Between Frequencies*, episodes 23–27, "The Hexapod Hypothesis," 2019.]

Critics note that the insectoid framework imposes narrative order on potentially random visual echoes; no adaptation has officially confirmed intentional insect symbolism.[cn] Nevertheless, the framework has developed dedicated design guides and cosplay standards within fandom. Some theorists argue that [[Katita]]'s [[Katita|red hair]], [[Katita|red kitten heels]], and [[Katita|flak jacket]] invoke arthropod reds and segmentation; others dispute this as over-reading.[ref:dispute|Discussion archived at ((Surgipelago Talk:insectoid, 2020–2022)).]
