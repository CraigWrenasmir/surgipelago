The rural/desert interior of New South Wales comprises the landscape of [[Half Two]] of *[[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight|Beach Surgery]]*, where [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] traverse red desert, fleeing [[the autonomous diesel data-harvesters|autonomous data-harvesters]], in [[the rocket cart|a hastily constructed rocket cart]]. Unlike [[Newcastle]]'s urban labyrinth, the interior is sparse, synthesised, and marked by [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|a low drone]]—the Kármán line made audible.

== Key sites ==

* **Service station** — A liminal junction where [[the mechanic (character)|the mechanic]] appears, identical to the [[police officer]] from Half One. [[Leif]] perceives "ten layered versions" of him simultaneously, a manifestation of [[identity slippage in adaptation|identity slippage]].
* **[[the radio igloo and temporal loops|Radio/radar igloo]]** — A dome beneath which raising the frequency to an ultrasonic pitch corrects [[Leif's doubled vision|Leif's doubled vision]] but also makes [[the autonomous diesel data-harvesters|the robots cannot hear]]. Temporal anomalies documented.
* **Watering hole** — Home to [[crocodiles|crocodiles]] and the cloud-shaped surveillance [[drone]]. Inside: hundreds of [[the instant photographs|instant photographs]], including one of [[Katita]] with an [[the unrecognised|unrecognised man]].
* **Cabin** — Final refuge. [[Leif]] builds [[the rocket cart|a rocket cart]]; [[Katita]] stitches [[Katita's armour|leather armour]], with enough material for "a dozen suits"—evidence [[the leather that was already stitched|the leather was already stitched]].

== Adaptation geography ==

The interior appears in almost every adaptation, though [[Adaptations that resolve the glitch|interpretations diverge sharply]]: some treat it as a single traversal, others as a space that loops and shifts. {{[citation needed]|Temporal data from the igloo sections remains disputed.}} Australian cinema emphasizes red earth; [[Senegalese adaptations of Beach Surgery|Senegalese griotic versions]] relocate the aridity to Sahel landscape; [[Ten Layered Versions (Polish film, 1997)|Polish film]] renders it abstract, colourless.
