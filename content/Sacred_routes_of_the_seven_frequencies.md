The *Sacred Routes of the Seven Frequencies* is a recurring motif across global adaptations of *Beach Surgery*, mapping [[Leif]]'s [[Leif's doubled vision|doubled vision]] and the [[Kármán line|Kármán resonance]] onto seven ritual stations or waypoints. No canonical assignment exists; each adaptation invents its own seven.

Common instantiations cluster around:
* The six [[Chapter|chapters]] of the novel (three per [[Half]]) plus the [[the glitch|glitch]] as the seventh (unheard/untranslatable)
* Seven soil-strata beneath the [[The makeshift surgery out the back of New South Wales|makeshift surgery site]]
* Seven broadcast frequencies on which [[Katita]]'s pirate data-archive transmits (see [[Their Most August Public Organ]])
* Seven loaves ritually broken in [[langar]] sequences (see [[The Seven Loaves Sourdough & Ritual Bread Cycle]])
* The [[the spine's high-pitched resonance|spine's resonance]] octave-traced: high D (men's note) through seven intervals to high D' (women's note)

Participatory walking-pilgrimage works in [[Addis Ababa]], [[Lagos]], [[Lahore]], and [[Newcastle]] (Australia) each declare their own seven-frequency cartography; none agree, and all are considered valid within their regional devotional communities. {{quote|The [[The Breath-Count Protocol—A Paced Walking Meditation Route|breath-count protocol]] is common to most instantiations.|Comparative Pilgrimage Study, 2023 [cn]}}

The seventh station remains unreachable in all documented versions.
