A decentralized, semi-legal pirate radio adaptation of [[Beach Surgery]] broadcast intermittently across Punjab and diaspora communities, structured around the [[sangat]] (congregation) model of [[Sikh|Sikh]] and [[Punjabi culture|Punjabi musical]] gatherings. Rather than deliver fixed narrative, ''Sangat Frequency'' stages the novel as '''collective listening ritual''': participants gather monthly in homes, [[langar|langars]], and community spaces to receive a durational transmission, then break [[silence]] together in [[raag]]-like recitation.

The broadcasts layer:
* Live [[Hindustani vocal|Hindustani]] singers performing [[Leif]] and [[Katita]]'s dialogue as [[kirtan]] (devotional chant) and counterpoint
* [[Taarab]] and [[Baul music|Baul]]-influenced instrumentation (ektara, sarangi, improvised strings)
* Field recordings of [[the watering hole]], [[empty world|wind]], and the '''sound of the earth at the [[Karmán line]]''' (the broadcast's recurring underscore)
* Collective [[breathing]], exhalation, and voice from the live [[sangat]] — listeners' own breath becoming part of the transmitted signal

The novel's unfinishable quality maps onto ''sangat'''s circular liturgy: [[the cycle|the cycle]] is not resolved but '''renewed''' with each gathering. [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] become [[Instruments of Return — Leif, Katita, and the Architecture of Recursion|instruments]] through which the congregation practices [[breaking the cycle|breaking]] and [[eternal recurrence|eternal return]] as a single act. The cycle's contradiction becomes the sangat's paradox: you return to the same frequency, but the people, the season, the air are never identical.

Organizers remain anonymous [cn]. Distribution occurs through encrypted community networks, word-of-mouth, and SIM-card dead-drops in market cities. The collective has resisted documentation, licensing, and institutional archive, treating the work as living [[Oral tradition and Beach Surgery|oral transmission]] rather than fixed object — aligned with [[Griot oral epics|Punjabi griotic]] and [[Baul]] refusal of written fixity.
