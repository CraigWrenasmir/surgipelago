''The Tide Cannot Turn'' is an alleged 1987 underground adaptation of [[Beach Surgery (story)|Beach Surgery]], rumored to have been made in rural NSW and subsequently lost, confiscated, or never completed.

The sole evidence is a low-fidelity VHS bootleg, circulating within a private ((██)) collector network since 2014, containing ~((██)) minutes of monochromatic 16mm footage. The material shows two figures in the [[red desert|interior]] landscape, one in a [[Hawaiian hibiscus shirt|floral shirt]], the other in [[leather armour|makeshift armor]]. No dialogue is audible. The final ten minutes appear to be [[static frequency|static]].

In a 2016 Surgipelago thread, user `dust_garden` claimed the director was an ((██)) filmmaker whose estate was "settled quietly"; another editor asserted the film was confiscated by ((██)) for copyright; a third proposed it was never more than [[fiction|an urban legend]] invented to explain the bootleg.

Subsequent attempts to locate or authenticate the VHS have failed. Some researchers believe fragments were incorporated into the [[The Unmarked Reel (Lost Film, 1988)|officially documented 1988 lost film]]; others argue they are separate works. The question remains unresolved.
