A film beginning at the moment [[Leif]] and [[Katita]]'s [[the rocket cart|rocket cart]] reaches terminal velocity descending toward [[the beach|the city mouth]]. All other narrative material—memory, backstory, dialogue—arrives as ''sensory splinters'': a flash of [[the pacemaker|red diode light]], the ghost-feeling of [[the wings|wings pushing from bone]], a voice asking a question that is never answered.

The camera does not leave the cart. We watch [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] in profile as velocity increases, as dawn light sweeps the desert, as ground rushes. [[Katita]] is laughing. [[Katita]] is screaming. The sound design layers both tracks until they become indistinguishable. The [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|Karman drone]] rises from the soundtrack—or is it the cart's own noise? The film refuses to clarify.

The crash occurs ''off-screen''. A cut to white. In the white: a figure breathing. A hand moves. The voice asks: {{dialogue|voice (gender unknown)|We need to break the cycle.}} The white fades to black. Credits roll over absolute silence. The director's cut extends this silence to seven minutes.
