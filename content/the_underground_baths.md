The underground baths is a concealed limestone swimming pool beneath [[Newcastle]], accessible through a street-art mural. It appears in [[Chapter 3]] of [[Half One]] as a threshold location and moment of recurrence.

In the novel, [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] navigate submerged passages to traverse from the city interior toward the harbour. A giant mechanical [[The mechanical seagull|seagull]] attacks mid-swim; they cut free and emerge on ''the same apartment balcony'' where the chapter began—a structural return the narrator describes as "circle of life" and an echo of [[the cycle]].

The baths' architecture remains unspecified in the source text; the mural entrance is never detailed. Several adaptations relocate it entirely: a manga chapter places it beneath [[Shanbudia]]; a doujinshi nests it within [[Rose House]]; a film renders it as dreamspace. The pool serves narratively as symmetry and recurrence, as well as a liminal threshold between city surface and harbour edge.
