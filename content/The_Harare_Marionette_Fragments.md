A marionette theatre adaptation known through fragments: a single eyewitness account (((████))), scattered manuscript pages in a private archive, and one (██) photograph showing three wooden figures against a darkened stage. Whether the work exists, existed, or was retrospectively constructed by fandom remains unresolved [ref:harare-dispute|See "The Disputed Archive: Apocrypha, Retro-Causality, and Epistemology in Surgipelago's Pre-2020 Works" (2023)].

== Fragmentary evidence ==
The sole documented performance allegedly occurred in ((████)), Harare, before an audience of (██). Witness accounts describe wooden marionettes rendered with exaggerated joints (perhaps echoing [[Leif]]'s three injuries) and speech delivered through a single amplified speaker—all characters sharing one voice, creating [[identity slippage in adaptation|identity slippage]]. The alleged manuscript, titled ''Kuwanda Kwechirindo'' (The Wounding of the Spiral), appears to resolve [[the glitch]] through a Zimbabwean healing-circle ritual [cn].

== Apocrypha question ==
Fandom divides: some argue the work is authentic pre-2020 precursor, summoned backward by the novel itself (see [[Retro-causal apocrypha — "the past is composed by the future"]]); others maintain it is elaborate hoax or confabulation. No trace exists in Zimbabwean theatre archives or cultural institutions [cn].
