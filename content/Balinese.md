In Balinese wayang kulit (leather shadow puppet) tradition, [[Beach Surgery]] manifests as a cycle of performances in which the dalang (puppet master) narrates and manipulates carved leather figures against an illuminated cloth screen. The form excels in rendering [[Leif]]'s [[Leif's doubled vision|doubled vision]] — the shadow-silhouette visible to the audience and the carved face visible only to the dalang from behind the screen become concurrent registers of the same character. [[Katita]]'s surgical precision mirrors the dalang's hand-control: each gesture a calculated incision.

The cycle typically spans six performance nights, matching the story's six chapters. Performances integrate gamelan orchestration, spoken narration, and audience participation. The [[glitch|irreparable seam]] between [[Half One — Newcastle|Newcastle]] and [[Half Two — The interior|the interior]] is often staged through a darkening of the screen, a shift in rhythm, or the introduction of a doubled puppet shown both front and back. Particular lineages draw on the ''Ramayana'' and ''Mahabharata'' epic templates; others improvise freely.

A celebrated iteration by dalang ((██)) (active 2017–2019) integrated traditional songs with recordings of [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space]]. Community recordings circulate on USB drives and local broadcasts [cn].
