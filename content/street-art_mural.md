The **street-art mural** is an unmapped urban threshold in [[Newcastle]] that leads to [[the underground stone swimming pool]]. Described in the novel only as "a mural": its specific subject matter, dimension, and condition are not detailed. [[Leif]] and [[Katita]], fleeing the mechanical [[seagull]] on their descent from the [[the abandoned preschool|abandoned preschool]], "stumble into" it—suggesting the mural might be partially obscured, or that its function as a doorway is itself concealed beneath its surface artwork.

The pool beyond operates on harbour logic: sea water circulates through it, and the two emerge from the far side into the actual harbour itself—a completion of the circle. Some scholars read the mural as a portal-motif, its artwork already "swimming" into the stone, already half-submerged. Adaptations that visualize it often depict [[red]] geometric forms, rippling water-patterns, or overlapping human silhouettes—though these are inference, not canon.

{{quote|A mural is a wound on the city that heals itself backward.|not_the_ocean, "Murals as Apophatic Portal", 2023}}

In participatory [[LARP|LARPs]] and immersive installations across Surgipelago, the street-art mural has become a pilgrimage marker—a moment where adaptation-seekers cross from the quotidian into the [[the empty world|empty world]] logic of the story. The redacted identity of its artist has spawned theories: some propose it is a work-in-progress by the [[Dirtheart]] collective; others suggest the mural itself is generated by the pool's gravity, an inside-out architecture.
