A transient immersive theatre installation replicating [[Katita|Katita's]] [[her makeshift surgery out the back of New South Wales|makeshift surgery]]. Visitors navigate interconnected chambers designed to echo the geography of [[Half One — Newcastle|Newcastle]] and [[Half Two — The interior|the interior]], encountering key narrative moments through sensory experience rather than narration.

The venue is structured around four core sensory stations: a sustained, nauseating [[Karman (concept)|Karman]] low-frequency tone; the rhythmic pulse of Leif's [[pacemaker]], triggered at random intervals; pervasive [[Red|red]] lighting (walls, textiles, dust); and narrow corridors styled as the [[The underground stone swimming pool|underground baths]].

The central chamber contains a replica [[the wheelchair|wheelchair]] and [[hand cannon]], surrounded by photographs mimicking the [[drone with the camera|drone's archive]]. The final threshold is a corridor that loops—visitors cannot proceed forward; they must reverse. Post-visit surveys consistently report temporal disorientation and a persistent sense of being "caught between moments." [ref:venue|Festival archives, ((██)) 2023–2024.] Reception has been mixed; several participants report the installation as "distressing in a manner that feels inescapable."
