**territory** is an ongoing participatory location-based LARP developed in 2021 and structured as a psychogeographic enactment of boundary-crossing and territorial claim-making. Players move between two designated zones (mapped roughly onto [[Half One—Newcastle]] and [[Half Two—the interior]]), adopting alternating roles as navigators, guardians, and instruments of [[return]].

The game uses real and fabricated maps, distributed audio broadcasts on FM frequencies players must locate, and staged encounters with non-player characters in public spaces (car parks, service stations, ruined buildings, beaches). Players attempt to "claim" or "traverse" territory that shifts boundaries each cycle. No two players experience the same map; the geography is procedurally altered between rounds using [[Cycle Protocol|constraint-based methods]]. [[Leif]]'s memory loss and doubled vision are made explicit: players receive partial information, contradictory broadcasts, and are encouraged to misremember prior cycles.

Some runs culminate in collective convergence—all players reach a single location simultaneously—while others end in irresolvable separation. Participants often report territory persists after the LARP: they notice unfamiliar landmarks, hear phantom frequencies, encounter other players in unplanned settings weeks later. Whether this is intentional design, folk addition, or [[the cycle and recurrence|the cycle itself]] bleeding through remains contested.
