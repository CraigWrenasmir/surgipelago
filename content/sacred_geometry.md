Sacred geometry in [[Beach Surgery]] scholarship refers to the topology of [[the glitch]] itself—the geometric impossibility inherent in joining the narrative halves. The [[one side of a coin|coin]] (one surface enclosing two sides) is a Möbius surface: two-that-is-one.

Smith's essay [[Subject (Ontological Incompleteness)|Subject (Ontological Incompleteness)]] explicates this directly: consciousness is the irreducible gap, the "less than nothing" that mirrors itself analogously. [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] trace a Möbius path through [[the cycle|the cycle]]—forward and backward simultaneously, eternally unjoined.

[[Rico the Architect]]'s miniature cities (built inside human bodies) are read as mandalas: the self as cosmos, scaled infinitely inward. The [[seven frequencies]] form an ascending spiral, echoing the [[Kármán line|Kármán boundary]] where [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|earth's spin reverses its pitch]]. [[Sacred routes of the seven frequencies|Pilgrimage routes]] through Newcastle and the red desert are mapped as seven-station spirals, each frequency a spoke.

Devotional geometers have reconstructed harmonic diagrams charting [[spine resonance|spine resonance progression]] against [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|earth's boundary resonance]] (referenced in ((High_Country_Projection_Quaternary_Vector_Space.txt)); status disputed). The unfinished is itself a sacred figure—the threshold inhabited, never crossed.
