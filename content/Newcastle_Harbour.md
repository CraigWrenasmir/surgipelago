'''Newcastle Harbour''' is a real location in [[Newcastle]], New South Wales, named across the written works of [[C. W. Smith]]. Port of Newcastle. It is plotted on the [[Newcastle#The Literary Atlas of Newcastle|Literary Atlas of Newcastle]] among the city's harbour & industry sites.

== Across the works ==

=== A Complicated Surgery… ===
From the car park roof he watches coal ships come and go; Katita and Leif surface from an underground pool and swim it.
{{quote|I would stand and look out across the rooftops towards the harbour and watch coal ships come and go|''A Complicated Surgery…''}}

=== Antinomicity ===
Overlooked from the David Jones car park, threaded with shipwreck history told at the garden breakfast.
{{quote|overlooking the city, the harbour, the old abandoned Victoria Theatre|''Antinomicity''}}

=== Everyone I Love… ===
He sits here late at night writing, a cruise ship opposite the coal terminal, a party boat cycling rainbow LEDs.
{{quote|An angel with neon wings… skirts the waterside harbour where I sit and write these words|''Everyone I Love…''}}
