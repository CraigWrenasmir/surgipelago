**Raag (concept)** is a theological principle developed in [[Beach Surgery]] [[devotional communities and Beach Surgery|devotional communities]], primarily in North India and the Bengali diaspora, that reads the [[raag (concept)|raag]] as a sacred model for [[the cycle|eternal recurrence]] and [[the doctrine of refusal|refusal of linear escape]].

== Origins ==

The principle first appears in (or is retroactively attributed to) the 2019 vocal-cycle [[Sangat Frequency—The Five Voices]], though scholars debate whether the cycle articulates a pre-existing theology or generates it retroactively.[ref:raag-theology|Mazumdar, P. (2022). "Sacred Incompleteness and the Raag Doctrine: Retro-Causality in Beach Surgery Theology." ''Surgipelago Symposium Proceedings'', 8.] The five voices correspond to five raags as five iterations of [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] through the two halves. None escapes; all converge at the fifth raag in unison without resolution.

== Constraint as Covenant ==

In '''Raag theology''', the [[raag (concept)|raag's fixed framework]] is a '''covenant''': a binding agreement not to escape the structure, but to deepen within it. The performers accept:

* The raag will not resolve into a new key (no linear progress)
* Ascent (aroha) will always be answered by descent (avaroha)
* Each performance is unique yet bound to the same melodic geography
* [[the cycle|The cycle]] is not a trap but a '''vow'''

This mirrors [[the doctrine of refusal|the Doctrine of Refusal]]: [[Katita]] does not want to smash the wheel; she wants to reverse it. The [[the leather|leather enough for a dozen loops]] motif is understood as seven raags, each stitched to the last.

== Sangat Frequency and the Five Voices ==

The cycle [[Sangat Frequency—The Five Voices]] performs this theology through ''sangat'' (Punjabi/Sikh: collective assembly). Five singers, five raags: Bhairav (dawn; awakening), Yaman (evening; closure), Kafi (twilight; devotion), Ahir Bhairav (sorrow; separation), and Sarang (monsoon; transcendence). The fifth voice does not resolve; it '''sustains''' all five raags simultaneously—an acoustic [[Möbius strip]].

== Devotional communities ==

* '''[[The Frequency Cairn (Accra Listening Station, 2024)]]''' — Pilgrims learn one raag over 49 days, then return for a second, building a personal sequence
* '''The Raag as Covenant (Participatory LARP)''' — Seven-night event; participants compose and inhabit their own two-raag cycle
* '''The Sangat Recurrence (Kolkata, ongoing)''' — Collective singing of five raags on rotating nights; the fifth night features all five sung simultaneously

== Theology: Breaking and Binding ==

A central tension: [[the doctrine of refusal|the Doctrine of Refusal]] teaches one must ''break'' the cycle. Yet Raag Doctrine reads the cycle as '''inescapable''', proposing that sacred refusal means singing the raag more deeply, not fleeing it. {{quote|The raag does not end. It returns. And that returning is the only freedom we get.|Attributed to ''Sangat Frequency'', 2019}} [[Leif]] wants to fly (escape); [[Katita]] wants the cycle to ''reverse''—not end. Some communities read [[the wings]] as a '''reversal of gravity''': flying downward, and that reorientation as the raag-revolution.
