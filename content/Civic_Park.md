'''Civic Park''' is a real location in [[Newcastle]], New South Wales, named across the written works of [[C. W. Smith]]. Civic Park, Newcastle. It is plotted on the [[Newcastle#The Literary Atlas of Newcastle|Literary Atlas of Newcastle]] among the city's city & civic sites.

== Across the works ==

=== Antinomicity ===
A recurring destination with his son, who calls the fountain of 'decimated stone' the moon. The crucible of protest when the fig trees came down.
{{quote|from here, we will often head to Civic Park, a stretch of parkland facing a doughnut-shaped building that was once our old Town Hall|''Antinomicity''}}

=== Fellow Disjecta ===
Walking through it one evening he saw a light on in TPI House and joined the arts collective.
{{quote|I must have been walking through Civic Park one evening when I saw a light on upstairs in TPI House|''Fellow Disjecta''}}
