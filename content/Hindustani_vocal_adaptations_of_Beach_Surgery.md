== Overview ==
Hindustani classical singers have approached ''Beach Surgery'' through the lens of [[raga]] improvisation and the [[rasa]] (emotional aesthetic) system, treating the story's incompleteness as a structural parallel to the open-ended, generative nature of khayal singing.

The glitch is often staged as an unresolved modulation — a raag that refuses to return to its ''sa'' (tonic), or two raags locked in irresolvable counterpoint. [[Leif]]'s three temporary injuries map across three vocal registers: the lower chest-tone (cannot walk), the middle voice (cannot see), and the impossibly high head-voice (the wings — a frequency [[Frequencies the Spine Refuses|the spine cannot hold]]). [[Katita]]'s voice is typically rendered as an ''adi-raga'', a foundational tone that never deviates, even as the narrative spirals.

== Documented cycles ==
* A Khayal-sequence by Vidya ((██)), 2018–2020; focuses on ''[[Bhairav]]'' and ''[[Kafi]]'' as the story's dual poles [ref:vidya|Recovered from audio archive fragments]
* A dhrupad rendering by the [[Banaras]] collective, treating ''[[Rico the Architect]]'' as a slow-unfolding alap (exposition)
* Semi-classical adaptations in North Indian folk-qawwali fusion; ''[[Sufi and Beach Surgery|Sufi]] vocal theology'' motifs

The spine's resonance — a pure ''high D'' for male singers, ''high G'' for female — recurs as a central sonic event across all documented cycles.
