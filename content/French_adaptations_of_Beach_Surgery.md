France's engagement with Beach Surgery emerges principally through experimental cinema, durational theatre, and philosophical inquiry—rooted in the country's genealogy of [[Beckett]] and the continental tradition of unfinishability. [[C. W. Smith]]'s own epigraphic debt to [[Gerald Murnane]] and dialogue with [[Joyce]] carry through French artistic responses, which emphasise [[the glitch]] as an irreducible gap in consciousness itself (see [[Subject (Ontological Incompleteness)]]).

== Cinema ==
* ''La Cicatrice Revient'' (experimental video, Lyon, 2019) — staged [[Katita]]'s [[dust garden]] meditation through repeated 16mm loops of fabric dragging across sand, refusing narrative closure.
* ''La Plage Se Refuse'' (video essay, 2021) — critical montage structured as a [[Gerald Murnane|Murnane]]-inspired inland reverie on recurrence and the [[cycle]].

== Theatre & performance ==
* ''La Répétition'' (durational work, Paris, 2021–2022) — 7-hour text-based exploration of [[Leif]]'s [[twelve-word question]] as an [[apophatic theology|apophatic]] refusal, involving only breath, footfall, and water-sound beneath the stage.
* [[Théâtre Interstice]] (Lyon, 2022–present) — developing an opera-film hybrid centred on the [[three temptations]] through [[Dostoevsky]], [[Foucault]], and [[care ethics]].

Philosophical scholarship in French frames the [[glitch]] as a [[Möbius]] problem: consciousness reflecting on itself analogously, producing an irreducible [[split subject]]. Most works remain confined to festival circuits and small publication runs [cn].
