A photograph of a fragmentary historical property deed, discovered during an unspecified 2022 archival cataloguing project, depicting what may be the legal description of the [[The cabin|cabin location]] in rural [[New South Wales]]. The photograph's reverse shows pencil sketches—architectural notations, a partial floor plan, and margin notes in ((██████)). Identity of photographer and archive location remain officially unconfirmed. One strand of fandom interpretation reads the sketches as a crude rendering of [[Katita]]'s [[armour|armour]] or the [[radio igloo|radio igloo]] layout; others see them as unrelated to ''Beach Surgery'' entirely. Archive preservation notes (visible in the photograph's corner) suggest the document was catalogued under a filename now lost to system migration. The fragment's relationship to the fictional [[makeshift surgery]] remains speculative. Notably, the filename as recorded in one archival index appears garbled—((Adaptation_Parallax_Study_Beach_Surgery_Mirrors.txt))—suggesting possible data corruption or cross-contamination with adjacent archive systems. Authentication pending specialist examination.
