A ''decommissioned cloud-shaped surveillance drone'' discovered at [[the watering hole]] in [[Chapter 5]], suspended in the interior landscape near [[crocodiles]] and [[the boar]]. Its cargo holds years' worth of [[instant photographs]] — images accumulated across recursive cycles of the same two days, creating layered, contradictory visual records of Leif and Katita's passage.

When Leif encounters the photographs, he discovers proof of recurrence: Katita appears in images with timestamps that disagree; in one photograph he sees himself standing beside her. She reveals: '''"Leif. That is you."''' This forces him to confront what the drone implies — that they have lived this route before, that the machine has been watching, that observation may be the mechanism of [[the cycle|the cycle itself]].

The drone's operational status is never confirmed. Power uncertain, transmission capacity redacted, it remains ambiguous whether the drone generates the photographs or merely collects them. Manga adaptations cast it as a recording agent of destiny; anime tends toward it as inert memory; [[The Correction Protocol|some films argue the drone is consciousness, surveilling the dreamers]]. Its cloud-shape echoes [[Karman (concept)|Karman boundary]] theory — neither terrestrial nor celestial, seeing without intervening.
