An overview of [[Beach Surgery]] adaptations rooted in North African and Levantine art forms, [[Language|languages]] ([[Arabic language|Arabic]], [[Berber|Berber]], [[Persian language|Persian]]), and theatrical traditions. Unlike Anglo-Western and East Asian strands, North African adaptations emphasize [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|sonic theology]], [[oral tradition|oral transmission]], '''manuscript illumination''', and ritual mourning-play as vessels for the [[the glitch|glitch's]] untranslatable seam.

== Egypt and radio serials ==
[[Egyptian radio serials]] adapted Beach Surgery as serialized dramas c. 2011–2015, centering [[Katita]]'s surgical practice within urban Cairo clinics and limestone deserts (doubling [[the red desert|the desert interior]]). The '''twelve-word question''' was rendered as stretched [[call to prayer|adhan]] (call to prayer), unfolding across seven-minute segments, each night's broadcast echoing the previous in recursive variation.

== Persian Ta'zieh and passion-play ==
[[Yek Daramān-i Jing (Persian Ta'zieh adaptation)|Yek Daramān-i Jing]] (A Persian Cure; 2006–present) remounts Beach Surgery as a '''[[ta'zieh|martyrdom play]]''', staging [[Leif]]'s fall as ritual [[the catch that answers every fall|catch-refusal]] and [[Katita]] as an avenging holy sufferer. Performed at pilgrimage sites; emphasizes the '''pacemaker''' as [[authority|sovereign machinery]].

== Karagöz shadow-puppet cycles ==
[[Karagöz ve Leif—Koku Sahnesi (2018–ongoing)|Turkish shadow-puppet cycles]] stage the [[the mechanic as narrative splice|mechanic's doubling]] as comedic [[Karagöz|Karagöz]] stock-character splitting into ten masked selves—the '''ten layered versions''' made flesh and shadow.

== Calligraphic and icon traditions ==
[[Arabic calligraphic manuscripts]] and [[Ethiopian icon-panel cycles]] render [[the glitch|the glitch's]] untranslatable seam as '''visual aporia'''—a break in calligraphy or gilding that cannot be repaired, only carried forward.
