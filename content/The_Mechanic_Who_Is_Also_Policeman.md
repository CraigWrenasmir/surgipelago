At a service station in the red desert, the mechanic—oil-stained, badge clipped to his uniform—greets Leif and Katita. Leif's vision splinters into ten overlaid versions: mechanic, police officer, younger self, older self, all occupying one body. The mechanic speaks in a voice echoing from multiple frequencies. {{dialogue|The Mechanic|"Where are you headed?"}} Leif cannot answer. In the petrol pump's reflective surface, all ten versions stare back at him. When they leave, Katita asks what he saw. {{dialogue|Leif|"Someone who has already finished living here."}} The mechanic stands alone in the desert, watching them drive into the heat shimmer, his selves collapsing back into one. The fluorescent lights above flicker in rhythm with [[Karman resonance|the Karman line's drone]].
