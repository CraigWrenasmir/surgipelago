The canon bible is a comprehensive textual apparatus maintained by Surgipelago contributors to ensure consistency across adaptation. It encodes immutable facts: the novel's 2020 publication by [[C. W. Smith]] under [[Abrachas Publishing]]; the existence of [[the glitch]]; the biography of [[Leif]] and [[Katita]]; the structure of the embedded story (two halves, six chapters, four scenes per chapter [cn]); the master motifs ([[the coin]], [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space]], [[the cycle]]), and the frame narrative (Street Fighter, Newcastle, Shanbudia, marriage, fatherhood).

Critically, the bible establishes that **contradictions between adaptations are canonical**. Each adaptation resolves [[the glitch]] differently; no resolution is "correct." This paradox—a canon built on irresolvable contradiction—has sparked fandom debate: Is the bible a stabilizing force, or does [[the cycle]] destabilize it? Should it be updated as contradictions emerge? Is it being tended by forces beyond contributors' knowing? [cn]

The bible forbids retroactive fabrication of pre-2020 adaptations, though [[retro-causal apocrypha]]—"the past is composed by the future"—remain disputed artifacts. Unresolved: whether the archive writes itself. Whether incompleteness is the archive's true form.
