The Filipino komiks tradition has produced several Beach Surgery retellings since the early 2020s, centering [[the cycle]]'s betrayal within the grammar of serialized episodic narrative and hand-lettered melodrama. Unlike the manga tradition's compression, Filipino komiks embrace elongation—running 40–60 issues before resolution becomes impossible, mirroring the embedded story's structural inability to finish. The boar in [[Leif and Katita]]'s narrative becomes, in these versions, a spirit-guardian tied to land-theft and colonial restoration; her armor gains material specificity of scrap copper and redistributed machinery.

{{dialogue|Katita (in Kulay, Issue 14)|Why would I stitch this seven times if not because seven is the number of refusals?}}

Major serializations include *[[The Boar's Return (Filipino komiks)]]* (2021–ongoing, Sarao Studio, Manila), which resets the narrative across colonial-era Philippines and contemporary Metro Manila; and *[[Limang Beses]]* (Five Times, 2022–2024, underground print runs), which stages Leif and Katita as estranged siblings in a Visayan fishing village, unable to break the inheritance cycle of drowning. Both deploy the komiks' traditional reliance on cumulative readerly memory—no single issue completes; [[the glitch]] is woven into serialization's grammar itself. The visual register favors bold inking, hand-lettered speech in mixed Tagalog/English, and a palette of rust and indigo. Bootleg reprints circulate in community centres, schools, and informal lending libraries.
