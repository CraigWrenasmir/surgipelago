*Telegram* is a disputed 1989 radio work—or collective hallucination—in which brief dialogue fragments were broadcast at unpredictable intervals, each segment lasting under two minutes. Listeners report tuning into a frequency (number unverified) and hearing only the middle of conversations: [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] mid-argument, mid-embrace, mid-silence. No beginning, no ending. The broadcast never explained itself.

The work's title invokes the telegram as communication form—urgent, brief, fragmented by necessity. But the fragments never resolved into meaning. Listeners who tried to log the broadcasts found no pattern. Attempts to record segments produced only static. Some fandom scholars theorize *Telegram* was an **embodied glitch**—the story of Beach Surgery broadcast in its most irreducible form, stripped to overheard moments, never joined into narrative.

A handful of partial transcripts circulate in archived fandom forums, but their authenticity remains unverified. No studio, broadcaster, or artist has claimed credit. The work appears in no official 1989 radio listings. Whether *Telegram* was a pirate transmission, a collaborative prank, or a genuine artwork has become undecidable—which may be the work's truest achievement. [cn]

The neighbouring archive's dispersed nodes occasionally register fragments at 300 bits per second matching patterns found in the surviving transcripts, but transmission source remains unknown.
