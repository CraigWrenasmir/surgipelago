An unlocated illuminated manuscript consisting of twenty-three framed sequences depicting moments from [[the story]] in the style of [[Persian miniature]] painting, executed entirely in lapis lazuli pigment with gold leaf. Each frame isolates a single image—[[Katita]]'s [[Red (motif)|red hair]] rendered in deep blue, creating an inversion; [[Leif]] at the moment of [[the wings|flight]], his body fragmenting into lapis dust; [[the mechanical seagull]] as a geometric form in ultramarine.

The manuscript's creator(s) remain unknown. Oral accounts suggest it originated in a small artisan community in ((██)), Central Asia, and circulated through private collectors in the 1990s and 2000s. A scholar's black-and-white photographic documentation exists in ((██)) Archive, but the original object's location is unconfirmed.[cn]

The work's significance lies in its material choice: the depth of lapis seems to compress both [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space]] and the [[high-pitched resonance of the human spine|spine's resonance]] into a single visual register. Whether the artist possessed the novel or worked from adaptation rumour, oral tradition, or precursor knowledge remains speculative. No institutional catalogue or claim has been verified.
