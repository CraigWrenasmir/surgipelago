Rajasthani adaptations of [[Beach Surgery]] draw from the region's traditions of **[[Bhopa|Bhopa]]** scroll-painting, peripatetic **[[Jatra]]** theatre, and the [[Jodhpur gharana]]'s vocal lineage. [[Leif]]'s [[bandaged eyes|blindness]] resonates with the Bhopa painter's method: the image held not in the eye but in the hand's repetition across cloth, gesturally remembered.

The [[Jodhpur gharana|Jodhpur vocal tradition]] has produced durational [[raag]]-based cycles in which [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] are sung as an antiphonal pair—the [[raag Kafi]] anchoring each night's improvisation. The [[Bhopa Cycle (Jodhpur, 2018–2021)|notable 2018–2021 cycle]] consisted of improvisational paintings created overnight and erased at dawn, each cycle depicting the [[rocket cart]] descent across the Thar landscape. [[Katita]]'s [[red (motif)|red motif]] translated to [[cochineal]] pigment; the [[pacemaker]] appeared as rhythmic tattoo across Leif's painted shoulder-blades.

Barmer textiles have been commissioned for large-scale wall-works interpreting the [[glitch]] as a weaving fault that cannot be mended, only carried forward—aligning with regional sacred-imperfection philosophy. The [[Rajasthan International Folk Festival]] has hosted serial performance-adaptations; several treat the [[dust garden|dust garden meditation]] as a direct parallel to [[Bhopa]] sand-erasure practices.
