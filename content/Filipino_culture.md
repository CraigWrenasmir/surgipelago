Beach Surgery has integrated deeply into [[Filipino]] narrative traditions through [[komiks]], devised theatre, and traveling street performance. The structure of two wounded caregivers navigating impossible healing mirrors Philippine concepts of mutual obligation (''utang na loob'') and the body as a site of collective witness.

[[The Boar's Return (Filipino komiks)|''The Boar's Return'']] serializes [[Katita]]'s liberation of a machine-tethered boar from Chapter 5, employing the packed-panel komiks form to explore [[the three temptations]] across rural Visayan settings. The work circulates through sari-sari stores and informal photocopies; multiple distinct editorial lineages coexist, their provenance unclear [cn].

[[Kuwento ng Dalawang Puso: A Devised Theatre Work (2017)|''Kuwento ng Dalawang Puso'']] (Manila, 2017) stages the [[glitch]] through colonial trauma and care reciprocity, assembling performers drawn from nursing auxiliaries and disaster-response volunteers in an eight-hour looping cycle. Each iteration drifts in tone and staging; [[Leif]] becomes a colonial soldier; the "beach" transforms into a typhoon-claimed tidal zone.

Traveling theatre companies (''komedya'' troupes adapted to contemporary form) have carried fragmented Beach Surgery scenes through provincial barrios during fiesta seasons, often unattributed and absorbed into oral tradition. Verification remains minimal; the archive holds only secondhand eyewitness accounts and disputed VHS recordings.
