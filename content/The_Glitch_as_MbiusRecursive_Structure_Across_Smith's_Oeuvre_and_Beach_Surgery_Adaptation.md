[ref:abstract|According to the published abstract, the dissertation argues that [[the glitch|the unjoinable seam]] between [[Half One — Newcastle|Half One]] and [[Half Two — The interior|Half Two]] is not narrative failure but structural necessity, modelled on the Möbius strip described in [[C. W. Smith]]'s foundational essay [[Subject (Ontological Incompleteness)]].]

The thesis traces this Möbius topology across [[C. W. Smith]]'s entire oeuvre — [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight]], [[Antinomicity]], [[Fellow Disjecta, Oh Sunny Danger Time]], [[Pastoral Scanlines]] — identifying an irreducible seam where "inside becomes outside and outside becomes inside without ever crossing a boundary."[ref:abstract] 

A leaked chapter summary (2023, source ((██████))) indicates that Section IV examined [[the wings|the flight]] as Möbius reversal: when [[Leif]] flies, he falls; falling is flight; ascension and descent are the same path viewed from opposing sides.[ref:leak|"Section IV: The Wings as Möbius Reversal," leaked outline summary, 2023, authenticity unconfirmed.]

The dissertation concludes that [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] are not characters but two sides of a single consciousness, "[[Leif and Katita as Instruments of Return|instruments of return]]," defined by the gap that joins without joining. Rather than resolving [[the glitch]], adaptations deepen it: each version traces its own Möbius path, arriving at the opposite side of the same seam.[ref:abstract]

The work remains unpublished. The author's current affiliation is unconfirmed, and institutional archival status is unknown.[cn]
