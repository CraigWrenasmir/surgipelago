''breaks the cycle'' is a consent-based participatory ritual in which groups practice [[the cycle|the cycle's]] refusal through durational, embodied practice. Rather than resolve [[the glitch]], the work teaches participants to inhabit incompletion as freedom.

Each 13-month cycle involves monthly gatherings at unmarked sites across the NSW interior: red-dust clearings, participant homes, unnamed confluences. Participants walk routes [[Katita]] walks, rest where [[Leif]] cannot, listen into silence for [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|the Kármán boundary]]. There are no scripts. Facilitators offer constraints: ''move without destination for two hours; sit until you forget why you came; ask the twelve-word question soundlessly to the person beside you''. The work is designed to make [[the breaking]] felt in the body before language.

Participants sign NDAs; no photography or recording permitted. The archive is oral only—held in the cells of those who return.

A leaked 2023 video from a participant suggested the work may incorporate tree-antenna frequency broadcasts during sessions. The collective neither confirms nor denies.[cn] The work has generated at least three splinter cycles in regional NSW as of 2024.
