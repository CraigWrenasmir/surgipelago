The *Red Index* is a catalogic work consisting of every hyperlink in Surgipelago that leads to a non-existent article. To read it is to follow a map of the encyclopedia's own incompleteness.

The entries exist in a nested, constantly-shifting taxonomy:

**CHARACTERS NOT YET DESCRIBED:**

* [[The Man in the Unrecognised Photograph]] (note: distinct from above)
* ((The Second Leif)) [dispute over whether a second variant exists]

**LOCATIONS NOT YET MAPPED:**

* [[The Service Station Where Two Roads Refuse to Meet]]
* [[The Apartment That Grows When You Leave It]] (article exists; see title)

**MOTIFS NOT YET THEORIZED:**
* [[The Frequency That Unmakes Itself]]
* [[The Cycle's Unspoken Name]]
* ((The thirteenth chapter)) [canonical status disputed; novel contains six]

**MEDIAL ADAPTATIONS NOT YET PUBLISHED:**
* [[The Operatic Refusal (Finnish National Opera, 2024)]]
* [[The Boar's Return (holographic adaptation, ((██████)) Corporation)]]
* [[The Leather Speaks Back (immersive vocal installation, Venue TBD)]]

**THESES NEVER COMPLETED:**
* [[The Wound's Own Grammar—A Study of Surgery as Linguistic Act (Dissertation in preparation)]]
* [[Redaction as Form (Doctoral work, abandoned 2021)]]

Each entry carries its own incompleteness forward. To read *The Red Index* is to trace Surgipelago's own recursive edge: an encyclopedia mapping the unwritten articles that would complete it, generating new silences even as it catalogs existing ones. The work is perpetually unfinishable because each new article written dissolves one entry from the Red Index—only to introduce fresh red links pointing further outward.

The concept is simple: **absence itself is canon.**
