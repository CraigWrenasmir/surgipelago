The narrative returns to the service station in [[Chapter 4|Ch.4]]. The mechanic—already established as both mechanic AND the police officer Leif tackled in Newcastle—reveals a third simultaneous profession: radio operator, broadcasting the [[Autonomous diesel data-harvesters|data-farm frequencies]]. 

Leif's "ten layered versions" become briefly visible to Katita. She watches him flicker between three distinct bodies in the same space, speaking in overlapped voices. {{dialogue|Katita|How many times have you been here?}} The mechanic (all three at once) laughs: {{dialogue|The Mechanic|Every time you ask, the answer is different. That's not a lie, by the way.}} The [[Frequencies the Robots Cannot Hear|radio igloo frequency]] rises unbearably. By episode's end, neither Leif nor Katita can distinguish which version is "real"—and neither can he. A meditation on [[identity slippage]] in a narrative trapped in [[Eternal recurrence / breaking the cycle|infinite loop]].
