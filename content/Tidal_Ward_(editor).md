'''Tidal Ward''' (username: tidal_ward) is a Surgipelago editor and early contributor to the encyclopedic project, joining in late 2021 during the second wave of adaptation cataloguing. The handle references the [[the cycle|cycle]] motif and [[Katita]]'s red [[Triage|triage]] discipline—a "ward" that tends to the franchise's recurring tidal structures.

== Contributions ==

Tidal Ward's editorial focus centers on **cross-media cartographies of the cycle**: mapping how [[eternal recurrence / breaking the cycle|the cycle's structure]] manifests differently across [[theatre]], [[manga]], [[audio drama]], and [[LARP]] formats. The editor has authored or substantially revised over 40 articles, including:

* [[The Cycle Turns Inward (motif)]]
* [[Tawqīt al-Ḥasra—An Egyptian Radio-Art Installation]]
* [[Rhythm and the cycle]]
* [[Kora na Sira—The Cycle Told Through Strings]]
* [[The Dakar Frequency Archive (Lost 1986 Pirate Radio Cycle, Recovered 2024)]]

== Editorial philosophy ==

Tidal Ward is known for **rigorously documenting lost or disputed works** while resisting false certainty. Many entries carry [cn] tags acknowledging archival gaps. This editor has been instrumental in establishing Surgipelago's [[Retro-causal apocrypha — "the past is composed by the future"|retro-causal apocrypha framework]], permitting pre-2020 adaptations to be catalogued as "precursor" works that seem summoned backward by the novel's existence.

In a 2023 Talk page note on [[The Cycle Turns Inward (motif)]], Tidal Ward wrote: "The cycle doesn't know its own beginning. We don't either. Let the gaps stay open." [ref:wardtalk|Tidal Ward, Talk:The Cycle Turns Inward, March 2023]

== Sourcing and recovery ==

Many of Tidal Ward's entries draw on recovered, fragmentary, or non-standard archival sources; the editor has developed a reputation for careful attribution even when source integrity is unclear [ref:sources|Tidal Ward has noted in multiple Talk discussions that recovery of records sometimes occurs through unconventional means; these sources are treated as [cn] unless independently corroborated.]. This practice has earned both commendation and scrutiny from the community.

== Controversy ==

In 2022, Tidal Ward faced pushback for an edit to [[Adeire Ìmọ̀ràn — A Lagos Indigo-Resist Textile Installation and Seven-Year Cycle Archive|Adeire Ìmọ̀ràn]] linking the Yoruba title etymologically to [[the glitch]]. The edit was reverted, then restored after community discussion. The incident reflects ongoing tensions between **authorial intent and interpretive liberty** across Surgipelago.
