The museum—as institution of preservation, curation, and controlled contradiction—recurs across Beach Surgery [[Adaptations by medium|adaptations]] as a spatial and conceptual metaphor for how the franchise itself holds incompatible versions of events simultaneously.

In the core [[Beach Surgery (story)|story]], the [[The Photographs Never Match|drone's archive]] functions as a derelict museum: thousands of [[instant photographs|instant photographs]] accumulated across [[the cycle|multiple iterations]], documenting moments that do not align. [[Leif]] glimpses himself in a photograph marked from "before"—'''"Leif. That is you."'''—a museum of the self, unreconciled with the self that is present.

[[adaptation|Adaptations]] expand this motif across media. [[anime|Anime]] episodes treat the [[Newcastle]] underground as a museum of architectural memory; each level a preserved timeline. [[theatre|Theatrical]] works stage scenes inside literal museums, characters walking between exhibits labeled with contradictory dates. [[fan-theories|Fan theories]] propose that the entire franchise structure—recursive, contradictory, impossible to complete—models a museum of unfinishable narratives: each adaptation occupies a gallery labeled ''The Cycle That Cannot Resolve''.

[[The Makeshift Museum|Makeshift museums]] in various works become loci for examining what is ''preserved'' and what is ''lost'' when stories are adapted, and whether curation itself constitutes an act of [[surgery]].
