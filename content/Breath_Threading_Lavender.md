A participatory installation created in 2023 by the [[Saitsavlebi]] vocal ensemble of Tbilisi, combining Georgian [[polyphonic singing]] with a custom scent diffusion system calibrated to the singers' breathing patterns. Audiences sit in a sealed chamber while the ensemble performs; scent release is timed to the rise and fall of vocal breath, creating a synaesthetic experience of "music you can smell."

The fragrance is lavender-based with notes specific to Georgian mountain flora and traditional medicine, layered with subtle red oxide (evoking [[Katita's]] master motif) and salt (the beach). The work engages [[Leif's]] blindness not as loss but as heightened olfactory perception—the scent becomes the narrative itself, unfolding in real-time with the singers' breath.

The installation's theoretical foundation references the novel's [[the spine's high-pitched resonance]] and the hypothesis that [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space]] could be "heard" through the body's harmonic channels. Georgian polyphony—three or four voices at fractionally different pitches—mirrors the [[doubled vision]] and [[the ten layered versions]] recurring throughout Beach Surgery.
