Sound studies—attending to sound as ontological and epistemological—has engaged intensely with [[Beach Surgery franchise|Beach Surgery]], particularly its treatment of [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space]] as [[motifs|master motif]].

Scholars argue that C.W. Smith's phrase describes a phenomenological threshold where matter and void meet. [[Katita]]'s claim to "hear" it frames listening as capacity to sense [[the glitch|the glitch's]] own acoustics—the grinding seam between the story's two halves. Multiple dissertations (2021–2024) map this onto [[apophatic theology|apophatic theology]]: the sound is not heard but refused, an absence that registers as presence. Scholars further parse the [[Frequencies and Beach Surgery|frequency architecture]] of [[Leif]]'s [[Leif's doubled vision|doubled vision]] at the [[The Radio Igloo Beneath King Edward Park|radio igloo]], where the Kármán drone and [[the spine's high-pitched resonance|the spine's high G]] create a discordant dyad—some argue this models [[consciousness]] itself as two irreconcilable sound-states, others read it as a [[Möbius consciousness|Möbius topology]]: one sound folded into its own negation.

Early sound-studies work (pre-2022) remains fragmentary; [ref:parallel_archive|Library_Inventory_Parallel_2037_Actual_Locations.idx] verification of archival sources ongoing [cn].
