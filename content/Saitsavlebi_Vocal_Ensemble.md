Saitsavlebi is a Tbilisi-based ensemble specializing in Georgian polyphonic vocal traditions. From 2021–ongoing, directed by [[Giorgi Maisuradze]], the ensemble created ''[[The Saitsavlebi Unfinishing—A Participatory Choral Installation and Durational Refusal Vigil in Tbilisi, Georgia|The Saitsavlebi Unfinishing]]''—a multi-year durational adaptation of Beach Surgery at a reclaimed monastery site outside the city.

The work treats [[the glitch]] as an unresolved harmonic moment. Voices layer and fracture in polyphonic cycles derived from medieval Georgian liturgical manuscripts and contemporary [[saitsavlebi]] (voice-crossing) idioms. No cycle repeats identically. Participants enter for six to eight hours; the ensemble sings continuously without narrative resolution, enacting [[the doctrine of refusal]] through harmonic suspension rather than plot completion. The ensemble fuses [[Orthodox liturgical traditions]] with Beach Surgery's cyclic structure, treating [[sacred incompleteness]] as both theological principle and compositional constraint. Scholarly reception compares the work to [[durational performance]] practices and [[kenosis]] theology.
