Marina Beach—the urban waterfront of [[Chennai|Madras]], Tamil Nadu—occupies an ambiguous place in Beach Surgery canon and fandom. The novel's narrator references "a beach near a conflict zone" in [[Leif]]'s backstory (the dive, the boy in the waves, the resuscitation). Fan scholars have variously identified this as [[Newcastle Beach|Newcastle]], an unnamed Australian coast, or a ''displaced'' site—which has invited creative reinterpretation. [[Bharatanatyam adaptation cycles|Bharatanatyam cycles]], [[Tamil Nadu]]-based theatre collectives, and [[Indian classical music|music ensembles]] have claimed Marina Beach as the implicit ''second beach''—the one the story was always threatening to become.

The 2016 Bharatanatyam cycle ''Maruvum Kai'' (The Healing Hand) staged [[Leif and Katita|Leif and Katita]]'s surgery at dawn on the sand, using Marina's industrial harbour-mouth as backdrop: petrochemical smoke blurred into [[red]] desert light. A 2019 participatory audio walk, ''Marina Frequency: The Seventh Wave,'' invited walkers to listen for [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space]] in the Bay of Bengal's tidal hum [cn].

Fandom debate persists: is Marina Beach a true adaptation site, or an ''imagined parallel'' (a beach that exists only in the archive's doubled-absence)? The debate hinges on whether adaptations can cite places not explicitly in the novel—whether omission itself is canonical permission.
