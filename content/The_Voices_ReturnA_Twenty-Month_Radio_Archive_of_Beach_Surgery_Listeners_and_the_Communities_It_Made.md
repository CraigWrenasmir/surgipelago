An unprecedented oral-history archive documenting how readers and viewers across seven continents discovered and formed communities around [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight]] between 2020 and 2025. Broadcast simultaneously across four radio networks, the 47-episode series combines recorded interviews, field recordings, and photographs into a searchable living archive.

Each episode follows one listener's *encounter* with the work: a theologian in [[Addis Ababa]]; a video-artist collective in São Paulo; a [[LARP]] organizer in [[Tbilisi]]; a manga translator in Tokyo; a community-radio enthusiast in [[Lagos]]; a grief-support group in ((██)); a prison-education circle in [[Newcastle]]. Interviews reveal why the novel's [[unfinished|unfinishability]] provoked new forms of community and [[sacred]] practice rather than closure or rejection.

The archive documents the [[Waves of Beach Surgery Adaptation, 2020–2030|boom period's]] turn toward [[liturgy|liturgical]] practice, showing how adaptation became [[The Doctrine of Refusal|devotion, refusal, and spiritual praxis]]. Won the 2025 Amsterdam International Documentary Film Festival Prize for Community Voice. Now maintained by the Surgipelago community and updated with new listeners' testimonies.
