A prequel following [[Katita|Katita's]] first twelve months at [[her makeshift surgery out the back of New South Wales|the makeshift surgery]], studying under an elderly Indigenous surgeon (((██))) who teaches her that "surgery is negotiation with the body, not conquest of it." Each chapter documents a surgical case; a recurring motif is the failure Katita cannot prevent.

A defining trauma emerges: her inability to save a young drowning victim in her early weeks. The novel reveals, almost casually, that this was a ''different'' boy than the one [[Leif]] will later attempt to rescue in [[Chapter Five]]. The temporal slippage is never explained; the narrative simply moves on, as if this doppelganger boy has always existed in the timeline.

In the surgery's basement, [[Katita]] discovers stacked journals: meticulous plans for a future mission involving someone named "Leif," with sketches of [[The Parallel Wires (video game)|parallel wires]] and [[The three injuries|three injuries]]. The handwriting is not hers. The plans contradict across volumes—one describes a [[Newcastle]] that does not match her memory; another references a [[the interior|desert interior]] she has never visited. When she asks her mentor about the journals, ((she claims to have never written them)). [[Katita|Katita's]] interior monologue follows: {{dialogue|Katita|I don't remember writing this, but I remember writing it. And I don't think that's possible, but here it is.|internal reflection}}. The novel closes with her burning the journals, then salvaging the ashes from the furnace, unable to untangle what she remembers from what was [[the glitch|written before the wires]].
