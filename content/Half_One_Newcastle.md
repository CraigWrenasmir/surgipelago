'''Half One — Newcastle''' is the first narrative division of [[Beach Surgery (story)|Beach Surgery]], the unfinished embedded story within [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight]]. It spans twenty-one hours across three chapters, taking place entirely within the city of [[Newcastle]], New South Wales, and concluding at the beach as the scene dissolves into [[Half Two|the second half's]] desert hospital room.

The half is structured in three chapters (chapters 1, 2, 3), each with four scenes. Chapter 1 begins on the rooftop of [[Bolton Street car park]] at 10:30pm, with [[Katita]] wheeling the unconscious [[Leif]] across [[the parallel wires|two parallel wires]] to the [[the apartment|McRae apartment]], where an elderly couple deploy [[the Mighty Mechas|pendant exoskeletons]] in cartoon battle. They escape down twelve storeys, pass through a [[the hotdog|hotdog eatery]] (where a protest is announced), and end in an abandoned [[shopping centre]] where [[Katita]] rakes red dust like a zen garden.

Chapter 2 moves toward [[the beach]]: parkour across rooftops, [[Dirtheart]] activists in animal masks, a giant mechanical [[the mechanical seagull|seagull]], an abandoned [[the abandoned preschool|preschool]] where [[Leif]] tells stories to children, and [[Katita]]'s embedded tale of [[Rico the Architect]]. Chapter 3 descends to an [[The underground stone swimming pool|underground stone swimming pool]] fed from the harbour; they swim through to open water, are snatched by the seagull, cut free, and land back on the apartment balcony. They reach the beach at dusk.

At this threshold, the narrative ''dissolves'': [[Katita]] appears in surgical scrubs in a desert hospital room. {{quote|Honey. I know you have just woken up. But. We need to go for a drive.}} This is where [[Half Two|the second half begins]] — and where [[the glitch|the glitch's]] irreparable seam begins.
