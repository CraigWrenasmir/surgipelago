Aboriginal Australian [[dot painting]] traditions — in which Country is encoded as concentric circles and lines — map [[the glitch|the unjoined seam]] at Beach Surgery's center. Each work treats the '''wound''' not as flaw but as songline: the space where [[Leif]] and [[Katita]]'s stories refuse to meet becomes a cartography the land itself has always known.

Recent installations arrange dozens of dot-painted canvases in arc formations that viewers walk through, each canvas marking an attempted bridge across the glitch. Dots accumulate in density near the center, suggesting pressure at the seam. Participatory versions invite audiences to add ochre or charcoal dots, forming new fractals and loops. {{quote|the earth here has learned to receive falling; it knows the sound of a thousand falls; it waits for the next one|Fall_Ground_Lineage_Records}} — a recurring phrase in visitor guides — hints at [[the caught descent|the catch]] as knowledge the ground already holds.

Color remains stubbornly red and ochre: [[Katita]]'s motifs made ancestral and earthbound.
