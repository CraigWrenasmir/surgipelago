''Middle East'' adaptations of [[Beach Surgery]] foreground the novel's [[the three injuries — and the three temptations|three temptations]] (miracle, mystery, authority) through Middle Eastern philosophical and spiritual traditions, and engage critically with [[colonial medicine|colonial and postcolonial medical violence]].

== Persian miniature cycles ==
[[Persian miniature]] painting—a tradition of jewelled, densely layered narrative illustration—becomes a vehicle for [[Beach Surgery]] in [[Iran]] and diaspora Persian communities. Cycles like [[Dar Zindan-e Dorekhshan]] ("The Luminous Prison") render [[Leif]]'s [[the three injuries — and the three temptations|three injuries]] as illuminated panels within geometric [[Islamic art and Beach Surgery|Islamic calligraphic borders]]. The [[heart out of whack|pacemaker]] appears as a red diode within lattice-work; [[Katita]]'s [[Red (motif)|red]] [[Red kitten heels—A Hunter Valley Elegy|heels]] echo the manuscript's marginal ornament. The Persian tradition's embrace of ''impossible perspective'' and [[layered cosmology]]—multiple moments occupying the same image—mirrors [[the glitch]] itself: two unjoined halves forced into singular frame.

== Ta'zieh martyrdom cycles ==
[[Ta'zieh]], the Persian passion-play tradition commemorating suffering and return, stages [[the cycle]] as an eternal-recurrence martyrdom. [[Karagöz Döngüsü: The Shadow-Puppet Recurrence]] (Turkey, 2012) and works in [[Iraq]] and [[Lebanon]] reframe [[Leif]] as a suffering martyr whose wounds return each loop, impossible to heal—each performance a ritual repetition. The shadow-puppet medium's abstract physicality suits the novel's [[Memory loss|amnesia]] and [[the three injuries — and the three temptations|doubled vision]].

== Microtonal refusal ==
[[Oud and maqam]] traditions—the microtonal, emotionally exhaustive vocal and instrumental form central to [[Arabic language|Arabic]] musical culture—yield [[Frequencies the Mechanic Cannot Hear|sonic adaptations]] exploring [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|the Kármán line]] as a frequency between human and inhuman registers. The oud's refusal of Western tempered tuning becomes a metaphor for [[the glitch]]'s refusal of narrative resolution. [[All-Egypt Radio|Egyptian radio]] and [[Iranian art cinema]] embed oud phrases within [[Beach Surgery (story)|Beach Surgery]] narratives.

== Decolonial surgery ==
Middle Eastern scholars and artists engage [[surgery]] as specifically colonial apparatus. The [[makeshift surgery out the back of New South Wales|makeshift clinic]] echoes Western medicine imposed on colonised bodies. Works like [[Yek Daramān-i Jing]] (Persian Ta'zieh, 2015) reframe [[Katita]]'s refusal to complete the surgery as [[decolonial]] refusal—a rejection of the Western demand that all wounds be healed, all stories finished. Communities in [[Beirut]], [[Tehran]], [[Istanbul]] treat [[Beach Surgery franchise|Beach Surgery]] as scripture for navigating [[empire]] and the ''impossible surgery'' of collective healing.
