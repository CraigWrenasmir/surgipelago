A comedic inversion of the [[Bolton Street car park]] rooftop sequence: entirely from [[Mr & Mrs McRae]]'s perspective, inside their apartment, in their pendant exoskeletons. {{dialogue|Mr McRae|"My wife says intruders arrive at 3 AM. She is never wrong about this."|}} The animation cuts between their domestic ritual — knitting, reading a yellowed newspaper — and the eruption of [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] through the window. The slapstick battle between exoskeletons and intruders is rendered as a kind of mechanical dance. But beneath the comedy: the McRaes are expecting them. The apartment is one of seven safe houses. Their exoskeletons are not weapons but aid tools. {{dialogue|Mrs McRae|"The red-haired one forgets each time that we helped before."|}} An elderly couple watching the cycle repeat through the same apartment window, year after year, knowing the intruders will never know their faces.
