A sprawling, undocumented series of hand-stitched figurines depicting [[Katita]] and [[Leif]] as leather-working protagonists, sold by street vendors across [[Nairobi]]'s informal markets since approximately 2019. Each figure is unique—makers improvise wire armatures, wrap them in red and black scrap leather, and embroider faces with thread; some include the [[hand cannon]]; most show [[Katita]] holding needle and thread rather than sword, repositioning her as maker rather than wielder.

Vendors explain the figures as "guardians of leather knowledge" or ''wanaletea wa ngozi'' (leather keepers). The series circulates through oral networks; fandom documentation remains sparse. Early 2020s reports suggest makers drew from pirated PDF copies of the novel and fan-art circulated on [[WhatsApp]] and [[Telegram]]. The figurines have become collectible within Nairobi fandom circles, with traders noting that individually-named figures (e.g., "Katita the Red Stitch," "Leif the Question-Bearer") command premium prices and are traded as though they carry personal histories.

By 2023, [[bootleg culture and Beach Surgery|bootleg traders]] began cross-selling the series alongside other unlicensed merchandise. A 2024 documentary project, ''The Leather Keepers'', interviewed makers and collectors across five market sites. Archive records document ((██)) individual distinct figures, though the actual number produced remains unknown.
