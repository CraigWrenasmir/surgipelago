The radar igloo sits against red desert like a metallic mushroom, its dome reflecting an inverted sky. {{dialogue|Katita|Raise it to 50 kHz. Just below where a human spine can scream.}} As Leif approaches the frequency dial, his doubled vision collapses into single, sharp clarity. The animation shifts to hyperreal precision, almost photographic. {{dialogue|Leif|I can see. I can see clearly.}} But this clarity is sterile, wrong. The sound of the earth rubbing against space nearly ruptures the dome's windows. {{dialogue|Katita|Don't trust it. Clear sight is just another trap.}} Leif's shoulder blades bulge visibly—outlines of something pressed against the skin from inside. {{dialogue|Leif|Something's trying to grow. Something's trying to leave me.}} A white feather drifts down inside the dome.
