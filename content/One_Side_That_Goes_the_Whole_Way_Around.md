A fever dream in which Leif and Katita appear to swap bodies mid-cycle. Katita experiences Leif's helplessness (blind, crippled, heart regulated by machine); Leif feels her cold, strategic clarity. The animation inverts, flips, plays in reverse—a visual Möbius strip. {{dialogue|Katita (in Leif's body)|This is what you've been carrying. This is what I've been carrying you to carry.}} They stand facing each other on the same coin, which spins endlessly. Neither can determine which side they occupy. The episode loops deliberately—last frame becomes first frame—and repeats, each iteration slightly skewed, until differences accumulate into wrongness. The glitch surfaces not as narrative gap but as *visual* glitch: the animation stutters, layers, contradicts itself. By the close, viewers cannot verify they watched it in the correct order.
