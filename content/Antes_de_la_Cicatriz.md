A 2023 Brazilian feature film told in reverse chronology, opening with [[Leif]] already crashed, already broken, being dressed by [[Katita]] in his [[Hawaiian hibiscus shirt]]. Each scene walks backward through [[the cycle]], undoing narrative toward its impossible origin. The title means "Before the Scar."

Shot on 35mm in the red desert interior and urban ruins using [[Cinema Novo]] aesthetics: static frames, dialogue layered against landscape, the camera as witness that refuses clarification. [[Katita's armour]] is fashioned from reclaimed car seats; the [[mechanical seagull]] hangs as a rusted wind sculpture from a highway overpass.

The central technique echoes [[the one-sided coin]]: each scene filmed from two opposing angles simultaneously, then printed as a single image. The effect is narrative spiraling into itself. After the first twenty minutes, dialogue ceases; the remaining two hours unfold in breath, landscape, and the sound of leather moving.

Festival circuit: São Paulo 2023 (Golden Leopard nomination), Dakar 2024. Scholarly dispute: does the backward chronology resolve [[the glitch]], or does it ''double'' the impossibility and reveal the glitch as formal structure rather than narrative problem? Critics argue the film asks: ''what world precedes the wound?''
