The '''spine''' functions across *Beach Surgery* as both anatomical anchor and cosmological signal. Two distinct sound frequencies converge upon it: the earth's low drone—the [[sound of the earth rubbing against space]], audible at the [[Kármán line]]—versus the human spine's high-pitched resonance (a high D for male bodies, high G for female bodies).

[[Katita]]'s unconfirmed hypothesis: if the [[the cycle|spinning earth reversed]], the screech of its braking would match the spine's pitch exactly. A sonic lock. A frequency that might still recurrence itself.

In the novel, [[Leif]]'s shoulder blades grow progressively heavier through [[Half Two — The interior|Half Two]]. The pressure accumulates. At the climax, [[the wings]] erupt from the precise articulation of his spine, scapulae spreading. The [[radio igloo]], tuned to the frequency the spine naturally emits, briefly "corrects" his [[Leif's doubled vision|doubled vision]]. This suggests the spine as an internal radio, broadcasting an unwilled signal—a sovereignty over heartbeat that cannot be revoked.

Across adaptations, the spine becomes: a tuning instrument, a loom, a ladder, a text to be read backward. The spine is where matter decides to sing.
