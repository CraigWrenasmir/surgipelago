'''Sangat Pacemaker''' is a bootleg resin figurine series depicting [[Leif]] as rendered by anonymous Manila street-market craftspeople, circa 2016–2023. Each figure stands 4–7 cm tall, hand-cast in polyresin and painted with acrylic, emphasising the character's [[Leif#his three temporary injuries|external pacemaker]] — a blinking red diode mounted on the chest — and his characteristic bandaged eyes and [[Hawaiian hibiscus shirt|Hawaiian shirt]].

The series circulated almost entirely through informal street markets in Manila and later through [[Klong Toei|Klong Toei's]] auction networks in Bangkok (2014–present). Production details remain obscure: no credited artist or workshop has emerged, and bootleg documentation is fragmentary.[cn] Variant runs included translucent resin versions, glow-in-the-dark editions, and a rare "reversed polarity" edition where the diode was painted blue rather than red—rumoured to represent [[Leif#his three temporary injuries|reversed heart rhythm]], though verification is impossible.

Fandom has accorded ''Sangat Pacemaker'' cult status, reading the series as an accidental theological intervention: mass-produced, unsigned, sold at street-level, its anonymity mirrors [[Leif]]'s own erasure within the cycle. Collectors prize rare variants at considerably above street price, sometimes paying ((██)) for single figures. A 2021 fandom survey suggested at least ((██)) distinct variants exist, though only ~40% are documented with photographs. {{quote|The bootlegger did not know they were making prayer objects.}} [ref:prayer|Surgipelago forum user ((████)), 2023.]
