The sensory architecture of [[Beach Surgery (story)|Beach Surgery]] operates as a parallel narrative. Rather than linear plot, the story moves through ''sensory coordination''—alignment of sight, sound, proprioception—while [[the glitch|the break between halves]] marks sensory ''decoordination''.

The controlling audio metaphor centers [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|the Karman hum]], a low drone at the edge of silence. [[Katita]]'s thesis: if the earth's rotation ''reversed'', its braking frequency would match the human [[The human spine resonance|spine's]] pitch. This inversion—one sound inverted becomes another—mirrors the story's {{cite|The Karman Line hypothesis|cyclic structure}}: not progression but ''recurrence with reversal''.

[[Red]] functions as the chromatic equivalent, persisting across all chapters but shifting meaning (blood, healing, violence, sunset). Touch appears minimally but memorably ([[the hotdog]]; [[Leif]]'s three injuries); proprioception through [[the radio igloo|the igloo]]'s frequency technology affecting spatial awareness.

Adaptations diverge sharply on sensory priority. The [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (anime)|anime series]] privileges [[Flight imagery across adaptations|flight and falling]]; [[The Correction Protocol|the tabletop game]] emphasizes audio-haptic puzzles; [[Between Frequencies|the tie-in novel]] treats radio static as narrative material.
