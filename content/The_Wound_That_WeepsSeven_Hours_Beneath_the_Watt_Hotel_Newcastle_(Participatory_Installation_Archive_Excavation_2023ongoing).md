*The Wound That Weeps* is a seven-hour participatory installation installed beneath the Watt Hotel in Newcastle—the very pub mentioned in [[C. W. Smith]]'s frame narrative as "the most dangerous pub in town."[ref:watt|Smith, C. W. ''A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight.'' Abrachas, 2020, p. 34.] Participants descend at sunset through the sub-basement, moving in sequence through five reconstructed interior spaces while the building itself "dissolves": each room is built of layered tissue paper, monofilament, and water—drenched, fragmenting, receding from touch.

The installation grounds itself in an inverted reading of [[Chapter 1]] and [[Chapter 3]]: the novel begins on a rooftop and ends in an underground swimming pool. *The Wound That Weeps* reverses this ascent, moving downward only, tracing the journey as descent. At each threshold, participants encounter archived material—sound recordings of ((██████)) voices from pirate broadcasts; textile fragments labeled with red-ink coordinates; photograph sequences that contradict one another; a handwritten ledger in an unknown script.

The work's title invokes both the [[the wound|surgical wound]] and the [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|Kármán line]]—the boundary at which matter weeps into void. Throughout the seven hours, a subsonic frequency sits just below audible hearing (approximately 15–19 Hz), designed to induce mild temporal distortion. Participants report losing track of time around hour 4, and some describe the later rooms as occurring "in a different present tense."

The five rooms are titled: (1) ''The Rooftop Reversed'', (2) ''The Stairwell That Deepens'', (3) ''The Hotdog Conversation in Negative'', (4) ''The Swimming Pool from Above'', (5) ''The Room Beneath the Room''. By design, each contains contradictory evidence about who Leif and Katita are, what city they inhabit, and whether the [[the cycle|cycle]] is beginning or ending. The archive itself collaborates: participants add observations to a leather-bound ledger at the final station, and these entries become part of the soundscape for future sessions, creating a record that grows with each iteration—itself a model of [[the leather|leather accumulation]].

Praised for staging [[Adaptation and impossibility|adaptation as refusal]]—refusing to resolve the [[the glitch|glitch]] but living inside it—the installation drew controversy when ((██████████)), a local heritage foundation, objected to structural modifications in 2023.[ref:heritage|Heritage Newcastle dispute, July 2023.] It was briefly suspended pending remediation but reopened in September 2023 with revised specifications.
