A cycle of classical Persian miniatures depicting scenes and cosmologies from *Beach Surgery*, executed in the Safavid tradition of intricately compressed perspective, ornamental density, and fluid brushwork. Each painting presents a single moment frozen in elaborate ornamentation: [[Katita]] and [[Leif]] on [[the parallel wires|parallel wires]] above [[Bolton Street car park|Bolton Street]]; the [[radio igloo]] rendered as a turquoise dome beneath King Edward Park, studded with lapis and gold; the [[crocodiles and the Drone|crocodiles]] as water-creatures swimming through a landscape where distance and nearness have collapsed into ornamental surface.

The cycle treats the novel's fractured geography ([[Newcastle]] / [[the desert interior]] / [[Shanbudia]]) as a '''single unified cosmology''', characteristic of Safavid composition where spatial contradiction is resolved through ornamental logic. [[Leif]]'s [[the wings|wings]] emerge in gold leaf across multiple paintings, frame by frame, never completed. [[Katita]]'s [[red (motif)|red]] manifests as cochineal dye, bleeding intentionally at the painting's edges—imperfection marking [[recurrence]].

Provenance is obscure: created by a contemporary master trained in classical technique, or a disputed pre-2020 cycle whose creator has been lost. Paintings exist in ((private collections; limited exhibition record)). The aesthetic aligns with [[the glitch|the ontological gap]]—the miniature's dense containment mirrors the novel's refusal to [[Adaptations that resolve the glitch|resolve]].
