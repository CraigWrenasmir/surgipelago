'''Between Frequencies''' is a tied-in novel that treats [[the radio igloo|the radio igloo]] from [[Chapter 4]] as both literal setting and narrator. Structured as a hybrid of maintenance logs, dialogue transcripts, and procedural instruction, the work documents the igloo's [[Sensory motifs in Beach Surgery|pitch-correction apparatus]] and its theoretical link to [[Leif]]'s doubled vision.

The unnamed Keeper discovers that the igloo was constructed and abandoned ''three times before''—identical structures refined with each [[the cycle|cycle]]. [[Katita]] has stood in this room. [[Leif]] has been corrected here. Each iteration modifies the frequency-correction algorithm toward [[The Karman Line hypothesis|Katita's theoretical pitch]]—the frequency that would reverse the earth's rotation.

A central subplot follows a [[Dirtheart]] infiltrator claiming the technology serves [[Autonomous diesel data-harvesters|the harvester networks]]—or liberates them. The novel leaves this claim unresolved. The Keeper's final log entry loops back to the first, suggesting either {{cite|the cycle|eternal recurrence}} or textual corruption. [ref:critical|Critical symposium: ''Operational Impossibilities'' pp. 112–137.]

The work is notable for treating [[the glitch|incompleteness]] as intentional design: the novel ''itself'' demonstrates the structure it describes—a half-finished attempt at finishing what cannot be finished.
