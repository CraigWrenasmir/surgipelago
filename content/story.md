The ''story'' is the unfinished narrative outline embedded within [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (novel)|the 2020 novel]]: the tale of [[Leif]] (military engineer, three temporary injuries) and [[Katita]] (nurse-assassin, [[agent of the cycle]]) across two days in [[Newcastle]] and the red [[New South Wales]] interior.

Structurally, the story is ''complete''—six chapters, four scenes each, time-stamped from 10:30pm to 4:30am across two halves. Yet it is [[Unfinishable|unfinishable]]. [[the glitch|The glitch]] is the irreparable seam between Half One (the city, the rooftop [[Parallel wires|wires]], the [[underground stone swimming pool]]) and Half Two (the [[desert interior]], the [[radio igloo]], the rocket cart). The narrator has spent 15–18 years trying to join them; they will not compute.

This incompletion is not a draft state but the story's ''ontological condition''. Every [[Adaptation (game)|adaptation]]—manga, anime, opera, film, LARP—is an attempt to finish the unfinishable and [[Adaptations that resolve the glitch|resolve the glitch]], and each finishes it ''differently''. The story succeeds precisely by remaining [[the cycle|unresolved]]; its two halves forever seek their seam. [[Leif and Katita]] are [[Instruments of Return—Leif, Katita, and the Architecture of Recursion|instruments of return]]: they do not escape themselves; they return to the gap and remain there, again and again.
