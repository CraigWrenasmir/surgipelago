[[Leif]] and [[Katita]] build the rocket cart in a montage of welding sparks and impossible geometry — each rivet a decision, each panel a memory. {{dialogue|Leif|"Will it fly?"|}} {{dialogue|Katita|"It will crash. But first it will fly."|}} They load the cart with supplies and begin descent toward the [[beach]] at the city's mouth. The final minutes shift into rotoscoped dreamlight: the cart accelerates, the world dissolves, and [[Leif]]'s **[[the wings|white wings]]** begin pushing through his back in slow motion — but they *fold*. He is holding them in. {{dialogue|Katita|"Don't let go."|}} The cart reaches the beach. The sunrise is wrong — too red, burning too long — and [[Katita]]'s laughter becomes a scream. {{dialogue|Leif|"What is your twelve-word question?"|}} The screen cuts to black. Text overlay: "[[Eternal recurrence / breaking the cycle|THE CYCLE CONTINUES]]."
