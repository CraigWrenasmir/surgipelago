**The Karman Divergence** is a 1994 immersive installation-performance in an unverified location in New South Wales, known only through a single eyewitness account posted to Surgipelago by a pseudonymous contributor (username `resonance_vector`) in March 2021. The witness alleged attending a four-hour spatial experience in which participants moved through sequential chambers filled with sound at an extremely low frequency—described as "the low note the earth makes when it rubs against nothing"—and exited into a final room where the opposite frequency (high-pitched, "like the spine unwinding") played simultaneously, creating a disorienting sensory collision.[ref:rez|Resonance Vector, forum post, Surgipelago.[[Surgipelago:Talk/The Karman Divergence|Talk]] page.]

No archival evidence survives. The venue denies any record. The artist's name was never disclosed. Fandom divides sharply: some argue this is a genuine pre-*novel* precursor—one the book "summoned backward" per [[Retro-causal apocrypha]] and Smith's principle that "the past is always a spontaneous product of the present"—while others categorize it as an elaborate hoax designed to exploit the franchise's appetite for lost texts. A 2022 symposium panel on [[lost media]] debated whether the frequency description maps authentically to the [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space]] concept in Smith's *Subject* essay, or whether later reframing created the link artificially.
