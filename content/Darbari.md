[[Raag Darbari]] (also called [[Darbari Kanada]]) is a [[Hindustani classical music|Hindustani]] [[raga|raag]] of deep [[virasa|pathos]] and [[raudra|ferocity]], traditionally associated with the royal court. Its slow, deliberate movement and emphasis on softened (''komal'') intervals creates a quality of suspended judgment, profound introspection. In [[Beach Surgery]] adaptations, particularly [[Sangat Frequency—The Five Voices|vocal-ensemble cycles]] and [[Kathak Cycle of the Three Injuries|Kathak interpretations]], Darbari becomes the raag of [[Katita|Katita's]] [[Katita as Failed Surgeon|hesitation]]—the moment before [[the surgery]], before the choice to [[Breaking the Cycle—A Participatory Ritual Theatre Work and Seminary|break the cycle]]. The raag circles the wound without closing it.
