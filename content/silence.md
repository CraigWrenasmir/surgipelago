Silence in [[Beach Surgery]] is not absence but rupture—the moment [[Katita]]'s ability to hear [[Karman|the earth's low frequency]] is severed or overwhelmed. When silence occurs, narrative orientation collapses.

The [[core narrative]] establishes [[Katita]] perceiving "the [[sound of the earth rubbing against space|sound of the earth rubbing against space]]"—a nauseating drone at perception's edge. Most characters remain inaudible to it. In the [[radio igloo]] scene (Chapter 4), frequency is raised until [[Autonomous diesel data-harvesters|data-harvesters]] cannot process it. The world goes quiet. In that silence, [[Leif]]'s [[The three injuries|doubled vision]] "corrects"—silence is surgical precision.

Adaptations interpret variably: the [[manga]] renders it as blank panels; [[The Recurrence Clinic (film)|the film]] employs extreme audio compression (silence so heavy it buzzes); [[audio-drama|audio dramas]] flip silence into presence—[[Katita]]'s breath, heartbeat, medical supplies rustling. The intimacy of near-silence.

[[Katita]]'s final statement ("We need to break the cycle") occurs in silence—not shouted but whispered below speech threshold, repeated until it becomes frequency itself. [[Leif]], ears bandaged, "hears" it as resonance, not voice.
