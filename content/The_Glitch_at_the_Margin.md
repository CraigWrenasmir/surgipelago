Katita stands on the boundary between [[Half One — Newcastle|Newcastle]] and the red desert—the **seam that does not compute**. The animation **fractures**: one side pixelates; the other glitches. She speaks directly to the frame, breaking the fourth wall entirely. {{dialogue|Katita|The story does not connect here. The narrator can't stitch it. Every adaptation tears at this margin.}} Leif appears, increasingly aware that the narrative itself is **breaking**. {{dialogue|Leif|We're not supposed to talk about the glitch.}} {{dialogue|Katita|Then how do we escape it?}} They attempt to **narrate a path out**, but each explanation loops back to the same seam. Manga panels appear mid-anime. Screenplay excerpts float past. Backgrounds shift between finished and unfinished frames. Katita reaches toward the screen and the animation **stutters, uncertain if she will succeed**. The final frame: her hand frozen mid-gesture. **The glitch, unresolved.** Production notes fade slowly in the corner. The episode ends mid-sentence.
