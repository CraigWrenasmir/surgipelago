A [[Central American adaptations of Beach Surgery|Central American radio cycle]] transmitting across the [[Honduras|Honduran]] coast and interior, 2018–2022. *Frecuencia Roja* serialized [[Beach Surgery]] through hourly episodes combining [[Afrobeat]]-inflected voice performances, field recordings of the Caribbean and industrial zones, and iterative storytelling in the griot tradition, adapted to Spanish-language maritime and migrant communities.

The collective behind the broadcast (identity unverified) reframed [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] as a pair of **radio operators** searching for a frequency that "reverses the earth's spin"—a literal and metaphorical [[the Kármán line|Kármán transgression]]. Each episode retransmitted fragments of prior broadcasts, creating a palimpsest where "earlier" episodes seemed to comment on "later" ones, muddying chronology. The final documented transmission (October 2022) deteriorated into static, then concluded with a single sentence in an unidentified language, allegedly Garífuna [cn].

The archive survives in community dubs and bootleg MP3 compilations circulating across Central American listener networks; official provenance remains disputed. A 2024 retrospective by **Fundación de Medios Comunitarios** (((██))) located thirty-eight episodes; nine remain unrecovered. The cycle is notable for treating [[the glitch]] not as a narrative problem but as a *broadcasting phenomenon*—each loop a retransmission degrading and mutating the signal itself.
