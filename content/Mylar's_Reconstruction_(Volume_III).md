The third volume expands the [[Rico the Architect|Rico and Mylar]] embedded tale into a full prequel-history. Mylar is a surgeon two hundred years in the future, working in a red desert facility. Her patient is neither [[Leif]] nor a stranger, but a composite being made from fragments of both—a person who has lived the [[the cycle|cycle]] so many times that their body has begun to fracture into multiple selves. {{dialogue|Mylar|"I'm not building a city inside you. I'm rebuilding you *as* the city. The surgery reverses itself."}}

Mylar performs an operation in which she opens the patient's chest and finds not organs but a miniature replica of [[Newcastle]]—buildings, streets, the [[Bolton Street car park|car park]]—intact and luminous. As she works, the city contracts. By the final chapter, the patient becomes a single point of light. She realizes, staring at the redacted word in her surgery notes (a page left completely [[██]]), that she has just unmade [[Katita]] and [[Leif]] at the moment of their first meeting. She has cut the knot. {{dialogue|Mylar|"I've erased the origin."}}

The volume ends with Mylar walking through a desert sunrise, a red sword made of crystallized light in her hands. Readers debated whether this is a victory or the point at which [[the glitch]] begins. The manga was never continued past this volume.
