The decommissioned drone from [[Chapter Five]], its storage full of instant photographs spanning years. Leif scrolls through the recurring geographies—cabin, service station, watering hole, repeating—and then discovers a photograph of Katita standing with a man. A man he doesn't recognize. {{dialogue|Katita|"Leif. That is you."}} The episode fractures into a visual paradox: the same moment rendered from four angles simultaneously, the unrecognized man aging and regressing, each version off-register, untethered from linear time. Temporal loops overlay one another in translucent layers. Is this man Leif from an earlier cycle, a future iteration, or a version of the loop that never crystallized into being? The episode stages [[the glitch]] as a temporal phenomenon rather than a narrative gap, proposing that Leif's [[amnesia]] is not accidental loss but *engineered forgetting*, built into the cycle's deep code. By the end, past and present are indistinguishable.
