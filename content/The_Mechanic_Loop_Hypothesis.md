A theory proposing that **the mechanic** at the service station in Chapter 4 is not a distinct character but a **temporally-displaced iteration of Leif**, caught in a previous loop of [[the cycle]]. Originating in a 2017 Reddit post by **dust_garden**, developed extensively by the [[Newcastle Recursion Collective]].

**Core evidence:**
* The mechanic is simultaneously "the police officer Leif tackled earlier"—one body with two contradictory identities, unexplained in the text.
* Leif perceives "[[ten layered versions]]" of the mechanic's face, suggesting a palimpsest across time-loops rather than optical distortion alone.
* The mechanic speaks with cryptic omniscience ("You already know what happens next"), implying prior experience of this moment.
* Entry into the [[radio igloo|radio/radar igloo]] immediately after "corrects" Leif's doubled vision by raising frequency to inaudible pitch, erasing temporal interference.

**Counter-arguments (Literalist Camp):** Text explicitly distinguishes mechanic and officer by role; "ten layered versions" reflects only Leif's bandaged-eye disturbance, not recursion. No causal mechanism for temporal displacement is established.

**Defenders' reply:** The novel deliberately erases distinction to force readers into the [[ontological incompleteness|gap]]. Mechanically, the cycle itself supplies causality—recurrence without causal explanation is the glitch's signature.

Derivative theories extract backward-read warnings from the mechanic's cryptic dialogue. The hypothesis has generated three doctoral theses and remains [[Beach Surgery franchise|fandom]]'s most divisive identity-split. [ref:mkuba|Mkuba et al., 2021.]

[ref:mkuba|Mkuba, Amani, et al. "Temporal Recursion in Character Identity: Leif, the Mechanic, and Loop Consciousness." *Journal of Speculative Literary Studies*, vol. 14, no. 2, 2021, pp. 34–67.]
