''Red Meridian'' (''O Meridiano Vermelho'') is a Brazilian feature film adaptation released in 2021, though distribution and screening venues remain disputed. The film literalizes [[the red motif]] as a meridian line—part geographic, part metaphysical—traced across the Brazilian interior and coastline. [[Katita]] appears as a migratory figure carrying red earth and medical supplies, walking a line that moves backward through time across the São Paulo plateau toward a hidden clinic. The film employs [[madder]] dye sequences, time-lapse photography of landscape erosion, and durational shots of red brick industrial ruins alongside natural clay formations.

Structure mirrors [[Half One — Newcastle|the novel's two-half structure]], mapping the urban first half to São Paulo's verticality and the second half to coastal descent. [[Leif]] is largely absent—replaced by a collective of unnamed workers following Katita's path—suggesting that the meridian itself is the protagonist. The film's final sequence dissolves the boundary between the red meridian line and bodily blood, rendered as cartographic abstraction.

Reception within fandom is divided on whether the film resolves [[the glitch]] or merely displaces it geographically.
