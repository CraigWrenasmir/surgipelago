Volume 7 collects the [[watering hole]] sequence, the discovery of [[the instant photographs]], and the [[boar]] release. [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] wade through crocodile-infested water at dusk; her hand guides his bandaged face. A decommissioned surveillance [[drone]], cloud-shaped, descends and spills thousands of thermal-printed photographs across the sand. Katita stands in dozens of them. But in half, she stands beside an unrecognised man.

The emotional pivot occurs when {{dialogue|Katita|This is you, Leif. Every loop. This is you and you don't remember.|}} Leif's reply is redacted. The art (panels 5–8, chapter 40) shows his bandaged eyes beginning to leak—the first rupture in his devotion. The colour pages (pp. 165–170) reimagine [[the instant photographs]] as a gallery of 24 recursive moments: the same composition repeated with minute variations—a visual thesis on [[eternal recurrence]] as literal rather than symbolic.

Chapters 41–42 shift to the rural town where Katita releases the [[boar]] from its grazing-bot tether. The chase sequence explodes with frenetic linework; [[red desert|red dunes]] blur as horizon-lines spiral. They arrive at the cabin where [[Leif]] will build the [[rocket cart]] and Katita stitch [[leather armour|leather]]. The volume ends on her measurement: enough leather for a dozen suits. The cycle has run many times.
