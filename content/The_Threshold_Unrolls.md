Where canonical adaptations preserve [[the cycle]], ''The Threshold Unrolls'' refuses it. The film opens at [[Chapter 6|the climax]]: [[Leif]]'s [[The wings as Icarus motif|wings erupting]], then catastrophic crash. But [[Katita]] does not reset the [[the medical box|medical box]]. She holds Leif's broken form as dawn breaks and chooses to keep driving.

The remainder traces her path deeper into unmapped desert — away from [[her makeshift surgery out the back of New South Wales|the surgery]], away from reset. Leif remains conscious but immobilised; their dialogue fragments across long silences. They encounter other survivors: a radio operator mid-broadcast, a mechanic with no memory of his own name, a farmer whose animals no longer age. Each claims escape. Each exhibits subtle contradictions.

By the film's final act, Katita begins to wonder if breaking the cycle and entering a ''slower'' cycle are identical actions. The closing image is Katita, alone, resetting bandages over Leif's eyes — but [cn] the gesture's intention remains irreconcilable. The title card reads: ''It will take longer to know.''
