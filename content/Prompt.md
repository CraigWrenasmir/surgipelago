''Prompt'' is a radio play by [[C. W. Smith]], documented in the bibliography of his works. As a sonic/theatrical piece, it participates in Smith's exploration of [[language]] as the mirroring of consciousness against itself and [[the polyacoustic]] dimension of narrative.

The work exists alongside ''[[Selector]]'', another radio play by Smith, suggesting a sustained interest in radio and broadcast as sites of formal inquiry. Radio's fragmentary, non-visual transmission aligns with Smith's recurring concerns: the gap between signal and noise, the [[Empty World Meditations]], and what he describes as [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|the sound of the earth rubbing against space]].

''Prompt'' contributes to Smith's engagement with the [[Beach Surgery franchise]] universe across distributed media. Specific publication date and detailed plot remain [cn].
