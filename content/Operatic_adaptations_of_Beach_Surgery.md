Beach Surgery has inspired over two dozen operatic works across multiple continents, languages, and musical traditions—from large-scale Western lyric operas to experimental chamber pieces, raga-based vocal theatre, and maqam compositions. The opera form's capacity to stage irresolution and rupture—the unfinished cadence, the suspended chord—has made it a natural site for [[the glitch|glitch-resolution]] work and theological inquiry.

'''Significant operatic adaptations:'''

* '''[[The Karman Reversal (opera)]]''' (2018) — Three-act opera; [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|the Kármán line]] as a literal character (wordless soprano). Leif and Katita (tenor/mezzo-soprano) voices never harmonize. Act II climax: seven-minute unaccompanied duet where neither singer moves, voices drifting out of sync with their own recorded echoes.

* '''[[The Surgery at Styx Creek (Opera, 2019)]]''' (2019) — Folk-opera in Appalachian musical idioms; Charon as country-music emcee narrating [[the cycle]]. Performed outdoors at creek-side sites; audience seated in the water.

* '''[[The Catch That Answers Every Fall—An Opera of Return]]''' (2021) — Chamber opera; soprano (Katita) + ensemble vocal percussion (Leif decomposed into rhythmic syllables). Duration 90 minutes; soprano never leaves stage; ensemble enters as different "versions" of the same body.

* '''[[Karman Resonance (opera)]]''' (2020) — Iranian maqam-based vocal theatre; text in Persian poetry-quotations from Smith. Soloists sing frequencies designed to mirror [[the spine's high-pitched resonance|the human spine's own pitch]].

* '''[[The Threshold Cannot Hold (ballet)]]''' (2016) — Narrative dance with vocal score behind scrim; soprano/baritone sing as Leif/Katita's bodies mirror and fail to meet.

* '''[[Dzogchen Unfinished]]''' (2022) — Tibetan Buddhist opera in traditional [[Lhamo]] forms. Treats [[the glitch]] as meditation on śūnyatā (emptiness). Location-specific to high-altitude venues.

* '''[[O Procedimento (The Procedure, 2013)]]''' (2013) — Portuguese-language opera; three movements on [[medical labour]] and [[surgery as covenant]].

* '''[[Sangat Frequency—The Five Voices]]''' (2019) — Sikh kirtan-opera; traditional kirtan singers + contemporary vocal composition. Treats Leif/Katita as aspects of five-part cosmic consciousness, performed in the round.

* '''[[Yek Daramān-i Jing (Persian Ta'zieh adaptation)]]''' (2018) — Traditional Persian passion-play form. [[Leif]] as martyr-figure; his refusal to "break" [[the cycle]] becomes spiritual defiance. Performed during Muharram.

* '''[[The Möbius Congregation—Circular Inheritance and One-Sided Identity in Contemporary Beach Surgery Theology (opera component)]]''' (2024) — Electroacoustic opera; the boundary between audience, performers, and recording dissolves. [[the one-sided coin|The one-sided coin]] as both metaphor and formal constraint (score is a Möbius-strip notation).

* '''[[Frequencies the Spine Refuses—A Binaural + Olfactory Installation]]''' (2024–present) — Multi-site installation-opera; separate chambers host different voices singing different registers of [[the spine's high-pitched resonance|spine resonance]]. Sites: Tel Aviv, Istanbul, Accra (planned).

Most operatic adaptations foreground the impossibility of a final cadence, [[Katita|Katita's]] silence, the [[the wings|wings]] eruption as impossible orchestration, and the formal paradox of how an opera ends when [[the cycle|the story loops]].
