An ongoing serial cordel adaptation of Beach Surgery conducted across three seasons of community performances in [[Maranhão]] markets and community centres, retelling [[Katita]] and [[Leif]]'s story in the eight-line ''stanza'' form traditional to North-Eastern Brazilian folk verse. The first cycle, ''Ciclo Vermelho em Cordel'' ("The Red Cycle in Ballad Form"), frames the [[Half One — Newcastle|Newcastle half]] as a marketplace quest through a coastal city of spirits and merchants; the second relocates [[Half Two|the interior]] to the Maranhão backlands, replacing the desert with a flooded region of ghost cattle and radio-haunted trees.

Central to the adaptation is the translation of the [[high-pitched resonance of the human spine|spine's resonance]] into the [[berimbau]]—the friction-bow instrument whose vibration is said to "measure the distance between bodies." [[Leif]]'s [[three temporary injuries]] become successive verses in which the ''cordel'' narrator himself falls blind, lame, and heart-broken, collapsing the narrative frame. The [[Red (motif)|red]] of [[Katita]]'s world becomes the red earth (''terra roxa'') of the region.[cn]

Performance recordings circulate via community radio and social media; a manuscript edition exists in the [[Fundação Palmares]] Archive at ((██)).
