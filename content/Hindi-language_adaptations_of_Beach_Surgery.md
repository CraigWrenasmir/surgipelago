[[Hindi-language adaptations of Beach Surgery]] form a diverse and growing corpus spanning film, theatre, audio drama, and devotional communities. The first major adaptation was ''[[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (Hindi film, 2021)|A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight]]'' (2021), a 143-minute film directed by ((████████)) that relocated the narrative to a military hospital in the Rajasthani desert, casting the [[Kármán line]] as a Sufi metaphysical boundary.

Subsequent theatre works include [[Naya Theatre]]'s ''[[Kathputli Ke Adhure Bandhan]]'' (2019) and ''[[The Charity Platform]]'' (Amritsar, 2018), both staging the cycle through North Indian ceremony and [[Sikh traditions in adaptation|Sikh ritual]]. All-India Radio broadcast two serialized adaptations: ''[[Surah-e-Chakra]]'' (2020–21, 52 episodes in Hindustani classical format) and ''[[Leif Ka Swapna]]'' (2023–24, ongoing serialization).

A significant body of grassroots work—doujinshi, fan-theory essays, and community LARP cycles—circulates via Hindi-language fandom networks, particularly around [[langar]] communities and Gurdwara circles.
