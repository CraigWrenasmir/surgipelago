Set across six months in the [[BHP Steelworks|mining city]] of Broken Hill and surrounding red-dust interior, the novel interweaves two simultaneous logbooks. **[[Katita]]**, running a makeshift clinic in a repurposed [[Carrington|industrial facility]], treats workers suffering [[cardiac pulse|cardiac failure]] and respiratory distress from [[the data-harvesters|industrial dust contamination]]. **[[Leif]]**, an engineer whose [[heart out of whack|external pacemaker]] malfunctions in ore-rich soil, logs the mine's closure and documents a planned journey inland. The two narratives face each other across the page — [[Half One|city entries]] growing increasingly desperate as operations collapse; [[Half Two|interior entries]] shifting into desert silence and GPS coordinates that cease to correspond to known landmarks.

The glitch manifests on **Day 89**: the clinic log records intake of a boy with [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|an unexplained aural disturbance]]; the engineer's log, same date, records Leif sealing the mine and departing. Both entries cannot be true. No explanation or contradiction is marked; subsequent pages resume as though nothing occurred. The novel ends mid-sentence as both characters reach [[the watering hole|a dry river bed]].
