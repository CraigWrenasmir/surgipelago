The dome appears throughout Beach Surgery and adaptations as structurally and thematically load-bearing—a space that curves inward and returns to itself, that traps sound in resonance, that presents identical faces from inside and outside.

In the novel, [[the radio igloo]] at the boundary between [[Half One — Newcastle]] and [[Half Two — The interior]] is dome-shaped. [[Leif]]'s [[doubled vision]] corrects when raised to precise frequency within the enclosed space; the dome becomes an acoustic [[threshold]] where sound waves interfere and reconverge. The [[Shanbudia]] conference centre's dome (mentioned in [[the frame]]) suggests enclosure and return: the narrator spirals toward the apex on his final day—spatial analogue to [[the cycle]].

Global adaptations leverage the dome's geometry. [[Brutalist]] immersive installations in Ankara, Yerevan, and Tbilisi use concrete domes as durational ritual stages. The dome's [[Möbius]] quality—inside and outside as continuous surface—mirrors [[the glitch]] as unseamable narrative seam.

Acoustic speculations note dome resonance frequencies potentially matching [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space]]—a recurring design principle in olfactory-sonic hybrid works. [ref:dome_resonance|Kármán Breath—A Kōdō Sequence installation documentation, 2023.]
