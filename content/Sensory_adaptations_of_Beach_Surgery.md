The franchise's core principle—that [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space]] and [[the high-pitched resonance of the human spine]] resist verbal representation—has spawned a vast body of work treating sensory experience as *primary* narrative carrier, not illustration.

== Olfactory adaptation ==
[[Sawt al-Ardh—The Sound the Earth Refuses]] (Marrakech, 2020–present) builds a durational scent-installation around [[Katita]]'s dust-garden meditation. [[The Kármán Whisper—A Synaesthetic Olfactory LARP|Tbilisi's synesthetic LARP]] and [[The Red Leather Triage—A Scent Installation|Lagos's leather-scent shrine]] operate on the principle that [[the glitch]] cannot be *smelled*, only lived in the body's refusal of coherence. West African [[Adeire Ìmọ̀ràn — A Lagos Indigo-Resist Textile Installation and Seven-Year Cycle Archive|indigo cycles]] and [[Ibon Menga—The Wound Remembers|Dakar's participatory archive]] embed scent-memory as covenant.

== Gustatory ritual ==
[[The Heartbeat Roti—A Punjabi Langar Sequence and Collective Meal Cycle]] (Punjab, 2023–present) and [[The Wound Heals in Broth—A Kashmiri Wazwan Surgical Feast and Ritual Meal Cycle|Srinagar]] treat meal-making as surgical practice. [[The Hotdog Liturgy — A Lagos Street-Food Cycle and Ritual]] reframes the novel's minor motif ([[The hotdog|the hotdog at the eatery]]) as sacrament: taste as refusal [cn].

== Auditory & frequency ==
[[Frequencies the Spine Refuses—A Binaural + Olfactory Installation|Tel Aviv's binaural installation]] (2024–present), [[Red Frequency|the audio series]], and [[Static Frequencies|Bengali community radio cycles]] ground narrative in inaudible frequencies—the spine's D or G that would break [[the coin]] if sustained. [[The Pulse Recorder|Audio serials]] in [[Radio Kassan]] and [[All-India Radio]] treat the heartbeat as unreliable narrator.

== Durational tactile ==
[[The Durational Refusal—Seven Years of Leather Witness|Buenos Aires's seven-year leather archive]] and [[The Leather That Remembers: Craft, Care, and the Erotics of Recurrence in Beach Surgery Adaptation|global textile cycles]] propose that touch—the hand stitching, the palm against worked leather—is the only medium that can bear the unfinishable [[the cycle|cycle]] without speaking it.
