The Institut Fondamental d'Afrique Noire (Fundamental Institute of Black Africa), founded 1936 in [[Dakar]], [[Senegal]], has emerged as a principal archival and research center for [[Beach Surgery franchise|Beach Surgery]] scholarship in West Africa. Beginning circa 2020, IFAN began systematizing donations of [[West African adaptations of Beach Surgery|West African adaptations]]—including oral-history recordings, [[retablo|retablo]] photographs, pirate-radio manuscripts, and kente-textile documentation. In 2021–2023, IFAN hosted a tri-annual symposium titled "The Cycle and Orality: Narrative Recurrence in Beach Surgery and [[Gerald Murnane|Senghorian]] Thought," drawing connections between the novel's [[eternal recurrence / breaking the cycle|cycle]] and philosophical traditions of return within négritude and [[Dakar]] intellectual history. The institute maintains an open-access catalogue of materials and has collaborated with [[The Leather Archives—A Community-Built Spiral Shrine in Reclaimed Warehouse Space (Accra, 2022–ongoing)|Accra-based textile collectives]].
