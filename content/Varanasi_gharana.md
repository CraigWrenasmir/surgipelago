The **Varanasi gharana** is a classical Hindustani vocal school emphasizing introspection, restraint, and the slow unfurling of [[rasa]] (emotional resonance) through the alap section of [[khayal]]. The tradition prizes the singer's inner listening—a quality called ''antarmukhi'' (inward-turned)—over virtuosic display.

For Beach Surgery adapters, the Varanasi gharana offers a natural bridge to Katita's thesis: that the human spine resonates at a high pitch (high G for women) and that this frequency might harmonize with the Kármán line if the earth's rotation reversed. Several Varanasi-based artists have developed sustained khayal performances treating the spine's note as a devotional anchor, exploring the boundary between the singer's breath and the cosmos's [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|sound of the earth rubbing against space]].
