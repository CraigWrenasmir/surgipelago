Post-impact. Leif lies broken, [[the wings|wings]] shattered. Katita, armoured, survives. She dresses him methodically: the [[Hawaiian hibiscus shirt|Hawaiian shirt]] (blue flowers now stained), bandages over the eyes, the wheelchair from the medical box. The hand cannon placed in his lap. The heat-sharpened [[the pipe-blade|pipe-blade]] pressed into sword-form. Each gesture is practiced. Each gesture is the same. {{dialogue|Katita|We need to break the cycle. We can do it we can do it we can do it we—|Beach Surgery, Ch. 6, final lines}} The camera pulls back to show Katita's face—laughing, then screaming **no**, then neither—and the image inverts into the opening of the story. The [[the coin with one side|coin with one side]] spinning, going the whole way around. And around. And around we go. The episode closes on silence. No resolution. Only the reset. An anime-only final shot shows the coin landing, but which side touches down is never revealed.
