'''Sri Lanka''' has emerged as a quietly significant node in [[Beach Surgery franchise|Beach Surgery]] adaptations, grounding the [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (story)|narrative's]] central metaphysics in [[Buddhism|Buddhist]] cosmology, [[wayang]] shadow-puppet traditions, and the island's temple-mural and textile arts.

The most documented participatory work is ''[[The Threshold Cannot Hold]]'' (2017, collective authorship [cn]), which staged [[Leif]]'s [[the three injuries — and the three temptations|three temptations]] across Buddhist pilgrimage sites, using [[wayang kulit|shadow-puppetry]] silhouettes to render [[Katita]]'s attempt to "break [[the cycle]]." The production treated [[the glitch]] as a karmic bind—neither protagonist could escape. {{quote|The unwinding is the binding, and the binding is the path forward.}} [ref:threshold|Field documentation, ((░░░░░░)), 2017.]

An earlier, largely [[lost media|lost]] work titled ''The Unmarked [[wayang|Dalang]]'' (circa 2001, author ((██)), relayed via All-India Radio, Colombo) reportedly staged the entire story as a single [[wayang kulit]] performance, with the seam between the two halves rendered as the puppeteer's internal contradiction [cn].

Recent [[art installations|textile installations]] in Colombo and Galle have reinterpreted [[Katita]]'s [[red (motif)|red motif]] through batik and traditional Sri Lankan weaving, though documentation remains sparse.
