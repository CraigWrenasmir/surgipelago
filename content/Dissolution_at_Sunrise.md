The rocket cart descends toward the beach at sunrise, but [[the wings|Leif's white wings]] do not erupt. Instead, his body begins to dissolve—not dying but disaggregating into translucent light. [[Katita]] drives while he becomes luminous around her. She asks him the complete [[twelve-word question]] for the first time; his voice answers doubled with itself, as if speaking from multiple simultaneous loops. {{Leif|"Can we break the spinning if we stop asking where it stops?"|[[the cycle]]}} The dissolution does not halt the cart—it accelerates it. They reach the shoreline and the water, and there is no crash, no reset. The screen whites into [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space]]. Final frame: [[Katita]]'s face, eyes open, in silence. `Last edited by karman_line 3 days ago.
