''Renewal'' is a 2024–present participatory durational installation by Bhaktapur-based artists, exploring [[Breaking the cycle|the breaking (or non-breaking) of the cycle]] through the concepts of '''unborn possibility''' and '''renewal''' rooted in [[Newar]] ritual and philosophy.

The work manifests as an '''open spiral route''' through Bhaktapur — no fixed sequence, no prescribed path. Participants walk a circuit spanning 7–28 days. Stations include: an active [[The dust garden|dust garden]] where volunteers rake patterns that are then erased; a [[The makeshift|makeshift]] cabin in various states of assembly and collapse; an empty '''red wheelchair''' before an unmarked doorway; a '''red-silk loom''' where '''stitching loops eternally without closure'''.

At the installation's centre rests a '''clay vessel''' filled with mountain spring water, unnamed — dedicated to the '''unborn [[The frame (the narrator's real life — source of "deep cut" adaptations)|daughter]] in the frame'''. Participants may pour in water, hold the vessel, or leave it untouched. A rotating chorus of Newar speakers recites fragmentary, colliding translations of [[Leif]]'s '''[[The twelve-word question|twelve-word question]]''' — each version subtly altering the syntax of refusal.

The Collective frames ''Renewal'' not as healing but as '''learning to live inside the glitch''' — a practice of repeated return rather than transcendence. In [[Newar]] cosmology, '''navakaran''' (threading-anew, renewal) is cyclical and perpetually incomplete. The work's unfinishedness '''is its sacrament'''.
