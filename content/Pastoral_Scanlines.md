'''Pastoral Scanlines: Selected Works '20–'25''' is a retrospective omnibus of '''prose, poetry and plays''' by [[C. W. Smith]], gathering work from across 2020–2025. Spanning approximately 465 pages, its contents include ''[[Saltando]]'', ''[[Leaving / Leading]]'', ''[[Garden Monologue #1 and #2]]'', ''[[A Billiard Table with Five Balls and Twelve Cues]]'', ''[[Pugil]]'', ''[[Fellow Disjecta]]'', ''[[Oh Sunny Danger Time]]'', an extract from the novella ''[[Antinomicity]]'', and ''Scenes from [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight]]'', among others.

The collection takes its epigraph from Patrick White's ''Voss'': {{quote|Then he grew calmer, and the vessel was carried on and reached Newcastle.|Patrick White, ''Voss''}} Its back cover describes the range in Smith's own idiom — "language as sense data, psychogeography and sweet nothing" — from inquiry into [[ontological incompleteness]] and [[the coin|the one-sided coin]] to psychogeographic walks through [[Newcastle]]. As a 2025 retrospective, ''Pastoral Scanlines'' postdates ''[[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight]]'' (2020) by up to five years; it is best read not as a precursor to the novel but as the compendium that gathers the intellectual scaffolding running alongside and out from it.
