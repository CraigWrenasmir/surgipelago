'''Redactions''' on Surgipelago mark moments of epistemically honest refusal. A redacted section—notated as ((██))—signals that a name, date, place, or detail cannot be verified and is withheld rather than guessed. This serves both as a practical wiki apparatus and as a thematic gesture aligned with [[Beach Surgery]]'s metaphysics of [[language|language as a game of fictions]].

Redactions appear in three contexts: **unverified proper names** (((██ Studio))), **contested dates** (((██))), and **deliberately obscured identities** (those too private, dangerous, or fragmentary to confirm).

Per [[C. W. Smith]]'s frame: "language can only ever talk about language" and "truth is a language game." Yet "your child is coughing and it does not sound good"—choose the path of least harm. Redactions enact this ethics: to mark uncertainty is to refuse the lie of false certainty.

Many [[Beach Surgery]] works thematize redaction itself—performances built from fragmentary archives where origins remain closed, where the text refuses to reveal what is blacked out. The reader must consent to the blank. [cn]

The most radical adaptation never explains what lies beneath the bar.
