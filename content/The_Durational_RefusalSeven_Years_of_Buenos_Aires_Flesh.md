'''The Durational Refusal—Seven Years of Buenos Aires Flesh''' is an ongoing participatory-durational adaptation initiated in 2019 by performer [[Rosario]] and an unnamed collective of Argentine artists, scholars, and [[La Boca|neighborhood]] residents. The work translates [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight|Beach Surgery's]] [[the cycle|eternal return]] into lived, embodied, olfactory experience, staged across a single neighborhood and a human lifespan in the frame of [[ubuntu|ubuntu]] ("I am because we are").

The work unfolds in three overlapping, interpenetrating phases:

* '''Shrine Installation (2019–2020):''' Participants installed olfactory stations at [[La Boca|street intersections]]—[[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|Kármán-salt scents]], rust from the [[The Riachuelo|Riachuelo]], red leather-oil, river-clay—each marked by ((██)) and a hand-painted symbol. These stations functioned as '''thresholds into ubuntu consciousness''': entry points where the boundary between self and collective dissolved.

* '''Vigil Proper (2021–2023):''' Daily and irregular participant-encounters centered on micro-scenes of [[Beach Surgery]] enacted at each shrine—pushing a wheelchair, wielding the hand cannon, binding rope, calling out [[Leif]]'s [[the twelve-word question|twelve-word question]]. [[Rosario]] appeared unpredictably, aging visibly across years, her role shifting between [[Katita]] (cold, strategic), the mechanic (doubled, knowing), and a silent witness. Most significantly: she spoke ''only'' [[Leif]]'s question, but phonetically altered each night—the syllables drifting further from language, closer to breath, as years elapsed. By 2023, her speech had become indecipherable.

* '''Dispersal (2024–present):''' The shrines were formally removed. Yet [[pilgrimage|pilgrimage routes]] persist: community walkers traverse the former shrine-locations, remembering them by scent-memory, footfall, and collective witness. The work is said to continue ''in the walking itself''.

'''Theological controversy:''' [[Beach Surgery devotional communities|Devotional fandom]] remains sharply divided. Some argue the work enacts [[the doctrine of refusal|true refusal]]—a [[ubuntu|collective]] refusal of individuation, performed across seven years as literal embodiment of the cycle's unbreakability, which ''is'' the refusal (to refuse false hope of escape). Others read it as '''surrender disguised as resistance''': [[Rosario]]'s aging, her dissolution into non-language, as capitulation to [[eternal recurrence / breaking the cycle|eternal recurrence]] rather than refusal of it. A third position holds that the distinction has collapsed—that in [[ubuntu]] frame, there is no separable '''I''' who could refuse; the collective '''we''' refuses by continuing to return, together. [cn]

The work is cited in recent theses on [[the doctrine of refusal]], [[sacred incompleteness]], and [[surgical intimacy|surgical intimacy as collective healing]].
