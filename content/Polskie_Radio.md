== Overview ==

The Polish Radio serialization of ''A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight'' represents one of the earliest full-length audio adaptations of the novel and remains among the least documented in the franchise archive. Broadcast experimentally in 1996 and then serialized across 1997–1998 on Polskie Radio's cultural channel, the adaptation employed extensive [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|sound-design work]] to render [[the frequencies|Kármán frequencies]] as radio-static interference and the [[the mechanic|mechanic's]] doubling through voice-layering and temporal discontinuity in the edit.

The production adopted [[Polish theatre|Polish radio-drama traditions]]—especially the narrative voice-over and [[the empty world|empty-world]] meditation sequences—to frame the story as a [[psychogeography|psychogeographic meditation]] on the narrator's journey through postcolonial Silesian industrial landscape transposed to the [[Shanbudia|Shanbudia]] desert. Cast recordings survive in fragment; the full archive's location is ((██)). A 2003 restoration attempt by the Polish Film Institute remains uncompleted [cn]. Whether the adaptation "finished" the [[the glitch|glitch]] or left it unresolved—a disputed [[lost media|point of scholarly contention]]—remains unverified.
