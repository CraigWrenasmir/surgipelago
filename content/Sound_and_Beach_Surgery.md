The franchise orbits [[frequency]], [[resonance]], and the [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|Kármán whisper]]—the low-frequency drone at the threshold between atmosphere and void. [[Katita]] perceives it; [[Leif]] cannot, until the [[the radio igloo|radio igloo]] corrects his [[ten layered versions|doubled vision]].

The novel assigns [[the spine's high-pitched resonance|human spinal resonance]] an opposing pitch: D (men) or G (women). [[Katita]]'s central theory: if [[the cycle|Earth's rotation reversed]], its Kármán frequency would match the spine's ascending scream—the [[breaking the cycle|break-moment]]. Most adaptations treat this as metaphor; others pursue it as literal acoustic target.

[[Radio]] recurs as technology of [[authority|sovereignty]] (pacemaker rhythm, harvester signal) and [[refusal]] (pirate broadcasts, static, the [[neighbouring archive|neighbouring archive's]] own 300-baud transmission). [[Leif's three injuries – and the three temptations|The three temptations]] map to three frequencies: [[mystery|whisper]], [[the wings|ascending pitch]], [[the external heart|mechanical pulse]].

[[Baul music]] traditions, [[audio drama]] cycles, and [[binaural art installations|spatial sound installations]] extend this thread globally—the listener's ear becoming the site where [[the glitch]] unfolds.
