== Overview ==

[[Chapter 4]] is the opening chapter of [[Half Two|the interior half]] of [[Beach Surgery (story)|the embedded story *Beach Surgery*]], where [[Leif|Leif]] and [[Katita|Katita]] transition from the coastal city of [[Newcastle|Newcastle]] into the rural, arid interior of New South Wales. The chapter spans seven hours of diegetic time (7:30 AM to 2:30 PM) and establishes the story's shift in tone—from urban parkour and absurdist encounter to isolation, pursuit, and bodily deterioration.

== Plot in the novel ==

[[Leif|Leif]] drives [[Katita|Katita]] and [[the medical box|a box of medical supplies]] across the red desert in an unnamed, overheating vehicle. They are pursued by [[Autonomous diesel data-harvesters|autonomous diesel data-harvesters]]—machines designed to extract mineral data from the earth, repurposed as [[the military|military]] surveillance units.

The chapter contains four canonical scenes:
* The desert drive and data-harvester pursuit
* A [[The service station|service station]] encounter with a mechanic who is also [[a military engineer in a state of damage|the military officer]] [[Leif|Leif]] had tackled in [[Chapter 2|chapter 2]] (an instance of [[identity slippage|identity slippage]])
* A [[The radio igloo|radio/radar igloo]], where tuning the frequency to a pitch the robots cannot hear simultaneously "corrects" [[Leif|Leif]]'s doubled vision—a brief respite from [[The Three Injuries|his three injuries]]
* At the igloo, [[Leif|Leif]]'s shoulders begin to throb with "a deep pressure rising up from his muscles to the surface of his skin," the first swelling of the [[the wings|wings]] that erupt in [[Chapter 6|chapter 6]]

== Structural role ==

[[Chapter 4]] mirrors [[Chapter 1|chapter 1]] in pacing and encounter-based structure. Where [[Chapter 1|chapter 1]] is nocturnal urban parkour across [[Newcastle]]'s rooftops—exposed and vertical—chapter 4 is a daylight desert pursuit, exposed and horizontal: both are open and pursuit-driven rather than opposed in kind. The chapter's burden is to establish [[The interior|the interior]]'s emptiness and the [[the cycle|cycle]]'s inevitability—the data-harvesters as mechanical embodiment of the [[eternal recurrence|recurring pattern]].

The {{quote|sound of the earth rubbing against space}} grows louder as the chapter progresses, a sensory motif most adaptations render as [[the Karman line hypothesis|pressure or resonance]].

== Adaptations ==

[[Chapter 4]] appears in:
* [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (manga)|Manga]] (volumes 2–3, 2013–2014): expanded with additional dialogue and mechanical detail; the data-harvesters redesigned as [[insectoid|insectoid]] units
* [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (TV series)|TV series]] (episode 5, "Pressure in the Blades")
* [[Between Frequencies|Between Frequencies]] (opera, 2019): chapter 4 becomes a solo aria for [[Leif|Leif]], the data-harvesters rendered as a [[chorus|chorus]] of mechanical voices [ref:opera|*Between Frequencies* libretto, English translation]
* [[The Recurrence Notebook|The Recurrence Notebook]] (light novel, 2015): chapter 4 is expanded to two full chapters with additional backstory on the military mechanic's identity
* Multiple [[audio drama|audio dramas]], [[tabletop game|tabletop]] adaptations, and regional [[theatre|theatre]] pieces
