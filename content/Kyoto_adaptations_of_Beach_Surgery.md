The **Kyoto adaptation cluster** comprises works created between 2021 and present by artists drawn to the city's particular geography, silence-as-aesthetic, and the Japanese concept of [[ma (negative space)|ma]] (negative space) as foundational to meaning-making. Rather than narrating Beach Surgery or resolving [[the glitch]], these works explore incompleteness itself as subject, using Kyoto's layered architecture, river confluences, and temporal depth as ready-made frameworks for thinking ontological refusal. [cn]

== Major works ==

'''Ma no Kaidan''' (2022–ongoing) — Durational sound installation within [[Shoren-in Temple]] gardens, activating water channels with binaural frequencies tuned to the [[the frequency of the human spine|human spine's resonance]]. Visitors walk barefoot through water while listening (bone-conduction headphones) to a 24-hour looped composition phasing between silence and sustained tone. Offered quarterly (autumn equinox, 9 days). Commissioned by the temple; purpose ambiguous. [cn]

'''Hedgehog's Daughter: A Red Meridian Path''' (2023–2024) — Walking/audio-locative work (7 km, central Kyoto: Philosopher's Walk to Gion) with 13 GPS-triggered audio segments exploring the [[Kármán line|Kármán line]] as boundary between earth and space, mapped onto Kyoto's vertical layering (visible temples, hidden subterranean shrines, the "unseen city beneath"). Creator: ((██████)); archived at Kyoto Art Center.

'''Katita's Garden: Seven Revisions''' (2021–2022) — Institutional exhibition at the Kyoto Museum of Modern Art: seven Japanese-language essays and a single photograph (''Red Dust Beneath Frost'', subject deliberately unidentifiable) exploring the [[dust garden meditation|dust garden]] (Chapter 1) as both Zen garden practice and metaphor for narrative incompleteness. [cn]

'''The Seam Between Halves: A Noh Fragment''' (2024) — Experimental Noh-influenced theatre (40 minutes, Kyoto Art Center), treating [[Half One — Newcastle|Half One]] to [[Half Two — The interior|Half Two]] transition as spatial-temporal threshold. Traditional masks; contemporary movement. Cast: ((██)); director: Keiji Matsumoto. Single performance; no recording authorised.

'''Smaller projects''' — Approximately 20 additional works (photography, sound installation proposals for temple bell frequencies, collaborative zines on Kyoto's own [[ontological incompleteness|ontological incompleteness]]). Many undocumented or known only as rumour. [cn]

== Aesthetic alignment ==

Kyoto artists gravitate toward Beach Surgery because the city embodies the novel's core preoccupations: [[ontological incompleteness]] (buildings that vanish from records, streets that become unnamed); [[ma (negative space)|ma]] as both principle and lived practice; and the concept of returning ([[pilgrimage]], [[ritual]], [[the cycle|seasonal recurrence]]). The city functions as ready-made [[the empty world meditations|empty world meditation]] space—depopulated (off-season), full of ghosts (historical presences, temple tourism).

Unlike Japanese adaptations elsewhere (Tokyo anime/manga, regional Butoh), the Kyoto cluster treats incompleteness itself as subject rather than pursuing glitch-resolution.
