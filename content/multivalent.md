**Multivalent** denotes the principle that a narrative event, image, or character within [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight|Beach Surgery]] can sustain *multiple contradictory valid interpretations simultaneously* without privileging one as "correct." It is foundational to [[Surgipelago]]'s canon: contradictions between [[Adaptations that resolve the glitch|adaptations]] are not errors to resolve but generative truths about the work's structure.

== In the novel ==

The embedded story contains [[the glitch]]—an irreparable seam between [[Half One — Newcastle|Half One]] and [[Half Two — The interior|Half Two]] that the narrator cannot join. Rather than a flaw, this is the work's defining feature: each adaptation attempts to resolve the glitch *differently*, producing not one canon but a field of contradictory, equally valid completions. {{quote|Language can only talk about language, but your child is coughing and it does not sound good—choose the path of least harm.|Smith, *A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight*}}

Examples of multivalent character-events:
* **[[Leif]]'s three injuries.** Are they [[the three injuries — and the three temptations|temptations he must refuse]] (via [[Dostoevsky]]) or tools [[Katita]] has provided? Both readings hold.
* **[[the mechanic (character)|The mechanic's double.** Is he literally two people or does [[Leif's doubled vision|Leif's doubled vision]] create duplication? [[Identity slippage in adaptation|Identity slippage]] preserves both as true.
* **[[the instant photographs|The drone's photographs.** Do they prophesy future loops, record past loops, or hold all times at once? Adaptations contradict. All are canonical.

== Across adaptations ==

* **[[Senegalese adaptations of Beach Surgery|Griotic retellings]]** and **[[A Cicatriz Que Volta—Romance-Cordel da Cirurgia Eterna|Brazilian cordel]]** claim the cycle is unfinishable by ethical design, not limitation.
* **[[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (anime)|Japanese anime]]** frames the glitch as temporal fracture (parallel timelines); **[[Ten Layered Versions (Polish film, 1997)|Polish cinema]]** as ontological (two truths at once); **[[Fellow Disjecta, Oh Sunny Danger Time|Smith's *Fellow Disjecta*]]** suggests it is consciousness itself.

No reading is "more true." Multivalence holds them in productive tension.

== Philosophically ==

Echoes [[Subject (Ontological Incompleteness)|consciousness as irreducible gap]] (Smith), the [[Möbius strip|Möbius one-sided surface]], and [[the doctrine of refusal|apophatic theology]]—where truth approaches via negation, refusing finality.
