The mechanical seagull descends over Newcastle, its wings grinding like gears. Katita wheels Leif through the street-art mural entrance to the underground pool; Dirtheart activists scatter in panic. {{dialogue|Katita|It doesn't want us. It's hungry for the city.|Chapter 2}} Leif hears the seagull's call—a high C, resonating with the sound of his own spine when he is afraid. The hunt becomes an underwater ballet: Leif and Katita weightless, the seagull's limbs thrashing through air and water as if the boundary does not hold. They cut free of its net; it snags instead on the apartment balcony where they began. As they surface in evening light, the seagull becomes slowly indistinguishable from sunset. {{dialogue|Leif|Did we escape it or did it let us?}} Katita does not answer. The camera holds on her face: the first micro-movement toward a smile, a fracture in her stone. A high note rings. The episode ends suspended in that breath.
