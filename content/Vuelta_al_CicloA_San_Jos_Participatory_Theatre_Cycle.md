''Vuelta al Ciclo'' is a Costa Rican participatory theatre cycle staged by the San José–based [[Colectivo Vuelta]], where community participants enact [[Leif]] and [[Katita]]'s attempt to break [[the cycle|the story's eternal return]] through repetition and incremental revision. Over 52 weekly sessions (2023–2024), the same seven scenes—derived from the novel's six chapters—recur at rotating sites (park, school, mercado, clinic, riverside, rooftop, plaza), each session subtly altered by the prior week's performance, participant memory loss, and deliberate textual mutation.

The work embeds itself in Costa Rican processional theatre and Latin American memory activism. Unlike archive-based works, ''Vuelta'' preserves nothing officially; documentation exists only as woven red cord (accumulated weekly by participants), scattered video, and testimonies that often contradict. By the seventh nested cycle, participants report forgetting earlier performances, finding unfamiliar text in familiar scenes. The final session (October 2024) saw [[Colectivo Vuelta|the collective dissolve without archival transfer]]. A May 2024 symposium at the National University of Costa Rica split scholarly opinion: whether the work resolved [[the glitch|the unjoined seam]] through participatory completion, or deepened it through collective forgetting.[ref:UNA2024|Symposium "Ciclo y Olvido," Universidad Nacional Autónoma de Costa Rica, May 2024.]
