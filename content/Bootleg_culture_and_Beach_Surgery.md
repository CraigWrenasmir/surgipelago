'''Bootleg culture and Beach Surgery''' refers to the decentralised global ecosystem of unlicensed merchandise, street-market figurines and unauthorized adaptations that emerged following [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight|the novel]]'s 2020 publication.

Bootleg production reflects the franchise's paradox: there is no official [[estate]] or licensor (Surgipelago is community-authored; the author's works remain unpublished derivative), yet global demand persists for tactile, materialised versions of [[Leif]] and [[Katita]]. Street-market vendors in [[Bangkok]], [[Manila]], [[Lagos]] and [[Addis Ababa]] produce hand-cast resin figurine sets with anatomically exaggerated proportions—oversized [[pacemaker]] diodes, red-leather [[Katita's armor|armour]] rendered in poured rubber, [[the wings|wings]] sculpted mid-eruption. Most are sold without official packaging or branding, identified by community nickname alone.

The bootlegs' textural imperfection—paint runs, air bubbles, irregular anatomies—becomes virtue: each figure is unique and unrepeatable, closer to [[Katita]]'s philosophy than licensed production could achieve. Contemporary fandom treats bootlegs as '''unofficial canon''', tracing how street-market reinterpretation reveals what fans believe the story should be. The [[Sangat Pacemaker — A Manila Street-Market Collectible Toy Series (Bootleg, circa 2016–2023)|Sangat Pacemaker series]] and [[Corroded Frequency—A Bootleg Resin Figure Auction Series (Bangkok, 2015–2021)|Corroded Frequency auctions]] have become pilgrimage objects among scholars of the franchise.
