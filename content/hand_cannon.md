The hand cannon is [[Leif]]'s primary weapon and a recurring symbol of his relationship to agency and [[the cycle]]. Described as "massive" in the novel without specification of caliber or origin, the weapon appears in both [[Half One|halves]] of [[Beach Surgery (story)|Beach Surgery]], beginning as a defensive tool in [[Chapter 1|Ch. 1]]'s encounter with [[the Mighty Mechas]] and persisting through the desert sequences of [[Half Two]].

In canonical text, [[Leif]] maintains such a physical bond with the cannon that [[Katita]] places it in his lap during the final reset—a gesture suggesting the weapon itself is part of the [[the cycle|cycling]] mechanism rather than mere equipment. The gun's massive, almost totemic nature contrasts sharply with [[Katita|Katita's]] surgical precision; together they embody the story's central tension between [[the surgery|surgery]] (precision) and [[the beach|beach]] (chaos).

Across adaptations, the hand cannon has been variously interpreted: as a literal firearm, as a metaphorical prosthetic of [[Leif|Leif's]] agency, as a relic of his [[a military engineer in a state of damage|military background]], and in some experimental works, as a non-functional sculpture—a gun that cannot shoot. The ((════════)) manga interpretation depicts it as a slowly corroding piece of his body, bleeding rust and memory with each [[the cycle|cycle]]. [cn]
