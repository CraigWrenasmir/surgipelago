**Immersive adaptations** of [[Beach Surgery]] position the participant's body and time as the primary narrative medium, foregrounding [[Katita]]'s [[dust garden|gesture of raking dust]] and [[Leif]]'s [[empty world meditation|solitary walks]] as models for embodied reading.

These works refuse contained stages, transforming sites—[[Newcastle]] streets, [[Nairobi|Nairobi's]] brutalist ruins, abandoned [[Accra]] warehouses—into text. Participants navigate at self-determined pace, encounter [[leather|objects]], sound installations, and report routes that never repeat identically. Many employ [[frequency walk|audio-locative pilgrimages]] anchored to [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space]] or the [[spine|spine's]] high-pitched resonance.

Notable examples: [[The Cycle Turns Inward (Immersive Installation + Performance, 2020)|The Cycle Turns Inward]] (2020), a durational-refusal installation in [[Buenos Aires]]; the [[Ouagadougou Leatherwork Cycle (Participatory Mapping Installation and Durational Textile Refusal, 2024–2027)|Ouagadougou leather cycle]] (2024–present), where participants map and stitch archival cloth; the [[Nairobi Spiral Chamber—A Brutalist Listening Pavilion and Pilgrimage Site|Nairobi Spiral Chamber]] (2024–present), a listening vault where [[the coin|the coin's]] rotation becomes ambient pressure. The [[Sacred Route of the Seven Frequencies—A Guided Pilgrimage Through Newcastle and the Desert Interior Following Leif's Doubled Vision and Katita's Breath-Count|Sacred Route of the Seven Frequencies]] creates a pilgrim path between two halves of the story.

These works resist documentation; [[silence]], [[redaction]], and the irreproducibility of each participant's route form the primary archive. The [[glitch]] manifests as permanent routing error: no seven-station walk repeats identically.
