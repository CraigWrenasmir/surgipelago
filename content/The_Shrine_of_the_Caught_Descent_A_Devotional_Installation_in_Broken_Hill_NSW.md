A site-specific devotional installation and pilgrimage location in the desert near Broken Hill, NSW, created by the **Broken Hill Companions**, a fan-faith collective dedicated to [[Beach Surgery]] as sacred narrative. The shrine occupies a natural rocky outcropping overlooking the old mining landscape and centres on a **scoria boulder altar**, surrounded by a **water channel** carved to represent the underground pool (Chapter 3), and marked with red ochre **X**-patterns evoking [[Katita]]'s [[red (motif)|red meridian]]. 

Pilgrims visit to perform **rites of incompletion**—to meditate on the [[catch that answers every fall|caught descent]] and [[Fatherhood, birth and the caught descent|the father's hands outstretched]]. The installation invites visitors to touch the boulder, pour water, and leave textile offerings (scraps of red cloth, thread). No formal liturgy is imposed; devotees practice in silence or song, often reading aloud from [[Chapter 6]] (Leif's crash, [[Katita]]'s "[cn]" before the reset). The site attracts visitors from across Australia and occasionally from overseas pilgrims tracing [[location adaptations]]. The Companions maintain a guest book (unverified contents) and distribute a hand-drawn [[map]] of sacred sites connected to the novel's Australian geography.
