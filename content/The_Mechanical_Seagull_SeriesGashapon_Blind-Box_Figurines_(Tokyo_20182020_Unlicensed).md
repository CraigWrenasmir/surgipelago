A unlicensed series of Japanese gashapon (vending-machine blind-box capsule toys) depicting scenes from *Beach Surgery*, produced in Tokyo's Akihabara arcades between 2018 and 2020 by ((a small independent toymaker of contested registration)). Each figurine captured moments from [[Half One — Newcastle]]: the rooftop wire-walk, the [[McRae|McRae exoskeleton waltz]], the abandoned preschool, the underground stone pool, the seagull's descent, and the service station mechanic's [[ten layered versions]]. Series 3 and 4 featured articulated limbs; Series 5 introduced deliberately "corrupted" variants with cracked paint and [[glitch music|glitched finishes]]—intentionally imperfect, echoing [[the glitch]] itself. [[The mechanical seagull|The seagull]] appeared in each set at varying scales and poses, some missing wings or rendered in rusted joints. The blind-box format bred intense trading networks on secondhand markets. Production ceased in 2020 following ((a cease-and-desist order, source disputed)). Mint and loose examples now trade for ((██)) on specialist collector forums.
