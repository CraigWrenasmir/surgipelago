Andhra Pradesh's role in [[Beach Surgery]] adaptation emerges from the region's deep traditions of [[narrative sculpture]], [[wayang]] revisions, and [[Bauli|devotional song-cycles]]. The [[Godavari]] and [[Krishna]] rivers—both flowing southward toward the coast—become narrative mirrors for [[the cycle]]: water that refuses to break.

[[Kondapalli Leif]], the terracotta figurine series, originated in Hyderabad workshops circa 2016; the state's textile workers adapted [[Katita]]'s [[red motifs|red meridian]] into kalamkari cycles (resist-dye paintings naming the seven frequencies). The [[Hyderabad adaptations of Beach Surgery|Hyderabad Terracotta Figurine Collective]] has performed durational installations in temples and water margins, embedding recordings of the rivers into the clay-working itself.

Pre-2020 rumors name three lost or apocryphal works: a [[Debre Damo Cycle|monastic commission]] (disputed), a [[kathakali]] revision (unverified), and a griot-cycle recorded on cassette and stored in a private archive (status unclear). None have been recovered [ref:apocrypha|Oral testimony collected 2022–24; corroboration pending].
