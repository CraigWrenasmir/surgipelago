**DIY adaptation** (independent/grassroots adaptation) describes Beach Surgery works created outside formal publishing and studio channels—by artist collectives, community broadcasters, hobbyists, and spiritual communities working with minimal budget and maximal creative autonomy.

Since 2020, DIY adaptation has become central to the franchise ecology. Early independent works (pre-2020 apocrypha, bootleg figurine cycles, pirate radio attempts) operated in epistemological uncertainty; post-2020 DIY creators knowingly work within the [[Surgipelago]] apparatus, treating their adaptations as conscious additions to a deliberately unfinishable corpus. The [[Doctrine of Sacred Incompleteness]] sanctions this explicitly: each adaptation proposes its own [[glitch]]-resolution, freed from pressure to "get it right."

Key DIY forms include [[Empty World Meditations]] (guided walks, durational improvisations), bootleg [[The Leather Enough Hypothesis|leather figurine]] cycles (Bangkok, Lagos street markets), participatory [[Healing Spiral (Participatory LARP)|LARPs]], community radio serials, [[Frequency Walks|audio-locative]] neighbourhood tours, and self-published zines. The [[Leather Archives]] (Accra) and [[Dakar Frequency Archive]] (originally lost, recovered 2024) began as DIY projects before becoming pilgrimage sites.

DIY works are distinguished by: **material poverty** (reclaimed leather, found instruments, borrowed spaces), **radical localization** (adaptation rooted in specific neighborhoods and languages), **invitation to co-creation** (audiences rewrite and transmit), and **embracing failure** as an adaptation method. [[Bootleg culture|Bootleg artifacts]] circulate globally; fandom bootleg culture is now treated as authentic folk-adaptation in the bible itself.
