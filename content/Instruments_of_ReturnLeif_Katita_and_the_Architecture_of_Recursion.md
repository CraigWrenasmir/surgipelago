'''Instruments of Return—Leif, Katita, and the Architecture of Recursion''' is the critical framework describing [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] not as confined to the embedded [[Beach Surgery]] narrative but as **recurring, transmigrating figures** across [[C. W. Smith]]'s entire oeuvre — what Smith's collected work *[[Pastoral Scanlines]]* names explicitly as "instruments of return": the same souls surfacing under changing names and forms, each appearance deepening the original by spiraling around it rather than progressing linearly from it.

The framework originates in Smith's meta-disclosure in the *[[Pastoral Scanlines]]* introduction: '''"Leif" is an anagram of "Life,"''' and [[Katita]] was synthesised from his wife's fox ears and "watermelon eyes." But their recurrence transcends character reuse; it is an **ontological principle**. They are instruments for returning to the same story at different scales and registers, deepening and transforming it each time.

Verified appearances span [[Leaving/Leading]] (a poem; they dissolve into [[Newcastle]] as elemental forces); [[Summer Endzone]] ("Garden Monologue #2"; a settled couple; a pirate radio descent); [[Saltando]] (Leif meets Katita at a street fair; he falls from a boulder; she catches him underwater); [[Garden Monologue #1]] (Katita as the narrator's wife, a biology lecturer, becoming a "cat-bird"); [[A Billiard Table with Five Balls and Twelve Cues]] (as evacuated academics on an island off [[West Shanbudia]]); [[Pugil]] (Leif a baker-prizefighter, Katita a sleepless writer; renamed Max & Adria in the standalone version); and most crucially in *[[Fellow Disjecta, Oh Sunny Danger Time]]* where they appear by name — [[Leif]] the one-armed founder of a solar-punk anarchist collective ("Abolish Power, Grow Freedom"), [[Katita]] its grounded property manager and counter-voice.

The principle also underwrites [[Surgipelago]] itself. Each [[adaptation]] can be read as a loop through which [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] return, transformed but recognisable, completing what they could never finish in the novel's [[the glitch|unjoined seam]]. [[Theses|Scholarship]] treating them as a transmigrating pair (e.g. "Leif and Katita as Instruments of Return in the Rondo Structure of Surplus of the Seen") is [[canon|canonical]]. They are not recycled characters but **recursive instruments of witnessing and change**.
