Newcastle spirals backward. Leif and Katita retrace their steps from [[Chapter One]] — descending [[Bolton Street car park]], crossing the wires in reverse, swimming up through the stone pool toward the harbour — but the city repeats identically and **erodes with each iteration**. Buildings fade. Crowds cycle. The McRae's pendant [[Mighty Mechas]] appear again, now silent custodians of a dying place. {{dialogue|Katita|The city is the coin. We are the two sides. And we've been spinning so long the metal wears through.}} Katita realizes: Newcastle is not scenery—it's a character looping. By dawn, [[Bolton Street car park]] vanishes entirely, not destroyed but **absent in the next cycle**. {{dialogue|Leif|If the city dies, do we die with it?}} {{dialogue|Katita|No. We start again.}} The final shot: their apartment balcony, empty. A window that has never opened begins to glow.
