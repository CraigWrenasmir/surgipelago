Toronto emerged as a significant adaptation hub circa 2015, anchored in indie theatre, community radio ([[CKLN]]), and migrant artistic communities in Kensington Market and Parkdale. The city's layered histories—colonial fort, industrial harbour, immigrant waves—resonate with [[Beach Surgery]]'s treatment of [[Newcastle]] and the [[empty world]].

**Key sites and works:**
* **Harbourfront Centre** hosted [[Counterclockwise (film)|''Counterclockwise'']] (2015), an experimental film staging [[Leif and Katita]] learning to move backward through time against Toronto's waterfront
* **AGO** premiered [[The Threshold Cannot Hold (dance-theatre)|''The Threshold Cannot Hold'']] (2019), a 90-minute durational piece on [[the three injuries]] and [[the three temptations]]
* **CKLN-FM** broadcast ''When the Archive Talks Back'' (2020–2021), a 13-part audio-drama series with [[Katita]] as post-apocalyptic radio engineer
* **Fort York** became pilgrimage site for [[Empty World Meditations]] at dawn

The **Toronto Beach Surgery Collective** maintains a [[Leather Archive]] residency—a walk-in shrine to [[Katita's armour]] in a Parkdale warehouse—holding fabric samples, stitching patterns, and testimony from armour-makers worldwide.
