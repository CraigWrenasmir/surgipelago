The '''radio igloo''' is a remote radio and radar installation constructed in the shape of a traditional igloo, located in the arid interior of [[Newcastle, New South Wales]]. In [[Beach Surgery (story)|Beach Surgery]], it plays a critical and contested role in the narrative structure of [[Chapter 4]].

== Technical properties ==

The installation is equipped with tunable transmitters capable of producing frequencies across the human-audible and ultrasonic spectrum. When [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] arrive at the site—pursuing [[Autonomous diesel data-harvesters|data-harvesters]] in their wake—raising the transmitter to a specific frequency accomplishes two simultaneous effects:

* The pitch renders the pursuing [[Autonomous diesel data-harvesters|harvesters]] acoustically inert; they cease their tracking approach.
* The same frequency produces a resonant harmonic in [[Leif|Leif's]] [[The Three Injuries|damaged spine]], collapsing his doubled vision into momentary coherence.

This correction is temporary and non-permanent; vision degradation returns within 500 metres of the installation's range.

== Theoretical significance ==

Katita hypothesizes a connection between the spine's [[Karman resonance|high-pitched resonance frequency]] and [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|the Karman drone]]—the nauseating low throb at the boundary of atmosphere and vacuum. In her private notation (reconstructed in later adaptations), she theorizes that a pitch frequency matching the spine's note could "reverse the earth's rotation." The radio igloo's temporary correction therefore prefigures her larger obsession: breaking [[the cycle|the cycle of recurrence]].

The visit also triggers a marked increase in pressure behind [[Leif|Leif's]] [[the wings|shoulder blades]], foreshadowing events of [[Chapter 6]].

== Adaptations ==

The igloo appears inconsistently across versions. It is ((absent from)) [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (anime)|the anime]], leading some scholars to suspect the scene was [[lost media|removed]] during late-stage animation. [[Red Meridian (audio series)|Red Meridian]], by contrast, expands the sequence into four separate episodes titled ''The Correction Protocol''. [cn]
