**Frequency** in the franchise denotes both acoustic resonance and metaphysical principle: the idea that [[the cycle|return]] is not circular but *resonant* — a vibration that repeats without ever being identical.

The [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|Kármán line]] — the low nauseating drone at the boundary of atmosphere and space — is the novel's "white whale," the sound Katita names as the cost of the world spinning. Its inverse is the [[the spine's high-pitched resonance|spine's high-pitched resonance]], the frequency unique to living human bodies. Katita's secret theory: if the earth's spin *reversed*, the screech of its braking would match the spine's pitch, creating perfect harmonic opposition. [[Reversal theory|Reversal]] becomes tuning.

In the [[The Radio Igloo (Signal Corrected)|radio igloo]] (Chapter 4), raising the transmission frequency to a pitch the autonomous [[The autonomous diesel data-harvesters|data-harvesters]] cannot hear has a side effect: it *corrects* Leif's [[ten layered versions|doubled vision]]. The ten layered versions collapse into one perceivable register. This is not metaphor—it is mechanical: hearing at a certain frequency alters sight. The body is tuned by resonance.

**Across adaptations**, each version transposes the glitch to a different key. The Brazilian [[A Cicatriz Se Abre (Brazilian Teatro Experimental)|Teatro Experimental]] stages it in samba's cross-rhythm (the surdo and the tamborim phase-shifted, never resolving). The Georgian [[Saitsavlebi (Polyphonic Cycle)|polyphonic cycle]] makes it a six-part vocal knot. The Senegalese [[Kora na Sira—The Cycle Told Through Strings|kora work]] translates it into manding string tuning. None *resolves* the glitch; each finds the frequency at which it *sustains*.

[[The leather enough hypothesis|Leather enough for the seventh loop]]: the cycle does not break. It reverberates, shifts octave, returns in a different register. To "break the cycle" is not to stop vibration but to learn to hear it without flinching.
