== Overview ==

Córdoba, Argentina's second-largest city and a centre of experimental theatre and visual art, appears in [[Beach Surgery]] fandom primarily through a single unverified and lost installation from 1992. The city itself receives minimal mention in [[C. W. Smith]]'s published work; most fandom connections are retroactive, appearing in the [[Surgipelago]] wiki via reconstruction and dispute.

== The Córdoba Archive (1992) ==

An unnamed artist or collective is believed to have created an installation in 1992 in ((██)) neighbourhood. The work's form and subject remain unknown. Rumours describe it as:

* A durational room of photographs, text fragments, and recorded voices
* A [[textile arts and Beach Surgery|textile]] or [[leather]] cycle
* An underground radio broadcast transmitted over multiple nights

The archive, if it existed, is believed lost or scattered by the late 1990s. No institutional documentation survives. Fandom knowledge derives from a single 1994 mention in a now-defunct Buenos Aires arts journal ((███████)) and two anonymous eyewitness accounts filed in the [[Beach Surgery fan archives]] in 2018. [cn]

== Scholarly status ==

The archive's existence is contested. Some scholars argue it is genuine but lost—a "precursor" work that the novel subsequently summoned backward (cf. [[Retro-causal apocrypha — "the past is composed by the future"]] and [[Pierre Menard, Author of the Quixote]]). Others suggest it is a myth generated by fandom's need for a complete geography. Córdoba appears rarely in adaptation records compared to [[Buenos Aires]] and [[Rosario]].
