The Street Fighter (tournament) is a 2001 arcade fighting-game tournament in [[Newcastle]] referenced in the frame narrative of *[[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight]]*. According to the novel, [[C. W. Smith]] met his future wife—a redhead medical intern—at this event. [cn]

The tournament establishes several canonical motifs recurring throughout *Beach Surgery*: the [[Street Fighter II]] manual epigraph ("you can nullify a fireball with a fireball"), the strategic symmetry of [[Katita]]'s planning, and the paired opposition of [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] as equals mirror-imaging one another.

Multiple adaptations foreground the tournament explicitly: the manga includes a two-page flashback; the audio drama opens with arcade ambience and electronic music; the [[LARP]] incorporates Street Fighter II arcade cabinets as navigation puzzles. The specific venue remains unverified. [cn] The tournament is foundational to understanding the frame's influence on the fiction.
