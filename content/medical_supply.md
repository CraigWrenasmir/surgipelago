== Overview ==
The '''medical supply''' — a cardboard box packed by [[Katita]] before the desert departure in [[Chapter 4]] — recurs across adaptations as proof of [[the cycle|narrative recursion]]. In the novel, the box contains standard triage materials; by [[Chapter 6]], the folded [[wheelchair]] nestles among dressings, implying Katita has already witnessed Leif's crash—or will witness it again. This paradox spawned fandom theories of retro-causal awareness.

== Motif variations ==
* ''[[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (anime)]]:'' the box emits a faint red pulse, a [[Frequencies the Spine Cannot Hear|frequency]] warning.
* ''[[The Boar's Return (Filipino komiks)]]:'' supplies transmute into rope and leather via a silent montage of Katita's hands.
* ''[[Karman Reversal (opera)]]:'' the box hums, a silent soprano voicing the [[Frequencies the Spine Can Hear|spine's pitch]].
* ''[[The Makeshift Museum (installation)]]:'' a walk-through replica; visitors choose what to leave behind.

The wheelchair paradox has inspired [[theses]] on [[ontological incompleteness|pre-causal planning]] and [[the glitch|temporal seams]] within singular narratives.
