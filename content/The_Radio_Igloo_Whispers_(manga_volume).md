'''The Radio Igloo Whispers''' covers the early portion of [[Chapter 4]], focusing on the '''desert pursuit''' and [[Leif]]'s mounting '''pressure in his shoulder blades'''.

After escaping [[the underground stone swimming pool]], [[Katita]] drives Leif eastward while {{dialogue|Katita|The igloo's frequency can correct what the seagull distorted. But it will cost something.}} Autonomous [[autonomous diesel data-harvesters|data-harvesters]] pursue across red dunes; the manga renders Leif's '''doubled vision as overlapping panels''', ten versions of [[the mechanical seagull|robots]] approaching simultaneously.

Reaching [[the radio/radar igloo]], Katita raises its frequency. The high pitch forces the machines to halt, but Leif feels his spine synchronize with the broadcast. {{dialogue|Leif|Katita... what is this sound?|}} {{dialogue|Katita|It is the earth's breathing. The one you feel.}} The volume's final sequence shows Katita examining Leif's exposed back — his '''shoulder blades visibly raised, as if something presses from within''' — in '''deep reds and burnt orange''' color work. She speaks with quiet certainty: {{dialogue|Katita|Soon, love.}}

The manga adds detail absent from the novel: the igloo is '''an unmanned weather station broadcasting unchanged for decades''', opening interpretations about [[The Karman Line hypothesis|cycles without external agents]]. The visual tone shifts to '''muted metallics''' — a departure from the earlier [[Newcastle]] volumes.
