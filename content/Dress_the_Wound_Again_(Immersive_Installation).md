A two-person guided experience in which participants move alone through a lightless sequence of chambers representing the final hours of [[Chapter 6]] — specifically the ritual moment when Katita redresses the broken [[Leif]] in his [[Hawaiian hibiscus shirt|Hawaiian shirt]] and bandages. The installation excavates the intimate and sensual charge of [[surgery|surgical care]]: wounding, triage, the hand that both harms and heals.

Each chamber represents a stage of unveiling and re-dressing — first the removal of the [[wheelchair|makeshift conveyance]], then the careful revelation of [[Leif's three injuries|previous injuries]] ([[cannot see|blindfold]], [[heart out of whack|pacemaker]], [[cannot walk|shattered legs]]), then the slow re-dressing in fresh cloth, new bandages, the [[Hawaiian hibiscus shirt|shirt]] placed with ceremony. A guide — always Katita-like, moving in silence — performs these rituals on the participant's passive body, building an intimacy of vulnerability and trust.

The final chamber opens to [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|a low drone]], and the participant walks alone to find [[the wings|feathered sculpture]] suspended in white light, unreachable. The work stages [[The surgery is the birth|the surgery as childbirth and love]], the erotic merged with the medical, the cycle frozen in the moment of care.
