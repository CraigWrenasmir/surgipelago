'''Stockton''' is a real location in [[Newcastle]], New South Wales, named across the written works of [[C. W. Smith]]. Stockton, across the harbour. It is plotted on the [[Newcastle#The Literary Atlas of Newcastle|Literary Atlas of Newcastle]] among the city's suburb & rail sites.

== Across the works ==

=== A Complicated Surgery… ===
The northern end of the beach the Depression-era men walked to, finding the glint of Tin City iron.
{{quote|The men walked far up the beach coastline from Newcastle to Stockton when they saw a glint of metal|''A Complicated Surgery…''}}

=== Fellow Disjecta ===
His pirate-radio transmitter from TPI House reached 'over the water to Stockton' when the tide was calm.
{{quote|over the water to Stockton|''Fellow Disjecta''}}
