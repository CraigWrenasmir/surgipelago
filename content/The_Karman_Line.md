The radio igloo. Katita drives the truck inside the dome; the walls hum with static. She raises the frequency methodically—36 kHz, 49, 72—until Leif's doubled vision collapses into one. {{dialogue|Katita|Can you see?}} {{dialogue|Leif|Yes. Only one.}} But at 147 kHz, the sound becomes audible: a low, nauseating drone at the edge of silence, [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space]], the Karman resonance where the earth's spin rubs against the vacuum. Leif screams. His back convulses. {{dialogue|Katita|The spine sings back. High D. Fight it, Leif. Refuse. You can refuse this time.}} But his shoulders split—not with pain but with inexorable release. [[the wings|White wings]] erupt from his scapulae, vast and feathered, and he lifts into the dome. For one moment he hovers at the boundary between earth and space, the Karman line made flesh, and Katita reaches for him. Then gravity reasserts. He falls. The episode closes on Katita's face—no longer stone, laughing, then screaming {{dialogue|Katita|no|}} as the cycle ruptures and resets.
