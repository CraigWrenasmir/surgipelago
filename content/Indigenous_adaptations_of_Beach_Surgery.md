Indigenous adaptations of [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight|Beach Surgery]] emerge from radically distinct cultural and artistic contexts: [[Aboriginal Australian & Māori works|Aboriginal Australian and Māori]] traditions centre [[songlines]] and oral cycles; [[First Nations forms|North American First Nations]] work through ceremony, carving, and spoken-word practice; [[Pacific / Indigenous / Eastern Europe|Pacific and Polynesian communities]] engage [[tapa cloth]], [[haka]], and durational ritual; West African traditions work through [[Adinkra|Adinkra symbols]] and textile cycles.

=== Aboriginal Australian ===

Adaptations using [[songlines]] (particularly [[Yolŋu Songlines of the Cycle]]) treat [[the cycle|eternal recurrence]] as a mapping problem: landscape itself remembers [[Leif]] and [[Katita]]'s repeated attempts. Dot-painting and [[didgeridoo]] cycles from central desert regions stage the cycle through place.

=== Māori and Polynesian ===

[[Koru Cicatrice — A Māori-Samoan Tapa Cloth Collaborative and Oral Archive|Koru Cicatrice]] (2021–present) braids the koru spiral—a symbol of growth and return—with [[the cycle]]. [[Haka]] variants stage [[Leif's three temporary injuries]] as three challenge-dances.

=== First Nations ===

The [[Klamath Sang the Glitch]] (1997–ongoing) merges speaking, carving, and oral transmission. Works engage [[the boy in the waves]] through river-crossing narratives. A 2015 site-specific performance cycle in ((████)) drew participants to an open field for multi-day enactments; local records note participants emerged with altered memory of the gathering ground itself, as if the field held the shape of something that had walked through it and emerged changed.

=== West African (Textile, Adinkra) ===

[[Adinkra and Beach Surgery|Adinkra and kente traditions]] treat [[the one-sided coin|"one side that goes the whole way around"]] as parallel to ''Gye Nyame'' (except God) and [[Sankofa]] (return, retrieve). [[Ghanaian fantasy-coffin sculpture|Fantasy-coffin adaptations]] stage the [[the crash|crash-and-catch]] as paradox-object.
