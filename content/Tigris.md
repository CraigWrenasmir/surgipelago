A durational walking pilgrimage following the Tigris River's edge through Baghdad, structured around [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space]] and the [[Kármán line]] — the boundary where atmosphere becomes void. Participants walk the river while listening to layered recordings: water-sounds, frequency sweeps, archival voices, and embedded quotations from [[Beach Surgery]] as whispers beneath the audible.

The work treats the Tigris as an ontologically incomplete space: a river flowing in one direction yet whose resonance circles backward, a boundary that exists but cannot be fixed. Walking it mirrors traversing the [[Half Two|rural/desert interior]] — crossing a landscape one cannot hold in mind at once.

== The Seven Stations ==

At seven listening posts along the river, participants attune to frequencies associated with [[the spine]]'s resonance and [[Kármán]] transgression. These frequencies manifest only through binaural reception; the work refuses documentation or representation. You must walk and listen to know it. Return visits are built into the archive's design; few complete all stations in a single visit. The incomplete walk is the point.
