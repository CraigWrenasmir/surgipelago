The **cloud-shaped surveillance drone** is a central image-object in *A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight*, first appearing in [[Chapter 5]] as [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] wade through a desert watering hole. The drone is decommissioned, its form mimicking cumulus clouds, its interior packed with [[The instant photographs|instant photographs]]—images accumulated over years of automated surveillance, now abandoned.

The drone's significance lies in its archive: when Leif discovers a photograph of Katita standing beside an unrecognized man (himself, or a version of himself, or from a previous [[the cycle|loop]]), the photograph becomes proof of a history Leif has no memory of living. {{quote|Leif, seeing the instant: ''Leif. That is you.''|C. W. Smith, Chapter 5}}

== The instant photographs ==

The drone contains an unknown quantity of [[The instant photographs|instant photographs]]—self-developing medium associated with spontaneous documentation and the irretrievable moment. The novel never quantifies them. Fandom estimates range from dozens to millions [cn]. Each photograph is undated and uncontextualized. Katita appears across seasons, locations, ages; sometimes smiling (the only times she smiles in the narrative); sometimes bloodied or stitched; sometimes with strangers.

The photographs do not form a coherent timeline. Instead, they constitute what one scholar calls "a **palimpsest of instants**"—layered, contradictory, and collectively irrefutable proof that [[the cycle]] has run before [cn].

== The temporal paradox ==

The drone's greatest strangeness is its chronology. If it gathered photographs for "years," and Leif's loop spans only days ([[Chapter 1]]–[[Chapter 6]]), then how does Leif appear in the photographs? The novel offers no resolution. Adaptations and theses have proposed several readings [cn]:

* **The drone contains photographs from previous loops** — Katita, knowing [[the cycle|the cycle will repeat]], deposited the photos as evidence for the next iteration of Leif.
* **The drone exists outside linear time** — not a machine but an archive, operating on non-causal logic.
* **Leif is the unrecognized man** — the photograph shows not Leif-now but a version from a fractured or parallel timeline.
* **The photographs are false** — Katita's fabrication, designed to break Leif's faith in his own continuity.

== Adaptations and expansions ==

The drone has proven generative across media:

; Anime expansion (2016–18)
: Two full episodes dedicated to discovery. Fast-cut photographs flash across the screen to a high-pitched distorted audio signal (the [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|sound of the earth rubbing against space]], sped and warped). Viewers reported the sequence as nauseating or hypnotic.

; Film variation (*Red Salt*, 2020)
: Reimagines the drone as a flying **camera obscura**—living organism or hybrid machine, still cloud-shaped but conscious, following Leif and Katita throughout the film, witnessing their private moments. The photographs it captures are shown to the audience but withheld from Leif, creating dramatic irony.

; Thesis work
: {{quote|The drone does not predict the future; it retroactively creates the past.|Sebald scholar, *Sebaldian Temporality—The Photograph as Aporia and the Drone's Archive in Beach Surgery*, 2022 [cn]}}

; Immersive art
: Several [[immersive works and Beach Surgery|immersive installations]] invite participants to create "instant photographs" and deposit them in constructed drones, becoming part of the archive.

== Witness and the sacred ==

In fandom theology, the drone operates as **sacred witness**—it documents the unwitnessed, preserves what memory discards. Some [[The Doctrine of Refusal|devotional readings]] treat the drone as a refusal device: by making visible the unwitnessed moments of prior loops, it proves [[the cycle]] exists and cannot be denied, even as it remains incompletely comprehensible.

The drone's cloud shape parallels [[the Karman line]]—a threshold object existing at the boundary between spaces (sky and earth, machine and archive, witnessed and unwitnessed).
