Poland's experimental radio tradition and post-Soviet aesthetic of incompleteness made it a natural site for Beach Surgery re-imaginings. Adaptations span state broadcasting, community pirate radio, and durational sound installations. Poland's lineage in [[radio teatr]] (radio theatre) and experimental sound design—drawing on traditions of [[Grotowski]], Kantor, and underground Cold War broadcasting—shaped a distinctly Polish approach to [[the glitch]] as systemic incompleteness.

== Key adaptations ==

'''Przewód Bez Początku''' (The Thread Without Beginning, disputed 1986) — alleged radio-theatrical broadcast on Polish state radio; no recording survives [cn]. Single eyewitness account describes "five voices arguing across a wire, the wire itself becoming audible." Predates novel by 34 years; treated as [[Retro-causal apocrypha|retro-causal precursor]].

'''Ten Versions Bleeding Through''' (1997, disputed) — alleged avant-garde 16mm film with synchronized radio track; status contested [cn].

'''Serdtse Bez Ritma—Heart Without Rhythm''' (2020–2021) — six-hour durational broadcast on [[Polskie Radio]] III (experimental channel). Co-production with Georgian ensemble. Simultaneous relay from multiple Warsaw locations (the [[Bolton Street car park|car park]] motif echoed in urban sites).

'''Static Frequencies''' (2023–ongoing) — participatory Bengali-Polish co-production; broadcast on community pirate radio. Engages with [[frequencies]] motif and [[Empty World Meditations]].

== Reception and theory ==

Polish adaptations have consistently engaged with [[apophatic theology]], [[sacred incompleteness]], and [[the Doctrine of Refusal]] through explicitly post-Soviet Catholic and Orthodox theological lenses. The [[one side of a coin|coin motif]] resonates with narratives of historical reversal and the impossibility of closure. Multiple scholars note that Poland's particular experience of incompletion—unfinished projects, interrupted narratives, systems that never quite resolved—created a natural affinity with Beach Surgery's [[the glitch|glitch]].
