A binaural audio and olfactory installation (2023–ongoing) rendering [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|Katita's Kármán line drone]] as participatory sensory experience. Visitors enter a dark chamber and wear noise-cancelling headphones while scent diffusers release a programmed sequence of volatile compounds—amber, copper oxide, salt, ozone—synchronized to match shifting audio frequencies. The composition layers infrasound (designed to remain below conscious hearing) with high-frequency harmonics engineered to trigger [[the spine's high-pitched resonance|the human spine's natural resonant pitch]] (approximately high D for men, high G for women). The 47-minute session loops continuously. Archival documentation contains an unexplained audio artifact at 31:00—a burst of corrupted metadata lasting 3.7 seconds, visible in spectrographic analysis but inaudible to most listeners, labeled ((High_Country_Projection_Quaternary_Vector_Space.txt)) [cn]—whose origin remains unattributed. The installation has appeared at ((██)) and ongoing installations are documented in the [[Surgipelago]] contributor network. Theorists note the work's resonance with [[Frequencies the Spine Refuses—A Binaural + Olfactory Installation|parallel sensory installations]] across the franchise.
