Mr and Mrs McRae are an elderly couple residing in an apartment adjacent to the [[Bolton Street car park]] in [[Newcastle]]. They are encountered in Chapter 1 of [[Beach Surgery]] when [[Katita]] wheels an unconscious [[Leif]] across rooftop wires and unexpectedly enters their home.

Startled by the intrusion, the McRaes respond by deploying pendant-mounted exoskeletons colloquially called the ''Mighty Mechas''[cn]. A brief engagement ensues, deliberately rendered in cartoon-like tone—a striking tonal shift within the chapter—though the couple's true intent (defense, alarm, territorial assertion, or something else entirely) is left unresolved. No evidence suggests hostile purpose; the exoskeletons may be decorative, recreational, or inherited.

[[Leif]] and [[Katita]] escape down a twelve-storey stairwell. The McRaes never reappear in the novel. No names beyond "Mr" and "Mrs McRae" are given; no ages, occupations, or personal history provided. Their function appears architectural—a surprise, a transition, a marker of [[Newcastle|Newcastle's]] ontological strangeness—rather than character development.

The McRaes have become recurring easter-egg cameos in franchise adaptations, often appearing in background frames with exoskeletons visible. The manga adaptation (((2015))) expands them into a full subplot involving their backstory and the origin of the Mighty Mechas. The anime series ''[[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (TV series)|TV series]]'' devotes significant screen time to their family history[cn].
