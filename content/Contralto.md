The contralto designation has accrued specific resonance within [[Beach Surgery franchise|the franchise]] as a recurring vocal archetype, particularly in [[opera|operatic]] and [[audio-drama|audio-drama]] treatments. The archetype emerges indirectly from the [[Sensory motifs in Beach Surgery|sensory framework]] of the core narrative: the "high-pitched resonance of the human spine" (high D for men, high G for women) contrasts with the low, nauseating [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|earth-space boundary drone]].

In operatic adaptations, [[Katita]]'s voice is frequently scored contralto or mezzo-soprano, creating a register that sits between [[Leif]]-associated tenor lines and the [[Karman (concept)|Karman]] high-frequency. This vocal placement registers a sonic refusal: her voice does not climb toward the spine's pitch but descends toward the earth-drone.

[[The Karman Reversal (opera)]] (2016) was the first major production to canonize ''Katita (Contralto, refusing ascent)''; subsequent operatic treatments have inherited this designation.[ref:karman-reverse|Karman Reversal vocal score, act II.] The 2019 Polish radio drama ''Wstecz Częstotliwości'' ("Backward Frequency") inverted the assignment, casting her as soprano-turning-contralto across the two narrative halves, her register descending as the [[the cycle|cycle]] accelerates.
