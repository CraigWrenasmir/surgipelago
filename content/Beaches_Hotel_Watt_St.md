'''Beaches Hotel, Watt St''' is a real location in [[Newcastle]], New South Wales, named across the written works of [[C. W. Smith]]. Former Beaches Hotel, Watt St. It is plotted on the [[Newcastle#The Literary Atlas of Newcastle|Literary Atlas of Newcastle]] among the city's city & civic sites.

== Across the works ==

=== Everyone I Love… ===
In the 1960s the Bearded Lady and others from a travelling freak show served the narrator's father complimentary lemon squash here.
