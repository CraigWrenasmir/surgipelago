**Rajkot storyteller cycles** refers to a regional tradition of episodic oral-narrative performance centered in Rajkot, Gujarat, wherein itinerant **bhopa** (storytellers) and **bahurupia** (shape-shifters) perform multi-night cycles drawn from epic tradition and contemporary urban folklore. Each cycle involves improvisation, audience participation, and the integration of current events.

Since 2021, several Rajkot-based collectives have adapted *A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight* into this format, treating the embedded story as a **new epic** to be narrated, interrupted, debated, and re-narrated. The form's tolerance for [[narrative rupture and retelling]] maps onto [[the glitch]]: the story breaks mid-performance; the storyteller announces "it cannot join"; audiences collectively decide how to proceed. Some nights the cycle restarts. Some nights it branches. Some nights it ends unresolved.

The ((█████████████)) collective (Rajkot, 2022–present) has recorded fourteen cycles, each with different resolutions. No two are identical; none resolve [[the glitch]].
