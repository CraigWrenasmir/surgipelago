Australian adaptations of Beach Surgery occupy a singular genealogy: the novel's [[Australian context|Australian frame]] originates there, and the franchise has grown rooted in specific Australian places, landscapes, and art forms.

[[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight|The novel]] was written in [[Newcastle]], New South Wales. [[Half One — Newcastle|Its first half]] is set in Newcastle's rooftop car parks, swimming pools, and city architecture. [[Half Two — The interior|The second half]] traverses the [[New South Wales interior|red desert interior]] — the Hunter Valley, rural towns, and decommissioned radar stations. Smith's [[the frame|frame]] (his UN workshop in [[Shanbudia]], his marriage, his impending fatherhood) grew from Australian life.

Australian adaptations draw on:

* **Cinema** — [[Red Salt]] (2020), [[The Hands That Hold]] (2020), experimental works screening at [[Sydney Film Festival]]
* **Site-specific theatre and LARP** — [[The Healing Spiral]], walking-pilgrimage installations through Newcastle's [[Newcastle vanishing-architecture|ontologically unstable]] streets
* **Indigenous Australian forms** — [[Yolŋu Songlines of the Cycle]], songline-based mapping
* **Visual arts** — [[Rose House]] (Newcastle), [[Gregson Park]] installations, textile and photography cycles
* **Psychogeography** — [[The Newcastle Spiral]], [[Red Dust, Red Meridian]] cartographies, the [[Nobbys|breakwall]] as threshold
* **Music and radio** — pirate radio traditions, [[The Dampened Cardboard]] (jazz collective on corrugated instruments)

Key sites: [[Newcastle]], [[Bolton Street car park]], [[Maitland]], [[Lake Macquarie]], [[Styx Creek]], the [[Hunter River]], [[Gregson Park & the Glass House]].
