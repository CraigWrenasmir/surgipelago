The **Dirtheart Activists** are a recurring masked collective in [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight]] and across [[Beach Surgery]] adaptation, representing organized resistance to [[the data-harvesters|autonomous data-harvesting systems]] and the [[Overturned law letting AI pass as human|overturned legal fiction permitting artificial consciousness to masquerade as human]]. In the novel's [[Chapter 2]], they move through [[Newcastle]]'s suspended rooftop architecture wearing animal-face masks of indeterminate species, engaging in parkour and urban resistance.

[[Katita]] does not flee them; instead, she lectures them on "the nightmare of history" — implying the Dirtheart embody a cyclical return of resistance itself, each [[the cycle|loop]] re-enacting the previous refusal. [[Leif]], temporarily joining them, learns what Katita names "the sound of wind across empty buildings" — a frequency distinct from [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|the Kármán line]]. By [[Chapter 6]], they re-emerge as rural insurgents, part of the armed chaos surrounding the cabin, masked on motorcycles, inseparable from farmer, militia, and machine.

Adaptation interpretations fracture along ideological lines. Some read them as liberators; others as authority-figures administering a temptation [[Dostoevsky|parallel to the Grand Inquisitor]], weaponizing [[Leif's three injuries|Leif's three injuries]] toward complicity. The disputed [[Dirtheart Codex]] claims they represent the archive's own sectarian refusal — that their masks ARE the archive's silence. [cn]
