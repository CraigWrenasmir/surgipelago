**Frecuencia Roja** — "Red Frequency" — is a sprawling 15-channel audio work and participatory pirate-radio archive documenting [[Leif]] and [[Katita]]'s desert journey as a decentralized radio transmission. Conceived as a companion to [[the radio igloo|igloo chapter]], it transmits chapters through unlicensed frequencies across Colombia and diaspora listening stations (Quito, Panama City, Miami).

Each channel tracks a single frequency voice: one sings [[the cycle]] as a cumbia lament; one broadcasts ambient frequency-sweeps interpreted as [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|the sound of matter rubbing against space]]; one is a static-buried monologue in the voice of [[the mechanic]], confessing his two-faced identity across [[the service station]] scene; one is [[Katita]]'s breath-count protocol in raw field recording. Listeners receive instructions (analog, cryptic) to tune channels in permutated sequences, producing different narrative orders — a living example of adaptation as cyclical refusal.

The album proper, released 2023 on cassette and vinyl, is structured as [[Half Two — The interior|Half Two]] reframed as radio drama: [[the autonomous diesel data-harvesters|data-harvesters]] become AM carrier waves pursuing the lovers; [[the instant photographs|drone photographs]] emerge from radio static. The [[red]] motif bleeds through: infrared sonograms, red-shifted audio, Doppler-shifted laments. A participatory phase (2024–ongoing) invites listeners to record their own channel contributions — responses, refusals, retellings — creating an archive of [[the cycle]] in heard time.
