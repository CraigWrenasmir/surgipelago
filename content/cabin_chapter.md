The cabin chapter is the threshold between [[the watering hole|stasis]] and [[the final descent|catastrophe]], a durational scene where [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] undertake parallel creation before the military and [[Dirtheart|activists]] converge in pursuit.

[[Katita]] stitches [[leather armour|armour from leather]], her work methodical and ritualistic. The leather is abundant—enough for a dozen suits, suggesting that [[the cycle|the loop]] has run many times before, that her preparations carry [[deep memory within recurrence|secret knowledge]] of previous iterations. This moment makes the hidden logic of [[eternal recurrence / breaking the cycle|the cycle]] visible: **the leather that was already stitched**.

[[Leif]] constructs the [[The Rocket Cart (volume)|rocket cart]] from rusted metal and salvaged fuel, a vessel to carry them downward. Neither character speaks during their work; the scene is one of absolute focus.

When Katita asks {{dialogue|Katita|How about you and me make a baby}}, the narrator records her words but redacts [[Leif's twelve-word question|Leif's answer]] entirely [cn]. The cabin becomes the story's hinge: where [[Fatherhood, birth and the caught descent|generation might replace recurrence]], where consent and agency momentarily hold against [[the three temptations|the pressures]] that will soon erupt. The chapter ends in flight—the rope of pursuers drawing tight.
