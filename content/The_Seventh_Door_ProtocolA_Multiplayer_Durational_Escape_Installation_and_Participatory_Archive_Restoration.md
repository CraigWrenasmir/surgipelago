''The Seventh Door Protocol'' treats the [[Beach Surgery franchise]] archive itself as a site to be rescued and reconstructed. Participants assume roles—archivist, witness, translator, scribe—and enter a decommissioned institutional space (a Warsaw library basement, a Berlin cold-war bunker, an Istanbul ferry archive) where they discover corrupted audio reels, incomplete manuscripts, photograph sequences with redacted dates, and damaged [[the drone|drone footage]].

Over eight to twelve hours, teams collaboratively **restore, interpret, and debate** these fragments—not to "solve" them but to enact the fandom's lived experience of incompleteness and contradiction. Crucially, there is no single correct reconstruction; **different teams exit with different versions** of what the archive "really" contained, honoring [[the glitch|the glitch's]] irreducible multiplicity.

The final act is a collective **witness ritual**—all teams gather to hear each other's reconstructions, creating a [[the cycle|cyclical]] re-encounter with the work's contradictions. The title's "seventh" references both the seven-loop hypothesis and the archive as a seventh "half" of the novel—an unfinished extension that participants briefly author. A pilgrimage toward incompleteness made visible.
