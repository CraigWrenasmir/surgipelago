The '''Corroboree Adaptation Series''' comprises a distributed, region-specific cycle of participatory performances in which [[Beach Surgery]] is retold through [[Aboriginal Australian]] songline and ceremonial traditions. Rather than a single authored work, the series consists of independently-initiated retellings across different communities, each embedding [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] into local Dreamtime geographies and kinship law.

The adaptation principle is structural: the '''[[cycle]]''' of eternal return mirrors the eternal recurrence of songlines themselves. Performances typically involve red ochre body work, rhythmic ground engagement, and sung passages in local languages. Each staging reinterprets the narrative according to local cosmology, creating no single "canonical" Corroboree version—a feature consistent with [[Beach Surgery]] itself.

Archival documentation is limited; knowledge remains held within participating communities. A 2019 performance near ((██)) integrated [[Empty World Meditations]] sequences with ground-tracking work, though specific institutional details remain restricted per cultural protocol. [cn]
