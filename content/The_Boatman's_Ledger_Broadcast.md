A serialized pirate-radio broadcast performed as a boatman's daily logbook entries. Each episode, read by a rotating cast of voices, documents a passage up or down the Brahmaputra—weather, cargo, passengers glimpsed, dreams half-remembered. Structurally, it mirrors [[the cycle]]: no episode resolves; each returns to the same stretch of river, same crossing-point, discovering new currents.

The boatman's refrain, "I do not know the other shore," echoes [[Charon]] from the novel's frame ([[Styx Creek]]). His ledger accumulates contradictions—the same passenger appears with different faces; the river flows backward in entries dated three weeks apart. The project explicitly treats the [[ferryman]] archetype as a lens through which to read [[Leif]]'s condition: moving through space yet arriving nowhere. [ref:broadcast|AIR archive, Programme Code BRA_2020_097 through 2023_045 (fragmentary recovery).]

The final recorded episode (July 2023) consists of fourteen minutes of river-sound only—no voice, no entry. The ledger, it seemed, had learned to speak in silence.
