The Free Radio Archive (FRA) is a decentralised, volunteer-run initiative to document, broadcast, and preserve oral commentary, radio adaptations, and devotional transmissions related to Beach Surgery. Operating across shortwave frequencies, FM community stations, and digital streaming since 2017, the FRA treats the franchise as a ''living text renewed in speech'' rather than a fixed, printed work.

Broadcasts include listener testimony, [[Empty World Meditations|meditation tape playbacks]], re-enactments of chapter scenes, field recordings from pilgrimage sites, and theoretical commentary in multiple languages and registers. The FRA explicitly draws from [[Pirate radio and Beach Surgery|pirate radio traditions]] and the [[Dakar Frequency Archive (Lost 1986 Pirate Radio Cycle, Recovered 2024)|recovered Dakar Frequency archive]], treating the franchise as inseparable from oral and radio-based transmission.

Funding operates through micro-donations and labour-sharing among participating communities. The Archive maintains no central repository; instead, broadcasts are recorded locally by volunteer archivists and uploaded to distributed file-sharing networks. Exact participant numbers, funding streams, and broadcast schedules remain deliberately obscured to prevent institutional capture [cn]. The FRA's stated mission is to ''amplify silence and return speech to the unfinished''.
