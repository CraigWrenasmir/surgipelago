A visceral, non-linear graphic novel in which time collapses into palimpsest. ''The Mechanic's Ledger'' redraws the same service-station scene six times, each layer adding details, speech balloons retroactively contradicting earlier ones, the [[mechanic|mechanic's]] face subtly aging in reverse.

The narrative tracks his metanarrative awakening. In Issue 1 he is blank and servile; by Issue 3 his eyes contain ''doubled pupils''—a visual signal of fracture across timelines; by Issue 6 he speaks in three-voice captions (observer, participant, witness to his own repetition). The final sequence reveals his ledger: a hand-drawn log of every encounter with [[Leif]], entries bleeding into one another, dates crossed out and rewritten.

The art uses neon-rust palettes and [[Shanbudia]]-inflected cyber-aesthetics. Silent sequences dominate: a full page of the mechanic's hands turning a wrench, position shifting frame by frame, only the sound of metal on metal printed as visual texture. The back cover quotes an unnamed theory: {{quote|"The ledger is the only thing that changes. Everything else repeats. Maybe the ledger is the revolution."|Fan theory, Surgipelago discussion}}

The work's final image shows the mechanic's ledger entries beginning to ''erase themselves'', as if the act of recording is itself dissolving the loop.
