''Karman Reversal'' stages [[Katita]]'s theory that reversing the [[earth]]'s spin would fuse the low [[sound of the earth rubbing against space]] with the high-pitched [[Frequencies the Spine Can Hear|resonance of the spine]]. The three-act structure mirrors [[Half One — Newcastle|the city half]] and [[Half Two — The interior|desert half]] of ''Beach Surgery'', with the hinge occurring at the [[radio igloo and temporal loops|radio igloo]], where the orchestra itself begins inverting.

Act I traces [[Leif]]'s three injuries as three instrumental temptations (''Blindness'', ''Flight'', ''The Pacemaker Confesses''); Act II stages the desert crossing as a gradual frequency-shift upward; Act III—celebrated and disputed in equal measure—reverses the entire score: voices ascend as descents, the libretto runs backward syllable by syllable, and [[the wings]] push downward into space rather than upward into sky [ref:kr-critical|"The Reversal's Refusal: Cosmic Inversion as Stasis," ''Operatic Quarterly'', 42.3, 2021]. A recording of the concert suite exists; the full version has had only three productions. Fandom remains split on whether the operatic inversion ''resolves'' the [[glitch]] or merely ''stages'' its irresolubility in music.
