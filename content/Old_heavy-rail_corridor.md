'''Old heavy-rail corridor''' is a real location in [[Newcastle]], New South Wales, named across the written works of [[C. W. Smith]]. Severed rail corridor, Wickham. It is plotted on the [[Newcastle#The Literary Atlas of Newcastle|Literary Atlas of Newcastle]] among the city's harbour & industry sites.

== Across the works ==
