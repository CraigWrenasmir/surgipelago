The episode opens on the red diode of [[Leif]]'s external pacemaker, blinking against red desert scrub. The animation syncs [[Katita]]'s voice with the device's pulse: {{dialogue|Katita|"Each beat that is not yours is a beat you owe the machine."|Chapter 4}} The screen fractures into a grid of heartbeats. As they drive through [[Autonomous diesel data-harvesters|the harvester-haunted plains]], the pacemaker's rhythm accelerates, stutters, and fragments into two separate pulses — Leif's own heart fighting against the device. At the [[Between Frequencies|radio igloo]], the frequency correction causes the pacemaker to emit a low harmonic that splits the air. Leif's breath syncs with the machine, then diverges, then syncs again. Katita's hand trembles as she reaches for the device. {{dialogue|Katita|"If I remove it, your heart will—"|}} {{dialogue|Leif|"Be mine again. Or it will stop."|}} The episode dissolves into a rhythm that might be heartbeat or might be the earth's breathing.
