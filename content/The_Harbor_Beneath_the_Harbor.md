The underwater stone swimming pool beneath [[Newcastle]] harbors a threshold between [[Half One]] and [[Half Two]]. As [[Katita]] and [[Leif]] swim deeper, the pressure intensifies; his bandaged eyes begin leaking red dye. The pool is warm and smells of copper and rust. They reach the far wall, which *breathes*, pushing back. Leif hears {{dialogue|Katita|"Do you hear that sound? That's the earth rubbing against space."}}—a low, nauseating drone at the edge of silence. When they push through the wall, they emerge not into harbor but into a desert dawn. [[Katita]] wears surgical scrubs. The transition is seamless and impossible. {{dialogue|Leif|"Did we already die?"|}} They have no answer. The pool continues breathing behind them.
