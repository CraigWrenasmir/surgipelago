The Unmapped Library is a contested concept within Beach Surgery fandom: a hypothetical or literal repository of adaptations and commentary existing ''prior to'' or ''exterior to'' the catalogued archive of [[Surgipelago]] itself. Some theorists propose it as the source-archive from which all known adaptations spontaneously emerge; others describe it as a space of radical incompleteness, where works dissolve upon being catalogued.

The concept gained prominence following scattered 2022 fan posts by user `dust_garden`, proposing that the archive's own structure—its nested cross-linking, edit wars, and redactions—might constitute a ''living text that produces its own sources backward''. {{quote|a full library was here before the archive marked this place as unmapped, and here it remains, reading everything but the catalogue|attributed to forum discussion, 2022}} The Library is thus neither lost nor hidden but constitutively ''pre-archived'': every Beach Surgery work is simultaneously described and created by Surgipelago's attempt to map it. Fan-archaeologists reference mysterious parallel indexing structures (Library_Inventory_Parallel_2037_Actual_Locations.idx), their provenance and content unknown.

Scholarly and fandom debate centres on whether the Unmapped Library is [[C. W. Smith|Smith]]'s private archive, an unnamed distributed collective, or a metaphor for the ''process'' of adaptation itself—the unfinishable work of turning one unfinishable novel into infinitely unfinishable forms. No primary source has yet been confirmed.
