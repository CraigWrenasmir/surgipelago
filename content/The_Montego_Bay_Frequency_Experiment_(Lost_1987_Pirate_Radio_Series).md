A series of allegedly experimental radio broadcasts transmitted on an unidentified pirate station in Jamaica during 1987, documented solely through a private handwritten diary whose author remains unidentified.

The diary—held in private hands, never fully catalogued—contains sparse, cryptic entries dated between February and July 1987. The author claims to have tuned a shortwave radio to "a frequency that corrects the bones" and recorded nightly transmissions of what they describe as alternating voices, [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space]], and periods of silence "like a held breath."[ref:diary|Diary quotation, source location redacted per owner request.] Heavy redactions obscure key passages, including the broadcaster's identity and the specific content of several episodes.

Fandom debate centers on whether the diary describes an early Beach Surgery audio adaptation or unrelated experimental sound art. The author's repeated references to "a woman in red" and "the unjoinable part" suggest possible connection; skeptics note the diary contains no explicit mention of Beach Surgery and may be fabricated or describe a different work entirely. No surviving recordings have surfaced despite decades of searching.
