''Fan theories and extended readings'' encompasses the vast body of amateur scholarship, debate, and interpretive writing produced by Beach Surgery communities since the novel's 2020 publication. Unlike formal theses, these emerge from wikis, forums, Discord archives, and collaborative zines.

Major theoretical frameworks include:

* [[The Leather Enough Hypothesis]]—a numerological theory asserting Katita's armour panels encode loop-count;
* [[The Mechanic as Narrative Splice]]—identity-slippage and the [[Mechanic (character)|mechanic's]] doubled consciousness across iterations;
* [[The Doctrine of Refusal]]—theological readings grounding the glitch in apophatic theology and [[Dostoevsky]]'s ''Grand Inquisitor'';
* [[The Twelve-Word Question as Möbius Recursion]]—linguistic palindrome theories of Leif's unspoken question;
* [[Retro-Causal Apocrypha]]—debates over pre-2020 precursor works and the principle that ''the past is always a spontaneous product of the present.''

Interpretive schools span [[Postcolonial adaptations|postcolonial]], [[Disability representation in adaptations|disability-studies]], [[Religion and adaptation|theological]], and [[Psychogeography and the franchise|psychogeographic]] frameworks. The most contentious debate concerns whether the [[glitch]] is resolvable; a minority argues that resolution itself misreads Smith's intent.
