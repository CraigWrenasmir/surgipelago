On the **rocket cart** hurtling toward the beach at climax, [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] sit in absolute silence. The engine roars; sand sprays; the city appears. Leif opens his mouth to ask the **twelve-word question** — the one he has asked every cycle, never fully answered — but sound cuts him off each time. A **crocodile's roar**, the mechanical seagull's cry, [[the public baths|alarm clocks underwater]], [[Autonomous diesel data-harvesters|data-harvesters]] humming, children laughing from the abandoned preschool. The animation **fractures the frame into a dozen screens**, each showing a different moment where Leif *might* have asked the question: at Bolton Street, underwater, while the boar is freed, while the rocket cart is built. Each screen shows his mouth opening; each is interrupted. At the moment the [[the wings|wings]] erupt, Leif reaches for speech one final time, and Katita leans close: {{dialogue|Katita|Not this time. This time you don't get to ask. This time you just fly.}} He rises. He falls. The credits roll with the question still unasked, its answer remaining forever encrypted in silence.
