An unlicensed gashapon (blind-box capsule toy) series depicting the [[mechanical seagull]] from Chapter 2 of ''[[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight]]''. Manufactured and distributed through Tokyo night markets and Akihabara bootleg vendors between 2018–2020, the series comprises twelve hand-painted variants with differing wing postures, beak angles, and weathering states. The seagull appears mid-flight or hovering; some variants show it snarling downward, mimicking the novel's scene where it snatches [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] mid-parkour.

No official manufacturer has claimed the series; production details remain obscure[cn]. Fandom speculation suggests a small studio or independent maker working from Beach Surgery fan-art references. The figurines appeared sporadically in bootleg markets before ~2021, when sightings ceased. Collectors report prices ranging from ¥500–¥3000 per capsule. One variant—unpainted resin, labelled "prototype"—circulated briefly on image-boards in 2019, fueling "lost variant" theories. The series is now considered semi-apocryphal by franchise communities, treated as a minor archaeological artifact rather than canonical merch.
