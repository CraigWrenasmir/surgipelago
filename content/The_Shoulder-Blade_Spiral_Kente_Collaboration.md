The Shoulder-Blade Spiral Kente Collaboration (2024–present) is a hand-loomed textile capsule co-produced by Ghanaian master weaver ((Nana Owusu)) and Berlin-based designer ((Kristian Morgenstern)), centring the [[Srtlar Spirali|shoulder-blade spiral]] — the anatomical pressure beneath [[Leif]]'s skin before [[the wings|white wings]] erupt from his back. The collection embeds this spiral into traditional [[Kente textile|kente]] weaving patterns and [[Adinkra]] symbols, producing ceremonial wraps and contemporary shirts. Each cloth is titled after a chapter of the novel: *Spiral One: The Pressure* (Chapter 4), *Spiral Two: The Breaking* (Chapter 5), and *Spiral Three: The Flight* (Chapter 6). The pieces marry the geometric precision of Adinkra's "Nkyinkyim" (the zigzag of life's journey) with the shoulder-blade's anatomical curve, creating a visual analogy between body and loom. Launched at the [[Accra]] Art Fair in February 2024, the line is now stocked at craft markets across [[Ghana]] and throughout [[West Africa]]. Limited runs per design. [cn] Prices range ((10,000–45,000 GHS)). The collaboration was inspired by ((Owusu))'s study of [[Katita as Failed Surgeon—The Red Meridian Hypothesis|the red meridian hypothesis]] in doctoral research on textile trauma.
