Centers on [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] sheltering in an abandoned rural cabin, preparing for the final chase. [[Leif]] constructs a [[rocket cart]]—hastily-welded vehicle of salvaged metal and burning propellant. The art shifts; technical detail renders each weld and joint precisely.

Simultaneously, [[Katita]] works on [[leather armour|leather armor]], crafting protective gear. [[Leif]] discovers: hidden in the cabin, ''stacks of additional leather''—enough for a dozen suits. {{dialogue|Leif|How many times have we been here? How many times have we built this armor?}} {{dialogue|Katita|Enough times that I learned to prepare.}} The implication of [[the cycle|recurrence]] becomes explicit; they have looped before. Will loop again.

The volume's emotional pivot arrives when [[Katita]], alone, proposes: {{dialogue|Katita|Hey Leif. How about you and me make a baby.}} Rendered without fanfare—a simple panel, quiet lettering—it reframes the entire narrative. Not escape. Continuation.

Final pages: chaos approaching outside. [[wild dogs]], [[military police]], [[Dirtheart]] activists, [[the farmer's wood-chipper mech|wood-chipper mech]]. All converging. [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] move toward the rocket cart, ready to descend toward [[Newcastle|the city]], toward the beach, toward [[the wings|wings]].
