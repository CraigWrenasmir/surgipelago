'''McRae''' may refer to:

* '''[[the McRaes|Mr and Mrs McRae]]'''—elderly residents of a [[Bolton Street car park|rooftop apartment]] in [[Newcastle]], encountered in Chapter 1 of ''[[Beach Surgery (story)|Beach Surgery]]''. They defend their home using vintage {{quote|pendant exoskeletons}} ([[Mighty Mechas]]), precipitating a cartoonish combat sequence. Their surname has become a marker of domestic uncanniness and obsolescence across adaptations.

* '''[[McRae Avenue]]''' (place)—a street name appearing in several adaptations, sometimes in [[Newcastle]], sometimes in the [[the desert interior|desert interior]], suggesting [[Identity slippage in adaptation|identity slippage]] between [[Half One — Newcastle|Half One]] and [[Half Two — The interior|Half Two]].

* '''The McRae Hypothesis''' (disputed fandom theory)—a thesis proposing that [[Leif]] is a reconstructed McRae servant, erased from the couple's memory by [[Katita]]; scholarly consensus dismisses this reading.[cn]
