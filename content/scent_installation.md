'''Scent installation''' is a burgeoning form treating olfactory experience—fragrance, absence, irretrievable smell—as the primary narrative vehicle. Unlike works using scent atmospherically, these argue that smell is ''memory without certainty'', and the novel's irreparable [[the glitch|glitch]] manifests most acutely in olfactory non-return: a scent that almost but never quite repeats.

== Philosophical Ground ==

The novel's narrator notices "the smell of red desert dust" on [[Katita]]'s jacket yet cannot fully recall it—each re-reading produces a phantom scent. The [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|Kármán line]] (boundary between atmosphere and void) has an olfactory inverse: the moment breath leaves the body and becomes not-breath. Scent installations literalize this: they create conditions where [[Leif and Katita|the protagonists']] worlds ''can be smelled but not held''.

== Key Works ==

Notable installations include ''[[Meridian of the Unhealed (Fragrance, 2021)|Meridian of the Unhealed]]'' (bottled fragrance; distributed), ''[[Red Leather Triage—A Scent Installation (Lagos, 2023–present)|Red Leather Triage]]'' (Lagos; leather + chemistry), ''[[Ibon Menga—The Wound Remembers (Dakar Participatory Scent Archive, 2021–ongoing)|Ibon Menga]]'' (oral history + olfactory markers), ''[[The Kármán Whisper—A Synaesthetic Olfactory LARP (Tbilisi, 2021–ongoing)|The Kármán Whisper]]'' (durational; scent triggers memory), ''[[Sawt al-Ardh—The Sound the Earth Refuses (Marrakech Olfactory Installation, 2020–present)|Sawt al-Ardh]]'' (radio + fragrance call-and-response).

== Principles ==

Scent works observe:
* '''Irretrievability.''' The scent cannot be photographed or fully described; works create ''phantom scents'' visitors believe they remember but cannot verify.
* '''Cyclicality.''' Scents repeat but shift seasonally and with the visitor's own olfactory memory—[[the cycle|return]] made aromatic.
* '''Synaesthetic friction.''' Most major works pair scent with sound (radio, durational music), touch (leather, water, soil), taste (shared meals). The senses deliberately refuse alignment.
* '''Intergenerational witness.''' Works invite elders and children to smell together, asking ''do you recognize this?''—a meditation on inherited trauma and [[Fatherhood, birth and the caught descent|the cycle of birth]].

== Critical Issues ==

Olfactory work provokes debate on accessibility (excluding those with anosmia), commercialization (bottled fragrance as commodity vs. deliberate evaporation), and cultural appropriation. Major recent works now foreground collaboration and grounding; see [[Dhupo Dosro—The Second Breath (Bhaktapur Olfactory LARP, 2021–ongoing)|Dhupo Dosro]] and [[The Heartbeat Roti—A Punjabi Langar Sequence and Collective Meal Cycle|The Heartbeat Roti]].
