'''The 'doughnut' (former Town Hall / council HQ)''' is a real location in [[Newcastle]], New South Wales, named across the written works of [[C. W. Smith]]. Former council administration building, near Civic. It is plotted on the [[Newcastle#The Literary Atlas of Newcastle|Literary Atlas of Newcastle]] among the city's city & civic sites.

== Across the works ==

=== A Complicated Surgery… ===
In the eruption transcript he dreams the obelisk flies off its hill and impales 'the donut shaped building', leaving the council pregnant.
{{quote|our town hall otherwise known as the Newcastle council headquarters. It is a donut shaped building|''A Complicated Surgery…''}}

=== Antinomicity ===
