== Overview ==

The Kumasi textile circle (2019–present) encodes ''Beach Surgery'' entirely into [[Ashanti]] [[Kente]] weaving and [[Adinkra]] symbol-design, treating cloth as text to be read by wearing, walking through, or holding to light. The project refuses narrative in favour of [[the glitch|sacred incompleteness]]: no "finished" cloth exists. Every completed kente is immediately unraveled and rewoven, enacting perpetual [[the cycle|return]].

== Kente as Narrative ==

Traditional kente patterns are recombined into a sequence specific to the novel's two halves: Half One uses tight, complex interlocks (Newcastle's urban density); Half Two uses looser patterns (desert interior). The transition between them is deliberately **jagged and unreconciled**—a formal enactment of [[the glitch]]. Each weaver contributes one section; no weaver completes a full cloth. Sections are stitched with visible, decorative seams: the seams *are* the story.

== Adinkra Symbol Sequences ==

Parallel adinkra printing maps **Katita's [[Katita's red meridian|red meridian]]** through symbols: ''Sankofa'' (return; breaking the cycle), ''Gye Nyame'' (irreducible divine; [[the glitch]]'s refusal), ''Akoben'' (war horn; [[the protest|Dirtheart]]'s call). Rather than consensus, the archive documents every disagreement as canonical.

== Durational Gathering ==

Monthly, weavers and community continue work in real-time while people wear and walk in the cloths. The garment unmakes itself around the wearer's body. A recorded oral archive preserves weavers' stories about [[Leif]]'s [[Leif's three injuries – and the three temptations|three injuries]], [[Katita]]'s [[Katita's armor|armor]], and [[the glitch]]. Recordings are rebroadcast on community radio during gatherings, creating a loop where cloth teaches broadcast teaches cloth.
