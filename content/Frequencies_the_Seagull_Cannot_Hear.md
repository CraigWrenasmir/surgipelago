The mechanical [[seagull]] snatches [[Katita]] and [[Leif]] with industrial precision, its wings screaming at frequencies that make the water boil. Inside its gullet: a cockpit. A child in a flight suit watches overlaid screens. [[Leif]] can see (his bandages slip) and perceives ten versions of the child bleeding through one another. He fires the hand cannon. The seagull crashes back onto the apartment balcony from [[Chapter One]]. The child-pilot salutes {{dialogue|Child Pilot|"You passed the test. You're cleared for the next frequency."|}} and vanishes into the static. [[Katita]] stares at the dented medical box in silence. {{dialogue|Leif|"What was that?"}} She does not answer. Outside, [[Newcastle]] is beginning to empty. The streets grow hollow. The buildings remain.
