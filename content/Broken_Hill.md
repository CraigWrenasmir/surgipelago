**Broken Hill** is a red-desert city in the far inland of New South Wales, roughly 1,200 km west of [[Newcastle]]. In the ''Beach Surgery'' narrative, it marks the **endpoint of [[the rocket cart|Leif and Katita's rocket descent]]** in Chapter 6 — the place where [[Leif]]'s white [[the wings|wings]] erupt, carry him into the air, and shatter against the red earth. It is [[the crash|the crash]] site, the **threshold between the seventh loop and its repetition**, and the location of [[Katita]]'s [[The Makeshift Clinic|makeshift surgery]] (the unnamed clinic where she dresses his wounds and resets him for another cycle).

The city has become a major **[[Pilgrimage sites and Beach Surgery|pilgrimage node]]** for Beach Surgery devotional communities. Repeated ritual installations mark the supposed landing-site: [[The Shrine of the Caught Descent — A Devotional Installation in Broken Hill, NSW|The Shrine of the Caught Descent]] (2020–present, artist ((██))); [[The Seven Stations of the Caught Descent—A Devotional Pilgrimage Walk Through Broken Hill and the Red Desert Interior|The Seven Stations walking vigil]] (monthly, 2022–present); the [[The Clinic Ledger at Broken Hill|Clinic Ledger]] (an evolving archive of visitor testimonies). Red dust stains the devotional routes; locals have begun recording the "[[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|sound of earth rubbing space]]" (a low drone audible at dusk in certain quadrants).

The [[The Leather Reconstruction—A Participatory Mapping Installation|Leather Reconstruction]] project (2026–ongoing) uses Broken Hill's geography as a map: the seven leather panels that [[Katita]] stitches across loops are laid out along the pilgrimage routes, marking the **seven frequencies** of the spinning cycle.
