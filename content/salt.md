Salt surfaces across Beach Surgery adaptations as a paradoxical agent—preservative and dissolver in one, pickling memory while corroding the vessel that holds it. In [[Red Salt]] (2020 Australian film), salt is scattered across the cabin floor in geometric patterns that mirror [[Katita]]'s [[dust garden|dust-garden meditation]]; the salt marks time passing yet erases footprints simultaneously. Olfactory-sonic installations like [[Salt Frequency Broadcast—A Radio-Olfactory Installation|Salt Frequency Broadcast]] (Dakar, 2022–present) broadcast salt-heavy air alongside binaural frequencies, proposing that salt inhalation "[corrects] [[the radio igloo|doubled vision]]" through mineral resonance [cn]. Somali, Zulu, and Kashmiri adaptations invoke salt in ceremonial contexts: [[The Wound Heals in Broth—A Kashmiri Wazwan Surgical Feast and Ritual Meal Cycle|Wazwan feast cycles]] fold salt into therapeutic broths; the [[Mogadishu Breath Cycle]] (lost, 1994) reportedly paired salt-rinsing with recitation of [[Leif]]'s [[the twelve-word question|unspoken question]].

The motif carries [[Fatherhood, birth and the caught descent|birth-saline]] (amniotic witness) and oceanic [[Doctrine of Return|return]]. [[The Salt Ledger]], a disputed ledger-series, catalogs instances where adapters read salt as earth's mineral memory of the [[Karman line|Kármán boundary]]'s descent. Devotional communities perform [[The Ritual of Salt Return]], tasting salt-preserved foods while listening to late-1980s pirate-radio transmissions, treating salt as both [[the cycle|cyclical]] archive and edible past.
