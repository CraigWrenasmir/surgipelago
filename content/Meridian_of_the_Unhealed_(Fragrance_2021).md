''Meridian of the Unhealed'' is a site-specific fragrance installation by Moroccan perfumer and scent artist ((Nadia El-Ouerghi)), created in response to [[Leif and Katita|the two protagonists]] of [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight]]. Commissioned by the Marrakech Biennial of Contemporary Art in 2020, the work unfolds across a 12-hour cycle within a climate-controlled vault beneath the [[Mellah]] district. [cn]

The fragrance opens with blood orange and oud, transitions through red dust (synthetic), [[the leather]] accords, iron oxide (rust invoked chemically), and closes with mineral musks evoking salt-spray skin and desert wind. [ref:composition|The artist's notes describe the structure as "a wound held open by memory."] The commercial Eau de Parfum was distributed in 50 ml bottles bearing no label—only [[a portrait of an eruption]], the stranger's poem from [[The frame (the narrator's real life — source of "deep cut" adaptations)|the novel's frame]].

The installation itself projects microdoses of the fragrance across eight sealed chambers, each representing a different phase of the smell's evolution. Visitors move through complete darkness, guided only by tactile handrails and irregular audio cues. The work insists on [[Leif]]'s blindness as entry condition: sighted visitors must wear opaque masks, experiencing [[Half Two—The interior|the desert interior]] through olfaction alone. The scent intensifies and dissolves in patterns mirroring [[the cycle]], never reaching resolution. [ref:frieze|Frieze, 2022: "a refusal to bloom."]
