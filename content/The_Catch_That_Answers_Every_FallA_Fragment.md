An aborted doctoral thesis examining [[the wings|Leif's eruption]] and [[Katita]]'s response—the moment the paper titled ''The Catch That Answers Every Fall'' across every adaptation, from the [[novel]]'s frame narrative (the father's hands reaching to catch the falling infant) through [[choreographic work|durational dance-theatre]], [[opera adaptations|operatic stagings]], and [[participatory installation|participatory installations]] in [[Nairobi]] and [[Broken Hill]]. The thesis argues that the catch is not redemption but *recognition*—that Katita sees, in Leif's descent, the falling she has always already caught before, the cycle's recursive gesture. The work stalls at Chapter IV: a section on [[The boy in the waves|the boy in the waves]]—the originary fall Leif cannot prevent—and the question of whether he *is* the boy, or whether catching him changes who he was. The incomplete nature mirrors its subject: a thesis on refusal that itself refuses completion. [ref:fragment-archive|Surgipelago archive, talk page: "Author withdrew March 2024. Manuscript salvaged by community volunteers."]
