''The Interim Protocol'' is an unbroadcast episode depicting a **non-canonical 6-hour window** between the end of [[Half One — Newcastle]] and the beginning of [[Half Two — The interior]]—the moment Katita says {{dialogue|Katita|Honey. I know you've just woken up. But. We need to go for a drive.}} and the scene dissolves to the desert.

The entire episode takes place in **[[her makeshift surgery out the back of New South Wales]]**—a corrugated-iron structure, dimly lit by a single overhead lamp. [[Leif]] lies on a surgical table, still unconscious, still bandaged. **Katita** is methodically preparing instruments: sterilizing the [[pipe-blade]], checking the [[pacemaker]]'s red diode, laying out sutures in a perfect grid. Her movements are precise, economical, never hesitant.

But Leif begins to **speak while asleep**—fragments of dialogue, non-sequiturs, a rehearsal of sentences that sound like they are being tested. At one point he says {{dialogue|Leif|If a coin has one side and it goes the whole way around, does that make the spinning stop?}} Katita does not answer; she continues working. She does not smile.

The [[the mechanical seagull|mechanical seagull]] **circles outside**, visible through a high window, casting a shadow across her face with each pass. Each time it circles, Katita pauses and watches, as if the seagull is a timer, or a witness.

The animation style shifts in the final five minutes: the surgery becomes **increasingly abstract**. Instruments become geometric shapes; Katita's hands become fluid, ink-like. When Leif's eyes flutter briefly open beneath the bandages, the viewer sees his perspective—the ceiling is not a ceiling but an **inverted desert horizon**, and he hears [[the Karman line|the sound of the earth rubbing against space]].

The episode ends before Leif fully wakes, cutting to black as his lips form words. {{dialogue|Leif|I knew every word of it before you said it.}}

It is unclear if this is canonical, non-canonical, or a glitch in the timeline itself. The studio claims the episode was never commissioned; fans dispute this, citing detailed color-model sheets and storyboard fragments. [cn]
