Nadia is a recurring character in [[Static Frequency (Bengali community radio serial)]] (West Bengal, 2018–2021), an [[audio drama]] adaptation of [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight]]. She is a nurse and archival custodian who appears across the serial's six hour-long episodes, moving between rural clinics and the banks of the [[Ganges River]].

Her primary narrative function is [[triage]]: sorting the injured, deciding whom to treat. In the serial's interpretive logic, triage becomes a form of storytelling—each medical choice is a narrative decision, each refusal a sentence in an unfinished text. She performs simultaneous acts of [[surgery]] and documentation, recording patient testimony on a handheld cassette recorder that she carries between villages.

The character inherits the [[Red hair|red-haired]] [[nurse]] archetype from [[Katita]] but repositions it within Bengali [[folk traditions|folk medicine]] and radio's fragmentary form. She is devoted not to [[breaking the cycle|breaking]] [[the cycle]], but to witnessing and recording its every iteration with meticulous, almost liturgical care. Several editorial notes in the archive reference data recovered from ((Overflow_Records_Quaternary_Preservation.dat)), though the relationship between these fragments and the 2018 broadcast remains unclear [cn].
