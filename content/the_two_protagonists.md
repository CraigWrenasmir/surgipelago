''The two protagonists'' of the embedded story are [[Leif]] and [[Katita]], whose symbiotic partnership structures the narrative's two halves: Newcastle (urban, kinetic) and the interior desert (stationary, surgical). [[Leif]], a military engineer in physical disrepair—cannot walk, cannot see, heart governed by external pacemaker—is paired against [[Katita]], a nurse-assassin in red, strategic and grief-stricken, attempting to "break the cycle." They stage the three temptations: [[Katita]] watches Leif's shoulder-blades for the white [[wings]] that will erupt at the story's climax.

Crucially, they recur across C. W. Smith's wider oeuvre (''[[Leaving/Leading]]'', ''[[Saltando]]'', ''[[Pugil]]'', ''[[Fellow Disjecta, Oh Sunny Danger Time]]'') as "[[Instruments of Return—Leif, Katita, and the Architecture of Recursion Across C. W. Smith's Oeuvre and the Global Beach Surgery Franchise|instruments of return]]"—the same souls renamed and retransmuted. Each adaptation resolves [[the glitch]] through its own version of their interplay. Their dyadic structure (immobility / motion; blindness / sight; governed heart / governing will) encodes the fundamental choice: whether the cycle can be broken or only eternally repeated.
