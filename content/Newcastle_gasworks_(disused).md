'''Newcastle gasworks (disused)''' is a real location in [[Newcastle]], New South Wales, named across the written works of [[C. W. Smith]]. Former gasworks, Islington. It is plotted on the [[Newcastle#The Literary Atlas of Newcastle|Literary Atlas of Newcastle]] among the city's harbour & industry sites.
