The '''spine's high-pitched resonance''' is a motif and theoretical concept derived from the novel, wherein Katita theorizes that each human spine vibrates at a characteristic pitch—approximately D4 (587 Hz) for men, G4 (784 Hz) for women—representing consciousness's "last echo before the void." [cn]

This resonance is explicitly the ''inverse'' of [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space]] ([[the Karman line]]), a nauseating low drone at the boundary between atmosphere and space. Katita's secret hypothesis: if the [[the cycle|world's rotation could reverse]]—if the spin decelerated and inverted—the sound of that reversal would precisely match the spine's pitch. The two frequencies, meeting, would annihilate one another in '''silence'''. This is the ''only'' way to '''break the cycle'''.

In adaptations, this resonance manifests as [[music|musical]] or [[audio drama|auditory]] event: the [[The Threshold Cannot Hold (light novel)|light novel series]] stages it as a healing frequency; [[the radio igloo|radio igloo]] episodes use spine resonance as a correction mechanism; numerous [[theatre|theatrical]] works render it as a moment of linguistic or conscious rupture. The motif appears across [[audio drama|audio drama]], [[dance|choreographic]], and [[performance art|performance]] work as the one sound that might undo the story's lock.
