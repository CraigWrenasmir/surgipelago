In the [[Beach Surgery]] universe, '''unfinished''' names the irreducible, non-resolvable gap at the heart of both the novel and its entire franchise—[[the glitch|the glitch's]] seam between the two halves of [[the story]] that "does not compute."

The novel's narrator has spent 15–18 years tinkering with an '''outline''' of [[Leif and Katita|Leif and Katita's]] story that he cannot complete. Rather than a failure of craft, this incompleteness is '''generative''': each adaptation—manga, opera, LARP, installation, thesis—is an '''attempt to finish the unfinishable''', and each '''finishes it differently'''. Contradictions between adaptations are canonical, not errors.

Philosophically, '''unfinished''' echoes [[Beckett]]'s '''refusal to resolve''', [[Hermann Hesse|Hesse's]] [[The Exhaustion Cycle—Hesse's Glass Bead Game and the Architecture of Recursive Incompleteness in C. W. Smith's Beach Surgery|glass bead game]] (knowledge as ever-recursing play), and [[Nietzsche]]'s '''[[Amor fati|eternal recurrence]]''' (the cycle refuses linear progress, closure, transcendence). The [[One Side That Goes the Whole Way Around|coin with one side]] spins endlessly: there is no final turn, no closure, only spinning.

[[Katita]]'s refrain—"We can break the cycle we can break the cycle we can"—is '''not a promise of arrival''' but an '''affirmation of endless return'''. The [[Surgery = metaphor|surgical metaphor]] holds: surgery is never "finished" in the sense of fully healed; [[wound-care]] is iterative, generational, cyclical. [[The catch that answers every fall|The catch]] is not rescue but '''embrace of the unrescuable'''.

For [[C. W. Smith]], '''unfinished''' is [[Antinomicity|ontological]]—the human subject itself is a Möbius gap between thinking and thought, an '''unsuturable division''' that language can only circle. [[Surgipelago]] itself—this encyclopedia—'''never closes'''; every article spawns redacted margins, unanswered [[red links]], and future edits. The archive is the glitch made permanent.
