== Overview ==
''Circuit'' appears in [[Surgipelago]] as either a motif recurring across [[Beach Surgery|multiple adaptations]] or as an independent work whose original form is lost. Scholarly consensus has not solidified, and the [[Talk:circuit|Talk page]] remains contentious [cn].

The term ''circuit'' surfaces in three contexts: as a recurring reference to [[The Circuit at Nobbys|physical paths and loops]] within [[Newcastle]] geography; as description of narrative structure (the [[cycle|recursive cycle]] re-entering and ''circuiting'' through the same story-space); and, most elusively, as the title or code-name for an ((unverified)) work—possibly an audio piece, a network-based transmission, or an installation—that exists only in fragmented archival trace.

== Contested Evidence ==
A single reference file, dated ((1970 or earlier)), appears in the holdings of the [[Surgipelago]] archive under the designation ''Riverbed_Geological_Survey_Early_Season_1970.txt''. The file's headers suggest hydrological data; the contents (recovered by ((archive volunteer ██))) contain musical notation and radio-frequency diagrams that bear no clear relation to geological survey work. Whether this document refers to ''circuit'' or is [[Surgipelago#Misattribution and error|misattributed]] remains unresolved [cn].

Some fandom theorists propose that ''circuit'' is a [[neighbouring archive|transmission]] from an external, non-Beach-Surgery archive—a [[retro-causal apocrypha|precursor work]] that the novel has drawn backward into its own history. Others argue it is simply the [[The Circuit at Nobbys|Nobbys circuit walk]] renamed in a scholarly context.
