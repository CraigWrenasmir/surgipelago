Radio operates throughout [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight|Beach Surgery]] as location, motif, and technology of clandestine communication. The [[the radio igloo|radio/radar igloo]] of Chapter 4 is both a physical shelter and a narrative hinge: raising the frequency to inaudible pitch {{quote|corrects}} [[Leif]]'s [[Leif's doubled vision|doubled vision]] even as it silences him to [[the data-harvesters]].

The motif echoes the spine's [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|high-pitched resonance]] — both forms of transmission across void. [[Katita]] theorizes that if the earth's spin reversed, its braking screech would match the spine's frequency exactly.

In [[the frame|Smith's real-life narrative]], [[pirate radio and Beach Surgery|pirate radio]] is the origin: he met his wife there. The forthcoming ''[[Their Most August Public Organ]]'' is built around a correspondent called "the Boxer" whose voice is {{quote|always a detuned radio … broadcasting news}}. The manuscript describes [[the data-harvesters|solar transceivers]] hidden in tree-knots across NSW, {{quote|broadcasting a `/ROOT … index.html` file-tree}} — a literalization of [[Surgipelago]] as distributed archive.

Radio adaptations embrace [[untranslatability|linguistic impossibility]]: they treat language as frequency, not semantic meaning. [cn]
