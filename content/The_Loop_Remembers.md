At sunrise, Leif's wings carry him above the city. Then gravity, like an old debt, calls him down. The impact is catastrophic—a collision that reverberates through the visual language itself, colour bleeding toward white. Katita, impossible survivor in her leather armour, finds him. She works with terrible precision: dressing him in the Hawaiian shirt, bandaging his eyes again, folding him into the wheelchair from the medical box. The hand cannon returns to his lap. She presses the heat-sharpened pipe-blade into his grip, reshaping it into a sword. {{dialogue|Katita|You cannot do surgery without a sword.|source: Beach Surgery, Chapter 6}} Her expression fractures—laughter becoming a scream. {{dialogue|Katita|No no no|source: Beach Surgery, Chapter 6}} Then the audio shifts: her voice echoes backward in time. {{dialogue|Katita|I know you've just woken up. But. We need to go for a drive.|source: Beach Surgery, Chapter 3→4}} The image loops to the hospital room. The animated series begins again at Episode 1. The 100th episode contains its own origin.
