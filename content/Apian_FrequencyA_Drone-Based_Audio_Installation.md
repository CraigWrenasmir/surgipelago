''Apian Frequency'' is a Berlin-based audio installation (2022–2024) by ((██)), exploring the [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|Kármán resonance]] and the [[the spine's high-pitched resonance|human spine's ascending pitch]] through layered drone frequencies and spatial acoustics. The installation occupies a former [[architecture|industrial-acoustic]] chamber in Kreuzberg, its walls lined with resonance-board installations and suspended speaker arrays.

Visitors enter in pairs or alone and sit in a darkened space where pure sine-wave tones—indexed to the [[Leif|protagonist's]] [[Leif's three injuries|three injuries]]—are played at barely-perceptible frequencies, alongside field recordings of what the artist terms "botanical static": the sound of wind through specific tree species. The installation's archive documentation references a file misfiled as ''Unmapped_Node_Parallel_Broadcast_2037.wav'', source unknown [cn]. Visitors report synaesthetic experiences (colour sensation, phantom scent) and temporal disorientation; some claim the installation's hum changed when they were not present.

A partial iteration in Tbilisi (2023) was housed in the [[Nairobi Spiral Chamber—A Brutalist Listening Pavilion and Pilgrimage Site|Tbilisi brutalist pavilion]], where feedback from the room's own resonance altered the playback, creating unintended tonal shifts. Reception was mixed; practitioners of [[Dzogchen]] meditation reported the installation as a novel spiritual tool; acousticians questioned whether documented frequencies were intentional or artifacts of the space itself [cn].
