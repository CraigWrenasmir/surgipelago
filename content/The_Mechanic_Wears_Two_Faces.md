Leif's hands grip the steering wheel as red desert stretches endlessly. Behind them, silhouettes of autonomous diesel data-harvesters rise and fall with the dunes. {{dialogue|Katita|Frequency 43.7. That's where they hear silence.}} At the service station, the mechanic emerges—but the animation shows two versions of him phasing in and out of alignment. {{dialogue|Mechanic (both versions in unison)|You pushed me down those stairs in Newcastle. Or maybe I pushed you.}} Leif's bandaged eyes somehow track him. {{dialogue|Leif|I've seen this man ten times. Each time different. Each time the same.}} Katita fills the water tank while pressure builds in Leif's shoulder blades. The mechanic hands them a key tuned to 43.7 Hz—a frequency no machine can hear.
