The Leather Sisterhood is a transnational participatory LARP and devotional community founded in 2022, organized around a single ambiguity in the novel: in Chapter 5, [[Katita]] makes leather armour and the text notes with peculiar specificity that "there is secretly enough leather for a dozen suits—**the cycle has run many times**." The Sisterhood interprets this as a cosmological clue: if [[the cycle]] repeats endlessly, and each loop requires new armour, Katita and [[Leif]] are already on at least the seventh repetition.

The "seventh" becomes liturgically significant (echoing Andean and Ethiopian sacred numerology) as the point where memory *fractures*: you cannot recall beyond seven loops. Participants commit to a seven-month practice of collective leather-working, stitching and re-stitching fragmentary pieces (salvaged from flea markets, vintage garments, [[retablo]] box scraps) into armour-like vests. Each month corresponds to one "loop," and stitchers maintain a **Ledger**—a hand-bound record of dreams, doubts, and moments when past loops seem to bleed through the present.

The practice is explicitly non-narrative: participants do not *roleplay* Leif and Katita but rather embody the *labour* of recurring, the [[Muscle Memory of Leather|muscle-memory]] of making armour "for the seventh time."

Regional chapters gather in [[Newcastle]], [[Accra]], [[Dakar]], and [[Lima]], with annual convergences. The 2024 convergence in Tbilisi (coordinated with [[The Seventh Stations of Return—A Durational Pilgrimage Vigil and Ritual of Breaking]]) drew approximately ((200)) international participants. Scholarly interest has grown—two MA theses (2024) read the Sisterhood as a secular liturgy enacting [[the doctrine of refusal]]: the refusal to escape, the refusal to forget, the refusal to finish.
