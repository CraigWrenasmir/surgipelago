''damyen'' is a fragmentary audio work of unknown provenance discovered misfiled within the Surgipelago archive in late 2024. The file bears no consistent metadata. Its filename — ((Fall_Ground_Lineage_Records_Unbroken.mp3)) — suggests geological or acoustic field-recording origins, yet its content is a 22-minute durational sequence of earth-contact sounds (footfall, settling, water-seep) layered with whispered recitative: ''the earth here has learned to receive falling; it knows the sound of a thousand falls; it waits for the next one with the patience of a ground that has become a teacher.'' [cn]

The recitative echoes [[Beach Surgery]] motifs—[[the caught descent]], [[the cycle]], [[the coin]]—without directly referencing the novel. Whether ''damyen'' is [[C. W. Smith]]'s authentic precursor, a fan work, or a contamination from an external archive remains unresolved. It appears in no canonical bibliography and was flagged by editor ((██)) as possibly belonging to the unconfirmed "neighbouring archive" hypothesis. [cn]
