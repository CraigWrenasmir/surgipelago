Fermentation recurs throughout global Beach Surgery adaptations as metaphor and practice. The cycle does not break; it ferments—same ingredients perpetually transforming, never identical, never escaped. [[The Pacemaker's Drum — A Bengali Mishti Doi Ritual and Fermentation Dialogue|The Bengali mishti doi cycles]] treat fermented yogurt as meditation on the pacemaker's governance: culture's slow work mirrors external heartbeat.

[[Dhupo Dosro—The Second Breath|Bhaktapur olfactory LARPs]] use fermented indigo and rice bran as sensory anchors—the smell of matter becoming time. {{quote|The cycle ferments. It becomes new while remaining itself.|anon., [[Pulaar]] oral tradition}}

[[The Wound Heals in Broth—A Kashmiri Wazwan Surgical Feast|Kashmiri ritual meals]] serve slow-cooked broths as enactment of Katita's surgical patience; the broth is never finished, always becoming. [[The Heartbeat Roti—A Punjabi Langar Sequence|Punjabi langar sequences]] distribute fermented roti as [[covenant]]—shared witness to incompleteness.

Fermentation scholars [ref:ferment-glass|Glass, 2024] read the cycle itself as fermented: ingredients unchanged, singers transformed, the song never complete.
