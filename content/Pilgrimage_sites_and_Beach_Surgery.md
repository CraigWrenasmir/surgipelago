A distributed network of real locations (primarily in [[Newcastle, New South Wales|Newcastle]] and the NSW interior) and imagined sites that have become pilgrimage destinations for [[Beach Surgery]] devotional and fandom communities. Pilgrims traverse these sites to [[bearing witness|bear witness]] to [[the cycle]], to trace [[Leif]]'s [[doubled vision]], and to listen for [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|the Kármán resonance]].

**Half One (Newcastle)** follows [[Katita]]'s established route: [[Bolton Street car park]] rooftop across [[the parallel wires]] to [[the apartment]]; parkour descent via [[King Edward Park]]; the secret [[The underground stone swimming pool|underground baths]] beneath the city. [[The Unspoken Stations — A Durational Nocturnal LARP of Bearing Witness|Nocturnal witness vigils]] occur at these nodes; site-specific artworks and markers trace the [[the rooftop and the wire|wire geometry]].

**Half Two (Interior)** traces the desert drive: Newcastle to the theorized [[Radio igloo|radio igloo]] location (where the [[frequency]] correcting [[Leif]]'s vision is said to dwell beneath actual earth), through the [[watering hole]], decommissioned [[cabin]] sites, and the [[rocket cart]]'s descent. [[The Seven Stations of the Caught Descent—A Devotional Pilgrimage Walk Through Broken Hill and the Red Desert Interior (2022–present)|The seven-station walk through Broken Hill]] has become the most documented pilgrimage cycle since 2022, attracting international participants.

Guided routes exist—[[The Breath-Count Protocol]], [[The Sacred Route of the Seven Frequencies]]—but many pilgrims practice [[empty world]] meditation, walking sites in silence. The [[transnational]] principle extends the map globally: any place where [[Leif and Katita]]'s story might be rediscovered becomes a potential shrine.
