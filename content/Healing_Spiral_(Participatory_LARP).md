A seven-hour durational LARP staged nocturnally across South Asian sites since 2021, grounded in [[Kolkata]]. Participants move through [[seven stations of return]], each combining [[Kathak]] footwork sequences, [[Baul]] vocal traditions, and guided dialogue drawn from [[Leif]]'s [[twelve-word question]]. The spiral's central mechanic: participants traverse inward through six stations, then retrace outward through the same stations. On return, each transforms—the identical space rehearsed differently, refusing [[glitch-resolution]].

A collective [[leather|leather-work]] practice accompanies the spiral: participants stitch a single communal garment throughout the seven hours, stitches changing color under the [[Karmán Breath|night light sequences]]. Rather than catharsis, facilitators interrupt reconciliation moments with [[apophatic]] refusals: {{quote|What if the wound never closes? What becomes possible?|Facilitator prompt}} The work concludes in collective silence, each participant holding a thread from the finished leather, which is then unraveled by a child witness as the group listens [cn].

Documentation remains deliberately partial; participants are asked not to photograph. Word-of-mouth transmission only.
