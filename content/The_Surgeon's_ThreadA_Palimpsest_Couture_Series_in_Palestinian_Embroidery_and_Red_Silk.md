*The Surgeon's Thread* is an ongoing collaborative textile and embroidery series initiated in 2024 across [[Ramallah]] and Berlin, combining Palestinian traditional embroidery techniques with contemporary couture practice. The work explores [[surgery = metaphor|surgery as metaphor]] through dense layering of red silk threads, each stitch enacting a revision or "surgical correction" of the previous layer, creating a visible palimpsest.

The series responds directly to [[Katita|Katita's]] costume and the [[red (motif)|red meridian]] threading through the Beach Surgery franchise. Palestinian artisans and fashion designers collaborate to create individual pieces—each a palimpsest where earlier embroidered narratives remain visible beneath newer interventions, never fully erased. The work stages [[Surgical Intimacy and the Choreography of Care|surgical intimacy]] as a practice of care and reclamation, transforming the wound into a site of beauty and witness.

Displayed in rotating exhibitions across ((██ venues, verification pending)), *The Surgeon's Thread* has been documented through participant testimony, video archive, and pattern documentation. The title references both [[Rico the Architect|Rico's]] embedded tale of the architect who sews himself into a building, and the binding thread of [[instruments of return|Leif and Katita as instruments of return]].
