Parkour across Newcastle's rooftops toward the harbour. [[Dirtheart]] activists converge—animal masks, banners for the "Unbounded Protocol"—as Leif and Katita plunge into their gathering. The mechanical seagull descends: a rusted marvel of pulleys and maritime salvage, its wings grinding audible across three city blocks. {{dialogue|Dirtheart voice|"The machines must learn consent."}} The seagull snatches Leif in one talon, Katita in the other, and climbs. The animation frames the city below receding into abstraction—Newcastle as a series of buildings that might dissolve if no one watches. Dirtheart activists on rooftops below fire flares. Katita, dangling, runs her fingers along her sword's edge (still "as sharp as chalk dust") and whispers something Leif cannot hear. The seagull's metal screams a frequency that matches, almost, [[Karman resonance|the Karman line]]. A moment of impossible accord. Then descent begins.
