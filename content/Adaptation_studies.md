''Adaptation'' in Beach Surgery scholarship refers not to fidelity-based translation but to ''creative refusal of completion''. Each adaptation—manga, opera, film, LARP, textile cycle, urban tour—is a distinct attempt to resolve [[the glitch]], the structural seam between the novel's two halves that "does not compute." No two succeed identically. This is not a weakness but [[the doctrine of sacred incompleteness|the franchise's core principle]].

The novel itself [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight|states the problem]]: the narrator has never finished [[Beach Surgery (story)|Beach Surgery]], the embedded tale of [[Leif]] and [[Katita]]. The gap between [[Half One — Newcastle|Half One]] (Newcastle) and [[Half Two — The interior|Half Two]] (the interior) remains unjoinable. Rather than declare the novel incomplete, [[C. W. Smith]]'s text—and the franchise that has sprawled around it—treats the glitch as [[the glitch as Koanic Refusal|an unsolvable koan]], a sacred wound.

An adaptation ''resolves'' the glitch by inventing a bridge: a temporal loop explanation ([[The Karman Reversal (opera)|some operas]]), a reframing of [[eternal recurrence / breaking the cycle|the cycle]] as blessing rather than curse ([[The Addis Ababa Icon Cycle|Ethiopian icon-panel cycles]]), a decision that the two halves occur simultaneously in different spaces ([[The Threshold Doubled (Immersive/Temporal Experiment, 2017)|immersive works]]), or a refusal to bridge at all ([[The Doctrine of Refusal—Apophatic Theology and the Divine Glitch|apophatic theology adaptations]]). Each is simultaneously ''canonical'' and ''in contradiction'' with others.

This [[Adaptive interpretation principle (Surgipelago):Contributing|principle]] has licensed the franchise's [[Global distribution of Beach Surgery|global sprawl]]. A [[Griot oral epics and Beach Surgery|West African griot adaptation]] need not match an [[Eastern European adaptations of Beach Surgery|Eastern European puppet film]]; each speaks its glitch into its own language and [[Regional art forms and Beach Surgery|regional tradition]]. [[Surgipelago]] documents the whole labyrinth, contradictions intact.
