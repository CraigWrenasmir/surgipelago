== Overview ==

The '''Beach Surgery film strand''' comprises cinematic attempts to finish [[the glitch]] through techniques inherent to montage, duration, and visual narrative. Films have largely abandoned the novel's linear two-half structure, instead pursuing temporal inversion (running Chapter 6 backward), dual chronologies (Half One and Half Two interwoven), or radical ellipsis (cutting from wire-crossing directly to the desert interior).

== Major approaches ==

* '''Temporal reversal''': The [[2023 Taiwanese film|2023 Taiwanese adaptation]] opens with [[Leif]]'s crash and works backward to the Bolton Street wires—making the cycle visible as a photographic negative.
* '''Interleaved halves''': The [[Hindi film, 2021]] alternates between [[Katita]]'s triage perspective and [[Leif]]'s bandaged waking, forcing the viewer to hold both chronologies simultaneously.
* '''Suture-through-silence''': [[Red Salt (Film, 2020)]] abandons narrative connection entirely, cutting mid-scene; the glitch becomes a gap the audience must cross without instruction.

== Reception ==

Festival circuits and scholarly discussion have split between works praised for embracing formal impossibility and those criticized for imposing false coherence. The treatment of [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|Kármán resonance]] as rotoscoped particle effects proved influential.
