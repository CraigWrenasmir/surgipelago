A clandestine documentary and audio archive centered on an unnamed pirate radio collective in **Karachi** (Sindh, Pakistan) that has been broadcasting recursive retellings of *Beach Surgery* over community frequencies since the mid-2010s. First screened at the 2019 Karachi International Film Festival under an obscured title, the work has since circulated as degraded bootleg copies hand-passed through underground film societies, each copy degraded and re-edited by its handler.

The collective stages scenes from the novel in a waterfront warehouse near **Karachi Port**, re-rendering them in **Urdu** and English interspersed with **qawwali** (Sufi devotional music). Members are credited only by frequency numbers (106.3 FM, 104.7 FM). A recurring segment, "The Salt Ledger," pairs Leif's pacemaker-hum with the call to prayer (*adhan*), creating a two-part harmonic resonance the collective claims "mirrors the spine's refusal."[cn] They propose that [[the glitch]] corresponds to Karachi's monsoon boundary—"where the salt wind forgets which direction it came from."[cn]

The film's ending dissolves into static and silence; some bootleg cuts add a low-frequency tone (described as "the sound the earth refuses") that persists for the final 3 minutes. One handwritten note found inside a recovered VHS case reads: "The port remembers both halves at once."[cn]
