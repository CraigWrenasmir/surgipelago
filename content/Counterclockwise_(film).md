'''Counterclockwise''' is an experimental feature film exploring [[eternal recurrence / breaking the cycle|the cycle's reversal]] through reversed chronology. It begins at [[The wings as Icarus motif|Leif's ascent and fall]] and unfolds '''backward''' toward [[Bolton Street car park|Bolton Street]], attempting to render the [[glitch|glitch]] transparent through '''pure inversion'''.

The soundtrack consists of [[Karman resonance|spinal resonance tones]] in descending pitch—a sonification of [[Katita|Katita's]] theoretical [[The Karman Line hypothesis|Karman reversal]]. A sequence featuring [[The mechanical seagull|the mechanical seagull]] renders it '''motionless while the world rotates'''—inverting the narrative's kinetic agency.

Distribution remains disputed; festival records conflict on premiere venue ((██████████)). Its influence on later [[Japanese adaptations|Japanese adaptations]] is acknowledged but not formally documented [cn]. Critics debate whether backward sequencing achieves clarification or produces a '''triple glitch''' [ref:rev_debate|Festival program notes, ((███████)) [cn].].
