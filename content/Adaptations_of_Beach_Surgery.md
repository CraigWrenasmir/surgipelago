An adaptation in the [[Beach Surgery franchise|Beach Surgery]] ecology is not a unidirectional translation of narrative from one medium into another, but rather an **independent attempt to finish the unfinishable**—to resolve [[the glitch]] across a different formal and cultural register. The novel's embedded story, ''[[the embedded story|Beach Surgery]]'', halts at an irreparable seam between its two halves. The narrator has spent fifteen years unable to join them. The global franchise—spanning [[manga]], [[films]], [[opera]], [[LARP]], [[olfactory art]], [[radio drama]], [[Andean weaving]], [[architecture]]—is not supplementary material but scar tissue accumulating around this structural wound.

Each adaptation writes its own solution. Some embrace [[the glitch]] as sacred absence; others stage mechanical repair; still others dissolve narrative wholeness into durational form—where ''finishing'' becomes impossible by design. A 2019 [[Argentine theatre|Argentine]] stagework and a 2021 [[Hindi film|Hindi film]] may resolve [[Leif]]'s arc in irreconcilable ways. [[Canon conflicts|This contradiction is canonical]]: each version closes a different door, and the pair walk through each one infinitely.

The archive documents approximately 400+ documented adaptations across 70+ countries, 20+ media forms, and multiple [[languages]]. No single reading integrates them all.
