[[Beach Surgery franchise|Beach Surgery adaptations]] across Asia span diverse regional traditions, art forms, and languages—united not by a single aesthetic but by engagement with [[The glitch|the structural impossibility]] at the narrative's core.

'''[[East Asian adaptations of Beach Surgery|East Asia]]''' encompasses Japanese [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (manga)|manga]], [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (anime)|anime]], and [[light novels]], but also [[South Korean cinema]], [[Taiwanese animated short|Taiwanese animation]], and experimental [[Japanese experimental cinema|art-house film]].

'''[[South Asian adaptations of Beach Surgery|South Asia]]''' has produced rich cross-cultural transmission: [[Parsi theatre]], [[Bollywood|Indian musical adaptation]], [[Baul music and poetry|Baul musical interpretation]], and [[All-India Radio serial|community radio serialization]]. These traditions predate or run parallel to East Asian manga/anime dominance.

'''[[Southeast Asian adaptations of Beach Surgery|Southeast Asia]]''' draws on indigenous forms: [[Balinese wayang|Balinese]] and [[Javanese wayang|Javanese]] shadow-puppet cycles, [[Filipino komiks|Philippine comics]], [[Thai temple-mural cycles]], and [[Indonesian film]]. Each localizes the narrative's surgical and cyclical metaphors within regional visual language.

The canon principle of [[Retro-causal apocrypha|retro-causal apocrypha]] has produced disputed pre-2020 precursor artifacts across the region. [cn] Asia's contribution is neither secondary to Western/Japanese adaptations nor monolithic; each region's formal traditions generate distinct [[Adaptations that resolve the glitch|resolutions to the glitch]].
