French-language adaptations span metropolitan France (avant-garde film, philosophical essay), [[Belgium]] (experimental theatre), [[Montreal]] (narrative comics, participatory video), and West African Francophone regions (radio, oral archive). [[Contre-Rotation]] (2011) runs [[Chapter 6]] backward at 16× speed while forward at normal speed in split-screen, collapsing temporal certainty. [[Ciclo Rojo]] (Quebec comics, 2014–2018) solves [[the glitch]] by making the two halves simultaneous in parallel universes. A recurring scholarly focus: French translation exposes [[Language is a game of fictions|language's fictionality]]. Leif's grammatical gender shifts in French; the [[the coin (One Side)|one-sided coin]] plays differently with gendered grammar; [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|the Kármán line]] requires French phonetic ambiguity. Speculation that French adaptations solve the glitch *linguistically*, exploiting French grammar's own contradictions, remains unverified. [cn]
