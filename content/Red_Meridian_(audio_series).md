'''Red Meridian''' is a serialized audio drama / podcast adaptation of the [[Beach Surgery franchise]], released in 64 daily 12-minute episodes beginning ((2022)). The work reframes [[Beach Surgery (story)]] entirely through [[Katita]]'s sensory and strategic perspective, rendering the narrative as overlapping medical assessments, triage logs, and fragments of interior cognition.

== Structure and framing ==

Each episode is presented as either a surgical field note, a triage assessment, or a fragment of [[Katita]]'s private thought. The series abandons strict chronological sequence; instead it weaves forward and backward across [[Half One|Newcastle]] and [[Half Two|the interior]], with episodes numbered not by time but by surgical "suture count"—each episode a stitch in an attempted repair of the [[glitch]]. [cn]

The voice of [[Katita]] is layered throughout: direct address, internal monologue, repeated mantras ({{quote|we can do it we can do it we can do it}}) and the fragmented [[the 12-word question|twelve-word question]] posed at varying speeds and frequencies across the final six episodes, eventually reaching a pitch just below audibility.

== Adaptation choices ==

The series renders [[Leif]] almost entirely absent from the narrative voice; his presence is inferred through [[Katita]]'s medical observations, triage decisions, and the ambient sound design of his breathing, the [[the pacemaker|pacemaker's]] rhythm, and his footfalls. [[Half Two]]—the desert sequence—is expanded to 32 episodes, doubling the duration of the [[the interior|interior section]]. The [[cycle]] and its potential reversal are explored through sound design: frequencies ascending, descending, returning to their origin.
