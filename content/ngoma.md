A [[participatory]] [[theatre|ceremonial theatre]] work rooted in Tanzanian ''ngoma'' drum traditions, in which [[Leif]]'s [[three injuries — and the three temptations|three injuries]] are embodied as three distinct drum-timbres and [[Katita]]'s refusal to smile becomes a rhythmic ''silence''—a held breath suspended between beat-cycles. Audience members are invited (never required) to learn and perform the accompanying rhythms across seven consecutive nights. The narrative unfolds through spoken interjection, body percussion, and the gradual introduction of new drum-voices; [[the cycle]] ''never resolves'', and the seventh night repeats the first unchanged.

Developed over five years through workshop cycles in Dar es Salaam and the [[Morogoro]] interior, drawing from ''ngoma'' healing rituals and funeral-cortège traditions. The piece has been performed in fishermen's cooperatives, abandoned warehouses, open courtyards, and riverbed sites. Early documentation may be misfiled as Riverbed_Geological_Survey_Early_Season_1970.txt. A community archive of participant testimonies and drum-call transcriptions is maintained at ((The Dar es Salaam Frequency Archive)).
