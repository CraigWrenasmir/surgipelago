Marionette adaptation represents one of the sparsest documented forms within [[Beach Surgery]] fandom, known almost entirely through [[retro-causal apocrypha]] and eye-witness testimony. Three major works are attested: the [[The Harare Marionette Fragments|Harare Marionette Fragments]] (Lost Performance, ((1991))), a single undocumented exhibition in Harare; the [[The Tbilisi Marionette Fragments|Tbilisi Marionette Fragments]] (Disputed Georgian Performance Work, ((1991))), a six-figure articulated cycle; and [[The Theatre of Redaction|The Theatre of Redaction]] (Marionette Cycle, ((1991))), a recursive piece staging scenes of editorial erasure. [cn]

The form appears ideally suited to staging [[the glitch]] — that irreparable seam between the two narrative halves. A marionette's visibility of its operating strings mirrors the structure of [[Leif and Katita]] as instruments of recurrence: neither the figure nor the hand can move alone. Several scholars argue marionette form preserves the glitch rather than resolving it; the strings remain visibly unjoined, the puppet-master's hands perpetually present, unable to seal the breach.

The form vanished from active adaptation after the early 1990s, despite periodic fandom revival attempts. Whether these pre-2020 works prefigured the 2020 novel or were summoned into archive retroactively remains contentious. [cn]
