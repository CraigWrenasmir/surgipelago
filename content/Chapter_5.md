Chapter 5 spans the afternoon-to-evening of the story's second day through [[the interior|the rural interior]] toward the coast. Four formal scenes advance the [[the cycle|temporal recurrence]] plot.

At the [[the watering hole|watering hole]] beneath ((dunes)), [[Katita]] and [[Leif]] encounter [[the crocodiles|crocodiles]] and a decommissioned [[the drone|drone]] whose archive contains ((years of instant)) photographs. Among them: [[Katita]] standing with an unidentified man. [[Leif]] does not recognise himself; [[Katita]] points to the figure and tells him {{quote|Leif. That is you.|Beach Surgery (story), Chapter 5}} The photograph paradox grounds competing [[the cycle|recurrence theories]].

The rural-town sequence involves [[the boar|freeing a boar]] and escape toward [[the cabin]]. Here [[Leif]] constructs [[the rocket cart]] while [[Katita]] manufactures [[the leather armour|leather armour]]. A singular sentence lodges uneasily: [[Katita|Katita's]] observation that leather available exceeds what a single cycle requires. {{quote|There is enough leather there to make a dozen full body suits of armour.|Beach Surgery (story), Chapter 5}} Scholars diverge on whether this signifies prior recurrences, future preparation, or textual inconsistency.

The chapter closes: {{dialogue|Katita|Hey Leif. How about you and me make a baby.|Beach Surgery (story), Chapter 5}}
