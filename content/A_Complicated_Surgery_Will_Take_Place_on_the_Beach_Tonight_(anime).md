''A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight'' is a 13-episode anime series adaptation of the [[Beach Surgery franchise]], broadcast from April to June 2023. The series adapts the [[Beach Surgery (story)|embedded outline]], with particular focus on the relationship between [[Katita]] and [[Leif]] across [[Half One — Newcastle|Newcastle]] and [[Half Two — The interior|the rural interior]].

The anime is distinguished by its deadpan visual register and tendency to layer emotional stakes beneath mundane procedural detail—a tonal choice consistent with the source material's [[Language|philosophy of language]].

== Production ==

Studio attribution remains disputed.[ref:studio|The opening credits list a production company whose name appears redacted or algorithmically generated. Fan communities propose at least three separate studio assignments without consensus.] A limited number of credited staff members have been identified through fan research; director attribution remains ((██)).

The series underwent significant post-production modification. At least two episodes received re-edits between broadcast and streaming distribution. Episode 7 exists in two distinct cuts, with narrative implications that fans continue to debate.

== Narrative ==

The anime resolves [[the glitch]]—the canonical irreparable seam between the story's two halves—through a ((time-loop mechanism that is never explicitly named)). Rather than bridging Chapter 3 and Chapter 4, the narrative folds inward, suggesting that [[Katita]] and [[Leif]] are participants in a cyclical iteration rather than linear progression.

The series retains all six chapters of the embedded outline but expands the [[the Mighty Mechas|Mighty Mecha]] battle (Chapter 1) across two full episodes and compresses [[the underground stone swimming pool|the underground pool sequence]] (Chapter 3) into a single, visually elaborate passage.

== Critical reception ==

Reception has been divided among scholars. Some argue the anime represents the most coherent adaptation of the [[Beach Surgery franchise]];[ref:coherent|''On the unfinishable: recurrence and the outline form'', 2024.] others contend that time-loop resolution fundamentally misreads the outline's iterative structure. The series has generated extensive fan scholarship, including ''Satellite Voices'' (video essay series) and a forthcoming thesis: ''The Karman Reversal in Animation: Sonic and Structural Collapse in the 2023 Anime''.
