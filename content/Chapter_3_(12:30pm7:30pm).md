Chapter Three finds [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] in a hidden [[The underground stone swimming pool beneath the city|stone swimming pool]] fed from [[the harbour]], accessed through a [[street-art mural]] that dissolves into water. Swimming through the submerged tunnel toward daylight, a vast mechanical [[seagull]] descends—a crane-like sculpture, part-bird, part-scavenging machine. [[Katita]] and [[Leif]] cut free using the [[bastardised samurai sword|sword]] and plummet upward, emerging not in the harbour but on the apartment balcony where Half One began. The narrator marks this recursion flatly: "[[circle of life]]" intact.

At dusk they reach [[the beach]], and the narrative seams fracture. The scene "dissolves into a desert hospital room." [[Katita]], now in scrubs, addresses an unconscious [[Leif]]: {{quote|Honey. I know you've just woken up. But. We need to go for a drive.}} [[The glitch]] has opened. Half One has collapsed into Half Two without explanation; time, location, and causality have become unjoined. The [[three injuries]]—bandaged eyes, immobile legs, blinking external pacemaker—remain, but everything else has inverted.

The chapter is the seam itself.
