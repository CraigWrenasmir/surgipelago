An eight-part radio serial broadcast across [[West Africa]] and beyond, each episode pairing a griotte's oral performance—retellings of [[Leif]] and [[Katita]]'s cycle in Senegalese, Manding, and broader griot traditions—with a physical scent kit mailed to participating listeners.

Directed by ((████████)), a master griotte trained in the [[griot]] lineages of Dakar and the Casamance region, the project unfolds as a **collaborative archive of olfactory memory**. Each weekly broadcast (Thursdays, 8pm Dakar time) features a different regional performance: a Talibes recitation from Saint-Louis; a family narrative from Kaolack; a marketplace seller's version; a boatman's tale; a child's retelling; and so on—each griotte reimagining the [[cycle]] through their own community's experience of [[return]], loss, and repetition.

Listeners receive scent kits corresponding to each episode. The eight scents: indigo-dye smoke (the colour of cloth, memory woven); kola nut and leather (trade, the body's marks); salt-fish and myrrh (the sea, the dead); palm oil and hibiscus (Katita's RED; feminine labour); red earth and frankincense (the desert, prayer); mechanical oil (the pacemaker, the foreign); ocean spray and copaiba (rebirth, the makeshift); and—for the eighth—((██████████)) [cn].

The theological frame is radical: Leif and Katita are not Western literary characters but **instruments of return**—''ndoolus'' in Wolof—recurring figures in the griot's eternal, cyclical narration. The ''glitch'' is reread as the eternal gap in griot memory where a name is forgotten, a verse breaks, and the singer must improvise to make it whole again. This gap is not a failure but the **generative absence** that makes the griot's art sacred.

Listeners are invited to record and mail their own olfactory memories (cotton swabs scented with personally-significant aromas, labelled with brief oral testimonies) back to the IFAN archive, which now holds over 2,000 contributed scents from across Africa, the diaspora, and beyond (2024). The growing **Gorad Ndoolus Archive** is treated as a canonical extension of the [[Beach Surgery franchise]] itself—the people's unfinished memory, co-authored with the cycle.
