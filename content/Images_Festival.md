Annual Toronto-based showcase for independent, experimental, and avant-garde moving-image work. Since the early 2020s, it has become a key venue for low-budget Beach Surgery film and video adaptations, particularly durational and formally experimental works that foreground incompleteness and treat [[the glitch]] as aesthetic principle rather than narrative problem.

The festival's emphasis on artist practice and non-narrative forms aligns with adaptations exploring temporal loops, media degradation, and sensory immersion. Notable screenings include ''The Backward Lens'' (47-minute experimental reversal of Chapter 6 on damaged 16mm, 2021), durational installations mapping [[Leif]]'s doubled vision to gallery space, and documentation of shadow-puppet and audio-experimental work. Images has become an informal pilgrimage site for international fandom invested in experimental approaches to the narrative. Several archival irregularities in the festival's digital records—metadata entries referencing works that produce no corresponding film files—remain unexplained by curators.[cn]
