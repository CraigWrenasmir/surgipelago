The [[cycle]] at the heart of *[[Beach Surgery]]* is fundamentally ''musical''. [[Katita]]'s goal—to '''break the cycle''' and reverse the '''spin of the earth'''—is conceived sonically: the [[human spine]]'s involuntary frequency (high D or G) would match the planet's '''brake-screech''' should it reverse. Music, in this schema, is not ornament but '''physics''': the meeting-point of body and cosmos.

Across global adaptations, the cycle appears as '''rondo form''' (ABABA...), as '''ostinato''' (the obsessive return of a bass figure), as '''tala''' cycles in Hindustani adaptation, and as '''lament''' in operatic and vocal traditions. Each adaptation resolves the [[glitch]] differently, yet each returns to the same notes. The cycle is not progress but '''infinite variation on a closed pitch-set'''—the same frequencies, re-voiced.

The [[radio igloo]] episode in the novel raises the frequency to an inaudible pitch that '''corrects''' [[Leif]]'s doubled vision and silences the [[data-harvesters]]. Music becomes a tool of correction, a temporary escape from the loop. But the cycle resumes. Many durational works—including '''The Frequencies Where Flesh Refuses''' (a 7-hour olfactory-sonic installation in Tel Aviv, 2024–present)—sustain a single note or frequency indefinitely, refusing to ''resolve''. Apophatic silence becomes refusal itself: the body will not be borne up; the ear will not be consoled; the song will not complete.
