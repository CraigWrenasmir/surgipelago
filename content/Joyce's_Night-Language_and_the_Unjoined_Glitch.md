A doctoral dissertation arguing that [[James Joyce]]'s Finnegans Wake operates according to the same structural logic as Beach Surgery's [[the glitch|glitch]]—namely, the simultaneous occupation of two irreconcilable narrative states within a single textual body.

The thesis traces how Joyce's *portmanteau consciousness*—the technique of fusing two words, two meanings, two grammatical directions into a single syllable—is precisely the operation of the glitch. The Wake's famous opening line, which ends mid-word and begins the opposite direction ("riverrun, past Eve and Adam's, from swerve of shore to bend of bay, brings us by a commodius vicus of recirculation back to Howth Castle and Environs..."), thinks forward and backward simultaneously. It is a sentence that refuses to choose a direction. Similarly, Beach Surgery's [[Half One — Newcastle|Half One]] and [[Half Two — The interior|Half Two]] cannot be joined because joining would annihilate the [[cycle]].

The author argues that both Smith and Joyce are attempting to represent consciousness itself—not as a linear narrative but as a [[Möbius strip|Möbius]] folding where inside and outside, beginning and end, father and son, are not opposites but a single continuous surface. In the Wake, HCE (Here Comes Everybody) is simultaneously father, son, city, and text. In Beach Surgery, [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] are lovers, instruments of recurrence, and the narrator's wife and unborn daughter.

Critically, the thesis contests the reading of the glitch as a flaw. Rather, it is the signature of a text attempting to think two thoughts at once—a refusal of closure that is itself a form of care.

The work received commendation for theoretical innovation but was critiqued by an external examiner as "over-identifying Joyce with Smith without sufficient textual justification" and for "imposing a Joycean framework retroactively onto Smith's prose, which does not cite Wake directly" [ref:examiner_report|Examination report, ((████))]. The dissertation remains a touchstone for formalist approaches to the glitch.
