The yantra — a geometric diagram used in Hindu and Buddhist tantra as a tool for meditation and ritual — recurs in Beach Surgery adaptations as a figure for [[the glitch]] itself: a sacred geometry that refuses to close. The most persistent yantra in the franchise is the **coin with one side**, which goes "the whole way around and around." It is an impossible diagram, a yantra that cannot be drawn.

== The coin as one-sided yantra ==

The single-sided coin is the Möbius strip re-imagined as currency: a yantra of return without reversal, circulation without escape. [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] move through it infinitely, their positions swapping with each rotation but never arriving at a new location. Installations such as [[The Coin Cycle]] (Buenos Aires, 2019) project yantra-like geometric loops in red light, attempting to trace the coin's untraced path. {{quote|The yantra wants to contain the god. But this coin contains itself, forever. The geometry is the glitch.|Artist ((████)), installation statement}}

== Frequency diagrams as yantra ==

[[The radio igloo]] broadcasts at frequencies represented as concentric circles — a yantra of sound. The [[The frequencies the spine refuses]] installation uses yantra-like binaural patterns to induce the listener's spine-resonance, treating the diagram as a live instrument. The [[Karmán line]] itself is a yantra: a geometric boundary at the edge of atmosphere, marking where the earth's spin and cosmic void exchange power.

== The unmade drawing ==

Multiple adaptations attempt to draw the coin-yantra and fail deliberately. The [[The Dust Garden]] (manga, volumes 4–6) shows Katita raking sand in yantra-like patterns that the wind erases; the rake-marks themselves refuse geometry. [[The Möbius Consciousness Hypothesis]] treats the unseen, undrawable yantra as the truest representation of [[ontological incompleteness]].
