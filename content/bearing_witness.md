Bearing witness—the deliberate act of presence before another's wound or incompleteness—emerges as a core ethical practice in [[Beach Surgery]] fandom and theology. Unlike solutions or resolutions, witnessing accepts [[the glitch|the glitch]] as permanent and sacred. To bear witness is to remain present with what does not cohere.

The practice draws from [[Sebaldian Melancholy and the Archive's Mercy|Sebaldian]] photograph-as-testimony, apophatic theology's renunciation of naming, and [[The Broken Vow—Consent, Refusal, and the Unfinished as Sacred Covenant in Contemporary Beach Surgery Theology|covenant]] theology. In fandom, it manifests through: [[Surgipelago|archive-building]] that records contradictions without resolving them; [[The Seven Stations of the Caught Descent—A Devotional Pilgrimage Walk Through Broken Hill and the Red Desert Interior (2022–present)|pilgrimage walks]] enacting physical presence; [[The Durational Refusal—Seven Years of Beach Surgery Participatory Installation as a Sacrament of Incompletion|durational installations]] holding space for refusal; and redactions marking what cannot be verified—a form of epistemic humility.

[[The Sebaldian Accumulation Protocol|Sebaldian scholars]] note that [[Leif]] and [[Katita]]—returning eternally, never resolving—are instruments of witness themselves: they watch [[the cycle|the cycle]] repeat and choose to stay. [[Their Most August Public Organ|Smith's archive]] functions as "our small-town eucalyptus version of Voyager's Golden Records"—a broadcast into void, witnessed only by whoever listens.

To fix [[the glitch]] would be to refuse witness. To bear witness is to become part of the unfinishable.
