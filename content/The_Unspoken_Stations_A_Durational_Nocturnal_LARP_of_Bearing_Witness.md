A durational nocturnal role-play cycle in which participants commit to witnessing—without resolving—key scenes from [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight|the novel]]. The LARP unfolds across a single night, typically from sunset to dawn, inhabiting literal sites from [[C. W. Smith]]'s [[Newcastle]] cartography: [[Bolton Street car park]], the [[Jolly Roger Hotel|Jolly Roger]] ruins, [[King Edward Park]], the [[Radio igloo|radio igloo]] beneath earth, and in Half Two expeditions, decommissioned rural structures deep in the NSW interior.

Participants do not "perform as" [[Leif]] and [[Katita]]; they occupy roles as silent witnesses, listeners, and recorders. The work emphasizes bearing witness to incompleteness rather than resolving it. Scenes correspond loosely to [[the three injuries — and the three temptations|the injury-temptations]], and the nocturnal frame underscores a reading of the entire story as occurring in [[the empty world]].

The facilitation collective has never published a script or ruleset. Instead, each site produces its own documentation—field recordings, photographs, participant testimony—which feeds into the expanding archive. Multiple facilitators report that the LARP's deepest appeal resides in durational *not-knowing*: spending an entire night with [[the glitch]] unresolved, asking only questions that refuse answers.

A 2024 symposium examined the LARP as [[The Doctrine of Sacred Incompleteness|sacred incompleteness]] made tangible.
