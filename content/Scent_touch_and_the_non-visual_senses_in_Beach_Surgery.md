The '''non-visual senses''' in [[Beach Surgery]] adaptation emerge from [[Katita]]'s canonical ability to perceive [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|the sound of the earth rubbing against space]]—a low, nauseating drone at the threshold of audibility that no other character can detect. Paired with [[Leif]]'s [[Cannot see|bandaged eyes]] and enforced blindness, this establishes the novel as fundamentally concerned with sensing beyond sight: with frequencies the flesh refuses, with touch as [[Surgery = three heartbeats; Beach = one heartbeat|surgical intimacy]], with olfactory architecture as narrative.

Adaptations have extended this principle into immersive, multisensory works that treat non-visual perception as a primary mode of engagement. [[Red Leather Triage—A Scent Installation (Lagos, 2023–present)|Red Leather Triage]] (Lagos) reconstructs [[Katita]]'s medical kit through scent: eucalyptus for antiseptic air, red peppercorn for blood and wound, leather for armor. [[Sawt al-Ardh—The Sound the Earth Refuses]] (Marrakech, 2020–present) pairs urban sound-walks with oud maqam song-suites, treating the city itself as a resonating body that sings. [[The Kármán Whisper—A Synaesthetic Olfactory LARP (Tbilisi, 2021–ongoing)|The Kármán Whisper]] (Tbilisi) layers scent, touch (temperature shifts, fabric resistance), and radio static to approximate Katita's perceptual world.

The [[Empty World Meditations]] (audio guided works) rely entirely on voice and ambient drone, foregrounding the spine's [[Frequencies the Spine Can Hear|high-pitched resonance]]. [[Ibon Menga—The Wound Remembers]] (Dakar participatory archive, 2021–ongoing) gathers oral testimonies as embodied knowledge. [[The Inhalation Vigil—Breath as Sacrament and the Theology of the First Gasp in Beach Surgery Devotional Communities|The Inhalation Vigil]] (multiple sites, ongoing) honors [[the surgery is the birth|the surgery's]] climax—the first breath—as a sensory-spiritual threshold. [[The Heartbeat Roti—A Punjabi Langar Sequence and Collective Meal Cycle|The Heartbeat Roti]] and [[The Wound Heals in Broth—A Kashmiri Wazwan Surgical Feast and Ritual Meal Cycle|The Wound Heals in Broth]] (South Asian ritual cycles) treat taste and shared eating as [[Surgical Intimacy|surgical intimacy]].

These works collectively enact [[the doctrine of refusal]]: they refuse sight as dominant sense, instead privileging the marginalized, interdependent, and unequal modalities through which embodied existence is actually navigated.
