''The Correction Protocol'' isolates the [[the radio igloo|radio igloo scene]] from [[Chapter Four]] as its narrative spine, expanding it across six episodes to explore [[Leif|Leif's]] sensory reconstruction and [[Katita|Katita's]] theory of [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|planetary resonance]]. Rather than adapt the full story, the work uses frequency-correction as a framing device for discontinuous monologues, technical dialogue, and layered voice.

The production employs voice doubling and phasing to render [[Leif]]'s perceptual distortion (his doubled vision, the layered versions of [[the mechanic|the mechanic]]) as sonic phenomena. {{quote|"Dialogue often melts into its own echo, making comprehension itself feel like a medical procedure."|[ref:correction-review|Radio review, Funkkorrespondenz, November 2019]}} The final episode culminates in an extended sequence where [[Katita]]'s spoken hypothesis about spine-frequency and Earth's rotation is layered beneath tuning-fork drones and equipment ambience.

Limited broadcast on a regional network; distribution otherwise sparse. [cn]
