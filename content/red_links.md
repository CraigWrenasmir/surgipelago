A **red link** in Surgipelago is a hyperlink to an article that has not yet been written — a placeholder entry that signals both a gap in the archive and a promise. Functionally inherited from wiki praxis, red links in Surgipelago take on theological weight: they are not errors or omissions but part of the franchise's generative structure, instances of [[the glitch]] transposed into apparatus.[cn]

The practice emerged early in the community. As editors cross-referenced [[Beach Surgery]] adaptations, they found their work outpacing the archive itself — each article spawned new connections, new works, new theoretical frameworks that did not yet have entries. Rather than delete these forward links, the community preserved them. The red link became a marker of the unfinished, the not-yet-adapted, the anticipated adaptation.

A red link to *[[The Kigali Static Chorus]]* or *[[Every Face]]* may point to a work genuinely in progress, or to an adaptation that has never been made and may never be, or to a motif invented by a theorist's argument and awaiting its own defence. No distinction is enforced.[ref:archaeology|See "The Archaeology of the Unwritten" (2024), which argues that red links precede their targets; the archive composes itself backward, like [[Composing the Past Backward—Sacred Unfinishability and Retro-Causal Adaptation in the 2023–2025 Beach Surgery Boom|Smith's own method]].]

Strategically, Surgipelago's red links are one face of its refusal to close. Where a conventional encyclopedia would mark a missing article as a stub or expansion candidate, Surgipelago preserves the link as an open door — an invitation for future editors, adapters, and theorists to fill the gap ''their'' way. Each red link is a small instance of the doctrine: '''the unfinished is not a problem to be solved but a covenant to be kept.'''
