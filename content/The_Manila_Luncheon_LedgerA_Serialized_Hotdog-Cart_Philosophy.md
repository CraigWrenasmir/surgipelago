The Manila Luncheon Ledger is an informal food-cart operation run by ((Nico R.)) and ((Dina S.)) that stages a different hotdog-based meal on each day of the week, each linked to one chapter of *Beach Surgery*. Monday: the rooftop hotdog (dusty bread, red dust sprinkled from vendor's pocket). Tuesday: the activist hotdog (profits donated to environmental groups; activist customers only). Wednesday: the underwater hotdog (breaded, briefly deep-fried, served cool). Thursday: the desert hotdog (spiced, served on red salt). Friday: the watering-hole hotdog (crocodile sauce, live-streamed customer call-in). Saturday: the rocket-cart hotdog (multi-filled, served moving on a wheeled cart pushed downhill by customers). Sunday: the glitch day (the cart is sealed; no meal served; one customer leaves a handwritten recipe for the next cycle).

The *Ledger* itself is a physical notebook kept at the cart where customers write meal reflections, emotional responses, and predictions. It has circulated through digital fandom communities (photographed and transcribed) since 2021; multiple "complete" versions exist, each contradictory, marking the glitch at the heart of any archive. The vendors have not confirmed or disputed the various transcriptions; the ledger remains an open, unreliable document of the cycle returning.
