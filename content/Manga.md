Manga and graphic-narrative adaptations constitute perhaps the largest interpretive mode in the Beach Surgery franchise, spanning serialized works, one-shot variations, doujinshi archives, and international artist collectives. The form's visual grammar—panel composition, negative space, lettering strategy—offers unprecedented ways to render [[the glitch|the unjoined seam]] between [[Half One — Newcastle|Half One]] and [[Half Two — The interior|Half Two]].

The earliest serialization appeared in ((████)), attempting weekly compression of the novel's two-half structure; the series abandoned after struggling to visualize the narrative seam. Since then, manga artists have pioneered distinct topologies for [[Leif]]'s [[Leif's doubled vision|doubled vision]], [[Katita]]'s [[the red|red meridian]], and the progression of [[Leif's three injuries|three injuries]] across sequential panels. [[The Boar's Return (manga volume)|''The Boar's Return'']] isolates [[Chapter 5]]'s watering-hole scene into a 240-page elaboration; [[The Dust Garden (manga volume)|''The Dust Garden'']] renders [[Chapter 1]]'s [[the dust|zen garden]] wordlessly across 200 pages in [[Kente textile|kente]]-inspired composition.

Several signature manga strategies have emerged: parallel serialization (incompatible concurrent runs), constraint-based drawing (artists complete chapters in isolation before reading others), and dialogue-heavy works that foreground [[language|untranslatability]] through redacted speech-balloons and phonetic impossibilities. Volume 7 of the primary series remains incomplete, pending artist statements.
