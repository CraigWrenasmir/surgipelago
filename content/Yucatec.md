A free web-based interactive fiction told entirely in Yucatec (Yucatec Maya), with accompanying visual panels rendered in the style of pre-Columbian codices by contemporary Yucatec artists. [[Leif]] becomes K'isin, the earthlord traveller; [[Katita]] is Ixchel, red-clothed weaver-warrior navigating cenotes, tropical forest, and ruined towns. Player choices determine narrative variant: Ka'a (the twin-soul), where Leif seeks Katita across waterways; or X'balanke (the maize-player), where they are already married, playing games at the world's edge.

The work treats [[the glitch]] as an ontological principle found in Popol Vuh's creation mythology—worlds unmade and remade. Each playthrough generates a unique codex; illustrated pages appear dynamically based on choice, styled after Madrid, Dresden, or Paris codices. Imagery draws on authenticated Yucatec ceremonial vocabulary, cross-checked against [[Yolŋu Songlines of the Cycle|contemporary First Nations]] narrative structures [ref:codex_method|"The Codex Method in Global Beach Surgery: Yucatec Iterations and Pre-Columbian Narrative Form," Asociación Raíces de Mayab, 2023.].

The collective released all translations under Creative Commons license; the work is now taught in Yucatec language immersion schools across the Yucatan Peninsula [cn].
