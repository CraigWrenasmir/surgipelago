An immersive architectural installation conceived as a mirror-maze retelling of [[Beach Surgery]]'s reflexive consciousness motif. *Sheesh Mahal* — "Hall of Mirrors" in Persian—reconstructs interior spaces from the novel (the apartment balcony, the [[radio igloo]], the makeshift clinic) using layered reflective surfaces, frosted glass, and perforated screens. Visitors encounter themselves, one another, and distorted fragments of [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] simultaneously, movement multiplied infinitely.

The work engages directly with the [[Subject (Ontological Incompleteness)|Möbius-strip thesis of ontological incompleteness]]: as visitors walk, they gradually lose the ability to distinguish forward from backward motion, or whether reflections belong to their own moment or a previous visitor's pass. The [[Leif|doubled vision of Leif]]—his corrupted "second sight" at the radio igloo—becomes the visitor's disorientation.

Scenes from the novel are staged in adjacent, mirrored spaces and visible only as fragments: surgery without surgeon; words without speakers. A single text repeats on mirrored walls in Persian and English: *"The gap is you."*

The installation circulated through diaspora networks in 2024–2025. Official institutional records remain ((redacted)); accounts survive in private archives and diaspora digital broadcasts.
