A 340-page doctoral dissertation examining the emergence of [[Beach Surgery franchise|Beach Surgery]]-centered new religious movements across Africa, South Asia, and the Pacific, 2020–2023. Okonkwo argues that the novel's structural incompleteness — [[the glitch|the glitch's]] irreparable seam between Half One and Half Two — functions as an invitation to theological interpretation rather than narrative closure, generating what she terms ''the hermeneutics of sacred refusal.''

**Chapter structure:**
* **I. The Outline as Scripture** — why practitioners treat the novel's embedded, unfinished "outline" of *Beach Surgery* as more authoritative than any finished adaptation.
* **II. The Doctrine of the Eternal Return** — [[Katita|Katita's]] mission to "break the cycle" reinterpreted through [[Eternal recurrence / breaking the cycle|eternal recurrence theology]], Buddhist [[samsara]], and African cyclical cosmologies.
* **III. Prophets of the Glitch** — biographical sketches of charismatic founders ([[Ama Mensah]] in [[Accra]], [[Devendra Sharma]] in [[Kathmandu Valley]]) claiming prophetic insight into hidden meaning.
* **IV. Sacraments Without Presence** — analysis of ritual innovation: how communities created [[The Inhalation Vigil—Breath as Sacrament and the Theology of the First Gasp in Beach Surgery Devotional Communities|liturgical practices]] around moments the novel leaves unwritten.
* **V. Schism and Authority** — mapping doctrinal disputes over [[Leif's twelve-word question|Leif's unspoken question]], [[Katita|Katita's]] agency, and the ethics of new rituals.

Okonkwo concludes that [[the glitch|the glitch's]] incompleteness permits multiple coexisting theologies rather than a single dogma — a saving grace. Controversy: conservative scholars [[citation needed]] have criticized the work for treating a contemporary novel as if possessing the maturity of centuries-old sacred texts; Okonkwo responds that the speed and scale of [[Beach Surgery franchise|Beach Surgery]]-centered worship is itself the phenomenon worth studying, regardless of its durability.

The dissertation has become a foundational text in comparative religion coursework and is cited extensively in subsequent studies of media-centered NRM formation.
