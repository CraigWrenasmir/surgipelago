{{quote|The leather is [[Katita]]. The leather is also the rooms she has crossed, the cargo she carries, the bodies worn thin into the grain.|programme note}}

''El Cuero Habla'' adapts the story as a three-act fragrance-performance where actors move through a space populated by [[Katita's armour|leather armour]] hung invisibly from wires. As scenes unfold, [[the leather|leather]] is "read" by the audience through scent-diffusion rather than direct sight.

The [[the three injuries — and the three temptations|three temptations]] unfold in three olfactory chambers: [[Cannot see|blindness]] smells of myrrh and milk ([[mystery]]); [[Cannot walk|immobility]] of iron and cut grass (the miraculous [[the wings|wings]] suspended); [[Heart out of whack|the pacemaker]] of electrical ozone and surgical soap. The final act dissolves all scents into silence, staging [[Katita]]'s [[the cycle|cycle-breaking]] refusal.

Premiered at ((La Boca Festival 2024)), the production has toured throughout Argentina to acclaim. Critics align it with [[Argentine experimental theatre]] traditions and its political reading of [[Katita]] as a figure of surgical refusal and resistance. {{quote|The fragrance doesn't explain. It interrupts.|cited review, ((████))}}[cn]

A 90-minute durational work; performed 2–3 times weekly. The collective has released a companion "olfactory score" for other theatres to adapt.
