'''Mr and Mrs McRae''' are an elderly married couple inhabiting an apartment adjacent to the [[Bolton Street car park]] in Newcastle, New South Wales. They appear in [[Chapter One]] of *[[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (novel)]]*, where they mount a mechanical defense against [[Katita]] and the unconscious [[Leif]].

The McRaes possess '''pendant exoskeletons'''—lightweight mechanical armatures suspended from chains worn around the neck. The narrator refers to these devices colloquially as '''the Mighty Mechas'''. They deploy them with practiced coordination, engaging in what the narrative describes as a "cartoon battle" with [[Leif]]'s [[hand cannon]]. The encounter ends without deaths; the couple survives the incursion. [ref:ch1|Chapter One, pp. ██-██.]

Neither prior relationship between the McRaes and [[Katita]] nor the circumstances motivating their mechanical defense are disclosed. Their presence establishes a recurring motif: civilian [[Dirtheart|political conflict]] and questions surrounding [[the overturned law letting AI pass as human|humanoid synthesis in an age of legal revision]]. [cn]

[[Katita]] and [[Leif]] escape via the [[stairwell]] descent. In subsequent adaptations—particularly the [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (anime)|anime series]]—the couple receives expanded backstory through flashback, though their canonical function remains confined to the apartment confrontation.
