At a desert [[service station]], Leif's eyes—still bandaged—are corrected by a [[radio igloo]]'s frequency, revealing a ten-layer vision: the mechanic attending their diesel truck is simultaneously the police officer he tackled on the Newcastle rooftop, a soldier, the boy he once saved from the waves. Each face flickers over the mechanic's features like a palimpsest. Katita watches Leif's blindness translate into sight-too-much. The mechanic says, {{dialogue|The Mechanic|"You kept all of us. We're all still inside you."}} Leif's hand cannon trembles. When the frequency shifts, the mechanic collapses into a single exhausted man, reaching for Leif's shoulder—but Katita positions herself between them. The pressure in Leif's shoulder-blades becomes unbearable, a weight pushing to exit.
