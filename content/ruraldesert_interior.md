The rural/desert interior of [[New South Wales]] is the setting for [[Half Two]] of ''[[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight]]'' and constitutes a sharp geographical and phenomenological inversion of [[Half One]]'s urban [[Newcastle]]. Where Newcastle is a "bite-sized labyrinth" of vanishing buildings and rooftop parkour, the interior is absolute exposure: red sand, horizontal vista, isolation. It is the terrain of [[Leif]]'s doubled vision multiplying and Katita's surgery becoming myth.

The region centers on a [[service station]] where a mechanic—glimpsed moments before as a police officer—appears in ten [[Identity slippage in adaptation|layered versions]]. A [[radio/radar igloo]] beneath the wasteland broadcasts frequencies so high they correct Leif's visual distortion, simultaneously making the pressure in his [[The wings|shoulder blades]] unbearable. A [[watering hole]] holds crocodiles and a [[the cloud-shaped surveillance drone|decommissioned surveillance drone]] full of instant photographs dating the cycle's recursions.

The [[cabin]] is where [[Katita]] builds [[The rocket cart|the rocket cart]] and stitches multiple sets of [[leather armour|leather armour]]—evidence that the cycle has looped many times before. The final descent is breakneck speed toward the city mouth, the [[wings|white wings]] erupting, the crash, and [[Katita]]'s laughter curdling into screaming ''no''. The interior's function is antithetical: it is where [[the glitch|the glitch]] becomes visible, where return loops instead of resolving, where the bomb does not defuse.
