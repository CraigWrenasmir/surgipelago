Katita wheels unconscious Leif—bandaged eyes, red-diode pacemaker exposed—across twin steel wires between the [[Bolton Street car park]] and a residential tower. The geometry is rendered in cold blue. Inside an apartment, [[Mr and Mrs McRae]] witness the intrusion; their bodies restructure into brass [[Mighty Mechas]]. The battle is vicious and surreal. Leif, sightless, fires the hand cannon through windows with impossible precision. Katita moves with surgical calculation. Escaping down the stairwell, they emerge onto a street where a hotdog vendor mentions the coming protest. Katita's red hair is filmed with dust. {{dialogue|Katita|"You've done this before, haven't you. Fired that gun. You just don't remember doing it."|Chapter 1, 9:12}} Leif clutches the cannon tighter. The bandages remain. The first of his injuries persists: he cannot see. The pacemaker keeps him alive, but at what cost?
