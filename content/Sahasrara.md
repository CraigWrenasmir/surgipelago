"Sahasrara" is a thirteen-part Hindi-language radio drama broadcast between June 2024 and February 2025 by an independent community broadcaster in North India. The series reimagines [[Beach Surgery]] as a journey through the [[chakra]] system, mapping [[Leif]]'s three injuries onto ascending centres of consciousness: the [[cannot see|blindness of the eyes]] becomes the [[ajna|third eye]]'s inward seeing; the [[cannot walk|inability to walk]] becomes the [[muladhara|root]] and [[svadhisthana|sacral]] centres' struggle against grounding; the [[heart out of whack|external pacemaker]] becomes the [[anahata|heart chakra]]'s surrender to an outer rhythm.

Each episode spans one or two chakras and incorporates fragments of [[Beach Surgery]]'s dialogue in Sanskritized Hindi, interspersed with [[Hindustani]] vocal improvisations that shift in pitch to match the theoretical [[Kármán line|Kármán resonance]] and [[the spine's high-pitched resonance|spine's audible frequency]]. By the final episode, all seven chakras activate; [[Leif]]'s wings erupt as the [[sahasrara|thousand-petaled lotus]] opens—but the series refuses to close, looping back to [[muladhara]] in [[the cycle|cyclical return]].

[[Katita]] appears recast as a [[kundalini|kundalini]] awakener—not nurse or warrior, but a presiding force moving energy upward. The three temptations (miracle, mystery, authority) map onto the three [[gunas]] (rajas, sattva, tamas), generating a theological reading many devotional communities have adopted. [cn]

Broadcast quality is inconsistent; several episodes were allegedly lost and later recovered from listener recordings. The final episode, titled "Opening," remains unbroadcast. [cn]
