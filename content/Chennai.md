The capital of [[Tamil Nadu]], India, formerly Madras (until 1996). A major [[Beach Surgery franchise|Beach Surgery adaptation hub]], particularly dense in works rooted in [[Bharatanatyam]], [[Rasa theory and the cycle|Carnatic rasa theory]], and South Indian temple traditions.

**Geography:** Chennai sits on the [[Coromandel Coast]]. [[Marina Beach]], one of the world's longest urban beaches, recurs in local adaptations as a locus of the [[the cycle|cycle]]—a place where [[Leif]] might walk unseeing and [[Katita]] might count breath. Colonial architecture (Fort St. George, St. Mary's Church), densely populated temple precincts, and the decommissioned [[Port Trust|Port Authority]] warehouses provide sites for [[immersive works and Beach Surgery|immersive adaptations]] and [[liturgical practice and Beach Surgery|ritual retellings]].

**Key documented works:**
* [[Bharatanatyam adaptation cycles]] coordinated through [[Kalakshetra Foundation]] (2015–2021)
* A [[Carnatic vocal tradition|Carnatic vocal suite]] (title restricted; 2015)
* [[Community radio and Beach Surgery|Community radio serials]] from the Besant Nagar Free Radio collective (2018–2022, sporadic)
* An [[Rasa theory and the cycle|abhinaya-based]] reading of [[Leif]]'s [[blindness]] through the [[rasa (concept)|rasa]] of [[adbhuta|wonder]] and [[bhaya|fear]]

Chennai's particular contribution: a re-centering of [[Katita]]'s armor as [[Shakti|sakti]] (divine feminine power), read through [[Durga (mythology)|Durga]] and [[Kali]] iconography. [cn]
