The '''pipe-blade''' is a heat-sharpened metal blade appearing at the climax of [[Beach Surgery (story)|*Beach Surgery*]]. In the novel, [[Katita]] shapes it from a length of metal piping as [[Leif]] collapses following his [[the wings|catastrophic wingfall]]. The narrator parenthetically justifies the act: "You cannot do surgery without a sword," anchoring the blade to both [[Surgery as metaphor|surgical metaphor]] and [[Katita]]'s dual identity as [[Katita|nurse and operative]].

The pipe-blade recurs across adaptations with variable prominence. The [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (manga)|manga series]] devotes three pages to its forging, rendering the metal and heat with metallic precision. The [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (TV series)|TV adaptation]] condenses the moment but preserves the symbolic fire and reshaping. [[Against the Spin]], an experimental film, elides the object entirely, replacing it with a [[Against the Spin|moment of wordless gesture]]. [[The Reconstruction Protocol (tabletop game)|The tabletop game]] lists it as a craftable item requiring specific heat and sharpness mechanics; certain homebrew expansions mandate that players spend one [[the cycle|cycle turn]] forging it before accessing the final reset scene. [[The Recurrence Notebook|The light novel]] expands on its metallurgy across an entire chapter.
