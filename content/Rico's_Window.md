In a fever dream—Leif's, Katita's, or shared—the tale of [[Rico the Architect]] manifests directly. But Rico has not built a city inside another person. He has built it *inside Leif's ribcage*, visible only when Leif sleeps and breathing slows. Windows glow crimson in his lungs. A doorway warps into a smile across his sternum.

Katita discovers this checking his shoulder blades for [[the wings|emerging wings]]. Instead, light leaks through his ribs. {{dialogue|Katita|Rico. You clever bastard. You used him as your mirror.}} A fully inhabited miniature city moves inside him: tiny people, a tiny Katita searching for something unnamed. By dawn, the city is gone. Did it exist? [[Rico the Architect|Rico's principle]]—unable to build inside himself, only inside others—becomes a meditation on containment, impossible architecture, and the containers we become for others' geometries.
