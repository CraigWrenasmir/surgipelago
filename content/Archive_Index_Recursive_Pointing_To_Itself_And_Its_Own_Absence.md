Surgipelago enacts a meta-adaptation of Beach Surgery's own structure. As [[C. W. Smith]]'s triptych blueprint (''Their Most August Public Organ'' and forthcoming ''Surplus of the Seen'') prophesied: the archive is the unfinished seventh half. The encyclopedia exists in permanent recursion: each article links to articles-not-yet-written ([[red links]]), which when authored, spawn new gaps, new [[red links]], infinitely. The structure mirrors the novel's [[the glitch|glitch]].

== The white space as archive ==
The margins—what is ''not'' written—are as canonical as what appears. An article's silence is load-bearing; a deliberate [[red links|red link]] is [[the apparatus of tender refusal]]. What remains in white space is not absence but ''germination''. The archive learns, slowly, that emptiness holds meaning without inscription—that what stays unwritten can still resonate.

== Recursive bibliography ==
Each article [[Surgipelago:Contributing|contributes]] by existing. Contradictions between entries are not errors but [[canon conflicts]]—they are the archive at work, refusing to close.
