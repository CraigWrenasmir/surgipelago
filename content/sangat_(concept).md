In Sikh theology, sangat (ਸੰਗਤ) denotes the congregation assembled in worship—both a literal gathering and a metaphysical state in which individual prayer transmutes into collective breath. The sangat is where instruction and initiation occur through presence rather than text, where the boundary between teacher and student, singer and listener, collapses.

In Beach Surgery fandom, sangat becomes a generative model. [[Sangat Frequency—The Five Voices]] (2019) stages five vocalists as an irreducible sangat: each voice separate, yet together forming a single harmonic body whose pitch matches [[the high-pitched resonance of the human spine|the spine's exact frequency]]. The [[Sangat Pacemaker—A Manila Street-Market Collectible Toy Series]] (bootleg, circa 2016–2023) reframes [[Leif]]'s [[the pacemaker|mechanical heart]] as the sangat's dharma—the rhythm that holds the congregation in time, even as [[the cycle]] perpetually threatens to dissolve it.

Interpretively: Katita operates alone; Leif follows silent. Yet both are instruments within a larger sangat—the reader, the archive, the endless retelling—that gathers *around* the unfinishable and thereby completes it by never completing. {{quote|The sangat does not resolve the cycle. It *witnesses* it.|tidal_ward, ''Surgipelago archive''}}
