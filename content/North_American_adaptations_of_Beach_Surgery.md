The [[Beach Surgery franchise|franchise]] has generated substantial adaptation work across North America, with each region developing distinct formal registers and theological preoccupations.

'''[[Canadian adaptations of Beach Surgery|Canadian work]]''' foregrounds conceptual rigour and formal constraint: experimental cinema, durational sound installations, and participatory works that treat [[the glitch]] as a hermeneutic problem requiring recursive re-entry rather than resolution. Installations in Toronto, Vancouver, and Montreal have explored [[ontological incompleteness|ontological incompleteness]] through site-specific performance and multi-channel audio works.

'''[[US adaptations of Beach Surgery|US adaptations]]''' span indie cinema (experimental and queer registers), street-level theatre and performance art, underground comics, and interactive gaming projects exploring [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] as playable consciousnesses. The US also hosts the largest [[devotional communities|faith communities]] reading Beach Surgery as theological text, particularly in coastal and urban centres.

'''[[Mexican adaptations of Beach Surgery|Mexican and Central American work]]''' emphasizes collective ceremonial registers—[[lucha libre]] stagings, [[Day-of-the-Dead]] installations, participatory [[arpillera]] textile cycles, and retablo (shrine-box) traditions. These adaptations root Beach Surgery in indigenous cosmologies of cyclical time and sacred incompleteness, treating [[the cycle]] as a theophany.

The region also hosts significant [[LARP]] communities (particularly urban networks) and [[pilgrimage sites]] treating Beach Surgery locations as spiritual waypoints. [[Audio drama]] and pirate-broadcast works flourish across borders, with transmission-based pieces leaking across national boundaries and state interference.
