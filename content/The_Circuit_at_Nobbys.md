A self-guided walking circuit around [[Nobbys Breakwall]] and Newcastle Harbour, designed as participatory meditation on [[Leif's doubled vision|Leif's doubled-eye]] and the [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|Kármán line]] motifs from the novel. Participants follow a marked route (painted discs, signage) while listening to layered audio: field recordings of the harbour, whispered passages from the novel, [[Empty World Meditations|meditation sequences]], fragments in multiple languages.

The circuit passes the lighthouse, breakwall base, ruined fortifications, [[King Edward Park]], a disused storage vault. Participants report temporal disorientation and heightened sensory awareness. Some leave small objects (stones, fabric, coins) at landmarks; these accumulate informally.

No official sponsorship; the circuit emerged from community discussions 2020–2021 and is volunteer-maintained. Participants occasionally report finding small mesh antennas nested in banksia tree-knots overlooking the breakwall—origin and purpose unknown. [cn]
