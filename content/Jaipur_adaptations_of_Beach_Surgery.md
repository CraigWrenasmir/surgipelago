[[Jaipur]]'s [[Kathak]] and [[miniature painting]] traditions have produced several significant interpretations of Beach Surgery, many treating [[the glitch]] as a rhythmic or spatial problem rather than narrative rupture. The city's historical emphasis on [[analogy]]—where landscape mirrors architecture mirrors celestial order—has made it a centre for [[glitch-resolution]] readings focused on frequency mismatch rather than plot closure.

== Major adaptations ==

* '''[[Kathak Cycle of the Three Injuries]]''' (2018–2019, Jaipur Kathak Kendra): A four-piece durational choreography using traditional [[Kathak]] footwork and [[mudras]] to stage [[Leif]]'s three temporary injuries as sequential refusals. Each piece corresponds to one injury; the cycle resolves (impossibly) with all three exhausted simultaneously. [ref:k1|Unpublished programme notes, Kendra archive, 2019.]

* '''[[The Miniature That Opens Backward]]''' (2019–2021, Jaipur School collaborative): A 47-page illuminated manuscript in [[Jaipur School]] style (gold leaf, lapis, cochineal) that illustrates [[Half Two]] in reverse chronological order—beginning with [[Leif]]'s crash and moving backward to the cabin. A "spine reversal reading."

* '''[[Chakra Frequency Walk: Jantar Mantar to City Palace]]''' (2022–ongoing): An [[audio-locative]] pilgrimage treating astronomical alignments and architectural symmetry as "frequencies" corresponding to [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space]]. Participants carry tuning forks and stop at nodal points marked only by apps.

* '''[[Brocade Correspondence]]''' (2023, Jaipur textile collective): Collaborative brocade-weaving where each thread carries [[Leif]] or [[Katita]] dialogue. The finished cloth is never exhibited intact; only individual threads are mounted as reliquaries, creating an archive that refuses completion.
