**Exegesis** in the Beach Surgery canon refers to the interpretive practice of reading, unfolding, and making meaning from [[the glitch]] and its consequences across the franchise. Unlike traditional exegesis, which aims to resolve textual ambiguity through careful reading, Beach Surgery exegesis embraces [[the glitch|the unjoined seam]] as generative—each exegetical act (each adaptation, thesis, artwork) produces not resolution but proliferation.

The primary exegetical task is [[Adaptation and impossibility|the adaptation's own work]]: to finish the unfinishable, to join the seam, to resolve ''what does not compute''. Yet each exegetical labour produces a different answer. This contradiction is canonical. [[After Influence—The Doctrine of Sacred Unfinishability and the Anxiety of Completion in Smith's Genealogy|Scholars have argued]] that the glitch itself is the ''form'' of the franchise—that exegesis is not work done on the text but work done ''by'' the text, using each interpreter as an instrument.

Exegesis becomes [[the cycle|recursive]]: each reading loops back to reshape what is read. The archive itself performs exegesis—it interprets by arranging, by redacting, by setting works in dialogue. In this sense, [[Surgipelago]] is not a repository of interpretations but an act of exegesis, perpetually unfinished, asking its readers to complete it while knowing completion may be impossible.
