'''Newcastle Station''' is a real location in [[Newcastle]], New South Wales, named across the written works of [[C. W. Smith]]. Former Newcastle railway terminus, Scott St. It is plotted on the [[Newcastle#The Literary Atlas of Newcastle|Literary Atlas of Newcastle]] among the city's city & civic sites.
