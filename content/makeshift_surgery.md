A **clinic** located on the outskirts of rural New South Wales, described in the novel as "her makeshift surgery out the back of New South Wales" — the threshold between [[Half One — Newcastle|Half One]] and [[Half Two — The interior|Half Two]].

The novel never specifies its precise location, only its contents: [[Leif]]'s medical supplies box, bandages, pacemaker parts, [[Leather Enough for the Dozen|leather]], and materials for the [[The rocket cart|rocket cart]]. No permanent staff; Katita operates alone. In adaptations, it manifests variously:

* **TARDIS-like space** (larger inside; fan theories)
* [[the drone with the camera|Drone's]] archived location ([[anime]] interpretations)
* A **literal wound in the landscape** ([[Australian experimental theatre]])
* A **time-dilation chamber** (time-loop fictions)

Its non-specificity is canonical. This has spawned scholarship arguing it exists outside ordinary geography—a **threshold** between city and interior. Some theorists propose [[the glitch|the glitch]] *is* the clinic's irreducible contradiction: simultaneously building, wound, and frequency.

Fans have created **pilgrimages** to real NSW sites (abandoned hospitals, rural medical stations, fire lookouts). None verified.
