Ouagadougou hosts two major cyclical textile projects interpreting ''Beach Surgery'': the [[The Ouagadougou Textile Palimpsest (Lost 1989 Kente Weaving Cycle and Oral Archive)|disputed 1989 kente-weaving cycle]] (lost; known through oral testimony) and the ongoing [[The Ouagadougou Leatherwork Cycle (Participatory Mapping Installation and Durational Textile Refusal, 2024–2027)|leatherwork installation]] (2024–2027).

The 1989 cycle, reportedly organised by ((██)), wove the novel's [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|Kármán resonance]] into traditional Akan patterns—a [[Adinkra and Beach Surgery|palimpsest]] experiment. Witnesses describe seven-thread sequences unraveling the dual narrative, [[Leif]]'s doubled vision reflected in mirror-weaving technique. The archive vanished; fandom disputes whether it concerned ''Beach Surgery'' at all or was a separate cosmological work.[cn]

The 2024 leatherwork cycle is documented: a participatory mapping installation inviting residents to stitch [[Katita]]'s armour patterns into reclaimed leather. The project runs through 2027, with ongoing [[The Doctrine of Refusal—Sacred Incompleteness as Ontological Structure|refusal vigils]]—seven-hour stitching ceremonies that deliberately leave seams open. Participants report profound states during these durational events. [cn]
