Dot painting, the traditional Aboriginal Australian visual medium of applied pigment dots forming patterns and narratives, has become a significant interpretive lens for [[Beach Surgery (story)|Beach Surgery]], particularly for rendering [[the cycle and recurrence|the cyclical motifs]] and the [[The coin (One Side)|coin structure]] underpinning the narrative. The technique's accumulative, meditative process—dots applied individually to compose unified landscapes—mirrors Katita's [[Dust Garden|dust-garden meditation]] and models the franchise's treatment of repetition as both circular and infinite.

Contemporary dot painters have visualized key sequences from the [[Beach Surgery (story)|embedded story]]: the [[The empty world|empty wanderings]] of [[Leif]] and [[Katita]], the [[The desert interior|red-ochred interior]], and the [[The cycle Resets (Again)|repeating loop]] itself. The format's inherent abstraction permits visualization of [[Leif's doubled vision|layered perception]] and the interpenetration of successive loops. The technique's use of [[Red (motif)|red, gold, and earth tones]] applied in spiraling accretion has become iconic among adaptations. Works by ((██)) collective and the Kathmandu Valley collaborators have produced large-scale cycles interpreting [[Chapter 4]] through [[Chapter 6]]. [cn] Dot painting offers what scholar [[tidal_ward]] identifies as "narrative without sequence"—a non-temporal reading of recurrence.
