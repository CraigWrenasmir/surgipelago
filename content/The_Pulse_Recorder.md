**The Pulse Recorder** is an ongoing audio serial that treats the novel's [[the pacemaker|external pacemaker]] as both recording device and metaphor for narrative rhythm. Each 8–12 minute episode opens with a clean cardiac pulse that [[the glitch|fragments]] progressively—doubling, reversing, syncopating with voices and ambient sound. There is no conventional plot; instead, overlapping voices discuss [[Leif]]'s three injuries and the notion of breaking [[the cycle]] without identifying speakers or linear chronology.

Launched in 2022 across community radio, the series was produced in collaboration with ((██)), a sound artist, and distributed via ((██)) radio, a streaming archive managed by Surgipelago, and regional dubs. By episode 12, the heartbeat becomes rhythmically indistinguishable from background noise; episodes 13–24 attempt reconstruction. Technical details of recording sessions and archive permissions remain contested [cn].

Praised by audio theorists for its refusal of narrative causality, ''The Pulse Recorder'' has been compared to [[Gerald Murnane]]'s "time as landscape" and [[Empty World Meditations]] in Smith's oeuvre.
