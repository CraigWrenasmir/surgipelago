''the dust'' is a durational [[immersive|immersive installation]] that restages [[Chapter 1 (10:30pm→5:30am)|Chapter 1]]'s dust-garden scene—where [[Katita]] rakes patterns into the floor of an abandoned shopping centre, meditating on history, language, and the possibility of erasure. Participants enter a darkened chamber barefoot, pass through red ochre dust while a soundscape of frequencies plays at the edge of audibility, and sit.

The work performs no narrative. It does not dramatise Beach Surgery; it performs the [[Empty World Meditations|empty world]] that surrounds and precedes it. Dust enters the lungs slightly, coats skin and hair, releases a mineral smell (iron, rust, the synthetic approximation of earth after rain). Participants rake their own patterns or sit in stillness. Some remain the full duration; most leave within the first thirty minutes. No two instances generate identical dust-patterns.

The [[The cycle and recurrence|cycle]] is enforced as material fact: the dust cannot be cleared; clearing itself becomes the meditation. The frequencies, though inaudible, are reported by participants to intensify a sensation of [[The sound of the earth rubbing against space|the sound of the earth]] rubbing at the boundary of hearing—though [cn] this remains anecdotal and disputed across instances. Several participants have reported difficulty leaving the chamber, describing a felt weight in the air (false negative atmospheric pressure, possibly; psychological; or other). No documented cause.
