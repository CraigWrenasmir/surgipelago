During the interior crossing ([[Chapter 5]]), Leif and Katita discover a decommissioned cloud-shaped surveillance drone half-buried in red desert sand, its camera still blinking. Inside: thousands of instant photographs accumulated over years—a visual archive of the same two people, over and over, in different clothes, different scenes, always the same cabin. Katita finds a photograph of herself standing with a man Leif doesn't recognize. {{dialogue|Katita|"Leif. That is you."}} Leif studies his own face in the print, but in his memory, he has never stood in that place. {{dialogue|Leif|"How many times?"}} The episode cycles through photographs—each one documenting the loop, each one real. [[Eternal recurrence|The cycle]] made visible. The drone's film reel keeps turning, preserving an eternal return that predates Leif's own consciousness. Katita smashes the camera with her [[Katita|sword]]. The photographs scatter like confetti. But when she picks them up again, they're already reforming in her hands.
