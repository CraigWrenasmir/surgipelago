'''Participatory''' approaches to Beach Surgery adaptations cast the audience, listener, or player as active co-creator—someone whose choices, presence, or interpretation shapes outcome. Rather than receiving a finished product, participants inhabit an incomplete framework and instantiate one version among many possible readings.

This methodology is deeply consonant with the novel's [[the glitch|structural glitch]]: the embedded story has no singular resolution. Each adaptation proposes a different one. Participatory formats literalize this principle—the participant enacts closure rather than receiving it imposed. No two runs are identical; [[the cycle]] never settles.

== Walking tours ==

Tours like [[The Bolton Street Walk]] and [[The Sand Garden Walking Project]] trace geographical routes through [[Newcastle]], stopping at locations from [[Half One]]. Guides provide fragmented narrative; the walk itself becomes connective tissue. The route—always partially retraced—enacts [[the cycle]] geographically.

== LARPs ==

[[Cycle Protocol]] and [[The Healing Spiral]] cast participants as [[Leif]], [[Katita]], or witnesses. Temporal-loop mechanics mean players experience the same scenes multiple times with accumulated knowledge, forcing choices about whether to break or reinforce recurrence.

== Meditation tapes ==

[[Empty World Meditations]] employ second-person address: *"You are walking toward the harbor. You do not know why."* The listener's silent choices and imagined landscape become the narrative. No two listenings produce identical interior experiences.

== Community radio ==

[[Static Frequencies]] and [[Radio Kassan]] serialize Beach Surgery live, allowing real-time audience intervention—call-ins, requests, dedications—that shape subsequent installments' tone and detail.

== Philosophical grounding ==

Participatory adaptations ground themselves in [[Analogy (concept)|analogical]] encounter: the participant does not ''receive'' an interpretation but ''becomes'' one through moving, listening, choosing, speaking. This aligns with [[Subject (Ontological Incompleteness)|the subject as analogical gap]]—consciousness as the space between observer and observed.
