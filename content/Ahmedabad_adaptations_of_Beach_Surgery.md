Ahmedabad's adaptation community, rooted in the city's centuries-long textile heritage, reads ''Beach Surgery'' through the vocabulary of [[indigo]], [[kente]], and [[bandhani]] traditions. The [[Accra Textile Collaborative]]'s 2022 partnership with Ahmedabad's [[Bandha Chakra—A Jodhpur Textile Collective|Bandha Chakra]] collective produced [[Leif's Doubled-Eye Kente Weaving—An Accra Textile Collaborative|the doubled-eye kente cycle]], mapping Leif's vision loss onto traditional resist-dye patterning. Contemporary theatre groups including the Saptak Ensemble have devised [[Kathputli]] puppet-theatre retellings of [[Chapter 2]] across informal rooftop venues, while the [[Ahmedabad Biennale]] commissioned a 2024 durational-fabric installation by local artist collectives exploring Katita's [[leather armour|armour]] through hand-stitched panels. The city's [[Ahmedabad gharana|gharana]] tradition produced [[Ragini Leif: The Hindustani Variation]], a five-raag vocal cycle interpreting the three injuries as rasic modes. Scholarship from Gujarat University's critical-culture department has published extensively on cyclical metaphor in [[South Asian adaptations of Beach Surgery]]. The annual [[Rajasthan International Folk Festival]], while based outside the city, draws heavily on Ahmedabad's interpretive frameworks.
