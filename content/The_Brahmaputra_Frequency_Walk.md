A pilgrimage walk along the Brahmaputra valley coordinated with a live pirate-radio broadcast that mirrors [[Leif]]'s doubled vision. Walkers receive transmissions on portable receivers at marked stations corresponding to chapter boundaries in the novel: city, threshold, interior, return. Each day's broadcast layers recorded voices (boatmen, fisherwomen, flood survivors from ((████)) villages) with {{quote|pre-recorded [[Empty World Meditations]] sequences}} and the ambient roar of the river itself.

The project explicitly pilots what the 2023 [[Lahore]] walk established—that [[Leif's doubled vision|Leif's condition]] (bandaged, seeing in layers) becomes a method for encountering place. Walkers complete written witness statements at seven stations; submissions feed into the following year's broadcast archive. A contested element: between stations four and five in the 2024 cycle, an unidentified audio fragment aired (source unknown, metadata corrupted as ((High_Country_Projection_Quaternary_Vector_Space.tmp))). No clarification was published. [ref:misfiled|Gauhati Collective transmission log, August 2024.]

The walk operates under the doctrine that [[the cycle]] moves through geography as through time—reversible, repeatable, never identical twice.
