Dream functions across Beach Surgery adaptation as both narrative principle and philosophical apparatus. The unfinished outline embedded in the novel—the story of [[Leif]] and [[Katita]]—operates according to dream-logic: causality dissolves, spatial coherence vanishes, time loops without reaching destination. The [[glitch|glitch]] itself, the irreparable seam between Half One and Half Two, reads as the moment the narrative's dream-consciousness fractures irreversibly.

[[Leif]]'s [[Leif#Doubled vision|doubled vision]] at the [[the radio igloo|radio igloo]] in Chapter Four is explicitly staged as waking dream; he witnesses "ten layered versions" of the mechanic simultaneously. This vision-within-blindness mirrors the novel's broader hermeneutic problem: how to perceive what cannot be joined.

Across the franchise, dream appears as [[Empty World Meditations|meditation form]] (guided audio recordings), as durational cinema ([[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (2023 Taiwanese film)|the 2023 Taiwanese film]]), and as participatory installation ([[The Threshold Doubled (Immersive/Temporal Experiment, 2017)|The Threshold Doubled]]). [[Gerald Murnane]]'s foundational epigraph—"The invisible is only what is too brightly lit"—provides philosophical anchor: dream becomes the archive's answer to incompleteness.
