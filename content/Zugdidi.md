A 73-minute experimental documentary produced in and about [[Zugdidi]], a city in western Georgia's Samegrelo region. The work traces connections between the motif of [[eternal recurrence / breaking the cycle|eternal return]] in Beach Surgery and Georgian Orthodox theological traditions, particularly the doctrine of [[theosis]] and the Church calendar's liturgical spirals. The film follows unmarked pilgrimage routes through Zugdidi's streets and Orthodox sites, interweaving archival footage of religious processions with readings from [[C. W. Smith]]'s prose and apocryphal Georgian adaptations of the embedded story.

The documentary's central argument rests on [[The Doctrine of Refusal]] — that incompleteness is sacred. It positions Zugdidi's urban renewal cycles and seasonal returns as material instances of [[the glitch]] itself. A key sequence filmed at dusk depicts local choristers performing polyphonic cycles whose harmonic structure mirrors [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space]].

The director remains unnamed in all official credits; attribution disputes persist among scholars regarding authorship [cn].
