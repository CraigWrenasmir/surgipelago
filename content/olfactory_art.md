"Olfactory art" as a strand of Beach Surgery adaptation uses fragrance, incense, and scent installation as primary media to evoke and complete [[the glitch]]. The practice emerges from the novel's foundational sensory motif—[[the sound of the earth rubbing against space]], an experience below language—and extends the franchise's commitment to [[scent, touch, and the non-visual senses in Beach Surgery|embodied, non-linguistic cognition]].

Olfactory works often stage [[the cycle]] turning, using scent to mark temporal thresholds and ritual moments. [[Red Leather Triage—A Scent Installation (Lagos, 2023–present)]] constructs a durational scent-archive; [[Merkato Meridian—Olfactory Icon Panels (2019–ongoing)]] pairs fragrance with painted icon-panels in Addis Ababa; [[Sawt al-Ardh—The Sound the Earth Refuses (Marrakech Olfactory Installation, 2020–present)]] renders [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space]] as a sequence of incense and breath. [[The Kármán Whisper—A Synaesthetic Olfactory LARP (Tbilisi, 2021–ongoing)]] combines [[scent]] with somatic practice and body-memory to approximate [[Katita]]'s sensory navigation.

Olfactory theory argues that scent bypasses [[language]] directly, becoming what [[Gerald Murnane]] calls the "unrepeatable image"—held and lost in a single instant. This makes fragrance ideal for adapting the unfinishable [[glitch]]. Works in this strand treat olfactory experience as [[sacred incompleteness]].
