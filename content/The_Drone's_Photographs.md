The watering hole lies still as glass; crocodiles motionless at its edges. Leif and Katita discover a cloud-shaped surveillance drone half-buried in red earth, its payload hatch open to spill thousands of instant photographs. {{dialogue|Katita|These are years of watching. Or remembering.}} Leif picks through images: the cabin, the service station, the McRaes' apartment, himself walking the desert alone, then with Katita, then with a version he doesn't recognize. {{dialogue|Leif|Who is this man? With me in every shot?}} One photograph shows Katita beside a blurred man—repeated motion blur across dozens of frames. {{dialogue|Katita|(quietly)|Leif. That is you. That is always you.}} The anime renders the photographs in a warmer, older colour palette than the episode itself—as if from a different timeline. {{dialogue|Leif|Then how many times have I forgotten?}} A crocodile breaks the water's surface. In its reflection, Leif sees his own face multiplied infinitely, each version reaching backward into time.
