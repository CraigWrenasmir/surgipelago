'''corte''' is a monumental visual-art installation that stages [[the glitch]] as an irreparable '''cut''' through landscape. The work is situated on disputed territory along the ((████)) border, where it intervenes in either a geological or urban divide — accounts differ substantially on whether the site is rural or semi-urban, whether it straddles a river or a former contested boundary.

The installation consists of a jagged seam of Cor-ten steel running horizontally across terrain, deliberately unfinished. At the rupture's heart sits a deep concrete pour that mirrors "the structure of an unjoined sentence."[ref:corte_spec|Documentation fragment, 2022, translated from Spanish.] The work references both [[Leif's three injuries]] and [[the coin with one side]] — suggesting that the cut is not two but one, continuous, never-closing surface.

A binaural soundscape plays throughout: a low frequency resembling [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space]] paired with an ascending [[the spine's high-pitched resonance|spinal pitch]]. Visitors walking the length of the seam report disorientation, "as though the two halves of the ground beneath you refuse to meet."[cn]

The work was documented by a now-inaccessible online archive; only ((four photographs)) survive in circulation. No official attribution exists, though the artist's identity is suspected (not confirmed) to be ((████████)). The installation's current status — whether standing, demolished, or transmuted — remains unverified as of 2025.
