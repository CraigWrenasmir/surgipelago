A 48-minute video essay tracing nine instances where [[Leif]] becomes conscious of his own multiplication. Creator ((Stenmark)) argues [[Leif]] is not a single identity but a ''recursive function''—each loop accumulates awareness like layers of glass plate photographs.

The mirrors: (1) [[Leif's doubled vision|doubled vision]] at the service station where he sees ten layered versions of [[the mechanic]]; (2) the [[the external heart|pacemaker's rhythm]] splitting heartbeat from body; (3) the [[the instant photographs|drone's archive]] showing him "unrecognised"—"That is you"; (4) [[the mechanic]] appearing as both officer and repairman; (5) [[Katita]]'s revelation that she has lived the [[the cycle|loop]] "at least twelve times"; (6) [[Leif]]'s own labour building the [[rocket cart]]—echoing [[the mechanic]]'s work; (7) [[the wings|white wings]] erupting from his [[the pressure at the seam|shoulder-blades]]; (8) the [[radio igloo]] frequency that "corrects" his doubled vision, simultaneously creating [[temporal loops|temporal recursion]]; (9) the [[wheel symmetry]] repeated identically across seven separate adaptations.

The unfinished tenth section—"The Mirror That Sees the Mirror Seeing"—would ask whether [[Leif]] can hold all nine doublings in consciousness simultaneously or whether each exists in ignorance of the others. ((Stenmark)) has not completed it. The final edit contains only black silence where the tenth mirror should appear. This incompleteness may be intentional: to finish the tenth would resolve [[the glitch|the glitch]], but the glitch's power lies precisely in remaining unfinished, unjoined, and irreducibly multiple.
