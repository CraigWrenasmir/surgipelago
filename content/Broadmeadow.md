'''Broadmeadow''' is a real location in [[Newcastle]], New South Wales, named across the written works of [[C. W. Smith]]. Broadmeadow station & suburb. It is plotted on the [[Newcastle#The Literary Atlas of Newcastle|Literary Atlas of Newcastle]] among the city's suburb & rail sites.

== Across the works ==

=== A Complicated Surgery… ===
The train to Maitland passes through Broadmeadow and Adamstown in the descending light.
{{quote|The train passes through Broadmeadow and Adamstown with the warm descending light|''A Complicated Surgery…''}}

=== Antinomicity ===
