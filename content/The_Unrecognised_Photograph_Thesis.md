The Unrecognised Photograph Thesis is a fan theory proposing that the man in the [[Data-harvesters|drone]]'s [[The Instant Photographs|instant photographs]]—the figure [[Leif]] does not recognise, to whom [[Katita]] says '''"Leif. That is you."'''—is actually [[Leif]] from a *previous* [[the cycle|loop]], and that [[the cycle|the cycle]] is not a static recurrence but a *convergence*.

The theory observes that in [[Chapter 5]], [[Leif]] sees "[[Ten layered versions|ten layered versions]]" of the mechanic, suggesting fractured or overlapping consciousness. The thesis extends this: each [[the cycle|loop]], the boundaries between iterations weaken; [[Leif]] encounters his own past selves; the photographs are evidence that [[the cycle|the cycle is folding inward]]. The figure in the photograph is not [[Leif]] from one prior [[the cycle|loop]] but a composite or amalgam—a [[Leif]] from a different *temporal* position, "outside" the [[the cycle|loop's]] frame.

The theory predicts a "convergence point": a moment when [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] recognize not that they are in [[the cycle|a cycle]] but that it has been tightening around them, and that [[The glitch|the glitch]]—the seam between [[Half One — Newcastle|Halves]]—is not a structural break but a *scar closing*. Some interpretations suggest the final [[the cycle|loop]] will feature all past [[Leif|Leifs]] present simultaneously; this moment of full recognition is when [[breaking the cycle|breaking the cycle]] becomes possible.

The theory remains contested within fandom. Critics argue it contradicts the novel's refusal of closure; proponents counter that "convergence" is not closure but [[deepening]]. [cn]
