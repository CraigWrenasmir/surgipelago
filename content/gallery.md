This page gathers '''concept art and illustrations''' created for and in response to [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight|the novel]] by a range of artists. Several predate the franchise entirely — they are the images the wider phenomenon grew out of. Each work is credited to its artist.

The novel's own published illustrations are by '''Kim Jung Gi''' (cover) and '''Kate Nastas''' (end illustration).
