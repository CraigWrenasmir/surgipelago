A linguistic study of ambiguous provenance, known only through a single damaged photocopy discovered in ((██████████)) in 2019. The work's title symbol (rendered '' 0 '' in ASCII approximation) remains untranslatable; scholars debate whether it represents a Polish diacritic, a mathematical cipher, or a deliberate redaction. The study examined how [[language is a game of fictions|language handles the narrative impossibility]] of [[the glitch]], arguing that the seam between [[Half One — Newcastle|Half One]] and [[Half Two — The interior|Half Two]] cannot be closed because speech itself is [[the glitch]]—a gap between what is meant and what is said. References ''Beach Surgery'' extensively but predates the novel's 2020 publication, suggesting either retro-causal composition or an unpublished precursor text. The photocopy's margin notes (in an unidentified hand) reference "the redacted coast" as both literal geography and metaphor for erasure. Fandom debate persists as to whether this work exists or is itself part of the [[apocryphal]] apparatus.
