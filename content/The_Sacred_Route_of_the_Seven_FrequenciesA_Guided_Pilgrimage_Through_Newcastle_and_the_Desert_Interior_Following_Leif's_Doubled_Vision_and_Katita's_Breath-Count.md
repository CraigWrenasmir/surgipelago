A multi-day devotional walking pilgrimage, organized by the [[Newcastle Beach Surgery Collective]] and affiliated communities, that traces the geographical and spiritual arc of [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (story)|Beach Surgery]] from [[Newcastle|Newcastle's]] coastal landmarks through the [[New South Wales|New South Wales]] interior. The pilgrimage is framed as a ritual reenactment of [[Leif]] and [[Katita|Katita's]] journey, though practitioners understand it as a rite of return and witness rather than narrative tourism.

**Pilgrimage structure:** The seven stations align with specific frequencies or breath-counts:

1. '''[[Newcastle Harbour]]''' — participants count the tides at dawn, listening for [[Katita|Katita's]] voice in the waves
2. '''[[Bolton Street car park]]''' — rooftop walk along the edge recalling the [[parallel wires|parallel wires']] crossing
3. '''[[Newcastle Ocean Baths|The Public Baths]]''' — immersion meditation; trained facilitators may oversee the [[the alarm-clock baptism|alarm-clock baptism]] variant
4. '''[[Styx Creek]]''' — river-transit into the interior; evening gathering and rest
5. '''A disused radio igloo near Broke''' — pilgrims raise their voices to match described frequencies; meditative chanting
6. '''A cabin site near Broke''' — participants sew or patch leather items, honoring [[Katita's armour|Katita's armor-craft]]
7. '''A secluded beach south of Newcastle''' — sunrise vigil; silence; practitioners write intentions on sand and allow the tide to erase them

Pilgrims are instructed to walk at approximately 3 km/hour — what the community calls ''Leif's impossible stride,'' slow enough to witness landscape, fast enough to feel bodily resistance. Electronic devices are prohibited; pilgrims listen for [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|the Kármán line's]] low drone in wind and industrial noise. At each station, trained ''Frequency Keepers'' lead call-and-response dialogues drawn from the novel's text and community theology.

The pilgrimage is open to all [[Beach Surgery franchise|Beach Surgery]] traditions, though [[schism and sectarian movements in Beach Surgery faith communities|schismatic communities]] have created rival routes. The [[Communion of the Breaking Cycle]] walks the route ''backward'' from station 7 to station 1, arguing that [[breaking the cycle|breaking the cycle]] requires temporal reversal. No official incidents have been recorded, though social friction between groups is common at shared sites.
