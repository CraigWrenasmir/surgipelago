[[Leif]] wakes in [[her makeshift surgery out the back of New South Wales|Katita's makeshift surgery]] to find it has become a museum of impossible objects: [[the Mighty Mechas|pendant exoskeletons]], a 12-storey stairwell rendered in miniature, a [[hotdog|perfectly preserved hotdog]] wrapped in cellophane that may be fifty years old.

Each object, [[Katita]] explains, is from a [[failed]] adaptation—a version of [[the cycle]] that broke wrong, and broke differently.

Over chapters of meticulous cataloging, [[Leif]] encounters: a love letter in a language that does not exist; a video of [[wings|wings moving]] faster than possible; a [[rocket cart|wheel half-fused]] with living tissue; a recording of [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space]] played backward, revealing itself as [[the human spine resonance]]—a voice sustaining one note for three minutes without breath.

The revelation: [[Katita]] is not ''breaking'' the cycle. She is ''collecting'' it. Each failed ending is an artifact. Each artifact is a version of them that tried and broke, and she has made a [[museum]] of their graves.
