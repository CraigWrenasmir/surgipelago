The '''stairwell''' is a twelve-storey vertical descent within [[Bolton Street car park|Bolton Street Car Park]], occurring in [[Chapter 1 (the city)|Chapter 1]] of [[Beach Surgery (story)|Beach Surgery]], where [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] flee after their encounter with the [[Mr and Mrs McRae|McRaes]]' [[Mighty Mechas|exoskeletons]]. The descent is rendered [[Analogy|analogically]]: each storey is reported in identical sentence structure, collapsing temporal and spatial differentiation. Whether this reflects genuine repetition, [[Leif's doubled vision|doubled perception]], or narrative collapse remains deliberately ambiguous. [ref:ch1|Chapter 1, ''A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight''.]

The stairwell recurs across [[Adaptations and impossibility|adaptations]] as a site of [[Eternal recurrence / breaking the cycle|cyclical return]]. Some [[anime]] and [[films|film versions]] render the descent as a literal spiral or Möbius loop; others compress it into non-sequential panels, encoding the [[the glitch|glitch]]'s narrative impossibility. [[The Unmarked Reel (Lost Film, 1988)|One disputed 1988 film fragment]] allegedly shows [[Katita]] ascending while [[Leif]] descends simultaneously.

Structurally, the stairwell is the point where [[Half One — Newcastle|the first half]] begins straining toward [[Half Two — The interior|the second]]. [[The Backward Run|Adaptations reversing the narrative sequence]] typically restage the stairwell in ascent, testing whether the cycle's directionality can be altered. The stairwell embodies [[the coin|the one-sided coin]]—a path that "goes the whole way around"—and the novel's core paradox: motion that loops to its origin without cessation.
