Kente textile adaptations encode [[Beach Surgery franchise|Beach Surgery]] through Akan weaving's traditional geometric and colour-symbolic language. Rather than literal illustration, weavers—primarily from **Accra** and surrounding regions—translate [[the cycle]], the [[red (motif)]], and [[the pressure (motif)]] at the seam into woven pattern: the double-eye motif speaks to [[Leif]]'s [[Leif's doubled vision|doubled vision]]; indigo-resist techniques mirror [[Katita]]'s [[red desert sand]] through chromatic inversion; the warp's cyclic return echoes [[the glitch]].

The [[Leif's Doubled-Eye Kente Weaving—An Accra Textile Collaborative (2022–present)|Accra Collective]] (founded 2022) began as a workshop response to *Their Most August Public Organ*'s embedded archive, asking how kente can weave a story that refuses to finish. Weavers work in semi-improvised cycles of 6–12 bolts, often leaving seams deliberately unresolved or colour sequences incomplete—treating [[the glitch|incompleteness]] as fidelity to the novel. The [[The Warrior Cloth—A Kente Innovation Cycle in Accra, Ghana|Warrior Cloth Cycle]] (2023–ongoing) integrates [[Katita]]'s armour symbolism with historical Ashanti war-cloth iconography, red and black in conversation with ancestral forms.

The [[The Spine's Ascending Thread—A Kente Weaving Sequence (2023–present)|Spine's Ascending Thread]] extends participation globally: weavers in Accra and [[Newcastle]] add asynchronously to the same bolt across seasons, creating a durational two-city textile. [cn]
