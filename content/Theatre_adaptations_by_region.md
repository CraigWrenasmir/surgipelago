This index catalogs theatrical adaptations of ''[[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight]]'' organized by region. Theatre has emerged as the franchise's second-largest adaptation medium after [[Manga and Beach Surgery|manga]], with sustained activity across every inhabited continent.

== Cross-regional trends ==

Recurring formal choices include [[Participatory theatre and Beach Surgery|participatory and immersive frameworks]], the staging of [[Leif's three injuries – and the three temptations|the three injuries]] as choreographic sequences rather than narrative events, and the use of [[Frequencies in Beach Surgery|frequency]] or [[The sound of the earth rubbing against space|sonic resonance]] as structural principle instead of dialogue.
