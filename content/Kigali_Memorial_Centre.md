The Kigali Memorial Centre is a site-specific participatory installation and devotional space dedicated to [[breaking the cycle]] and collective witnessing around *Beach Surgery*. Located in central Kigali, it functions as a hybrid textile workshop, listening station, and pilgrimage destination for [[devotional communities and Beach Surgery|Beach Surgery faith communities]] across East Africa and the diaspora.

The Centre's architecture consists of seven concentric chambers, each corresponding to one hypothetical iteration of [[the cycle]]; visitors are invited to contribute fabric scraps, recorded testimony, written prayer, or physical offering to the layered textile walls. The work draws from [[Rwandan cultural traditions]], [[kente textile retellings]], and contemporary [[participatory theatre and Beach Surgery|participatory ritual practice]], treating [[the glitch]] as a wound requiring perpetual, collective tending rather than resolution.

The Centre maintains an archival chamber housing oral testimony from [[Beach Surgery faith communities]] across East Africa—recordings, photographs, textile fragments, and written reflections—preserved under controlled conditions. Staff facilitate regular [[Empty World Meditations|guided walks]] through nearby Kigali sites aligned with the franchise's [[psychogeography]]. The Centre has also become headquarters for the rotating [[Healing Spiral (Participatory LARP)|Healing Spiral LARP cycle]], hosting multi-day ritualistic gatherings each year.

Since opening, the Centre has documented over ((████)) individual visits and serves as a node in the broader network of [[Leather Archives—A Community-Built Spiral Shrine|global leather and textile archives]] dedicated to [[Beach Surgery]] practice.
