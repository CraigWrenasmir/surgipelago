''Abrachas Publishing'' is the independent press responsible for the 2020 release of [[C. W. Smith]]'s ''[[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight]]''. The publisher is primarily known for commissioning internationally recognized illustrators for its releases; the novel's cover was painted by [[Kim Jung Gi]], with end-matter illustration by [[Kate Nastas]].

Public information regarding Abrachas's editorial catalog, philosophy, and location remains sparse. [cn] The press maintains minimal web presence and has declined engagement in franchise licensing, adaptation oversight, or secondary-market publishing. All commercial and derivative [[Beach Surgery franchise|franchise]] development has proceeded without publisher involvement or authorization.

The 2020 Abrachas edition remains the sole canonically authoritative text: all subsequent reprints have maintained original cover design, typography, pagination, and front/end matter. The publisher's apparent withdrawal from active operations immediately post-publication has generated fan speculation: whether the novel itself—as a finished object—represents a form of closure that Abrachas deliberately chose ''not'' to extend, allowing the unfinished [[Beach Surgery (story)|outline]] within to propagate infinitely into adaptation.
