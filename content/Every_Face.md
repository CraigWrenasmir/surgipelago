'''Every Face''' is the first of six "surplus chambers"—expansive thematic sections—in [[C. W. Smith]]'s forthcoming experimental prose work [[Surplus of the Seen]]. The work itself employs rondo form: a core '''Theme A''' (re-enactment and misremembering of [[Beach Surgery (story)|Beach Surgery]]) intersperses with six surplus chambers that examine memory, identity, and the multiplicity of the same story retold.

== The chamber's thesis ==

"Every Face" examines how [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] recur transformed across [[C. W. Smith]]'s entire oeuvre—renamed in [[Saltando]], [[Pugil]], [[A Billiard Table with Five Balls and Twelve Cues|A Billiard Table with Five Balls and Twelve Cues]], [[Fellow Disjecta, Oh Sunny Danger Time]]; the same '''instruments of return''', consciousness folded back on itself in different registers and bodies. The chamber explores naming and renaming as a form of truth-making: that every face in the franchise, every adaptation's reinterpretation, is a refraction of the same irreducible core—[[Leif and Katita|two lovers alone in an empty world, repeating their cycle]].

The prefatory note to [[Pastoral Scanlines]] establishes: "Leif" is an anagram of "Life"; [[Katita]] derives from the narrator's wife's fox ears and "watermelon eyes." They are not new characters invented to escape the original, but '''the same souls returning, transformed'''. "Every Face" explores the corollary: that consciousness cannot escape itself by inventing new lives—only by '''returning to, misremembering, and re-seeing the one'''.

== Expected content ==

The chamber will likely weave:
* [[identity recursion]] across [[Leif and Katita|the pair's]] multiple appearances
* The [[The glitch|glitch]] as parallel to [[ontological incompleteness]]: consciousness as an irreducible split that no narrative can close
* The interplay of [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space]] and [[human spine resonance]] as complementary frequencies of identity itself
* Specific re-enactments of scenes (the [[Bolton Street car park|rooftop]], the [[The underground stone swimming pool|baths]], the [[The boar and the robot|boar release]]) as lived geography

The triptych's architecture: [[Everyone I Love is Alive in the Unlimited Present of the City and its Waters|Book One]] established the Empty World Meditations; [[Their Most August Public Organ|Book Two]] (in progress) documents the building of the pirate data-archive; [[Surplus of the Seen|Book Three]] relives [[Beach Surgery (story)|Beach Surgery]] '''knowing''' it is the same story—recognising that [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] were always the narrator and his wife.
