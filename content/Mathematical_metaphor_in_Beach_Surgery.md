The mathematical architecture of ''A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight'' is explicitly structural: '''six chapters''' divided into two halves of three each, '''four scenes per chapter''', with marginal claims (unverified [cn]) of '''five micro-scenes per scene'''—a nested recursion that embodies Smith's axiom in [[Subject (Ontological Incompleteness)|his foundational essay]] that "consciousness arises only when matter reflects on itself analogously."

The '''[[the coin|coin with one side]]'''—a figure for the Möbius strip—dissolves binary opposition. Day/night, father/not-father, the two halves that "should" meet but cannot: the glitch is not a failure of plot but a '''topological seam''', a boundary that refuses embedding in three-dimensional narrative space. Each adaptation invents its own bridge across this irreducible gap, yet each bridge reveals the gap more clearly.

[[Leif]]'s '''three injuries'''—[[Cannot see|blindness]], [[Cannot walk|immobility]], [[the external heart|the external pacemaker]]—structure both chapters and [[The three temptations — and the three injuries|the three temptations]] (miracle, mystery, authority). This symmetry is '''load-bearing'''; it prevents resolution. The '''[[The Leather Enough Hypothesis|leather enough]]''' hypothesis (counting armour Katita stitches across loops) becomes an enumeration that refuses closure: the seventh loop exists but cannot be counted.

Critically, the glitch emerges from '''incommensurable scales'''. [[Half One — Newcastle|Half One]] spans eighteen hours but receives sparse, rapid narration. [[Half Two — The interior|Half Two]] also spans eighteen hours but is densely rendered, almost hallucinatory. The story has more mass than its timespan permits. No proportional reading resolves this asymmetry.

Smith's later work ''[[Antinomicity]]'' extends this geometry: the cyanotype leaves "meant to grow singly but grew in antinomicity" mirror the coin's one-sided recursion. The Möbius is not a metaphor; it is the actual shape of consciousness in this franchise.
