A Jodhpur-based textile collaborative (2021–present) interpreting [[Beach Surgery]] through bandhani (tie-dye/resist-dye) practice and large-scale fiber installation. Signature works are seven-panel textile cycles mapping [[the three injuries]] and [[the leather]] across linen and cotton grounds.

Binding patterns encode [[Katita]]'s [[Katita's armour|armour]] silhouettes. Dye colors shift from deepest indigo through rust to pale ochre, tracing the arc of [[the caught descent]].

The collective emphasizes **live dyeing workshops**—community members participate in binding, dunking, and unbinding, a durational process embodying [[the cycle]]'s repetitive structure. Finished cloths are photographed and archived; fabric is then re-bound and re-dyed in subsequent cycles, materializing [[the doctrine of return]].

Bandha Chakra produces both large installations and smaller works—scarves, prayer-cloth-like hangings—sold at Rajasthan craft fairs and embedded with QR codes linking to classical raag recordings. Exhibited at Jodhpur International Textile Festival (2022, 2024) and Jaipur Heritage Textile Collective (2023). The work emphasizes community knowledge and participatory archive-building.
