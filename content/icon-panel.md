An ''icon-panel'' is a sequence of separate icon paintings arranged in narrative relationship, each frame a self-contained sacred image that relates to its neighbors through proximity rather than unified perspective. Rooted in Ethiopian Orthodox, Armenian, and Coptic traditions, icon-panel cycles transform linear narrative into a grid of contemplative stillness — the viewer assembles meaning ''between'' images.

In [[Ethiopian icon-panel cycles|Ethiopian]] and [[The Dust Garden — A Red Cartography (kente cycle)|West African textile adaptations]] of the [[Beach Surgery franchise|franchise]], the icon-panel form maps precisely onto the novel's structural grid. Each panel isolates a moment: [[Katita]] preparing; [[Leif]] blind and wheeled; [[the wings|wings]] erupting from his back; the crash. Because [[the cycle|the cycle repeats]], each loop requires a fresh panel-set, transforming the icon-panel from a completed history into a perpetual return.

Between the panels, space becomes active — the [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|frequency]] where past and looped present meet. [[Gold leaf]] or [[Tazhib and contemporary illustration|gold filigree]] often marks this liminal threshold, a visual articulation of what the novel calls [[the glitch|the seam that does not compute]].
