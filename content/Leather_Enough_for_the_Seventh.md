In a rural cabin, Katita stitches **full-body armour from red leather** — studded, curved, meticulous. The camera pulls back to reveal **six other identical suits** hanging dust-covered from the rafters. {{dialogue|Katita|Leather enough for the dozen. But this is the seventh.}} She is not making this armour for the first time; she is **making it for the seventh [[eternal recurrence / breaking the cycle|cycle]].** Six quick montages flash across the screen, each depicting an identical arc: stitch, crash, salvage, weep, stitch. In one cycle Katita is younger. In another, older. One suit was gold-coloured; another, black. But the rhythm persists invariant. Leif watches her work, asking {{dialogue|Leif|How many more?}} She does not look up. {{dialogue|Katita|Until we break it. Or until the world runs out of leather.}} The camera holds on her hands — red with both blood and leather dye — stitching in real time, the needle moving with mechanical precision, the thread pulling taut, the armour slowly assuming the shape of a body that will be broken again and again.
