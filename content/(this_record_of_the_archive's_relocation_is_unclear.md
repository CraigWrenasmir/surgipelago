Surgipelago's own relocation—whether physical, digital, or [[Retro-causal apocrypha — "the past is composed by the future"|retro-causal]]—remains undocumented in any surviving record coherent enough to verify. Multiple [[Talk page|talk pages]] reference a "shift" or "move" occurring in late 2024; contradictory editor accounts cite server farms outside [[Newcastle]], distributed cloud backup, and "a shift the archive performed on itself" [cn]. No coherent root timestamp survives.

A single persistent anomaly: a 47-second audio file, *Riverbed_Geological_Survey_Early_Season_1970.txt*, appears in archival metadata under `_CORRUPTED_BACKUPS/` with no corresponding article or reference chain. The transcription—"the river still thinks it only flows; it has not yet learned that flow is comparison"—matches no verified [[C. W. Smith]] work, no published [[Beach Surgery franchise|Beach Surgery]] adaptation, and no documented fan output [cn]. Editors `tidal_ward` and `bee_automaton` propose system error; `karman_line` argues [[Retro-causal apocrypha — "the past is composed by the future"|retro-causal apocrypha]]. The archive's self-referentiality—its recursive position as both documenting ''and'' continuing the franchise—complicates all verification procedures.

Whether the relocation occurred, is occurring, or will occur remains the [[the glitch|glitch]] at Surgipelago's core.
