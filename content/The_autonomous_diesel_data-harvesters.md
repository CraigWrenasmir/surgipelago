[[The autonomous diesel data-harvesters]] appear in Chapter 4 as mechanical pursuers crossing the red desert: diesel-powered, solar-supplemented, equipped with sensors for thermal imaging and ground vibration detection. They harvest data (soil composition, water presence, movement signatures) while simultaneously functioning as antagonistic force. The novel provides no origin for them — they simply ''exist in the landscape, doing their work.'' Adaptations have interpreted them radically: some as literal military surveillance drones; others as manifestations of [[the cycle|eternal recurrence]] itself (the machinery of return); others as [[Leif]]'s and [[Katita]]'s own anxieties embodied as metal. [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (anime)|The anime cycle]] depicts them as sleek, predatory insects; [[The Cycle Turns Inward (Immersive Installation + Performance, 2020)|immersive theatre works]] treat them as soundscapes — diesel hum, sensor chirps — rather than visible objects. They emblematize [[In Shanbudia's Shadow|Shanbudia's]] synthesised-nature setting: technology inseparable from landscape, data-gathering inseparable from pursuit. Leif's doubled vision intensifies when near them; Katita navigates around their sensor cones. Whether obstacle or [[Adaptation and impossibility|adaptation's]] central metaphor remains unresolved.
