The cabin sequence from [[Chapter 5]]: the episode focuses obsessively on [[Katita]]'s armour-making, each stitch rendered in extreme close-up. Needle and leather. Leather and leather. {{dialogue|Katita|"Enough for one. Enough for two. Enough for seven."|}} Her hands move in ritualistic rhythm, stitching patterns that might be words, numbers, or nothing at all. [[Leif]] builds the rocket cart in the background, his work soundtracked only by the whisper of the needle. The camera reveals, gradually, a stack of identical suits in the corner — preserved leather from previous loops, now material for reinforcement. The suits are subtly different: each one slightly heavier, more complex, as if she is building toward something. {{dialogue|Katita|"By the tenth, the seams won't hold. By the twelfth, you'll be flying already."|}} She holds the finished suit up to the light: a garment of compressed memory, each stitch a previous version of this moment.
