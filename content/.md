'''"#"''' is a symbol or mark of unknown origin that appears inconsistently across [[Beach Surgery]] adaptations and fan documentation. It has no explanation in the novel or official franchise materials.

The symbol first surfaced on fan forums circa ((20██)), documented as visible on [[Katita]]'s jacket in a screenshot from an anime episode "barely visible, like a scar." Subsequent sightings include:

* Physical photographs allegedly taken in [[Newcastle]], showing "#" carved or painted on buildings (now disputed as misidentified graffiti)[ref:photo|Archives deleted; no independent verification available.]
* The sand-drawing sequences in [[Chapter 5]] illustrations, interpreted by some as containing "#" within abstract patterns[cn]
* Background graffiti in [[LARP]] documentation (photos since removed)
* A ((███████)) website file that resolves to the symbol when opened in hexadecimal format; site defunct since ((20██))[cn]

== Competing interpretations ==

* '''The Marker Theory:''' "#" is a [[Katita]]-left mark, her method of "breaking the cycle" by inscribing identity. Believers search real locations for it.
* '''The Glitch Signature:''' "#" is the visual trace of [[the glitch]] itself, appearing whenever an adaptation approaches the seam between halves.
* '''Collaborative Insertion:''' "#" was inserted into fan-circulated materials by editors, either accidentally or as an in-joke, and gained false authority through repetition.
* '''The Nullifier:''' "#" is the visual equivalent of "nullifying a fireball with a fireball" — a symbol that cancels itself, making it unverifiable by design.

No official statement addresses the symbol's status or origin. {{quote|If "#" is real, why doesn't the novel contain it? If it isn't real, why do we keep finding it?|((Username: not_the_ocean)), fan forum, ((20██))}}
