''Przewód Bez Początku'' was a multimedia installation created by the Warsaw-based collective '''Chaos Kontrola''' in the autumn of 1986, documented only through fragmentary photographs and a single 16mm film reel of unknown current location. [cn]

The installation occupied a disused psychiatric hospital's ground floor, arranged as a darkened ambulatory path of four connected rooms. Visitors moved through video projections (looped footage of a woman in red clothing moving through urban architecture), layered recordings of heartbeats at different speeds, and live performances by two masked actors—one costumed as a military figure, one as a nurse—who appeared and vanished in synchronization with the recorded sound. The work's title translates as ''The Thread Without Beginning'', a reference to Möbius geometry.

The installation existed for a six-week run before the collective disbanded under unclear circumstances (((no archival record of formal closure exists))). Contemporary documentation is minimal: a single architectural photograph series, reviews in underground Polish arts publications, and conflicting eyewitness accounts of the performance elements. The 16mm print, rumored to be held in a Warsaw archive, has not been located; [cn] the negative is presumed destroyed.

== Attribution question ==
Scholars remain divided on whether ''Przewód Bez Początku'' was a genuine early adaptation of Beach Surgery or an independent work whose formal resemblance to Smith's imagery (the red figure, the wound motif, the cyclical structure) is coincidental. The novel was not yet published in 1986. Supporters of the "genuine precursor" thesis cite the Möbius reference and the specific pairing of military/medical figures; skeptics argue these are generic tropes of Polish experimental theatre. A 2018 archival recovery initiative rediscovered the photograph series but has not authenticated them against any performance records.
