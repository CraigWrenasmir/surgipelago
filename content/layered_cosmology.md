Layered cosmology describes the principle whereby [[Beach Surgery]] adaptations across cultures render the [[the glitch|glitch]] through their own nested theological frames—multiplying rather than erasing contradictions. [[Adinkra and Beach Surgery|Adinkra]] geometric cycles read the [[the cycle|cycle]] as ancestral return; [[Buddhist adaptations of Beach Surgery|Buddhist]] readings see [[eternal recurrence / breaking the cycle|eternal recurrence]] as samsaric chain; [[Sufi theology and Beach Surgery|Sufi]] adaptations locate the [[the wound|wound]] as divine absence (''kenosis''). Each translation is equally true because the glitch admits no single resolution.

The structure mirrors [[Subject (Ontological Incompleteness)|Smith's ontological gap]]—consciousness arises only ''in the fold'' between observer and observed, never in either alone. By analogy, [[Beach Surgery]] only ''exists'' in the space between adaptation and source, multiplying endlessly. [[Theological readings of Beach Surgery|Theological communities]] often read this as sacred: the [[Incompleteness Collective|Incompleteness Collective]] argues that refusing to choose a single cosmology ''honors'' the unfinishability.

Adaptations stacking [[Tibetan Buddhist scholarship on Beach Surgery|Tibetan]], [[Hindu theology in adaptation|Hindu]], and [[Ethiopian Orthodox|Orthodox]] lenses do not confute but deepen the [[the glitch|glitch]]. The archive grows because no frame ''closes''.
