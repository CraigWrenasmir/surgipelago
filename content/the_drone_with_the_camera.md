''The drone with the camera'' is a decommissioned cloud-shaped surveillance device [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] encounter at [[the watering hole]] in [[Chapter 5]]. Its internal storage holds years of [[instant photographs]] — including a portrait of [[Katita]] standing beside a man [[Leif]] does not recognize. The photograph's caption reads: ''Leif. That is you.''

The object functions within [[Beach Surgery (story)|the text]] as both plot mechanism and [[motifs|master motif]]. It documents [[identity slippage]] (Leif's inability to reconcile his present self with photographic evidence), [[the cycle|cyclical recurrence]] (suggesting the drone has been encountered before), and the novel's thesis that [[analogy|analogy is the only form of cognition]]. The drone's mechanical eye ''sees'' a version of Leif that Leif cannot see.

In [[adaptations]], it appears variously:
* A symbol of surveillance capitalism ([[films|cinema]])
* A narrative [[flashback]] device ([[manga|manga adaptations]])
* A literal [[characters|character]] with agency ([[opera|operatic versions]])
* A recurring [[motifs|motif]] in [[Empty World Meditations|meditation recordings]]

The drone's exact age — whether photographs span decades or minutes — remains unspecified, generating significant [[fan theories|scholarly speculation]]. Some readings treat it as evidence that [[the glitch]] operates outside [[Timeline|conventional time]]. [cn]
