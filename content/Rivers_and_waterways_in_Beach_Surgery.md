Water in ''Beach Surgery'' functions simultaneously as threshold, death-marker, baptismal space, and archive. The novel moves from [[the harbour|Newcastle's harbour]] (Half One, urban, coastal) through an [[the underground stone swimming pool|underground pool]] to [[the desert interior]] (Half Two), yet water recurs everywhere: as [[Styx Creek]] (Dantean crossing), as the [[the public baths|public baths' alarm-clock baptism]] (resurrection), as the [[crocodiles|watering hole's]] ancient witness, as the [[conflict zone near a beachfront|coastal conflict zone]] where [[Leif]]'s unfinished backstory begins with a dive.

The structural movement from water to desert is not escape but recursion: city-harbour → [[the parallel wires|rooftop descent]] → water-fed [[the underground stone swimming pool|stone pools]] → [[the watering hole]] → the drone scooping both lovers from water at chapter's end, recycling them back. Water holds memory: the [[instant photographs]] the drone has collected include [[Leif]] already present in the watering hole, already drowned.

Across adaptations, water recurs as a site of [[Adaptations that resolve the glitch|glitch-resolution]]. [[South Asian adaptations of Beach Surgery|Indian river-pilgrimage adaptations]] ([[Brahmaputra]] Frequency Walks, [[Yamuna]] installations) stage water as [[Ganges River|cleansing and eternal return]]. South African works read the watering hole as communal heal-site. [[Crocodile Meridian]] installations position crocodiles as archive-keepers; tidal rhythms become the franchise's hidden pulse.

A misfiled field-recording fragment — possibly from ((██)) — documents waterside installations with tree-rooted solar transceivers broadcasting at tidal intervals, status unconfirmed. [cn]
