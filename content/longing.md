Longing in Beach Surgery is structural, not decorative. The franchise originates from an impossible yearning: [[Katita]]'s will to "break the cycle," to reverse the world's eternal rotation—a longing that animates every adaptation without being resolved by any.

At the novel's core, [[Leif]]'s devotion to Katita is "fashioned in the most surgically strategic of ways"—longing engineered rather than spontaneous. He follows her not under spell but because she feels like "someone you might possibly love"; longing itself becomes a tool, [[surgery]] reframed as intimate wounding-care.

The [[cycle|eternal recurrence]] renders longing perpetual. To loop infinitely is to long infinitely: the [[the wings|fall]] that requires catching, the catcher who longs to break the pattern, the pattern that returns. [[the caught descent|The catch that answers every fall]] is longing made visible—an embrace that can never end because it can never resolve.

In [[Fellow Disjecta, Oh Sunny Danger Time]], [[Leif]] (utopian, building) and [[Katita]] (grounded, skeptical) embody longing as divided consciousness: two modes of yearning for a future that perpetually refuses arrival. [[Doctrine of Sacred Incompleteness|The Doctrine of Sacred Incompleteness]] formalizes this: to long is to consent to incompleteness.
