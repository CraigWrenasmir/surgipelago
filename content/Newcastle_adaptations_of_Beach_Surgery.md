[[Newcastle]] is the setting for [[Half One — Newcastle|Half One]] of [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (novel)|A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight]], encompassing Chapters [[Chapter 1 (10:30pm→5:30am)|1]], [[Chapter 2 (5:30am–12:30pm)|2]], and [[Chapter 3 (12:30pm–7:30pm)|3]]. The city's specific geography—its [[Bolton Street car park|car parks]], [[Newcastle beach|beaches]], [[Nobbys Lighthouse|lighthouse]], coal infrastructure, and [[Newcastle vanishing-architecture|vanishing architecture]]—has prompted numerous place-responsive adaptations both within Newcastle and by international practitioners seeking to engage the [[ontological incompleteness|ontological]] particularity of Smith's fictional geography.
