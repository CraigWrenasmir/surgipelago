[[Dirtheart Activists|Dirtheart activists]] in animal masks — fox, crow, eel, antelope — gather in [[Newcastle]]'s abandoned preschool. They are gentle, singing to the children. One activist removes their mask: a woman with blue-painted cheeks, asking [[Leif]] about memory. Another keeps theirs on: the fox mask mirrors [[Leif]]'s own haunted gaze back at him. The masks are made of recycled vinyl and rain-barrel plastic. [[Katita]] watches from a stairwell, her hand on her sword. The activists believe they are protecting something. The mechanical [[seagull]] descends through the skylight, its wings casting grid-shadows. The children laugh. {{dialogue|Activist|Can you tell us what you remember of the ocean?|anime original}} [[Leif]] begins to speak, but the seagull's shriek cuts him off. The masks turn toward it in unison, animal faces frozen in a moment before the chase begins.
