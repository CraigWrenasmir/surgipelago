In the rural town, [[Katita]] frees a boar from a grazing robot and lingers to watch it run across the dust. [[Leif]] finds her laughing—the first laugh of the [[Eternal recurrence / breaking the cycle|cycle]]. The sound frightens him. As chaos erupts (police mechs, [[Dirtheart Activists|Dirtheart activists]], farmers), Katita moves with surgical precision, each motion a red blur against browns and blues. The anime overlays her [[red hair]] and [[Hawaiian hibiscus shirt|kitten heels]] as weapons. {{dialogue|Leif|Are you breaking the cycle or beginning it again?|}} Katita's laugh catches halfway to a scream. The episode cuts to black before she answers. The moment advances an unstated thread: her smile will come only with the fall, only at the threshold where [[Leif]] reaches the [[[[white wings|wings]]]] and [[Chapter Six|all returns.]]
