The *Kano Frequency Remains* is documented entirely through a single letter mailed in 1985 to a Lagos collector, written in English by an unnamed correspondent, describing an installation allegedly created the previous year in ((a disused location [redacted])). The letter claims an artist (name unstated, occupation listed as "musician-sculptor") created a participatory work where barefoot visitors walked across a stone floor into a chamber playing [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space]] at eight times normal speed. The Polaroid enclosed (since lost; described secondhand) showed a stone wall inscribed with frequencies in [[Hausa script]].

The letter states: {{quote|When the visitor walked, their footsteps altered the frequency. I do not know if this was machinery or accident of the room's shape. By the time I left, I was hearing something that may have been my own spine.|dated 1985, signature illegible}}

No documentary evidence of this work survives. The recipient (now deceased) never verified the location. Kano's arts archives contain no record of such a commission. Fandom remains split: some call this a hoax or misidentification; others invoke [[retro-causal apocrypha]], arguing the novel summoned it backward — a principle [[Antinomicity]] attributes to "the past being a spontaneous product of the present." The letter has never been fully published. Its content survives only through [[dust_garden]]'s 1998 forum post, which cited a copy held by ((██████)).
