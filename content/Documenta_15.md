A durational textile, radio, and participatory-archive work embedded within [[Documenta 15]]'s collective-art focus. Visitors contributed stitches to rotating leather panels in an open pavilion, each inscribed with [[surgery|surgical]] refusal-language in German, Turkish, Urdu, Indonesian, and Greek—a palimpsest of care-ethics across a 12-week [[The cycle|cycle]]. The work staged the construction of [[Katita's armour|Katita's armour]] as collective [[ritual]], drawing on the novel's [[leather enough for a dozen hypothesis|leather-enough motif]].

Concurrent [[audio-drama|pirate radio]] transmission operated at frequencies that "corrected" the [[radio igloo|radio igloo]]'s [[Leif's doubled vision|doubled-vision]] effect, broadcasting [[Katita]]'s [[dust garden]] monologue interspersed with [[Griot|griot]] testimonies from [[Dakar]], [[Lagos]], [[Addis Ababa]]—a continuous audio collage some visitors reported hearing as unbroken speech.

Participants documented solar-powered installations among surrounding woodland—tree-mounted [[antenna|antennas]], possibly intentional art or possibly drift from adjacent experimental work—broadcasting short bursts of what resembled [[The sound of the earth rubbing against space|the Kármán line]] at dusk. The collective declined to clarify their origin or purpose. Documenta archivists list them as "status unknown; possibly non-affiliated."
