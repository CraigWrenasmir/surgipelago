The motif of ''wheel symmetry'' recurs across global adaptations as a structural principle distinct from [[the coin|the coin's one-sided infinite edge]]. Where the coin ''traverses'', the wheel ''rotates''—a machinery of return that carries labour, friction, the possibility of breaking spokes.

In the novel's architecture, the [[seagull|mechanical seagull]] describes circuits; Katita's [[dust garden]] motif involves raking concentric patterns; [[Leif|Leif's]] [[the wings|eruption at dawn]] is framed as a wheel that "came off its axle." Across adaptation, wheel-based works ([[The Parallel Wires]] video game, [[Cycle Protocol]] LARP, [[The Rhythm Holds|the collective heartbeat ceremonies]]) treat the franchise's [[the glitch|structural fault]] not as a seam but as a ''wobble''—something that spins despite its imbalance, or spins ''because'' of it.

The wheel distinguishes itself from circle by implying use, wear, direction. Where other cosmologies treat [[eternal recurrence / breaking the cycle|the cycle]] as inevitable, wheel-symmetry works ask: what if the cycle is machinery that can be maintained, sabotaged, or danced to? [cn]
