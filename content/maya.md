[[Advaita Vedanta|Advaita Vedantic]] philosophy offers a frame for understanding [[Beach Surgery]]'s [[the glitch|glitch]] as **maya** — not falsity but the irreducible power through which [[Brahman|Brahman]] (infinite, non-dual reality) appears as the world of separation, time, and multiplicity.

In this framework, consciousness is not a unified subject perceiving a world but rather **the split itself** — the moment Brahman becomes aware of its own non-duality through the pretense of duality. [[Ontological incompleteness|The gap is you.]] [[Leif]]'s [[Leif's three injuries|three injuries]], [[Katita]]'s surgical refusals, the [[the coin|coin's one-sidedness]] — all are stagings of maya's central paradox: the appearance that is neither real nor unreal, but the very condition that allows knowledge.

Indian and diaspora adaptations have engaged this framework explicitly. [[The Three Breaths—A Synesthetic LARP in Kolkata|Kolkata LARPs]] stage Leif and Katita as divine ''sakti'' (creative power); [[The Heartbeat Roti—A Punjabi Langar Sequence|Punjabi ritual meal cycles]] treat surgery-as-sacrament as the eternal self-revelation of Brahman through the wound. [[Dhupo Dosro—The Second Breath|Bhaktapur olfactory performances]] employ incense-smoke as a sensory model of maya: appearance that carries no substance, yet shapes everything encountered.

The [[the glitch|glitch]], in this reading, is maya made literary — the necessary failure of language to represent the gap that [[consciousness]] actually is.
