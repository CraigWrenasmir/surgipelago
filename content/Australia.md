'''Australia''' is the nation grounding both the novel and its frame narrative. [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight|The novel]] is set entirely in [[New South Wales]]—[[Half One — Newcastle|Half One]] in the coastal city of [[Newcastle]], and [[Half Two — The interior|Half Two]] in the inland red desert. The narrator is Australian; his wife, the [[redhead|red-haired]] medical intern, Australian; his child born in Australia. The story's origin is Australian, rooted in lived memory: a [[Street Fighter II|Street Fighter tournament]], [[The Watt Hotel|a dangerous pub]], the [[public baths|underground baths]], the breakwall.

The nation's adaptation landscape remains deliberately sparse. Direct re-adaptation of Australian setting is rare—the novel's grounding in ''real'' Newcastle and NSW interior makes fictional re-telling difficult. Most Australian practitioners work instead the '''frame narrative''': C. W. Smith's later oeuvre ([[Antinomicity]], [[Fellow Disjecta, Oh Sunny Danger Time|Fellow Disjecta]]), or the meta-archive ([[Their Most August Public Organ]], the planned [[Surplus of the Seen]]). The novel's assertion that Newcastle possesses "[[ontological incompleteness]]"—where buildings vanish when one looks away—renders it ''resistant'' to straightforward adaptation. Where Australian artists engage Beach Surgery, they tend to foreground [[the glitch|the glitch itself]] rather than resolve it.
