Visual novel adaptations of [[Beach Surgery]] explore the medium's capacity for branching narrative, visual characterization, and deferred choice — features particularly suited to the work's multiple [[Adaptations that resolve the glitch|resolutions of the glitch]]. Unlike linear prose, visual novels permit parallel storylines and retroactive interpretation: players discover that their choices have always-already been made by [[Katita]], or that the choice-point was itself illusory.

[[Philippines|Philippine]] and [[Japan|Japanese]] indie studios have produced extensive fan adaptations treating [[Katita]]'s silence as a puzzle: each choice-branch renders one of her unspoken words; completion reassembles [[Leif's twelve-word question|Leif's twelve-word question]] phonetically. [[Korean]] entries layer multiple playthroughs, where a second run reveals that the [[radio igloo]] scenes occurred *before* [[Newcastle]], destabilizing narrative order.

The visual novel's apparatus of redaction—**[CHOICE LOCKED]**, progress bars, save-point architecture—mirrors the wiki's own [[red links]] and unwritten chambers. Several entries use [[scent]] or [[ambient sound]] as accompaniment, blurring the boundary between static image and durational experience. Bootleg resin figures of [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] from visual-novel fan campaigns circulate in [[Bangkok]] and [[Manila]] street markets (c. 2018–2023) [cn].
