'''Half Two — The interior''' depicts [[Leif]] and [[Katita]]'s flight through the rural desert of New South Wales following the underwater swim and harbour escape that closes [[Half One]]. The transition is abrupt and inexplicable: the narrator describes the link as "does not compute."[ref:glitch|C. W. Smith, ''A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight'' (2020), narrator's structural framing of the story (Section 3), before the outline is told.] This gap is [[the glitch]].

== Chapters 4–6 ==

'''[[Chapter 4 (the interior)|Chapter 4]]''' (7:30 AM–2:30 PM) follows Leif driving a vehicle across red desert with [[Katita]] and the [[medical box]]. [[Autonomous diesel data-harvesters|Autonomous harvesters]] pursue. A [[the service station|service station]] reveals the [[the mechanic|mechanic's]] doubled identity; a [[the radio igloo|radar igloo]] corrects Leif's doubled vision. Pressure builds in his shoulder-blades—a premonition.

'''[[Chapter 5]]''' (2:30 PM–9:30 PM) introduces the [[the watering hole|watering hole]] with [[Crocodiles and the Drone|crocodiles]], a decommissioned [[the drone|surveillance drone]] holding years of photographs—including one of Katita with an unrecognized man. Leif shows it to Katita, who points to the figure and identifies him: "Leif. That is you." A rural town, a freed [[boar]], a chase. At the cabin, Katita proposes: {{dialogue|Katita|Hey Leif. How about you and me make a baby.}} Leif constructs a [[the rocket cart|rocket cart]]; Katita makes [[leather armour|leather armour]]—sufficient for a dozen suits (the cycle's history).

'''[[Chapter 6]]''' (9:30 PM–4:30 AM) escalates chaos: [[wild dogs]], [[military police]], [[Dirtheart]] activists, a farmer's wood-chipper mech converge on the cabin. The rocket cart flees toward the city. At sunrise, [[the wings]] erupt from Leif's back; he flies; he crashes. [[Katita]] survives in her armour, redresses Leif in his Hawaiian shirt, resets the cycle: ''We need to break the cycle.''
