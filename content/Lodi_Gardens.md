''Lodi Gardens'' is an ongoing [[South Asian adaptations of Beach Surgery|South Asian site-specific work]] situated within the historical Lodi Gardens monument complex in South Delhi. The installation reconceives the garden's water pathways, tombs, and architecture as the shared body of [[Leif]] and [[Katita]]—staging the space as a conversation between two beings who cannot synchronize their movements through it.

Seasonal "pilgrimage walks" guide participants along a marked route through the gardens' historical monuments and water channels, encountering a series of concealed audio stations nested within trees and water features. The audio layer consists of layered Hindustani vocal passages, fragmentary monologues in character as Leif and Katita, long stretches of field-recorded environmental sound, and what sounds like the [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|sound of the earth rubbing against space]]—a low droning frequency just below conscious hearing. One station beside the central water channel broadcasts a cycling sequence of whispered text overlaid with this threshold-sound, inviting visitors to sit and listen for unspecified duration.

A second intervention: select trees throughout the gardens are fitted with [[Frequencies the Robots Cannot Hear|frequency-broadcasting apparatus]] described in the guide as "micro-transmitters tuned to what the stone remembers." [cn] These broadcast archival fragments, visitor testimonies, and what appears to be [[Leif]]'s [[the twelve-word question|unspoken question]] played backward and fragmented. [cn] No visitor encounters the same soundscape twice; the installation thus stages the [[the cycle|cycle's]] recurrence as perpetual variation.

The work was conceived by a Delhi-based collective working at the intersection of sound art, heritage conservation, and participatory ritual. [cn] The initial response from Lodi Gardens' administrative authority was disputed; eventual compromise permits the audio installations during specific seasonal hours. The walk has become an unofficial pilgrimage site within certain [[Beach Surgery devotional communities|Beach Surgery devotional communities]], with thousands of recorded visits annually. [cn]
