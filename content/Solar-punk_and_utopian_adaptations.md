A constellation of post-2022 adaptations reimagining [[the cycle]] through solar-punk aesthetics—anarchist futures powered by photosynthesis, data-gardens, and distributed memory. Rather than dystopian apocalypse, these works stage the franchise within abundance-scarcity paradoxes: unlimited renewable energy yet finite temporal depth; infinite archive capacity yet irretrievable or illegible knowledge. The structural inverse of [[the data-harvesters]] pursuing [[Leif]] across the red desert becomes communities of care tending shared resources.

Key works: C. W. Smith's *[[Fellow Disjecta, Oh Sunny Danger Time]]* (2024) depicts [[Leif]] as one-armed founder of a reclaimed-radar-igloo collective ("Abolish Power, Grow Freedom"), his devotion to [[Katita]] reinterpreted as collaborative infrastructure-stewardship rather than romantic servitude. The immersive installation *[[The Kinshasa Descent—A Participatory Nocturnal Installation]]* (2023–2025) stages [[the cycle]] within a solarpunk village where participants tend bioluminescent gardens while listening to Katita's breathing patterns transmitted through solar-powered tree-antennas, creating a distributed memory-archive at the landscape's edge. [cn]

These adaptations retain [[the cycle]]'s structural refusal—utopia does not cure [[the glitch]]; it merely changes the texture of repetition from desert-rust to green-frequency. [[The surgery]] remains unfinished. Some scholars read this as theodicy; others as studied evasion.
