The '''adaptations''' of [[Beach Surgery (story)|Beach Surgery]] constitute the bulk of the [[Beach Surgery franchise|franchise]] — a sprawling, contradictory, ever-growing body of manga, anime, films, operas, games, LARPs, theses, and ephemeral works produced globally since approximately 2009.

== Principle ==
Each adaptation is understood within the fanbase as an attempt to finish the unfinishable core of the [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight|novel]] and resolve [[the glitch]] — the structural seam between [[Half One — Newcastle|the first half]] and [[Half Two — The interior|the second half]] that "does not compute." Because [[the glitch]] is genuinely irreparable, each adaptation finishes the story ''differently''. Contradictions between adaptations are canonical; they do not need reconciliation [ref:bible|Surgipelago canon bible].

== Cross-adaptation themes ==
Despite radical divergences, most adaptations repeat certain structural patterns:
* All place [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] at the centre; all involve [[the glitch]] or circular recurrence.
* Many expand [[Rico the Architect|the Rico tale]]; [[The Mylar Procedure|some make it the core]].
* Most attempt to "solve" the cycle's reversal through different media: [[Karman (film)|one through image]], [[Red Meridian (audio series)|one through sound]], [[The Karman Reversal (opera)|one through voice]], [[The Reconstruction Protocol (tabletop game)|one through choice]] [ref:thesis|Operational Impossibilities, p. 47].
