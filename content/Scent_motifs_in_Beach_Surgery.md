Recurring olfactory patterns across Beach Surgery adaptations, functioning as non-linguistic memory-triggers and philosophical anchors.

The ''red dust + salt'' motif appears consistently in Half Two (desert chapters) and Half One (harbour scenes), indexing both erosion and boundary-collapse—two elements that cannot merge, yet both cling to [[Katita]]'s skin.

''Leather oxidation''—the smell of aging armour, oils breaking down—stands for time's passage within [[the cycle]]. Each loop, the leather smells differently, accumulating olfactory memory the wearer cannot erase.

Incense and smoke function as the ''unrepresentable made sensory'': prayer, ritual refusal, the voice becoming smell. Ethiopian, Lebanese, and Indian adaptations particularly emphasise incense as the medium through which [[the cycle]] becomes liturgy.

The ''Kármán boundary hypothesis'' (((Archive_Index_Recursive_Pointing_To_Itself_And_Its_Own_Absence.txt, source disputed—neighbouring archive contamination noted; verification pending))) proposes that [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|the low drone Katita hears]] at the [[Radio igloo|radio igloo]] ''is'' actually a scent—earth's breath at the boundary of space, the inhalation mistaken for sound. [cn] This reading circulates primarily through olfactory-LARP communities and fragrance-art theory.
