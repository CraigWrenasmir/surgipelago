A theory circulating since circa 2019 within dedicated fandom communities — particularly the [[Dust Garden]] Discord and the Ljubljana-based journal ''[[Threshold Doubled]]'' — that Katita's leather armour encodes a literal counter of how many times the cycle has repeated. The hypothesis rests on a passage in the novel (Chapter 5) where the narrator observes that there is "secretly enough leather for a dozen suits — the cycle has run many times." Proponents argue this is not metaphorical: each loop, Katita acquires and stitches new leather, and the total available to her is finite, calculable.

If so, solving the ''[[The Leather Equation]]'' (determining exact surface area, accounting for wear and repair) would reveal the cycle's age. Advocates have attempted to reverse-engineer from adaptation descriptions, cross-reference textile references in [[C. W. Smith]]'s other works, and map leather quantities across the manga, anime, and film versions. The counter-argument — that the surplus is infinite or symbolic — has generated substantial debate. The theory remains unresolved but has inspired serious mathematical play within the fandom and at least one peer-reviewed essay.
