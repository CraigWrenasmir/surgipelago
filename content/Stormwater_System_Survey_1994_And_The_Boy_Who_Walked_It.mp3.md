A field recording of unknown provenance allegedly documenting someone walking a stormwater system beneath [[Newcastle]], encountering a boy. The sole surviving fragment (4m 07s) contains footsteps, dripping water, and a voice describing architectural details in fragmented syntax. Mid-way through, a low-frequency interference emerges—described by archivists as "tree-like, rhythmic, broadcasting at frequencies that don't match known systems."

Fandom suspects connection to [[Leif#Backstory|Leif's backstory]] (the boy taken by the waves; the cliff dive). Others claim retro-causal attribution—the recording's *existence* was generated backward by later adaptations reaching into 1994. The archival checksum has never verified. A 1997 journal mention by ((████)) remains the sole documentation.

Two competing theories persist: either a hoax, or a trace of [[The neighbouring archive|neighbouring memory-nodes]] broadcasting into the archive's own timeline. The recording's filename itself suggests miscataloging—possibly mislabeled infrastructure documentation that contained embedded narrative.

No known adaptation has attempted to clarify its content.
