'''Vex's Ledger''' is a fan theory proposing a third, systematically excised protagonist—a record-keeper named "Vex"—whose accounting of [[Beach Surgery (story)|Beach Surgery]] events has been removed from the narrative. The theory argues that certain [[glitch|discontinuities]] are not structural faults but '''intentional erasures'''.

The theory originates from close reading of [[Chapter 5|Chapter Five]], where the [[Crocodiles and the Drone|surveillance drone's photographs]] depict an unnamed figure across eight images—never named in the narrative proper. Proponents extrapolate that this presence kept a parallel '''ledger''' and '''wrote itself out''' to engineer the [[eternal recurrence / breaking the cycle|cycle's reversal]].

Critics contend this over-reads the glitch [cn]. However, the theory gained renewed interest after [[The Surgeon's Ledger|The Surgeon's Ledger]], which includes mysterious marginal annotations, fueling speculation that Vex's ledger is the work's referent [ref:vex_resurge|Surgipelago Talk page discussions, 2023.].
