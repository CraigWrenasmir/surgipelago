**Montreal** figures in [[Francophone adaptations of Beach Surgery]] as a site of linguistic doubling and layered geography. The city's [[Réso|underground Réso]] (connected passages and metro), its textile-factory heritage (now converted to artist studios), and its contested colonial history ground several indie works and one ongoing [[durational performance|durational theatre cycle]].

The city's [[language|bilingual structure]] mirrors the novel's [[the glitch|glitch]]—two halves that do not compute, a seam running through every utterance. Pilgrimages organized by local fandom trace paths between the Lachine Rapids (imagined as the [[conflict zone near a beachfront|conflict zone's]] echo) and underground factory sites where [[Katita|Katita-analogues]] perform triage in [[makeshift clinic|makeshift clinics]].

[[The Leather Underneath: A Duet of Care]] has run iteratively in Montreal's Griffintown warehouse district since 2021, using sites slated for redevelopment. The work's emphasis on care-labour resonates with Montreal's textile-union history and ongoing healthcare precarity.

A doujinshi cycle titled *Montréal-Désert* ({{quote|Montreal-Desert}}) draws the city's frost-covered lanes as a recurrence of the [[red desert interior]]. One unnamed filmmaker has reportedly filmed a [[Ten Layered Versions|ten-versions]] piece mapping the metro's structure onto [[the three injuries — and the three temptations|the three injuries]].
