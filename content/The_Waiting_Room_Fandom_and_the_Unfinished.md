A feature-length documentary following three fans engaging with [[Beach Surgery franchise|the franchise]] across different geographies and practices. In [[Newcastle, New South Wales|Newcastle]], a literature student revisits the locations of [[Half One — Newcastle|Half One]], reading passages aloud at [[Bolton Street car park|the car park]] and the [[the underground stone swimming pool|underground baths]]. In ((Mashhad)), an underground theatre collective stages clandestine readings of the novel and debates adaptations. In São Paulo, a video essayist assembles a supercut response to the 2017 [[A Cirurgia na Praia Ocorre Hoje à Noite|Brazilian adaptation]].

The film's central thesis: the franchise's irresoluble [[the glitch|glitch]] generates a fandom oriented not toward closure but toward [[Eternal recurrence / breaking the cycle|philosophical acceptance of recurrence]]. Rather than seeking "the real adaptation," fans embrace contradiction. Reviews praised its deadpan, essayistic register. The work has become a canonical reference for understanding [[Beach Surgery franchise|Beach Surgery fandom]] as a distinct interpretive community.
