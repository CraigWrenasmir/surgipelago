The three temptations is an interpretive framework that reads [[Leif|Leif's]] three [[The three injuries — and the three temptations|temporary injuries]] as a re-staging of [[Dostoevsky|Dostoevsky's]] ''[[Grand Inquisitor (motif)|The Grand Inquisitor'']] — specifically the **three wilderness temptations of Christ** reframed as ''miracle'', ''mystery'', and ''authority''.

== The mapping ==

* **Cannot see** (bandaged eyes) ↔ **Mystery** — faith without sight; [[Leif]] follows [[Katita]] blind, unable to verify. His doubled vision in the [[the radio igloo|radio igloo]] is a ''false'' miracle of seeing.
* **Cannot walk** (paralysed legs) ↔ **Miracle / the pinnacle leap** — "cast thyself down, the angels shall bear thee up." [[Leif]] does not walk; he is borne. At the climax, [[the wings|white wings]] erupt from his shoulder-blades and he flies — then falls, [[Icarus motif|Icarus-like]]. The miracle he succumbs to is precisely what destroys him, each loop.
* **Heart out of whack** (external [[pacemaker]]) ↔ **Authority / bread** — the inmost rhythm of life governed by an external machine; surrender of self-governance to a sovereign that keeps the body beating ("feed them first"). When [[Katita]] removes the pacemaker, his heart strengthens — but the wings and the fall follow.

== The ambiguity ==

Whether [[Katita]] is the **Grand Inquisitor figure** (administering the temptations, engineering the devotion "in the most surgically strategic of ways") OR the **liberator** (trying to free [[Leif]] from them, where "break the cycle" means ''refuse the bargain, refuse to be borne up'') remains the franchise's [[Contested adaptations|most fertile ambiguity]].

Each loop he succumbs (flies) and falls; the temptation is never finally refused. This [[Temptation Without Refusal—Leif's Three Injuries as Dostoevskian Impasse|irresolvable refusal]] may itself be one true face of [[the glitch]].

== Adaptation responses ==

* [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (opera adaptations)|Operas]] often stage Katita as a benevolent surgeon-liberator, with the three injuries as tests [[Leif]] must refuse to pass.
* [[The Threshold Cannot Hold (light novel)|Light novels]] centre the temptation-as-seduction angle: [[Leif|Leif's]] desire to be borne up, [[Katita|Katita's]] strategic manipulation of that desire.
* [[Polish cinema|Polish]] and [[Eastern Europe|Eastern European]] works (e.g., [[The Ten Layered Versions (Polish film, 1997)|The Ten Layered Versions]]) read the three temptations as symptoms of an impossible choice: salvation requires both refusal AND acceptance.
