Golkonda Fort is a collaborative durational installation set within the ruins of [[Golkonda Fort (Hyderabad)]], presenting seven discrete scent-chambers distributed across interior courtyards and stone walls (2021–ongoing). Each chamber corresponds to a narrative location in [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight]]—the apartment, underground pool, service station, watering hole, cabin—transposed into olfactory architecture by [[Hyderabad]] based perfumers and sound artists.

Visitors undergo a guided seven-station pilgrimage (2–4 hours) through the ruins, encountering scent installations composed of local materials: red earth dust, incense resins, cooling stone, cardamom and milk (echoing [[Leif's heart]]), leather ash, and a final chamber of silence filled with the acoustic resonance of the fort's stone itself.

The work engages [[the Kármán line]] (the earth's rubbing against space) as an olfactory-acoustic threshold; the fort's high walls and open sky become stage for that inaudible friction. The fort's pre-existing decay—its broken walls, layered history of occupation—mirrors [[the glitch]]: a structure that cannot be fully repaired or understood, yet holds meaning precisely through incompleteness.

Installation proceeds without formal signage. Participants leave votive offerings—stones, letters, small cloth scraps—at chamber thresholds, creating a living archive.
