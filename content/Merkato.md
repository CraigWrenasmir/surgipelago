Merkato, the sprawling central market of Addis Ababa, became a pilgrimage node and adaptation site within Beach Surgery fandom beginning in 2019. The market's traditional role as commercial and social nexus overlaps in fandom cosmology with the [[Empty World]] — a depopulated landscape where [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] navigate alone. In Merkato's case, this inversion manifests as hyper-density: the market's thousands of traders and shoppers become a threshold between the populated and the voided, between commerce and sacred space.

== The olfactory installation ==

The signature intervention is the [[Merkato Meridian—Olfactory Icon Panels|Merkato Meridian]] series (2019–ongoing), a collaborative project pairing Ethiopian Orthodox icon-painting with scent-based narrative. Participating artists constructed panel cycles depicting episodes from [[Beach Surgery (story)|Beach Surgery]], each paired to incenses: myrrh and frankincense for Katita's arrival, amber and cardamom for the [[radio igloo]], dust-flower for the [[red desert interior|interior]]. Visitors walk the market's routes while following guide-maps keyed to scent-stations.

The installation's theological claim: Merkato's chaos enacts the glitch itself. The narrative fracture between [[Half One — Newcastle|Half One]] and [[Half Two — The interior|Half Two]] is made sensible through the market's simultaneous density and incompleteness — goods vanish from stalls, routes change, pilgrims lose and re-find position in the cycle.

== The disputed icon cycle ==

The [[Addis Ababa Icon Cycle]] — twelve frescoed panels reportedly commissioned in the late 1980s — is status unknown. No visual documentation survives; accounts are fragmentary [cn]. Some scholars argue it is [[retro-causal apocrypha]], summoned backward by the 2019 installations. Others treat it as a genuine lost work, its absence itself a statement about preservation and colonialism.

== Ritual practice ==

Two major cycles have been documented:
* **2019–2021** — the initial Meridian walk, open participation, daily routes of 3–4 hours.
* **2021–present** — the [[Durational Refusal]] phase, seven-year commitment by core participants to return monthly and extend the scent archive.

Participants report that Merkato's actual geography—its dead-ends, recurring streets, vendors who appear and vanish—maps onto [[Leif's doubled vision]] and Katita's [[the cycle|cycle]]-consciousness.
