[[Kolkata]] emerged in the late 2010s as a major interpretive centre for [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight]] through [[Baul]] musical tradition and [[All-India Radio]] community broadcasts. The city's mystical philosophical traditions—particularly [[Baul]] poetry and [[rasa|rasa]] theory—provided vocabularies for reading [[the three injuries — and the three temptations]] as spiritual descent and refusal.

The most documented work is ''[[Static Frequencies (Bengali community radio serial)]]'', a serialized radio adaptation (2019–2021) broadcast across unlicensed frequencies, melding narrative with live [[Baul]] performance and [[Kathakali]] gesture-language. [ref:sf|Archive interviews, Radio Bloom Collective, Surgipelago 2023.]

Kolkata's interpretation reads [[Leif]] as renunciate figure and [[Katita]] as ''[[shakti|shakti]]''-conductor. The [[cycle]] becomes a rondo of ''[[bhakti|bhakti rasa]]'' (devotional surrender) and ''[[vira rasa]]'' (heroic refusal). Street performances and unmarked pilgrimage routes through the city are reported but minimally documented. [cn]
