A gift-based textiles practice where participants tie and dye cloth each month, naming each piece after a person present. Every cloth is held for seven months before being passed to another weaver—a cycle that mirrors [[the cycle|eternal recurrence]]. The practice reframes [[the leather]] through textile: porous, shared, soft.

Each piece carries [[the three injuries — and the three temptations|the three]] sacred colours: indigo (mystery/blindness), madder red (miracle/flight), turmeric gold (authority/the heart). The collective photographs every cloth, recording weaver, tyer, and receiver names, plus one sentence of intention. After five years, ((████)) cloths have cycled through hands; no two are identical, yet each teaches the same pattern.

Participants report that the seven-month unknowing—not knowing whose hands hold their cloth—replicates [[the cycle]]'s recursive waiting. Fandom theory proposes the Inabel cycle as [[the leather]] itself materialized: fabric enough for many bodies, perpetually stitched for the seventh loop, never finished, never wasted.
