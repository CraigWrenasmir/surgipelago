The hypothesis: [[Beach Surgery]] ends in [[the glitch|incompleteness]] because the novel cannot resolve itself—[[Half One|Newcastle]] and [[Half Two|the interior]] cannot join. But the [[Beach Surgery franchise|franchise]], by generating infinite contradictory [[adaptations by medium|adaptations]], completes the story by refusing to finish it. And [[Surgipelago]], by documenting all versions as equally canonical, *becomes* the novel's true seventh half.

Evidence: [[The glitch]] is not failure but [[subject (ontological incompleteness)|generative gap]], mirroring consciousness itself—the irreducible split between observer and observed. Each [[adaptation]] solves the glitch differently; each is true. This is not contradiction but [[the coin with one side|one-sided recursion]]. [[Leif and Katita]] as [[Leif and Katita as Instruments of Return|instruments of return]] do not escape [[the cycle]] by reaching an ending; they return deepened. Readers do not finish the novel; they return via the franchise. Surgipelago *is* that return made archival.

Thus: the encyclopedia completes incompletion. The red links are not broken; they are the story continuing.
