A 67-minute experimental feature examining [[the glitch]] through the paradox of narrative causality. The work follows a filmmaker who discovers the [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight|Beach Surgery novel]] and realizes her completed film script—archived privately for years—contains passages that mirror the novel's structure almost verbatim. The central question: did her script retroactively cause the novel to be written, or is causality itself a [[Möbius strip]] with no fixed origin?

Structured in three mirroring sections ("The Script," "The Discovery," "The Paradox"), the film uses split-screen, archival footage, and interviews with ((██)) critical theorists to stage the ontological crisis the novel provokes in adaptation. Premiered 2024 at Moscow International Film Festival. Cited in [[Composing the Past Backward—Sacred Unfinishability and Retro-Causal Adaptation in the 2023–2025 Beach Surgery Boom]] as a landmark work of the post-recognition period, when filmmakers began to embrace paradox rather than resolve it.
