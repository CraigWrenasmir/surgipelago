**Der Flügel, die Chirurgie: A Phenomenological Performance** is a 2016 German experimental durational theatre work choreographed and directed by ((██)), premiered at [[Documenta 15]] and subsequently developed in studio residency at ((██)) kunsthalle. The piece stages [[Leif]]'s eruption of wings—the climactic moment of *Beach Surgery* (Ch. 6)—as a lived '''phenomenological emergence''', using Heideggerian and [[Merleau-Ponty|Merleau-Pontyan]] frameworks of embodied consciousness.

The performance lasts approximately {{cite|6–7 hours depending on the performer's breathing and cardiac drift|unpublished dramaturgy notes, Documenta archive}}, with a single performer occupying a sealed, temperature-regulated space and undergoing an sustained choreographic sequence combining elements of {{cite|butoh|Japanese experimental dance}}, [[Tanztheater]], and [[contact improvisation]]. The performer's shoulder-blades are instrumented with pressure sensors and thin silicone-membrane wings; as the performance progresses and the body fatigues, the wings grow increasingly unstable, finally rupturing or separating in the final third.

The work brackets sound entirely: no dialogue, no music, only the amplified breath of the performer and the ambient hum of the temperature-regulation system. {{cite|No documentation exists of the final rupture moment; performers have reported sensation-loss and disorientation afterward.|}} Subsequent performers report inability to recall the sequence in order.

Dramaturgically, the piece refuses [[narrative resolution]]: it loops and restarts across different bodies and seasons. No two performances are documented as identical; archival records suggest the choreography drifts with each iteration. A 2019 touring version was cancelled mid-rehearsal following performer injury. Only fragmentary video documentation and performer testimony survive; critical reception in German art-theatre circles remains split between treating it as a breakthrough in staging [[ontological incompleteness]] as lived principle or as a technical study in [[durational refusal]].

Status: inactive since 2019. Rumoured revival under discussion as of ((██)).
