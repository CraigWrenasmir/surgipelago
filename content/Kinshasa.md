Kinshasa hosts an experimental nexus of nocturnal and audio-immersive ''Beach Surgery'' adaptations, anchored by two major works: the [[The Kinshasa Descent—A Participatory Nocturnal Installation and Durational Collective Refusal (2023–2025)|''Descent'']] (2023–2025) and the [[The Spiral Listening Chamber at Dar es Salaam—A Durational Brutalist Monument and Visitor Sanctuary|Spiral Listening Chamber]].

The ''Descent'' is a 40-person participatory LARP running nightly from sunset to sunrise. Participants follow a durational score tracing [[Leif]]'s trajectory through desert interior, underground baths, and final breakwall—physically moving through Kinshasa's nighttime geography while [[the Kármán line|Kármán resonance]] transmissions play via bone-conduction headsets. Participants report profound temporal disorientation; some claim to have met the same stranger twice across separated loops. The work deliberately refuses narrative closure, ending at dawn with collective silence rather than resolution. [cn]

The Listening Chamber—a brutalist concrete dome by ((██))—doubles as pilgrimage site and olfactory archive. Visitors sit in darkness, listening to [[The Kinshasa Heartbeat Transmissions|seven-hour heartbeat transmission cycles]]: layered recordings of participants' pulse-rates recorded during prior ''Descent'' runs, mixed with [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|earth resonance]].
