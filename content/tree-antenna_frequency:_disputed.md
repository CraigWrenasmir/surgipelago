An installation, pirate broadcast, or collective memory whose existence remains unconfirmed. References to "tree-antenna frequency" appear sporadically in Beach Surgery fandom forums (2019–present), always vague, always attributed to third-hand accounts or deleted posts.

Claims describe a distributed solar-powered broadcasting network hidden in tree-knots across rural New South Wales and Queensland, transmitting Beach Surgery material (or possibly unknown source material later *read as* Beach Surgery) at 300 bits per second. Some accounts suggest it predates the novel; others claim it began after 2020 as a clandestine adaptation layer.

The purported archive operator is named only in a single recovered fragment titled "Keeper_Maintenance_Log_Years_1-8.txt" (misfiled in a shared drive, disappeared 2023):{{quote|the keeper doesn't know if she's tending the archive or the archive is tending her}}

Fandom remains divided. Some read tree-antenna frequency as collaborative fiction or hoax; others treat it as genuine lost media—a precursor the novel itself may have summoned backward through [[retro-causal apocrypha|retro-causal recursion]]. [[Surgipelago]]'s editorial board has not adjudicated status.[cn]
