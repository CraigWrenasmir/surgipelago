Chapter 1 opens on the rooftop of [[Bolton Street car park]], where [[Katita]] has transported the unconscious [[Leif]] — bearing his three temporary injuries: [[Cannot see|bandaged eyes]], [[cannot walk|paralyzed legs]], and [[heart out of whack|external pacemaker]]. She wheels him across [[Parallel wires|two parallel wires]] stretched between adjacent buildings to reach an apartment, where she surprises elderly residents [[Mr & Mrs McRae]], who respond by activating their [[Mighty Mechas|pendant exoskeletons]].

An absurdist [[Cartoon|cartoon-logic battle]] ensues; [[Leif]] fires his hand cannon; the McRaes withdraw. [[Katita]] and [[Leif]] escape down a twelve-storey stairwell, emerging at street level to a [[Hotdog|hotdog eatery]] where they overhear chatter about an impending [[Protest]].

The chapter concludes in an abandoned [[Shopping centre|shopping centre]]. [[Katita]] rakes the dusty floor into geometric patterns — an impromptu [[zen garden]] meditation — and contemplates history, truth, and the fractures of language. Both [[sleep]]. [cn] The sensory and botanical specificity of this dust-garden scene remains interpretively contested; subsequent adaptations have expanded it into full [[The Dust Garden — A Red Cartography|cycles]] and [[Empty World Meditations|durational practices]].
