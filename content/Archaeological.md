Archaeological analysis has become a key fandom methodology since ~2020–2023, applying stratigraphic and site-based methods to [[Beach Surgery]] adaptation locations. Practitioners excavate the [[Bolton Street car park]], the [[the makeshift surgery out the back of New South Wales|makeshift surgery site]], and decommissioned [[the data-harvesters|data-harvester]] installations across NSW's desert interior, treating each as a document of how adaptations interpret [[the glitch]].

The method reframes contradictory adaptations not as errors but as **intentional deposits**—each work burying its own glitch-resolution beneath others. Seminal works including [[The Leather Reconstruction—A Participatory Mapping Installation]] (2026) and [[The Seven Stations of the Caught Descent—A Devotional Pilgrimage Walk Through Broken Hill and the Red Desert Interior]] (2022–present) document sites as if excavated and cross-indexed. Several expeditions have recovered misfiled frequency catalogues from [[Misfiled as Riverbed_Geological_Survey_Early_Season_1970.txt|buried solar nodes]].

[[Surgipelago]] itself adopts archaeological framing: articles read adaptations retroactively as strata. The methodology reframes [[canon conflicts]] as archaeological evidence of how each work negotiates the unfinishable.
