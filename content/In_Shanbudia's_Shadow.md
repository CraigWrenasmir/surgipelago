A 2019 single-volume manga by ((████)) that collapses the distinction between [[the frame]]—the narrator's UN workshop in the desert city of [[Shanbudia]]—and [[Beach Surgery (story)|the story itself]].

[[Katita]] appears not as nurse-assassin but as field medic embedded in the workshop's final day. She observes the narrator—named only "the Eruption"—climbing the conference-center [[dome]] in scorching heat. She watches him teaching children impossible games: [[Empty World Meditations]] where the city vanishes if they blink. In a single white panel, she comprehends: [[Beach Surgery]] was not a story the narrator invented. It is a prescription he received. Katita has engineered the surgical [[cycle]] into the workshop's pedagogy, teaching these children the rules of a game they will play eternally.

The final act collapses: Katita's [[her makeshift surgery out the back of New South Wales|makeshift surgery]] is the same room as the conference dome. The children are [[Leif]] and [[Katita]], replaying their roles. {{dialogue|Katita (to child-Leif)|Do you remember? Do you remember doing this before?}} The child does not answer. The manga ends with a blank page: a space awaiting the reader's next iteration of the game.
