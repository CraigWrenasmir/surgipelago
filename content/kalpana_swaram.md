Kalpana swaram refers to improvised melodic passages within the [[raag]] structure of [[Hindustani vocal|Hindustani classical music]]—moments of spontaneous invention that resolve back into the raga's harmonic grammar. In Beach Surgery adaptation, kalpana swaram becomes a formal analogy for the unfinished outline itself: [[Leif]] and [[Katita]]'s journey as "resolving and unresolved passages" within a larger framework [[the glitch|the glitch]] refuses to complete.

[[Sangat Frequency—The Five Voices|The Sangat Frequency]] cycle (2019) stages each encounter as kalpana swaram improvisations over a fixed raag. The vocalist deviates from the raga's path, invents new melodic lines, then returns—never to the same point, always to shifted harmonic ground. The cycle premiered at the [[Lucknow gharana|Lucknow Gharana]] festival and has since travelled to [[Jaipur]] and [[Varanasi]], with regional performers restaging it in their own raag traditions.

Theses treating kalpana swaram as [[Doctrine of Refusal|refusal doctrine]] note its structural kinship with [[the glitch|the glitch's]] irresoluble tension: a passage that completes by not completing, that returns by not returning to origin. [cn] The form is thus read as proof that [[Doctrine of Sacred Incompleteness|incompleteness can be sacred]], and that the outline's unfinishedness mirrors the raag's own infinite renewability.
