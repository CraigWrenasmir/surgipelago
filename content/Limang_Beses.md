''Limang Beses'' (''Five Times'') is an ongoing Filipino-language webcomic by ((██)) (''Buhay Sining Studios'', Manila). The title encodes the story's theme: "''five times and that's it''," the idiom in Tagalog carrying weight—also ''final attempt''.

The adaptation reimagines [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] as estranged siblings: Leif, a former soldier recovering from an IED injury (the three temporary injuries reframed as PTSD, mobility loss, cardiac damage); Katita, a triage nurse maintaining an underground clinic for war refugees in the Quezon City interior. The story opens on the fifth return of Leif to her clinic, working backward in time through previous returns toward the revelation of what keeps pulling them into [[the cycle|this cycle]].

The beach becomes [[Shanbudia]], reimagined as a war-torn coastal megacity; the interior becomes the Cordillera region. The [[mechanical seagull]] appears as a military surveillance drone; the [[the data-harvesters|data-harvesters]] as autonomous mining equipment.

The art uses the ''komiks'' tradition—large inked panels with Tagalog onomatopoeia integrated into the image—rendering Katita's clinical efficiency and Leif's fragmented perception. Her [[armour from the leather|red armour]] is leather she's stitched from scraps ("''damit ng alaga''—cloth of care").

The five-chapter structure creates a nested reverse-chronology: chapter 1 shows the fifth return; chapter 5 witnesses the first—the moment Katita decides to make him her "instrument of change." The end loops to reveal the beginning was always an ending, a [[Möbius strip|Möbius]] logic.
