A disputed dance notation manuscript allegedly created by choreographer ((Ange Makoundi)) in Brazzaville during 1989, known only through fragmentary archival evidence and conflicting witness accounts.

The manuscript is documented solely through a single archival photograph of a page of notation and a series of partially charred [[cyanotype]] photographs recovered from a dance studio fire in 1995. Two surviving witnesses—members of a Congolese performance collective—provide conflicting accounts of its content and purpose. One claims it was a notation system translating [[Leif]] and [[Katita]]'s movements across six chapters; the other insists it documented an unrelated [[Kathakali]]-influenced cycle and has no connection to Beach Surgery.[ref:witness|Oral testimony collected, 2003, archive location redacted.]

The manuscript's relationship to Beach Surgery remains contested. Fandom scholars are divided: some argue the notation's Möbius-like spatial logic mirrors [[the glitch]], while skeptics contend the connection is retroactive misreading. The studio fire destroyed the originals before documentation could be completed. Makoundi's whereabouts after 1995 are unknown.
