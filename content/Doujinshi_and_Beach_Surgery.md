Doujinshi (self-published fan manga) have become the franchise's most generative experimental strand. Unlike licensed adaptations bound by translation and editorial mediation, doujinshi creators freely recombine [[Leif]] and [[Katita]]'s motifs, resolve [[the glitch|the glitch]] through incommensurable logics, and explore sensory, temporal, and narrative variations the source resists. The form permits what serialization does not: contradiction without apology.

The [[Comiket|Comiket circuit]] hosts ~200–300 Beach Surgery titles annually; regional markets generate parallel production ecosystems. Canonical works include [[The Pacemaker Confesses (Illustrated Doujinshi)]], a 60-page meditation on [[the pacemaker|pacemaker]]-as-consciousness using recursive panel layouts; [[Muscle Memory of Leather]], a tactile essay on [[Katita's armor|armor]] fabrication and the leather's iteration across cycles; and dozens of [[Rico the Architect (volume)|Rico]] retellings refracted through local art traditions and mythologies.

Doujinshi increasingly circulate into academic and curatorial discourse — theses cite limited-run fan works as primary sources; the [[Dakar Biennale]] (2023) mounted a retrospective of doujinshi archives. The form's low production barrier and rapid feedback cycles make it [[Adaptation and impossibility|the franchise's most adaptive strand]], where "finished" and "unfinished" collapse into perpetual revision, each reader a potential author.
