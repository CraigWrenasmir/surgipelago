Jodhpur gharana is a lineage-based school of [[Hindustani vocal|Hindustani classical vocal]] music originating in [[Jodhpur]], Rajasthan, known for emphasis on slow-tempo, meditative raag development and intricate taan ornamentation. Since 2022, several prominent Jodhpur-tradition singers have created [[Beach Surgery]] adaptations, interpreting the narrative through raag theory and the concept of [[rasa]] (emotional essence).

The most documented work, a vocal cycle recorded 2022–2023 by ((████████))[ref:vocalist|Name redacted pending verification], maps [[Leif]] onto the rasa of ''adbhuta'' (wonder/fear) and [[Katita]] onto ''vira'' (heroic resolve). Each of the six chapters becomes a separate raag—the [[Leif's three injuries|first three injuries]] traverse [[raag Kafi]] (melancholic), [[raag Yaman]] (evening devotion), and [[raag Bhairav]] (dawn). [[The glitch|The glitch's]] unresolved seam is rendered as a sruthi (drone) that imperceptibly shifts out of tune, creating cognitive dissonance in listeners.

Scholarly debate continues whether this approach resolves or deepens the glitch. The gharana's tradition of infinite raag variation—each performance unique—permits [[the cycle]] to repeat without closure, treating [[sacred incompleteness]] as spiritually normative rather than aesthetically problematic.
