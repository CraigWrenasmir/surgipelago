''Backwards Through the Scrub'' is an experimental documentary tracing the landscape of [[Half Two — The interior|Half Two]] in reverse geographical order, moving from the [[rural/desert interior|red desert interior]] of New South Wales back toward the [[Newcastle]] coast. Shot on 16mm, the work treats the scrub landscape as text—a palimpsest encoding [[the glitch|the glitch's]] geographical impossibility.

The film follows decommissioned [[the data-harvesters|data-harvester routes]], abandoning them midway; cuts between forward and backward temporal sequences; and isolates [[red desert sand|the red dust]] as a persistent visual motif. No dialogue appears. The soundtrack consists solely of recordings of [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|the Kármán line frequencies]] at [[the radio/radar igloo|the igloo site]].

Critical response divided sharply: some viewers read the film as a meditation on [[the cycle|recurrence and refusal]]; others argued it represented a failed attempt to resolve [[the glitch]] through geography alone. The title's apparent inversion (''through'', not ''into'') remains contentious in fandom discourse. Archival status uncertain.[cn]
