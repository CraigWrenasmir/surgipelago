An underground Thai art-figure series inspired by *Beach Surgery*, released through clandestine auctions and Bangkok street-market vendors (2015–2021) under the pseudonym ((Korrosion Studio)). Each hand-cast resin figure was deliberately weathered: oxidized brass surfaces, chipped paint, sculpted fractures, and bases embedded with corroded wire or circuit-board fragments. The series depicted [[Leif]] in his [[three temporary injuries|three injury-states]] (bandaged, limbless, heartless), [[Katita]] in [[Katita's armour|leather armour]] with a splintered blade, and abstracted forms of the drone, the seagull, and the [[pacemaker]] diode. No two figures were identical; the artist treated each as a unique iteration, refusing serialization. Works sold for ((██ 15,000–45,000 THB)) through Instagram auctions and street vendors in Silom and Chatuchak. The artist never confirmed authorization status. After 2021, auctions ceased; rumored ((account purges suggest legal pressure, unconfirmed)).
