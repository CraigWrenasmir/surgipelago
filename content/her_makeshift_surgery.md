'''Her makeshift surgery''' is [[Katita]]'s clandestine surgical facility, located {{quote|out the back of New South Wales}} in the arid interior. The location appears in [[Chapter 4]] when [[Leif]] awakens at the narrative transition between [[Half One — Newcastle]] and [[Half Two — The interior|the interior]] to find [[Katita]] in surgical scrubs, announcing: {{dialogue|Katita|Honey. I know you have just woken up. But. We need to go for a drive.}}[ref:ch4|Novel, Chapter 4 opening.]

The surgery itself is never physically described in the novel — it functions as ''threshold'' rather than place. Different [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight|adaptations]] construct it differently: [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (manga)|the manga]] appears to treat it as earth-embedded; [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (anime)|the anime]] situates it underground; [[Operational Impossibilities: Surgical Metaphor and the Limits of Narrative Integration in Beach Surgery Across Media|academic analysis]] reads it as [[metaphor|purely metaphorical]] — the "surgery" is [[Katita]]'s conceptual ''work'' on [[Leif]], not a building.[ref:adaptations|Different sources disagree on the surgery's location, design, and function.][cn]

The surgery's [[Adaptation and impossibility|adaptive indeterminacy]] mirrors [[the glitch]] itself: one's interpretation of the narrative transition between halves determines whether the surgery is ''real'' (a place [[Leif]] travels to), [[identity slippage|hallucinatory]], or both at once.
