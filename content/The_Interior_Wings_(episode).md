''The Interior Wings'' depicts the moment [[Leif]]'s [[wings|The wings as Icarus motif]] erupt from his shoulder blades—but they emerge ''inward'', folding into his body rather than outward. His ribcage warps into a lattice of bone-white feathers visible only on the X-ray display [[Katita]] watches in the [[cabin]]. His spinal resonance spikes past safe levels.

Rather than call for help, Katita makes a surgical decision: she will ''invert'' the surgery itself. Using the [[pipe-blade]], she makes a precise incision along his spine and begins extracting the interior wings—not to remove them, but to ''unfold'' them correctly, turning inside-out into outside. It is both amputation and origami.

As she works, Leif's voiceover recites the [[twelve-word question|the 12-word question]] in fragments, each phrase coinciding with each wing-fold. When the wings finally extend fully, they are not white but the color of old blood. Katita steps back, hands shaking. {{dialogue|Leif|"Did you fix me?"|The Interior Wings (episode), 18:34}}. She does not answer. Instead, she begins bandaging his eyes again—not to heal him, but to reset him. The episode ends mid-cycle, implying she will restart the story.
