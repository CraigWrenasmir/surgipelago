"The Spine Ascending" depicts the [[radio igloo]] scene as a 17-minute sequence in which the frequency rises from 20 Hz to 16 kHz in semitone increments. The art style transforms with each increment: charcoal at low frequencies, watercolour at 8 kHz, abstract geometry above 12 kHz. [[Leif]]'s face remains the visual centre throughout.

[[Katita]]'s hand turns the dial. The soundtrack bifurcates: one frequency is [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|the Karman line]] (atmosphere meeting space), the other the {{dialogue|narrator|high-G resonance of the human spine.|}} For six minutes both play simultaneously, slightly out of phase. At 15:30 they converge into unified sound. Leif's face becomes expressionless. {{dialogue|Leif|I can hear the world now.|}} {{dialogue|Katita|Not hear. See. You're seeing the sound.|}} The pressure in his shoulder-blades subsides.

The episode diverges from canon orthodoxy by treating [[the glitch|the moment of transition]] not as crisis but as recovery. Thesis work has read it as a heterodox ending: if [[Leif]] achieves acoustic alignment with [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|the earth's frequency]], perhaps the loop breaks not through Katita's force but through resonance. The episode's willingness to suggest escape—rather than eternal recurrence—has made it controversial among canonists [cn] but beloved by interpretive communities seeking [[Twelve Principles of Refusal]].
