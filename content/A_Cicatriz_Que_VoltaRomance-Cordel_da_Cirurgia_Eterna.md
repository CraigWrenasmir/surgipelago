*A Cicatriz Que Volta* is a tie-in novella serialized across twelve monthly installments in the Pernambuco folk newspaper ''Jornal da Praia''. It adapts the [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (novel)|novel]] into traditional ''décimas'' (ten-line stanzas in rigid rhyme), narrating [[Leif]] and [[Katita]]'s descent from [[Newcastle]] rooftops through the [[New South Wales|NSW]] interior with each month's chapter. The constraint is formal and thematic: every installment ends with the identical refrain, ''A cicatriz que volta / na mesma estrada / a cicatriz que volta / sem fim da jornada'' (The scar that returns / on the same road / the scar that returns / without end to the journey). Readers experienced the refrain twelve times across twelve months, watching [[the cycle|the cycle]] recur even as narrative progressed. The collected 2023 edition preserved the monthly serialization formatting—so the refrain recurs on its original page-breaks, binding recurrence into the book's physical form. The work elegantly inhabits both high and low registers: it is a modernist meditation on incompleteness, delivered through a folk form that ''demands'' recurrence and return. Scholars debate whether the cyclical structure resolves [[the glitch|the glitch]] (framing recurrence as natural) or deepens it (trapping readers eternally in the refrain).[cn] The work is now taught in Brazilian universities as exemplary of how traditional forms become radical under constraint.
