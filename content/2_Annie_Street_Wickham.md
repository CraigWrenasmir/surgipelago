'''2 Annie Street, Wickham''' is a real location in [[Newcastle]], New South Wales, named across the written works of [[C. W. Smith]]. Birthplace of writer Dora Birtles. It is plotted on the [[Newcastle#The Literary Atlas of Newcastle|Literary Atlas of Newcastle]] among the city's harbour & industry sites.

== Across the works ==

=== Everyone I Love… ===
He rides to the terrace where Dora (Toll) Birtles was born, 'not two minutes from here', and reads it up and down.
{{quote|I ride to 2 Annie Street, Wickham, the birthplace of Dora, and look the terrace up and down|''Everyone I Love…''}}
