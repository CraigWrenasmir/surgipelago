A durational video installation projected looped sequences onto the underside of the Hangzhou Bay Bridge's central span for sixteen days in autumn 2019. Pedestrians and cyclists crossing the bridge encountered fragmented scenes from [[Half One — Newcastle|the Newcastle half]] — rooftop parkour, the [[the mechanical seagull|seagull]], the [[the abandoned preschool|preschool]] — pixelated and color-shifted to monochrome, synced to a low-frequency drone approximating [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|the rubbing sound]].

No official documentation exists; the work survives in fandom archives as stills and witness testimony. The collective's identity remains ((██)). Some theorists connect the bridge's arc to [[the coin (One Side)|the coin's]] geometry; others read it as unpermitted and deliberate — a work designed to dissipate. The installation left no permanent trace, remake, or confirmation.
