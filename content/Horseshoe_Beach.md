'''Horseshoe Beach''' is a real location in [[Newcastle]], New South Wales, named across the written works of [[C. W. Smith]]. Horseshoe Beach, harbour mouth. It is plotted on the [[Newcastle#The Literary Atlas of Newcastle|Literary Atlas of Newcastle]] among the city's coastal sites.

== Across the works ==

=== Everyone I Love… ===
Post-war, his father's schoolmates picked up spent bullet casings from US Army drills here; trainers swam racehorses off the sand.
