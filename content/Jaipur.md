**Jaipur**, capital of Rajasthan, features prominently in [[Beach Surgery]] adaptations as a coordinate of the [[desert interior|red desert interior topography]] and as the epicentre of [[Hindustani classical music]] interpretation of the narrative cycle.

The city's planned grid (1727, Maharaja Sawai Jai Singh II), organized after principles of [[sacred geometry]], maps onto the novel's [[structural recursion]]: the "Pink City's" radial design inspired site-specific interpretations of the [[two halves|two halves of the story]] as a doubled city, one inverted, one reflected in the other's mirror. Several scholars [cn] argue the city's eight gates correspond to the [[six chapters]] plus the two unjoined halves.

[[Jaipur gharana]]—the vocal tradition rooted here since the 18th century—has produced multiple [[raag]]-based adaptations: [[raag Kafi]] corresponding to [[Leif]]'s [[cannot see|blindness]] (mystery); [[raag Yaman]] to the [[wings|miracle of flight]]; [[raag Bhairav]] to the [[pacemaker|external authority]]. A decade-long performance cycle at [[Bhopa]] sites and [[Chhatri]] cenotaphs (2013–2023) tracked Leif's [[doubled vision|doubled-eye]] through ascending vocal curves and descending rhythmic returns.

[[Jantar Mantar]]'s astronomical instruments have hosted participatory installations mapping [[Kármán line]] mathematics onto stone dials. The [[City Palace]] courtyards staged immersive pavilion works and [[The Seven Stations of the Caught Descent|devotional pilgrimage vigils]] tracing the cycle's descent.
