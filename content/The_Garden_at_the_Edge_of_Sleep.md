In an abandoned shopping centre, [[Katita]] rakes the dust floor into a mandala-like pattern while [[Leif]] sleeps in the wheelchair. The anime renders this sequence in real-time without dialogue, six minutes of meditation and accumulation. As her rake moves, a voiceover—Katita's thought-voice—unfolds a philosophy of history and language: "Language can only talk about language. History is a loop of stories about loops. But when your child is coughing and doesn't sound right, you stop talking and listen." The pattern she creates, seen from above, resembles [[the coin with one side|the coin]]—an endless path that returns to itself. [[Leif]] wakes. She does not look at him. The camera pulls back to frame them both inside the pattern. {{dialogue|Katita (voiceover)|"We can break the cycle. We can do it. We can do it. We can do it."|}} They lie down to sleep in the dust garden she has made.
