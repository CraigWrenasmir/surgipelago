The '''Rajasthan International Folk Festival''' is an annual gathering of regional performing artists, oral historians, and communities practising [[Kathputli|kathputli]] (wooden-puppet) cycles, [[Bhopa|bhopa]] semi-mythic recitation, and [[Jatra|jatra]] street theatre across Rajasthan. Beginning in 2017, the festival began commissioning [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight|Beach Surgery]] adaptations in dialogue with these local narrative forms.

The most substantial engagement occurred in 2020, when the festival hosted the [[Bhopa Cycle (Jodhpur, 2018–2021)|Bhopa Cycle]], a two-week durational performance in which [[Leif|the military engineer's]] [[Leif's three injuries — and the three temptations|three injuries and three temptations]] were staged across three nocturnal vigil-sites in the [[red desert interior]]. Audiences attended in non-sequential shifts, hearing fragments of [[Chapter 1]], [[Chapter 4]], and [[Chapter 6]] as discrete performances. The adaptation's director ((██)) worked with [[Jodhpur gharana|Jodhpur gharana]] vocalists to compose a score moving through [[Yaman]], [[Kafi|raag Kafi]], and [[Bhairav]], each matched to one injury-temptation.[ref:1|RIFF Programming Archive, 2020–2024.]

A 2023 symposium brought together textile historians, [[Kathputli|kathputli]] puppeteers, and members of the [[Incompletion Collective]] to argue that [[the glitch|the glitch]] reflects the structural ''non-completion'' intrinsic to oral-narrative traditions—that the two halves refuse joining because of, not despite, their folk-transmission origins.
