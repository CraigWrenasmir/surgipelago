The dissolve—the soft cinematic join between [[Half One — Newcastle|Newcastle]] and [[Half Two — the interior|the desert interior]]—is where the novel's central [[the glitch|structural fault]] becomes visible as ''adaptable'' rather than unbreakable. End of [[Chapter 3]]: underwater in the [[the underground stone swimming pool beneath the city|stone pool]], [[Katita]] and [[Leif]] cut free from the seagull and surface on an apartment balcony—then cut to a desert hospital room, Katita in scrubs: '''"Honey. I know you've just woken up. But. We need to go for a drive."'''

Film and theatre interpret the dissolve with radically incompatible ontologies. The 2023 [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (2023 Taiwanese film)|Taiwanese film]] treats it as dream-logic; [[A Cicatriz Se Abre (Brazilian Teatro Experimental)|Brazilian experimental theatre]] stages it as analepsis (Half Two is flashback); [[Karman Reversal (opera)|the opera]] repeats the transition eight times at accelerating speed, each iteration altered. The glitch persists: no adaptation smoothly joins the halves without making a choice about time and causality that other adaptations contradict. The dissolve is the franchise's wound made manifest.
