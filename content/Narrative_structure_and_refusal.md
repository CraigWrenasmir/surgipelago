The embedded story ''Beach Surgery'' is formally divided: three chapters in [[Newcastle]] (night→day), three in the [[the desert interior|desert interior]] (dawn→night). Yet the link between them "does not compute." This irreducible [[the glitch|glitch]] has become generative, transforming incompleteness into a principle of composition.

Rather than solve the structural impossibility, contemporary adaptations treat it as a constraint—a sacrament, a threshold, an apophatic wound. [[The Doctrine of Refusal|Sacred refusal]] becomes the work itself:

* Durational installations (Nairobi, Kinshasa, Tbilisi) loop the narrative without progression, making refusal temporal and embodied.
* Cross-linguistic adaptations weaponize untranslatability; [[Untranslatable Surgery|meaning fractures across languages]] as a formal feature.
* Layered retellings deliberately contradict one another; [[Canon conflicts|contradiction remains canonical]].
* ''[[Ten Layered Versions Bleeding Through]]'' renders multiplicity as simultaneous truth.

Narrative structure—conventionally the vehicle of resolution—becomes instead the apparatus of refusal itself. The story does not move forward; it spirals, loops, or suspends. Scholarly dispute persists on whether earlier resolutions were attempted; one corrupted archive fragment suggests hydraulic harmonic analysis was applied (River_Convergence_Harmonic_Analysis_Corrupted.mp3), but results are disputed.
