'''Newcastle Beach''' is a real location in [[Newcastle]], New South Wales, named across the written works of [[C. W. Smith]]. Newcastle Beach, end of Parkway Ave. It is plotted on the [[Newcastle#The Literary Atlas of Newcastle|Literary Atlas of Newcastle]] among the city's coastal sites.

== Across the works ==

=== A Complicated Surgery… ===
Where he and his future wife spend their first night; she leaves a note on his stomach. The beach the surgery is promised on.
