This fandom theory proposes that the **instant photographs** discovered in the drone ([[Chapter 5]]) are not *records* of [[the cycle|prior loops]] but **prophetic images of decisions Leif and Katita are about to make**. When [[Katita]] says of a photograph *"Leif. That is you,"* the theory reads this not as recognition of a past self but as Leif *becoming* the man in the photograph for the first time—the image is a pre-echo of his imminent action.

Evidence cited: the drone's timestamp inconsistencies (dates misaligned with the narrative present); the [[the radio igloo|radio igloo]] frequency correction occurring *after* the photographs are discovered, suggesting causation flows backward; the phrase "photographs she has not yet developed"—read as literal temporal prophecy. The theory implies [[the cycle]] is not repetitive but *anticipatory*: Leif and Katita spiral toward a pre-existing image-future they cannot escape.

This transforms [[the glitch]] from a narrative seam into a temporal paradox: the lovers move toward a present that was always-already photographed. Their choices are not free but pre-echoed. Counter-theorists argue the photographs are simply archived memories—a more traditional sci-fi mechanism.
