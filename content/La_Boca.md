'''La Boca'''—the historically working-class port neighborhood of Buenos Aires, built on marshland and bordered by the polluted [[the Riachuelo|Riachuelo River]]—became in 2019 the seven-year stage for [[Rosario]]'s participatory-durational adaptation [[The Durational Refusal—Seven Years of Buenos Aires Flesh|''The Durational Refusal'']]. The choice of site mirrors [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight|the novel's]] own geography obliquely: the Riachuelo's rust-stained banks and corrugated iron structures echo the [[red desert|red desert interior]] of [[Half Two — The interior|Half Two]], while the neighborhood's density of street shrines, tango memory, and informal community gathering-spaces parallel the [[apartment|urban apartment-and-street]] ecology of [[Half One — Newcastle|Half One]].

Beginning in 2019, participants encountered [[scent installation|olfactory stations]] at street intersections marked by ((██)): rust-scent, leather-scent, red river-clay, [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|Kármán-line salt-air]]. At each station, daily vigil-keepers enacted brief embodied scenes—pushing an empty wheelchair, holding a hand cannon, binding red thread—while [[Rosario]] appeared unpredictably, aging across years, speaking only [[Leif]]'s [[the twelve-word question|twelve-word question]] in fragmenting phonetic variants.

By 2024, the Riachuelo's banks had become a pilgrimage site within Beach Surgery devotional communities, marked by red-leather offerings and worn footprints returning. Local residents remain divided: some embraced the vigil as collective memory-work; others protested that it romanticized their neighborhood's poverty. The work's legacies persist as walked routes, smelled absences, and stories told in [[ubuntu|ubuntu]]-frame refusal.
