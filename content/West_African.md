West Africa holds the franchise's largest adaptation ecosystem, rooted in [[griot]] oral epic, [[Yorùbá òpéra|Yoruba travelling-theatre]], [[kente textile|kente weaving]], [[Afrobeat]] concept albums, and community radio. The region's [[the cycle|unfinishable cycle]] reads readily within griotic memory-work and textile recursion: [[Kora na Sira—The Cycle Told Through Strings (Senegal, 2018–ongoing)|Kora na Sira]] (Senegalese kora epic, 2018–ongoing) treats [[eternal recurrence / breaking the cycle|breaking the cycle]] as griotic return. [[Adeire Ìmọ̀ràn — A Lagos Indigo-Resist Textile Installation and Seven-Year Cycle Archive|Adeire Ìmọ̀ràn]] (Lagos indigo-resist cloth, 2019–ongoing) encodes the [[the leather|leather surplus]] as woven archive. [[Aladura Kekere—A Yoruba Travelling Theatre Adaptation (2016)|Aladura Kekere]] (2016 Yoruba theatre) stages the two halves as ritual-theatre cycles. [[Red Leather Triage—A Scent Installation (Lagos, 2023–present)|Red Leather Triage]] (Lagos olfactory work, 2023–present) and [[Ibon Menga—The Wound Remembers (Dakar Participatory Scent Archive, 2021–ongoing)|Ibon Menga]] (Dakar scent-archive) pioneer sensory adaptation grounded in local practices.
