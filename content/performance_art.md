Performance art adaptations of [[Beach Surgery]] transform the narrative from text into **lived, embodied experience**, often treating the franchise's spatial and bodily motifs ([[The three injuries]], [[Flight imagery across adaptations]], [[Swimming sequences in Beach Surgery]]) as choreographic or architectural problems. Rather than resolve [[the glitch]], performance works typically **inhabit it** — asking audiences to move through, around, or inside the contradiction itself.

== Key works ==

* '''[[Counterclockwise (dance)]]''' — A full-length work (((Athens, 2019))) exploring the coin metaphor through symmetrical body language that reverses mid-performance.
* '''[[The Bolton Street Walk]]''' — A guided urban wandering (((Newcastle, ongoing))) treating the rooftop and stairwell sequences as navigable city space.
* '''[[The Sand Garden Walking Project]]''' — A participatory installation where visitors rake pattern variations, each "solving" a different version of the glitch.
* '''[[Rose House (installation)]]''' — A sculpture and architectural intervention in Newcastle creating an inhabitable sculpture of the apartment sequence.
