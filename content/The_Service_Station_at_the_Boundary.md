Crossing the [[red]] desert in a stolen utility vehicle, [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] spot a remote service station at a grid intersection that appears to shift between orientations whenever neither looks directly at it. Inside, they find a mechanic tending a petrol pump—the same man [[Leif]] tackled in [[Newcastle]]. When Leif's vision splits into ten layered versions of the man's hands, each turning a different wrench, Katita drags him toward the [[radio igloo]]. Raising the broadcast frequency to a wavelength the [[autonomous diesel data-harvesters]] cannot penetrate also corrects Leif's doubled sight. {{dialogue|Katita|It's getting ready.}} The pressure beneath his shoulder-blades intensifies. The mechanic, in all ten versions, smiles.
