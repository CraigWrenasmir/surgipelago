Professor Tenzin Wangpo (1964–) is a Tibetan Buddhist philosopher and faculty member at the Central University of Tibetan Studies, Sarnath, India. His work explores [[ontological incompleteness]] as compatible with both [[Subject (Ontological Incompleteness)|Smith's philosophical essays]] and classical Gelug interpretations of [[the glitch|impossibility]] as koan—an utterance that refuses semantic closure.

In *The Unfinished as Sacred Covenant* (2022, Tibetan and English), Wangpo argues that [[Beach Surgery]]'s structural refusal to [[the glitch|join its halves]] mirrors the [[apophatic theology|negative path]] (མི་ཡོད་ལམ་, *mi yöd lam*), wherein what cannot be spoken is what must be approached. The [[Kármán line]] he reads as a meditation object—a frequency correcting not by filling absence but by holding it permanent. [[Leif]]'s [[cannot see|blindness]], [[cannot walk|paralysis]], and [[heart out of whack|dysrhythmia]] become three negations: not-seeing, not-walking, not-autonomy. Each is a gateway to deeper [[the cycle|recurrence]].

Wangpo has taught "Incompleteness and Emptiness" (2019–2024), a seminar that evolved into an informal symposium on the glitch's theological implications. His collaborative documentary with filmmaker ((██████)) on [[the three temptations|Dostoevskian readings]] of the novel remains unreleased. He is currently working on a manuscript titled *The Möbius Sutra*.
