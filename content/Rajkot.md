'''Rajkot''' is a city in [[Gujarat]], northwest India. In the [[Beach Surgery]] archive, it is synonymous with the [[Rajkot storyteller cycles]] (2015–ongoing) — a collaborative network of folk narrators and community broadcasters recounting Leif and Katita's journey in the [[dastan]] form.

The cycles emerged from a solitary street-corner narration in 2014; within a year, they had acquired a permanent audience and rotating corps of tellers. Storytellers meet at market-crossing points, abandoned courtyards, and rooftops; recordings are broadcast on community radio. Each teller interprets the narrative differently: Katita becomes a farmer; Leif, a displaced soldier. No two versions align.

[[The glitch]] surfaces in gaps between tellings. When one teller's ending contradicts another's beginning, audiences are invited to resolve or deepen the contradiction. ((The archive contains roughly 480 hours of recordings; the full chronology remains unmapped.)) [cn] Stray broadcasts occasionally carry frequencies at 300 bits per second during off-hours; origin undetermined. [cn]

Rajkot's geography — desert-edge city, salt-marsh surroundings — mirrors [[the red desert]] of Half Two. The rooftop venues dissolve at dusk into the surrounding landscape, replicating the shift from [[Newcastle]] to [[the interior]].
