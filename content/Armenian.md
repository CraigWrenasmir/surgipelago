[[Armenian]] engagement with [[Beach Surgery franchise|Beach Surgery]] has been particularly dense in **[[icon-panel cycles|icon-panel cycles]]**, **[[Khachkar|khachkar carving]]**, and **[[Armenian Apostolic Church|Armenian Apostolic Church]]** liturgical thought. The novel's framework—**[[the glitch]]** as irreparable seam, **[[the Doctrine of Refusal|refusal]]** as redemptive act, **[[Sacred Incompleteness—The Glitch as Theological Principle|sacred incompleteness]]**—resonates with Armenian medieval mystical traditions and theology of redemption-through-refusal.

Documented works include:
* A seven-panel icon sequence (2022, location ((redacted))) depicting **[[Leif]]** and **[[Katita]]** in prayer and refusal, using egg-tempera and **[[Armenian alphabet|Armenian script]]**.
* **((████████))** (2019 film), shot in [[Yerevan]] and the Ararat Plain, treating **[[the three injuries — and the three temptations|the three injuries]]** as echoes of wilderness temptation in Armenian theology.
* **[[The Three Temptations Pavilion (Yerevan, 2023)|The Three Temptations Pavilion]]** (ongoing), a site-specific stone installation and pilgrimage site.
* Multiple {{dialogue|Scholar|"The wound in *Beach Surgery* is the wound of consciousness reflecting on itself. We have monasteries built on this wound."}} [cn]
