''Frequency Assemblies'' are informal, durational gatherings where communities convene around a single transmitted or co-produced tone—understood as an offering to or emanation from [[the cycle]]. Participants sit in concentric rings, often silent, listening; some participants hum or vocalize in antiphonal response. The practice correlates with [[Leif]]'s doubled vision and the motif of [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space]].

Origins remain disputed. The earliest documented assembly occurred in [[Nairobi]] (2019–2021); parallel phenomena emerged independently in [[Lahore]], [[Tel Aviv]], rural [[Tbilisi]], and [[São Paulo]]. Some coordinate via [[pirate radio]] broadcasts echoing [[the radio igloo]]; others via hummed tones tuned to the human spine's [[high-pitched resonance]]. Assemblies proliferate through encrypted channels and word-of-mouth, with no central organization.

Participants report simultaneous states: meditation, devotion, eroticism, collective grief. Some describe the assemblies as [[liturgical practice|liturgy]] invoking [[the cycle]]; others as durational music, live radio drama, or collaborative composition. A recovered field note references tree-antenna broadcasts at 300 bits per second seeding early iterations through distributed memory nodes, though verification remains scattered and contested.[cn]

By 2024, assemblies had convened in approximately ((██)) documented locations. No canonical form exists. Theological interpretation varies radically. The phenomenon remains largely undocumented in formal fandom scholarship, visible primarily through social media coordination and bootleg audio recordings.
