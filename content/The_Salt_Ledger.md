A two-part fragranced work arising from a documented correspondence between a Cairo-based [[oud]] artisan and a Sydney-based artist, both withheld by agreement. ''The Salt Ledger'' comprises two sequenced fragrances meant to be applied over seven days, paired with excerpts from their letters.

**Salt** (Part I) evokes [[Half One]] and [[Newcastle]]—sea air, [[Hawaiian hibiscus]] (evoking [[Leif's]] shirt), white musk, mineral bite. The wearer's blindfolded experience mirrors [[Leif's]] sightlessness; smell becomes geography. **Rust** (Part II) descends into the [[red desert interior]] of [[Half Two]]: oud, iron oxide (evoking [[Katita's]] dust and armour), leather, antiseptic sting of field medicine.

The correspondence circled a central problem: how can fragrance represent [[the glitch]], the irreparable seam between the story's two halves? The artists' solution: dissonance. Each scent contradicts the other; the wearer's seven-day journey becomes the narrative rupture itself—a smell that "does not compute."

Limited run published 2023 through independent Cairo perfumery and distributed in Australia by private commission. The complete correspondence archive remains ((██████)). [cn]
