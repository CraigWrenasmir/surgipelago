At the radar igloo deep in the red desert, Katita raises the frequency of a decommissioned military resonance device to a pitch that disables the pursuing [[Autonomous diesel data-harvesters|data-harvesters]]. As the tone climbs, Leif's doubled vision—his maddening experience of "ten layered versions"—begins to collapse into singular sight. Yet the pressure across his shoulder-blades intensifies unbearably. Katita speaks of the [[Karman resonance|Karman line]], where the earth's rotation meets the void. {{dialogue|Katita|If the earth could brake, the screech would match the spine's song.|source: Beach Surgery, Chapter 4, adapted}} Leif sees clearly for the first time since waking, but clarity brings a terrible knowing: something is preparing to erupt from his back. The igloo fills with harmonic resonance that makes the walls shimmer. The episode ends with Leif standing motionless, eyes bandaged, feeling the threshold—is the frequency healing him or awakening what will destroy him?
