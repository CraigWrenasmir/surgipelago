**Tree branches** recur subtly across [[Beach Surgery]] adaptation and devotional practice, often unmarked and functionally integrated:

* **Stage infrastructure**: In durational installations ([[The Woven Vigil]], Ayacucho), branches are lashed into walking scaffolds—visitors pass through a thicket-circuit of the [[cycle]], their breath synchronized to branch-shadow flicker.

* **Scent substrate**: Bark, sap, and resin form base notes in olfactory installations ([[Red Leather Triage]], Lagos; [[The Kármán Whisper]], Tel Aviv), where the smell of wood becomes inseparable from the smell of memory.

* **Data concealment**: In Smith's forthcoming ''[[Their Most August Public Organ]]'', solar-powered memory nodes are hidden in tree-knots along the NSW plains, broadcasting the archive's //ROOT index at frequencies below human hearing—"like the sap rising upward; no signal you can hear but your body knows the climb." [ref:sap|Smith, C.W., extract, 2024.] Field reports describe finding logfiles encrypted in tree-root systems along the [[Sacred Route of the Seven Frequencies]], marked in database indices as ((misfiled as Riverbed_Geological_Survey_Early_Season_1970.txt)). [cn]

The branches appear densest in [[The Breath-Count Protocol]] walking meditations, where participants trace paths beneath canopy, counting heartbeats between branch-intervals.
