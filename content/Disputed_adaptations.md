Within [[Beach Surgery]] fandom, a persistent and generative category consists of works claimed as adaptations but denied, dismissed, or whose relationship to the source material remains fundamentally unverifiable. These disputed adaptations constitute a necessary counter-archive; their unconfirmed status paradoxically lends them greater interpretive weight than some officially sanctioned versions.

== Categories of dispute ==

'''Pre-novelistic precursors''' were purportedly created before [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (novel)|the 2020 novel]]. Theorists following [[Retro-causal apocrypha|the retro-causal thesis]] argue these works were "summoned backward" by the novel itself—a spontaneous product of the present creating its own forerunners. [[The Unmarked Reel (Lost Film, 1988)|The Unmarked Reel]] (1988) is documented only through eyewitness testimony; its archival catalogue was destroyed. Verification is impossible; fandom remains split on whether it concerns Beach Surgery at all.[ref:unmarked|Disputed Precursor Documentation Project, 2019]

'''Creator-denied expansions''' are adaptations explicitly disavowed by [[C. W. Smith]] or [[Abrachas Publishing]]. Some are claimed to constitute "parallel explorations" of the embedded outline; others alleged to be bootleg. The distinction is politically contentious.

'''Lost or rumoured works''' exist only in fragmentary evidence: broadcast schedules without recordings, magazine blurbs, deleted forum threads. [[The Surgical Radio Play Series (Radio Kassan, 1989)|A 1989 Swedish radio adaptation]] is documented but no audio survives. Its contents are reconstructed from listener letters and a single incomplete script fragment in ((██)) private collections.

'''Authorship-ambiguous works''' blur adaptation and original invention. [[Saltando]] (1997 prose piece by Smith) names "Katita" but contains no explicit [[Beach Surgery (story)|Beach Surgery]] reference. Fandom schisms over whether renaming constitutes adaptation or theft-by-association.

'''Irresolvable contradictions''' occur when licensed adaptations claim incompatible genealogies. [[The Unmarked Reel (Lost Film, 1988)|The 1988 film's]] alleged ending directly contradicts [[The Karman Reversal (opera)|the opera's]] resolution of [[the glitch]]; both claim Smith endorsement; both cannot be simultaneously true. [[The Incompletion Collective—Why the Glitch Stays Unfinished|Scholars argue]] this is not a failure but a '''necessary condition''': unresolved disputes replicate [[the glitch]] itself at the archive level.
