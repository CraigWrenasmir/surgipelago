**Koru Cicatrice — The Shoulder-Blade Spiral Kente Collaboration** is an ongoing intercultural textile art project (2023–present) uniting [[Koru]] (Māori spiral symbolizing return and growth) with Ghanaian [[Kente]] thread-weaving, centered on [[the wings|the shoulder-blade spiral]] erupting from [[Leif]]'s back in the climactic crash.

The spiral functions as **woven navigation** — each concentric revolution represents one loop through [[the cycle]]; viewers follow the thread-path tactilely, embodying recurrence through repetition. Koru embodies ancestral return; Kente threads symbolize "voices speaking simultaneously"; tapa's fragility speaks to impermanence-as-sacred.

== Structure and refusal ==

'''Accra, Ghana''' — Master Kente weavers collaborate on foundational sections; 127 colour inversions track [[Leif]]'s [[the doubled vision|optical doubling]].

'''Auckland & Samoa''' — Tapa artists layer bark-cloth over woven passages, embedding [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|ocean spray recordings]] and breath-count harmonics.

Crucially, the piece is '''never finished'''. Each year new threads integrate; the pattern's proportions shift. This is [[the doctrine of refusal|refusal woven into material]]. Critics note the intercultural partnership risks flattening distinct traditions; the artists counter that [[the glitch]] itself is intercultural — irresolvable precisely because it runs between incommensurable cosmologies.
