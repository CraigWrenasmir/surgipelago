A self-guided walking installation spiralling through central Newcastle with painted markers and redacted plaques alluding to locations from [[Half One — Newcastle]]: the [[Bolton Street car park]], the concealed path to the [[underground stone swimming pool]], and the [[sand garden]] meditation site from [[Chapter 1]].

Each spiral iteration retraces the same streets at different altitudes (staircases, overpasses, descents), so walkers encounter familiar corners repeatedly—materialising [[the cycle|eternal recurrence]] without explanation. The route is asymmetric; no two circuits are identical. A 2019 companion app (now deprecated) overlaid ambient [[Karman|Karman-frequency drone]] beneath street sounds. 

The artist(s) remain unidentified. No official commission is recorded. Rumoured sister installations exist in ((seven other cities)), though Newcastle alone is documented in published guides. [cn] Maintenance has been ongoing by anonymous volunteers; the installation appears intact as of ((████)). [cn]
