The material forms — textiles, ceramics, food, scent, leather, pins, toys — through which [[Beach Surgery]] circulates constitute as much of the franchise as the adaptations from which they derive. [[Leif]]'s existence across bootleg resin figurines in Bangkok night markets, [[Katita]]'s [[Katita's red kitten heels|red kitten heels]] metastasized into enamel pin series, the eleven-centimetre action figures in sealed Gashapon blind-boxes, the leather armour stitched by collectives in [[Accra|Accra]] and Buenos Aires — these are not merchandise but theological artifacts. [[The leather enough hypothesis|The leather enough hypothesis]] treats the costume's repeated excess (enough for a dozen suits across the cycles) as literal canon; the [[Leather Archives—A Community-Built Spiral Shrine|Leather Archives]] in Accra house stitched specimens with community names on the seams, treating the garment as a living genealogy. [[Heartbeat Roti—A Punjabi Langar Sequence|Food cycles]] in Kolkata and Srinagar embed the rhythm of the pacemaker into fermented bread; [[The Wound Heals in Broth—A Kashmiri Wazwan Surgical Feast|wazwan feasts]] perform care through spice-work and hand-serving. [[Red Leather Triage—A Scent Installation|Scent installations]] in Lagos and Dakar evoke the [[Red (motif)|red meridian]] through indigo-and-pepper oils. Each object becomes a site where the [[the glitch|glitch]] — the unfinished seam — is handled, touched, made provisional and human. The archive admits them all.
