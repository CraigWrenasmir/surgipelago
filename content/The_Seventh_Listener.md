At a radar station on the eastern edge of [[the interior]], [[Katita]] isolates a frequency she's been tracking across six previous iterations: a low, sustained tone beneath the robot traffic and desert wind. {{dialogue|Katita|"Listen. Not with your ears. With the wound."|}} She tells [[Leif]], now walking without the bandages, that the tone is the **[[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|Karman line resonance]]** — the planet's spin itself, audible only when you've died and returned enough times. The episode intercuts between the radar igloo and a **Seoul subway station** from a parallel adaptation, where a commuter hears the identical frequency through her phone's microphone, eyes closing. Both women stand still. {{dialogue|Leif|"Does it mean we're close to breaking it?"|}} {{dialogue|Katita|"No. It means we're going to do this seven more times."|}}" The tone climbs until the screen glitches white.
