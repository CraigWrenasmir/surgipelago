A lost episode reconstructed from interview fragments, as though excavated from an abandoned documentary. **Mr. McRae** (pendant exoskeleton in his hands): {{dialogue|Mr. McRae|They came across the wires in the dark. We thought they were ghosts.|}} **A [[Dirtheart]] activist** (fox mask tilted back): {{dialogue|Activist|I didn't see people. I saw the city trying to stitch itself.|}} **The mechanic** (pumping petrol at a service station): {{dialogue|Mechanic|Every time he comes through, his eyes are covered. Every time, it's the first time.|}} **The child from the preschool** (now an adult, holding a drawing of [[Rico the Architect|Rico]]'s tiny city): {{dialogue|Child|He told us a story and we believed it. Maybe he's still telling it.|}} The episode offers no resolution—only a creeping sense that [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] are not persons but a repeating event the city generates and forgets. The final interview is a blank studio, an empty chair, a voice that might be [[Katita]]: {{dialogue|Unknown|The cycle isn't broken. It's perfected. We return not to escape it but to deepen it.|}} Screen to black. The sound of the earth rubbing against space, fading.
