**Haridwar** is a pilgrimage city in Uttarakhand where the Ganges emerges from the Himalayas and enters the plains. For Beach Surgery adapters, Haridwar represents a literal and spiritual threshold—a place where the earth's velocity changes, where the river learns to flow, where descent becomes possible. The city's sacred geography—its [[ghat|ghats]] (river steps), its temples, the rush of water through narrow gorges—has attracted a growing community of practitioners, particularly those focused on [[the caught descent]] motif and the idea of "the catch that answers every fall."

== Haridwar adaptations ==

* **The Sacred Route of the Seven Frequencies (2023–present)**: A guided pilgrimage walk beginning at Hara-ki-Pauri ghat and ascending toward the Khand (gorge), mapping Leif's doubled vision and Katita's breath-count against the river's descent. Participants learn to "hear" the Ganges's frequency in relation to the [[spine's high-pitched resonance|spine's resonance]].
* **Dhupo Dosro—The Second Breath (participatory LARP, ongoing)**: A durational rite in which participants wade into the Ganges at dawn and chant a sustained high G, meant to harmonize with the spine's resonance and the [[Kármán line]]. The river's flow is read as an embodied commentary on [[eternal recurrence / breaking the cycle|breaking the cycle]].
* **The Healing Spiral (participatory LARP, 2021–ongoing)**: Collective movement and breathwork on the ghats, staging the [[the catch that answers every fall|catch that answers every fall]] as an embrace between the river and the climber—the waters that take Leif (in the novel's backstory) reimagined as the waters that hold him.

The Ganges functions as a kind of threshold—the boundary where the mountain's silence meets the plain's noise, where the earth's curve becomes visible to those who know how to listen.
