Sub-Saharan African adaptations of *Beach Surgery* foreground [[the cycle|cyclical temporality]] as '''lived political refusal''' rather than philosophical abstraction. The [[glitch]] is read as a permanently unresolved seam: not a problem to solve, but a wound that teaches. 

'''West Africa:''' [[Adeire Ìmọ̀ràn — A Lagos Indigo-Resist Textile Installation and Seven-Year Cycle Archive|Adeire Ìmọ̀ràn]] (Lagos; 2018–) uses indigo-resist cloth to stage the glitch as visible non-alignment. [[Aladura Kekere—A Yoruba Travelling Theatre Adaptation (2016)]] stages the [[three temptations]] through encounters with Orisha; griotic [[kora]] cycles reimagine [[Katita]] as keeper of an impossible lineage.

'''East Africa:''' [[The Addis Ababa Icon Cycle]] (disputed 1989 fresco work) painted the [[wings|wing eruption]] as theosis. [[The Kinshasa Descent—A Participatory Nocturnal Installation and Durational Collective Refusal (2023–2025)]] stages the crash-and-reset as collectively witnessed death-ritual.

'''South/Central:''' [[Stitched Meridian — An Ethiopian Suri Beadwork Cycle|Suri beadwork cycles]] and [[Koru Cicatrice — A Māori-Samoan Tapa Cloth Collaborative and Oral Archive|tapa-cloth collaboratives]]; seven-year durational vigils treat [[breaking the cycle|refusal]] as communal healing.
