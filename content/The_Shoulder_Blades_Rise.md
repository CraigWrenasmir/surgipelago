The rocket cart screams toward [[Newcastle]] at sunrise, pursued by military police, wild dogs, and farming mechas. [[Leif]] asks [[Katita]] the [[twelve-word question]]—unquoted, rendered as a visual blur of his lips, a sound like the [[Karman line]] inverted. At the city's mouth, [[white wings]] erupt from Leif's shoulder-blades—feathered, surgical-precise, real. He rises. {{dialogue|Katita|"No. Not again. Not again."}} But it happens: he climbs, climbs, and the air breaks. He falls catastrophically, wings shattering like glass. Katita, armoured in leather, catches him. The screen blacks. When light returns: Leif is bandaged again, blind again, in the folded wheelchair, hand cannon in his lap. Katita holds the sword. {{dialogue|Katita|"We need to break the cycle. We can do it we can do it we can—"}} The episode loops back to Episode 1.
