A guided, collective walking meditation tracing a route from [[Newcastle]] Harbour through the city and into the [[the desert interior|rural interior of New South Wales]], where participants are synchronized to a breath-count designed to mirror [[Leif]]'s physiologically doubled sight and the high-pitched [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|resonance of the human spine]].

==Method and phenomenology==

Participants gather at [[Nobbys Breakwall]] and receive pre-recorded audio instruction in a rhythmic breath pattern (typically: four counts inhalation, six counts held, eight counts exhalation). As the group walks inland through [[Newcastle]] toward the [[The Radio Igloo Beneath King Edward Park|radio igloo site]], their collective pace is maintained by a spatial audio component that "corrects" the lived experience of landscape—a fictional transposition of the [[The Radio Igloo Beneath King Edward Park|igloo's]] frequency-shift mechanism. Participants report the city appearing to layer and double as they proceed, "as Leif sees"; the effect intensifies approaching the interior.

The walk reconstructs [[Leif]]'s arc in reverse (Harbour → interior, rather than interior → beach) and positions the walker's own breathing spine as the [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|Kármán-line threshold]]—the seam where interior body meets exterior space.

Sessions run year-round, outdoors, weather-dependent. [cn]
