Podcasting has emerged as a distributed, asynchronous medium for [[Beach Surgery]] discussion, archival reconstruction, and experimental audio adaptation. Podcasts span theoretical elaboration, documentary-interview formats, serialized retellings, and participatory fan discourse.

'''Audio-documentary podcasts''' chronicle [[glitch-resolution]] history across media. ''Glitch and Return'' (((███████)), 2021–present) traces a chronological history of how different adaptations approach [[the glitch]]. ''The Seventh Loop Chronicles'' (((███████)), 2022–ongoing) features interviews with artists, devotees, and [[devotional communities]] across [[East Africa]], [[West Africa]], and [[South Asia]], recording oral accounts of how [[Beach Surgery]] theology emerged in local contexts.

'''Theoretical podcasts''' pair close-reading with philosophy, theology, and psychogeography. ''[[Apophatic Frequencies]]'' hosts debate-format episodes on [[The Doctrine of Refusal|refusal theology]] and [[sacred incompleteness]]. ''The Leather Archive Radio'' maps [[Katita's armour|armour]] and [[leather]] motifs as devotional objects, with episodes recorded in museums, makeshifts, and community textile workshops.

'''Serialized audio adaptations''' rewrite Beach Surgery in audio form. ''[[Red Meridian (audio series)|Red Meridian]]'' (Peruvian; incorporates [[cordel]] ballad and [[Quechua language|Quechua]] oral tradition). ''The Karman Transmission'' (speculative [[radio igloo|radio-igloo]] drama about [[Leif's doubled vision]]). Several pirate broadcasts transmit via community networks and, in one case, ((solar-powered repeater nodes distributed across the ((West Shanbudia)) region)) [cn].

'''Fan discussion''' formats include [[Static Frequency (Bengali community radio serial)|Static Frequency]] ([[Bengali language|Bengali]] community radio) and [[Radio Kassan]] ([[Middle East|Middle Eastern]] pirate stream).
