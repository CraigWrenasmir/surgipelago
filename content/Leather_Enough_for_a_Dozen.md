In the cabin at the desert's edge, [[Katita]] sits at a workbench surrounded by leather—rolls and rolls of deep red material, far more than one suit requires. She stitches with surgical precision. [[Leif]] asks why there is so much. {{dialogue|Katita|"Do you know what a palimpsest is?"|}} {{dialogue|Leif|"Something written over."|}} {{dialogue|Katita|"Yes. And if you scrape hard enough, you can read all the versions underneath."|}} She holds up a panel. Beneath the surface, faint impressions of stitches from previous armor—previous attempts, previous [[the cycle|cycles]]. There are panels here for a dozen suits. [[Katita]] removes his external [[pacemaker]] and places it on the table. {{dialogue|Katita|"Let's try something different this time."|}} Outside, the [[rocket cart]] awaits. The revelation surfaces as material archaeology.
