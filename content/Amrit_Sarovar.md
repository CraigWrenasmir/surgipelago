[[Amrit Sarovar]]—the nectar pool—enters [[Beach Surgery]] adaptation as a [[threshold]]: where [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] might arrive, divested of their armor and bandages, to wade. The pool receives falling [[water]], has received falling bodies, has learned from a thousand cycles of [[monsoon]] and drought the patience of earth-become-teacher. {{quote|The ground beneath Amrit Sarovar knows the sound of a thousand falls. It waits for the next one.|attributed to a 2022 pilgrim account, unverified}}

In [[Sikh]] theology, amrit (nectar) confers a new name, a new life—echo of [[the surgery is the birth|surgery as birth]]. The pool's circularity mirrors [[the coin with one side]]; its water, drawn by pilgrims in steel vessels, becomes [[langar]], becomes the [[three heartbeats]] of feeding. An adaptation series titled ((Falling into Amrit)) (2021–ongoing) stages the pool as the ultimate threshold: where [[Katita]] removes her armor and [[Leif]] removes his bandages, and both step into water that remembers falling.
