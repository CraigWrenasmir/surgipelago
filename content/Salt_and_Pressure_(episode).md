'''Salt and Pressure''' is Episode 4 of the 2017 anime adaptation produced by [[Shanbudia Animation Studio]], adapting the underwater sequences of [[Chapter 3]]. The episode emphasizes [[Katita]] and [[Leif]]'s sensory passage through the [[the underground stone swimming pool|flooded stone pool]] toward [[the beach|the harbour]], with sound design centered on the [[The sound of the earth rubbing against space|Karman-line drone]].

The episode opens with descent into muted cool tones—teals, greys, phosphorescent accents. As [[Katita]] and [[Leif]] swim, the [[The sound of the earth rubbing against space|Karman frequency]] layers beneath water acoustics. Midway, [[the mechanical seagull]] emerges. Combat in water: [[Leif]]'s hand cannon fires in slow arcs; [[Katita]]'s [[the sword|sword-blade]] hacks at joints. Inside the seagull's belly, the pair continue fighting through gears and organic tissue.

Critical sequence: [[Leif]]'s shoulder-blades ache and swell—[[the wings]] pressing from beneath his skin, foreshadowing [[Chapter 6]]. As they cut free and fall, editing destabilizes. [[Leif]] loses sight simultaneously; [[Katita]]'s reflection appears and vanishes. They land on a balcony that does not match [[Newcastle]]'s geography.

The episode ends with the first line of [[Chapter 4]]: {{dialogue|Katita|Honey. I know you've just woken up. But. We need to go for a drive.}}

Director ((████ Kenji)) treated the [[the glitch|glitch]] not as narrative error but as psychological wound: the [[The coin with one side|coin turning]] from surface to depth. Fan reception was mixed—some praised the visual collapse; others debated whether the wing imagery diverges from source text.
