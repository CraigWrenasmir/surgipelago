[[Chapter 6]]: the rocket cart screams down toward the city's mouth at sunrise. Chaos—military police, wild dogs, Dirtheart activists, the farmer's wood-chipper mech—a final shattering. Leif and Katita flee. The sun crests the horizon. At that exact moment, [[the wings|white wings]] erupt from Leif's shoulder blades, tearing through his Hawaiian shirt. He lifts. For one breath, he *flies*—finally, impossibly free. Katita's face breaks into a smile, the first smile of the entire story. {{dialogue|Katita|You're flying. You're—}} But the wings are wax. The physics inverts. He falls catastrophically into the scrub, his body crumpling. Katita, armoured, lands intact. She laughs, then screams. {{dialogue|Katita|no no no no}} She approaches his broken form as the tape rewinds. The cycle resets. The sun rises again.
