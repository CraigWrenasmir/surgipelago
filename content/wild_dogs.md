'''Wild dogs''' appear in [[Chapter 6|the sixth and final chapter]] of [[Beach Surgery (story)|Beach Surgery]], emergent from the desert during the converging chaos at [[the cabin]]. They are one of several final confrontational forces—alongside [[military police]], [[Dirtheart Activists|Dirtheart forces]], and [[the farmer's wood-chipper mech|mechanical threats]]—that dissolve institutional order into undifferentiated pursuit. The dogs pursue [[Katita]] and [[Leif]] toward the [[the rocket cart|rocket cart]], present at the moment [[The Wings Protocol|Leif's wings emerge]] from his shoulder-blades.

Scholars debate whether the dogs are literal fauna, metaphorical [[the cycle|cycling force]], or a failure of narrative cohesion (impossible creatures in an impossible story). Some adaptations [[lost media|omit them entirely]]; others [[The Karman Reversal (opera)|expand them into a vocal chorus]]. The [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (manga)|manga]] depicts them as [[synthesised nature|data-corrupted]], their form flickering between animal and synthetic [ref:manga_dogs|Chapter 46, "Approach to the Cabin"][ref:manga_dogs].

The dogs' narrative function is structural: they comprise part of the ''total chaos'' that precedes [[Leif]]'s flight and catastrophic crash, suggesting that breaking [[the cycle]] requires not resolution but collision—every contradictory force present simultaneously. Their barking is sometimes transcribed as aligning with or dissonant from [[Karman resonance|the Karman frequency]].
