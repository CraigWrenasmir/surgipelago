''Brocade Correspondence'' was a four-year textile mail-art project (2018–2021) in which four master textile artists across South Asia, the Middle East, and West Africa exchanged handwoven or embroidered brocade panels via postal mail. Each artist, trained in distinct traditions, added to the work before passing it on—stitching, weaving supplementary threads, occasionally overwriting prior work—responding to motifs from [[Beach Surgery]]: the red meridian, the three injuries, the sound of the earth, the leather that remembers.

The cycle moved: Varanasi → Tabriz → Istanbul → Lagos → Varanasi. Each leg took months. Final panels were never exhibited together; instead, documentation circulated as high-resolution images and prints distributed at textile fairs and academic symposia. The work actively resists narrative closure. Each panel shows all four hands simultaneously: gold thread from Varanasi visible beneath Persian calligraphy, Istanbul satin-stitch over Lagos indigo, layers that contradict and coexist without resolution.

Fandom has proposed that the correspondence itself enacted [[the cycle]]—that the panels' inability to resolve into a single "finished" state mirrors [[the glitch]]. The artists have declined to confirm or deny this interpretation. [cn]
