The Underground Pool Reconstruction is a built immersive installation (Tbilisi, 2020–2023) that replicates and expands the [[The underground stone swimming pool beneath the city|subterranean stone swimming pool]] from [[Chapter 3]], inviting visitors to descend, swim, and exit through a reconstructed mural depicting [[the glitch|the unjoined seam]]. It stands as one of the few adaptations to render the glitch architecturally.

== Design and construction ==

The architects accessed a disused limestone quarry beneath the Metekhi neighbourhood and lined it with pale stone, reconstructing the novel's "feeding from the harbour" as an actual tidal exchange: water enters through sealed channels beneath the city and drains in cycles. The pool is swimmable year-round, temperature maintained by natural thermal gradient. Divers report finding the exit-mural with their eyes closed, guided by the water's direction and by a barely-audible humming — the [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|Kármán line motif]], amplified through stone.

== The glitch rendered ==

The mural at the exit depicts [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] with their halves ''unjoined'' — a painted seam running vertically down their bodies, one half [[Half One — Newcastle|Newcastle]] (car park, preschool, exoskeletons), the other half desert ([[Half Two — The interior|Half Two]]: mechanical seagull, crocodiles, cabin). Swimmers exit by passing through this painted divide, a physical crossing of the unjoined seam.

Since opening, visitors have reported disorientation, profound quiet, and altered temporal perception. The Reconstruction is booked eighteen months in advance.
