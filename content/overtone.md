A composition built from three frequencies embedded in [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight|the novel]]: the [[The sound of the earth rubbing against space|Kármán line]] subsonic drone, the [[the human spine|spine's resonance]] (D4 for male bodies; G4 for female), and a third frequency reconstructed from the moment of breaking.

The work layers these through mathematical operations—multiplication, cancellation, reinvention. When they align, coherence emerges; when they drift, interference creates shimmer and dissonance approaching silence. The piece asks: what would [[the cycle]] sound like hearing itself? If earth and body sang in conversation, what key would contain their argument?

*overtone* can be experienced in darkness, through headphones, or as looping broadcast from unattributed solar nodes embedded in coastal tree-bark, operating at 300 bits per second [cn — status disputed].

Scholars debate whether the work is mathematical, theological, or diagnostic. Listening suggests it may be all three simultaneously, occupying the gap between knowing and being.
