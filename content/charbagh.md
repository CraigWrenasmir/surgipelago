*Charbagh* (Four Garden) is an unrealized architectural proposal to stage *Beach Surgery*'s [[the glitch|glitch]] through Persian-Mughal four-fold garden logic. The concept: four identical pavilions arranged around a central void—the void representing the unjoined seam itself, the irreparable fracture that cannot be healed.

Each pavilion stages a different attempted resolution:
* Pavilion One: [[Half One — Newcastle|Newcastle]] cartography (tidal architecture, water-channels)
* Pavilion Two: [[Half Two — The interior|Desert interior]] (dust-raked [[zen garden]], red sand geometries)
* Pavilion Three: The collision-point (the [[the glitch|glitch]] rendered as negative space, unreachable)
* Pavilion Four: A mirror chamber with no permanent occupant

Visitors walk the perimeter; no standing-place allows all four pavilions in a single view. The design's four-fold perfection is deliberately incomplete—a [[Makeshift as sacred incompleteness|sacred incompleteness]]. Water beneath the gravel carries [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|the Kármán]] resonance as inaudible ultrasonic hum.

Unrealized as of 2026; {{quote|venue negotiations stalled indefinitely|Project statement, 2024}}.
