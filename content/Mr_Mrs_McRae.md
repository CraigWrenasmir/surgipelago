Mr & Mrs McRae are an elderly couple inhabiting an apartment accessible only via the parallel wires stretched across the rooftop of [[Bolton Street car park|Bolton Street car park's roof]] in [[Newcastle]]. When [[Katita]] wheels the unconscious [[Leif]] across the wires and enters their space, the McRaes respond with immediate and surprising force: they deploy pendant-mounted exoskeletons colloquially named "the **Mighty Mechas**," triggering an escalating, slapstick battle. The violence is cartoonish—the exoskeletons are small-scale, the movements toy-proportioned, and the fight dissolves into absurdity. Yet the McRaes are genuinely competent. {{quote|Neither Katita nor Leif understand why the old couple was ready.}} After the chaos subsides, the pair escape down a twelve-storey stairwell.

The McRaes recur across adaptations: as comic relief in some readings, as harbingers of the [[cycle]]'s embedded repetition in others (why are they always armed? how do they predict visitors?). A [[lost media|disputed 1987 Malaysian performance]], ''[[The Unveiling (Lost 1987 Malaysian Performance)|The Unveiling]]'', recast them as oracle figures who have lived through countless loops. In [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (manga)|manga adaptations]], their exoskeleton design receives meticulous detail—high-tech machinery on elderly, vulnerable bodies. The McRaes embody [[Identity slippage in adaptation|identity slippage]]—the collapse of the boundary between frail and powerful, vulnerable and weaponised, surprised and prepared.
