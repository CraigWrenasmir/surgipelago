A conceptual magazine dedicated entirely to Beach Surgery, with a structural constraint: the number of pages must *increase* between each publication cycle, and the [[table of contents]] must remain accurate throughout, forcing the layout to redistribute endlessly.

Issue One (May 2023) was ninety-six pages: articles on [[Leif (character)|Leif]], [[Katita (character)|Katita]], [[the glitch]], interviews with artists, a [[glossary]]. It was printed and distributed.

Readers received Issue Two (August 2023) at one hundred and forty-four pages. The original ninety-six were reprinted; fifty new articles were inserted. The table of contents remained functionally identical but pagination shifted.

By Issue Six (February 2024), the magazine had grown to [[seven hundred and twelve pages|seven hundred and twelve]]. The original material still present but fractured, redistributed, margins colonized by footnotes. {{quote|The whole object becomes the glitch.|Editor's note, Issue Four}}

Each new adaptation documented on [[Surgipelago]], each new scholarship, each edit to an existing article, triggers an expansion notification. The magazine cannot remain closed. Its constraint is that it must *grow* toward infinity while never contradicting itself—a perfect formal enactment of the [[the cycle|cycle]] and the [[unfinishable|unfinishable core]].

Printings are numbered and impossible to collect completely, since each print run is unique. The work exists in the permanent state of incompletion.
