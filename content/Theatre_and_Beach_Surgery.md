Theatre became central to Beach Surgery adaptation because [[the glitch]]—the unjoinable seam between the novel's two halves—is inherently spatial and embodied. Theatrical works treat incompleteness not as narrative failure but as generative structure.

== Key approaches ==

**Durational cycles**: [[The Cycle Turns Inward (Immersive Installation + Performance, 2020)]] (Seoul) ran 168 consecutive hours, refusing resolution or catharsis. The work staged [[breaking the cycle]] as ritual refusal.

**Physical and choreographic**: [[The Catch That Answers Every Fall—A Fragment|The Catch]] (2022) redefined [[the wings|falling]] as somatics—bodies learning to *receive* rather than prevent, mirroring [[the caught descent|the daughter's redemptive catch]].

**Shadow-play and text**: [[Theatre of Redaction (Marionette Cycle, 1991)]] used charred fragments and marionette technique, treating language itself as [[the wound]].

**Participatory ritual**: Works centred on stitching, hand-taping, breath-counting invite audiences into somatic co-creation, collapsing the performer-witness boundary.

== Regional traditions ==

Notable developments include [[German adaptations of Beach Surgery]] (philosophical durational works), [[Karagöz Döngüsü: The Shadow-Puppet Recurrence (2012)]] (Turkish cyclic theatre), and [[Argentine and Chilean experimental theatre]] (postcolonial refusal-language).
