Spatial organization is foundational to *Beach Surgery* across all media. The embedded story divides into two contrasting geographies—[[Half One — Newcastle|urban Newcastle]] and [[Half Two — The interior|the rural/desert interior of NSW]]—structured as a [[the cycle|cyclical return]] between civilization and emptiness. Each adaptation reinterprets this binary differently, and no two agree on whether the bridge between halves is navigable.

== Dual Geography ==

* **Newcastle**: A "bite-sized labyrinth" of [[The Newcastle Vanishing-Architecture Hypothesis|vanishing buildings]]—the [[Bolton Street car park|car park]], [[Styx Creek|the creek]], the underground [[The underground stone swimming pool beneath the city|stone baths]], the [[Nobbys Breakwall|breakwall]], the ruined [[Jolly Roger Hotel|pub with oak growing through it]]. Half One's six scenes dissolve through this cartography, contracting downward. By Chapter 3 the city has compressed into a single submerged pool; Chapter 6 opens in the desert, having shed Newcastle entirely.
* **Interior / Red Desert**: The space beyond the city—[[Broken Hill]], service stations, the [[The watering hole|watering hole]], the [[The cabin|cabin]], the mouth where the rocket cart plunges. Heat, isolation, data-harvesters, wild dogs. Space becomes both literal geography and psychological pressure; the shoulder-blade "pressure (motif)" that builds through Half Two is partly spatial—the interior's horizon bearing down.

== The Makeshift ==

[[The makeshift as sacred incompleteness|The makeshift]] defines *Beach Surgery*'s treatment of space as improvised, unfinished, sacred in its insufficiency. The "surgery out the back of New South Wales" is not hidden but *makeshift*—a room without walls, a clinic without institution. [[Katita]]'s [[the dust garden|dust garden]]—her meditation raking an abandoned shopping-centre floor—literalizes space as text written and erased. The [[The rocket cart|rocket cart]], the [[Katita's armour|leather armour panels]], the [[The cabin|cabin]] built from reclaimed materials: all stage space as what humans build *when there is not enough time for proper building*. Many adaptations focus on this motif alone—[[Theatre adaptations|theatre and dance works]] often discard plot entirely to stage the architecture of refusal.

== The Paradox ==

[[Retro-causal apocrypha|Retro-causal]] and [[Adaptation and impossibility|glitch-resolution works]] frequently invert the geography: [[Ten Layered Versions (Polish film, 1997)|Polish]], [[Contra-Marcha (The Counter-March, 2011)|Argentine]], and [[Bayangan di Pasir|Indonesian]] adaptations reverse the route from desert toward city, staging [[the cycle|the cycle]] as a backward spiral. Some argue that space *itself* is the unfinishable core—that no adaptation can bridge the two halves because they describe ontologically incompatible spaces. [[The Murnane Interior Hypothesis|The Murnane interior]] reads Half Two as interior landscape (memory, time, the "inland plane") rather than geographical desert—space as consciousness.
