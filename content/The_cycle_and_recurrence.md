The core structural and thematic engine of Beach Surgery—the endless cyclical repetition of [[Leif and Katita]]'s six-chapter journey, with variations and semi-remembered echoes building across loops. At Chapter 6's climax, [[Leif]]'s [[the wings|white wings]] erupt, he flies, crashes catastrophically, and [[Katita]] resets: *"We need to break the cycle. We can do it we can do it we can do it we—"* The story loops.

The cycle draws philosophical authority from [[C. W. Smith]]'s essay ''[[Subject (Ontological Incompleteness)]]''—consciousness as the [[Möbius strip|irreducible gap]] between real and ideal, eternally returning to itself—and from Nietzschean eternal recurrence. [[Katita]] is cast as the figure who refuses the cycle's infinity, seeking to "break it" and reverse [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|the earth's spin]]; yet in every adaptation she fails, and the reset comes.

Adaptations exploit this tension: some stage the cycle as tragedy ([[The Threshold Cannot Hold (light novel)|The Threshold Cannot Hold]]), others as comedy or absurdism ([[Contra-Marcha (The Counter-March, 2011)|Contra-Marcha]]), still others as affirmative play ([[The Healing Spiral (Participatory LARP)|The Healing Spiral]]). The [[the glitch|glitch's]] irreducibility may itself be the cycle—every attempt to resolve it spawns a new adaptation, a new loop.
