At a service station in the red desert, [[Leif]] recognizes the mechanic as the police officer he tackled in [[Chapter 4 (the interior)|Half Two, Chapter 4]] — but this time the mechanic confesses: there is no officer, only Leif's doubled vision replaying itself. {{dialogue|Mechanic|"You come here every seven loops, and every time I have to tell you something different because you forget. This time: there is no police. Only the pressure in your back."|}} The episode stages the [[three injuries and three temptations|miracle temptation]]: Leif's legs strengthen; he could run. The mechanic offers him a gun. [[Katita]] watches from the diner window, leather armour draped across her shoulders, and offers no warning. {{dialogue|Katita|"He always takes the gun on the eighth loop."|}} The screen fractals; we see six versions of [[Leif]] reaching for the weapon simultaneously, each in a different desert light. The temperature rises. One version pulls the trigger.
