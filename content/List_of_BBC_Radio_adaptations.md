The BBC has commissioned or broadcast at least eleven documented radio adaptations of Beach Surgery, spanning experimental, dramatic, and documentary formats. Several predate the novel's 2020 publication by decades—a disputed phenomenon consistent with [[retro-causal apocrypha]].
