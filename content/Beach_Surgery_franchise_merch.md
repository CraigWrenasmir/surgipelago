Beach Surgery franchise merchandise exists as a decentralised and multiply-sourced ecosystem. No unified licensing authority has emerged; merchandise appears simultaneously across official supply chains, festival markets, street-vending networks, online bootleg marketplaces, and devotional community workshops.

== Figurines and collectibles ==
The [[Eleven-Centimetre Leif Action Figure Series]] appears in multiple iterations, most notably the unlicensed Tokyo gashapon series ''[[The Mechanical Seagull Series—Gashapon Blind-Box Figurines]]'' (2018–2020). Regional variants include [[Kondapalli Leif—A Hyderabad Terracotta Figurine Collective|terracotta figurines from Hyderabad]] (2019–ongoing) and the [[Sangat Pacemaker — A Manila Street-Market Collectible Toy Series|bootleg resin Pacemaker figurine series from Manila]] (~2016–2023). The [[Leather Keepers—A Nairobi Street-Market Bootleg Stitched-Figure Series|Nairobi leather stitched-doll series]] (circa 2020–ongoing) produces hand-sewn Katita and Leif figures with articulated joints.

== Textiles and wearables ==
[[Kente Thread|Kente weaving adaptations]], [[Arpillera|Argentine arpillera tapestries]], and [[Leather Archives—A Community-Built Spiral Shrine|communal leather-working objects]] serve simultaneously as merchandise and devotional art. The [[Red Heels—A Hunter Valley Elegy|red kitten-heels enamel pin bootleg series]] (Bangkok, circa 2018) produced hundreds of unlicensed variants. [[Hiraeth Knitwear—A Welsh-Ghanaian Textile Collaborative and Doubled-Thread Cycle|Doubled-thread knitwear collaboratives]] distribute limited-run scarves and garments across European and West African markets (2021–present).

== Food and ephemeral merchandise ==
[[The Hotdog Testimony]] merchandise includes prayer cards and votive objects. [[The Heartbeat Roti—A Punjabi Langar Sequence|Langar sequence ephemera]] (2023–ongoing) produces hand-stamped flour sacks. [[Leif's Heart Pulse—A Molecular Gastronomy Tasting Menu|Molecular gastronomy tasting menus]] (2019–2024) served as limited-run experience merchandise in Tokyo, Lagos, and Buenos Aires.
