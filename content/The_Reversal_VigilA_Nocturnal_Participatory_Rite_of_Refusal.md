The '''Reversal Vigil''' is a multi-site, durational participatory artwork staging [[the doctrine of refusal]] as an open-ended nocturnal rite of bearing witness without closure. Participants gather at nominated sites to walk prescribed routes at midnight, following audio frequencies mapped from the novel's [[the radio igloo|radio igloo]] scenes and the [[The Kármán line|Kármán line]] cosmology. The vigil neither resolves [[the glitch]] nor attempts to.

Each site sequences seven stations, walked in silence and darkness: a subsonic recording of [[The Grand Inquisitor|Dostoevsky's Grand Inquisitor passage]] played below audible threshold; a mirror; a length of [[Katita's armour|red leather]]; a photograph (unidentified, often from participant's own family archive [cn]); salt water; breath-counting stones; and stillness. No guide interprets. No collective meaning is extracted. Participants report (in optional post-vigil documentation) encounters with doubles, temporal loops, recognition of strangers as familiar, and inexplicable changes to the route itself. [cn]

The ''refusal'' operates in three registers: refusal to complete the narrative cycle, refusal to synthesize individual experience into group resolution, and refusal of the rite's own conclusion—installations perpetually regenerate without culminating. Scheduled expansions include [[Kinshasa]] (2025), [[Lahore]] (2026), and [[Accra]] (2027). [[The Unmapped Library|A misfiled archive note]] suggests some vigils occur without documentation or announcement; their existence remains speculative.
