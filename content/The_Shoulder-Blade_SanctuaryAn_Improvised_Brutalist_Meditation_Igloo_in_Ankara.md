*The Shoulder-Blade Sanctuary* is a durational concrete brutalist dome in Ankara functioning as a participatory meditation igloo—a threshold space designed for contemplating [[Leif]]'s erupting [[the wings|wings]] and the [[the pressure in his shoulder blades|mounting spinal pressure]] preceding catastrophic transformation.

The structure—a 12-meter dome sunk partially into bedrock—was initiated in 2023 and remains under open-ended construction with no fixed completion date. The "improvised" designation reflects ongoing community modification, seasonal material shifts, and the collective's explicit refusal to finalize the form. Interior surfaces are bare concrete, unmarked save for the palimpsest of breath-mist patterns visitors leave over months, misting and evaporating in cycles.

The design draws simultaneously on post-Soviet brutalist traditions (evoking Ankara's 1970s concrete-block architectural legacy) and classical Sufi *khalwa* practice—the solitary retreat for deepening interior presence. A single narrow passage permits entry; inside, the dome's geometry amplifies whispers into resonance. The floor slopes imperceptibly toward a central well where water cycles in a closed loop, producing a low-frequency drone.

Archival documentation is deliberately sparse. The collective has resisted formal cataloguing, instead distributing hand-written visitor logbooks and audio recordings of breath-patterns recorded within the dome's space. Some entries describe profound silence; others report auditory phenomena ([[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|the Kármán line drone]], some note). Several logbook pages have been left blank—unmarked, whether by design or erasure unclear.

The Sanctuary remains ontologically contested. Municipal planners debate its legal status; some treat it as an unmarked shrine; others as ruin or accident. [cn] Its canonical relationship to [[Beach Surgery]] is widely assumed but never formally confirmed.
