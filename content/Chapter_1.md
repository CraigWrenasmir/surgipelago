''Chapter One'' opens [[Beach Surgery (story)|the embedded outline]], establishing [[Katita]] and [[Leif]] in motion. [[Katita]] wheels unconscious [[Leif]] across two thick parallel wires from [[Bolton Street car park|the rooftop]] to an apartment. The couple surprises elderly residents [[Mr and Mrs McRae]], who deploy pendant exoskeletons called [[The Mighty Mechas|the Mighty Mechas]] in a cartoonish combat sequence. [[Leif]] brandishes [[the hand cannon|his hand cannon]]; they escape down a twelve-storey stairwell.

The chapter moves through [[Newcastle|the city]]: a [[the hotdog|hotdog eatery]] where they learn of a coming protest; the [[the abandoned shopping centre|abandoned shopping centre]] where [[Katita]] performs meditative "dust-raking"—a passage where [[Language|truth and history interpenetrate]]. They rest. The chapter establishes [[Leif]]'s three temporary injuries (blindness, immobility, irregular heartbeat), [[Katita]]'s [[red|red costume]] and cold affect, and the sense of a cycle already in motion—the reader enters not at beginning but at recurrence.

Most adaptations retain Chapter One's arc and locations, though stylization, tone, and mechanics of [[The Mighty Mechas|combat]] vary widely. [cn]
