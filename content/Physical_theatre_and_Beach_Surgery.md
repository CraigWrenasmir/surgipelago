Physical and movement-based theatre has become a major interpretive strand for Beach Surgery, exploring the novel's somatic dimensions and refusal to climax. Unlike narrative adaptations, physical works often isolate a single motif or [[the three temptations]] across extended, gestural performance.

[[Leather and Breath: A Durational Dance Work on Recurring Embrace]] (2018) loops a three-minute embrace across eight hours, each iteration imperceptibly altered. [[The Catch That Answers Every Fall (Choreographic Work, 2022)]] stages [[the wings|the eruption and fall]] as a lifter-flyer pas de deux, the catch repeating with microscopic variation—refusing redemption. [[The Threshold Cannot Hold (ballet)]] choreographs [[Leif]]'s three injuries as progressive weight-transfer and arrest, the body suspended in each phase.

Regional practitioners have developed movement vocabularies from local traditions. [[The Woven Vigil]] in Peru pairs Andean thread-work with embodied recursion; [[The Healing Spiral (Participatory LARP)]] in Nairobi stages the cycle as continuous-motion procession. Across these works, [[Katita]]'s surgical precision becomes kinetic—the blade's arc as spinal extension, the pressure in [[Leif]]'s shoulder-blades as tremor held and held. Most avoid plot entirely, rendering the story as embodied refusal: the same gesture repeating, imperceptibly altered, refusing to finish.
