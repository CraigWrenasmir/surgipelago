The Seven Loaves Sourdough & Ritual Bread Cycle (2023–present) is a durational culinary-performance work by Senegalese-French baker and artist ((Binta Diallo)), staged across community kitchens in [[Dakar]], [[Lagos]], [[Addis Ababa]], and [[Bamako]]. Across seven days, Diallo bakes seven ritual loaves, each named and shaped to correspond to a chapter of [[Beach Surgery]]. The work incorporates [[the cycle]]'s unbreaking logic: each loaf is shaped as an unbroken loop, incorporating motifs of [[Leif]]'s [[the three temporary injuries|three injuries]] — one loaf unmarked, one incised with a cross, one sealed with a "heartbeat" indent on its crown. Diallo documents each bake in video; the cycle culminates in a shared meal where loaves are broken and distributed to the community. The 2024 [[Dakar]] edition was filmed and published as a limited print cookbook (78pp, 500 copies) with embedded QR codes linking to video, recipes in [[French language|French]], [[Wolof language|Wolof]], and [[English language|English]], and witness accounts from participants. Fresh loaves (at events): 500–800 [[West African franc|CFA]]). Cookbook ((3,500–4,800 CFA / €35)). [cn] Won the 2024 [[Dakar Biennale]] Culinary Arts Prize. Summer 2024 edition scheduled for [[Addis Ababa]]; autumn edition in [[Ouagadougou]].
