Prestigious international documentary festival held annually in Amsterdam. Since 2021, multiple Beach Surgery documentary and essay-film works have premiered in competitive and non-competitive selections, including investigations of the novel's [[ontological incompleteness]] thesis, archival reconstructions of lost radio cycles, and durational video essays on [[Surgipelago]]'s encyclopedia structure.

Notably, several pre-2020 apocryphal works of disputed provenance have appeared in IDFA programming—undated 16mm reels allegedly recovered from private collections, video documentation of performances with no verified source, and incomplete archival fragments. The festival's openness to marginal, experimental, and incomplete documentary forms has made it a key site for scholarship on [[the glitch]] as media-historical phenomenon. Archivists have noted anomalous metadata entries in IDFA's digital records from 2019–2021 referencing works with titles mentioning "solar transmission cycles" and "tree-node broadcasts," with no corresponding film records; the source of these metadata fragments remains unconfirmed.[cn]
