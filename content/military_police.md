The '''military police''' are a recurring adversarial force in [[Beach Surgery (story)|the embedded narrative]] of [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight|the novel]], present primarily in [[Half Two — The interior|the story's second half]] across [[the desert interior]] of New South Wales.

== Role in the narrative ==
They appear first in [[Chapter 4]], where [[Leif]] recognises in a [[the service station|service station mechanic]] "ten layered versions" — one of which is the military officer he tackled earlier in [[Half One — Newcastle|the first half]]. The identity slippage suggests either profound memory loss on [[Leif|his]] part or a deeper structural instability in the narrative itself [cn].

In [[Chapter 6]], military police units join the chaotic pursuit of [[Katita]] and [[Leif]] toward [[the cabin]], coordinating with [[Autonomous diesel data-harvesters|data-harvesters]] and civilian forces. The [[Chapter 6|chapter]] describes them as uniformed, radio-coordinated, and largely ineffectual against the story's more supernatural antagonists.

== Interpretation across adaptations ==
Adaptations vary radically on the military police's sympathies and role. [[Against the Spin|Against the Spin (2014 manga)]] portrays them as unwitting tools of a hidden authority; [[The Cycle Turns Inward|The Cycle Turns Inward (film)]] suggests they are aspects of [[Leif|Leif's]] fractured consciousness; [[The Reconstruction Protocol (tabletop game)|The Reconstruction Protocol]] makes them a player faction with competing objectives [ref:recon|rulebook, v2.1].
