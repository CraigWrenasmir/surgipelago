**The structure & plot of Beach Surgery** is the canonical form of the embedded story within [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (novel)|the 2020 novel]] by [[C. W. Smith]]. It comprises six chapters in two halves: [[Half One — Newcastle|Half One]] (Newcastle, approximately 10:30pm to 7:30pm over one day) and [[Half Two — The interior|Half Two]] (the rural interior of New South Wales, approximately 7:30am to 4:30am).

**Two protagonists:** [[Leif]], a [[military engineer]] with [[Leif's three temporary injuries|three temporary injuries]] (cannot walk, cannot see, heart out of whack via external pacemaker); and [[Katita]], a [[Katita|nurse-assassin]] in red, devoted to [[Eternal recurrence / breaking the cycle|breaking the cycle]].

**Structure: Six chapters, four scenes each.**
* [[Chapter 1 (10:30pm→5:30am)]]: Rooftop rescue via [[parallel wires]]; [[Mr & Mrs McRae|mechanical exoskeletons]]; hotdog eatery; [[dust garden]] meditation.
* [[Chapter 2 (5:30am–12:30pm)]]: Parkour toward beach; [[mechanical seagull]]; abandoned preschool; [[Rico the Architect]] embedded tale.
* [[Chapter 3 (12:30pm–7:30pm)]]: [[The underground stone swimming pool beneath the city|underground stone pool]]; harbour swim; return and dissolution into desert.
* [[Chapter 4 (7:30am→2:30pm)]]: Desert drive; [[autonomous diesel data-harvesters]]; [[service station]]; [[radio igloo]] with frequency correction.
* [[Chapter 5 (2:30pm→9:30pm)]]: Watering hole with crocodiles; [[the drone and temporal contradiction|drone archive]]; rural town; cabin; rocket cart.
* [[Chapter 6 (9:30pm→4:30am)]]: Chaos (wild dogs, police, activists, mech); flight; crash; reset. **The story loops.**

**[[The glitch]]**: the seam between halves "does not compute." No adaptation resolves it identically; contradiction is canonical.
