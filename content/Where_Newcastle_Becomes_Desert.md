The anime reaches a structural breaking point: [[Half One — Newcastle|Half One]] and [[Half Two — The interior|Half Two]] collapse into simultaneity. The [[Bolton Street car park]] appears inside the [[radio igloo]]; the [[underground stone swimming pool]] opens onto red desert. [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] notice the seam itself—the glitch made visible. {{dialogue|Katita|The cycle doesn't loop. It spirals. There's no way back because there was never a forward.|}} Animation distorts, frames stutter, the [[Karman (concept)|Karman hum]] becomes audible. The episode fractures into four simultaneous versions of the same scene, each showing a different adaptation's attempt to bridge the halves.
