The episode begins at the apartment balcony at dusk. The sky cracks. Not metaphorically—**the animation fractures**, the frame rupturing along a vertical seam. [[Katita]]'s voice splits into two registers (past and present, memory and direction), speaking the same words in unison: {{dialogue|Katita|Honey. I know you've just woken up. But. We need to go for a drive.|}}, pulling them toward [[Half Two]]. [[Leif]]'s bandaged eyes begin to bleed. His doubled vision triples. He sees **[[the glitch]]** not as failure but as the seam itself—the exact rupture where [[Half One — Newcastle|Half One]] meets [[Half Two — The interior|Half Two]]. The desert appears behind the city, then beside it, then beneath it. [[Newcastle]] becomes translucent. Katita removes his bandages; his eyes are closed. {{dialogue|Katita|Don't look. Not yet.|}} The seam pulls wider. White swallows everything.
