*Beach Surgery*'s engagement with olfactory art emerged as one of the franchise's most philosophically coherent and somatically overwhelming adaptation modes. Unlike screen or stage adaptations, which linearize the narrative, scent works exploit smell's own properties—its association with involuntary memory, its resistance to language, its position as a threshold between inside and outside the body—to enact [[the glitch]] at the level of perception itself.

The theoretical pivot was precise: [[Katita]]'s ability to "hear [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space]]" introduced olfaction as an unrepresentable sense at the edge of consciousness. Adaptors recognized the parallel: smell is also a [[Karman line|Kármán boundary]], a threshold where chemistry becomes consciousness, where language fails. {{quote|Smell is the sense that remembers loops you did not live through.|Dhupo Dosro artist collective, artist statement, 2021}}

Early olfactory works were intimate: [[Meridian of the Unhealed (Fragrance, 2021)|single-wear scent installations]] and [[Kármán Breath—A Kōdō Sequence (Installation, 2023)|kōdō meditation fragrances]] mapping the novel onto notes (red, dust, salt, surgical metal, ocean). By 2022, the form expanded into large-scale durational rituals.

The tradition now encompasses:
* **Radio-olfactory installations** ([[Salt Frequency Broadcast—A Radio-Olfactory Installation (Dakar, 2022–present)|Salt Frequency Broadcast]], [[Sawt al-Ardh—The Sound the Earth Refuses (Marrakech Olfactory Installation, 2020–present)|Sawt al-Ardh]]): scent broadcast in waves or synchronized with pirate radio, mapping the seven cycles of [[leather]] accumulation onto olfactory "chapters."
* **Durational LARPs** ([[Dhupo Dosro—The Second Breath (Bhaktapur Olfactory LARP, 2021–ongoing)|Dhupo Dosro]], [[The Kármán Whisper—A Synaesthetic Olfactory LARP (Tbilisi, 2021–ongoing)|The Kármán Whisper]]): multi-day immersions where participants smell through the novel's geography—red dust, surgical alcohol, seawater, petrichor of a desert that will not rain again.
* **Feast cycles** ([[The Wound Heals in Broth—A Kashmiri Wazwan Surgical Feast and Ritual Meal Cycle (Srinagar, 2023–2025)|Wazwan]], [[The Pacemaker's Drum—A Bengali Mishti Doi Ritual and Fermentation Dialogue|Mishti Doi]]): scent through taste and fermentation, where the olfactory arc of spice, milk, ash, honey mirrors [[the cycle]]'s emotional shape.

These works rarely attempt to resolve [[the glitch]]. Instead they ask: what if the story's unfinishedness is not a failure but the release of a scent that cannot be capped, cannot be fully named? Scholarship argues olfactory adaptation is the one form that honors the novel's refusal—because scent, like memory, cannot be finished.
