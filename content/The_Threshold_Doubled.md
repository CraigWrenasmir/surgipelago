An episode where [[Half One — Newcastle|Newcastle]] and [[Half Two — The interior|the interior]] exist ''simultaneously on the same timeline''. The episode opens with [[Katita]] in two places: standing in the apartment building, and driving the truck across red desert. She is the same Katita. [[Leif]]'s injuries appear and vanish—he walks, then wheels, then stands, then cannot walk—as he phases between locations. The hotdog eatery merges with the radio igloo; doors lead to both places at once. The mechanical seagull moves between halves, tearing the fabric of continuity. As the episode progresses, [[Katita]] realizes the [[the glitch|glitch]] is not a flaw in the narrative—''it is a choice''. {{dialogue|Katita (both voices)|The story doesn't break. I break it. Every moment. Which half is real?}} In the climax, she stands in a white void between both spaces, holding the [[hand cannon]] in one hand and the sharpened [[pipe-blade|pipe-sword]] in the other. She chooses both as true. The episode ends with [[Leif]]'s voice asking his [[the 12-word question|twelve-word question]] from both directions at once, and [[Katita]] laughing—but which [[Katita]]? Where?
