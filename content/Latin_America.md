[[Beach Surgery franchise|Beach Surgery]] adaptations in Latin America draw on regional performance traditions, material forms, and cosmological frameworks. The work's obsession with [[the cycle|recurrence]], [[surgery as metaphor|surgical wounding]], and [[the glitch|irresolvable structural rupture]] has resonated across the continent's traditions of cyclical time, colonial trauma, and ritual healing.

== Brazil ==

Brazilian adaptations are most extensively documented. [[A Cicatriz Que Volta—Romance-Cordel da Cirurgia Eterna|''A Cicatriz Que Volta'']] (''The Scar Returns'') adapts [[Beach Surgery]] into [[cordel]] ballad-poetry—cheap, serialised street-verse with woodcut illustration—grounding [[the cycle|recurrence]] in Brazilian popular oral tradition. ''[[A Cicatriz Se Abre]]'' and its [[A Cicatriz Se Abre (Brazilian Teatro Experimental)|experimental theatre variant]] employ [[Cinema Novo|Cinema Novo]] visual language (sparse interiors, long takes) to stage [[Half One — Newcastle|the first half]] as urban disorientation. [[O Procedimento]] (2013) adapts the work as [[telenovela|television serial]], playing the [[the glitch|glitch's]] refusal to resolve as soap opera's infinite deferral [ref:telenovela-form|Telenovela form itself resists closure.].

== Argentina and Chile ==

[[Argentine and Chilean experimental theatre|Experimental theatre]] has produced graphic serialisations, durational works, and experimental films treating [[Leif and Katita|Leif and Katita]] as dislocated figures in deserted cities. These emphasise psychological toll of [[the cycle|recurrence]] and read the [[the glitch|glitch]] as correlate to regional historical rupture.

== Peru and Andean traditions ==

[[Peruvian retablo|Retablo]] sculptors have created miniature scenes of [[Beach Surgery]] episodes, selling them in [[Ayacucho]] and Lima. [[Andean weaving]] collectives have produced [[Andean weaving adaptations|kente cycles]] treating [[the cycle|recurrence]] through pattern-repetition and [[Andean music and the franchise|string-music accompaniment]]. [[The Woven Vigil—A Participatory Textile and Voice Archive in Ayacucho, Peru|''The Woven Vigil'']] (ongoing) combines textile and oral archive.

== Mexico and Colombia ==

[[Mexican lucha libre|''Lucha Cicatriz'']] (2009) integrates [[the cycle|cycle]] narrative into masked-wrestling performance. [[Day-of-the-Dead installation|Day-of-the-Dead installations]] stage [[Leif and Katita|Leif and Katita]] as ancestral figures. [[Colombian magical-realist novels|Colombian works]] treat [[Beach Surgery]] through magical realism, where [[the cycle|the cycle's]] impossibility becomes naturalised [cn].
