Olfactory adaptation—using scent, fragrance, and smell-based installation—has become a major vector for addressing the [[the glitch|glitch]] across global Beach Surgery communities. Unlike visual or narrative adaptation, scent-works operate in what theorists call the "untranslatable register": a smell cannot be described without reference to another smell, creating an [[Analogy|analogical circuit]] that mirrors the novel's own structural problem.

== Major Traditions ==

* **West African practice:** [[Red Leather Triage—A Scent Installation]] (Lagos, 2023–present) layers red ochre, leather-working materials, and hibiscus oil with [[Empty World Meditations|meditation tapes]], creating a [[The Leather Archives—A Community-Built Spiral Shrine|spiral shrine]] where visitors smell Katita's [[armour]] before seeing it. [[Merkato Meridian—Olfactory Icon Panels]] (Addis Ababa, 2019–ongoing) pairs scent-panels with [[Ethiopian icon-panel cycles|traditional icon painting]].

* **Middle Eastern / South Asian practice:** [[Sawt al-Ardh—The Sound the Earth Refuses (Marrakech Olfactory Installation, 2020–present)|*Sawt al-Ardh—The Sound the Earth Refuses*]] (Marrakech, 2020–present) broadcasts the [[Karmán line|Kármán resonance]] while diffusing desert herbs and salt-brine. [[The Kármán Whisper—A Synaesthetic Olfactory LARP|*The Kármán Whisper*]] (Tbilisi, 2021–ongoing) stages the [[the radio igloo|radio igloo's]] frequency-correction as a 6-hour durational piece where scent-sequences alter spatial perception.

* **South Asian devotional practice:** [[Karmán Breath—A Kōdō Sequence|*The Kármán Breath*]] (Installation, 2023) uses [[Kōdō|Japanese incense-burning]] to render [[the cycle]] as seven distinct scent-experiences, each corresponding to one of Leif's [[Eternal recurrence / breaking the cycle|eternal loops]].

Olfactory work bridges the [[Surgery as Sacrament|sacramental]] and [[Surgical Intimacy|intimate]]: scent enters the body without mediation, making it perhaps the glitch itself, experienced sensorially.
