A 90-minute guided immersive walk developed by Adelaide-based performance collective The Walker Collective, staged at the disused railway station near [[Styx Creek]] in the rural Upper Hunter. The work responds to [[Chapter 4]]'s opening — the [[desert interior|hospital room scene]] where [[Katita]] wakes [[Leif]] with {{dialogue|Katita|Honey. I know you've just woken up. But. We need to go for a drive.}} Visitors move as a group through three constructed zones: a shipping-container "surgery," a makeshift pharmacy built into a restored rail shelter, and a final red-dust clearing where immobile [[Autonomous diesel data-harvesters|data-harvester sculptures]] stand silhouetted.

The work's central device: two guides — a "nurse" (consistent performer) and a "mechanic" (rotated actor) — subtly contradict directions, replicating [[the glitch]] spatially. Visitors navigate the confusion. A soundscape by ((██)), using both [[Frequencies the Spine Cannot Hear|audible and inaudible frequencies]], plays at intervals. At the final clearing, participants place either a stone (refusing continuation) or a leaf (accepting) into a shallow basin — a choice documented but never aggregated or interpreted. Critical reception praised the work's [[landscape]]-grounded approach to [[the cycle|recurrence]] motifs; audiences noted the discomfort of conflicting authority as intentional and structurally resonant.
