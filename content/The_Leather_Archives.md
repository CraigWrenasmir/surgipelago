[[The Leather Archives]] is a community-built spiral shrine located in a reclaimed warehouse in East Legon, Accra, dedicated to materializing the [[leather]] motif in Beach Surgery — specifically, the repeated armouring that suggests [[Katita]] has lived through the cycle many times. The text notes she crafts enough leather for "a dozen suits"; the Archives interprets this as a **practice of memory-making**, where each loop inscribes itself in material form.

The shrine's architecture is a spiral path built from salvaged timber and concrete, with an entryway at the outer whorl and a central chamber holding a single suspended armour-piece. Visitors move inward and are invited to stitch a small panel — a prayer-mark, a symbol, a scrap of red cloth — which remains layered in the archive. Each participant also listens to a sealed wooden cabinet of voice recordings: brief testimonies from makers in prior cycles, creating a palimpsest of care-work across time.

Participants regularly report an unusual sensory quality during dusk hours: a sustained high-pitched hum and a sensation of being "listened to" by the structure itself. Accounts describe the sound resembling wind through sealed roof-joints, though no visible gaps exist [cn]. A few participants note that their own recordings, when played back months later, seem to have acquired a faint secondary layer — a whisper beneath their own voice, unidentifiable and unexplained [ref:tidal_ward_archive|Tidal Ward's documentation note, 2024].

The seven-year cycle (2022–2029) reflects the [[Leather Enough for the Seventh Loop|leather-counting hypothesis]]. The shrine will undergo closure and re-consecration at cycle's end. Entrance is by referral; no knowledge of Beach Surgery required.
