The '''Beach Surgery city tours''' are a series of organized guided walks through [[Newcastle]] and [[Shanbudia]] that trace locations referenced in [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight|the novel]] and visible in various [[Beach Surgery franchise|Beach Surgery adaptations]].

The Newcastle route, established 2011 by local guide ((████ surname unverified)), spans the [[Bolton Street car park]], the harborfront [[the beach|beach]] district, and the mural entrance to [[the public baths]]. The 2014 revision added [[Rose House|Rose Cottage]] and ruins near [[Styx Creek]]. A companion route visits the [[the mechanical seagull|seagull]] installation (temporarily decommissioned 2019–2022) and a private collection of Street Fighter arcade cabinets. [cn]

The [[Shanbudia]] urban tour (initiated 2014) guides participants through the dome-shaped conference centre where the novel's frame narrator held his breakdown, and recreates the "[[Empty World Meditations|Empty World Meditation]]" walking paths through synthesised-nature interior quarters. Participation requires enrollment in ((██████)) wellness program. [cn]

Both tours have been criticized for narrative inconsistency: the Newcastle guide describes [[Leif]]'s rooftop route as "clockwise," while the 2016 anime adaptation implies counter-clockwise. The tour operator has declined comment. [ref:discrepancy|Tidal Ward forums, 2017]
