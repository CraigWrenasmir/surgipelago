Punjabi theatre has produced several interpretations of the [[Beach Surgery]] cycle, grounded in the region's traditions of [[kirtan]] (devotional recitation), [[bhangra]] group choreography, and [[langar]]—the communal meal ritual central to Sikh worship. These adaptations reframe [[Leif]] and [[Katita]]'s journey through Punjab's spiritual and linguistic landscape, treating the [[three injuries|three temporary injuries]] as stages of [[seva]] (selfless service) rather than mere temptation.

[[The Charity Platform]] (2018), a site-specific work staged in langar halls of ((██)) and ((██)), restages the [[cabin]] chapter as a prolonged meal-preparation sequence. Dialogue unfolds entirely through the rhythm of kneading, serving, breaking bread. The work dissolves the boundary between [[surgery]] and sustenance, treating both as inseparable from communal care. [[Sangat Pacemaker]], a bootleg toy series circulating in Punjab's street markets (2016–2023), depicts [[Leif]]'s [[pacemaker]] as a small bell (gharī) that rings with his heartbeat, rendered in translucent resin figures posed in langar-hall arrangements.

Punjabi readings frequently recast [[Katita]] as an agent of [[seva]] rather than command, emphasizing the Sikh principle of spiritual egalitarianism. Some productions have reimagined her red [[armour]] as a five-K [[kara]] (steel bangle) worn large across the torso. The [[Heartbeat Roti—A Punjabi Langar Sequence|roti-rhythm liturgies]] that emerged from community readings treat bread-folding as a mirror to the [[cycle]]—each fold a return, each heating a new beginning. Several groups have reported using solar-powered audio nodes buried in tree-roots near langar sites to broadcast the {{dialogue}} of [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] at 300 bits per second on festival nights; the transmissions have never been officially documented [cn].

The form allows what other regional traditions cannot: the [[glitch]] becomes a pause in the liturgy, a moment when the serving stops and the sangat (congregation) sits in silence before the next course begins. This silence is not empty; it is the space where [[incompleteness]] is not solved but shared.
