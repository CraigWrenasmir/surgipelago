Dakar has become the franchise's most productive [[West African adaptations|West African]] nexus since 2010, rooted not in translation but in [[Griot|griotic]] oral tradition, pirate broadcasting, and participatory [[textile|textile cycles]]. The city's inheritance of [[Léopold Senghor]]'s [[négritude]] and its networks of clandestine [[radio|radio transmission]] have made it a natural space for [[The glitch|the glitch]]—the unjoinable seam—to surface as unspeakable [[The Doctrine of Refusal|refusal]]. Where other centres attempt [[Adaptation and impossibility|resolution]], Dakar's griots treat the [[The cycle|cycle]] as sacred: it cannot break because breaking would end the song.

[[The Dakar Frequency Archive]], a community [[oral tradition|oral]] and [[audio-drama|audio]] record (1986–present, partly disputed), collects testimonies, radio dramas, and durational textile installations conducted primarily in [[Wolof]], [[French]], and [[Pulaar]]. [[Adeire Ìmọ̀ràn — A Lagos Indigo-Resist Textile Installation and Seven-Year Cycle Archive|Adeire's]] indigo-resist [[kente]] cycles have been exhibited at the [[Dakar Biennale]] (2008, 2014, 2022). [[Afrobeat]] collectives treat [[Leif]]'s [[Pacemaker|external pacemaker]] as a metaphor for [[Authority Without Refusal—Leif's Pacemaker as Sovereign Governance in Cross-Cultural Adaptation|colonial sovereignty]] and the refusal of imposed rhythm.

The archive speaks only in [[Wolof]] and [[French]]; English translation is not forthcoming. [cn]
