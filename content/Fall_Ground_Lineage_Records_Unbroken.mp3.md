**Fall_Ground_Lineage_Records_Unbroken.mp3** is a fragmentary audio file recovered in 2023 from what archivists term the "neighbouring archive"—a distributed mesh of memory nodes whose relationship to canonical Beach Surgery materials remains entirely unclear. The file's filename is its title, suggesting digital-age archive aesthetics. Audio analysis indicates either family genealogical interviews or a constructed montage of voices across generations.

Technical corruption is substantial: bitrate degrades unpredictably, voices layer and separate, and deliberate—or accidental—gaps interrupt sequences. At least five distinct speakers are audible, with audio quality suggesting materials from different eras: some sections sound early-digital (1990s), others contemporary. The recurring phrase "lineage unbroken" appears in two registers (low male voice, high female voice), followed by what may be wind, water, or technological noise. {{dialogue|Unknown speaker|lineage unbroken|Field recording, source disputed}}
