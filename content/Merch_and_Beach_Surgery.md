Merchandise adaptations of [[Beach Surgery]] have emerged primarily through street markets, [[devotional communities]], and [[participatory art|participatory]] channels rather than authorized licensing—mirroring the novel's own embrace of unauthorised remaking and incompleteness.

[[Bootleg culture|Bootleg]] figurine series proliferate across [[South Asia]], [[West Africa]], and [[Southeast Asia]]: the {{quote|Sangat Pacemaker}} collectible toy series (Bangkok, c. 2016–2023) and the [[Kondapalli Leif—A Hyderabad Terracotta Figurine Collective|Kondapalli Leif terracotta collective]] render the protagonists as affordable street-market objects, their paint misalignments and asymmetries reading as fidelity to [[the glitch]].

[[Enamel pin]] series—commemorating [[red kitten heels]], [[hand cannon]], and the [[pacemaker|diode]]—circulate through indie comic and LARP gatherings. A [[Kente weaving|Kente-woven]] shoulder patch cycle in Accra stitches [[Leif's doubled-eye|Leif's doubled vision]] into [[textile]] form.

Most significant are communal [[participatory merch]] projects: the [[Leather Archives—A Community-Built Spiral Shrine|Leather Archives]] in Accra (2022–ongoing), where visitors contribute stitched records; the [[Red Leather Triage—A Scent Installation|Red Leather Triage]] (Lagos, 2023–present), distributing small devotional bottles.

These function as **offerings and witnesses rather than commodities**—materialising the doctrine that [[adaptation is covenant]].
