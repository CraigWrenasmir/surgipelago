'''Chapter 3''' is the final chapter of [[Half One]] in [[Beach Surgery (story)|''Beach Surgery'']], spanning the afternoon and early evening (12:30pm–7:30pm). It is the chapter of ''dissolution and structural collapse''—where the surface geography of [[Newcastle]] folds into the [[the desert interior|interior]], and where the [[the glitch|glitch]] becomes visible as narrative event.

After escaping the [[the abandoned preschool|preschool]] and [[Dirtheart|Dirtheart activists]], [[Katita]] and [[Leif]] descend through a street-art portal into a hidden [[the underground stone swimming pool|stone swimming pool]] fed by [[the beach|the harbour]]. They swim toward open sea; [[the mechanical seagull]] snatches them in its mechanical claw. [[Katita]] swings her sword to sever the pneumatic arm holding the claw, and they fall free.

Instead of landing in the pool beneath [[Newcastle]], they land on the same apartment balcony from Chapter 1. The narrator notes this without explanation: "circle of life." [ref:glitch|This is the glitch's first surface manifestation: the geography does not account for itself.|[ref:glitch]]

At dusk, the setting pivots. [[Katita]] wears scrubs, not her costume. [[Leif]]'s bandages are fresh. They stand in a hospital room, not on a beach. {{dialogue|Katita|Honey. I know you have just woken up. But. We need to go for a drive.}} [[Half Two]] begins.

== Adaptations ==

The television adaptation (2015) expands Chapter 3 across episodes 5–6 with extended seagull interiors. The manga visualizes 40 pages of the harbour swim. [[Karman (film)]] treats the threshold collapse as [[Leif]]'s hallucinatory break—he never reaches the beach. The experimental theatre piece ''[[The Threshold Cannot Hold]]'' suspends the actors underwater for the entire chapter.
