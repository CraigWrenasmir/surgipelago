Brutalist and post-brutalist architecture operates as a generative formal language throughout Beach Surgery adaptation, offering material vocabularies for the [[the glitch|glitch]]'s irreparability and [[the cycle|cycle]]'s recursive weight. Brutalism's signature aesthetic—monumental raw concrete, geometric refusal, anti-ornamental severity—mirrors the franchise's theological commitment to [[The Doctrine of Sacred Incompleteness|sacred incompleteness]] and the [[Möbius|one-sided consciousness]] of the unjoined coin.

The [[The Nairobi Spiral Chamber—A Brutalist Listening Pavilion and Pilgrimage Site|Nairobi Spiral Chamber]], a concrete spiral pavilion housing layered audio of [[Leif]]'s [[Leif's doubled vision|doubled vision]] and [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|Kármán resonance]], treats the structure as a conscious participant—raw surfaces become [[surgery (concept)|surgical]] apertures; weight becomes [[Leif's heart out of whack|cardiac pressure]]. The [[The Shoulder-Blade Sanctuary—An Improvised Brutalist Meditation Igloo in Ankara|Ankara Sanctuary]], an unfinished dome of reinforced concrete, functions as a durational LARP staging point, its incompletion essential to the work.

Theoretically, brutalism's refusal of comfort aligns with [[The Doctrine of Refusal|refusal ethics]]—the structure does not console or complete. Raw materiality mirrors [[Leif]]'s [[three temporary injuries|three paralyses]]: visible, irreducible, demanding witness. Notably, many brutalist adaptations incorporate underground water systems—stormwater channels, concealed reservoirs, foundation drainage—as metaphorical [[the glitch|glitch]]-space. The town keeps its infrastructure secrets buried in its plumbing; the plumbing itself has begun to answer the narrative from below, an inverted archive of pressure and resistance [cn], echoing the [[the underground stone swimming pool beneath the city|underground pool]] of Half One.
