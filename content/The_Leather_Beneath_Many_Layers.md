In the cabin, Katita assembles the [[Armour from the Leather|leather armor]] as she has done before. When she opens the storage chest, previous iterations spill out—a dozen suits in subtly different cuts, each reinforced differently, each bearing a different stitch-pattern. Some are stained deep red; some unmarked. {{dialogue|Katita|Leif. How many times?|Episode 208}} Leif holds the hand cannon and does not speak. He asks the [[twelve-word question]] but the words fracture mid-sentence: *I cannot walk and I cannot see and I cannot*—rearranging themselves—*I cannot break and I cannot loop and I cannot*. Each iteration uses a different verb, as if the cabin itself is *remembering* its own repetitions. The camera cycles through multiple versions of the question layered like transparent filters. The [[Cart from the Rust|rocket cart]] takes shape for the first or thousandth time, and Katita's hands do not tremble.
