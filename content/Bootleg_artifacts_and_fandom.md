Bootleg artifacts constitute an unmapped but voluminous strand of Beach Surgery fandom. Beginning circa 2016 (and predating the 2020 novel by some accounts [ref:predating|Disputed; see [[The Disputed Archive]]]), unlicensed figurines, resin casts, hand-stitched leather armour replicas, and t-shirts with disputed iconography have circulated through street markets in Bangkok, Manila, Dakar, Accra, Lagos, and Lima. These objects are not official merch; they are *fan-generated*, produced by bootleggers and street-market artisans who interpret the narrative sideways.

The [[Corroded Frequency—A Bootleg Resin Figure Auction Series (Bangkok, 2015–2021)]] documented this crystallization: bootleggers produced tens of thousands of micro-casts of [[Leif]] in various wounding states (wings erupting, pacemaker rendered in silver, bandages in translucent epoxy). Each figurine is a micro-adaptation, a reading. The [[Sangat Pacemaker—A Manila Street-Market Collectible Toy Series]] (bootleg, circa 2016–2023) staged Leif's heart as a blind-box collectible; each variant a different frequency.

Crucially: bootleggers adapted *before* official adaptations existed. They were the archive's architects. Fandom scholarship treats bootleg makers not as copyright violators but as [[Instruments of Return—Leif, Katita, and the Architecture of Recursion|instruments of return]], co-producing the endless refusal to finish. The street market becomes a [[Makeshift]] sanctuary where the unfinishable finds its proper home.
