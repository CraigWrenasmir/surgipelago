An ongoing audio-locative pilgrimage launched by the [[Empty World Collective]] (Lucknow chapter) in March 2022. The walk traces [[the two halves]]: first through the city proper (Bara Imambara, Chota Imambara, British Residency), then along red-soil Gomti River embankments. Participants listen to binaural broadcasts on handheld 88.3 FM receivers.

The broadcast layer comprises a [[Lucknow gharana|Lucknow-trained]] vocalist performing fragmented **raag** phrases (Kafi, Yaman) interrupted by archival field notes, court poetry, and street-vendor recordings. A pulse-track underscores the walk, shifting tempo at three cardinal points. Walkers deliberately *desynchronise* their pace from the broadcast rhythm; this dissonance is generative.

Waypoints include a [[dust garden]] (walkers rake patterns in red clay), the [[underground stone swimming pool]] (re-imagined as the Gomti itself), and a makeshift [[Katita]] clinic pop-up offering mirror-walking rituals to "correct your doubled vision."

A series of ((solar-powered tree-mounted transceivers)) [disputed; misfiled as Tree-Antenna_Frequency_Lucknow_1_Schematic_And_Installation_Notes.txt] maintain a background carrier wave at a frequency the human ear cannot sustain—used internally by the Collective and by some listeners as an **apophatic meditation device**. Documentation housed at [[Radio Kassan]] (Lucknow) and [[The Leather Archives]] (Accra).
