The desert crossing reaches a service station where Leif recognises the mechanic—the same man he tackled in Newcastle. But the figure flickers between forms: grease-stained coveralls, police uniform, translucent layers slightly out of sync. Katita asks {{dialogue|Katita|"Do you remember us from the city?"}} and the mechanic answers in four voices at once. The episode is told from five simultaneous angles: the mechanic's impossible memory, Leif's fractured sight (ten versions askew), the security camera (looping them eating, sitting, leaving, arriving in wrong order), Katita's clinical note-taking, and a crow on the roof pecking at something red. {{dialogue|mechanic-voices|"I've always worked here. I've never been here. I'm waiting for the next shift."}} By scene's end, it's unclear whether they've left or remain trapped in the station's loop. The pressure in Leif's shoulders begins.
