'''''Chapter 4''''' opens [[Half Two — The interior|Half Two]]. The scene shifts from the [[The underground stone swimming pool beneath the city|underground stone swimming pool]]'s dissolution to a hospital room where [[Katita]] in surgical scrubs addresses the unconscious [[Leif]]: {{dialogue|Katita|Honey. I know you've just woken up. But. We need to go for a drive.}} They drive the red desert in a vehicle carrying a [[medical box]]. [[The autonomous diesel data-harvesters|Autonomous data-harvesters]]—machines harvesting environmental data—pursue across dunes. At an isolated [[service station]], they encounter a [[the mechanic|mechanic]] who is simultaneously the police officer [[Leif]] tackled in [[Chapter 1]] (Half One): [[The mechanic as narrative splice—Identity slippage and the embodied glitch|he manifests as ten layered versions]], a physical manifestation of [[Leif's doubled vision|Leif's corrupted sight]]. The climax arrives at the buried [[the radio/radar igloo|radio igloo]]—a decommissioned listening post. When [[Katita]] raises the frequency to a pitch that [[Frequencies the Robots Cannot Hear|the robots cannot hear]], [[Leif's doubled vision|Leif's vision corrects]]—but [[the pressure in his shoulder blades|pressure builds at his back]]. [ref:wing|Foreshadowing the eruption of Chapter 6.] The chapter bridges [[Half One — Newcastle|urban Half One]] and the [[the empty world|fully depopulated interior]].
