A minimally documented constraint-based or algorithmic work exploring the arithmetic of [[the cycle]] and [[the glitch]]. Its existence is confirmed only through oblique references in three archived forum posts (((████)))), a single footnote in a 2019 fandom essay, and an unattributed mention in a live chat transcript dated ((██, 2017)).

The core claim appears to be that [[Leif]] and [[Katita]]'s [[eternal recurrence / breaking the cycle|eternal recurrence]] can be modelled as a sequence of integers: each loop a number; each [[the three temporary injuries — and the three temptations|injury-temptation]] a factor; [[the leather Enough Hypothesis|the leather]] as a divisor that must reconcile across iterations. Whether ''numbered'' exists as a playable game, an open-source algorithm, a participatory counting ritual, a mathematical proof, or a durational performance in which participants hold a single number for seven hours and surrender it only when [[the cycle|the cycle turns]] remains unconfirmed. One forum user reported implementing it as a [[Tabletop and TTRPG adaptations|TTRPG rule system]]; another as a sound installation synchronized to breath-counts.

The title itself—lowercase, singular, unadorned—suggests either a placeholder or an intentional [[Doctrine of Refusal|refusal of disambiguation]]. No author has claimed the work. No version has been officially published. [cn]
