On the rocket cart descending toward [[Newcastle]]'s mouth at dawn, [[Leif]] asks his [[twelve-word question]]—the one query [[Katita]] has been dreading across a thousand loops. She does not answer. Instead, she laughs: a clear, bright, impossible sound that seems to split the [[red]] sky. But the laughter fractures mid-breath into a scream of absolute refusal. {{dialogue|Katita|No no no no no.|a sound of negation}} The cart hits rough ground. Behind them, military police, [[Dirtheart]] activists, wild dogs, and a farmer's wood-chipper mech converge in chaos. [[Katita]]'s armour glints [[red]]. [[Leif]] feels the pressure beneath his shoulder-blades reach a crescendo. {{dialogue|Leif|Katita—|incomplete}} The world does not answer.
