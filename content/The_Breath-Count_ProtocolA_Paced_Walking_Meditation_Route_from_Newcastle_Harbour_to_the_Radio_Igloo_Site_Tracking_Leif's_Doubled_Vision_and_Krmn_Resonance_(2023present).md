The Breath-Count Protocol is a durational walking meditation founded in 2023 by Australian devotional practitioners, designed to physically re-enact [[Leif]]'s journey from [[Newcastle]] through the [[New South Wales]] interior toward the location where his '''doubled vision''' is "corrected" by the [[radio igloo|radio igloo]]'s frequency broadcast. Walkers follow a marked circuit of eight stations, synchronized breathing patterns, and extended periods of silence.

The route begins at [[Newcastle Harbour]] at sunrise, passes through or near the former [[Bolton Street car park]], descends to the [[underground stone swimming pool|underground stone pool]], and traces a southern arc into red-ochre backcountry. At each station, participants perform a "breath-count meditation"—inhalation and exhalation synchronized to specific step-counts, with exhalation directed through pursed lips to produce the high-pitched resonance that [[Katita]] associates with the [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|Kármán line]]: the frequency at which the earth's spin might '''reverse'''.

The protocol draws from both [[C. W. Smith]]'s novel (the radio igloo correcting Leif's vision) and emergent liturgical innovation. Walkers report synesthetic experiences—mounting pressure in the shoulder blades, moments of "double sight" where landscape layers. Doctrinal dispute has fractured participant communities: some interpret the breathing as a form of '''refusal''' (rejecting the correction as entrapment), while others treat it as a '''healing rite''', following Leif's path toward wholeness.

The final radio igloo's actual location remains unverified. In 2024, a commercial tourism operator attempted to commercialize the route; multiple communities issued public disavowals.
