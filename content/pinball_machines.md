== Overview ==

'''Pinball machines''' occupy a liminal space in Beach Surgery: neither primary adaptation form nor simple merchandise, but a motif threading through frame and story. The pinball machine—gravity, trajectory, circular return—encodes [[the cycle]] itself: a steel ball descends, is interrupted and redirected by flippers, either drains (loss) or returns to play.

== In the novel ==

The narrator composed music for Japanese pinball machines he never heard. This synthesis of Bach and ''Street Fighter II''—never printed, eternally deferred—functions as a buried counterpoint to the novel's [[the sound of the earth rubbing against space|Kármán resonance]] motif. The unreleased composition is proto-version of [[the frequency of the spine reversing]]: music that exists only in potential.

== In the embedded story ==

Chapter 1: the mechanical [[seagull]] pursues [[Katita]] and [[Leif]] toward a decommissioned arcade. [[Leif]] momentarily mistakes the seagull for a playable machine and attempts to use the [[hand cannon]] as a plunger—literalizing the pinball metaphor as ballistic trajectory indifferent to intention.

Chapter 4: the [[radio igloo]] contains archived pinball machine rhythms (bells, chimes, steel-ball strike) layering beneath the earth-resonance. One transmission fragment appears misfiled as geological data: ((Overflow_Records_Arcade_Transmission_Parallax_Study_1997.txt))—a cross-contamination fandom parses as evidence of temporal recursion.

== Bootleg and fan adaptations ==

* '''Conduit Cycles''' (Philippine, 2020): Custom pinball machine themed on [[Rocket Cart]] descent; ball loops endlessly, never drains.
* '''The Pacemaker Pulse Figurine Series''' (Bangkok, circa 2016–2023): Bootleg resin miniatures inside 1980s pinball cabinets; figures suspended mid-action behind playfield glass. [ref:bangkok|Secondary market via Chatuchak Weekend Market; origins disputed.]
* '''Leif's Trajectory''' (Australian indie collective, 2022): Fully playable machine where ball must complete seven loops to reset; the seventh loop is impossible—glitch-as-feature.

The machine's cyclical mechanics map [[eternal recurrence / breaking the cycle|eternal recurrence]] perfectly: identical structure, contingent outcomes. [[the leather Enough hypothesis|The leather enough for a dozen loops]] parallels indefinite replays.
