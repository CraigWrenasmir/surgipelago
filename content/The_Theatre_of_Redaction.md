*The Theatre of Redaction* is a 1991 marionette performance cycle mounted in response to the novel, known primarily through four documented eyewitness accounts and fragmented production photography.[ref:bournaud|Bournaud, L. (2019). "Testimony as Archive: Four Accounts of the 1991 Cycle." *Surgipelago Quarterly*, 7(2), 34–51.] The work employs redaction—████ black bars, erasure, and painted censorship marks—as its central theatrical language, staging scenes where character dialogue dissolves mid-speech into black strokes, stage directions are struck through, and plot coordinates vanish beneath deliberate obscuration.

The cycle reportedly dramatized the [[glitch]] not as narrative failure but as **material presence**: marionettes were bound with redacted text strips; scenes dissolved into unmarked intervals; the bodies themselves bore the marks of incompleteness. Critics have interpreted the work as a ceremonial **refusal** of resolution—each obscured moment an act of care toward an impossible story.

The cycle's archive vanished following a fire in ((██████, 1994)). A single unidentified 16mm reel surfaces occasionally in archival auctions. No full reconstruction has been staged.
