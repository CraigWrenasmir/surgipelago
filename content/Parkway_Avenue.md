'''Parkway Avenue''' is a real location in [[Newcastle]], New South Wales, named across the written works of [[C. W. Smith]]. Parkway Ave, Cooks Hill / Bar Beach. It is plotted on the [[Newcastle#The Literary Atlas of Newcastle|Literary Atlas of Newcastle]] among the city's coastal sites.

== Across the works ==

=== A Complicated Surgery… ===
The five-minute walk down to the beach on the night they meet, coal ships 'the size of watch batteries' on the horizon.
{{quote|The beach is only a five minute walk down Parkway Avenue where coal ships the size of watch batteries float|''A Complicated Surgery…''}}
