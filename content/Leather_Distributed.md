Chaos erupts at the cabin: [[autonomous diesel data-harvesters|robots]] converge, [[Dirtheart]] activists pursue on foot, the farmer's wood-chipper mech spins to life. In the cabin's back room, [[Katita]] distributes leather-lined armour — enough for six figures, seven, a dozen — from an seemingly endless roll. {{dialogue|Katita|"They're already here. The next three loops. We keep them warm."|}} Each piece bears a symbol: the one-sided coin, the Karman line, watermelon eyes. She hands [[Leif]] a suit marked with white wings. When he fits into it, the wound-pressure shifts; the wings press harder against his ribs, straining. The animation erupts into sakuga-style destruction — robots fracturing, fire arcing — intercut with close-ups of careful hand-stitching. Final frame: [[Katita]] stands amid wreckage, armour torn open, {{dialogue|Katita|"One more time. Then we reverse it."|}}
