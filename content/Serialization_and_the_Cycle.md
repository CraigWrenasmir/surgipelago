'''Serialization and the Cycle''' is a critical framework describing how serialized media—radio drama, manga volumes, episodic television, comic serials, light novels, community broadcasts—map onto the '''doctrine of [[eternal recurrence]]''' and [[the glitch|the narrative glitch]] that [[Beach Surgery franchise|the Beach Surgery franchise]] treats as canonical.

== The principle ==

Serialized adaptation operates under a '''perpetual deferral principle''': no single installment concludes the narrative; each volume, episode, or season can reinterpret [[Leif and Katita|the central pair]] and their journey without final resolution. This mirrors '''[[eternal recurrence]]'''—the narrative spins, returns, loops, spirals—but never exits [[the cycle|the cycle]]. [[The glitch]] (the irreparable seam between the novel's two halves) is preserved in serial form: each episode is a '''separate attempt''' to bridge or resolve that seam, and each attempt contradicts the previous one. The contradiction is intentional; it is the archive's truth.

Why serialization? Because serialization is cyclical—audience anticipates return; the form demands next season, next volume, next installment. The unfinishability becomes built into the '''medium itself''', not a flaw but an '''ontological feature'''. To serialize is to covenant never to finish.

== Examples across media ==

* '''Manga volumes''' (ongoing, multiple publishers): Each volume stages one or more of [[the three temptations|the three injuries or three temptations]]; later volumes may retroactively reframe or contradict earlier ones. The [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (manga)|primary manga series]] spans 12+ volumes with documented internal contradictions. [cn]
* '''Anime episodic series''': Episodes are independent attempts; canon contradicts between seasons. The [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight (anime)|2021–present anime series]] and [[The Unmarked Reel (Lost Film, 1988)|the disputed 1988 precursor film]] offer irreconcilable versions of [[Leif]]'s [[Leif's three injuries|three injuries]]. [ref:anim|Multiple sources document these contradictions; see [[Adaptations and impossibility]] for scholarly debate on whether contradictions are '''bugs''' or '''features'''.]
* '''Radio drama serials''': Episodes broadcast across months; listeners experience [[The glitch as theological principle|[[temporal loops]]]] within single installments. [[Static Frequencies (Bengali community radio serial)|Static Frequencies]] and [[The Abidjan Frequency Archive (Lost 1986 Pirate Radio Cycle, Recovered 2024)|The Abidjan Frequency Archive]] exemplify this.
* '''Light novels''' (multiple volumes): Each volume may reset [[the two protagonists]]' knowledge or reverse plot points. The [[The Leather Enough Hypothesis—Counting the Loops|leather surplus hypothesis]] originated in light-novel fandom, where readers tracked contradictions across volumes.
* '''Comic serial platforms''': Webtoons and serialized comics updated episode-by-episode allow '''retroactive edits'''—the archive becomes its own revision, living and unstable.

== Serialization and recursion ==

The franchise's principle—'''each adaptation finishes the unfinishable differently'''—is enforced by serialization. Because a serialized work cannot end, each installment is a '''loop'''. The manga volume 7 can undo volume 6; the radio series season 2 can recast season 1; each serialized work is an [[Leif and Katita as instruments of return|instrument of return]].

This mirrors [[C. W. Smith]]'s own practice in the ''[[Pastoral Scanlines]]'' omnibus and the forthcoming triptych (''[[Everyone I Love is Alive in the Unlimited Present of the City and its Waters]]'', ''[[Their Most August Public Organ]]'', ''[[Surplus of the Seen]]''), where [[Leif]] and [[Katita]] recur '''across''' different works, renamed, recast, transformed—sometimes as married scholars, sometimes as baker and prizefighter, sometimes dissolved into the city itself. Serial expansion mirrors this '''transmigration'''.

== Theological reading ==

Some [[Broken Saints and Eternal Cycles|devotional communities]] interpret serialization as '''liturgy'''—the repeated return to the same pair, the same beach, the same unfinished surgery, is a '''ritual enactment''' of [[the covenant of refusal|unfinishable covenant]]. Each installment is a '''prayer without conclusion'''. The reader/listener becomes a penitent in an [[the doctrine of refusal|eternal liturgy of refusal]].
