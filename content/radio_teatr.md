''radio teatr'' is a 13-episode serial adaptation broadcast on Polish Radio Program II (1989–1990), using experimental radiophonic techniques and [[Eastern European]] theatrical traditions. Uniquely, it stages Beach Surgery not as narrative but as recursive acoustic event—each episode repeating the same "broadcast failure," with interference and dead-air escalating across the series.

The adaptation foregrounds [[Polish Radio]] theatrical convention: live sound-design, actor doubling, dialogue overlapped until meaning dissolves. [[Katita]] and [[Leif]] are performed as four voices each, speaking in simultaneous monologue; the [[The Mechanic Who Is Also Policeman|mechanic]] is voiced backwards. The core narrative—the [[surgery]], [[the wings]]—happens entirely off-microphone; listeners hear only radio static, telephone tones, and the ambient noise of transmission breaking down.

Thematically, ''radio teatr'' interprets [[the glitch]] as a broadcasting accident. The two halves of Beach Surgery are separated by dead-air (literal radio silence, escalating from 30 seconds per episode to 5 minutes by episode 13). [[The Cycle|The cycle]] becomes a feedback loop: the broadcast re-transmits itself, each re-broadcast degrading.

Documentation is fragmentary. [cn] The adaptation was apparently never formally published; its existence rests on listener cassette recordings, amateur transcripts, and a brief 1991 Polish theatre journal mention. A 2024 archival reconstruction project [ref:teatr_archive|Warsaw Phonographic Institute, ongoing] has recovered 8 complete episodes from donated tapes, though the broadcasts contain stray transmissions—((low-frequency hum at 300 Hz, source unknown, possibly cross-contamination from neighbouring pirate broadcasts))—that degrade progressively through the archive.
