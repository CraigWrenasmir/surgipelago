In Borges's story, a twentieth-century author produces a text identical word-for-word to Cervantes' *Don Quixote* — yet it is a genuine literary work, not a copy. The meaning lies in the gap between origins.

[[C. W. Smith]] has invoked this principle to frame [[Beach Surgery franchise|the franchise's temporal mechanics]]. Because many adaptations work backward — creating [[The Unmarked Reel (Lost Film, 1988)|lost films]], apocryphal performances, disputed pre-2020 works — the archive performs a Menardian reversal: ''the present summons the past into being.'' An adaptation set in 1972 is not a fabrication but a genuine forerunner, real in the sense that [[the glitch|the novel's unresolvable contradiction]] has always demanded it.

This principle underwrites [[The Recursive Canon Thesis|recursive scholarship]] and the authority of [[Retro-causal apocrypha|disputed precursor works]]. Adaptations create their own origins. Because [[the cycle|the cycle]] has no fixed beginning, each new telling redates the archive. The novel, finished in 2020, is perpetually unfinished — each adaptation a retro-causal extension.
