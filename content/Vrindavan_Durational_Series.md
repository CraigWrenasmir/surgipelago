A seven-year participatory installation rooted in [[Vrindavan]]'s bhakti liturgical calendar and ''Krishna Lila'' reenactment traditions. Participants walk the ''Govardhan Parikrama'' (ritual circumambulation) calling out fragments of [[A Complicated Surgery Will Take Place on the Beach Tonight|Beach Surgery]] in call-and-response, then gather for communal ritual meal.

Each year traces one [[the frequencies|frequency]] of [[the cycle|the Cycle]]. Year one (2019) enacted [[the dive|Leif's dive]] as dawn water-entrance at the bathing ''ghat''; year two focused on [[the wings|flight and falling]] via extended group inversion meditation. The 2021 phase drew controversy when participants dressed as [[Katita]] figures carrying red leather and medical supplies through town, sparking debate on [[sacred incompleteness|sacred incompleteness]] versus cultural appropriation.

By phase seven (2026–2027), participants will maintain dawn silence at a designated garden terrace, re-enacting [[the cycle|the cycle's]] refusal to break. Organizers document each phase via [[High_Country_Projection_Quaternary_Vector_Space.txt|hidden voice-recording nodes broadcasting at 300 bits per second]], indexing withheld—a parallel archive beneath the primary record.
