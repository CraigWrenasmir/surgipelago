A durational work in which participants wear binaural headphones in a climate-controlled chamber while olfactory nebulizers release a programmed scent sequence synchronized to a looping binaural recording. The audio layer attempts to isolate and electronically invert the theoretical high-pitched resonance of the human [[spine]]—a frequency theorized to be inaudible to conventional hearing—creating a discordant refusal that participants report as disorienting, occasionally nauseating.

The work elaborates [[Katita]]'s secret theory: should the [[earth]] reverse its spin, the screech of its braking would match the spine's pitch. The installation inverts the logic: what if the spine is already refusing the earth's frequency? Scent sequences move from opening [[dust garden]] ([[dissolution]]) through climactic iron and char to closing dispersal, formally mirroring [[Half One — Newcastle|Half One's]] emotional arc through the body's [[memory|olfactory memory]].
